# Local fonts

These unmodified webfonts are served locally from the official Google Fonts
distribution. Both families use the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the corresponding
license texts are included alongside the files.

| Family and use | Asset | Weights | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barlow Condensed, headings | `barlow-condensed-600-latin.woff2` | 600 | [Google Fonts CDN, v13](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/barlowcondensed/v13/HTxwL3I-JCGChYJ8VI-L6OO_au7B4873z3bWuQ.woff2) | [OFL-Barlow-Condensed.txt](OFL-Barlow-Condensed.txt) |
| Barlow Condensed, headings | `barlow-condensed-700-latin.woff2` | 700 | [Google Fonts CDN, v13](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/barlowcondensed/v13/HTxwL3I-JCGChYJ8VI-L6OO_au7B46r2z3bWuQ.woff2) | [OFL-Barlow-Condensed.txt](OFL-Barlow-Condensed.txt) |
| DM Sans, body | `dm-sans-variable-latin.woff2` | 400–700 variable | [Google Fonts CDN, v17](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/dmsans/v17/rP2Yp2ywxg089UriI5-g4vlH9VoD8Cmcqbu0-K4.woff2) | [OFL-DM-Sans.txt](OFL-DM-Sans.txt) |

The Latin subset covers Spanish accents, `ñ`, `ü`, and inverted punctuation.
Barlow Condensed is distributed here as the requested static 600 and 700 weights;
DM Sans is a variable font and covers regular and medium weights.
