# Turbo Lumber Evolution ("Timber"): visual geometry, preview

The **visual model** of the fifth catalog aircraft, shown as "Turbo Lumber Evolution" ("Turbo Tronco Evolución" in Spanish): a fictional name for a model inspired by the E-flite Turbo Timber Evolution 1.5 m, drawn by its author from photographs without manufacturer plans or artwork. "Timber" remains the internal codename of the track. The runtime asset is the author's r1 glTF game delivery, imported by Godot's scene importer; nothing is modelled procedurally. No physics data exists yet (TT-07), so the aircraft is a preview: it builds, poses and renders, and cannot fly. Plan and step status: [TIMBER-INTEGRATION-PLAN](../../../docs/TIMBER-INTEGRATION-PLAN.md).

## Frames and datum

Metres, +X right, +Y up, −Z nose. The origin D0 is the symmetry plane × the wing-root leading-edge station × the thrust-axis height, exactly as the r1 export; `AirplaneBuilder.datum()` is therefore `(0, 0)`. Aircraft-contract positions convert as `[x_aft, y_right, z_up] = [z_model, x_model, y_model]`.

## Builder contract

`build()` returns `{root, propeller, hinges, gear, has_propeller = true, data, aircraft_id}`. `root` is the imported scene root renamed `airplane` (one child, `TurboTimberEvolution`, identity at D0). Hinges (`aileron_left/right`, `elevator`, `rudder`, `flap_left/right`) are the imported pivot nodes with `{node, axis, rest, pivot, range_deg, positive, app_sign}`; a deflection sets `node.basis = Basis(axis, angle) * rest`, so zero restores the import exactly. `apply_surfaces()` takes the app's degrees (trailing edge up for ailerons/elevator, right for the rudder) and applies `app_sign` (−1, −1, +1) because the r1 pivots are positive trailing-edge down.

Nodes the app drives through Euler properties get a two-level wrapper (mount with the rest basis, identity child receiving the rotation) because writing `rotation.z` or `rotation.x` replaces the whole basis: `propeller` (child of `propeller_mount` under `thrust_frame`; its +Z is the thrust axis, so `rotation.z = +θ` is clockwise seen from behind, the author's sense) and `gear = {left, right, tail, steering}` (`wheel_*_spin` nodes on the axle centres rotating about the root +X, forward rolling negative as in `render/ground_gear.gd`; `tail_steering` rotating about +Y, positive = wheel trails right). The imported pivots keep their world transforms; no mesh is reparented.

## Requests for a r2 delivery

1. Reproducible rebuild: the documented command sequence omits the BREP cache step and reads/writes different directories than the scripts expect (TT-00-R1 §1); pin the environment.
2. A verifier that exits non-zero on a failed check (TT-00-R1 §4).
3. Nose/cowl length: 50–60 mm too long by the author's photo comparison; needs a scaled side photo or the manufacturer drawing before any correction (decision D4 keeps r1).
4. Mass ledger: replace "use for first flights only" with an explicit non-flight placeholder; the manual's 2.190 kg is the flight target (TT-07).
5. Optional: slats and floats as separate exports if the owner ever wants them (out of scope).
