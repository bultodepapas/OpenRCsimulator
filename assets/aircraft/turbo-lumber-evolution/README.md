# Turbo Lumber Evolution (Timber): source and integration guide

Checked 2026-10-20. The catalog airplane `turbo-lumber-evolution` uses the author's r2 glTF model and generated experimental flight data. Airborne and qualified runway starts are supported; fixed wheels, flaps up and assists off are the current configuration. The finite battery is the default; [BAT](../../../docs/plans/battery-realism.md) owns energy behavior and mode selection. [First launch](../../../docs/FIRST-LAUNCH.md) owns player controls; the [Timber plan](../../../docs/TIMBER-INTEGRATION-PLAN.md) owns remaining TT work and acceptance.

## Frames and datum

Model coordinates are metres, +X right, +Y up, −Z nose. D0 is the symmetry plane × wing-root leading-edge station × thrust-axis height. The model manifest declares a zero datum offset. Aircraft positions convert as `[x_aft, y_right, z_up] = [z_model, x_model, y_model]`; shared conventions belong to the [aircraft contract](../../../docs/AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md#coordinates-signs-and-units).

## Runtime model contract

[render/airplane.gd](../../../app/render/airplane.gd) resolves the version-2 model manifest and calls the shared [imported-model builder](../../../app/aircraft/imported_model.gd). The manifest supplies construction, node bindings, rest-frame records, section provider and material corrections. [timber_model.gd](../../../app/aircraft/timber_model.gd) is the compatibility facade for metrology/inspection (`build`, `apply_surfaces`, `set_flaps`, `manual_throws_deg`, `apply_controls`).

The checked model exposes `root` named `airplane`, `propeller`, `hinges`, `gear`, `has_propeller`, `data`, `aircraft_id` and `model_manifest`. Aileron/elevator signs convert the app's trailing-edge-up convention to the imported pivots; rudder is right-positive. Rest-frame wrappers preserve the tilted rotor and wheel/steering axes. The [common contract](../../../docs/AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md#model-and-package-contract) owns construction details; keep `propeller` and hinge names stable.

R2 meshes/pivots carry `openrc_section` tags, consumed directly by the crash system; the r1 generated triangle map is historical. Flight keeps flaps neutral. Inspector flap posing is a visual operation, not a flight-flap capability.

## Visual presentation

The catalog builder applies the Timber-only [finish/detail layer](../../../app/aircraft/timber_visual_details.gd) and [factory lamp anchors](../../../app/aircraft/timber_visual_lighting.gd). Per-instance materials use free CC0 foam and rubber maps. Added construction and fracture details follow their existing parts and carry `crash_no_bounds`; they do not enlarge crash shapes or create damage sections. The raw `timber_model.gd` facade remains available for source metrology.

[TimberVisualMotion](../../../app/aircraft/timber_visual_motion.gd) reads the existing session contact state after the shared wheel observer. Main legs hinge, cable/spring details follow their lower attachments, and the axle retains the existing compression height. Visual hinge motion is bounded to 6°; this is a presentation limit, not a qualified mechanical travel range. Wheel spin, tail steering, contact forces, friction, collision thresholds and damage decisions remain owned by their existing systems. Small tire-dust cues require loaded, slipping contact on loose surfaces and stop during pause or crash.

The factory layout uses paired lower-cowl landing lamps, wingtip navigation lenses and dorsal/ventral red beacons. Generic orientation strips and unsupported tail/tip lamps stay dark for this aircraft. Existing light controls still govern the kit. The damage report adds the localized aircraft identity and a red accent; short-window large-text layout remains scrollable without changing any estimate.

[TT-20](../../../docs/research/timber-integration/TT-20/README.md) records the photographs, technical references, inspected flight footage and native visual verification.

## Verification and review

```bash
"$GODOT" --headless --path app --audio-driver Dummy --script res://aircraft/verify_timber.gd
"$GODOT" --headless --path app --audio-driver Dummy \
  --script res://aircraft/verify_timber_clearance.gd -- crossings-only
"$GODOT" --headless --path app --audio-driver Dummy --script res://tests/test_timber_handling.gd
```

Other focused scripts are `test_timber_data.gd`, `test_timber_ground.gd` and `test_crash_sections.gd` under `app/tests/`. `app/test.sh` includes source/generated-data/model checks, focused suites and both battery-mode trace smokes. Use [flight logging](../../../docs/FLIGHT-LOGGING.md) for baseline/candidate comparisons. Inspect engine errors as well as exit status and bound probe runtimes; passing repeatability checks does not establish physical validation.

The clearance command above checks control-surface triangle crossings at defined poses. The full command without `crossings-only` also computes gaps and records whole-leg diagnostics; it can take minutes. Neither mode qualifies isolated wire/bracket travel, swept volumes or watertight containment.

For rendered review, choose a new output directory (the inspector refuses existing output). This command needs a display/Xvfb and OpenGL:

```bash
xvfb-run -a "$GODOT" --path app --rendering-driver opengl3 \
  --script res://aircraft/inspect_timber.gd \
  -- --output-dir="$PWD/tmp/timber-views" --suite=inspection
```

Suites are `inspection`, `orbit`, `distance` or `all`; captures record camera/pose identity and finish hashes. The default shows the production finish; `--finish=source` shows the raw facade and `--view-filter=nose` selects matching views. Retain owner review and target-machine frame measurements with the owning TT evidence.

## Current asset limitations

The imported r2 has 42,519 triangles, 21 mesh nodes and 49 material surfaces. Fewer triangles than r1 do not establish lower frame cost. [TT-19](../../../docs/research/timber-integration/TT-19/README.md) retains exact measurements, captures, package checks and clearance observations.

The source has overlapping foam and marking shells. The production finish repairs their material assignment on duplicated instance meshes and applies small render-only paint offsets; it preserves every original triangle position and winding. The raw facade retains the source finish for metrology. These presentation repairs do not make the source watertight or replace a corrected delivery; white wing-root closure faces remain part of the authored construction.

The author reports flap contact at 40° and wire/bracket contact beyond about 6°; these are distinct from Godot's control-triangle checks. Merged leg/fuselage overlap includes intended attachments and does not qualify suspension clearance. The bounded visual motion does not close that source-author review.

The model's approximately 1.116 m length still differs from the manual's 1.040 m. Flight retains the 2.190 kg manual reference mass; the delivery's 1.46 kg ledger is a non-flight placeholder. Measured geometry does not calibrate aerodynamics, mass/inertia, motor losses or the borrowed propeller map. Physical validation, owner readability and target-machine performance remain open in the plan.
