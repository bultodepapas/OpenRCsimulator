# Reference and provenance record

R59 is the user-provided detailed master. Geometry was developed in this project with Codex assistance. This record distinguishes reference consultation from geometry derivation; it is not a third-party rights clearance.

- Royal RC F4U-1D plan: registered and manually sampled/digitized for model proportions and stations during the R59 lineage. The resulting geometry is source-derived. The plan image is not included in g1.
- CARF Corsair manual: consulted for visible construction and gear/mechanism context. Not included as a document or texture.
- User-provided aircraft photographs, cutaways and wing erection diagram: consulted for shape, pose, joints and mechanical arrangement. No reference photographs or drawings are packed in g1 or its GLB.
- Museum/archive references: consulted for variant and dimensional comparisons. Smithsonian F4U-1D lists span 12.50 m and length 10.20 m; other Navy family summaries give 33 ft 4 in (10.16 m). Model endpoint differences remain documented instead of distorted by nonuniform scaling.
- g1 paint, stripe designs and number 308: newly authored procedural/raster layers. No source photos, real sponsor artwork or real-aircraft serial markings were used as textures. OpenRaster stores editable layers.
- g1 three-blade geometry and hub are taken from R59, with the blade set translated 0.17 m toward the nose in full-size coordinates before uniform scaling. Gunsight is a newly created visual proxy.
