# Roadmap

**2026-11-28 · Early alpha. The next flight milestone is an owner-flown Ugly Stik runway circuit with the actual transmitter.** It can establish what the pilot sees and feels, expose setup problems, and identify which maneuver needs independent measurement. Performance work can proceed on a named target host while field data is collected.

This page owns project priorities and dependencies. The [track register](docs/TRACKS.md) identifies the owner of each detailed contract. **Implemented** means there is a runtime path. **Qualified** applies only to the named build, configuration, device, workload and result. **Open** identifies missing evidence or delivery work. **Blocked** names a prerequisite. **Proposal** requires a separate adoption decision.

### Owner's field kit

1. **VAL-5:** weigh the identified assembly, locate CG, measure throws and record fuel/battery/gear state.
2. **VAL-6 / D11e:** perform the swing test on a calibrated rig. Keep raw periods, rig dimensions, repeatability and inertia uncertainty.
3. **VAL-7 / G1a:** measure installed thrust, RPM and throttle steps with the actual engine/motor and propeller. Record propeller dimensions, supply or fuel state, air density and calibration.
4. **VAL-8 / PT2:** capture takeoff, circuit, landing, roll, stall and glide with surveyed or calibrated video observations. Reserve independent observations for evaluation and report uncertainty.
5. **D6d / F4 / F6:** record the physical transmitter, firmware, USB mode, axes, calibration and platform, then film the input and visible response. [Measurement workflows](docs/TOOLS.md#aircraft-measurement-reducers) · [Radio diagnostics](docs/TOOLS.md#radio-diagnostics)

**Propulsion and atmosphere.** G1a/VAL-7 matched measurements precede installed-power claims. G1b online operating-range coverage, G1d stopped/windmilling drag, G2 coupling promotion and E0b low-speed wake promotion retain their own gates. Characterize two-stroke and true four-stroke installations separately. Electric finite-pack and ideal-supply routes exist. Installed motor/ESC/pack behavior needs matched data across load, depletion and stop events. Spatial wind, thermals and atmosphere have numerical, physical and cost gates. **Performance experiments.** Use delivered F0 timing on the affected complete route, including the Windows case. First distinguish an initiating late frame from the catch-up ticks that follow. Select F1 stage/session/observer, F2 late-frame or another attributed experiment from the measured cause. Retest after existing native contact, audio and statistics changes. Consider threading or an engine patch only for a named residual bottleneck with a preserved transaction and replay contract. **Display and renderer policy.** Qualify 1920×1080 first, then higher resolutions, then 1280×720. Apple Silicon on a Retina (2×) display targets full-screen native pixels and native-pixel reconstruction where MetalFX is used. Intel Macs and standard-density displays follow the general order. Record output, drawable and internal 3D pixels separately. FSR and MetalFX each require provider-specific dispatch, lifecycle, moving-aircraft and device evidence. Native *physics* selection on macOS is independent of MetalFX graphics eligibility. **Aircraft, imports and authoring.** ClearView v1 imported and test-flew 214 corpus roots on estimated OpenRC configurations or identified donors. The claim covers those trial flights. Faithful source-aircraft reproduction needs independent source/physics evidence. AC-12 numerical qualification remains open after a failed campaign. Experimental drafts and local versions can be saved. Promotion/publication of a qualified aircraft, saved-aircraft Home flight (AC-18), portable bundles (AC-19) and packaged-target evidence (AC-20) retain shared gates. Keep source rights and provenance with each import format. DATA strict load-time bounds and broader DATA/PERC authoring and pilot-perception research remain separate.
