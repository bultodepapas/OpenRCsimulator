# Trailer 2.0 — cinematic creative treatment

**Working line:** YOUR RC WORLD. YOUR WAY.  
**Genre:** spectacular gameplay trailer, playful/energetic with cinema-quality composition.  
**Audience:** RC airplane, FPV and car enthusiasts, new players and creators.  
**Objective:** convert curiosity into a download.

## Story through visual ideas
1. **You can fly and drive.** Hook with Corsair fly-by, turbine jet and nitro monster-truck jump; a 15-second lightning-fast introduction of all 13 options.
2. **You can fly whenever you want.** One aerobatic motion flows from midday to golden hour to blue hour and RGB-illuminated night. Lightning and rain-on-lens are punctuation, not empty cloud footage.
3. **You control what it becomes.** One Ugly Stick goes from tiny 10% to giant 500%, against the same runway or pilot reference, with a brief genuine Workshop control.
4. **You can make it your own.** Corsair skims the lake; original + true moving reflection both visible; paint schemes change in edit on beat, without pretending in-flight skin selection is gameplay.
5. **You can experiment.** Creator supports compatible STL, GLB, ClearView and Phoenix import paths in the appropriate development build. Show real preview/setup followed by an eligible trial, not an unsupported universal conversion.
6. **Even mistakes are vivid.** A car/aircraft impact sends dirt, grass, water spray or cracked camera lens. Stylized red curtain only for a validated person/pilot strike. Snap back into fast FPV gates and car action.
7. **Your next flight starts here.** End on triumphant play and logo, FREE / OPEN SOURCE / early-alpha qualification.

## Directional rules
- Viewers must understand the game muted and within 5–10 seconds. No opening logo animation.
- Make the **aircraft physically readable**: for hero shots favor close front-three-quarter, grounded low-angle, camera crossing and shore reflection rather than distant rear chase. Keep aircraft large enough on phone.
- Vehicle roster clips may be 1.1–1.2 s if a distinctive full body is visible; keep titles minimal. Hero moments breathe for 3–6 s so a maneuver can finish.
- The two trucks need visibly different personalities: Bisonte electric suspension, MONSTER CAR 2 nitro exhaust/dust, brake lights and green underglow; no identical car shots.
- Night should be deep blue, not black; aircraft silhouette, body position and lights must all register. Do not lift blacks so far that light effects disappear.
- Lake should include shore, trees/landmark and a distinguishable mirrored Corsair. An attractive generic water shader is not enough. A reflected jet can be a separately verified fallback.
- Scale must have same-depth anchor; do not make a normal plane larger solely by pushing camera closer. Disclose cinematic composite if 10/100/500% aircraft appear simultaneously.
- Alternate shot textures: cockpit FPV, ground pilot, slow hovering blimp, side-on nitro truck, mechanical Corsair wing, dynamic night lighting. Avoid five back-to-back shots of the same pond and red Stick.
- Change time of day with real in-game presets captured separately. Match cuts and speed ramps belong to editing, not physics.
- Never sell experimental physics as physically validated, or staged camera choreography as a user-accessible simultaneous multiplayer feature.

## Contrast with old film
The documented Every Input Matters v9.13 had 90 seconds, music from Scott Buckley's Uprising, an early cinematic formation, a Corsair orbit with skins, a three-size composition, night lights, car jump and many feature cards. Its review recorded issues: tiny subjects, repeated pond frames, nearly invisible night airplanes, a standalone storm cloud with no context, too much text in earlier fast versions, and rotor audio masking the score.

Version 2.0 changes the **hero**, **structure**, **sound** and **claim order**. No film-ready asset can be assumed until re-captured against the current engine and build.

## Editorial style and title hierarchy
- Primary labels, at most one per major feature: CHOOSE YOUR MACHINE. / FLY DAY OR NIGHT. / 10% TO 500%. / MAKE IT YOURS. / IMPORT. BUILD. TEST. / FEEL THE IMPACT. / PLAY FREE.
- Microshots get optional tiny vehicle nameplates, not explanatory paragraphs.
- Use restrained existing green/crystal branding where it does not obscure aircraft and menus.
- Logo is a final payoff; show actual link and beta/early-alpha disclaimer at the end.
- English master, Spanish text variant after picture lock. Do not overlay fake game interfaces on simulation footage.

## Light, palette and shot purpose
| Chapter | Film lighting | Camera and motion | Emotional job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fleet hook | clear morning / high sun | ground-low, cross-camera, FPV, close details | accessibility and energy |
| Day → night | warm to blue | match the wing attitude across time | discovery |
| Scale | neutral midday | fixed reference and measured angle | scale astonishment |
| Corsair lake | low warm sun, softened clouds | shoreline tracking, mirrored silhouette | memorable beauty |
| Creator | bright legible UI → trial flight | concise process montage | agency |
| Impact | detailed terrain and lens | safe controlled actual events | consequence |
| End | colorful daytime, bright night accent | fast varied actions → logo | excitement |

## Thumbnail and versions
The preferred thumbnail is an **actual captured Corsair-over-lake frame**, model and water reflection in same composition; not stock footage or generated art. Produce a 30 s discovery cut, 6 s microcut and age-appropriate non-blood variant only after the full footage set is verified. The original soundtrack remains a comparison, not a default selection.
