# OPEN RC SIMULATOR — TRAILER 2.0
## Director's screenplay — Revision 2: "EVERY VIEW. EVERY MACHINE."

**Historical working revision — followed by [R3](cinematic-script-r3.md); [R4](cinematic-script-r4.md) now owns current direction.**

**21 November 2026 · SCRIPT ONLY · Not a capture plan, implementation or approved edit**  
**Target runtime:** 90 seconds; no voice-over required. The picture and original game sound must tell the story even on mute.  
**Working positioning:** "Your RC world. Your way."  
**Historical authority:** Revision 2 superseded the first-pass timing when written. The current storyboard and shot bible now follow R4. Retain this text as the reviewed input; its timing, mandatory shots, wording and source assumptions are not current direction.

### Narrative principle
The first cut says **there is more than one kind of RC**. The second says **you're at the controls**. The last says **you can make this world your own**. We do not spend 90 seconds reading features; we watch them *happen*. Every change of camera, vehicle, weather, color or model scale should answer an emotional question: "What else can I do here?"

## THE FILM — 90-second script

### 00:00–00:06 | COLD OPEN — NO INTRO, JUST PLAY (R2-01 to R2-03)
**Picture:** Black for only the sound cue of a radial engine. A **Corsair F4U** suddenly slices across the lake. The aircraft and its moving mirror occupy the same frame, shoreline racing past behind them (reflection is a target for validation, not yet captured for this model). Match the movement to a **Vantage Jet crossing inverted** with its canopy unmistakably pointing down. Then a hard sound cut: **MONSTER CAR 2** launches from a dirt ramp toward a low camera and lands.

**Camera language:** shoreline/reflective low-angle → stationary fly-by looking upward at inverted jet → tire-height ground tracking. Three perspectives, three distinct sounds. Let the jet maintain genuinely inverted attitude in the source if the flight model supports the rehearsed maneuver; do not rotate the video to fake it.

**Sound:** radial thrum → rising turbine → aggressive nitro, tire impact on the first musical downbeat.  
**Text:** NONE. Three shots tell the player everything needed for the first five seconds.

### 00:06–00:19 | ONE WORLD, THIRTEEN MACHINES (R2-04)
**Picture:** 13 clean, mostly one-second action portraits, each with a different angle, landscape, speed, and silhouette:
1. Ugly Stick (gas) — pilot-station takeoff.
2. Ugly Stick Electric 6S — low electric pass.
3. Extra 300S — full, legible roll.
4. P-51D Mustang — warbird bank.
5. Turbo Lumber Evolution — high-wing runway skim.
6. MoonRunner X99 — flying-wing silhouette from below.
7. X-Cell .60 trainer helicopter — controlled hover.
8. ION R5 quad — tight entry to the first race gate.
9. 5 m blimp — gentle turn across a tree-lined field.
10. Bisonte MT8 — shocks compress into a ramp.
11. MONSTER CAR 2 — front tire, dust and nitro exhaust.
12. F4U Corsair — folded-wing / gear detail on ground (separate from the flying shot).
13. Vantage Jet — turbine pass to whip-pan into next chapter.

**Camera language:** no three consecutive rear-chase angles. Change the *type* of view: pilot, fly-by, chase, low-car orbit, quad FPV. Every model needs a distinct physical action, not a static turntable.

**Sound:** engine colors cut together as musical percussion; each vehicle is audible long enough to distinguish.  
**Text:** optional brief name tag per vehicle. At 00:06 the only headline is **"CHOOSE YOUR MACHINE."** Don't attempt to read thirteen feature descriptions.

### 00:19–00:29 | THE FPV RACE — FROM OUTSIDE TO INSIDE (R2-05 to R2-08)
**00:19–00:21:** The ION R5 idles on its pad. A colorful gate with its real racing LEDs fills the background. Real countdown lights turn red, then GREEN. The game says GO.

**00:21–00:25:** On the downbeat, cut into the **quad's actual rigid FPV camera**. Dive through an illuminated gate opening; roll into the next gate. We see the racing OSD at the edge—speed, altitude, flight mode, timer and next-gate cue—not an invented cockpit. The view must show unmistakable forward movement through at least two consecutively eligible gates.

**00:25–00:27:** Match-cut to **external chase** as the same type of turn finishes. A track-side view shows the quad whip through a glowing numbered gate. The gate guidance animates and a pass flashes if genuinely achieved.

**00:27–00:29:** Return to FPV for a fast finish/next-gate sequence. Use the live local lap/timer/record card only if a full valid run was captured; otherwise end on the real gate pass without a fictional medal.

**Sound:** close electric whine and gate cues.  
**Text:** **"FLY THROUGH THE LIMITS."** or shorter **"RACE THE GATES."** (final title not locked).

**Camera truth:** the drone supports FPV, chase and field pilot views. We can switch views in the edit, but not imply a built-in multicamera replay editor or synchronized dual-camera mode.

### 00:29–00:35 | A DIFFERENT KIND OF FLIGHT (R2-09 to R2-10)
**Picture:** Smash cut from quad rotor whine to the **X-Cell helicopter**. A short mechanical close-up of rotor head/swash as a *presentation* insert (if using the separate HX-700 inspector, label/credit it as such); cut to the **playable helicopter actually hovering and advancing** past the clubhouse, shot from a tracking side/orbit angle. When its rotor sound fades, a **5 m blimp** drifts past from a quiet three-quarter camera.

**Contrast:** high-frequency racing → precision helicopter control → quiet, unusual blimp motion. The blimp is a breath, not comic filler.  
**Text:** NONE.  
**Sound:** drone whine turns to heavy rotor blades, then soft electric motor and wind.

### 00:35–00:42 | SEE IT YOUR WAY — CAMERA VIEWS AND WIDGETS (R2-11 to R2-13)
**00:35–00:38:** One **Mustang or Extra** crosses frame. Over three editorial cuts, show the aircraft from genuine **ground-pilot**, **fly-by**, then **chase/orbit** views. The aircraft continues approximately the same movement and direction; this is a perceptual demonstration of available views, not an in-game live editor. A quick **close-up** perspective reveals the moving control surfaces.

**00:38–00:40:** On a separate legitimate flying shot, switch from a clean display to the **Player** widgets—airspeed, height, power/energy where supported, weather/wind. Leave most of the frame open to the aircraft.

**00:40–00:42:** Cut to another flight with the **Cockpit instruments preset**: attitude, airspeed, altimeter, heading and vertical-speed gauges. The camera itself remains a supported external flight view; these are **cockpit-style widgets, NOT a verified interior camera**. If panel legibility fails in two seconds, lengthen this insert by borrowing from the roster montage.

**Sound:** let engine audio remain audible under the interface.  
**Text:** **"EVERY ANGLE."** followed by **"LIVE FLIGHT DATA."** Use no long technical captions.

### 00:42–00:50 | THE WORLD CHANGES COLOR (R2-14 to R2-16)
**Picture:** An **Extra or compatible show-smoke airframe** pulls into a bright blue sky trailing a real **colored smoke** preset. Cut on the same direction of motion to warm **golden hour**, then to **blue hour**. At **night**, an aircraft using actual Workshop RGB/landing/nav lights performs a close, readable turn; strips and body are both visible. One short shot puts colored smoke against the sunset, where smoke is naturally legible. Optional distant thunder/lightning only if it illuminates actual flight rather than an empty cloud.

**Editorial flourish:** match a wing's orientation across three real time-of-day captures, as if a single move crosses time. Avoid suggesting the game currently transitions between times mid-maneuver with a special button.

**Text:** **"DAY. DUSK. NIGHT."**  
**Sound:** prop pulse against airy music, a brief storm accent, then the brightest synth beat arrives with the RGB aircraft.

### 00:50–00:59 | ONE AIRPLANE, THREE SIZES (R2-17 to R2-19)
**00:50–00:52:** A genuine Workshop control changes the supported Ugly Stick scale setting. No long UI explanation.

**00:52–00:55:** A real **giant 500% Stick** flies across a runway landmark. Next, a real **mini 10% Stick** crosses that same landmark from a comparable camera and distance. Both must be recognizable and actually represent eligible Workshop trial configurations.

**00:55–00:59:** The signature **three-aircraft comparison**: **500%, 100%, 10% Ugly Sticks in the same frame, flying abreast at the same depth**. Camera moves inward between the normal and tiny plane. Pin three readable labels to each airframe: **GIANT — 7.62 m / ORIGINAL — 1.52 m / MINI — 15 cm**. This deliberately reuses the successful concept documented in trailer v9.6. It is **cinematic size choreography, not three simultaneous user-controlled gameplay aircraft**; the film/description must disclose this distinction.

**Text:** **"SAME PLANE. ANY SIZE."**  
**Sound:** one musical swell with a playful low-to-high pitch accent as scale changes; avoid changing actual engine audio to suggest false physics.

### 00:59–01:11 | THE CORSAIR AND THE LAKE — THE HERO SHOT (R2-20 to R2-22)
**00:59–01:02:** A breath in the music. Wide, low, quiet lakeshore view. The **Corsair enters**, its gull wings clear against the clouds. We see water texture, the trees on the far bank and the plane's **own moving reflection**, if the optional High planar reflection passes the Corsair filming gate.

**01:02–01:08:** Same approach path, camera and lake. On successive beats, **Corsair liveries shift in the cut**: dark, bright, red or otherwise visually distinct approved skins. The airframe stays in the same place. Crucial: **the water reflection changes together with each skin**. This is an editorial skin montage from the game's model/paint system, not player-operated paint swapping during flight.

**01:08–01:11:** The final paint scheme holds long enough to read. A low side-on fly-by with the Corsair and its mirror moves together across the lake. The camera does not cut away before the audience recognizes the reflection.

**Text:** **"MAKE IT YOURS."** then a short **"PAINT SCHEMES"** label, no statistics over water.  
**Sound:** drop music for an instant at first entrance; radial engine comes in warm, then music returns.  
**Visual rule:** this is the **beauty centerpiece**. If no accurate Corsair planar reflection is achieved, do not composite one and call it gameplay. Adapt the concept only after testing.

### 01:11–01:18 | BRING YOUR OWN AIRCRAFT (R2-23 to R2-25)
**01:11–01:13:** An authentic **Aircraft Creator** import selection flashes past; compatible STL, GLB, ClearView ZIP and, if in the filmed development build, Phoenix models are visible. Choose only files the actual Creator accepts.

**01:13–01:15:** Rapid but readable steps: preview → size/orientation → balance/control settings → paint. Use at most two UI views, not a tutorial.

**01:15–01:18:** One **genuinely eligible imported configuration is test-flown in Creator**, from a supported start and scene. Its flight physics are OpenRC estimates/donor behavior as disclosed; no universal automatic conversion or normal saved-Home flight is implied.

**Text:** **"IMPORT. CONFIGURE. TEST-FLY."**  
**Sound:** mechanical UI beat, then real aircraft engine starts.  
**Variation idea:** a fast tiled gallery of three different imported *previews* can lead into a single qualifying trial; the gallery is previews, not three flyable claims.

### 01:18–01:22 | EVEN THE CAMERA FEELS THE IMPACT (R2-26 to R2-27)
**Picture:** A real close-up/FPV view hits eligible dirt/ground: **dust, grass, rain or mud** visibly lands on the lens; a second qualifying high-speed close-up/FPV contact produces **cracked lens glass**. If we include the playful stylized **red blood curtain**, it must be a *separate actual person/pilot-strike event*, not an ordinary terrain crash. The preferred public/family-safe cut uses **crack + mud/water**.

**Text:** none.  
**Sound:** brief impact/low-pass dropout lasting no longer than the visual punctuation.  
**Editorial purpose:** consequence, followed immediately by more game action—not a grim ending.

### 01:22–01:27 | BACK TO THE DIRT, BACK TO THE SKY (R2-28 to R2-30)
**01:22–01:24:** The **Bisonte MT8** accelerates, launches from a ramp and *lands*: show suspension rebound and dirt movement. Electric audio.

**01:24–01:26:** **MONSTER CAR 2** runs the course at dusk, headlights/auxiliary lights and green underbody neon visible; nitro exhaust and tire motion, then a sharp turn. Nitro audio takes over.

**01:26–01:27:** One explosive **colored-smoke airshow image**: a compatible single-plane smoke pass, or the existing presentation-only five-aircraft colored-smoke choreography with on-screen/documentary disclosure if used. No fake multiplayer.

**Text:** none.  
**Sound:** rhythm peaks, final musical hit sends us to the logo.

### 01:27–01:30 | TITLE AND INVITATION (R2-31)
**Picture:** OPEN RC SIMULATOR logo against clean dark atmospheric background. No competing flying plane or additional exposition.  
**Final card:** **"YOUR RC WORLD. YOUR WAY."** · **FREE · OPEN SOURCE · EARLY ALPHA** · real verified download link.  
**Sound:** resolved chord / distant engine trail, never an abrupt unfinished loop.

## The storyboard's storytelling hooks

- **Jet inverted pass:** read canopy-down against the unchanged real horizon. External fly-by is stronger than a camera that rolls with the model. Flight feasibility and clean angle remain an open shoot gate; no fake inverted shot.
- **FPV gates:** the green start light and actual gate glow give spectators a game objective, not just abstract flying. Match from outside to true rigid onboard FPV; spectators should see a gate *entered*, *crossed* and then *left behind*.
- **Cameras:** create excitement by linking supported views of a similar flight path; do not advertise fixed-wing "cockpit view" as implemented solely because a Cockpit **widget preset** exists. Quad has true onboard FPV.
- **Telemetry:** clean shot → Player widgets → classic Cockpit gauges, always real session information and actually supported.
- **Three-size moment:** the same-frame choreography is **specifically required**; it makes scale instantly understandable. Use a true real-trial scale proof immediately before.
- **Colored smoke:** include both a player's actual Workshop show-smoke demonstration and possibly the separately labeled cinematic multiaircraft airshow image. Don't make colored theatrical smoke into exhaust physics.
- **Helicopter:** full silhouette and actual controlled flight matter more than a distant spinning rotor; the articulated HX-700 inspector macro is distinct from the playable X-Cell session.
- **Car differentiation:** Bisonte is the electric suspension/jump beat; MONSTER CAR 2 is the nitro/exhaust/dusk-light beat.
- **Corsair lake:** the water is a second subject; the cut should not rush past its reflection.
- **Crash-camera:** authentic contact semantics, not blood on every crash.
- **Creator:** the visual "I can bring my own model" promise is exciting only if the distinction between preview and trial stays truthful.

## Text economy / alternative lines
Only 6–8 cards in 90 s, with visual phrases rather than product specifications:
CHOOSE YOUR MACHINE. · RACE THE GATES. · EVERY ANGLE. · LIVE FLIGHT DATA. · DAY. DUSK. NIGHT. · SAME PLANE. ANY SIZE. · MAKE IT YOURS. · IMPORT. CONFIGURE. TEST-FLY. · YOUR RC WORLD. YOUR WAY.

The final edit can combine or drop cards when the film is legible without them. No continuous voice-over is planned; a later narrated edition would be a separate creative decision.

## Open artistic questions for later — not requests to implement now
- Can the Vantage Jet perform a **clean, clearly inverted** sustained fly-by on its current flight physics without hitting the ground? If not, keep the shot as a storyboard concept and choose a physically accurate alternative only after real rehearsal.
- Can a full **three-lap local ION R5 race** be recorded with legible OSD and a satisfying gate crossing? If not, use genuine gate passes rather than invent race results.
- Which **Workshop aircraft** renders colored show smoke with strong legibility against sunlit clouds, and which can combine it with night lights without confusing source/profile restrictions?
- Does the current Windows RTX 3090 High + experimental planar path really render a Corsair and the right skin color in lake reflection?
- Which imported aircraft can actually execute an eligible Creator trial in the exact future build?
