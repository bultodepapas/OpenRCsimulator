## Rhythm and source continuity

A complete landing or recovery is more valuable than an arbitrary fast cut. The quiet blimp, valid gate passage, recovery payoff, ten-second orbit and six-second lake contrast with the mechanism details and import reel. Half-second previews are silhouette impressions, not model-name lessons. Each provider's first model gets one second; the remaining eight get half a second each. Their real proportions stay intact.

The studio inspector, calibrated panorama, storm and Creator are explicit changes of context. The Corsair orbit/lake retain the same frozen golden state and final paint. The sunset shore leads into neutral Creator UI; the daylight trial is its own session. The final stock night aircraft is labelled. These are editorial connections, not a fabricated continuous day or route.

No spare time pool remains. If rehearsal makes a shot unworkable, replace a named interval and regenerate all affected authorities.
