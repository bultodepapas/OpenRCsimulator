## What the audience should feel

First: **I want to try that.** A jet crosses the real horizon inverted; a nitro truck lands its jump. Next: **these machines respond.** Hinges, surfaces and blade pitch turn a command into visible movement. Then: **there is room for my kind of RC.** Flight, local FPV racing, different machines, instruments and lights lead into scale, liveries and an eligible imported-model trial. The Corsair orbit and lake become the emotional reward, not another item in a catalogue.

The pace changes by chapter. The ground checks are the only deliberately rapid detail montage. Gate passages, a complete truck landing, the orbit and the lake get uninterrupted attention. The film ends with successful play and a clear invitation.
