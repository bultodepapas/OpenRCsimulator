## Continuity and scope

- **Instruments:** real values, real presets, actual external camera. A transmitter picture is not proof of a physical radio connection. The longer preset examples preserve coverage moved out of the master.
- **Touch-and-go:** confirm the actual detailed statistic. The default detector requires at least 0.5 seconds of qualified ground support followed by two seconds clearly airborne. The session widget only shows aggregate landings. This does not invent a dedicated landing-challenge mode.
- **Natural Random:** its own stock-airplane/daylight pool, Club field and airborne start. Keep the actual generated choice between briefing and flight. It does not inherit saved ordinary-flight failure settings.
- **Energy:** use supported configurations and actual elapsed-state changes. A short edit is not a real-time battery endurance claim. Do not connect a practice-injected power loss to fuel exhaustion. A depleted battery is not a valid turtle recovery.
- **Aftermath:** use one separate fixed-wing event. The quad's master recovery supplies neither this report nor a repair-cost result. Wreck breakup is cosmetic and damage valuation estimated/possibly incomplete; no repair economy is promised.
- **Scenery:** Green paradise and the calibrated photo panorama are separate sessions. The panorama is fixed-eye imagery with estimated runway/contact, not free-camera 3D terrain.
- **Living field:** nonzero wind for the sock, supported vegetation and suitable scenery quality for gaze. A source feature can exist and still be too small in a particular composition.
- **Rain/night:** the traced rain route is ordinary airplane flight. Pilot view has no glass beads by default; choose a supported chase/orbit view with effects enabled. FN-20a and FN-20b are required separate night compositions, so moon, field lighting and stars have explicit coverage even when the master keeps a simpler Lumber pass. Their settings/cloud/exposure can differ; the edit does not imply one continuous sky transformation. The master already supplies the storm-flash shot.

The [feature integration ledger](feature-opportunities.md) links every implementation source. The [verification gates](verification-gates.md) apply to these films as well as the master. If a selected behavior cannot be shown, record the omission and revise the cut; an untested fallback is not accepted footage.

## Status and later work

These are scripts, not additional finished videos. Their purpose is to integrate the complete opportunity list without hiding menu/statistics features in half-second master inserts. The initial offline shot-plan tools and opt-in livery hold-last change are implemented and narrowly tested for the master; additional capture adapters, source assets, actual takes, visual readability and soundtrack remain pending.
