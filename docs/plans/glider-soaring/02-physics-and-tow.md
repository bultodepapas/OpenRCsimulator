# Glider aerodynamics, energy, and coupled towing

**Owner:** physics and simulation, with SF, AS, M5, GP/CR and aircraft owners.  
**Status:** proposed work for [SG-01…12 and SG-16](../glider-soaring.md#7-delivery-sequence-and-canonical-step-status); this document reports no implementation or flight-test result.  
**Source baseline:** `ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f`. Read the [source review](../../research/glider-soaring/2026-11-15-source-review.md), [launch/control contract](01-launch-and-control.md), and [aircraft/weather dossier](03-aircraft-and-weather.md) with this document.

Start with one unpowered rigid-body glider and its actual controls, a bounded low-Reynolds-number aerodynamic model, and valid trimmed descent in still air. For towing, apply the same compliant line's loads to two independently simulated rigid bodies at their real hook positions, using the same candidate state and stage time. A drawn line, a prescribed following position, or two sessions exchanging forces one tick late do not model that interaction. The first tow feasibility demonstration is **reference-only, airborne, and contact-disabled**; ground aerotow follows the joint-body and event-composition decision in SG-07.

The intended named target is the documented pure-glider configuration of the MULTIPLEX EasyGlider 4 (EG4), with a manufacturer-documented FunCub pairing as a candidate tug. Exact selected-build mass, wing area, CG, inertia, polar, hook installation and towline properties are not established by this plan. An estimated development fixture is useful, but is not an independently validated EG4 or a universal RC reference. The dossier owns aircraft-specific source values and the status of each missing input.

## 2. Low-Re aerodynamics and controls

Local Reynolds number is `Re = rho * V * c / mu`, using local section chord `c`, air-relative speed `V`, density `rho` and dynamic viscosity `mu`. As an arithmetic illustration only, `rho=1.2 kg/m³`, `V=10 m/s`, `c=0.18 m` and `mu=1.8e-5 Pa s` give `Re=120,000`. This is neither an EG4 setting nor an operating-speed recommendation. Surface finish, hinge gaps, contamination and transition can change section performance, so a geometrically scaled full-size polar is not a validation source.

The [UIUC low-speed wind-tunnel collection](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pd.html) contains measured section data, and its [test manifesto](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pd/manifest.html) describes low-Re test ranges. Choose matching airfoil, Reynolds number, transition and measurement conditions. Those section data do not by themselves include the whole airframe, fuselage, tail trim or finite-wing behavior. The UIUC data page states redistribution, attribution, copyright and license conditions; inspect the actual license before packaging data.

The [XFLR5 author's limitations](https://www.xflr5.tech/docs/Part%20IV%3A%20Limitations.pdf) explain that its 3-D potential-flow methods approximate viscous behavior using 2-D data and have other geometry and interaction limits. Record the method, version, geometry, Reynolds/transition inputs, convergence and missing operating points. These are estimated inputs, not wind-tunnel or flight measurements. An independent [small-UAV motion-tracking experiment](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/aeronautical-journal/article/offboard-aerodynamic-measurements-of-smalluavs-in-glide-flight-using-motion-tracking/4F2CB54FF6A9C3DF9870305D0EA92044) demonstrates whole-aircraft glide measurements and associated uncertainty.

The existing contract describes local strips, controls, stall continuation and optional tabulated wing polars. Use them only inside their admitted regimes. A viable first glider uses evidence-bearing lift/drag/moment coefficients and a bounded, explicitly estimated continuation outside the measured attached-flow region. Qualify the resulting **trimmed whole-aircraft** behavior against withheld flights. The pinned `tabulated-wing-v1` accepts a finite-wing strip-reference incidence table with shape-preserving angle interpolation and numerical endpoint continuation; it has no Reynolds axis. A 2D airfoil table is not automatically a compatible input. Record source dimensionality, finish/transition, sweep direction and Re domain, then qualify any conversion and a restricted reference envelope. Add versioned multidimensional interpolation only when justified by independent data; a smooth interpolation must not erase a documented transition branch. See [GI-01/02](../../research/glider-soaring/2026-11-15-investigations-01-aerodynamics.md). Do not treat a section `CL_max`, one fitted `L/D` point or one author's prediction as validation of the entire aircraft.

At zero and near-zero relative speed, angles and nondimensional rate terms can become undefined. Specify branch limits, finite forces and moments, bounded control contributions, and explicit refusal for any unsupported low-speed state. A speed denominator clamp alone can manufacture loads or hide a discontinuity. When all local surface velocities relative to air are zero, aerodynamic force and moment should vanish; a rotating body in still air can still have nonzero local airflow. Check signed positive/negative incidence, lateral flow, reverse-flow transitions and local wing/tail separation against the chosen model's domain. Make stall and deep-stall continuation finite and continuous where the law claims continuity; report the absence of measurements and defer dynamic-stall hysteresis or spin accuracy until separately justified. A glider can stall during a turn, launch or tow, not just at one indicated airspeed.

Elevator, rudder and ailerons change the appropriate forces **and** moments through the existing control and servo law, including rate, saturation and trim. Camber flaps can also change lift, drag, pitching moment and stall; their actual sign, travel and mixing are installation-specific. Flaperons require the actual mixing and saturation policy. The EG4 manual describes a braking mix using both raised ailerons and elevator compensation. The current conventional [roll-only mapping](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/control_surfaces.gd#L23-L35) commands opposite left/right deflections, so it cannot provide that symmetric function. Require a versioned spoileron mix, input and achieved-actuator topology, saturation policy and matched aerodynamic/render bindings before admitting it; the independent-surface fixture seam is not production support. Do not reinterpret the existing flap law as this mix. The [aircraft dossier](03-aircraft-and-weather.md#surface-behavior-by-configuration) owns the installation details. A general separate spoiler/airbrake module is outside the documented active v2 model; add it only for an aircraft that has that surface, with admitted lift/drag/moment and actuator behavior. Its appearance alone is not a physics law.

The optional [ground-effect law](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/docs/AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md#physical-modules-and-parameter-effects) changes wing induced effects and tail downwash near a field surface; its coefficients are estimated, not measured on this glider. Reuse it only within its actual geometry/height/domain contract and do not add a second ground-effect boost. Solo landing and later ground tow also need separately qualified skid/belly or wheel contact, terrain datum, low-speed flow and two-body contact behavior. A visually plausible flare does not establish physical ground-effect or landing calibration.

## 3. Trim, polar, speeds, CG and launch

For steady, straight, unpowered flight in still uniform air, take `gamma>0` as the descent angle below horizontal:

~~~text
L = W cos(gamma)        D = W sin(gamma)        tan(gamma) = D/L
V_horizontal = V cos(gamma)                    V_sink = V sin(gamma)
V_horizontal / V_sink = L/D
~~~

These force-balance relations do not hold as an instantaneous polar measurement during an accelerating throw, pull-up, turn or wind encounter. Solve a whole-aircraft trim with pitch, elevator/tail force and moment at each supported airspeed/configuration. Derive minimum-sink speed and best-`L/D` speed from that trimmed curve; they generally differ. Ground distance in wind is not identical to the calm-air glide ratio. The [FAA performance chapter](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/glider_handbook/gfh_chapter_5.pdf) explains the distinction and weight effects for full-size gliders; its example speeds are not RC targets.

The familiar near-level estimate `V_s ≈ sqrt(2W/(rho*S*CL_max))` depends on the selected wing and configuration. For unchanged effective `CL_max`, `V_s,n ≈ V_s,1*sqrt(n)` illustrates the effect of load factor. In an approximately level, coordinated turn `n≈1/cos(phi)` and `R≈V²/(g*tan(phi))`; a descending glider and a moving airmass need the corresponding full kinematics. These formulas are checks, not artificial stall or speed limiters. Determine hand-release, minimum-sink, best-glide, approach and tow envelopes from the selected aircraft, weight, control state and evidence; never substitute a universal RC value.

At fixed geometry, CG, density, configuration and coefficient polar, multiplying weight by `lambda` scales the speeds and sink rates at the same coefficient by approximately `sqrt(lambda)` while leaving `L/D` unchanged. Actual ballast also changes inertia and may move CG and alter trim or Reynolds number, so that scaling is conditional. Test valid forward/aft CG, static margin, servo authority and response as well as the full polar. Report unknown structural and flutter limits instead of inferring them from a performance curve.

The existing [airborne-reset seam](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/sim/flight_session.gd#L3846-L3907) starts from air-relative trim and adds local initial wind once. For an unpowered glider, the normal trimmed condition descends relative to still air. Reject an invalid trim, unsupported airflow or insufficient clearance. Hand launch instead follows the existing [ground-relative release path](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/sim/flight_session.gd#L3910-L3964): set release position, attitude, world velocity, angular rate and achieved controls, then obtain local air-relative velocity by subtracting wind. No hand force continues after release. A wind-dependent airspeed margin and realistic throw must be supported by the aircraft and launch evidence; full discus-launch dynamics are a separate scope.

A concrete RC research example is [Ramšak's Longshot 2 glide experiment](https://www.sv-jme.eu/wp-content/uploads/ns_articles/files/ojs/457/submission/copyedit/457-1090-1-CE.pdf), which identifies a 1.499 m span, 0.225 m² wing and 0.320 kg mass. It compares photogrammetric speed/glide-angle observations with CFD. The paper reports scattered lower-speed results, questionable steady-flight assumptions and disagreement in the best-glide region. Its described high-speed discus throw is a launch observation for that article, not a general cruise speed or EG4 calibration.

## 4. Airmass, energy and instruments

Query the same physical world at each aircraft's and relevant aerodynamic station's candidate position and RK stage time. Use the existing versioned spatial wind/thermal law and its admission, domain, epoch and replay contracts; the [weather dossier](03-aircraft-and-weather.md) owns concrete scenarios and finite agenda constraints. The current thermal is a balanced **kinematic** updraft with a surrounding sink region, not a measured thermodynamic cloud. It has finite spatial and temporal support. Current v5 rules exclude simultaneous legacy mean/gust/OU amplitudes; do not add those components a second time or reset the world when switching aircraft.

At every station, compute local air-relative velocity from body motion minus local wind. It changes local aerodynamic loads; do not add an extra thermal force or an extra wind-acceleration term to inertial dynamics. In a simplified steady glide through spatially uniform rising air, the ground climb rate is approximately updraft speed minus the still-air sink for the same trimmed state. A spatial edge can create differential wing loads, subject to the actual station and aerodynamic models.

Keep altitude rate, air-relative total-energy rate, and estimated netto distinct. For constant mass, constant gravity and center-of-mass position in an inertial up-positive frame, define `u = v_ground - w(x,t)` and let `F` include **all non-gravitational** center-of-mass forces:

~~~text
h_TE = h + |u|²/(2g)
d(h_TE)/dt = w_up + u·F/(m*g)
             - u·[partial_t(w) + (v_ground·grad)w]/g
~~~

The field derivative follows the **ground** trajectory. This is an energy ledger, not an additional force. In steady, spatially uniform air, with no propulsion or tow and an idealized point-aircraft aerodynamic force whose drag is `D` along `-u`, it simplifies to `w_up - D*V/(m*g)`. Distributed nonuniform flow, rotational energy, tow work, propulsion and changing mass need their own terms or qualification. In particular, TE rate alone does not equal the local vertical air velocity in shear or during a tow.

An estimated netto signal can add the aircraft's estimated still-air sink to filtered TE rate, but its meaning depends on the polar, load factor, measurement noise, filter and update cadence. For an example of instrument semantics, [ArduPilot's soaring documentation](https://ardupilot.org/plane/docs/soaring-4_1.html) distinguishes an air-mass estimate from aircraft climb; that implementation is not an OpenRC physics oracle. Compute energy height before differentiating, record filter state and latency, and label each indication. A pitch-up can exchange kinetic energy for height without a thermal.

## 5. Massless line, attachments, and energy

The following is a candidate tension-only spring/dashpot law. NASA's [two-body elastic-tether manual](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19740021191) and [straight-cable slung-load equations](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19900008325) are useful mechanics references, not RC rope calibration. The equations and conservation fixtures immediately below assume constant mass, fixed body-frame CG and rigid installed hooks. Let `t` and `g` denote tug and glider. For each body, let `p_i`, `v_i` be world-frame center-of-mass position and its time derivative, `R_i` its body-to-world rotation, `omega_i` its body-frame angular velocity, and `r_i` the fixed body-frame CG-to-hook vector. At each common stage:

~~~text
x_i       = p_i + R_i*r_i
v_hook_i  = v_i + R_i*(omega_i × r_i)
d         = x_t - x_g
ell       = |d|                     n = d/ell, when ell > 0
ell_dot   = n · (v_hook_t - v_hook_g)
e         = ell - L0

T = 0                                  if e <= 0 or line released
T = max(0, k*e + c*ell_dot)            otherwise
F_g =  T*n                             F_t = -T*n
tau_g = (R_g*r_g) × F_g                tau_t = (R_t*r_t) × F_t
~~~

`L0>0` is unloaded length in metres, `k>=0` is stiffness in N/m and `c>=0` is damping in N s/m. Convert the world-frame force and moment into each evaluator's expected frame. At zero separation the line is slack, so avoid normalizing `d=0`. An attached line does not push. The clipping can make tension zero even with `e>0` during rapid shortening; define the displayed slack/tensioned mode separately from geometric extension and preserve the chosen semantics in replay.

For a body-fixed datum `O`, rigid-hook geometry instead uses `x_hook=p_O+R*r_O` and `v_hook=v_O+R*(omega×r_O)`, with `v_O=d(p_O)/dt` expressed in world axes and fixed `r_O`. If position is represented by a moving mass centroid, differentiating `p_CM+R*r_CM(t)` also produces `R*dr_CM/dt`; do not silently equate centroid-coordinate velocity with the velocity of a fixed material point. Before admitting a finite-fuel tug, derive and qualify hook kinematics, moments and power against the repository's [fixed-datum, co-moving-efflux convention](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/fixed_datum_body.gd#L1-L6), including the expelled resource in any closed-system conservation claim. Prefer a qualified constant-mass electric tug for the first pair; finite-fuel support is a separate gate, not an inferred consequence of current powered-aircraft support.

The force pair is equal and opposite and lies on one line of action. Both hook moments are essential: omitting them changes tow stability, and omitting `omega × r` corrupts relative hook speed and damping. Hook placement and vehicle inertia matter to real towed dynamics, as the primary [NACA directional-stability study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20050019614) shows for its own full-size cases. An isolated, force-free pair conserves total linear and angular momentum about a fixed world origin within stated numerical tolerance; individual aircraft angular momenta need not be equal or opposite.

The equations assume a true rotation and consistent inverse. SG-07 must specify the trial-quaternion-to-rotation convention for both aircraft, hook geometry, aerodynamic transforms and power accounting; a finite nonzero trial quaternion alone does not establish orthogonality. A temporary normalized rotation for evaluation without changing the stored RK trial is one candidate, not an adopted kernel change. Qualify the selected extension, accepted-boundary projection, convergence and legacy behavior together.

Power must be computed at the hooks. With world angular velocity `Omega_i=R_i*omega_i`,

~~~text
P_bodies = Σ_i [F_i·v_i + tau_i·Omega_i]
         = F_g·v_hook_g + F_t·v_hook_t = -T*ell_dot
U_line   = (1/2)*k*e²                  for an attached stretched line
P_bodies + d(U_line)/dt = -c*ell_dot²   while e>0 and the force is unclipped
~~~

The clipped shortening branch must also be non-energy-producing; check it explicitly. When extension crosses from nonpositive to positive while separating, the unsmoothed damping term creates a finite force jump. This massless law does not resolve an impact wave. Either retain and test that piecewise behavior or introduce a separately versioned, validated regularization. On instantaneous release or break, stored elastic energy leaves this two-body model with the detached line; record that accounting boundary and do not inject an unexplained impulse.

This first model omits line mass, sag, bending, aerodynamic drag, waves, propwash on the line, rope-ground/propeller/craft entanglement and line collision. Slack shape can be cosmetic only. [NASA towline analysis](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19960033194) shows why drag can matter for long high-altitude tethers; it does not quantify a short RC line. A massless uniform line cannot resolve local peak stress, a weak-link wave or separate end tensions. Collect installed length and both hook locations, then characterize material force–extension, rate dependence, damping and strength in the relevant range. If data are absent, label `k` and `c` as synthetic development assumptions. Break is disabled or scripted as an explicitly nonphysical exercise until an authored, evidenced threshold and time/rate policy are available; never supply a universal break load or silently clamp a peak. The line is not a rigid constraint, a tether winch or a complete damage model.

## 7. Numerical and event gate

For a linearized taut spring joining nonrotating point masses, `m_eff = 1/(1/m_t + 1/m_g)` and `omega_n = sqrt(k/m_eff)`. With fixed instant geometry and world-frame hook arms `a_i=R_i*r_i` and world-frame inertia tensors `I_i`, the instantaneous constraint effective mass also includes rotation:

~~~text
1/m_eff = 1/m_t + 1/m_g
          + (a_t × n)·I_t^-1(a_t × n) + (a_g × n)·I_g^-1(a_g × n)
~~~

These positive inverse-inertia terms mean small mass, low rotational inertia, long hook arms or a stiff line can make the coupled mode faster. This local estimate does not include all moving-geometry, gyroscopic or aerodynamic effects. Bound it over admissible orientations, masses and offsets; increasing damping also creates a fast decay mode, so an overdamped system is not automatically easy to integrate.

Classical RK4's ideal **undamped linear oscillator** has an imaginary-axis stability boundary of approximately `h*omega_n < sqrt(8)`. This is a necessary idealized stability check, not an accuracy guarantee, and it does not cover slack transitions, damping, aircraft aerodynamics, nonlinear contact or event localization. Determine a conservative bounded physical parameter envelope and internal step size from the full coupled system. Measure convergence at fixed starting state with increasing subdivisions for trajectories, maximum tension, energy residual, transition timing and accepted/refused outcomes. Include low-mass and high-stiffness corners and complete target-host cost. Refuse a line/vehicle/event combination that cannot meet bounded error and runtime criteria; do not silently soften the line, extend it, cap force or reclassify an unstable step as realistic. If the required internal step cannot fit the current policy, establish an independently qualified solver or generic kernel extension before admission.

Define and version attach, slack/taut, pilot release, AI release and evidence-based break. One held input produces one release; a released or broken line cannot reattach without a validated reset. A tick-boundary command has a declared effective time. An overload threshold may rise and fall within one tick, so an endpoint-only check can miss it; define stage/trajectory bracketing, localized event timing and error bounds. An RK trial-stage force alone is not automatically the accepted trajectory. State deterministic priority and outcome for coincident release, break, contact and fault; if ordering or detection cannot be resolved within admitted bounds, refuse the entire transaction. Account for detached line energy and preserve the same event sequence under exact replay.

The critical acceptance gate is a converged, passive, source-identified **two-body** reference trajectory with correct hooks, stage timing, whole-pair rollback, validated secondary orientation and event behavior. Agreement with an analytic fixture and exact replay are necessary software evidence; neither demonstrates that an EG4, FunCub or real towline has been physically calibrated. Remaining finite assumptions must be explicit: aircraft mass/area/CG/inertia and polar, controls and installation, compatible tug and power, hook geometry, line stiffness/damping/strength and allowable envelope, chosen aerodynamic and contact domains, unresolved wake/line effects, and weather/measurement uncertainty. Until each is sourced or measured, label the corresponding result estimated and keep that capability within its demonstrated envelope.

## 8. Requirements strengthened by the additional investigations

The [research index](../../research/glider-soaring/2026-11-15-investigations.md) retains primary evidence and experiments; the requirements below extend the physical contracts above.

**Identification and rigid-airframe domain — GI-01…06/GI-22.** Identify whole-aircraft polars with measured or bounded vertical air motion, synchronized actual controls, density and build identity. Reserve complete flights/maneuvers for final validation; data inspected to choose or revise a model belong to development/selection, even if coefficients were not refitted. Obtain a fresh independent final holdout after such reuse. Compare inventory-derived mass properties with independent CG/inertia measurements before fitting aerodynamic damping. Predeclare the speed, load, finish, control and launch domain of the rigid model. Wing deformation, linkage compliance and modal evidence may require a narrower domain; numerical stability and ground modal testing do not establish flutter clearance. Never invent a structural threshold from an unmeasured limit.

**Installed rope and joint trim — GI-07/08.** For a physically supported installation, validate the chosen line law against independent loading/unloading histories, rate effects, residual set, conditioning and installed terminations. One static pull cannot identify damping. Fit and test within an explicit range; history-dependent mismatch requires narrower admission or a separately versioned constitutive law. A bounded experimental tow may instead use explicitly synthetic aircraft/line parameters, but still requires numerical, joint-trim, authority, event, clearance and lifecycle proofs and cannot qualify the physical installation. Solve or otherwise establish coupled force/moment trim and achievable transitions using both hooks, actuator authority, drag and available tug power. Shared airspeed limits alone do not establish climb or recoverability.

**Coupling, solvers and geometry — GI-09…12.** Close per-hook work, both-body translational/rotational energy, spring storage and physical damping independently. Compare intentionally delayed force exchange with same-stage integration to expose coupling error; same-stage RK still needs numerical refinement. Document why bounded explicit subdivision is adequate or assess an implicit compliant/rigid-unilateral alternative against accuracy, iteration/failure, event and cost criteria. A rigid unilateral constraint is a different law with different impulse/release accounting. Implementing all alternatives is not a release prerequisite; select the smallest defensible candidate and verify it. Use swept geometry for aircraft encounters and any claimed taut-line clearance. A slack line has no determined shape in the massless model; any conservative length-envelope bound needs explicit assumptions, endpoint exemptions and a separate uncertainty/incident policy. An intersecting bound is a possible contact, not proof of impact. Inconclusive clearance follows the [launch guide's refusal/unknown-outcome rule](01-launch-and-control.md#5-the-tow-activity-state-machine), never successful-tow credit based on the rendered rope.

**Field and instrument semantics — GI-13…18.** Reuse M5's boundary smoothness, density-weighted flux and spatial-coherence work. In a stationary density column with divergence-free velocity, compare thermal surface mass flux with the independently integrated `W·grad(rho)` volume term; do not require an unjustified zero residual. Test the standalone and composite providers only inside their actual domains. Coherence across stations requires phase/covariance and load-response evidence, not only matching RMS. Separate physical-truth diagnostics, sensor-derived TE/netto and assisted cues; declare TAS conversion, sensor cadence, noise, anti-alias filtering, warmup, delay, dropout and checkpoint state. Ambient-only towing remains wake-unqualified, and three diameters from one propeller experiment is not a clearance threshold.

**Evidence and identities — GI-18/21/22.** Preserve atmosphere, polar/controls, solver/backend, both controllers, field agenda/epoch and any physical RNG algorithm/state in paired identity. State a reference-Re limitation if viscosity or Re does not drive the runtime polar. Report numerical error, measurement uncertainty and model discrepancy separately against the intended use; exact replay establishes reproducibility under its pinned identity.
