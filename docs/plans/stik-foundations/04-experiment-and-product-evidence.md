# Stik experiment and product evidence

**Status: proposed, 2026-11-15.** This plan makes the flight card, runway capture and Electric landing statistics tell the truth about what was observed. It does not change flight physics or declare the Stik physically validated. FT owns card work; FL owns flight logs; SC owns runway capture; UI-FS owns statistics; simulation/session and aircraft owners retain accepted identity, contact and physical-model contracts. Coordinate shared boundaries under the [ownership registry](../../README.md#tracks-plans-and-step-ids). Local slice labels below are navigation aids, not new project-wide step IDs.

## Decisions and boundaries

**Version the card's meanings.** A new card version must record requested duration, observed trace interval, last stored sample time, termination cause and the time origin of each target. Keep successful execution, maneuver validity and individual metric availability separate. A floor stop can be an observed outcome, while a 30-second metric beyond its coverage is `null` with a reason. Specify boundary tolerance and whether interpolation is supported; do not fabricate it by repeating the last value. Update `card_power.gd` and other time-indexed consumers, `profile.json`/`profile.md`, `summarize.py`, `profile_io.py` and `compare.py` together. Preserve v5 artifacts and their old meanings; `--allow-card-change` is an explicit exploratory comparison, not permission to equate fields whose definitions changed.

Report `first_unsolved_mps` and the available solver reason, residual, units, seeds and bound information, with absent diagnostics explicitly unavailable. Do not reinterpret the legacy `first_untrimmable_mps`, claim infeasibility, or convert it into a maximum speed. Preserve raw real/complex roots, state order, derivative step, fixed-control/RPM and diagonal-block scope. For a complex pair distinguish natural period `2π/|λ|` from damped oscillation period `2π/|Im λ|`; a nonzero real root has growth/decay time, not an oscillation period; a zero root has no finite growth/decay time. Ambiguous mode identities stay unclassified. A new solver or a coupled physical-instability claim is outside this reporting slice.

**Consume accepted identity.** Read actual accepted session aircraft path and exact input-byte SHA-256, plus actual power configuration, propulsion installation and accepted pack details, from the existing `FlightSession.trace_meta()`/session interfaces where available. Keep the semantic-model hash distinct from the file-byte hash. Record the Godot engine binary/version and actual source/build identity separately from the installed glow engine or electric motor; a version string or the wrapper repository's commit alone does not identify an external `--app` checkout. Freeze or record selected code, data and relevant generated/imported dependencies at launch and detect mutation before promoting a comparison. Reuse the logging/provenance contracts; the [identity plan](02-aircraft-identity-and-provenance.md) owns any demonstrated accepted-session metadata gap, while this plan owns card/capture/report consumers. Horizon work and capture tests using existing fields can start now; no circular dependency or new universal evidence framework is required.

Preserve the fixture's ideal default for compatibility and offer an explicit Electric `ideal|pack` selection, rejecting invalid or unsupported requests. Derive the recorded mode from the accepted session, not the requested flag or current catalog. Glow is not applicable; historical missing identities are unknown. Compare different hashes when deliberately studying data changes, but label identity compatibility separately from state/image differences. Equal aircraft IDs or two missing hashes are not proof of equal accepted sources.

**Prove the declared capture schedule.** Version an explicit expected `(view, time)` roster: `pilot`, `chase`, `side` and `wide` at 0–10 and 12 s, plus `closeup` at 0, 2 and 4 s: 51 unique frames. Compare expected, observed, missing, unexpected and duplicate keys; a partial schedule cannot be complete even if two partial runs match. Retain actual image dimensions, renderer, adapter, engine, settings and image hashes, and keep sync outcome separate from source identity and schedule coverage. The current report's camera-centering check assumes 1280×720; a 1080p path must use actual capture dimensions and preserve 720p behavior. Native 1920×1080 evidence has priority; the historical 720p fixture does not qualify it.

**Install Electric statistics as an observer.** After independent role review, add the dedicated `stik-electric-6s` row derived in the candidate: two main contacts and three stop contacts, using its own CG-relative points and existing `1e-6 m` geometry tolerance. Do not alias Basic Stik or change physics. Validate ideal and pack accepted inputs and fail closed on mismatched identity, span, missing/duplicate contacts, geometry or bindings. The candidate's geometric rest height is approximately 0.256923 m and clearance 0.25 m; test real adapter samples around the resulting 0.506923 m terrain-relative threshold. These are authored geometry, not measured wheel sag.

The existing `config_hash` is an accepted input hash; the reducer groups by aircraft ID, combining both Electric modes. Show coverage and mode only when known, without silently splitting historical aggregates. Consider an additive, bounded per-flight profile/coverage marker after checking readers; rules version alone does not establish whether a row existed. Old missing metadata stays unknown and old unknowns do not become successes. Unknown landings remain outside the success-plus-failure denominator; a partial flight depends on observation continuity/outcome, not merely the absence of a profile.

## Evidence promotion and rollback

Use the existing [flight-card runner and comparator](../../../research/flight-test/README.md), [flight-log workflow](../../FLIGHT-LOGGING.md), statistics reducers, runway report and [debug bridge](../../../tools/debug/README.md). Freeze baseline and candidate source/engine/configuration, use fresh evidence directories, and retain run receipts, raw profiles, CSV/markers, accepted identities and summaries together. For feel/physics changes first preserve a baseline `.orfl` and establish a complete exact replay; compare one candidate on the same tick/input tape and inspect `complete` and `solver_changed`. An external `--app` path needs its own source identity. On enrolled shared hosts use the [test queue](../../../tools/testqueue/README.md) and session handle; focused headless checks go through its supported launch path. Do not reserve a real controller or native machine until the corresponding experiment is scheduled.

Promotion is claim-specific and independent: **numerical** requires complete, compatible observations, documented tolerances and exact replay where that is the claim; **physical** requires measured installation, uncertainty and held-out maneuvers against the separate flight-model plan; **native** requires the stated OS/device/engine/renderer and actual 1080p capture with full schedule; **pilot feel** requires a declared protocol, participants, controls and counterbalanced or blinded comparisons where feasible. Each outcome can pass, fail or remain unknown without promoting another. Agree practical thresholds before collection and retain exclusions and uncertainty; absent physical, native or pilot evidence leaves those gates open.

Ship each slice behind its versioned contract. Keep legacy readers and original artifacts available; roll back a new producer or statistics profile without rewriting prior records, and mark new data unsupported if an older reader cannot interpret it safely. Do not replace baselines merely to obtain green comparisons. Once accepted, move enduring behavior into the existing FT, SC and UI-FS guides and retain dated proof with the owner.
