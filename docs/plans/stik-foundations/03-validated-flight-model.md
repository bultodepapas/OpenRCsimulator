## The problem and the evidence

Prioritize **dead-stick glide** and **low-speed powered elevator/rudder authority**. In the current non-glow path, a propeller below 1 rpm produces no force or torque; the electric Stik's current table cannot supply the negative absorbed torque that would restart it. The basic Stik instead uses prescribed RPM, and experimental glow has a separate estimated annular law. The optional shared tail-wash path is absent from Stik data; the electric parser currently rejects that option. The Extra demonstrates an available capability, not measured Stik behavior. These are observed code and bounded equation findings; their effect on an actual glide or control response is a **hypothesis to measure**, not an established flight error. See [R06](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r06-stopped-propellers-and-windmilling.md), [R07](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r07-propwash-control-coupling.md) and the [original audit](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/physics-and-aerodynamics.md).

Mass distribution, CG, loaded servo response and actual propulsion are competing explanations for handling. The basic model includes a virtual balance item; the electric build is a distinct estimated installation. The visible rib is not a measured covered-wing polar, and current servo timings and aerodynamic coefficients are estimates or donor-derived. Before fitting a flight response, measure or bound those uncertainties and the input/sensor clocks. [R05](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r05-low-reynolds-airfoils.md), [R08](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r08-electric-motor-esc-battery.md), [R09](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r09-glow-operating-envelope.md), [R10](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r10-measured-mass-cg-inertia.md) and [R18](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r18-system-identification-and-validation.md) define the evidence and its limits.

## Choose between hypotheses with experiments

1. **Propeller:** Compare a signed force/torque surface with a bounded blade-element candidate. UIUC's nominal 12×8 powered measurements are useful checks, but stop near windmilling and do not supply locked or negative-power data. The shipped 6S APC table is a computational prediction. A high-advance-ratio row cannot define zero RPM, and an arbitrary drag constant cannot predict breakaway. Characterize the identified installed propeller in coast and, separately, any measured ESC brake mode across axial speed, near-zero RPM and both sides of zero shaft power. Record locked-blade azimuth, air density, body interference, torque, force, friction and uncertainty; do not infer regeneration. Compare held-out dead-stick sink, airspeed and RPM against both candidate and legacy.
2. **Tail wash:** Survey hub, axis, propeller, tail surfaces and their common datum. Compare the existing partial-immersion tail increment with no wash and a simpler scalar candidate, varying one of RPM, airspeed or surface angle at a time. Static tail force/velocity and independent powered/unpowered elevator and rudder steps discriminate the mechanisms. A plausible geometry can seed an **experimental** sensitivity study; it is not calibrated authority. Wing/aileron wash is a separate problem.
3. **Aerodynamics:** [R11](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r11-induced-flow-spanwise-coupling.md) identifies a `cos(β)` source weight in one isolated approximation. This is neither a measured force error nor proof of a full-aircraft yaw defect. Compare an independently configured attached-flow lifting-line or vortex-lattice reference with a receiver-local prototype; vary the zero-flow continuation and station spacing. Test matched geometry, density, rates and frames. If no finite, passive, convergent and useful improvement appears, keep the existing closure. [R12](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r12-dynamic-stall-and-spin.md) places dynamic stall later in a model hierarchy, not in the critical path to glide and tail authority.

The [prototype contract](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/second-round/s2-02-physics-prototype-contracts.md) supplies synthetic sign, energy, static-hold and two-receiver examples. Its numbers are numerical oracles, not measured aircraft parameters. The [integration backlog I06–I10](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/integration-backlog.md#physics-prototypes-and-measurements) owns the research handoff.

## Incremental pull requests

### 1. Baseline and independent numerical contracts — ready to start

Capture a complete baseline using the [flight-log workflow](../../FLIGHT-LOGGING.md) and existing [flight-test card](../../../research/flight-test/README.md), with Basic and Electric ideal/finite routes identified separately. Record source, aircraft, power mode, engine, conditions and initial checkpoint; retain markers, CSV and reports. Include dead-stick shutdown/glide and matched-speed elevator/rudder steps, explicitly distinguishing idle from stopped propulsion. Preserve identical accepted input tapes, not a second pilot's approximate inputs.

Add isolated, independent S2-02 reference fixtures under the existing physics/test ownership: signed force/torque at rest, hold versus forward breakaway, free-body rotor momentum and the two-strip equal/unequal-speed solution. Reuse [electric event tests](../../../app/tests/test_electric_event_localization.gd), [rotor coupling tests](../../../app/tests/test_rotor_coupling.gd) and [induced-strip tests](../../../app/tests/test_strip_induced.gd); test the analytic fixture, not synthetic constants presented as real aircraft data. Document measured versus estimated configuration fields and missing observations. **Gate:** complete identity-pinned baseline and exact same-solver replay, independent oracle values and units, and documented unavailable measurements. For the synthetic scalar examples use the S2-02 scaled `1e-10` numerical tolerance; it is not a physical-accuracy band. No physical aircraft or new production law is needed to implement and verify these numerical contracts.

### 2. Opt-in power-off propeller and breakaway

Under `G`/`EL`, prototype a versioned shared force **and** signed aerodynamic torque evaluator, initially using a declared synthetic domain. Coordinate [propulsion](../../../app/physics/propulsion.gd), [electric motor](../../../app/physics/electric_motor.gd), [flight-session coupling](../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd), [ground-source friction](../../../app/physics/aircraft_ground_source.gd), stop/event handling and battery composition. The electric exact-rest branch currently determines hold/breakaway from motor drive and body acceleration without direct aerodynamic torque; changing only the map leaves that seam incomplete. Account for the same external aerodynamic couple in body and shaft equations without adding it twice to total angular momentum; include relative-spin/body energy cross terms. Distinguish passive coast, explicitly modeled brake and unsupported reverse spin/flow or regeneration.

**Gate:** S2-02 fixed-carrier, motor-off examples: hold with `+0.02 N·m` aerodynamic rotor torque versus `0.05 N·m` static friction; breakaway with `+0.10 N·m` aerodynamic rotor torque versus that friction and `0.01 kg·m²` rotor inertia, giving initial acceleration `5 rad/s²`. Keep the separate free-body coupling case distinct. Also require finite declared limits around rest and the old cutoff, phase/azimuth convention, stop–hold–restart and timestep refinement; passive work under the stated settled-flow assumptions and complete event/rollback accounting. A stateful wake needs its own stored-energy/flux ledger. Run focused existing propeller, electric motor, rotor and event tests plus unchanged-law replay. Physical promotion additionally needs matched held-out propeller and glide observations; no borrowed glow surrogate or unsupported brake setting qualifies it.

### 3. Opt-in powered tail authority

Under `D`/`EL` and aircraft-data owners, extend strict admission in [aircraft_data.gd](../../../app/physics/aircraft_data.gd), then use [dynamics](../../../app/physics/dynamics.gd), [slipstream](../../../app/physics/slipstream.gd) and [wash transport](../../../app/physics/wash_transport.gd) with the correct stage-local hub inflow, thrust and torque. Preserve the shared washed-minus-unwashed tail load and its common wing-downwash input. Evaluate wake occupancy and decay, including residual transport after a throttle reduction; do not count shaft reaction or gyro twice. Generated electric data goes through [installation.json and its builder](../../../research/propulsion/stik-6s/README.md), never a hand edit of generated JSON.

**Gate:** rejected malformed capabilities; unchanged output with the option absent; sign/reversal, partial immersion, edge/zero-thrust continuity, decay, frame and timestep checks, and focused [slipstream](../../../app/tests/test_slipstream_downwash.gd), [wash transport](../../../app/tests/test_wash_transport.gd) and electric session coverage. Static and independent flight power-on/off measurements are required before claiming that the modeled increment is correct.

### 4. Bounded induced-flow prototype

Outside the default model, test receiver-local source circulation against S2-02's frozen two-strip oracle and an independently configured attached-flow reference. The receiver speed approaching zero makes a direct `Vj/Vi` division singular: use a justified finite vector/force continuation or explicitly reject that domain. Sweep stagnation, reverse flow, mirrored rates and controls, uniform sideslip in the **isolated 2D fixture**, density and speed; report force/moment convergence under span and timestep refinement without refitting. Check aerodynamic work/passivity in the candidate's declared domain. Coordinate the local/global blend, induced drag, wing wake, tail downwash and ground effect before any opt-in shared implementation; do not layer a second induced load onto the existing one. If no defensible continuation or benefit is demonstrated, stop at the offline result. [R16](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r16-integrator-and-actuator-coupling.md) explains why sampled servo/wake timing and pure stage evaluation must remain explicit.

### 5. Identify and validate, then consider stall memory

Use the existing [weighing](../../../research/validation/weighing/), [inertia](../../../research/validation/inertia/) and propulsion reducers. Identify actual installation, fuel/pack state, CG datum, throws, servo load/timing, motor/ESC firmware and coast/brake mode. Retain raw repeats, calibration, units, axes and correlated uncertainties; bound added-air and alignment effects in swing tests and establish a positive-definite tensor. Do not mistake in-air inertia for an automatically corrected rigid-body result. Separate practical engine/propeller limits, numerical table support and modeled failure; no magic RPM clamp.

With input and mass errors constrained, use informative positive/negative controls at multiple measured speeds and power levels. Check identifiability and parameter correlations; fit one declared model only on training runs, then freeze it and assess entire held-out flights, directions and conditions. Compare response histories, peaks, lag, damping, moments and uncertainty, rather than one attractive trajectory. If high-angle evidence warrants it, compare current quasi-steady separation first, then an opt-in bounded stateful separation model; more elaborate vortex/stall models remain deferred. A spin alone is not validation. For ground/flare differences, use the existing `GP` contact and ground-effect gates independently of landing-statistics classification ([R17](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r17-ground-contact-and-landing-evidence.md)).

## Acceptance, performance and rollback

**Numerical/software:** finite states, frame/sign/unit checks, analytic fixtures, symmetry only for symmetric configurations, conservation within stated assumptions, convergence, admission/refusal, event ordering and atomic rollback. Run affected session tests and [golden flights](../../../app/tests/test_golden.gd); re-record a golden only for an intentional documented physics change. A passing synthetic fixture proves its equation, not the airplane.

**Replay/comparison:** first reproduce the baseline with matching source/configuration and `complete` true. Record `solver_changed`: require false for aircraft-data-only candidates and hold aircraft data fixed for a solver comparison. Candidate replay is an open-loop, same-tape comparison, not exact equivalence or a newly trimmed flight; incompatible state layouts require their own proof and can be refused. Compare complete accepted ticks, shutdown, glide, control steps and the relevant flight-test cases; retain failures and identity. **Physical:** predeclare tolerances and uncertainty, then compare independent component observations and held-out aircraft flights. Apply the project's `abs(error) + Uerror <= limit` rule where the owning gate specifies it; ambiguous intervals remain inconclusive. **Pilot:** a later blinded or counterbalanced owner evaluation of glide, power transitions and control feel is a separate subjective result, not a substitute for measurements.

Hand the complete airborne, power-transition and ground workloads to [performance](05-deterministic-performance.md): baseline/candidate identities, identical tapes, wake/event counts, observer-off controls, per-tick median and tail cost, and 1080p-first target-host frame evidence. Do not buy headroom by reducing authoritative physics or skipping validation; Gate P remains with the ROADMAP/PT owners.

All new laws and data are opt-in with explicit version/configuration pins; an absent option preserves old behavior and old checkpoints/logs select the matching supported law or are refused, never silently migrated to different physics. Test restore, failure rollback and old-law replay; retain the old evaluator throughout the supported migration window. The [two-variant plan](../ugly-stik-two-variants.md) currently preserves the Basic ID, data bytes and behavior: use separate research candidates until that owner explicitly revises the preservation decision. Electric promotion likewise requires its owner and measured gates, with generators and manifests updated together. Do not change controller rates to conceal physics uncertainty, add a general wake rewrite, transplant another aircraft's polar, or claim braking, regeneration, physical realism or hardware qualification from software tests.
