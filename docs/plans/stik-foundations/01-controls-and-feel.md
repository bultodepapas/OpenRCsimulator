## Problem and evidence

- `FlightSession._input` forwards motion; `RcInput.on_motion`, `poll` and `sticks()` retain history, read the selected reader and supply commands. The pinned Godot poll reads an event-fed cache updated **before** dispatch; ordinary flight disables accumulated input. The injected event/poll disagreement in [R01](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r01-raw-input-observations.md) is a reader-seam test, not an established default-flight occurrence. Some SDL initial-axis paths suppress motion, with device/backend exceptions; repeated polls and delivered events are not necessarily independent hardware reports. See the [pinned-engine follow-up](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/second-round/s2-01-pinned-input-backend.md).
- `RcCalibration.sample` uses a short window of polled values and retains sweep extrema. A synthetic sequence with one `+1.0` outlier repeated through the cache widened a true `+0.8` endpoint and reduced normalized travel to about `0.8`; this is not a measured failure rate. A quiet, genuinely held endpoint can also yield repeated cached values. [R02](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r02-calibration-cadence-and-endpoints.md) compares these counterexamples.
- Registry generations and session disconnect/focus holds exist, but a receipt-time stamp does not prove a buffered event's origin. Old SDL instance events are filtered after removal; a converted buffered Godot event crossing slot reuse is conditional and untested in ordinary flight. The focus-loss LOW scenario was injected; actual background delivery depends on device/OS. See [R03](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r03-focus-reconnect-and-identity.md) and S2-01.
- Radio shaping, gamepad conditioning, aircraft rates and actuator response are distinct. The rate-limited servo is updated once per tick and held through force stages; the Stik's `0.14 s` neutral-to-full value is an estimate, not measured loaded response. No new law or tick rate is established as an improvement. See [R04](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r04-latency-and-control-feel.md), [R16](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r16-integrator-and-actuator-coupling.md), [R18](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/investigations/r18-system-identification-and-validation.md), and [backlog I01, I02 and I06](../../research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/research-round-02/integration-backlog.md).

## Desired end-to-end contract

1. **Identity and authority.** Associate usable observations with the selected flight role, connection generation, mapping revision and focus epoch where the backend establishes them. Separate diagnostic history from authority. Unavailable origin, initial value or health is **unknown**, not a fresh report or proof of a live radio. Silence, static values, polls and an enumerated receiver after transmitter-off do not prove link health. Preserve the separately qualified focused gamepad valid-poll path.
2. **Recovery and setup.** Selection, remap, disconnect and focus loss invalidate appropriate authority; pause and AUX behavior stay activity-specific. Retain radio low-throttle and explicit Continue requirements; HIGH after LOW revokes evidence. Preserve keyboard recovery, preflight surface inspection distinct from motor readiness, quiet controls, selected-device capture and release isolation. Qualify backend origin or a verified barrier before claiming event-origin protection.
3. **Calibration and shaping.** Preserve axis direction, independently calibrated asymmetric travel, inversion, center/low return, rejection of simultaneous ambiguous channels, compatible AUX, transactional draft/Save/Cancel and error recovery. Show whether the wizard is displaying a cached reading, a pilot-confirmed position or a qualified observation; never label UI poll counts as hardware reports. Record transmitter trims/rates/expo. Radios retain transmitter-shaped linear simulator input without a second aircraft rate/expo stage; gamepads retain rescaled scalar deadzone and one cubic expo, followed by applicable aircraft travel scaling without double expo; calibrated joysticks may use aircraft rates/expo. Absolute radio/trigger throttle and virtual rate throttle remain distinct, including hold semantics.
4. **Actuation and observation.** Publish accepted shaped command, trim, sampled servo position and timing without changing the plant. Preserve sampled-law exact replay unless a separately versioned, evidence-supported change is accepted. Measure physical surface angle and loaded response independently.

## Decisions and discriminating experiments

**Observation policy (D6/F1/F3).** Compare (a) a backend-qualified snapshot with availability and owner/epoch, (b) event-owned values plus initial/held-control handling, and (c) visible unqualified values with pilot confirmation and unknown health. Exercise immediate and buffered modes, held-at-connect, quiet controls, slot reuse, old-instance removal, remap and focus loss with LOW/HIGH. Capture origin at the earliest seam, independently observe stick position and report unavailable origin/report counters. Select only routes supported by native evidence; do not assume a heartbeat or require every axis to move.

**Endpoint policy (D6/UI-15).** Compare explicit per-end hold/confirm and report-aware candidates on the same deterministic traces and, where feasible, independently observed reports. Compare min/center/max, inversion and normalized travel for held ends and the retained outlier. Include slow/bursty input, short dwell, asymmetry, reversal, jitter, mixes and absent axes. A quantile may clip a real endpoint; neither quantiles nor elapsed polling prove independence. Derive thresholds from repeatability and instrument uncertainty; return inconclusive when evidence cannot discriminate.

**Latency, actuator and feel (F6/D6/physics).** At actual 1920×1080, use the existing `LatencyPatch` raw-axis threshold marker: film physical stick motion and the display marker in the same high-speed view, or synchronize a physical trigger and luminance sensor. Record axis/threshold, controller, firmware, transport, host/build/engine, display refresh and scanout position, camera exposure/frame rate/rolling shutter, workload, frame drops, clock offsets and measurement uncertainty. Randomize step timing; use the existing controller plan's minimum 30 filmed transitions per primary transport, reporting count, median, p95 and uncertainty. This physical motion-to-marker result includes input and rendering but excludes servo and aircraft response. Separately record command tick to sampled servo and aircraft response, using full-rate data when required and documenting sensor bandwidth, filtering and clock alignment. A flight log records mapped/conditioned rather than raw HID input; it cannot substitute for physical motion-to-marker measurement.

Measure actual surface neutral, endpoints, asymmetry, linkage, rates/trims, voltage and repeated steps/reversals under stated unloaded and representative loaded conditions. Record angles, times, saturation, uncertainty and repeatability. Compare the current sampled law, smaller **outer** ticks and an offline continuous-actuator reference with identical physical command timing before considering a versioned stage-coupled law. Finer internal RK stages alone do not resample the once-per-outer-tick servo. Preserve old-law replay and require convergence, event, cost and held-out component checks.

For subjective assessment, use the same controller, installation, task, scenario, display and workload; record each pilot's actual post-shaped commands rather than forcing identical live inputs. Predeclare participants, sample plan, questions and exclusions under [the evidence plan](04-experiment-and-product-evidence.md); blind labels where practical and counterbalance or randomize order. Retain individual ratings and inconclusive trials; preference does not establish aerodynamic truth.
