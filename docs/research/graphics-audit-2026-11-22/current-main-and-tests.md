## Coverage and next measurement choices

1. **Vehicle families:** V1 tour roster and manifest are fixed-wing only (`tools/godtest/catalog.py:10–18,148–160`); the actual driver refuses rotorcraft (`app/debug/godtest_driver.gd:160–164`). It is not an FPS baseline for cars, FPV drone racing, helicopter, Workshop or all menus. Define separate source-bound, representative routes: Nitro/electric car driving and contact; FPV race/impacts and HUD; aircraft flight and Home/Settings/Controls; cold/warm loading. Record replay/accepted-tick and identity without changing physics. Multirotor has only reference physics and the Mac ground path is reference (`docs/DECISIONS.md:43`, `docs/STACK.md:20–24,26–34`), so a blanket native-only requirement would exclude real product paths rather than qualify them.
2. **Retained non-tour vehicle evidence:** historical headless quad measurements on the development PC show about 1 ms/tick in open air and, after a contact change, a 12 m/s dive's worst tick 11.0→4.6 ms (`docs/plans/drone-racing.md:606–622`), plus a first-touch kit moved into loading (`:640`); no rendered FPS or target qualification. MT8 source/headless native Session timing 3.204/4.573 ms median/P95 and a separate 4.034/4.963 ms short profile exceed the 4.167 ms/tick P95 budget, subject to shared-host overlap (`docs/research/rc-monster-truck/RCV-handling/README.md:25–31,47,59,65–67`); neither is new nor Nitro evidence. Preserve their exact historical conditions rather than comparing numbers across versions/hosts.
3. **Per-phase attribution:** explicitly join engine and host clocks with bounded uncertainty; reject unsupported short phases at 1 Hz. Bind render adapter to utilization device and physical host/display. Capture actual panel, drawable and internal 3D dimensions/scaler. Attribute callback, GPU and presentation separately, with matched observer-off/on and stream-flush alignment. Need temperature/power/background controls; the M1 map did not record thermal state separately.
4. **V2 and repeats:** V1 is an airborne 20–140 m AGL orbit, not takeoff, low scenic pass, same-flight two laps, roll or intended crash (`PERFORMANCE_TOUR.md:62–104`; `docs/plans/god-test.md:165–232`). Three requested repeats always use fixed Stik→jet→Corsair order (`PERFORMANCE_TOUR.md:45–49`), so aircraft and progressive warming are confounded; compare matched within-aircraft pairs and record thermal order. Three short repeats do not replace cold/warm loading, endurance, broader lifecycle or target-specific Retina gates (`2026-11-20-plan-audit.md:224–235`).
5. **Admission and retained failures:** automatic nine-case lease budgeting is improved; direct preset can still submit a 3,600 s default or shorter-than-roster timeout and lacks full-roster minimum enforcement. Windows-local graphical admission is experimental; Linux/macOS graphical adapter and enrolled resume remain unimplemented (`PERFORMANCE_TOUR.md:106–156`). Keep all failed/retried cases, source/binary identity and relocation limitations explicit. Don't equate a completed suite or green exit with targets met (`analysis.py:569–596,618–624`).
