**Source:** main `843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef`. These paths are unchanged in the seventh-pass integration candidate. The findings establish repeated work and candidate boundaries. They do not establish elapsed time, allocation counts, a native controller qualification, or an FPS improvement. [Summary and validation priorities](seventh-pass.md) · [Reproduced arithmetic](seventh-pass-arithmetic.json).

## X02 — a drone race wrapper calculates speed without a consumer

The shipped drone installs the GDScript controlled plant and its sampled controller. When the selected course matches the field, the session wraps that plant in `RaceModel`; the default practice oval therefore also wraps IDLE free flight before a race. An empty or unmatched course uses the bare plant, and paused activity does not integrate. Another vehicle's native-backend selection does not make this controller native. [Plant installation](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/sim/multirotor/session.gd#L281-L335) · [Default activity and course selection](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/vehicles/multirotor/multirotor_scene.gd#L350-L420).

On every successful wrapped sample, `_sample` rotates body velocity into world coordinates and takes its norm. `RaceDirector.stage` reads that speed only for START from IDLE, FINISHED or ABORTED, after terminal and pad-position checks. Ordinary no-action samples calculate a value the director never reads. At 240 eligible samples per simulated second, this is 240 quaternion rotations and 240 norms. The helper also constructs packed input/output arrays; the source does not prove a corresponding heap-allocation count. This is once per sampled tick, separate from U07's per-RK-stage slices. [Producer and math helper](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/sim/race/race_model.gd#L241-L271) · [Only speed consumer](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/sim/race/race_director.gd#L121-L162) · [Quaternion operations](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/physics/math3d.gd#L112-L155).

**First experiment:** calculate the existing speed only for a validated START in those three phases, reading accepted phase after the inner sample callback at the current point. Retain dictionary shape if consumers require it. Preserve the existing rotation and norm on eligible starts: replacing them with a body-space norm is algebraically attractive but can change float64 rounding at the strict rest-speed threshold.

The normal kernel validates the 13 body values, but `_sample` itself does not check body length before indexing attitude and velocity. A direct malformed call or an inner callback can therefore make skipping the old computation observably different. Restrict the fast path to a proved valid, unchanged body/context or preserve the original fallback and error/refusal behavior. Keep callback ordering, sampled controller state, atomic publication, rollback and 240 Hz. [Kernel validation](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/sim/simulation.gd#L740-L751).

**Separately scoped endpoint extension:** an unfiltered endpoint rotates both path and previous velocities, subtracts and norms them for chord qualification. The director only consumes that qualification while scoring a RACING candidate. An eligible no-start, unfiltered non-RACING tick therefore performs three rotations and two norms across sample and endpoint: 720 and 480 per simulated second at 240 Hz. This is a conditional upper count, not a frequency measurement. [Endpoint](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/sim/race/race_model.gd#L293-L327) · [Director routing](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/sim/race/race_director.gd#L191-L226).

A GO transition can make the candidate RACING on this very tick: testing the previous phase would be wrong. Public mutable rules, malformed modes, arbitrary callbacks, and the acceleration-envelope read outside racing also constrain equivalence. Preserve impact/path/time construction and all racing qualification; do not combine this extension with the simpler sample guard until each is understood independently.

**Evidence needed:** exact old/new sampled modes/aux, accepted frames and events for no-action in every phase; start on/off pad and adjacent representable speeds; cancel, countdown/GO, crash/depletion, mutated callbacks/rules, fault/rollback, reset and replay. Use the existing race director/model/session/activity tests. Measure sample callback, complete tick and frame p95/p99 on matching ordinary and racing flights. Neither fewer rotations nor a faster isolated callback establishes a visible FPS gain.

## X03 — duplicate interpolation inside an already native battery stage

The admitted fixed-wing coupled-electric path reaches `ElectricPreparedSource` and native `pack_ecm_evaluate`. Fresh Turbo Lumber, MoonRunner and electric Stik catalog choices use a finite pack with one RC branch, 27 SOC knots and four temperature knots ([cell template](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/data/batteries/cells/lipo-bak503562-galeotti-v1.json#L145-L184)). Confirm the actual session backend: the fixed-wing admission rules can select reference, including with an RC circuit. [Native stage entry](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/research/native-kernel/src/nk_stage.hpp#L370-L380) · [Electric source](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/research/native-kernel/src/nk_electric_stage.hpp#L32-L52) · [Admission](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/sim/flight_session.gd#L773-L815) · [Catalog defaults](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/app_state/aircraft_catalog.gd#L125-L149).

Each successful stock stage prepares average-cell and weak-cell interpolation independently. Both use the same temperature, so its segment search and fraction are duplicated. Each samples OCV, resistance, RC resistance and time constant: eight table-sample calls in total. [Weights, samples and their callers](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/research/native-kernel/src/nk_pack_ecm.hpp#L196-L267).

**Native experiment:** share the same-temperature preparation within one evaluation. Compute both SOC weights using the original arithmetic. Reuse each average-cell table result for its weak-cell counterpart only if the relevant integer indices agree and the individual floating-point weights have identical bit patterns, with a proved valid domain. Otherwise run the original calculation. Preserve the weak-resistance multiplication, even with a factor of one. Use a C++17-compatible bit copy; numerical equality misses signed zero and raw-structure comparison can compare padding. `R` wraps a double. [Numeric representation](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/research/native-kernel/src/nk_math.hpp#L34-L39) · [Build contract](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/research/native-kernel/build.py#L138).

Balanced stock pack parameters do **not** prove identical SOC weights throughout flight. The source computes weak SOC as `q/Q` and average SOC as `initial_soc - (initial_charge-q)/Q`; those expressions can round differently. The full-charge initial state provides a concrete equal-weight case, while the live hit rate is unmeasured. For 240 successful one-piece RK4 ticks, four stages per tick and one RC branch:

\[
N_{samples}=240\times4\times8=7{,}680,\qquad
N_{saved}\le240\times4\times4=3{,}840.
\]

These are table-sample calls per simulated second. Event localization, substeps, pauses and refusals alter the count. Compiler optimization, guard cost and cache behavior can erase the advantage.

On 23 November, a standalone Windows x86-64 check compiled the unchanged weights and table sampler from `caaf80aee` with GCC 15.2 and `-ffp-contract=off`. It used the shipped Galeotti SOC knots, temperature knots and OCV table. The grid used a 4 Ah group at full initial SOC and zero weak-SOC offset, with 10,001 evenly spaced fractions of initial charge and seven temperatures (273.15, 278, 283.15, 295, 298.15, 304 and 313.15 K). Temperature weights matched bit for bit throughout. At capacity factor 1, the SOC index and weight matched for 38,675 of 70,007 evaluations, and the two sampled OCV values differed in 4,739 cases. At factor 0.92 the corresponding counts were 7 and 70,000. This rejects unconditional table reuse even for the balanced case. The grid leaves live hit rate and native stage time open.

**Secondary leads:** the same stock 11-value pack block is checked for finiteness by both the public block validator and evaluator. A rates vector is constructed, moved through the electric result and copied into the final derivative. Consider an already-validated private native entry and bounded rates storage only after profiling; preserve checked public entry points and invalid-input ordering. A vector constructor in source is not measured allocation evidence. [Validation and result construction](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/research/native-kernel/src/nk_pack_ecm.hpp#L223-L357) · [Derivative copy](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/research/native-kernel/src/nk_stage.hpp#L432-L434).

**Evidence needed:** guarded-hit counts and paired native component/tick/frame times, exact old/new stage outputs at knots and adjacent floats, signed zero, weak-cell imbalance, overflow/nonfinite input, 0–2 RC branches, temperature rows, charge/cutoff boundaries, event location and rollback. Retain native/reference qualification, matching-source replay and target package gates. No floating-point reassociation, weaker validation, reduced simulation rate, or cross-stage memoization is proposed.

## X04 — precomputed matrix work for saturated drone allocation

The armed, non-turtle controller allocates once per sample. Its existing inverse already handles in-box requests cheaply. Only saturation enters an active-set loop capped at 16 iterations, and fallback iterates 81 status codes, examining at most 81 reference sets; fixed rotor bounds reduce that count. Every iteration with free rotors rebuilds a reduced matrix from prepared `H` and repeats pivoted Gaussian elimination. Four rotors have at most 16 free subsets. [Controller](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/physics/multirotor/flight_controller.gd#L365-L394) · [Fast path and active set](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/physics/multirotor/allocator.gd#L135-L188) · [Reduced solve](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/physics/multirotor/allocator.gd#L417-L445) · [Elimination](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/physics/multirotor/allocator.gd#L507-L539).

First measure saturation fraction, iteration counts and fallbacks by maneuver. If material, prepare matrix-side pivots/factors per mask and apply the same ordered operations to each current RHS. A coarse native sampled-controller boundary could amortize crossings, but no admitted native quad controller is established by this audit.

**Critical cache constraint:** the allocator dictionary is public and mutable. Neither dictionary identity nor the existing controller `config.identity` proves its matrix contents unchanged; that identity omits `h`, `a`, `b`, `b_inverse`, `w_diag` and `h_norm`. A factor cache needs the current matrix shape/content and free mask, or a newly justified immutable prepared boundary. A whole native controller snapshot must guard every dependency. Preserve original fallback for mutations and malformed/nonfinite data. [Configuration identity](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/physics/multirotor/flight_controller.gd#L156-L173) · [Limited request validation](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/843f357c07af842054f3ccd6728f93beca9a57ef/app/physics/multirotor/allocator.gd#L348-L361).

Retain pivot ties, elimination order, bounds/releases, iterations, anti-windup, result fields and the independent reference fallback. Extend the seeded `test_mr_allocator.gd` cases with exact old/new boundary, singular/mutated-configuration and randomized comparisons; qualify sampled control, crash, checkpoint/rollback, replay and actual backend selection. Reject the added machinery if saturation is rare or validation costs exceed saved work. This remains a conditional investigation, separate from U07's four-residual result objective.

## Leads rejected or already covered

The stock quad's damped electrical bus already has a signed-current closed form; its 16-mask electric-bus solver is a conditional fallback. The first-order laboratory motor path and fixed-wing ESC supervisor are different consumers. Disarmed controller samples still update checkpointed gyro/derivative/setpoint state, so skipping them merely because motors are off is not justified. T01 already owns full quad telemetry evaluation, U07 race-context slices and F23 contact subdivision.
