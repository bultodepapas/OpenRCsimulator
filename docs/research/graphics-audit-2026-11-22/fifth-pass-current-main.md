## The new Linux report materially changes prioritization

The retained [PRIMIS/megatron comparison](../god-test/2026-11-22-linux-uhd630-comparison.md) is an exploratory campaign, not a fresh measurement performed in this audit. It records source `7f4db1b1b` plus harness changes, a matching 9,651-file digest across two hosts, the pinned Godot 4.8-beta2 binary, actual native physics, Intel UHD 630, Mesa 25.0.7 and 1920×1080 through a headless Weston compositor. Each reported case is a single pass; the hardware, source and capture qualifications in that report still apply. This audit read the retained report and current harness; it did not independently re-adjudicate every campaign artifact.

## Limits that must not be confused with missing optimization

| Control or bound | Current source | Meaning and audit decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit presentation cap | `DisplayPreferences.MAX_FPS_VALUES` includes 0, 30, 60, 90, 120, 144, 165 and 240. Default max FPS is 0; default VSync is enabled. `AppRoot._apply_presentation` sets `Engine.max_fps`. | Check the effective user's cap and presentation mode before interpreting a plateau. Source defaults alone do not tell us the active display/driver limit. No newly discovered universal 60-FPS cap. |
| Physics catch-up | `project.godot`: 240 ticks/s, at most 12 physics steps per engine main-loop frame. | Existing N8: real time needs at least 20 engine main-loop frames/s under this count bound, at ordinary time scale without pause or a fixed-FPS override. Raising the step limit adds work and can worsen stalls; it is not a free FPS optimization. |
| Frame-generation admission | `frame_generation_policy.gd`: 30 real FPS floor, 35 recovery rate, two-second rate window and three recovery bins, plus renderer/history/present proof. | These are safety/admission rules, not a general engine cap. Lowering them cannot make the underlying physics/render workload fast. Generated display frames must not be reported as additional accepted simulation ticks. |
| Scene detail versus ground shading | `SceneDetail.PROFILES` changes scenery, tree coverage, mesh-LOD threshold and worn-path turf; lower profiles already skip worn-path samples. | Its Low threshold is 4 px versus Medium 2 and High 1. The shared base grass macro/anti-tiling shader is not thereby replaced with a cheap terrain variant. Independent reconstruction/MSAA controls also exist. This explains a coverage gap; it is not a newly measured shader gain. |
| Rain capacity | Low/Medium/High/Ultra streak capacities are 8,000/20,000/36,000/56,000 after the zoom multiplier. Visible count depends on zoom and rain. | A bounded capacity can still incur a first-use preparation cost. Capacity is not the number visibly drawn, and it is not an FPS cap. See V09. |
| Glow audio lead | 50 ms target, 256-frame render chunks, 2 ms sleep on full/no-work worker polls. | Queue lead is an audio continuity/latency policy. The worker can refill without sleeping, so 500 iterations/s is not a global cap. This is existing AUD work, not a ninth new audio bottleneck. |

Source: [display preferences](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/0f859b5f358f4807877ec8bd7fe8009bc3856d3f/app/app_state/display_preferences.gd), [presentation application](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/0f859b5f358f4807877ec8bd7fe8009bc3856d3f/app/app_root.gd#L2118-L2175), [project configuration](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/0f859b5f358f4807877ec8bd7fe8009bc3856d3f/app/project.godot), [frame-generation policy](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/0f859b5f358f4807877ec8bd7fe8009bc3856d3f/app/render/frame_generation_policy.gd), [scene detail](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/0f859b5f358f4807877ec8bd7fe8009bc3856d3f/app/render/scene_detail.gd#L16-L28), and the [audio](fifth-pass-audio.md) and [lens](fifth-pass-lens.md) source traces.

For sustained engine main-loop/process-frame cadence \(F\), at ordinary time scale without pause or a fixed-FPS override, the count-limited maximum simulation pace is

\[
  P_{\max}=\min\left(1,\frac{12F}{240}\right)=\min(1,F/20).
\]

Here \(F\) is the cadence tied to the engine step limit, not physical display-present FPS or generated frames. The GOD TEST driver samples in `_process` (`app/debug/godtest_driver.gd:755–807`); drawable/drawn counters are distinct. At 13 such frames/s this bound is 0.65. Matching it supports the catch-up explanation; it does not identify whether CPU, GPU, audio synchronization, compositing or another cost caused 13 FPS. This formula remains the earlier finding, not a new repair proposal.
