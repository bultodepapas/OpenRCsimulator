# Fourth pass — drones and helicopters

Scope: source inspection only, with application files identified by the lead as identical to main `7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464`. These are additional candidate paths, not evidence of a measured FPS loss or a ranking of savings. No app, engine, tests, network, or hardware was run. The baseline fixed-wing stutter map does not measure these vehicles. Preserve float64, 240 Hz sampling, `Simulation` transaction and rollback, model/backend and source/replay identity. The quad is the reference GDScript controlled plant even if another vehicle has a native evaluator; the helicopter's requested native evaluator is installed only where admitted and falls back for a published lake (`app/helicopter_main.gd:110-112,159-169,180-188`; `app/sim/helicopter_flight_session.gd:218-255`; `app/sim/multirotor/session.gd:281-295,331-335`).

## U05. Helicopter candidate contact validates the complete prepared geometry on every candidate tick

**Path and cost.** Launch selects a rotorcraft, prepares `ImpactDetector` geometry in `setup`, and calls `reset` (`app/helicopter_main.gd:159-176,180-185`; `app/sim/helicopter_flight_session.gd:353-377,684-767`). Every integrated helicopter candidate that reaches the contact observer passes `_impact_geometry` to `ImpactDetector.evaluate` in its step policy, before the candidate can be accepted (`helicopter_flight_session.gd:299-314,329-350`; `app/sim/simulation.gd:565-584`). `evaluate` validates the mutable prepared structure at `app/physics/rotorcraft/impact_detector.gd:71-82`, before the sweep plan, the no-terrain/empty-world return, or scenery candidate gating at `:83-105`.

For the geometry created by this source, `_body_probes` constructs exactly 56 probes: 4 skids, 8 fuselage corners, 36 boom points, and 8 fin corners (`:267-291`). Each successful `_prepared_error` walks all 56 (`:161-199`): it checks per-probe dictionary fields and three finite coordinates, checks every coordinate against the packed hull, computes each point norm to reconstruct the maximum probe reach, rebuilds/checks two rotor source arrays, and computes the rotor bounds. Thus one normal candidate tick reaching the detector can do 56 probe checks and 56 norms even if the eventual contact broad phase has no hit. It is not 56 collisions or 56 scene queries, and earlier rejected candidate paths skip this observer. At full real-time throughput that is up to 240 invocations and 13,440 probe-check iterations per second, not a measured hotspot. It runs for both actual native-stage and reference-stage helicopters: the stage backend selection does not replace this GDScript step policy (`helicopter_flight_session.gd:218-255,259-272,299-333`). Paused sessions do not advance a candidate tick; direct manual stepping and reset behavior have separate reachability.

**Historical context.** [HELI-33 performance evidence](../helicopter/HELI-33/checks/performance/RESULTS.md) already measured the whole impact detector on older Godot 4.7.2/source, including an empty-airborne regression. It did not isolate this validation, and its final detector blob differs from current main. It motivates attribution; its timings are not current validation costs.

**Possible experiment.** Profile validation separately from broad phase, rotor/terrain sweep, total tick, and frame tails. If material, try moving the *same validation* into one native/built-in batch boundary while preserving value/type checks, comparison order, error text/order, finite checks and exactly qualified reach arithmetic. Alternatively use a truly immutable prepared representation plus a cheap proof that detects every currently observable source and cache mutation; this is an architectural change, not permission to trust a `Dictionary`'s identity or skip the check. Keep the public general-purpose `evaluate` behavior for malformed callers. Since current code explicitly accepts a mutable cache, an implementation that just validates once at setup is incorrect. Source proves that `test_heli_impact_detector.gd:120-129` expects mutated source and reach values to refuse, and `test_helicopter_lake_context.gd:136-153` proves an in-session stale geometry must refuse and roll back an actual tick. Contact faults also have precedence obligations (`test_heli_rotor_domain.gd:234-255`).

**Validation.** Compare old/new outputs, error and precedence for altered probe coordinates/metadata/count, hull, source skid/rotor geometry, reaches, nonfinite/malformed structures, no world, populated scenery, dry/lake terrain, high airborne, hover, ground, rotor strike and nonterminal contacts. Keep exact sweep/tie outcomes, pending-impact publication, checkpoint/reset/replay and refusal rollback. Run and extend `test_heli_impact_detector.gd`, `test_heli_scenery_response.gd`, `test_helicopter_lake_context.gd`, `test_heli_rotor_domain.gd`, `test_heli_ground_heading.gd` and applicable kernel/fault/replay tests; qualify the *actual* selected native and reference routes. Retain explicit helicopter source/engine identity, accepted-state tape and checkpoint replay plus rollback. The production helicopter records a `rotorcraft_recorder.gd` CSV trace (`helicopter_main.gd:181`); fixed-wing ORFL cannot replay helicopters, as [HELI-33](../helicopter/HELI-33/README.md) states. Changes under physics/sim also affect the shared FlightLog source hash (`app/sim/flight_log.gd:420–449`), but `solver_changed` applies to actual ORFL consumers and is not a claimed helicopter recording field. Distinct from F18's accepted-tick presentation derivations and F23's quad substeps/transaction cost.

## U06. Lake-clearance stages rebuild fixed helicopter body groups near the lake

**Path and guard.** The field publishes an optional normalized lake to the helicopter session (`app/helicopter_main.gd:166-169`; `app/sim/helicopter_flight_session.gd:445-462`); the field resolver generally enables it on the matching default field unless switched off or scenery excludes it (`app/render/lake/lake_world.gd:10-23`). A nonempty lake prevents native helicopter stage installation (`helicopter_flight_session.gd:218-225`), so lake flights use the reference stage, not the requested native path. Each reference `_stage` checks `_water_contact_possible(body, body)` before dynamics (`:774-784`), and the successful policy checks the full swept candidate after `StepAdapter.step` (`:299-309`). With one regular RK4 piece and no refusal, that is four stage checks plus one candidate check; rotor-stop event search/retries can change the count, and any early refusal shortens it (`app/sim/simulation.gd:474-484,518-541,565-573`; `app/physics/rotorcraft/simulation_step_adapter.gd:20-43,69-86,95-123`).

`_water_contact_possible` returns immediately for no lake and has vertical and shoreline-AABB clear-only returns (`helicopter_flight_session.gd:470-485`). Only when both gates admit a possible near-water approach does it assemble a dictionary grouping all 56 immutable-by-convention prepared probes by `part`, then recompute each visited group's three-axis bounds, center and radius (`:486-510`). A no-hit query visits all four stock part groups; a hit can return earlier during group bounds checks, but grouping itself has already traversed all 56. It then tests dynamic pose, rotation and the actual shoreline (`:513-535`). The source has concrete *admitted* near-bank and dry-inside-shore-AABB witnesses (`app/tests/test_helicopter_lake_context.gd:71-87`), so this is not limited to water-contact ticks that immediately refuse. The lake validity call precedes the broad-phase gates, but a successful one-entry deep-value cache exists (`app/physics/lake_surface.gd:268-293`); do not claim every call does full uncached definition validation.

**Possible experiment.** Measure calls passing the vertical/AABB gates and time the grouping separately on the default lake, its dry inner corner, normal high flight, no-lake field and near-bank motion. If worthwhile, prepare exact per-part group geometry once, or batch its validation and grouping natively, while applying every *current-state-dependent* sweep and shoreline check at each original stage and endpoint. The prepared dictionary is reachable and mutation-sensitive, and `_water_contact_possible` runs before U05's detector validation, so a cache must preserve current same-tick mutation/refusal and error ordering, not merely key by a lake or aircraft identity string. In particular, the existing detector only checks that `probe.part` is a String: changing a valid part label can change grouping while still passing that validator. Detect/recompute for valid in-place label/point changes as well as malformed data. A native validator must preserve numeric equality and `is_equal_approx` semantics rather than silently replace them with byte equality. Rebuild on setup/configuration and reset/restore where necessary; no cross-session cache without ownership proof. Do not change arithmetic grouping, conservative margins, false-positive boundaries, stop-event refinement, water refusal or transaction semantics.

**Validation.** Compare the original and candidate results over the stock bank witness and dry AABB corner, a long swept path, both sides of shoreline, high overflight, water entry, near-threshold rotation, degenerate and malformed/mutated geometry, changed lake, replacement, reset, checkpoint and later-stage fault. Use `test_helicopter_lake_context.gd`, `test_heli_impact_detector.gd`, `test_heli_rotor_domain.gd` and step/refusal/replay checks. Confirm actual lake-induced reference selection and benchmark full callbacks on target hardware; this near-water opportunity is not a universal 240 Hz 56-probe claim. It is distinct from U05's general prepared-impact validation and F18's visual work.

## U07. The race wrapper rebuilds its held plant context inside every RK stage

**Path and work.** The ION R5 activity normally selects a practice track when that track matches the loaded field (`app/vehicles/multirotor/multirotor_scene.gd:45-52,82-95,201-228,332-359,417-422`). A matching prepared session installs `RaceModel.wrap`; free flight on an unmatched/empty course installs the plant directly (`app/sim/multirotor/session.gd:281-314`). Therefore the wrapper also runs in its IDLE phase before a race, not only after starting one. Each process-physics callback samples input; unpaused successful work calls `session.tick_once`, which installs the input and steps the shared kernel (`multirotor_scene.gd:1130-1166`; `session.gd:640-675`). The kernel holds one sampled aux/mode context through the tick (`app/sim/simulation.gd:453-467,1182-1184`) and deliberately detaches and checks each stage context (`:1208-1237`).

On every wrapped stage, `RaceModel._stage` takes a fresh 31-float plant aux slice and 8-integer modes slice and constructs a plant context dictionary before dispatching the inner plant; in READY/COUNTDOWN it zeroes the four commands in the aux copy (`app/sim/race/race_model.gd:28-31,118-139,282-290`). A completed ordinary one-piece RK4 tick has four such stages, or 4n for n completed contact pieces (`simulation.gd:474-484,518-541,575-579,843-850`). At 240 accepted one-piece ticks/s this amounts to 1,920 wrapper slice calls and 960 temporary context dictionaries per second; the slices contain 299,520 logical payload bytes/s before overhead, not a measured allocated RSS figure or proof that every attempted tick completes. Contact refinement changes n and is already F23; the separate lead here is per-stage *wrapper projection*, without changing substeps. Paused callbacks sample input but return before `tick_once` (`multirotor_scene.gd:1133-1157`).

**Possible experiment.** After measuring stage/whole-tick time and allocations, add a narrowly specialized production-controlled-plant stage entry that can read the wrapper's already detached held context directly for ordinary phases; retain fresh command masking in READY/COUNTDOWN and the existing generic sliced path for arbitrary/replaced plant callbacks. Check the live inner `plant_model.stage_sampled` Callable identity, not only the outer race wrapper: today mutation of the inner slice is isolated, whereas passing through the wrapper context can turn it into an outer detached-context rejection. The current inner plant only indexes the plant prefix (`app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:358-380,384-410`), but its public callback presently receives plant-sized arrays; passing longer arrays to every possible injected callback would change the contract. Avoid a cache keyed only by tick/time, which can collide after reset/restore. Do not remove the kernel's detach, stage guard or mutation/reentry checks. A native combined pure-stage implementation is a larger candidate only after profiling, with a real selected/admitted quad native route and parity; engine-provided slice is already native, while the wrapper and plant control flow are GDScript.

**Validation.** Compare sampled contexts, plant result and final frame bitwise where the existing contract requires it across IDLE/READY/COUNTDOWN/RACING/abort, arming/disarming/turtle, normal and contact substeps, reset/restore and races with crash/bounce. READY/COUNTDOWN zeros duty commands but leaves the rest of motor/bus mode semantics intact; do not equate it to unconditional disarm. Exercise custom/replaced stage callbacks, mutation, reentry and refused stages (`app/tests/test_race_model.gd:294-320`, `test_race_session.gd`, `test_mr_activity_race.gd`, `test_mr_fast_paths.gd`), plus same-source replay, candidate source identity and transaction/rollback gates. This does not reuse an RK result for telemetry and is separate from T01's bus-only telemetry, T06's obstacle-index construction, and S09's rotor attach.

## U08. Analog-refresh and Off quad HUD frames compute flight display values that tracking does not consume

**Path and guard.** Every multirotor render process calls `hud.update` with drawn/interpolated body and accepted body (`app/vehicles/multirotor/multirotor_scene.gd:1185-1216`). `MultirotorHud.update` samples G-force, computes `Osd.flight_values` on the drawn body, calls `_track_flight`, updates the race card, *then* may return until the next analog OSD refresh (`app/vehicles/multirotor/multirotor_hud.gd:1054-1085`). `flight_values` slices quaternion and velocity, rotates velocity, derives pitch/roll with trigonometry and independently computes heading (`app/vehicles/multirotor/osd_model.gd:160-187`); `_track_flight` only uses `flight.north/east` to update home, distance and post-flight statistics (`multirotor_hud.gd:1209-1239`; `osd_model.gd:398-433`). Thus analog intermediate frames do those full display derivations even though no display values are refreshed on that call. In HD Off layout, `_track_flight` and warnings still matter, but those values are not needed by the visible flight layout. They are still consumed internally: the current Off refresh computes `home_info` with the heading (`multirotor_hud.gd:1121–1123`) and stores `flight`/`home` in observable `_draw_state` (`:1180–1191`; `test_mr_osd.gd:257–270`). A candidate must guard/replace the dependent work and explicitly preserve or revise that observable state contract, then force a fresh projection before reveal. Do not describe Off values as currently unconsumed.

Analog and Off are optional settings: the shipped default is HD Freestyle (`osd_model.gd:31-50`), whose visible display consumes these values. Frames can continue while paused. The G sampler already coalesces the same accepted tick (`:316-343`); do not claim it recomputes per paused frame or skip it merely because the layout is Off. Existing draw work is already separated and cached by layer/heading/tape; see `multirotor_hud.gd:1268-1295,1344-1379`. This is CPU-side value derivation and should not be presented as an extra draw call or GPU pass.

**Possible experiment and checks.** Split tracking's drawn-position extraction from full display projection, preserving the current short-body/invalid fallback. Perform full derivation only when an analog refresh is due or a current visible element consumes it; preserve per-render interpolated positions used for path integration, every observed accepted-tick G sample, arm/home transitions, race card and timers, warnings, and the existing analog presentation cadence. Force/produce current values when layouts, style, menu or views change: `_apply_config` resets the refresh deadline and forces redraw (`:621-636`), so stale `_draw_state` on the first reveal is a real risk. Compare current/changed frame outputs and statistics under analog/HD, Off/Race/Freestyle/Full/custom, pause/resume, arm/disarm, race countdown/result, battery warning, restart, and view/layout/style changes, including differing render/tick cadences. Extend `app/tests/test_mr_osd.gd` (notably Off and warning checks at `:302-308`), `test_mr_activity.gd` and `test_mr_activity_race.gd`; time complete HUD/update and rendering on target hardware. A small built-in/GDScript display projection preserves render-boundary ownership; native implementation is only warranted after attribution. This is separate from T01: its electric bus/pack telemetry, safety warnings and statistics must continue.

## Small negative/low-priority coverage

- `Allocator.allocate` computes a four-residual objective when producing its result (`app/physics/multirotor/allocator.gd:376-390`) although the armed flight controller reads only `ok`, thrusts, and wrench (`app/physics/multirotor/flight_controller.gd:365-394`). The non-turtle armed branch makes one call per controller sample, but the saved loop is very small and the public objective is tested/observable. Do not remove the reference allocator's separate objectives at `allocator.gd:295-332`: they select the fallback answer. A private projection can be explored only if isolated profiling warrants the extra interface; this is not promoted as a substantial FPS lead.
- Helicopter `_on_stepped` does byte conversions to compare pending contact preflight with accepted body, continuous state, and shaft phase (`app/sim/helicopter_flight_session.gd:896-912`), up to six `to_byte_array` calls when all earlier comparisons pass. They enforce exact bytes, not ordinary float equality. A native exact-byte comparator is a possible microbenchmark, but the arrays are short and callback/conversion overhead may exceed savings; preserve signed zero and exact rejection before considering replacement.
- No hidden quad telemetry evaluation per every 240 Hz tick is implied: `_current_telemetry` coalesces by accepted tick (`multirotor_scene.gd:1249-1256`). Do not retell T01 as an uncached per-tick duplicate. Helicopter hidden HUD/rotor-envelope presentation, quad contact substeps, three obstacle-index constructions and four rotor-profile attach attempts are already F18, F23, T06 and S09 respectively.
