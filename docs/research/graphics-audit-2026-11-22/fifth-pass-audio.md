## V07 — Propagation scans history and runs 40 refinements when it finds a bracket

**Entry and guard.** Fixed-wing ordinary interactive startup without scripted/capture mode creates `FlightAudio` when `--soundscape` is `on` (`app/main.gd:1291-1295`); its `_process` calls `soundscape.update_sound(snapshot, delta)` (`app/audio/flight_audio.gd:440-454`). Helicopter separately attaches `HelicopterAudio` when soundscape is enabled and neither trace nor capture is present (`app/helicopter_main.gd:211-214`), then calls the same soundscape update from its process callback (`app/audio/helicopter_audio.gd:63-67`). Soundscape invokes `heard_snapshot` before either the recorded powertrain or the glow branch (`app/audio/soundscape.gd:379-386,423-443`). `propagation_delay` defaults true, but `--audio-delay=off`, a snapshot with no `time`, an empty/unconfigured voice array, or an absent adapter avoids this particular solve (`soundscape.gd:34,379-386,499-502`; `flight_audio.gd:60-65`; `helicopter_audio.gd:24-30`).

**Work and bounds.** `heard_snapshot` retains a 5.5-second accepted-time window, replacing the last sample at the same time, tracking an expired prefix, and periodically compacting; rewind or a forward gap greater than 0.5 s clears it (`soundscape.gd:25-33,499-527,579-584`). It then calls `Model.retarded_emission` (`:528-548`): that checks the newest position, walks up to `n-1` earlier positions for `n` logically retained samples, and, when a bracket is found, performs **exactly 40** midpoint/position-lerp/distance evaluations (`app/audio/soundscape_model.gd:17-42`). An all-newest result or a history too short to bracket avoids the 40 refinements. The time window is not a universal hard sample-count cap for arbitrary API inputs. In ordinary 240 Hz flight, `Simulation.time()` is `tick * dt()` (`app/sim/simulation.gd:361-366`; `app/project.godot:51`), and only distinct accepted times observed by the presentation callback append. Thus an ideal 5.5-second window contains roughly 1,320 unique tick positions plus current/predecessor, often fewer at render rates below 240 Hz; repeated frames at a held tick replace rather than append. At a static 343 m and sound speed 343 m/s, a steady 60-observation/s history needs about 60 older-position checks to find the approximately one-second bracket, plus the fixed 40 refinements; an approximately 144-observation/s history needs about 144 plus 40. These are illustrative source-iteration counts, not speed measurements, allocations, or guaranteed camera scenarios.

**Why this extends, rather than replaces, earlier work.** AUD-11 already changed repeated history slicing to a logical retained prefix, then stopped scanning the expired prefix. It preserved newest-first order and the existing refinement; `docs/research/audio/AUD-11/README.md:16-34,89-93` reports an old 144 Hz/1,000 m isolated Windows beta1/Dummy comparison of **26.28 → 26.36 µs/call**, and a 2,500 m comparison of **56.17 → 39.34 µs/call**. The latter is outside the documented normal 1,500 m acoustic range, and neither number measures current source, a complete soundscape callback, real mixing, or FPS. `soundscape.gd:272-275` disables engine max-distance attenuation for an installed profile in favor of the manual gain law (`powertrain_audio.gd:100-101`), so do not infer that an off-range source skips this solve.

**Smallest discriminating next experiment.** Time and count the current exact-source solver alone, `heard_snapshot` including snapshot/history maintenance, and complete `FlightAudio._process` / helicopter audio callback, with matched near, approximately 343 m, 1,000 m and 1,500 m positions, 30/60/144 Hz and steady/slow/paused frames. Record the frame interval and distance histograms rather than extrapolating the historical far case. If material, prototype a single native Godot/GDExtension batch for the existing packed times/positions and 40-step loop, including method dispatch, packed-array access/copies, dictionary/result construction and the complete caller in the measurement; retain the GDScript reference/fallback. An indexed search is permissible only under a proven and maintained monotonicity/identity invariant with the exact old newest-crossing fallback: position teleports and malformed, duplicate, irregular and nonfinite times can produce multiple crossings. A blanket binary search, fewer bisections or an analytic approximation is not an equivalent change.

**Contract and checks.** Preserve current `Vector3`/float precision and operation order, newest matching crossing, `index/alpha/delay/truncated`, prefix clamp for mismatched arrays, discrete nearest-sample choice, current listener/wind versus delayed source fields, same-tick replacement, camera cuts, gap, pause, reset, restore, and source/scene identity. `app/tests/test_soundscape.gd:306-435,437-526,528-593` covers legacy live-history equivalence, bytes including signed zero/nonfinites, multiple crossings, short/empty/mismatched arrays, runtime direction and reset. Extend exact index/alpha/delay and full heard-snapshot comparisons to a candidate plus real variable-cadence flyby and forced malformed inputs. Obtain admitted Windows/Linux physical-audio/GPU runs at 1080p and Apple Silicon native Retina, recording actual backend rather than requested backend, renderer, frame CPU/p95/p99, mixer skips/capture and source hashes; headless parity alone is not device or FPS qualification.

## V08 — Default fixed-wing mechanical audio re-reads and re-normalizes the same accepted actuator endpoint

**Entry.** Fixed-wing `FlightAudio.configure` creates mechanical audio and installs accepted observation (`app/audio/flight_audio.gd:56-78,88-105`). On consecutive ticks the existing default `PresentationCadence` defers observations until `take()` once per render frame (`app/render/presentation_cadence.gd:10-16,56-74`; `flight_audio.gd:146-166`). A tick gap, force, cadence 0, pause notifications, and the 0.1-second interval bound can change that count. When `_process` drains a pending endpoint, `_observe_mechanical_endpoint` calls `accepted_mechanical_snapshot()` and `observe_accepted()` (`flight_audio.gd:161-166,176-184,440-444`). For an active valid session the endpoint reads `surface_angles_deg()` and `gear_state()`, building three aileron/elevator/rudder rows and one flap row if available (`:204-218`). Later in the same callback, `mechanical_snapshot()` independently calls those same two accessors again, reads up to those same four angle values, and adds current rendered hinge positions, listener, scene state, gear position, and a smoothed engine mask (`:398-437,464-467`; accessors `app/sim/flight_session.gd:4351-4366,4485-4495`). Thus a normal frame with a newly drained active endpoint makes **two calls to each accessor** along this audio path. The two values describe the same accepted endpoint only if the session and its relevant mutable inputs have not changed across the intervening soundscape/ground updates. A frame with no pending endpoint makes only the latter; other callers elsewhere can add calls.

**A distinct second boundary.** The accepted observer first normalizes and classifies its rows with `Motion.observe` (`app/audio/mechanical_audio.gd:95-142`; `app/audio/mechanical_motion.gd:14-77`). On a valid matching tick, render-time `_accepted_result` invokes `Motion.observe` again and then drains accepted events (`mechanical_audio.gd:159-182,314-323`). Its same-tick case re-reads/validates rows and gear and refreshes spatial/status data while preserving prior accepted travel (`mechanical_motion.gd:22-35,140-167,170-213`). The general public normalizer accepts **up to six** unique valid channels; the built-in fixed-wing adapter authors **three or four**, and gear is independently optional. This second normalization is bypassed by the current unpaused all-sources-distant early return at 60 m, although `mechanical_snapshot()` and its accessor calls still precede that guard (`mechanical_audio.gd:288-300,421-436`). These are source-level repeated calls/row passes, not proof that all rows allocate physically or that reuse matters to FPS.

**Counterexamples and history.** A cached endpoint is not a replacement for current rendered positions: camera and hinge transforms can move between ticks. Same-tick refresh also validates row IDs/finite values and updates gear status; unknown rows prime stationary. Above one-frame cadence the displayed tick may deliberately be the last observed tick while newer simulation values exist (`flight_audio.gd:421-423`). Paused and invalid values, stale-tick reset, distance cull, dynamic session replacement, reset, detach/reattach, and event overflow/discard are observable. The accepted transition queue is capped at **64** with ordered oldest-event eviction, and motion/event pools are separately limited to 6 and 2 (`mechanical_audio.gd:8-12,92-142`). `docs/research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-01c/tick-observers-2026-10-27/README.md:1-22` previously profiled mechanical accepted-tick work at **1.50 ms per approximately 11-tick profiler sample** on a shared Linux VM; that was an older per-tick design with profiler overhead, not a current per-frame saving or native-hardware result. The already-shipped cadence and far cull are counterevidence to a blanket 240 Hz/unbounded-work claim.

**Smallest discriminating experiment.** Instrument same-callback identity/tick, accessor counts, `accepted_mechanical_snapshot`, `Motion.observe` normalization, `mechanical_snapshot`, and both complete callback and `last_mechanical_update_usec` separately (the latter starts after `_observe_due_endpoints` and does not include accepted-endpoint collection) for default Stik and flap/retract-capable P-51/F4U, near/far listener, moving/stationary controls, 30/60/144 presentation FPS, cadence 0/1/2 and pause/resume. On a disposable candidate, compare an immutable same-endpoint value projection shared only where session, tick, input/configuration generation and validity still match; still refresh render anchors and gear presentation and keep the original dynamic/refusal path. If a compact typed/engine projection wins, compare its whole boundary; a native wrapper around these two small GDScript getter bodies may lose to marshalling and is not presumed faster. Do not move observations or change cadence to manufacture a saving.

**Validation.** Compare exact accepted event order/counters, emitted/evicted/dropped events, values and source positions, envelopes/pitch, invalid inputs and diagnostics for control start/reverse/stop, gear lock, dynamic row changes, distance cross, paused manual step, stale tick, overflow, rewind, replacement and reattach. Existing seams: `app/tests/test_flight_audio_accepted.gd:95-172,240-264`, `app/tests/test_mechanical_audio.gd:36-78,212-233,310-329,389-449`, `app/tests/test_presentation_cadence.gd:57-95,280-306`. Preserve complete simulation checkpoint identity. Measure complete callback and presented-frame tails on physical target platforms, with actual native physics selection and audio device confirmed.

## Existing AUD/DT-MAC continuation — glow worker copies a snapshot even on a full-queue poll; fallback is synchronous only when thread creation fails

**Actual selection.** The soundscape selects a recorded `PowertrainAudio` bank when `configure` admits at least one layer and discards its glow player (`app/audio/soundscape.gd:130-151,180-200`; `app/audio/powertrain_audio.gd:47-84`). The committed `app/assets/audio/powertrains/BANKS.json` provides gasoline, electric, turbine, and rotor families, but no glow family; when a glow family has no admitted bank, the glow generator remains available; the update selects it when the incoming/default glow family matches (`soundscape.gd:423-443,587-626`). Partial/invalid/missing banks, asset diagnostics, and fallback are observable, and a requested family does not alone prove actual selected playback.

**Existing, explicitly tracked residual.** This exact lazy coherent snapshot/revision candidate already appears in [the Apple Silicon plan](../../plans/apple-silicon-performance.md), DT-MAC-04b/08 + AUD external 03/07/09/10 (source lines 853–869). It is retained for continuity and is not a fifth-pass discovery. The plan also explicitly refuses to presume identical old scheduling or PCM under every interleaving.

**Source work.** The worker locks `_mutex` and performs `_latest_snapshot.duplicate(true)` **before** it queries generator availability (`app/audio/glow_audio_pump.gd:162-196`). Therefore a poll that finds its queue already at target still deep-duplicates telemetry and updates queue/skips status before a 2,000 µs requested sleep. A render iteration additionally calls `snapshot_for_render`, which deep-duplicates the already detached snapshot again to handle per-revision physical anchor keys (`:42-54,198-205`); GDScript-to-engine `AudioServer.lock/get_frames_available/get_skips/unlock` occurs on the poll, and another lock/availability/push occurs for a rendered chunk (`:179-184,207-222`). The main-frame publisher also creates filtered telemetry and deep-copies `source_vibration` when present (`:91-102,265-274`). This is a source-level duplicate-call and synchronization observation, not a claim about physical copy bytes, actual contention, scheduler wakeup rate, underruns or FPS. The intended full-queue idle sleep is 2 ms (an idealized ~500 sleeps/s if continuously full and ignoring overhead/scheduling); active below-target refill iterations can execute without that sleep, so 500 is **not** a global iteration-rate cap.

**Queue and fallback bounds.** At the production 22,050 Hz (`app/audio/glow_voice.gd:5`; `soundscape.gd:211-214,614-620`) the target `floor(0.05 * rate)` is **1,102 stereo frames**, while the configured generator `buffer_length=0.15` is capacity configuration, not the measured queue depth or latency. Capacity is read from playback and target is clamped to it. Each rendered chunk is at most **256 frames**; an idle empty queue with capacity at least target is filled in four 256-frame chunks and one 78-frame chunk, assuming no concurrent consumption. Fragmented availability may create more, smaller chunks. A successful thread start keeps synthesis on the worker, outside its audio-server critical sections; it is not the same as S01's synchronous Nitro car voice. If `Thread.start` fails, `_fallback_refill` runs on the caller during `start` and subsequent `update_telemetry` (`glow_audio_pump.gd:57-88,91-102,297-344`): at most 1,102 frames are rendered per fallback update, in individually bounded chunks. A 50 ms producer target is a capacity/latency policy, not itself a defect or an instruction to reduce sample quality.

**Prior evidence and negative controls.** The old `docs/research/audio/AUD-12/README.md:69` 2,043 µs/frame glow figure measured an earlier **main-thread** implementation on a contended llvmpipe VM; applying it to the current worker or as a current FPS gain would be incorrect. AUD-15 replaced that route: `docs/research/audio/AUD-15/validation.md:48-50,66-81` records the pre-pump skip failures and a later separate 125 s native-mixer pump stress with five 600 ms main-thread stalls, zero skips, zero worker errors and zero discarded PCM frames; it explicitly does not qualify complete-flight performance. A later long capture still had a strict WAV/wall-time mismatch despite zero glow skips (`:95-105`). Current `glow_voice.gd:255-259` also records 5,760 historical bit-exact block comparisons after hoisting per-block invariants. Neither result measures this poll/copy cost on the current source.

**Smallest discriminating experiment.** With the actual family/bank and `stats().fallback` logged, separately count/time worker polls, full-queue polls, snapshot copies, block rendering, both AudioServer lock waits/hold times, skip deltas, and main-thread callback in a current source-pinned glow flight on a native audio driver. Compare steady/low RPM, silent but present/far, rapidly changing accepted telemetry, paused, slow frame and injected main-thread-stall cases, while recording actual device buffer period and queue targets. First test whether full-queue snapshot cloning is measurable, and if so prototype an immutable, privately owned revision snapshot that preserves the **same snapshot/revision cut point** and existing fallback/reference semantics. Simply moving the capture after the queue check changes which accepted revision wins in a race. A native block producer is a separate candidate only if whole-worker time including dictionary/packed-buffer conversion, locks, `push_buffer`, and target OS extension load wins after prior hoisting. A test seam forcing failed thread start can separately characterize the rare synchronous fallback; do not generalize it to normal operation.

**Contract and checks.** Preserve exact PCM sample order and block splits, queue lead, source-vibration validation, phase/firing fraction and accepted physical anchor once per revision (`snapshot_for_render` strips anchors on repeats), skips, priming, failures, player pause/restart, stop-before-join/resource release, scene detach/reattach, and reset. `app/tests/test_glow_audio_pump.gd:21-63,66-105,114-196` tests revision continuity, queue target, stall, fallback reporting, real-driver skip gates and lifecycle, but explicitly marks dedicated-worker stall qualification N/A if fallback selected. `app/tests/test_glow_audio.gd` and the existing `tools/audio` native captures supplement it. Run matched production-path tests on each required physical Windows/Linux/macOS audio driver with actual backend/device selected; quantify both component time and real presented-frame pacing, and retain source and stream identities. Preserve 240 Hz simulation/checkpoint bytes.

## Other paths screened

Quad motor/rattle audio uses Godot-recorded streams and pauses both when silent (`app/vehicles/multirotor/multirotor_scene.gd:1279-1305,1310-1352`); the airship similarly uses two shared-stream players and pauses individual motors (`app/vehicles/airship/airship_scene.gd:752-777,798-802,822-830`). Neither warrants inventing a per-sample GDScript synthesis claim. Storm has an admitted-event pending queue capped at **12**, up to **3** players and a 256-entry seen set before its reset (`app/audio/storm_audio.gd:4-11,70-110,113-140,157-179`); its input event array is caller-supplied, so 12 is not a universal bound on the event-ingestion loop. Wildlife owns three slot timers but refuses a new event when **two** are occupied; it tracks four species (`app/audio/wildlife.gd:4-10,47-72`). These small, guarded paths are not promoted as measured bottlenecks. Helicopter skid contact, fixed-wing duplicate wind/air queries, master mute work, selected Nitro PCM, and helicopter/airship accepted presentation were intentionally left to existing F15/N4/F12/S01/F18 rather than relabeled.
