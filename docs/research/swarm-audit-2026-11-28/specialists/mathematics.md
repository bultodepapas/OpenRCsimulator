## A real box entry can disappear inside the rotational padding

The body probe `(0,0,1)` lies on the yaw axis. Yaw leaves its offset unchanged at every instant, including under the production attitude interpolation. Uniform translation therefore moves this point on a straight line. A 10 mm step starting 20 µm outside a flat face enters at fraction `0.002`. At 1.5° yaw the 1 m hull radius gives `1 − cos(0.75°) + 10⁻⁹ = 0.000085673426 m` of padding. The padded box already contains the probe, deeper than the `10⁻¹⁰ m` face tolerance, so the inside branch drops the entire segment. The no-yaw control detects entry. The 1 nm fixed padding accounts for its returned fraction of `0.0019999`.

In the two-tick witness, the join clearance is 84.5 µm. It exceeds the first tick’s 83.4041 µm padding, then falls inside the second tick’s 85.6734 µm padding. The first tick misses both surfaces, as it should. The second crosses the actual face at fraction `0.00845` and returns zero contacts. The states share the join pose, velocity and angular rate. The 11-case reference-engine receipt includes mirrored faces, a 37° shed proxy and controls. This confirms a missed first entry for a synthetic single-probe query. Frequency and consequences during stock flight remain unmeasured. See `app/sim/scenery_collision.gd` lines 133–159, 470–494 and 696–699.

## A capsule can report an event on a hidden internal cap

The production cylinder and both full endpoint spheres contribute roots to a single list. The sphere routine checks the approach direction but omits the axial test for the exposed hemisphere. At the selected root the code constructs a normal using the nearest point on the capsule axis segment. An internal sphere root can therefore pass the inward radial-speed check and become a hard-terminal event. See `app/sim/scenery_collision.gd` lines 243–250 and 548–581.

For a unit-radius capsule centered at the origin with axial half-length 2 m, move a center probe from `(0.81,0,1.39)` to `(0.80,0,1.41)` m. Throughout the tick its axial coordinate is between the cap centers. Its signed surface distance decreases monotonically from −0.19 to −0.20 m. Yet it enters the sphere centered at `(0,0,2)` on the hemisphere buried inside the cylinder. The root-run pinned-engine probe returned a terminal event at fraction `0.7006265212`, at signed distance `−0.1970062652 m`, with radial approach speed `2.4 m/s`, matching the analytic prediction. The 1 nm numerical padding affects only the last reported decimals. The mirrored negative end has the same result. The independent distance is `sqrt(r² + max(|z| − 2,0)²) − 1`.

This witness starts inside the obstacle. A low-priority repair can retain the outer cylinder and hemisphere roots by checking the axial coordinate of each cap candidate before accepting it. Regression cases should include the cap/cylinder join, both exterior tips, a true side entry and an already-overlapping interior segment. The separate historical CR-AUD-23 witness concerns a missed *external* sub-micrometre step. It demonstrates that earlier removal of a real entry can leave a session inside a capsule, but it did not exercise the hidden-sphere case. This review establishes no frequency or stock-flight consequence for the internal seam. All six `capsule_seam.gd` cases matched the expected signature. The two hidden-cap contacts reproduced. The interior mid-cylinder and exterior-clear controls returned zero, while genuine side and tip exterior entries both returned fractions within 5.1 × 10⁻⁸ of 0.5. ## A negative wake length changes decay into exponential growth

For a held source \(w\), the reference pre-step evolves the residual \(e=x-w\) as \(e_{k+1}=e_k\exp[-\Delta t V/L]\), where \(L=x_h-S/(4b)\) is in metres. The exponent is dimensionless. At positive airspeed a positive length contracts the residual, while a negative length amplifies it. In the one-field raw Stik edit the engine admitted and trimmed `L = −0.0761999672 m`. At 15 m/s its measured multiplier was `2.270977435`, versus `0.921519451` for stock. Under ordinary elevator input the custom lag-minus-wing-source residual reached `−1.24889309542 × 10¹¹ m/s` on the 40th committed tick (`1/6 s`) with no reported fault. That number describes an internal equivalent-lift-velocity proxy. The stock comparator reached `−0.599317 m/s`. This instability occurs in an exact sampled-lag update before the RK stages.

All 16 direct aircraft records declaring a gradient have positive derived lengths, ranging from `0.6072420273` to `1.4229946204 m`. The Creator checks conventional tail arms before generating its model. The demonstrated path is a directly loaded custom record on GDScript reference. Source agreement in native does not provide an executed native witness. Reject nonpositive lengths before installing the applicable model and test zero, negative and healthy inputs. The zero case follows from the undefined division and was not part of the recorded maneuver. ## The tabulated axial-power check passes on its stated interval

For positive shaft rotation and forward axial inflow, \(P_{shaft}-TU=\rho n^3D^5(C_P-JC_T)\). This is one necessary energy condition. On every overlap of one authored thrust segment and one power segment, the bracket is quadratic. The independent `verify_tables.py` reads each decimal exactly as a rational number and checks endpoints plus the interior minimum of every convex quadratic. `exact-tables.json` contains the complete results.

The 10 direct files comprise six catalog base paths and four synthetic Stik fixtures, with seven distinct table pairs. All have strictly positive minima on the overlapping authored forward-advance intervals. The smallest is `9929811/2426000000 ≈ 0.004093079555`, on the F4U at `J = 23643/24260 ≈ 0.974567189`. Extrapolated coefficients, uncoupled portions of a table's domain, oblique-force work, propeller-map fidelity and blade-element glow installations lie outside this result. The custom electrical power-bound witness and the Phoenix static figure-of-merit scale already belong to A36 R6-G01 and A36 F6 respectively. The correct static factor is \(\sqrt{2/\pi}\,C_T^{3/2}/C_P\), giving the recorded erroneous ratio of \(\pi/2\).
