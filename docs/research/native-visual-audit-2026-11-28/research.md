# Art direction and rendering research for an RC flying field

28 November 2026. Source baseline `71306784a250d634854f0c8bab3a927cc46a61b8`.

A pilot needs to see the aircraft’s bank as it crosses the treeline, judge its height on final and recognize wheel contact with the grass. Use those three moments to judge a field. The aircraft can be only a few pixels deep while the sky and field occupy almost the whole frame. A richer foreground earns its place when those cues survive motion. The project’s historical Stik clips give reproducible poses at 100 m, but their backgrounds and human orientation scores remain unqualified. ## Research applied in the candidate

The ten changes below run in existing project-owned GPU shaders. The evidence establishes a source-level cause or numerical invariant for each change. A GPU component test can exercise those equations; the complete field, pilot response and device frame time require separate evidence.

| Finding | Applied change and discriminating test | Technical references |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N1 · Lake absorption | Convert axial view depth to a straight-ray water path before applying the 12 m cap. Orthographic rays retain unity correction. At 1 m axial separation, 60° off axis and 0.46/m extinction, the transmitted fraction is 0.399 versus 0.631 on axis. The same **world segment** should retain its transmission as the camera pans. | [PBRT transmittance](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Volume_Scattering/Transmittance) · [Sousa on screen-space refraction](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems2/part-ii-shading-lighting-and-shadows/chapter-19-generic-refraction-simulation) |
| N2, ATM1 · Lens and rain | Square the signed lens-rim argument by multiplication. For the repeating rain stripe, sample at the wrapped coordinate using derivatives of the unwrapped coordinate. Check both sides of the lens profile and both rain-sheet seams during a pan. | [Khronos GLSL mathematical and texture functions](https://docs.vulkan.org/glsl/latest/chapters/builtinfunctions.html) |
| M1 · Roof normals | Test UV-frame conditioning relative to the derivative scale, handle collapsed geometry and preserve mirrored UV orientation. A valid roof tile covering 1080 pixels should keep its normal response as the camera approaches. | [Mikkelsen, surface-gradient bump mapping](https://jcgt.org/published/0009/03/04/paper.pdf) |
| M2, P1 · Lunar disc | Use two-dimensional disc-plane derivatives to choose texture detail. A symmetric four-pixel quad can have identical radii yet span about 65 lunar-map texels per screen pixel. The candidate’s filter is deliberately conservative. Zero lunar radiance can return before the derivative work. Check the limb, poles and quarter-pixel camera movements. | [PBRT texture coordinates and spherical differentials](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Textures_and_Materials/Texture_Coordinate_Generation) |
| PBR-01, PBR-02 · Countryside and windsock | Decode the baker’s authored sRGB vertex tints where the renderer expects linear RGB. Keep the photographs’ sampler conversion. | PBR-03 · Aserradero livery | Integrate the periodic wooden-board seam over the pixel footprint. A 1.1 mm seam across one 43 mm board occupies 2.56% for any sampling phase. ## Deferred decisions and the evidence that would change them

- **Shallow-water lighting and glints.** W2/L2 feeds the lit bed image into a diffuse surface, where it can receive illumination again. W3 clamps the GGX distribution and visibility terms to one even though a narrow distribution can legitimately peak above one. The corrections interact with exposure, fog and highlight clipping. Compare two known bed luminances under a surface-only light, then sweep a dim glint through the mirror angle on Compatibility and Forward+. Keep the art-directed reflection strength and optional planar roughness filtering (W4) separate. [Pinned refraction implementation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a19/scene/resources/material.cpp#L1804-L1817) · [PBRT microfacet normalization](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Reflection_Models/Roughness_Using_Microfacet_Theory) · [PBRT Fresnel reflection](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Reflection_Models/Specular_Reflection_and_Transmission)
- **Helicopter and field motion.** AVI1 applies the carbon blade tint twice in the HX-700 annulus. Compare both rotors at 0, 900 and 1,440 RPM before changing the mesh builder. AVI2 sends aircraft-position wind to grass and trees in fixed-wing flight. At a fixed clock and pilot view, move the aircraft between two heights in a shear layer; track the same grass patch, tree and mast windsock. A field-anchored wind sample needs a defined height and explicit consumers. The FAA wind-cone specification provides an equipment reference for the field cue. [NOAA wind shear definition](https://forecast.weather.gov/glossary.php?word=Shear) · [FAA wind-cone specification](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/150-5345-27F-Wind-Cone.pdf)
- **Sky and special finishes.** Ordinary cloud opacity deliberately fades near the horizon (ART-03). In cloudy moonlight the disc and perceived glare follow separate attenuation curves at different points in the display pipeline (ATM2). Chapa Noble and Vitral synthesize a cool reflected horizon, useful in previews without a sky probe (ART-02). Qualify them with a banked day/dusk comparison, a low cloud pan, and a fixed-exposure lunar occultation with glare on/off. For ART-01, present unlabeled flight clips and record bank direction and upright/inverted identification alongside the actual projected span and background. [Bruneton’s atmospheric reference comparisons](https://ebruneton.github.io/precomputed_atmospheric_scattering/index.html) · [WMO cloud lighting](https://cloudatlas.wmo.int/en/explanatory-remarks-and-special-clouds-cumulus.html)

## Comparing visual cost

Match output and internal resolution, camera, accepted simulation tick, material settings, driver and renderer. Review complete decoded images and feature-specific crops, then run alternating baseline/candidate trials on the named target GPU. Record wall-frame distributions, displayed intervals where available, pass timings with known query scope, and CPU and memory separately. A software renderer can verify coverage, color transfer and compile behavior. Its elapsed times describe that installation. No candidate adds a render pass, but newly eligible normal-mapped pixels can execute additional shading and sampling. The device trace decides the cost.
