# AFC-08 R1 — R2 runway-end geometry follow-up

**Date:** 2026-10-21. This report combines read-only R2 exit reconstruction with one isolated research arrival-target trial on the frozen D mirror. It does not alter R1/R2 evidence, production sources, or app physics. The [R2 matrix](../AFC-09-R2/README.md) remains the acceptance record: five of eight cases pass its full-clear gate, four pass both R1 and R2, and the three cases below fail the east/west end corridor. The original R1 results remain historical: east-north-crosswind passed the 22 gates; both south-crosswind cases timed out.

The machine-readable [verification index](verification.json) binds the trial specification, R2 baseline, flight and replay receipts, common-prefix comparison, clearance outputs, and research/runtime source hashes.

The checker [analyze_r2_corridor_geometry.py](../../../../research/ai-companions/sorties/analyze_r2_corridor_geometry.py) rebuilds all eight crash-hull points and three gear points using the loaded half-fuel CG (2.878662138 kg; LE `[0.109392437, 0, 0.000208376]` m). That transform reproduces each R2 minimum margin to better than `1e-9` m. Each exact replay completed with `solver_changed=false`. The raw R2 hashes and reports stay in [R2 verification.json](../AFC-09-R2/verification.json); the derived values are summarized in [geometry.json](geometry.json).

A first arc calculation used the rear-axle radius about the CG and overstated outward sweep. The corrected nominal arc starts at the loaded rear axle (`body x = −0.105607563 m`) and evaluates each footprint point relative to that axle. The earlier calculation and source hash are marked superseded in `geometry.json`; accepted-tick R2 margins and first-breach reconstructions did not change.

The corridor is `−50 ≤ east ≤ +50` m. R2 evaluates the transformed hull and gear points at accepted ticks; its minimum is not continuous collision certification. The taxi driver commands a north heading from yaw error and applies a capped throttle formula; it has no runway-end position plan. At taxi start, its steering demand is already saturated. The `cmd_yaw` trace includes the persistent `+0.01891866` yaw trim (`FlightSession.flown_commands` adds trims); `srv_yaw` is the actual normalized yaw-servo position. Ground contact uses that steering position times each contact's configured steering angle ([GroundContact](../../../../app/physics/ground_contact.gd#L72), [FlightSession](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L2774)). Thus the first two cases turn at about `−10.622°`, the west-south case at `+11.378°`, with a `0.351276` m wheelbase and `20°` maximum nose-wheel steering. This is measured control/servo response, not wind-specific compensation.

The nominal path integrates a no-slip, constant-steer arc about the loaded rear axle from each exact taxi-start yaw to north, checking the continuous east-coordinate extrema of every transformed hull and gear point. Its analytic extrema agree with an independent 100,001-angle sample check within `3.4e−11` m. It is a kinematic geometry bound only; it does not establish dynamic feasibility, terrain support, scenery clearance, or between-tick collision safety. For the two south starts, direct northbound taxi is nominally outside the end bounds even at full steering. A yaw-only increase is therefore not a supported fix. East-north's miss is a terminal wingtip overshoot after heading alignment, rather than a failure to turn.
