# Phoenix aircraft import audit, 2026-11-23

OpenRC `3cd6efa0596009ae28f5fd23822c655054b58f95`, Windows, Python 3.11.9. Inputs were the owner's Phoenix 5.5.k installation on D: and selected community releases. The [delivery plan](../../plans/phoenix-aircraft-import.md#72-priorities-after-the-installation-audit) owns the next steps.

The stock readers opened all 220 matched definitions and meshes. The Workshop admitted a start for 91 of the 114 aeroplanes and gliders tested. Seventeen more compiled but required more throttle to trim at the configured start. For handling, the clearest first experiment is to measure dual rates and servo response on the Apprentice. For community visuals, three conversions passed the glTF validator but stopped at the Workshop's embedded PNG checks. Earlier flight cards show why handling needs more than a speed target. The Habu 2 reached 36.3 m/s at full throttle yet had a high roll rate. ## Reproduction and scope

Use the [research tool guide](../../../research/aircraft-creator/phoenix/README.md) for the GEO census, Creator corpus and exact member selection. Run `phoenix_reference.py <installation> --out <new local JSON>` for the reference table. For the MOD census, call `inspect_luac.summarize` on each aircraft MOD and count the `modelTypes.*` assignments and diagnostics. For the GEO check count positive and negative dot products by each node's submesh vertex range, excluding zero. For a community member, verify the selected PKG SHA-256, inspect it, slice its verified inflated body at the reported member offset and length, and check the member SHA-256 before parsing. Keep original and converted aircraft files local.

To repeat the eight additional source-route checks on a checked Windows runner, copy the corpus harness to a disposable test script and insert these calls before its stock loop. Keep the installed Blade Nano CP X and DJI Phantom MODs in their original folders. For the Yak and Dominator, place each model's MOD, GEO, base paint, preview and any named canopy from its selected PKG in its own folder outside the installation. Keep shared installation files outside those two folders.

1. For each of the four MOD paths, call `Converter.convert(path)` and save `ok` or the first diagnostic.
