## Preregistration (2026-11-16, before the first flight)

Question: what does the frozen rule report on current inputs, and what differences remain
under fixed-rate repeats and further refinement? Current electric sessions select coupled
RK4; the historical general description of held rotor momentum is not a current-source
assumption. Compiler/definition source identities have also changed. This is not an exact
reproduction of the historical flights.

Reuse the unchanged campaign's cases, tapes, six-second duration, common 240 Hz sample
instants, metrics, tolerances, noise-floor branch and verdict calculation. A cache seam
makes that calculation consume the exact retained runs, without flying another comparison.
No package, solver, domain bound, tolerance, floor or flight-admission rule changes.

Fixed diagnostic cells:

| Case | Tape | Reason fixed before measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | pitch_doublet | Template control case |
| C02 | roll_doublet | Historical non-shrinking position difference |
| C02 | throttle_step | Historical non-shrinking rotor-speed difference |
| C04 | roll_doublet | Historical non-shrinking charge difference |
| C15 | roll_doublet | Largest historical angular-rate counterexample |

Each cell runs 240, 480, 960, 960, 1920, 1920 and 3840 Hz, in that order, from a fresh trimmed
Airborne session, calm weather, full rates and the explicitly selected GDScript reference
backend. A preliminary run may use only 240/480/960 to check the protocol, without substituting
for the registered calibration. Record actual backend, shaft scheme, timestep, source/engine,
compiled record/definition, environment, initial physical frame and command-edge identities.

Require exactly 1440 finite 17-value sample rows, one simulation-tick advance per call, the
requested fixed timestep and the expected common cadence. Reject partial prefixes, extra
rows, wrong layouts and nonfinite derived differences. Retain row-major little-endian
float64 samples and their hashes. Retain the initial body, continuous, auxiliary, mode and
input frame bit patterns and compare them across rates; different initial frames or
environments make a comparison uninterpretable as step convergence.

Record each 240 Hz baseline as `.orfl`; the existing reader must report complete, exact
replay, with `solver_changed=false`. Preserve its CSV, replay report and original solver
inputs before considering any later physics candidate.

Report same-rate equality of sampled rows and final physical-frame digests separately from
refinement differences. Equal repeats establish determinism only for those observations;
higher-rate differences still combine truncation, roundoff, start and sampled-input effects.
Neither result independently proves a numerical noise floor or causally isolates servo or
downwash sampling. Do not automatically derive or adopt a new floor. The inherited verdict
is a labelled current-input diagnostic, never a G05 decision or physical validation.

Operational completion requires every requested flight and baseline replay to complete,
identities to remain stable and all artifacts to persist. Numerical failures of the frozen
rule are retained as measurements, not hidden as a successful flight qualification. A fault
or incomplete run stops that cell and returns failure; never silently rerun or drop it.
