# EX-06 implementation audit: Extra 300S flight physics

Status: static source/runtime audit complete; EX-06b generator/data corrections are implemented and `derive_physics.py --check` passes. Engine evidence records [17/17 installation checks](after/installation.log) and [12/12 existing handling checks](after/handling.log) passing. See the [combined results](../README.md) for flight, ground and regression qualification.

## Scope and traced model

This audit follows the current GPMA0236 Extra 300S .60 model after aircraft-data v2 standardization. The code graph was used first to locate the derivation, loader, and runtime consumers. Its source snippets lag the current checkout in several places, so behavior claims below were checked against current files.

The path is:

1. `research/extra-300/ex05/derive_physics.py` reads measured model geometry, the manual-derived setup values, and selected legacy Ugly Stik physics. It now solves virtual ballast with the tank empty at the manual's balance datum, adds the unchanged fixed half-tank fuel, and derives loaded mass properties. It also derives the actual propeller hub and CG-relative thrust-line offset from the visual thrust frame, and writes manual-source right/down thrust angles. The script upgrades the legacy-shaped record to v2 and writes `app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json` plus the EX-05 derivation report. The conversion is schema/provenance work; it does not validate the flight estimates.
2. `app/physics/aircraft_data.gd` validates v2 and derives mass, inventory CG, six-component inertia, control radians/rates, envelope transitions, and surface data (`aircraft_data.gd:261–295,1255–1276,1282–1329,1504–1542`). It rejects a declared `balance.plan_cg` that differs from inventory CG by more than `cg_tolerance`; that field does not move the aircraft CG. This follows the [aircraft contract](../../../AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md#physical-modules-and-parameter-effects).
3. `app/sim/flight_session.gd` assembles live loads through `Dynamics` and `Aero`, `Propulsion`, optional `Slipstream`, and `GroundContact` (`flight_session.gd:1039–1059,1105–1129,1408–1437`); servo positions are converted through `Commands` to aerodynamic deflections. Extra has no declared slipstream block.
4. `app/tests/test_extra_handling.gd` checks that the model's own trim, coefficients, envelope, and controls produce selected responses. Its header correctly calls these consistency checks, not aircraft validation.

Detailed manual evidence and provenance are recorded in [the EX-06 primary-source audit](primary-sources.md), including the manual's empty-tank balance condition, low/high rates, power-on takeoff guidance, and thrust-angle drawing.

### Balance-condition calculation

Before EX-06b, the generator balanced its fuel-loaded inventory to the manual's empty-tank target, yielding about 3.364 kg loaded mass and roughly 0.0873 kg virtual ballast. Removing the 0.146 kg fuel without changing ballast put the estimated dry CG about 6.5 mm aft of the manual datum (approximately 32.1% MAC versus 30%). The corrected generator solves the dry inventory first: 3.131 kg before balance, 0.1425 kg virtual ballast, then 3.273 kg dry at `x_aft=0.1088 m` (29.99% MAC). Adding the unchanged half-tank fuel gives about 3.419 kg at `x_aft=0.10266 m` (27.98% MAC), 55.1 g heavier and about 6.14 mm forward of the dry target. This loaded CG is derived from a dry manual constraint; it is not a manual wet-balance target.

Recomputed box-plus-parallel-axis inertia is `Jxx≈0.1667`, `Jyy≈0.3199`, and `Jzz≈0.4634 kg·m²`; the principal change is from the additional derived nose ballast. With current `CLmax` and area, the 1-g stall estimate is 10.42 m/s; with the neutral point held fixed, static margin at loaded CG is 14.48% MAC. The generator report is authoritative for these rounded outputs; percentage differences in earlier audit arithmetic came from rounded MAC-reference inputs. All values are estimates from the component inventory and geometry, not aircraft measurements. The manual allows ±9.525 mm around its dry first-flight balance point, but the derived half-tank state must not be presented as a measured balance or ballast recommendation.

## Implemented EX-06b slice and acceptance evidence

The selected balance and installation slice is implemented in EX-06b: solve virtual ballast against the complete **dry** model inventory at the manual's empty-tank p43 station, then re-add the existing fixed half-tank fuel and derive the flight mass, loaded CG, and inertia; enable plan-derived thrust angles and derive the actual visual propeller hub offset. No fuel burn or slosh was added. The manual states the dry condition explicitly, and the v2 contract/runtime derive loaded mass properties and enforce their consistency.

`balance.plan_cg` is the **derived loaded CG** from the final inventory, with a source string that identifies the manual's dry balance target and the fixed half-tank addition. It must not be the manual dry target, because the v2 loader compares it to loaded inventory. Ballast is derived from estimated component masses/locations; the loaded CG is derived from the dry manual constraint plus the fixed fuel placement. Thrust-angle source kind is manual plan transcription; actual hub and thrust-line offset are derived from geometry and loaded CG.

Acceptance evidence and remaining limits:

- Generator order is dry inventory → dry manual-target ballast → fixed half-tank addition → loaded mass/CG/inertia. The JSON and report reproduce from the script; `python research/extra-300/ex05/derive_physics.py --check` passes.
- The independent installation test checks dry balance at `x_aft=0.1088 m` within 1 µm (observed error 0.098 µm), with 0.1425 kg virtual ballast. The generated half-tank reference rounds to 3.419 kg, `x_aft=0.10266 m`, and 27.98% MAC; `balance.plan_cg` is derived and agrees with inventory CG. This numerical agreement is not measurement precision.
- The plan angles are `[0.5,2.0]` degrees in `[down,right]` order, with manual provenance. The hub location uses the visual thrust-frame pivot/rotation and propeller child delta; the CG-relative arm uses loaded CG. The lead reports the installation checks pass. No independent real-aircraft thrust or CG measurement exists.
- The report gives 10.42 m/s 1-g stall with unchanged estimated `CLmax`; the 17 m/s start remains a derived, rounded estimate rather than a manual flight recommendation. Any changed trim, inertia, or goldens are deliberate physics changes, not reasons to relax checks.
- The Stik and shared runtime physics remain unchanged. Fuel mass stays fixed in flight under the current `prescribed_fuel` contract.

Primary risks are the estimated inventory and the reference build's unknown real hardware: the roughly 55 g ballast increase is bookkeeping, not a ballast recommendation or measurement. The loaded CG shifts forward of the dry manual target and changes trim/static margin and inertia; these may change flight traces. The thrust installation now uses the plan angles and actual visual hub, but that does not identify the installed propeller load map or model propwash, P-factor, or the manual's qualitative takeoff yaw. Keep source conditions and calculation evidence together; passing loader, installation, or handling checks does not validate the real model.

## Evidence boundaries

The manual and plan do not provide a section polar, `CLmax`, quantitative stall or snap/spin speeds, control derivatives, installed thrust map, or gear-force constants. EX-05's current roll, loop, stall, and spin checks are useful regression gates but are not independent aircraft validation. A model-specific low-Re polar, measured installed-prop data, or repeatable flight observations would be needed before narrowing those uncertainties. See [EX-05 derivation and known simplifications](../../../../research/extra-300/ex05/derivation.md) and [EX-06 primary sources](primary-sources.md).
