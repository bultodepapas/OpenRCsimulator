## Full-size evidence is not RC evidence

| Historical primary source | Evidence and limit |
| --- | --- |
| [NACA XP-51 flying-qualities and stall report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930092575) | Full-size **XP-51**, not P-51D and not an RC model. Its measured glide neutral point and stall observations can inform hypotheses, but do not establish the model’s neutral point, static margin, or stall break. The project’s 34.2% MAC neutral point is calibrated to this full-size evidence. |
| [NACA ACR 4K02: P-51B drag/rigging report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930092458) | Full-size **P-51B** rigging data; the cited dimension table gives +1°00′ root and −0°53′ tip incidence, a 1.88° root-to-tip washout. Not RC rigging evidence and not the target’s measured construction. |
| [NACA RM L6J25: P-51D rudder/tail tests](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20050019329) and [NACA MR L5E05b: extended-tail P-51D tests](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150019958) | Full-size P-51D flight tests with specific test configurations. | [NACA TN 1945 six-series section tests](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930082618) | Tunnel tests on six-series airfoils over Reynolds numbers beginning around 0.7 million. They are not tests of the P-51’s NAA/NACA 45-100 at the model’s lower section Reynolds numbers. |

## Audit findings for P51-15

- **CG and stability:** The project’s 27% MAC CG is estimated by converting CG positions from different RC P-51s and planforms. The 34.2% MAC neutral point is full-size XP-51-calibrated. No reviewed source provides a measured CG, neutral-point map, static margin, or trim curve for this target build. Target wing/stabilizer incidence is likewise unverified. The Ziroli decalage is a reason to keep the current full-size-derived +1° wing / +2° stabilizer setup provisional, not a direct correction value.
- **Propulsion:** The DA-120 page lists no four-blade propeller. The selected four-blade 26×12 has no reviewed manufacturer load table; its predicted BEM performance and estimated inertia inherit uncertainty from 2-/3-blade simulated data ([P51-06 report](../../../../research/p51/p51-06/README.md)). The DA power rating and rpm range do not specify the installed torque curve. The project’s 6,550 rpm static anchor is attributed to forum reports visible only in search snippets, so it is weaker than manufacturer evidence.
- **Mass properties:** The baseline P51-05 inventory collapsed paired servos and main-gear hardware into centerline boxes spanning both sides. The [revised inventory/derivation](../../../../research/p51/p51-05/derivation.md) places each pair at opposite lateral positions, preserving total mass, longitudinal/vertical centers, and symmetric zero lateral CG. This is a high-confidence inventory-geometry correction: separated masses add parallel-axis contributions to roll/yaw inertia that a centerline slab misses. Component locations/envelopes and the resulting total aircraft inertia remain estimates; there is no target-aircraft inertia measurement.
- **Stale provenance to repair without retuning:** The plan’s baseline audit found exported descriptions that disagree with implementation: Oswald factor 0.80 vs 0.75; parabolic vs integrated camber wording; an obsolete `CLmax` formula; throws described as millimetre-converted when selected degrees come from a manual; and a claim that solved rudder trim models fixed fin incidence. Correct the source descriptions and state omitted fin-incidence effects; do not silently recalibrate coefficients.
- **Physical validation:** Current “flown” envelope and handling results are scripted simulation checks. They test implementation behavior against internal bands, not a real P-51 RC flight. No pilot-identified model, target-build measurements, or logged 1/4-scale DA-120 flight data were found in the reviewed evidence.
