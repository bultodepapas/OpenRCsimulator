## R4-F01 — Tailless plus vertical-fin propwash: a missing Dictionary key at the powered stage

The [aircraft module registry](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/data/schema/aircraft-modules-v1.json#L198-L216) requires horizontal tail **absence** for a tailless configuration. [AircraftContract](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aircraft_contract.gd#L156-L193) couples tailless to elevon controls and excludes ground effect, but the inspected relations do not prohibit tailless and a compatible single-tractor propeller wake. [AircraftData._surfaces](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L2546-L2660) derives **vertical only**. Its [slipstream parser](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L1961-L2030) accepts an individual piece targeting vertical, with no checked requirement that a horizontal tail exists.

The free-wing [Aero local loads](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aero.gd#L649-L666) correctly skips an absent horizontal surface. However [Dynamics._components_from_air](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/dynamics.gd#L110-L155) calls the wake for a nonempty propeller-slipstream installation; [Slipstream.stage_loads](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/slipstream.gd#L110-L119) unconditionally evaluates surfaces.horizontal.has("free_slope") before visiting any piece. The [swirl increment](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/swirl_loads.gd#L30-L44) repeats that unsafe lookup.

An intentionally absent Dictionary key is not a zero-lift surface. This is a **source-confirmed runtime-access hazard conditional on an admitted tailless powered-wake configuration**. The [native wake](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/research/native-kernel/src/nk_wake.hpp#L502-L512) contains an explicit m.horizontal.present && m.horizontal.free guard. This is a **potential reference/native divergence**, not evidence that an actual native tailless flight was admitted or failed.

**Downstream effect chain:** valid-looking authoring -> setup/derived surface absence -> static and dynamic load evaluator -> missing-key engine error/tick refusal depending caller -> potential trim/flight/observer disruption. Do not assert a played crash, corrupted save, or native acceptance until the entire setup runs. Fix with a guarded horizontal-tail access in both source wake files **or** an explicit schema refusal of the unsupported cross-capability; qualify which behavior is physically appropriate. Validate nonzero-RPM vertical-fin loads, zero/unavailable horizontal loads, absence of diagnostics, actual native admission, checkpoint/recording identities, and 240 Hz replay.

## R4-F02 — Wake pieces cannot double the same patch of real aerodynamic surface

The [AircraftData._slipstream parser](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L1961-L2030) validates one piece's area, position, span, chord profile and covered surface type. Its [profile integral validation](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L2031-L2062) orders intervals and checks area **within** a piece. [AircraftContract._validate_slipstream_relations](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aircraft_contract.gd#L285-L320) checks profile-vs-chord selections. The inspected admission path **does not require distinct pieces to be disjoint**, does not establish a physical tail-patch ID or constrain the total summed coverage to the actual tail area.

[Slipstream.stage_loads](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/slipstream.gd#L135-L280) sums washed-minus-free increments once for **each declared piece**, regardless of whether two declarations represent precisely the same region. Two genuinely different nonoverlapping pieces should sum; two *identical* physical patches must not double a real pressure force. For the physical surface, the correct loading is the single integral F = integral over unique area of local aerodynamic load; pressure and shear cannot act twice merely because the geometry was entered twice. See [NASA Glenn's aerodynamic force integral](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/aerodynamic-force/).

**Independent numerical witness, not a complete GDScript flight:** choose a physical vertical tail of 0.15 m2, and create **two coincident, nominal 0.10 m2 tail-wake pieces**. Their individually declared areas are in the loader's permitted range, yet the sum counts **0.20 m2 / 0.15 m2 = 1.333333** times the physical fin area. For one 0.10 m2 patch, attached vertical-tail coefficients at rho = 1.225 kg/m3, lift slope = 4 rad^-1, CD0 = 0.02, k = 0.05, CD90 = 1.2, local side flow = 0.8 m/s, and axial air flow **12 m/s free -> 17 m/s washed** give:

| Source-matched tail-force expression | F_y |
| --- | ---: |
| Free one-piece tail side force | -2.370742255 N |
| Washed one-piece side force | -3.353593166 N |
| **One physical patch, wash increment** | **-0.982850911 N** |
| **Same patch duplicated as another piece, summed increment** | **-1.965701821 N** |

The exact source-formula mirror uses the [tail lift/drag law and local-plane projection](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aero.gd#L249-L273) and [vertical-tail coefficient calculation](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/aero.gd#L752-L769). It establishes the **2x duplicated-term algebra**, not the exact force of a real aircraft or proof that a complete duplicate fixture passes admission. A fully prescribed example also needs valid source references, hub geometry, fitted aircraft coefficients and correct tail volume/authority.

**Downstream effect:** authored wake geometry -> excess immersed area -> summed lateral force and yaw moment -> wrong trim and flight trajectory -> persisted replay of physically unqualified loads. Fix by versioned physical surface partition and disjointness validation or partition-of-unity load construction. If propeller wakes genuinely overlap, combine the flows at the **one physical surface location** and evaluate that location once; do not clip legitimate distinct surfaces or double count coincident ones. Required Godot evidence: actual full aircraft parser acceptance/refusal; one piece, duplicate piece and two disjoint half-piece property tests; stable moments/CG reference; GDScript/native agreement and old identity/replay handling.

## R4-O01 — repeated spatial-wind sampling and geometry derivation

**R5 deduplication:** this is the same prepared-station-geometry opportunity as **A6 O5** in the [6 November audit](../../../external%20Audits/developer-audit-remediation-2026-11-06.md), retained here as a continuation rather than an additional optimization count. The earlier unguarded prototype measured about 247.53 -> 225.00 microseconds per stage call, but a stale `NaN` station was accepted by the cache while the baseline correctly refused it. Those timings omitted the required invalidation/guard cost and are not a qualified production speedup.

[WindAircraftFlow._sample](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/wind_aircraft_flow.gd#L269-L358) reconstructs geometry(model) **for each stage**, then asks wind_world for the reference datum and every active aerodynamic wing/tail/propeller point. With a conventional model, six wing stations + two tails + one propulsion point are nine stations, plus the datum: **10 environmental samples per source stage call**. A hypothetical four-stage-evaluation-per-tick path at 240 Hz gives **9,600 point calls per simulated second**, not including extra wake quadrature sampling. This is an operation-count scenario, **not a measured runtime call count**, CPU/GPU duration or FPS regression; kernel reuse and substep policies change real call totals.

[Stage_air](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e28b3fdb78504e3409a6290947a3688d3d6de3db/app/physics/wind_aircraft_flow.gd#L49-L93) also instantiates an ephemeral point sampler per evaluation. A possible safe improvement is to precompute and retain immutable validated geometric station arms by exact model identity, while preserving the current point positions, time, wind gradients, seed, native/reference parity and failure behavior. Profile actual 1080p release-equivalent flight/thermal cases and count stage calls with wall-clock and no-instrumentation control before optimization claims. **Never cache wind by arbitrary time or location across RK stages.**
