# CR-22b — Fire and smoke by energy source

2026-10-13 · **Status:** implemented; presentation only. Rules and timing are estimates from the [source review](research.md). **Serves:** [crash realism plan](../../../plans/crash-realism.md) §3.4, decisions D1 and D3.

## What changed

- **Data.** Inventory items may carry an optional `energy_source`: `kerosene`, `glow_fuel`, `gasoline` or `lipo`. The field is declared once in the contract rule file and typed by `build_schema.py`. The generators tag their items:

  | Aircraft | Tagged item | Source |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Avanti / Ventage Jet | kerosene | `av06` generator |
  | Extra | glow fuel | `ex05` generator |
  | P-51 | gasoline (DA-120) | `p51-05` generator |
  | Timber | 4S pack (LiPo) | `tt07` generator |
  | Ugly Stik | glow fuel | canonical v2 file |

  The synthetic electric Stiks' donor "fuel (half tank)" ballast stays untagged, so they carry no energy source. The Timber file kept its reference floats with the reusable [preserve_reference_floats.py](../../../../research/crash-damage/cr-22b/preserve_reference_floats.py), the same four drift leaves as in CR-01d. The D8b-R2 candidates were regenerated by `make_candidates.gd`.
- **Rules.** [effects.gd](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd) `energy_outcome()` is pure:
  - **Kerosene:** fire at C3+ when the tank ruptures and the turbine is hot (CR-22a, unchanged).
  - **Gasoline:** fire only at C4 on a central hit with the engine running, at 0.05 kg/s for at most 40 s; otherwise a fuel stain.
  - **Glow fuel:** never a visible fire; a dark wet stain when the tank ruptures.
  - **LiPo:** a central C3+ hit vents grey-white smoke after 30 → 5 s (a harder hit vents sooner) for 20 s. A 3 s flare happens only at C4 with severity ≥ 0.8.

  `apply_energy()` shows them.
- **Wiring.** [director.gd](../../../../app/render/crash/director.gd) sums tagged kilograms per kind. It no longer matches "kerosene +" in item names, and it records `report.energy` and `report.energy_sources`.
- **Budget.** Particles are summed from the emitters: 700, exactly the CR-22a limit.

## Evidence

  - The full outcome matrix.
  - A real `Effects` instance: no smoke or flame at impact, quiet just before the delay, venting after it, a short flare, venting ending, one dark stain, and reset clearing pending venting.
  - Every shipped aircraft's tag; the synthetic donors carry none.
- **Unchanged checks:** the aircraft contract (2,159), aircraft data, `migrate --check-known`, schema and register freshness, the generators' `--check`, `test_pullup_cli.py`, trace acceptance, and the crash effects (58), ladder, integration, audio and cause suites.
- [Mutations](../../../../research/crash-damage/cr-22b/mutations.log): **10/10 killed**, run in a disposable worktree.

## Limits

- The LiPo pack position is not used: "central" means a fuselage, engine or propeller hit.
- There is no venting sound; the fire loop covers the flare only.
- Fuel quantities are declared states, not consumption.
- Every timing is a presentation compression of safety guidance, not a measurement.
