# CR-33a — Measurement contract for automatic damage billing

2026-10-15 · Proposed data contract for CR-33–35; no damage-capacity calibration or physical gate is claimed.

## Current evidence boundary

The accepted `openrc-aircraft-contact-events v1` record is a validated contact observation, not a component-failure result. It can carry source/contact identity, tick and response; optional probe/obstacle identity and swept fraction; vectors; and optional measures such as speed, gear compression/travel, effective mass or energy. Missing values remain absent. In particular, the gear-limit snapshot reports compression beyond its model travel limit, not measured gear force, mount moment or deforming-wheel velocity. Scenery events identify the aircraft probe and obstacle, but the closed `material` field currently distinguishes only water and bank ground; visual “wood” profiles and scenery cutoffs are gameplay estimates. `Incident` therefore leaves tree and gear outcomes unresolved for inspection. The current LiPo safety rule supports quarantine/replacement after an explicit damaged-pack finding, not a simulated impact-to-ignition law. See the [contact contract](../../../../AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md#contact-observations-and-checkpoints), [CR-17a limits](../CR-17a/README.md#limits), and [CR-31c receipt boundary](../CR-31c/README.md).

## Minimum measurement record

Every calibration sample needs a stable test ID; exact aircraft/configuration and physical-source SHA; installed hardware, component and joint IDs; solver/build identity when simulated; synchronized accepted tick/event IDs; SI units and coordinate frame; instrument/model provenance, calibration and uncertainty; initial and post-event state; and raw time series or a reproducible reference. Record test conditions, specimen/aircraft revision, mass/CG/inertia, boundary/support conditions, repetition count, censoring/failures, and the observed outcome separately from the fitted law. A single joule value or a visual damage label is not a failure envelope.

| Case | Required observed inputs before fitting | Outcome labels needed for a bounded resolver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tree / structure (CR-33/34)** | Aircraft local contact point, normal and relative velocity including obstacle motion; accepted impulse or force-time history and contact duration; aircraft mass properties and angular motion; trunk/branch/crown geometry, species or material, moisture/condition and support/compliance; mapped aircraft probe → structural component → joint IDs. Test representative airframe parts/joints and whole-assembly paths, recording direction, rate and specimen condition. | Measured deformation, joint slip/pullout, crush, tear or fracture; which economic groups actually require repair/replacement; post-test control/load-path function. Separate branch/canopy brush from trunk collision. Include uncertainty and held-out cases. Generic clear-wood properties cannot stand in for balsa, foam, film, plywood, glue lines or the assembled RC joint. |
| **Landing gear (CR-34)** | Stable gear/contact and mount IDs; time histories of compression/stroke and calibrated normal/side forces plus axle/mount moments; tire/wheel and strut behavior; ground snag/friction condition; gear state and geometry; aircraft mass, CG and impact vector. Instrument mount/firewall/leg or use a separately validated structural model. | Permanent bend, buckling, fracture, axle/wheel loss, mount tear-out, retract/trunnion or lock failure, and post-event alignment/rolling/retention checks. Resolve leg, wheel, axle and mount independently where the BOM supports them. A solver travel-limit crossing alone cannot choose a gear replacement package. |
| **Battery fire / thermal propagation (CR-35)** | Installed source ID, chemistry/cell and pack revision, SOC, cell/pack temperature and electrical state; location/orientation, enclosure and shielding; measured mechanical insult (force/displacement/time, crush or puncture geometry) and water/electrical-short condition; synchronized cell, pack and neighbor temperatures/heat flux, voltage/current, venting, gas/flame onset and duration. Test controlled, replicated cases across the declared SOC/temperature/impact envelope. | Distinguish pack damage/quarantine from short, self-heating, venting, ignition, sustained burn and propagation. Measure delayed onset, peak/duration and exposure at each installed component; record negative/no-ignition cases and unaffected remote components. Bound likelihood and uncertainty only within tested conditions. An energy inventory or damage flag alone does not establish ignition or a fire surcharge. |

Use a staged program: document exact specimens and non-destructive properties; instrument low-risk coupons/joints and gear assemblies; then compare complete airframe outcomes against held-out tests. Preserve raw traces and uncertainty; calibrate on a designated subset and report false-positive/negative classification on untouched cases. Do not derive universal thresholds from the current scenery energy cutoffs, presentation severity, estimated event energy, one crash video, or the worked 150 km/h tree scenario. The [economics plan](../../../plans/crash-damage-economics.md#4-contact-to-damage-calculation) already requires measured energy/load paths and distinct component outcomes. ASTM D143-25 controls specimen conditions for **small clear wood** tests; its stated use with structural-size tests is evidence that coupon values alone do not establish an aircraft-joint capacity. USDA Forest Service reports substantial natural wood-property variability. Neither source supplies RC balsa/foam/joint failure limits. ([ASTM D143-25](https://store.astm.org/d0143-25.html); [USDA Forest Products Laboratory, Wood Handbook ch. 5](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/62244).)

For lithium systems, FAA PackSafe establishes that damage can be a thermal-runaway hazard and damaged packs should not be treated as serviceable. FAA AC 20-184A is current aircraft-system test guidance, but is not a hobby-pack impact threshold or an RC ignition model. Use it as a hazard/testing reference only; characterize the actual installed RC pack and enclosure. ([FAA PackSafe](https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe/more_info/?hazmat=7); [FAA AC 20-184A](https://www.faa.gov/airports/resources/advisory_circulars/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/1045700), issued 2026-09-18.)

## Billing gate

Automatic tree, gear or fire billing requires all four: (1) a validated accepted event with enough measured inputs for the applicable tested domain; (2) a source-hash-pinned mapping from contact/energy source through affected physical component or joint to non-overlapping economic coverage; (3) a versioned outcome model with documented uncertainty and independent held-out validation; and (4) a priced replacement/repair package whose inclusions do not duplicate separately priced parts. If any link is missing, retain `inspect`/`unknown`, with no automatic damage surcharge or salvage credit. A known package price cannot fill a missing damage inference; a damage finding cannot invent a missing price. Keep an explicit damaged LiPo quarantine separate from thermal damage, and do not charge adjacent fire damage without measured/qualified exposure. Full physical gates CR-33, CR-34 and CR-35 remain open; see the [CR-29–32 implementation limits](../CR-29-32/README.md#remaining-gates).
