## P-51 canister price proxy

The physical [P-51 inventory](../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json) has one `muffler_pair` row for two in-cowl canister mufflers on a 120 cc twin. Desert Aircraft recommends the KS3086 rear exhaust for its [DA-120](https://www.desertaircraft.com/products/da-120). Its current [KS Canisters listing selected to KS 3086](https://www.desertaircraft.com/products/ks-canisters?variant=5330902529) identifies SKU `EKS3086`, variant `5330902529`, at $225 each. The product listing gives a 360 mm rear-exit canister with a 25 mm inlet, 60 mm diameter, and approximate 250 g weight.

The canonical `muffler_pair` row now uses two individually priced KS3086 canisters at $225 each, or **$450** for the physical row's count. This is a composite price proxy, not one $450 retail offer. The DA recommendation supports the product family for the engine, but the inventory does not identify its installed brand or SKU. The listing does not establish included headers, drop tubes, or installation hardware, so the installed exhaust assembly remains unverified. The known P-51 priced-part subtotal is now **$2,456.71**; it remains a partial subtotal, not a complete-build price, and `replacement_total_minor` stays null.

As a scope comparison only, Desert Aircraft's exact [DA-120 Slimline selection](https://www.desertaircraft.com/products/da-stock-mufflers?variant=5302647233) is SKU `EDA120C`, variant `5302647233`, at $210 for one left/right muffler pair. The manufacturer says DA-120 pairs include two exhaust gaskets and mounting hardware, and warns lead times can vary. This is a standard muffler pair, not the two canisters named by the P-51 inventory, so it is not mapped. The product page's unselected default is the DA-35 single muffler at $105; that amount does not apply to the DA-120 variant. The existing DA-120 engine listing separately includes two exhaust gaskets but does not include the installed mufflers/canisters.

## JetCat kit and regional prices

JetCat's [P100-RX installation kit](https://www.jetcat.de/en/productdetails/produkte/jetcat/produkte/zubehoer/Einbausaetze/Einbausaetze%20Fuer%20JetCat%20RX), part `71105-0010`, was listed at €698 including VAT (€586.55 net) with Add to Basket shown. It contains an ECU V12.0, cable and hose/connector sets, bus-system fuel pump, LED/I-O board, mounting clamps, telemetry cable, and USB cable. This is useful scope evidence for the Avanti's `turbine_accessories` group, but remains foreign-currency research: no USD conversion is used. The package does not list the inventory's UAT/hopper, and CARF's P100-RX page describes the turbine weight as including autostart and valves. Reconcile those boundaries before pricing the complete accessories row; the USD engine offer's original observation date remains unchanged.

CARF shows regional price differences for the same P100-RX item `850100`: [USA/Canada](https://carf-models.com/de/products/jetcat-p100rx/region/1) at $3,040, [other regions](https://carf-models.com/de/products/jetcat-p100rx/region/5) at $2,780, and [Europe/Germany](https://carf-models.com/de/products/jetcat-p100rx/region/2) at €2,449 including 19% German VAT (€2,057.98 before tax). CARF notes VAT may vary by delivery country. These pages were checked on 2026-10-16 to document regional display behavior. Preserve the existing $2,780 canonical offer at its original 2026-10-15 observation; do not re-date it or infer a landed price from these displays.
