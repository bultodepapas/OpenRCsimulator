# CR-31c — Explicit inspection receipts

2026-10-15. Implements the assessment-to-accounting boundary in [CR-31](../../../plans/crash-damage-economics.md). This is not an automatic structural or thermal predictor.

`Incident.inspection_context()` returns the frozen incident/aircraft/source/catalog identities and evidence revision. An assessment producer submits `openrc-accident-inspection v1` to `Incident.apply_inspection()` outside accepted-tick callbacks. The receipt declares its own ID, those identities, expected revision, full/partial inspection, whole-build disposition, evidence, `inspection` or `scenario_assumption` basis, and component decisions. `app/economy/inspection.gd` validates these against the installed dossier before changing the ledger. There is no player button that creates survival evidence or lowers the bill.

Full inspection must account for all economic rows, including assembly. Repair inspections account for every installed unit; total-loss receipts may explicitly identify only the reusable units in a group, with the remainder still replaced. Partial receipts cannot change the whole-aircraft disposition. Required money fields remain integer cents; missing prices remain unknown. The existing calculator performs the final financial validation.

Reapplying an identical receipt is a no-op, even after later revisions. Reusing an ID with different contents, an older revision, a different source/catalog, unknown component or malformed decision is rejected atomically. Sixteen receipts and a combined 64 KiB evidence limit bound the history; rejection retains the previous result. Accepted receipts are copied into the existing report/archive and their basis/evidence appears in its assumptions. Restart clears flight-local receipts and restrictions.

Confirmed propeller-strike evidence forbids repair/reuse of that propeller. Explicit thermal, electrical, damaged-LiPo or unretrieved conclusions block reuse/repair for that component in this first version. Later evidence can therefore revoke a prior servo salvage credit; removing a flag cannot undo that restriction within the same incident. Structural damage forbids direct reuse but allows an explicitly costed repair. These conservative disposition rules do not determine ignition, temperature, battery chemistry, damage onset or actual component survival.

`Incident.apply_repair_choice(receipt, repair_inspection, component_id, option_id, labor_mode, costing_basis)` invokes the [CR-31b planner](../CR-31b/README.md) against a recipe book frozen at incident start. The caller explicitly chooses DIY/paid labor and purchased/consumed material accounting. Aircraft/component allowlists prevent a film recipe from being applied to engines, foam aircraft or unsupported composite surfaces. The wrapper rejects contradictory structural/covering evidence, preserves the selected source/calculation/hash in `repair_choices`, and submits the result through the same receipt transaction. Equal rounded cost with different measurements is different evidence and cannot reuse a receipt ID. No inventory is debited by selecting consumption accounting.

The [Horizon lithium battery guidance](https://assets.horizonhobby.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-horizon-master/default/dwa92ae859/Manuals/SMART%20Batteries%20Safety%20Sheet%20Update%20Manual-MULTI.pdf), reviewed 2026-10-15, supports rejecting damaged packs from service. It supplies no universal impact threshold, ignition delay or quantitative fire budget; none is inferred here. Do not identify a NiMH receiver pack as LiPo from the aircraft being electric/glow.

## Verification scope

The [integrated verification record](verification-2026-10-15.json) records 500 passing Godot checks across catalog, calculator, repair planner, inspection, impact/thermal budgets, production incident integration, report and settlement, plus 22 passing Python tests. Generated-data freshness, changed-script parsing and local document links also passed on Windows with pinned Godot 4.7.2. This is a focused suite, not a run of every repository test.

`test_economy_inspection.gd` exercises the real baseline dossier and incident calculator. A conditional total-loss fixture with two reusable servos gives $926.35, with a $67.98 credit. Later explicit heat-damage evidence removes that credit and gives $994.33. Duplicate, stale, wrong-source, incomplete, contradictory, fractional and overflowing receipts are rejected without altering the result. Full inspection resolves known pending-inspection reasons but preserves lost-history/overflow uncertainty. Fixtures are labeled scenario assumptions, not physical validation.

The integrated repair test prices an explicit 10.5 sq in film patch at $0.14 consumed material plus $22.50 for 30 minutes of paid labor under the declared $45/hour planning assumption: $22.64. Buying a whole roll instead is a different accounting basis. The archive retains the selected basis and provenance and rejects malformed optional inspection/repair arrays.

Remaining: an independently qualified component-damage producer, thermal progression integration, retrieval state, complete journal/checkpoint replay and player-facing workshop decisions. Receipts are supplied by internal assessment/scenario producers; basis/evidence text is self-declared and is not authentication or physical validation. The report archive is still a frozen display record, not a resumable damage simulation.
