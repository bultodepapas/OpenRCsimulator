# Collision and damage improvement plan

**Status:** proposed work from the audit of baseline `a7de9b35676d891bc2034cc344b910d9e1ef9c61`. This branch records an audit and evidence; it makes no production fixes. Read [findings](findings.md) for the scope, evidence and severity of each issue and [verification](verification.md) for the actual commands, results and limitations. This plan describes acceptance work, not a claim that any item is complete.

**Owners:** simulation framework and physics (SF), ground/contact physics (GP), crash and damage (CR), aircraft contract/data (AS) and vehicle owners jointly own their boundaries. Landscape/scenery (LU/SC), helicopter (HELI), drone racing (DR), ground vehicles (RCV), airship (BL), propulsion owners, rendering/camera/audio (VQ/CAM/AUD), UI and test/performance owners participate as listed below. The [track registry](../../../README.md#tracks-plans-and-step-ids) determines actual file ownership and step IDs. Agree shared interfaces before editing them; add accepted work to the owning plans instead of making this audit a second permanent implementation backlog.

## Priorities and boundaries

The priorities below order engineering work; they are not substitutes for the severity and reachability assessments in the findings.

1. **P1 — reliable contact and evidence.** Resolve the accepted-contact/incident identity boundary, source-off propeller handling, helicopter nonterminal scenery response, the quad/blimp lake mismatch, and the reported cross-fleet geometry omissions. Preserve explicit refusal when a result cannot be supported. The lake and helicopter baseline probes passed 13/13 and 8/8 checks respectively; these are bounded software observations. The corrected car-scenery probe subsequently completed 19/19 checks, demonstrating the static-overlap coverage gap for both cars with injected-box controls. Earlier startup timeouts remain recorded in verification.
2. **P2 — vehicle and aftermath coverage.** Add car scenery and track-dressing coverage, close established collision-shape and sampling gaps to a declared operating envelope, and repair visual wreck mass, centres, shapes, timing and terrain/scenery handoff. Some omissions are already disclosed in vehicle plans; prioritize them by player exposure and a reproducible case rather than by the number of proxies.
3. **P3 — researched capability.** Develop progressive physical damage, structural strength and damaged-flight consequences as separate opt-in research. Do not infer a material failure law from a presentation severity class, particle animation, crash video, cost estimate or a passing collision test.

The existing [crash plan](../../../CRASH-DAMAGE-PLAN.md) already records sparse hull coverage, unfinished physical strength and damaged flight; the [drone plan](../../../plans/drone-racing.md) records bounded disc and prop-sweep limitations. Helicopter water contact is already deliberately refused and the cars already have estimated wet forces. Preserve these distinctions when fixing a defect or adding a new capability.

## Shared design contract

### Authoritative geometry and contact

Each activity must identify the geometry actually drawn and the geometry its physics uses: vehicle/configuration and scale, component or probe identity, field, terrain overlays, water, obstacle inventory and any mutable scenery state. Render meshes, contact probes, mass inventory items, wreck sections and priced parts are different entities. Bind them explicitly rather than treating names or array positions as interchangeable. The vehicle adapter supplies this contract to the kernel; do not add vehicle-specific branches to `Simulation`.

Use the existing float64 terrain, lake and collision-world definitions as authorities where their contracts apply. A single shared data source does not require every vehicle to use the same contact law: aircraft points, helicopter rotors/skids, car wheels, quad props and a blimp envelope have different geometry and response needs. Specify, per query, the represented solid or surface, swept interval, speed/rotation domain, tolerances, coordinate frames and what a positive or negative result proves. A sampled witness can prove an observed contact; absence of a witness is not a clearance certificate. Where a bounded search cannot decide, return a distinct unresolved/refused result and preserve the accepted frame rather than silently reporting clear.

Coordinate any geometry change with aircraft generators, scenery owners and existing visual transforms. Preserve stable part/hinge names and generate authored assets through their source pipelines. Include the active geometry and collision-law identities in checkpoints and replay compatibility. Keep historical results identifiable under their original law.

### Accepted events, identity and rollback

Define a versioned accepted-contact receipt that separates observed facts from estimates and unknowns: activity/generation, tick and any defensible time interval, vehicle and component/probe, obstacle/support identity, surface/material, point, normal, pre/post kinematics and the method and confidence of the observation. Include model, field, collision-world and law identities. Missing values stay unknown. A visual severity, assumed failure, repair recommendation or price must never become a measured physical fact through this adapter.

Assign deterministic event identities at the accepted transaction boundary. Distinct same-tick contacts, probes, obstacles, sources and episodes must remain distinguishable; a repeated delivery of the same immutable event must remain idempotent. Define ordering and conservative tie/uncertainty behavior across terrain, lake, scenery, propeller and race-scoring events; do not manufacture sub-tick precedence from endpoint timestamps. Specify bounded retention and an explicit incomplete/overflow result rather than silently dropping evidence.

Candidate RK stages and rejected ticks may create no published event, damage, charge, effects or economic action. A rollback/retry must not duplicate an event, leak candidate state or advance an incident revision. Once a frame has committed, an observer failure must follow the existing accepted-publication contract: retain the accepted frame and hold/fault visibly, rather than falsely claiming a precommit rollback. Version any changed checkpoint, identity, replay or archive semantics and document migration, historical reading and unsupported restores.

## Staged, independently reviewable pull requests

Dependencies describe the interfaces a change consumes, not a requirement to serialize unrelated research. A PR may ship a narrower slice when its acceptance checks and owning track agree on the boundary.

### 1. Contact receipt and incident identity — P1

**Lead:** SF/GP with CR and AS; consumers: economics, statistics, race, replay and presentation. **Prerequisite:** pin baseline reproductions and agree the shared contract above.

- Map existing aircraft hull, gear, lake, scenery and prop events and the other vehicle sessions to a common receipt without assuming equivalent sensors or inventing missing channels. Keep assessment and statistics as consumers of detached accepted facts.
- Repair CR-AUD-04's demonstrated loss of crossing-witness identity: preserve separate selected-contact and earliest-crossing IDs, time bases, validity and uncertainty. Retain the analytic 0.2-versus-0.8 two-probe regression. Distinct-event and duplicate-delivery tests are additional contract gates, not a claim that this audit reproduced an event-ID collision. Define identities independently from display names and mutable proxy order.
- Test two components against one obstacle, one component against two obstacles, simultaneous terrain/prop/scenery observations, multiple contacts on one tick, duplicate and conflicting deliveries, reordering, truncation and capacity exhaustion. Compare fresh, checkpoint-restored and replayed continuations.
- Inject failure before and after commit and during observer publication. Before commit, assert byte-equivalent accepted state, damage, tape and incident evidence; retry emits one equivalent event. After commit, assert one accepted generation, visible hold/fault and no duplicate publication. Run existing contact, crash-cost, statistics and checkpoint consumers.

**Done when:** evidence remains distinct, bounded and deterministic under those cases; rejected candidates have no side effects; older checkpoints are migrated or explicitly refused; the unchanged physical trajectory remains byte-equal when this PR changes observation only.

### 2. Source-off propeller and helicopter scenery response — P1

**Lead:** propulsion/aircraft session and HELI with SF/GP; **depends on:** the receipt boundary for published consequences, but geometric characterization can start independently.

- Determine propeller contact from the available accepted shaft speed and geometry, not solely from whether the power source is running. Distinguish powered rotation, unpowered/coasting rotation, known stopped blade orientation, and unavailable phase or shaft state. A fixed disc, stopped blade and unknown blade pose make different claims; do not invent phase or damage a definitely clear stopped blade. Preserve the correct stop/cut and restart behavior for each installed powertrain.
- Handle helicopter nonterminal scenery contacts with a declared bounded response, or explicitly refuse the unsupported candidate. Do not discard an accepted frangible, foliage or parked-airframe body/rotor contact merely because it is below a terminal threshold. Retain the existing terminal handling, skid support and conservative ambiguity refusal.
- Test the same prop geometry with power on/off at equal observed RPM, crossing and clear controls, RPM threshold boundaries, known stopped phase, missing phase, nonterminal strike followed by landing, and terminal simultaneous contacts. Cover the stock propulsion families and a technical configuration where its law differs.
- For the helicopter, compare the existing 2 m/s frangible body-probe fixture with hard and open-air controls, then add a real accepted-tick crossing, glancing/separating contacts, repeated contact, spinning and stopped rotor cases, reset and checkpoint behavior. Bound any response by the energy and momentum available in the selected model; its coefficients remain declared estimates until calibrated.

**Done when:** valid moving-prop contacts are not suppressed by power state, supported nonterminal helicopter contacts have an observable response or a clear refusal, no rejected trial produces a strike or sound, and hard/clear controls and existing rotor/skid suites pass.

### 3. Quad and blimp lake context — P1

**Lead:** DR and BL with LU/SC and SF. **Depends on:** a single resolved field context and versioned session identity; may proceed alongside PR 2.

- Pass the same validated, enabled/disabled lake definition used for the field to each session before preparation, reset and restore. Include lake identity and contact-law version in compatibility checks. The current mismatch is a context/authority defect; simply pointing ground contact at the lake bed does not model the water surface.
- Choose and document, per vehicle, either a qualified water-contact law or a conservative unsupported-water refusal. An initial fail-safe may refuse a candidate and explain why; it must retain the prior accepted state and leave ordinary dry and high-overflight cases usable. Do not copy the helicopter's bounds blindly or invent flotation, electrical failure or blimp envelope damage.
- Test default lake interior and both sides of the shoreline/bank; enabled versus disabled and custom translated lake where admitted; low crossing and clear high overflight; dry terrain control; malformed lake; reset and compatible/incompatible checkpoint restore. Compare the field's resolved identity with the session and the actual collision query. Add a scene-level test and rendered inspection that confirm visual and physical contexts match; the current 13-check session probe alone does not do so.

**Done when:** enabled water is never silently treated as dry ground, unsupported entry is observable and transactional or supported entry follows its stated model, and changing/disabling water changes the correct identity and behavior without affecting dry-field regression cases.

### 4. Ground vehicle scenery and impact attribution — P2

**Lead:** RCV with LU/SC and GP. **Depends on:** a selected scenery contact contract and the shared identity rules; first rerun the corrected baseline probe and retain its checked result.

- Feed cars the scenery and track dressing that the selected field actually exposes as collidable, using an owned adapter over the immutable collision world. Preserve ordinary wall, wheel, chassis, terrain and wet-road behavior. Reconcile the five existing track wall boxes with the shared dressing inventory so the same wall is not applied twice. Keep intentionally noncollidable scenery distinct.
- State the geometry and speed domain for wheel and chassis response to boxes, capsules and supported rotated proxies. Define deterministic candidate ordering, a bounded broad phase, and a visible refusal or qualified fallback when candidates or solver capacity are exceeded; the current eight-wall limit must not silently truncate a dense scene. Record collision and scenery-damage identity at reset/checkpoint boundaries.
- For both `mt8-bisonte` and `monster-car-2`, test known rendered/inventory-matched tyres and a field obstacle, alongside a track-wall positive control and clear-road negative control. Progress from static overlapping geometry to driven approaches, grazing and separating contacts, high-speed sweeps, wheel-only and hull-only impacts, repeated contact, reset/recovery and 240/480 Hz comparison. Keep baseline source-only omission and any newly measured runtime behavior separately labeled.
- Improve attribution only with independent evidence. The existing speed-drop gate reduced known false wear but total mechanical energy is not isolated normal impact work. Include paired braking, rolling, suspension and engine-spindown controls, particularly Nitro flywheel energy; a candidate must not convert unrelated losses to impact damage or erase a real glancing/rotational impact without a disclosed bound. Keep propulsion-only derating as the current model until a separate failure law is accepted.

**Done when:** selected scenery and dressing have declared, tested contact behavior for both cars, no duplicate wall force or silent capacity loss occurs, wet and dry controls remain valid, and any changed damage estimator is supported by isolated-work and replay evidence rather than assumed strength.

### 5. Fleet geometry and swept-contact coverage — P1/P2

**Lead:** SF/GP and AS with each vehicle owner and LU/SC. **Depends on:** the geometry and receipt contracts; family-specific slices may ship in parallel after those interfaces are agreed.

- Inventory every Home-selectable physical configuration and the three nonselectable Stik technical configurations; record the distinct rules rather than counting skins as separate airframes. Include representative supported Workshop scales and conversions where geometry changes. Audit fuselage, wing/tail, propeller, rotor/blade, wheel/skid, quad arm/frame and blimp envelope/appendage coverage against their authored sources.
- Replace a proven unsafe early-out with a valid bound over the **composed** terrain and motion, or refuse the unsupported query. Cover between-probe and between-tick cases, thin obstacles, corners, slopes, ramps, lake banks and transitions, rotating/translated geometry, collision-world boundaries and multiple competing contacts. Retain established fixes: the quad's previous shipped-ramp slope witness is now covered by its regression; remaining curved-disc clearance and endpoint prop sweep are separate work.
- Use independent analytic planes/boxes and high-resolution witness searches as appropriate. A scan that finds penetration is a hit oracle; an empty finite scan is not proof of clearance. Include exact tangency, a small clearance, real penetration, zero/stopped rotation and a moving blade. Compare subdivisions and 240/480 Hz, and test a representative 41.67 m/s aircraft path, an explicitly admitted synthetic 300 km/h (83.33 m/s) aircraft contact, and the quad's documented roughly 10 m/s thin-bar case within each candidate's admitted domain. These are qualification targets, not flown audit results.
- Bound time, space and candidate searches. Force each limit in a test: the result must remain correct within its proven envelope or explicitly report unresolved/refused with no state publication. Include broad-phase cell edges, obstacle order permutations and geometry identity changes. No replacement proxy may claim rendered alignment or physical fidelity without its own measurement.

**Done when:** each shipped family has an explicit coverage matrix and supported collision envelope, known reproducible misses are captured without introducing false clear results, unresolved cases are observable, and the stated tolerances are checked against independent fixtures and relevant visual geometry.

### 6. Visual wreck and aftermath — P2

**Lead:** CR/VQ with vehicle model, LU/SC, CAM and AUD owners. **Depends on:** a stable accepted impact handoff; presentation remains a consumer and cannot alter simulation, component damage or cost.

- Check total allocated mass, active colliding-body mass throughout every delayed release, and first moment across core, attached, carried and detached sections, including missing inventory, light components, minimum mass floors, repeated section kinds and all severity/release paths. Define a valid fallback when inventory cannot support a unique allocation. Assert mass closure to `max(1e-5 kg, 1e-5 × input mass)` and first-moment closure to an owner-approved, scale-aware tolerance specified before running tests; record the actual error. Test each active body's centre of mass relative to its own origin and the visible geometry, including off-centre inventory and asymmetric detachments.
- Require COM-based launch velocities and mass, first-moment, linear-momentum and angular-momentum closure across a zero-scatter C3 split; use consistent inertia tensors and a common reference origin. Report any deliberately added scatter impulse separately. These are visual-rigid-body transition invariants, not proof of authoritative flight breakup.
- Replace or qualify overly broad/flat box shapes with a bounded set of appropriate proxies. Ensure shape updates follow the transformed section and do not leave stale pooled shapes. Measure geometry mismatch and penetration against selected wings, tails, rotor blades and car-relevant parts; avoid representing decorative debris as a calibrated structural fracture.
- Define one pause-aware aftermath clock and release policy for the actual presentation architecture. Check delayed release, contact callbacks, equal-supplied-time 10/20/30/60/240 Hz update partitions and rendered rates with a common fixed-step schedule, long frames, pause/resume, repeated reset/restart and stale callbacks. At the same accepted schedule, release order and generation ownership must match; do not promise bit-exact cross-platform rigid-body simulation.
- Test core/fragment-to-scenery callbacks with engine-generated contacts and ground controls, and require equivalent nearby scenery coverage after ground-triggered and scenery-triggered crashes. Track staged collider arrival before fast fragments reach them.
- Test the field's actual terrain height and slope, banks, water boundary and nearby scenery using a bounded, owned aftermath representation, rather than assuming a universal datum plane. Cover contacts near cell edges, scene removal, quality toggles and teardown. Do not feed debris contacts back into the flight solver, assessment or repair cost.
- Reconcile CR-AUD-13's fuel sample across the typed report and all effects consumers. CR, audio and propulsion owners must select one accepted impact fuel sample with an explicit basis/unknown fallback. Test declared versus live kerosene, depleted and capped quantities, hot/cold and rupture conditions, reset, and gasoline/glow/LiPo controls. Matching software duration fields does not validate a real burn rate.
- Record before/after native captures at the primary 1920×1080 target, including asymmetric impact, slope, scenery, delayed separation and reset. Report renderer, GPU, quality settings, timing and any approximations. Include 720p only as a compatibility check; one machine or capture is not a release qualification.

**Done when:** mass/centre tests and lifecycle checks pass, visible proxy/contact limitations are measured, terrain and scenery interactions match the selected field within documented tolerances, and effects cannot affect accepted physics or billing.

### 7. Progressive physical damage research — P3

**Lead:** CR/GP and vehicle/AS owners with propulsion and controls. **Depends on:** accepted contact receipts, state/replay foundations and mass-property ownership; independent material research can begin earlier.

- For each proposed component and joint, define geometry, installed load path, mass/inertia, modes of failure, evidence source, units, loading rate, uncertainty, applicable construction and a held-out comparison. Obtain measured aircraft-specific strengths or explicitly label borrowed and estimated proxies. [Material and joint research](../03-rc-construction-material-joint-evidence.md) does not yet qualify an installed-aircraft failure threshold.
- Prototype a bounded, vehicle-owned damage state and energy/momentum allocation in a laboratory path. Treat crack initiation, detachable mass, rotor/prop dynamics, wheel/gear/support, control authority, propulsion and electrical/thermal/water exposure as separate causal questions. Moving a visual part or producing a severity class is not evidence of one of those failures.
- Test no-damage parity, below/at/above qualified bounds, repeat impacts with one incident budget, same-tick competition, conservation, failure ordering, loss of control authority, repair/reset, checkpoints, rejected trials and cross-backend behavior. Include uncertainty and `unknown` outcomes when evidence is inadequate. New gameplay estimates may be explored under an explicit experimental policy without being labeled measured.

**Done when:** the owner accepts a component-specific, traceable evidence package and a separately reviewed physical state transition; stable replay and software tests pass; the advertised fidelity stays within the measured regime. Otherwise the research remains nonproduction and the uncertainty is shown.

### 8. Integrated verification, observability and release qualification — P1 throughout

**Lead:** test/performance owners with every affected track. **Depends on:** each slice's changed interfaces; this is a cross-cutting gate, not a final substitute for focused tests.

- Repair CR-AUD-15's stale 19-piece shelter assertion using a semantic with/without-fleet proxy comparison. Preserve the current 24-piece sloped-roof contract and its independent collision controls; do not revert correct geometry to satisfy an obsolete count. Show that the revised oracle catches an added floor/support and an elevation error.
- Preserve the audit probes and their independent oracles. As each defect is fixed, promote its applicable desired-behavior assertion into the owning production test suite with the negative controls intact; keep this dated failing baseline as historical evidence. Do not make CI pass by changing an expected outcome to match the defect.
- Assign test-runner resource classes to small numerical checks versus full production-scene integration. Retain process signals, timeouts, completion markers, engine diagnostics, peak memory and concurrency/admission metadata separately. The audit's unexplained SIGKILL and startup timeouts do not prove an application collision failure or an out-of-memory cause. Qualify on an appropriate isolated runner and preserve both the failed attempt and successful rerun.
- Maintain a matrix of the eight selectable fixed-wing configurations, helicopter, quad, blimp and both cars, plus relevant nonselectable technical routes. Cover terrain, obstacles, rotor/prop/gear or wheel contact, water policy, nonterminal/terminal state, pause, reset and replay where each behavior applies. Distinguish `not applicable`, `unsupported/refused`, `not tested` and `passed`.
- Run the smallest relevant scripts during development, then affected session, consumer, replay, scenery, economics and native/reference parity tests. Run required CI; escalate to full local coverage for a shared cross-cutting candidate or unqualified CI. Preserve exact baseline and candidate hashes, engine/OS/backend, input tape, test counts, diagnostics and artifact paths. Re-record goldens only for an intentional explained physics change.
- Instrument candidate counts, bounded-search exhaustion, unresolved order, omitted/overflow evidence, contact and damage-event counts, reset/restore rejection and effect-pool saturation. Expose enough information to distinguish a clear result, a qualified fallback and a failed/refused query. Logging and telemetry must be bounded and must not change accepted physics.
- Measure unchanged and candidate paths on the same scenarios and hardware: open field, dense default scenery, simultaneous contacts, near-water geometry, worst admitted obstacle count, repeated impacts, crash handoff and long aftermath. Record median, p95, p99 and maximum for contact tick, end-to-end tick and rendered frames, memory and allocation/pool maxima. At 240 Hz the public tick interval is 4.167 ms; this is a scheduling constraint, not a claim that the baseline or every target already meets it. Obtain owner-approved budgets for each supported target and quality level before accepting changes; disabled effects must avoid unnecessary work.
- Qualify the 1920×1080 primary target first, then higher resolutions and finally 720p compatibility. Headless probes do not establish GPU, native UI, hardware-radio, visual or physical validation. Preserve limitations explicitly when a platform, renderer, target machine or physical reference is unavailable.

**Done when:** required CI and all relevant family/consumer checks pass on the integrated candidate; bounded failures are observable; the agreed hardware budgets and visual gates are met or explicitly remain open; retained evidence links to the owning track and identifies which claims it supports.
