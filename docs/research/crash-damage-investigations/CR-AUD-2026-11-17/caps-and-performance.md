# Collision, damage and aftermath limits

**Source baseline:** `a7de9b35676d891bc2034cc344b910d9e1ef9c61`, 2026-11-17 audit. This is an inventory of selected limits on collision, damage and their presentation; it is not a material-strength specification or a performance qualification. The authoritative simulation uses float64 vehicle state and vehicle-specific contact paths. The pooled Godot rigid bodies, chips, sounds and marks described below are disposable presentation; they do not feed their transforms or energy back into flight dynamics. Ground-vehicle damage is a separate gameplay sidecar, not a contact force law.

“Clipped” means a value is changed or an item omitted to meet a bound. “Refused” means the named API returns an error/failure; whether that becomes a paused tick depends on the caller. A declared operating domain is not necessarily enforced. Limits and thresholds below need the stated boundary and saturation tests before anyone can claim coverage of the full fleet.

## Shared clock and contact work

| Limit and owner | Behavior at the boundary | Consequence and required check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The project specifies **240 physics ticks/s** and **12 maximum catch-up physics steps/frame**, or 0.05 s of nominal tick time in one frame ([project.godot](../../../../app/project.godot#L44)). | Work is bounded; below about 20 rendered frames/s the project's own comment says simulation falls behind wall time. `Simulation.dt()` can use an explicitly fixed timestep, so 240 Hz is the project/session default, not a universal constant for every test ([simulation.gd](../../../../app/sim/simulation.gd#L361)). | A low-render-rate effect test must measure simulation time and rendered time separately; neither a nominal tick rate nor a passing headless test proves real-time performance. |
| Shared `Simulation` accepts a model-declared **integer 1–16 substeps per tick** ([simulation.gd](../../../../app/sim/simulation.gd#L544)). | Out-of-range/noninteger requests fail the step, restore the last valid state, set a fault and pause; they are not silently clamped ([failure path](../../../../app/sim/simulation.gd#L955)). An incompatible whole-tick step policy with more than one declared substep is also refused. | Exercise 0, 1, 16, 17 and malformed values; assert unchanged accepted state and explicit fault. Record whether a vehicle requests refinement rather than assuming every vehicle uses 16 pieces. |
| Aircraft landing-gear data has its own **maximum 8 substeps** derived from the heave-mode rule `omega * dt / substeps < 0.1` ([ground_contact.gd](../../../../app/physics/ground_contact.gd#L45), [aircraft_data.gd](../../../../app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L669)). | Loader refuses an installation that needs more than eight rather than clipping its requested count. This is separate from the kernel's 16-substep ceiling. | Test accepted and refused stiffness/mass combinations around the derived boundary at the actual configured timestep; verify flight data and affected starts are not partially installed. |
| The opt-in fixed-hub ground operator admits **1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 pieces**, up to **16 contact IDs**, and at most **1,024** QP iterations; its integrated-capacity alternative defaults to a **124 RHS-call** predictor budget ([aircraft_ground_step.gd](../../../../app/physics/aircraft_ground_step.gd#L28), [settings](../../../../app/physics/aircraft_ground_step.gd#L1097)). | Invalid budgets are refused. Exhausted whole-piece certification/work or refinement returns explicit `admission_exhausted` or `error_control_exhausted`, rather than publishing the best unqualified result ([failure paths](../../../../app/physics/aircraft_ground_step.gd#L198)). The operator has explicit applicability and is not evidence that all stock aircraft use it. | Force each exhaustion and assert no partial publication; exercise 16/17 contacts and verify reported work/receipt and unchanged state. Refinement checks compare the same physical case and report the declared order and tolerances. |
| Graded multirotor ground impact refinement targets **0.004 m CG descent per piece**, capped at **16 pieces** only when its ground-approach gate activates ([controlled_plant.gd](../../../../app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd#L690), [constants](../../../../app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd#L848)). | `min(fine, 16)` clips the requested count. At 240 Hz, vertical CG descent above 15.36 m/s would require more than 16 pieces to retain the 4 mm target; e.g. 30 m/s corresponds to about 7.8 mm per piece if this gate applies. This arithmetic is not an observed crash result or a flight-speed limit. | Sweep around the activation and 16-piece boundary, including tilted/sloping impacts; compare penetration, impact identity, post-impact energy and angular rate with a finer independent reference. Report saturation rather than declaring 4 mm coverage after clipping. |
| Ground vehicle allows at most **8 wall contact boxes** per road sample; malformed or larger arrays return failure ([vehicle_dynamics.gd](../../../../app/physics/ground_vehicle/vehicle_dynamics.gd#L44), [validation](../../../../app/physics/ground_vehicle/vehicle_dynamics.gd#L930)). Its wall integration requests pieces of at most **1/120 s**; wet surfaces use **1/480 s** ([step selection](../../../../app/physics/ground_vehicle/vehicle_dynamics.gd#L490)). | The wall-count boundary refuses the query; it does not drop the ninth box. The piece selections are different laws from the aircraft gear and shared-kernel substep ceilings. | Test 8/9 walls and simultaneous wheel/hull contacts; confirm the outer session propagates a failure without partially advancing. Compare wet, dry and wall cases against a finer timestep. |

## Authoritative collision and damage domains

| Path | Bound or approximation | Implication and verification gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Airplane/scenery | The world broad phase uses **32 m cells**; a sweep subdivides rotation using a nominal **2° maximum angle** and chord padding ([scenery_collision.gd](../../../../app/sim/scenery_collision.gd#L12), [sweep](../../../../app/sim/scenery_collision.gd#L73)). Valid response classes use estimated restitution **0.35 foliage, 0.20 frangible, 0.25 light airframe** and terminal normal speeds **22, 12, 14 m/s**, respectively; hard/unknown class is terminal ([classification](../../../../app/sim/scenery_collision.gd#L423)). | These are gameplay response parameters, not measured break strengths. The response falls back to zero restitution if requested restitution would increase kinetic energy, and returns failure if energy still increases ([response](../../../../app/sim/scenery_collision.gd#L295)). The lower-level `contacts()` returns an empty array for malformed inputs as well as a miss ([entry](../../../../app/sim/scenery_collision.gd#L62)); upstream validation must distinguish those cases. Test thin geometry, large rotations, grazing, simultaneous candidates, ordering and invalid inputs against independent swept geometry. |
| Helicopter | Terrain/body sweep spacing **0.05 m**, maximum **8,192 steps**, and **24** refinement iterations. Exceeding the body sweep budget returns an explicit invalid result ([impact_detector.gd](../../../../app/physics/rotorcraft/impact_detector.gd#L13), [sweep plan](../../../../app/physics/rotorcraft/impact_detector.gd#L306)). Rotor-scene contacts use **32 full-disc spokes** above the declared **20 rad/s** threshold, with padding; overlapping candidate onset intervals are refused as unresolved ([rotor path](../../../../app/physics/rotorcraft/impact_detector.gd#L818), [arbitration](../../../../app/physics/rotorcraft/impact_detector.gd#L1031)). | Sampling and conservative witnesses are not exact swept blade geometry. Test terrain, blade, boom and scenery candidates at budget edges and onset ties, including high angular rates; verify refusal reaches the session without an ambiguous accepted event. |
| Multirotor | The graded frame obstacle path checks the **endpoint and midpoint**, with frame support estimated using clearance/normal at its box center ([controlled_plant.gd](../../../../app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd#L977)). Prop obstacles are checked at the accepted pose ([prop path](../../../../app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd#L1024)). Graded frame terminal thresholds are **8 m/s** for hard/unknown, **10 m/s** for frangible/gate, **11 m/s** for light airframe and **12 m/s** for foliage; they are stated gameplay estimates ([thresholds](../../../../app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd#L869)). | These thresholds are not a 10 m/s continuous-collision guarantee. A sufficiently thin obstacle can lie between sampled poses. Test clear-endpoint crossings at multiple speeds, orientations, obstacle widths and rotor phases against an independent sweep; separately check threshold equality and the 16-piece saturation case. |
| Airship | Source documents its envelope witness sweep as **admitted below 10 m/s**. It inflates envelope samples by estimated one-tick witness travel; appendages remain discrete witnesses and sufficiently thin proxies can fall between them ([session.gd](../../../../app/sim/airship/session.gd#L332)). | This is a stated domain, not a universal simulator speed cap or an observed enforced rejection. Compare just below/at/above 10 m/s, including rotation, thin wires and missed appendage witnesses against a finer geometric reference; report conservative early contacts and missed contacts separately. |
| Lake and bank | Near the surface, the swept query rejects a horizontal segment over **2,000 m** and an interval needing over **8,192 subdivisions**; bisection uses at most **48 iterations** ([lake_surface.gd](../../../../app/physics/lake_surface.gd#L172), [budget](../../../../app/physics/lake_surface.gd#L241)). An airborne early-out precedes the 2,000 m guard. | These are bounded-query refusals, not a generic speed cap; callers must propagate invalid results without converting them into misses. Exercise shoreline, waves, tangencies and subdivision limits with independent geometry and explicit failure assertions. |
| Scenery damage | Profile thresholds are estimated **8–25 J**, with separate broken versus marked profile sets ([scenery_damage.gd](../../../../app/sim/scenery_damage.gd#L1)). | They are discrete gameplay outcomes, not measured structural failure data. Test just below, at and above each threshold, invalid energy, shared IDs and reset; separate actual collision response from scenery display state. |
| Ground-vehicle damage sidecar | Per-episode and last-impact values saturate at **2,000 J**; contact energy is additionally limited to **10,000 W × dt**. The sidecar requires **0.4 m/s** contact speed and **0.05 m/s** speed drop, and applies damage only above **2 J** episode energy ([vehicle_damage.gd](../../../../app/physics/ground_vehicle/vehicle_damage.gd#L3), [advance](../../../../app/physics/ground_vehicle/vehicle_damage.gd#L83), [increments](../../../../app/physics/ground_vehicle/vehicle_damage.gd#L441)). | Damage fractions clamp to `[0, 1]`; they do not feed forces into the truck solver. Test each threshold and saturation with controlled before/after states, duration and timestep refinement; distinguish omitted sidecar damage from a missed collision or a solver energy violation. |

## Fixed-wing wreck presentation

The following apply to the shared airplane wreck, not to every vehicle. Helicopter, multirotor and ground-vehicle aftermaths have distinct paths. All values are bounded presentation choices, not a validated fragmentation model.

| Limit | Behavior, risk and gate |
| --- | --- |
| **24 pooled bodies**, each with CCD enabled and at most **4 reported contacts** ([wreck.gd](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L550)). Group descriptors are resized to **23 total**, including the core, when over budget ([group cap](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L840)). | This currently permits at most 22 recorded non-core groups, despite 24 body slots. Excess groups are truncated, not rejected; observe which visual nodes remain attached and whether their collision proxy covers them. Test 22/23/24+ non-core groups, deterministic selection, omitted-group visibility and reset. Pool size alone does not establish full part coverage. |
| Input aircraft mass clipped to **0.1–80 kg**, each section has **0.015 kg minimum**, box edge has **0.035 m minimum** ([mass and speed](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L256), [section mass](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L925), [shape](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L1365)). | Floors and clipping support engine stability but distort small parts and extreme models. At low total mass with many floor-mass sections, test whether the core floor plus shares can exceed the requested mass; the aggregate inventory accounting and the active-body mass at every release are different assertions. The synthetic 4 kg C4 probe found only 3 kg active before the 1 kg frozen/hidden part released at 40 ms; the section was deliberately large and is not fleet prevalence. |
| Input translation **100 m/s**, body point translation **120 m/s**, angular speed **30 rad/s**, and scatter increment **14 m/s** ([constants](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L12), [launch](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L882)). | Inputs are finite-sanitized and clipped, not a flight-speed limit. Record frequency of each saturation per vehicle and compare trajectories on either side; do not use a capped wreck trajectory to infer actual impact speed. |
| Scatter budget is at most **12%** of the selected normal-energy estimate and **600 J**; the estimate is itself capped at **1,000,000 J**. C0–C2 receive no scatter ([budget](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L1128), [application](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L897)). | The reported increment is a bound on the implemented scatter addition, not proof that total fragment kinetic energy or momentum is physically conserved. Test zero/nonzero scatter, low masses, cap intersections and final all-parts totals; compare pre/post linear and angular momentum and energy with an explicitly stated external-impulse assumption. |
| Scheduled section delay is estimated from **15 m/s propagation** and clipped to **0.02–0.35 s** for contact-based delays; matching struck components can release immediately, while no-contact cases use kind-specific delays ([constants](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L23), [scheduling](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L329)). | These are staged visual timings, not a material stress-wave speed. Test equal-distance ordering, clamped extremes, pause/resume and frame-rate dependence. Audit mass, COM and proxy coverage both before and after each delayed release. |
| A secondary event is ignored below **0.35 J**, clipped at **250 J**, and followed by a **0.08 s global lockout**. C3 exposure accumulates to **1,000 J** and selects one later section by provisional **4/12/25/50 J** thresholds ([limits](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L32), [callback](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L1388)). | This is a relative-kinetic-energy **index**, not measured dissipated energy; overlapping contacts can be suppressed and the callback explicitly excludes scenery bodies. Exercise rapid contacts, multiple bodies, scenery versus ground, threshold equality, repeated kinds, reset and one-release-per-event ordering. |
| Air damping is **1.5 s⁻¹ linear** and **2.0 s⁻¹ angular**; launch-time ground push-out is capped at **0.35 m** and upward rebound at **5 m/s** ([damping note](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L145), [limiter](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L1373)). | Source explicitly calls the air damping too strong and retains it because prior lower-damping box tests tunnelled or failed to settle. It should not be presented as measured aerodynamic drag. Test matched free-flight decay, ground drops, bounce/slide distance and penetration across shape, surface, speed and timestep; do not lower damping without replacing the lost stability evidence. |
| Wreck ground uses the impact surface's estimated friction and restitution tables, e.g. grass **0.45 / 0.05** and concrete **0.60 / 0.22**, with unknown fallback **0.45 / 0.10** ([severity.gd](../../../../app/render/crash/severity.gd#L39)). Scenery bodies use one **0.7 friction / 0.04 bounce** material ([scenery_bodies.gd](../../../../app/render/landscape_upgrade/scenery_bodies.gd#L115)). | These are estimates rather than a measured aircraft-construction-by-surface material matrix. Surface ordering in a single drop/slide test does not calibrate coefficients, and the fixed `y=0` wreck plane does not follow terrain ([plane](../../../../app/render/crash/wreck.gd#L550)). Record surface changes during travel, slopes, heights and secondary obstacles; compare independently measured drop/slide data where available. |

## Presentation selection and effect budgets

- Scenery wreck proxies use a **65 m per-axis horizontal range plus proxy margin**, then sort accepted candidates by distance and ID and retain at most **160**. Foliage and already-broken damage IDs are skipped, and `omitted` reports candidates beyond the cap ([selection](../../../../app/render/landscape_upgrade/scenery_bodies.gd#L87)). This is not a 65 m spherical coverage guarantee. The director builds **16 initially**, then **24 per update**; because `update()` continues after `_start()`, the same update can add the first 24, for up to 40 when enough candidates exist ([director](../../../../app/render/crash/director.gd#L144), [start](../../../../app/render/crash/director.gd#L247)). These bodies are built on the initial scenery-triggered branch. Test 159/160/161 candidates, ID ties, broken proxies, dense fences, different frame rates, fast fragments reaching not-yet-built shapes, and ground-first crashes near scenery. Track both omitted count and actual creation frame.
- Effects allocate **160** splinter instances, **16** marks, and at most **8** supported decals in the world-level pool; the primary mark remains in slot zero and later marks cycle through the other 15 ([effects.gd](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd#L5), [marks](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd#L404)). Eight particle emitters reserve **700 particle slots** in total; active emissions depend on outcome ([allocation](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd#L91)). The formula for a single C4 yields at most **144** splinters at severity 1 before the 160 cap; the multirotor wrapper further clips its crash chips to **24** ([count](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd#L383), [quad wrapper](../../../../app/vehicles/multirotor/quad_crash_fx.gd#L138)). Count allocations, visible instances, expiry and repeated reset; test that lowering visual quality or disabling effects does not alter accepted simulation state.
- Effects clamp each supplied update delta to **0.05 s**, stop chip updates at effect age **12 s**, and use presentation estimates for burn and vent timing: kerosene at most **50 s**, gasoline **40 s**, LiPo vent starts after **5–30 s** and lasts **20 s** ([constants](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd#L26), [update](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd#L474)). The audit's direct-update probe supplied the same one second as 60 × 1/60 s versus 10 × 0.1 s and observed effect ages **1.0 vs 0.5 s**. That is an input-partition result, not a measured 10 fps wall-clock run. Verify clocks and visual pacing at controlled render rates and stalls, including pause and resume.
- The same probe supplied an impact at `y=10 m`: effect origin/dust were `y=0.04 m` and chips spawned between `0.191` and `0.735 m`. `Effects.start()` fixes the origin height and chip contact is a `y=0.012` plane ([start](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd#L299), [chip integration](../../../../app/render/crash/effects.gd#L499)); ground marks can sample terrain separately. Verify elevated wall, sloped ground and lake-bank events through the production director, with captures and positions in a common world frame.
- Airplane severity uses provisional class cuts **0.5, 2.5, 25 and 150 J/kg**, caps abrasion contribution at **C1**, and clips normalized severity/component damage to **0–1**. Its cumulative energy and cumulative specific-energy fields are each numerically capped at **1e12** in their respective units ([severity.gd](../../../../app/render/crash/severity.gd#L4), [accumulation](../../../../app/render/crash/severity.gd#L255)). These are presentation grades and exposure indices, not material limits or remaining energy. Test monotonicity and plateau at each boundary with separately checked normal, tangent and slapdown channels.

The source-specific findings and synthetic 18-check/12-failure probe receipt are recorded in the accompanying verification evidence. Directly emitted body signals test the callback, not solver-generated contact; synthetic shape-center checks do not by themselves demonstrate a rendered collision. The immediate no-scatter C3 split changed represented linear momentum by **6 kg·m/s** in the constructed fixture without a physics frame. This is a visual-physics handoff invariant, not a measurement of a real crash or of the authoritative flight kernel.

## Performance and fidelity qualification gates

1. **Establish baselines and budgets before optimization.** On an otherwise idle target machine, record exact source, engine version, backend, OS, CPU/GPU, driver, power mode, display, graphics preset, vsync, warm/cold state and scenario. Measure complete frame time and missed-frame distribution plus CPU/GPU attribution, p50/p95/p99 and worst impact-frame hitches, memory and retained object counts. Separate loading/prewarm, first collision, repeated collisions, dense scenery creation, sustained debris, settled aftermath and reset. Agree hardware-specific budgets from these baselines; none of the constants above supplies one.
2. **Qualify 1920×1080 first**, including representative low- and high-quality settings and near/distant crash cameras. Then test 1440p and 4K where supported; **1280×720 is the last fallback target**, not proof of 1080p readiness. Compare matched workloads and seeds, with competing GPU/CPU jobs excluded. Preserve raw logs, captures and hardware identity.
3. **Exercise saturation deliberately.** For each cap use below/at/above values and report observed clipping, omission or explicit refusal, including 16 substeps, 8 truck walls, helicopter/lake sweep budgets, 23 recorded wreck groups, section mass floors, velocity/energy caps, the 160-proxy limit, first-update staging and the effects pools. Verify deterministic tie ordering, valid failure propagation, reset, pause and no unbounded allocation. A bound that always passes by omitting content needs a separate fidelity result.
4. **Preserve complete vehicle coverage.** Cover fixed-wing, helicopter, multirotor, ground vehicle and airship using each vehicle's actual collision and aftermath route: ground, slopes, thin/rotating obstacles, simultaneous contacts, foliage/frangible/hard materials and water where supported. Include the airship's documented below-10 m/s sweep domain and explicit probes outside it; do not generalize that number to helicopter or quad sweeps. Include dense-scene and high-speed/high-angular-rate cases representative of actual vehicle configurations.
5. **Keep verification and physical validation separate.** Check state identity and replay parity across graphics settings, before/after accepted ticks, failures and restart. Use independent analytic or finer-resolution geometry, convergence, momentum/energy and timing oracles for software correctness. Use independent, configuration-matched drop/slide/impact measurements with uncertainty for physical fidelity; provisional thresholds, visual preference and self-consistency are not measured strength. Rendered captures and human review qualify visible alignment and readability; headless checks do not measure target GPU frame cost or perceptual quality.
