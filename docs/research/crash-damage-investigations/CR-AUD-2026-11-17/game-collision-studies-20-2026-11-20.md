# Twenty game studies for OpenRC collision realism — 20 November 2026

**Result:** twenty additional game/simulator investigations, completed as a first round of ten followed by a second round selected from its gaps. Eighteen entries inspect pinned public game code; BeamNG.drive and Teardown use first-party public authoring/API documentation. PicaSim's available source has noncommercial terms. Supporting engines, documentation pages and individual examples do not count as extra games. These are **new game studies**, separate from the [previous twenty general investigations](crash-inspiration-20-2026-11-19.md).

The work integrates evidence and bounded experiments into [Crash Realism §11](../../../plans/crash-realism.md#11-game-source-integration-two-rounds-of-ten--20-november-2026), using existing GP/CR/SM/AUD owners. **No runtime physics, damage threshold, aircraft data, visual asset or performance setting is changed.** No investigated game was executed; this is source/documentation research, not a claim about measured gameplay or a completed physics repair.

The [source index](game-collision-sources-2026-11-20.json) supplies all twenty game identities, exact revisions, per-file Git blob identities, source links, licensing observations, accepted/rejected transfers and future experiments. [Provenance](game-collision-provenance-2026-11-20.json) records the sequential selection, OpenRC comparison, review corrections and documentation verification.

## Selection and evidence method

The first round was chosen to cover complementary mechanisms: RC component sensing, deformable vehicles, graph-based authoring, wheels, contact feedback, relative-point collision response, material destruction, damaged-state/wreck handoff, fragment identity and secondary-event cost. Popularity alone was not a selection criterion. For code examples, inspect the real caller, configuration and downstream consumer where material; a function name or dormant implementation is insufficient evidence.

The first ten were frozen at **12:37:46 UTC on 20 November 2026**, before dispatching the second round. The transition record preserves their pins, draft hashes and the questions that drove the next selections. It is a research-process receipt, not an independently timestamped publication. Round two deliberately targets the remaining uncertainty:

| Gap after round one | Follow-up games | What the follow-up needed to establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rotating parts, moving support and transient water were not qualified | FlightGear | Actual query inputs and coverage; distinguish rim samples, disc envelopes and blades |
| Important debris support and remaining impact time needed scrutiny | DOOM 3; Warzone 2100 | Where time is discarded; whether fragments rest or simply disappear |
| Human/articulated contact and local identity were absent | Overgrowth | Body-to-owner callback path, region loss and cue policy |
| Scripted part presentation and practical emitter timing needed examples | Beyond All Reason; Veloren; Endless Sky | Active configuration, engine/version uncertainty, births, expiry and velocity policy |
| Finite sources, material memory and causal secondary events remained unclear | Mindustry; The Powder Toy; VoxeLibre | Source refresh, residual states, material-query roles and provenance loss |

The second round is better targeted, not assumed to contain better physics. Negative findings are useful: sampled rotor contact is not swept blade collision; a cap on one emitter is not a scene cap; a few animated bounces are not a support solution. No source in these twenty studies supplies a validated universal RC clearance, penetration, RPM or water-skimming limit.

## Round one — ten complementary mechanisms

### G01 — PicaSim: Separate component sensing from contact response

**Evidence:** pinned public game source [5b2c5871b82a](https://github.com/Rowlhouse/PicaSim/tree/5b2c5871b82aecae4ae6293b862f31600a6a949c); source reading only. **Existing owners:** E3d2, E3d3, GP-10, GP-14, GP-16, GP-17, CR-20. **Extends prior research:** 01, 05, 08, 13.

**Observed.** PicaSim builds a physical aircraft hull and separate no-response crash sensors. Its sensor callback reports overlap; the post-physics consumer can set a propeller crash flag, which the engine then uses to stop its propeller behavior. Wheel collapse has a separate suspension-force path. This is useful evidence of distinct component channels, but the overlap callback does not return a contact point, normal, blade phase or impulse. Sources: [G01-S02](https://github.com/Rowlhouse/PicaSim/blob/5b2c5871b82aecae4ae6293b862f31600a6a949c/source/PicaSim/AeroplanePhysics.cpp#L301-L337), [G01-S05](https://github.com/Rowlhouse/PicaSim/blob/5b2c5871b82aecae4ae6293b862f31600a6a949c/source/PicaSim/AeroplanePhysics.cpp#L915-L950), [G01-S06](https://github.com/Rowlhouse/PicaSim/blob/5b2c5871b82aecae4ae6293b862f31600a6a949c/source/PicaSim/AeroplanePhysics.cpp#L989-L1045), [G01-S07](https://github.com/Rowlhouse/PicaSim/blob/5b2c5871b82aecae4ae6293b862f31600a6a949c/source/PicaSim/PropellerEngine.cpp#L295-L304), [G01-S08](https://github.com/Rowlhouse/PicaSim/blob/5b2c5871b82aecae4ae6293b862f31600a6a949c/source/PicaSim/Wheel.cpp#L150-L158).

**OpenRC increment.** Give an accepted wingtip, propeller, wheel or skid observation an explicit component/capability identity before selecting feedback or damage. A wingtip scrape could produce a short local rub and scuff while the aircraft continues; a propeller observation must go through the supported blade/shaft response instead of reusing hull severity. Preserve current E3d1 continuation and E3d2 behavior. The new increment is better evidence and qualification of intermediate states, not another set of crash sensors.

**Do not transfer.** Do not copy an any-overlap failure rule or assume low RPM, shallow overlap or an engine-off state makes contact harmless. A coarse sensor is a candidate, not a physical witness. PicaSim is source-available under noncommercial terms, not a blanket open-source reuse grant.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Use a 3-by-3 matrix of clearance/contact depth and stopped/coasting/powered shaft states. Compare a candidate sensor with independently known geometry. Report local blade velocity only when the witness, frame and signed shaft state exist; otherwise record unavailable. Require clear controls to stay free of contact damage/cues.

### G02 — Rigs of Rods: Distinguish sliding, elastic response, permanent set and breakage

**Evidence:** pinned public game source [ebf31a2187bb](https://github.com/RigsOfRods/rigs-of-rods/tree/ebf31a2187bbc31a38331a86b233dfa7e60c52fb); source reading only. **Existing owners:** GP-11, GP-12, GP-16, GP-17, GP-19, CR-07. **Extends prior research:** 02, 03, 07, 08, 13.

**Observed.** Rigs of Rods separates normal reaction from tangential slip/adhesion in primitiveCollision. Its beam path distinguishes compression and tension yield, changes rest length during plastic response, and has a separate break decision with connectivity guards. These are inspectable mechanisms behind intermediate outcomes; they are not measured RC component properties. Sources: [G02-S03](https://github.com/RigsOfRods/rigs-of-rods/blob/ebf31a2187bbc31a38331a86b233dfa7e60c52fb/source/main/physics/collision/Collisions.cpp#L1257-L1336), [G02-S04](https://github.com/RigsOfRods/rigs-of-rods/blob/ebf31a2187bbc31a38331a86b233dfa7e60c52fb/source/main/physics/ActorForcesEuler.cpp#L1317-L1378), [G02-S05](https://github.com/RigsOfRods/rigs-of-rods/blob/ebf31a2187bbc31a38331a86b233dfa7e60c52fb/source/main/physics/ActorForcesEuler.cpp#L1380-L1437).

**OpenRC increment.** Extend one qualified GP-16/17 component trial with separate reversible deflection, permanent set and failed-attached states. Make a sustained scrape read through local friction cues and damage evidence, rather than treating speed as an instant breakup trigger. Instrument load direction, slip, recoverable energy and separately justified dissipative work so a bent gear leg and a lightly rubbed wingtip have different histories. Keep the existing compliant support and traction foundations.

**Do not transfer.** Do not transplant the high-node-count beam solver, material constants or time-step-dependent reaction formula. GP-19 endpoint friction power is not accumulated work. A passive diagnostic can be required to leave physics unchanged; disabling actual damage cannot be required to do so.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Run matched compression, tension and tangential-load histories on one authored component, including unload/reload. Keep an observation-only baseline. Predeclare tolerances for energy accounting and state transitions; calculate accepted work through the owning integrator, not a sum of endpoint power samples presented as exact work.

### G03 — BeamNG.drive: Use typed attachment groups and inspect unintended propagation

**Evidence:** first-party public documentation; proprietary implementation not inspected. **Existing owners:** CR-06, CR-07, GP-16, GP-17. **Extends prior research:** 06, 07, 08, 10.

**Observed.** BeamNG’s first-party JBeam documentation distinguishes deformation from breaking and provides trigger/follower break-group behavior. Its authoring guidance warns about costly deformation-trigger checks, poor node/beam choices and unstable mass/stiffness combinations. The debug documentation exposes stress, deformation and group views. This investigation reads public authoring contracts, not BeamNG’s proprietary solver. Sources: [G03-S01](https://documentation.beamng.com/modding/vehicle/sections/beams/), [G03-S02](https://documentation.beamng.com/modding/vehicle/intro_jbeam/jbeamtips/), [G03-S03](https://documentation.beamng.com/modding/vehicle/intro_jbeam/common_issues/), [G03-S04](https://documentation.beamng.com/modding/vehicle/intro_jbeam/debugtools/).

**OpenRC increment.** Add a small authored graph validator and debug view to the existing attachment work: identify a root load-bearing connection, a follower cover or fairing, and the explicit states that can change. A cover may peel after its supporting connection fails without making the opposite wing detach. Use sparse callbacks only where the visual or physical consumer needs them. Construction-specific failure evidence remains with CR-07 and the aircraft generators.

**Do not transfer.** Do not interpret graph connectivity as a force law, import BeamNG thresholds, or replace OpenRC’s flight kernel. A real asymmetric impact can move and load the entire aircraft; the mirror control should forbid spurious group-trigger damage, not legitimate transmitted force.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Validate a small left/right wing graph with one follower cover, one alternative connection and one deliberately misnamed group. Trigger one connection in isolation and inspect the separated sets; then use a separate physical fixture to distinguish genuine transmitted loads from group leakage.

### G04 — VDrift: Verify that a fracture path is active before learning from it

**Evidence:** pinned public game source [f4767dd0eb98](https://github.com/VDrift/vdrift/tree/f4767dd0eb98f55a61d64c8c1462e254e0a23fa6); source reading only. **Existing owners:** CR-09, GP-18, CR-AUD-06, CR-AUD-07, CR-AUD-08. **Extends prior research:** 08, 09, 10.

**Observed.** VDrift has wheel contact constraints, tangential contact-drag code and fracture-related callbacks. Crucially, the fracture callback body is guarded by BT_BULLET_VERSION < 281 while the same repository’s compilation guide requires Bullet 2.83 or newer. The inspected child-release code uses point velocity at the child-body origin and sets its angular velocity to zero. Neither the presence of this code nor that handoff proves a currently active, conserved fracture system. Sources: [G04-S01](https://github.com/VDrift/vdrift/blob/f4767dd0eb98f55a61d64c8c1462e254e0a23fa6/src/physics/cardynamics.cpp#L1311-L1369), [G04-S02](https://github.com/VDrift/vdrift/blob/f4767dd0eb98f55a61d64c8c1462e254e0a23fa6/src/physics/cardynamics.cpp#L1378-L1404), [G04-S05](https://github.com/VDrift/vdrift/blob/f4767dd0eb98f55a61d64c8c1462e254e0a23fa6/src/physics/dynamicsworld.cpp#L236-L286), [G04-S06](https://github.com/VDrift/vdrift/blob/f4767dd0eb98f55a61d64c8c1462e254e0a23fa6/src/physics/fracturebody.cpp#L121-L190), [G04-S07](https://github.com/VDrift/vdrift/blob/f4767dd0eb98f55a61d64c8c1462e254e0a23fa6/src/physics/cardynamics.cpp#L1213-L1228), [G04-S08](https://github.com/VDrift/vdrift/blob/f4767dd0eb98f55a61d64c8c1462e254e0a23fa6/docs/Compiling.md#L103).

**OpenRC increment.** Require an implementation receipt that traces configuration, build guard, caller and consumer for each proposed contact capability. For OpenRC release, inspect parent plus pending and active sections in one declared frame; retire the old wheel/aerodynamic/structural contribution when an authoritative component actually detaches. Use CR-AUD-06/07/08 to repair visual delayed-release accounting before more spectacular breakup.

**Do not transfer.** Do not port the inactive fracture path or call OpenRC’s existing inventory COM and bounded scatter a fully conserved atomic handoff. Current presentation clamps and delayed release are the diagnostic baseline. Decorative release timing must not delay real mass loss.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Trace one asymmetric delayed visual release and one separately supported authoritative detachment. Account for mass, COM, linear/angular momentum, external forces, damping, caps and explicit scatter immediately before/after each release. Record current discrepancies; only the repaired candidate is expected to satisfy its declared conservation contract.

### G05 — SuperTuxKart: Make grazing feedback local without suppressing real recontacts

**Evidence:** pinned public game source [b2a9f2233264](https://github.com/supertuxkart/stk-code/tree/b2a9f223326422aa2dd4eea28319b0c5115fde03); source reading only. **Existing owners:** GP-14, GP-19, CR-02c, CR-03c, AUD-11, AUD-15, AUD-16. **Extends prior research:** 02, 13, 19, 20.

**Observed.** SuperTuxKart queues and deduplicates selected collision pairs for later processing, but kart-track material callbacks also occur directly in solveGroup. Rear-wheel contact drives localized material effects. Its crash audio uses a tick guard and a speed measure that is not a general normal-closing-speed criterion. The source therefore demonstrates useful separation and locality, with limits on what the deduplication and severity policy mean. Sources: [G05-S01](https://github.com/supertuxkart/stk-code/blob/b2a9f223326422aa2dd4eea28319b0c5115fde03/src/physics/physics.cpp#L176), [G05-S02](https://github.com/supertuxkart/stk-code/blob/b2a9f223326422aa2dd4eea28319b0c5115fde03/src/physics/physics.hpp#L43), [G05-S03](https://github.com/supertuxkart/stk-code/blob/b2a9f223326422aa2dd4eea28319b0c5115fde03/src/karts/kart.cpp#L2062), [G05-S04](https://github.com/supertuxkart/stk-code/blob/b2a9f223326422aa2dd4eea28319b0c5115fde03/src/karts/kart_gfx.cpp#L391).

**OpenRC increment.** Build on GP-19’s existing paced scrape grains. Use material, contact patch, component and episode identity to place a grass brush, runway rub or rough-ground chatter at the actual feature. Coalesce true duplicate presentation requests, while retaining two simultaneous patches, distinct impact/tear layers and a later release/recontact. This should make a near accident audible and visible without requiring a terminal crash.

**Do not transfer.** Do not merge physical contacts just because they share an object pair, impose one cue per entire incident, or use total speed alone to trigger fatality. A cooldown that hides repeated solver callbacks can also hide a genuine second impact.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Replay a scrape crossing a material seam, two simultaneous wing/gear patches, and a brief release followed by recontact. Count identified onsets, paced grains and teardowns separately. Vary rendering and audio settings; accepted contact state and causal IDs must agree while cosmetic density may differ.

### G06 — Pioneer: Use local relative motion to distinguish a graze from a direct hit

**Evidence:** pinned public game source [f03f979f58cb](https://github.com/pioneerspacesim/pioneer/tree/f03f979f58cb3acad6c4e3187f44074732fec8bc); source reading only. **Existing owners:** E3d1, E3d3, GP-10, GP-14, GP-19, AFC-23. **Extends prior research:** 01, 02, 03, 13, 19.

**Observed.** Pioneer computes relative velocity at a contact point, including angular contributions, rejects separating contacts and applies a normal collision response. Its damage path can ignore a small collision while physical response continues. Its additional slide damping scales the whole linear velocity; the source itself does not present this as properly projected tangential friction. Damage constants and inertia treatment are game approximations. Sources: [G06-S01](https://github.com/pioneerspacesim/pioneer/blob/f03f979f58cb3acad6c4e3187f44074732fec8bc/src/Space.cpp#L903-L960), [G06-S02](https://github.com/pioneerspacesim/pioneer/blob/f03f979f58cb3acad6c4e3187f44074732fec8bc/src/Space.cpp#L962-L1027), [G06-S04](https://github.com/pioneerspacesim/pioneer/blob/f03f979f58cb3acad6c4e3187f44074732fec8bc/src/DynamicBody.cpp#L312-L335), [G06-S05](https://github.com/pioneerspacesim/pioneer/blob/f03f979f58cb3acad6c4e3187f44074732fec8bc/src/Ship.cpp#L554-L603), [G06-S06](https://github.com/pioneerspacesim/pioneer/blob/f03f979f58cb3acad6c4e3187f44074732fec8bc/src/Ship.cpp#L609-L653).

**OpenRC increment.** Expose the local normal and tangential components already required by the contact contract. At the same aircraft speed, a glancing wingtip contact and a nose-first strike should produce different loads, cues and possible outcomes. Add moving-surface-relative diagnostics where the interface supports them. Preserve independent physical response, damage and terminal decisions; a non-damaging contact can still change motion.

**Do not transfer.** Do not copy whole-body velocity damping, scalar-inertia shortcuts or game damage units as joules. Current scenery/parked-aircraft proxies do not establish a two-moving-aircraft solver; mutual dynamic exchange remains coordinated with AFC-23.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Use matched speed with shallow and steep approach angles, plus matched point-relative velocities on supported stationary/moving fixtures. Compare normal/tangential diagnostics, angular response and continuation. Include a separating control and a high tangential-speed clear pass.

### G07 — OpenClonk: Give breakup a material-specific mixture and explicit birth order

**Evidence:** pinned public game source [36de799544af](https://github.com/openclonk/openclonk/tree/36de799544af0459014ea088867fe263779a8bdd); source reading only. **Existing owners:** CR-06, CR-07, CR-09, CR-22c, CR-22e. **Extends prior research:** 07, 09, 10, 13, 14, 20.

**Observed.** OpenClonk’s explosion path applies object blast, incinerates terrain, emits effects and then frees landscape material. That ordering matters: newly freed material is not in the original object-blast pass. Material definitions control loose pixels/objects, and the game selects different sound and particle cues by medium. These are game destruction and presentation rules, not an airframe fracture model. Sources: [G07-S01](https://github.com/openclonk/openclonk/blob/36de799544af0459014ea088867fe263779a8bdd/planet/Objects.ocd/Items.ocd/Tools.ocd/Dynamite.ocd/Script.c#L195), [G07-S02](https://github.com/openclonk/openclonk/blob/36de799544af0459014ea088867fe263779a8bdd/planet/System.ocg/Explode.c#L115), [G07-S03](https://github.com/openclonk/openclonk/blob/36de799544af0459014ea088867fe263779a8bdd/src/landscape/C4Landscape.cpp#L612), [G07-S05](https://github.com/openclonk/openclonk/blob/36de799544af0459014ea088867fe263779a8bdd/planet/Objects.ocd/System.ocg/Effects.c#L12).

**OpenRC increment.** Select bounded cosmetic recipes from the existing RC construction evidence: a few visible balsa splinters, film strips, foam chips or a compact dust puff, with a retained major silhouette. Carry a birth-generation/request identity so a newly spawned visual fragment does not receive the originating event twice. Use the proposed common lifecycle and budget as adapters around existing pools.

**Do not transfer.** Do not derive removed physical mass from a cosmetic particle ratio, add a second fire owner, or implement voxel terrain destruction to solve wingtip scraping. Existing CR-09e/f already supply splinters and interior/film details; the increment is better material allocation and event ordering.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Feed one admitted break outcome into three construction recipes with identical physical state. Track major part IDs, decorative birth IDs and requested/admitted/dropped counts. Verify that the original damage is committed once and that later genuine fragment impacts remain separately observable.

### G08 — OpenRA: Red Alert: Keep damage cues and wreck handoff coherent

**Evidence:** pinned public game source [4cc9a9f6299c](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/tree/4cc9a9f6299c807c373bb233d9729518c0160c77); source reading only. **Existing owners:** CR-09, CR-22b, CR-22d, SM-19, SM-20, AUD-15. **Extends prior research:** 08, 09, 15, 18, 19, 20.

**Observed.** The Red Alert game in OpenRA uses damage-condition-driven trails separately from death/husk creation. SpawnActorOnDeath checks eligibility before removal and creates the replacement after removal callbacks. ThrowsParticle, actually configured on tank husks, follows an authored visual arc rather than collision-resolved rigid-body motion. These separate paths make state and ownership transitions inspectable. Sources: [G08-S01](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/4cc9a9f6299c807c373bb233d9729518c0160c77/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/SpawnActorOnDeath.cs), [G08-S02](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/4cc9a9f6299c807c373bb233d9729518c0160c77/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/ThrowsParticle.cs), [G08-S03](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/4cc9a9f6299c807c373bb233d9729518c0160c77/mods/ra/rules/husks.yaml), [G08-S04](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/4cc9a9f6299c807c373bb233d9729518c0160c77/mods/ra/rules/aircraft.yaml), [G08-S05](https://github.com/OpenRA/OpenRA/blob/4cc9a9f6299c807c373bb233d9729518c0160c77/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/Render/LeavesTrails.cs).

**OpenRC increment.** For a qualified damaged component, allow a restrained local cue before terminal destruction; a scratched wing must not acquire engine smoke merely because an aggregate health tier changed. During terminal handoff, retire the old source and bind any continuing emitter/voice to the surviving part or an explicit world fallback. Extend existing isolated random seeds with a recorded cross-effect seed only if replay requires it.

**Do not transfer.** Do not use a scripted turret arc for an important aircraft fragment or infer structural failure from a visual health state. Deduplication means at-most-once for each identified onset/teardown; paced scrape grains and independent layers remain valid. Pooled engine-instance IDs may differ across presets.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Replay a local nonterminal cue, terminal handoff, source deletion, reset and re-entry after culling. Assert stable causal identities, no duplicate onset/teardown and no source restart. Compare accepted state across presets while allowing different particle allocations.

### G09 — Teardown: Separate intact detachment, material removal and cosmetic settling

**Evidence:** first-party public documentation; proprietary implementation not inspected. **Existing owners:** CR-06, CR-09, CR-19, CR-22c, CR-22e. **Extends prior research:** 06, 09, 12, 14, 20.

**Observed.** Teardown’s public API distinguishes broken shape material from a shape changing body ownership. It exposes material-sensitive hole sizes, particle sticking/collision controls and body activity. These are API contracts; the proprietary collision, fracture and smoke implementations were not inspected. Sources: [G09-S01](https://teardowngame.com/modding/api.html).

**OpenRC increment.** Keep an intact detached panel distinct from a chipped edge: topology changes need not produce dust or remove material. Use support-aware settling for small decorative chips and explicit representation tiers for important parts, chips and smoke. A brief collision grace period may be considered for a cosmetic particle born inside its emitter geometry, with no effect on structural contacts.

**Do not transfer.** Do not turn a voxel-hole API into an RC fracture law, disable important-part collision to escape a bad birth position, or freeze a body to conceal sliding. Sleeping needs a qualified support and wake/recontact policy.

**Proposed discriminating experiment.** Release an intact panel and a chipped tip beside a wall and on a slope. ## Combined design: make the intermediate outcomes readable

The selected ideas extend the outcome contract in [§9](../../../plans/crash-realism.md#9-recoverable-contact-and-near-misses--19-november-2026). Contact, damage, detachment and presentation are separate decisions. A light touch can be visible and audible while producing little or no permanent damage; a small-looking blade contact can still be serious when its local motion and component evidence justify it. The following is a **proposed direction**, not a newly delivered behavior or a scale calibrated from these games.

| Situation | Physical admission and continuation | Presentation direction | Main new game inputs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| True clear pass | No physical contact when qualified geometry remains clear; unresolved geometry is not silently called clear | Optional aerodynamic/wash cues only, with no contact scuff, impact sound or damage | G01, G06, G11, G12 |
| Vegetation brush | Distinguish authored flexible vegetation from a solid ground proxy; do not equate rendered grass with terrain penetration | Sparse local grass/dust and a brief brush, scaled by the supported observation | G05, G07, G20 |
| Wingtip or skid touch | Resolve local point-relative normal/tangential motion, duration and material through the existing owner | Local onset, paced rub, small scuff; stop continuous rubbing on release | G02, G05, G06, G13 |
| Recoverable local damage | Commit only supported component work/state; keep retained support/aerodynamics consistent | Permanent local crease/tear or altered part state with restrained sound; no generic smoke | G02, G03, G08 |
| Propeller/rotor brush | Distinguish exact blade witness, conservative rotating envelope and unsupported state; account for signed shaft motion/work | A localized tick/chip/vibration cue only where the admitted outcome supports it | G01, G04, G11 |
| Brief water contact | Explicit transient wet-feature model with entry/release/recontact, interface and flow kinematics, energy and recovery gates | Short localized spray/wake; not an automatic full crash plume | G11, G16, G20 |
| Partial or complete separation | Authoritative detachment owns accepted mass/inertia/resources; visual schedule is a separate consumer | Preserve major silhouette and correct local birth; material-specific small details | G03, G04, G07, G09, G14 |
| Wreck settling | Qualify collision proxy, support, COM and wake before reducing damping | Separate impact cues from quiet rest; cheap chips may expire without removing important bodies | G09, G12, G17 |
| Fuel/battery aftermath | Use existing evidence and explicit estimates; no visual effect authorizes blast, thermal damage or charges | Finite source, optional delayed sequence, residual smoke and persistent marks | G08, G15, G18, G19, G20 |

At **300 km/h**, translation is about **83.33 m/s**, or **0.3472 m per 240 Hz tick**. That arithmetic motivates continuous admission and interior-of-tick checks; it is not a new measured defect or a reason to increase every tick rate. A nominal one-millimetre clearance says little without the authored collision geometry, deformation, terrain/vegetation distinction and rotating-part envelope. If the qualified geometry is truly clear, speed alone must not manufacture a collision. If contact exists, total speed alone cannot determine severity. Local normal approach, tangential motion, angular/shaft motion, material and accepted duration/work matter; the physical model must supply their supported relationship.

### Five implementation slices inside the existing plans

1. **Contact evidence and identity.** Preserve known contact/time controls and add capability, patch/episode and causal identity only where consumers need them. Correct the existing geometric and remaining-time defects under E3d3/GP-08/10/14. Enforce real person/object attribution under CR-AUD-30. A data record cannot manufacture a missing witness, blade state or exact event fraction.
2. **Convincing nonfatal feedback.** Extend GP-19's admitted dry-hull observations with qualified local material cues and release. Keep scenery, wet and wreck adapters distinct. Coalesce only identical requests; preserve simultaneous patches, cue layers, repeated grains and reimpacts. Progressive physical damage remains GP-16/17/CR-07, not a consequence of choosing a sound.
3. **Birth and support before richer breakup.** Repair pending/active ownership and mass/COM/momentum diagnostics under CR-09 and CR-AUD-06/07/08. Qualify support/secondary contacts under CR-09d3/19 and CR-AUD-05/09–11. Then compare less damping, material recipes and optional chip aerodynamics. An important body keeps support, recontact/wake and an emission anchor even when render representation changes.
4. **One finite aftermath source and clock.** Extend prior investigation 18, coordinated by CR-22b/d and smoke owners. Correct the existing frame-delta/lifetime and fuel-sampling issues before richer sequences. A 200-ms frame stall currently advances crash-effect age only 50 ms through the existing clamp; live profile smoke has a different rebase contract. Define manual pause versus terminal freeze, culling, replay, finite residual emission, handoff and dropped birth debt explicitly. No expired source restarts or refuels itself.
5. **One combined presentation allocation.** Extend prior investigation 20 under CR-22c/e, GP-22, SM-21 and AUD/PT. Include bodies, particles, geometry, marks/decals, lights, voices, memory, queued births and deferred callbacks. Keep existing local caps as bounds, then qualify scene concurrency. These slices do not require full structural fracture before useful scuff/audio improvements. Wet continuation, general blade damage, physical attachment graphs and mutual dynamic vehicle contact remain separately declared capabilities. Current fixed-wing water terminal policy cannot be made realistic merely by changing a terminal boolean. Static scenery/person/parked-aircraft proxies do not prove dynamic aircraft-to-aircraft, helicopter, quad, blimp or car behavior; each vehicle adapter retains its own acceptance gates.

### Practical qualification and efficiency

Every proposed experiment above is **future work**. Begin with the smallest existing fixture that distinguishes current behavior from the proposed change, pin the source/config/input trace and predeclare tolerances. Reuse accepted recordings where possible; the baseline can expose a known failure and must not be mislabeled as already passing. Numerical checks do not establish construction strength, real water survival or human-impact realism.

Across matched effects-off/low/high runs, compare accepted flight state, incident/outcome identity and independent economics. Cosmetic counts and pool-instance IDs may differ. Use controlled seed inputs where required; wreck/effects/smoke already isolate randomness, and audio has an explicit seed override. A recorded shared presentation seed is an extension only if the replay contract needs it.

Measure no-contact overhead, cold first contact, sustained rubbing, breakup and aftermath separately. Record p50/p95/p99 and worst tick/frame, native physics/mixer/GPU work, transparent screen coverage, uploads/allocations, RAM/VRAM, active and pending work, overflow/expiry, and repeated-session growth. Source inspection does not prove any proposed optimization is faster. Exercise the actual renderer/capability fallback; do not assume a GPU API feature is available from the engine version alone.

Use **1920×1080 first** on ordinary targets, then higher resolutions, then 720p fallback. The reviewed current-source Apple Silicon Retina policy instead requires the native panel target; retain output, drawable and internal 3D dimensions under the [Apple performance plan](../../../plans/apple-silicon-performance.md). A ten-source synthetic event fixture can qualify a presentation budget component, not production fleet crash attachment or physical realism.
