## Turbo Timber reference parts

Horizon Hobby's [EFL-1249 updated landing-gear set](https://www.horizonhobby.com/product/e-flite-complete-updated-landing-gear-set-with-heavy-duty-springs-turbo-timber-evolution-1.5m/EFL-1249.html) was live at **$16.99, in stock**. The page identifies Turbo Timber Evolution 1.5m PNP and BNF compatibility and names heavy-duty springs, but it does not enumerate the box contents. The offer already exists as `timber_evolution_replacement_landing_gear_horizon_20261015` in [price-research-fleet.json](../../../../research/crash-economics/price-research-fleet.json).

The [EFL5258 Timber wheel set](https://www.horizonhobby.com/product/wheel-set-timber/EFL5258.html) was live at **$19.99, in stock**. Its product page says the set contains two 108 mm main wheels and one tailwheel; the Turbo Timber Evolution manual includes EFL5258 in its replacement-parts list. This offer already exists as `timber_evolution_wheel_set_efl5258_horizon_20261016` in the same price-research file. The modeled `gear_main` row includes main gear and wheels, while `gear_tail` includes the tailwheel and fork. EFL5258 spans both rows and does not include the fork, so allocate the set once only after a commercial-to-fictional crosswalk is accepted.

The existing [EFL105254 landing-gear listing](https://www.horizonhobby.com/product/landing-gear-turbo-timber-evolution-1.5m/EFL105254.html) is also $16.99 and in stock in its 2026-10-16 record. Its package contents are not itemized. Do not sum it with EFL-1249: both are $16.99, and the available sources do not establish whether their revisions or contents overlap. Likewise, EFL5268 springs may overlap EFL-1249, while the EFL105255 retainer listing is only two plates and mounting screws. The individual prices are useful references; they do not yet establish complete coverage for either modeled gear row. Keep `gear_main` and `gear_tail` unpriced.

## Avanti pneumatic gear and P100-RX tube

The physical Avanti inventory separates left and right pneumatic main retracts (each with leg, wheel and brake), a pneumatic steering nose assembly, and a pneumatic system containing tanks, valves and lines. SebArt's [2014 official price list](https://www.sebart.it/download/2014-09%20JET%20PRICELIST%20KRILL%20F.C.%20EUROPE%20final%20customer.pdf) names the historical A200-11 complete set—mechanics, aluminum legs/wheels, and air tanks—at **€1,000**. It also lists A200-11a mechanics and tanks at €290, A200-11b legs and wheels at €490, and A200-11c wheels at €225. These components total €1,005, so they are alternatives to the €1,000 bundle, not additive prices. The list is dated 2014 and does not establish a current offer or stock. It does not state brake contents; its separate shipping note is €170 from the factory to Italy.

The only current exact P100-RX tube listing found was IMI Hobby's [SebArt SA-A200-13 dual-wall exhaust pipe](https://w.iminitra.sk/jet-exhaust-pipes--vyfukove-potrubie-pre-turbinu/produkt/7693/510). On 2026-10-16 its page showed **€139 including 23% VAT (€113.01 net), no stock**. This is a foreign-currency, out-of-stock observation, not a current USD procurement offer. The page describes the pipe product but does not itemize extra mounting hardware. SebArt's 2026 list does not show A200-13; that omission alone does not establish discontinuation.

Current electric alternatives remain outside the pneumatic inventory. The already-catalogued [JP Hobby ER-150 set](https://www.tmhobby.com/jp-hobby-er-150-control-box-v1-v2-electric-retract-landing-gear-set-for-sebart-2-0m-avanti-s-2-1m-avanti-s-fc-airplane.html) was $780 and in stock on 2026-10-15. It is electric. The current official [SebArt 2026 price list](https://sebart.it/download/sebart-pricelist.pdf) lists a €650 electric set for an Avanti S 2.3 m configuration, with 20% VAT stated in the list but no stock or lead time. Neither price belongs on the authored 2.0 m pneumatic gear rows. SebArt's [assembly manual](https://www.sebartusa.com/uploads/1/4/3/1/143147227/avanti_s-_manual.pdf) says the pneumatic gear's dual air valve is not included, so even the historical A200-11 tank/mechanics description does not establish complete `pneumatic_system` coverage.

## Other fleet gear gaps

No exact current USD offer was verified for the P-51's left/right 2.82 m retract assemblies and retractable tailwheel. The inventory does not name a gear manufacturer or SKU, so smaller P-51 replacement parts and general retract units cannot be used as exact prices. The Great Planes .60 Extra inventory likewise lacks a current aircraft-specific replacement offer for its fixed main assembly or tailwheel. General 60-inch Extra gear is not treated as an exact Great Planes part.

Keep the Avanti's pneumatic gear, pneumatic system and P100-RX tube unpriced in USD; keep the P-51 and Great Planes gear rows unpriced until a source-backed part identity is available. Preserve the observed EUR prices in their original currency. Do not add component offers to the alternative electric Avanti ARF package.
