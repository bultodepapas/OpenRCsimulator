The Savox fit link comes from the SC0252MGP product page, which names `CSC0252MG` in its Spare Parts section. This explicit listing supports the SC0252MGP crosswalk; it does not establish compatibility with other Savox models. Its companion `SGSC0252MG` gear-set listing is marked Sold Out and was excluded from the candidate prices.

The two O.S. items are listed in the manufacturer MAX-55AX diagram by exact part number. Those links do not establish compatibility with the MAX-61FX, FS-64V, or any other engine family. Their seller pages identify the priced packages and retailer codes, but do not show an on-hand quantity. The gasket contents are not itemized.

Use a part price only after inspection identifies damage to that part and the exact installed model. These offers do not establish that a crashed component is repairable, and include no labor, inspection, repair likelihood, or salvage credit. Prices exclude tax and shipping. Keep each repair option separate from whole-unit replacement and do not add both for the same unit.

All three are now cataloged with validated service crosswalks and remain unallocated. The [CR-38b review](../CR-38b/README.md) flags both O.S. sale quotes for rechecking; their displayed comparison prices of $9.99 and $24.99 are retained in source notes, not treated as separate available offers. No promotion expiry or checkout stock was established.
