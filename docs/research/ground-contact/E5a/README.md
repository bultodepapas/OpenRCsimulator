# E5a — Extra taildragger contacts and P-51 review

2026-10-09 · **Status: software extension verified on Linux; independent pilot/aircraft contrast remains open.** Owning step: [ROADMAP E5a](../../../../ROADMAP.md). [Reproduction tools](../../../../research/ground-contact/e5a/README.md).

The Extra 300S now uses fixed main-wheel and rudder-linked tailwheel spring/damper contacts through the existing E1/E2 force law. Its wheel bottoms have been removed from the crash hull; body strikes and excessive gear compression still cause crashes. The change is generated by [EX-05](../../../../research/extra-300/ex05/derive_physics.py), with provenance in the generated [aircraft data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json) and [derivation](../../../../research/extra-300/ex05/derivation.md). No shared force/integration algorithm or P-51 data changes were needed.

The [contact report](contact-checks.json) verifies source-to-loader coordinates within 2 µm, force/moment balance and wheel support. The Extra settles within 0.000002° of the geometric pose, with a compression spread of approximately 0.02 µm. This verifies the generated model's internal consistency, not the real landing gear.

The existing P-51 also balances weight and moments. Its raw-longitudinal-arm spring split settles at 14.572°, about 0.680° above its 13.892° wheel-bottom pose. That existing compliant behavior is retained and explicitly bounded. The [existing P-51 ground suite](p51-existing.log) passes all 11 checks, including its takeoff and landing scenarios; its class-based performance bands are not independent validation of the Extra.

## Verification

The [focused runner report](verification/verification.json) records 21 contact assertions and 63 real-session assertions, plus a passing fresh-clone control and five deliberately rejected data/source/control defects. The detailed contact and session JSON reports add one file-write assertion each. Source hashes identify the exact inputs.

- **Signs:** positive yaw steers the aft wheel left and produces nose-right yaw; reversing the command mirrors the response. In a separate rightward-slip diagnostic, isolated main contacts ahead of the CG give destabilizing yaw, while the aft tail contact gives a restoring moment. This does not predict ground-loop onset or pilot correction requirements.
- **Bare-contact refinement:** at 240/480 Hz, two-second taxi trajectories differ by about 1.46 µm for the P-51 and 2.05 µm for the Extra in maximum position component. These tests use constant steering and omit aero/engine forces.
- **Real session:** four three-second Extra cases at 240/480/960 Hz retain three wheel contacts and finish without crash or simulation fault: engine-off coasting with left/right yaw, idle from rest and a throttle pulse. The 240 Hz coast headings are −29.122°/+29.122°. Idle creeps 24.4 mm; the pulse travels 1.897 m. Separate assertions require idle RPM to remain running and the pulse to increase RPM and displacement relative to idle at all three tick rates. These are [model outputs](session-final.json), not field targets.
- **Transient refinement:** an [initial 1 mm / 0.001 attitude-rate threshold](initial-refinement.log) failed the coasting/pulse comparisons. Adding 960 Hz shows the differences shrink approximately twofold; sampled servo/engine updates precede RK4. Final checks separately bound position, velocity, quaternion and rates, and require decreasing differences. Worst coarse-to-medium position difference is about 1.18 mm. No physics parameter was tuned to pass this check. [Final log](session-final.log).
- **Fault detection:** reversed tail steering fails the sign assertion; a 20 mm tail-contact displacement or missing source wheel diameter fails the geometry assertion; adding a wheel back to the crash hull fails all twelve real-session completion checks; suppressing the throttle pulse fails six RPM/displacement response checks. Mutations run in a disposable clone, after its unmodified control passes; engine errors cannot count as successful detection.
- **Airborne preservation:** only `landing_gear` and `crash_hull` differ in the generated Extra JSON. All 721 numeric rows of the three-second real-app trimmed flight match exactly before/after. [Check](preservation.json), [before trace](extra-before.csv.gz), [after trace](extra-after.csv.gz). Metadata correctly changes with the aircraft input hash.

The [full existing app suite](app-tests.log.gz) passes all 153 sections, including 125 GDScript test programs, goldens, model contracts and trace/frame-rate checks. After adding the E5a hook to `app/test.sh`, its exact command block was [executed separately and passed](app-hook.log); the entire suite was not repeated for that shell registration. [Validation and file identities](validation.json) distinguish both runs. The app linter reports zero errors and twelve existing warnings; the two new GDScript checks report zero errors/warnings. The full suite's normal data-provenance warnings are retained rather than described as a warning-free application.

## Scope and next evidence

This completes the Extra software slice of E5a. Starts remain airborne in the product; the parked poses here are test fixtures. No caster, brakes, stiction anchors, retracts, wheel-circle geometry or visual suspension/steering animation is added. Point contacts rotate with the airframe; E6a owns the true wheel-circle contact correction. Propwash remains absent from Extra physics.

For physical acceptance, obtain configuration-matched taxi/steering and ground-loop observations, including CG, tyre/surface conditions, linkage angles and control history. The existing P-51 pilot scripts and these tests do not validate every taildragger. Keep future aircraft behind their own generator, geometry and evidence checks; do not copy estimated stiffness or friction as measured data.
