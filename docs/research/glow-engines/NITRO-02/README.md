# NITRO-02 — Installed two-stroke and four-stroke candidates

2026-10-13. **Both engine families are implemented and playable as experimental installations. Physical/pilot and release acceptance remain open.** [Operating/configuration guide](../../../GLOW-ENGINES.md), [plan](../../../plans/RC_NITRO_ENGINE_REALISM_PLAN.md), [2T calibration dossier](../NITRO-2T/README.md), [4T calibration dossier](../NITRO-4T/README.md), [audio evidence](../NITRO-audio/README.md).

## Implemented and tested

- Separate ported 360° and valve/intake/pumping 720° mean-cycle kernels, independent maps and fuel accounting; no family-wide power or efficiency multiplier.
- Closed finite-fuel aircraft extension; manufacturer-constrained candidate engine profiles and two generated propeller combinations per family. Exact engine/installation bytes participate in identity and replay.
- RK-stage engine/propeller/body coupling; a passive annular propeller surrogate with an additional ideal-disk power bound for positive forward thrust; forward windmill breakaway, stopped drag and visible refusal of unsupported reverse rotation.
- Transport-driven mixture, servo/charge/fuel response, temperature feedback, opportunity/success counters, warm starter, cut, stall, overheating and exact nonnegative tank exhaustion.
- Fixed-body-datum variable mass/CG/full inertia with co-moving efflux; independent force/momentum fixtures, no phantom inertia-derivative torque.
- Separate catalog selections, stable Stik renderer adapter, mixture/cut/start controls, telemetry, versioned checkpoints and trace recording, procedural 2T/4T native sound. Audio timbre is authored, not measured; the [piston source-excitation observer](../NITRO-audio/vibration.md) supplies phase-aligned mechanical orders. Mounted vibration is unimplemented and unqualified.
- Propeller diameter drives the rendered blade/blur, aerodynamic load and inventory. Relative shaft inertia includes the full inventory propeller polar plus estimated crank inertia; changing props changes their acceleration and gyroscopic contribution.

The ground solver correctly refused stationary idle starts when calculated idle thrust exceeded the wheels' static hold. Glow runway starts therefore use a stopped warm engine; explicit starting releases the aircraft into ordinary force-driven rolling. The software test commands full throttle, then elevator after three seconds, and checks both installations climb. It does not fit or validate a real takeoff distance.

## Momentum-bound correction

The earlier 4T polar estimates were passive but physically inconsistent in static thrust. For the surrogate's quasi-steady forward inflow `V ≥ 0` and thrust `T > 0`, ideal momentum theory gives disk speed `Vd = (V + sqrt(V² + 2T/(ρA)))/2`, with `A = πD²/4`, and minimum shaft power `Pideal = T Vd`. This follows from the momentum/Bernoulli relations in [NASA's propeller-thrust derivation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/propeller-thrust/); the static form is `T^1.5/sqrt(2ρA)`. Real losses require additional power. `QΩ − TV ≥ 0` alone misses the static requirement. The bound does not model transient energy storage in a wake.

The prior FS-64V estimates at `K=0.06` supplied only 0.9719 and 0.9952 times ideal power for the assumed 14×6 and 13×6 blades. The corrected, explicitly estimated coefficient `K=0.075` gives 1.0831 and 1.1101. The 2T coefficients stay at 0.06, with ratios 1.2167 and 1.0977. The [independent runtime regression](../../../../app/tests/test_glow_propeller_momentum.gd) covers all four installations, RPM/density/forward-inflow samples, specific rejection of both old 4T settings, overflow, rest and windmilling. Runtime refuses impossible forward-thrust points within a `1e-10` relative power tolerance; it never clamps thrust or invents shaft power. The 5% candidate screening margin is separate from that numerical tolerance. Reverse inflow and nonpositive thrust are outside this particular ideal-disk test.

Both calibration reports now read the generated installation bytes and screen their inferred propeller predictions. The [trajectory change record](momentum-change.json) preserves all saved 2T states and telemetry identically across 18 runs; all 18 corresponding 4T runs change intentionally. The retained momentum run passed all 12 cases at both comparison rates; the current [refinement report](refinement.json) has since been regenerated for the hub-v2 law and current profile identities, also with all 12 passing. The blank pilot template was regenerated with the corrected identities and remains `not_performed`. Earlier optimization/export/performance records retain their original hashes and must not be presented as measurements of this correction. Passing a necessary ideal-power bound does not qualify induced-flow iteration, Reynolds/Mach behavior, real thrust maps or physical calibration.

## Hub inflow correction

The current `glow-mean-cycle-finite-tank-hub-inflow-v2` law samples axial blade inflow at the installed hub: `v_hub = v_datum + omega_body × r_hub`, with locally uniform wind. The data arm `[aft, right, up]` is converted to FRD before transport. The same arm sets the external thrust moment, so `F · v_hub = F · v_datum + (r_hub × F) · omega_body`. This is rigid-body point kinematics ([MIT lecture](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003j-dynamics-and-control-i-spring-2007/resources/lec07/)); fuel depletion moves the centre of mass without moving the fixed aircraft datum or hub.

The previous flight adapter sampled datum velocity even though thrust acted at an offset hub. Both the coupled flight and independent dynamics routes now use hub inflow. A zero-rate static or translating fixture retains its prior propeller loads, while pitching/yawing/rolling fixtures can change thrust, shaft load, cooling and thrust work. The [focused regression](../../../../app/tests/test_glow_hub_inflow.gd) passes 168 checks across all four installations, including a tilted synthetic hub and force/moment power closure. Stage reuse passes 2,005 comparisons after the correction. The v2 law is carried by checkpoint/trace identity; checkpoints carrying the previous glow law are incompatible. The earlier `*-momentum` exports, timing and integration records above predate this kinematic change and retain their original identities.

This correction assumes locally uniform wind and retains the existing mean axial-propeller/relative-shaft convention. It does not resolve blade-by-blade body-rate inflow, propeller normal force, P-factor, radial flow or a wake. Those require a consistent additional reduction and validation; merely enabling legacy coefficient tables would not establish that coverage.

## Interpretation and remaining gates

The manufacturer rated power/RPM points constrain both profiles. MAX-55AX APC12×7 additionally has a single-condition static RPM observation used for a **conditional** surrogate-chord fit; the alternate APC13×6 is an inference, not a held-out measurement. FS-64V uses its own manufacturer constraints and analogous four-stroke research, with inferred shape/transients/thermal/propeller coefficients. The two dossiers retain disagreements, assumptions and uncertainty. This implements the user's permission to infer missing parameters; it does not relabel estimates as measured calibration.

Changes from the dated NITRO-01 fixture include FS-64V low-RPM torque hypotheses, an inferred high-RPM unloading tail, rich-combustion zero-completion support, and explicit manufacturer recommended versus numerical supported maxima. Old source hashes and standalone evidence remain historical, not silently refreshed.

The [independent crank-angle oracle](cycle-oracle.md) supplies a second pressure/work hypothesis and checks conditional cycle averaging for both families. Its inferred pressure waveform is not measured truth or a numerical oracle for the runtime’s different work closure. Runtime high-frequency torque ripple remains unresolved; mounted vibration and propeller side-force/P-factor/wake modules remain unimplemented/refused and unqualified. These gaps, full propeller data coverage, matched real-engine transients/flow/temperature, flight observations and experienced-pilot trials remain unclosed. The corrected native measurement still exceeds the plan's 500 µs target (see `performance-momentum.json` above). Neither family is certified physically validated or release-qualified by these software checks.
