# NITRO-01 — Standalone profiles and independent 2T/4T mean-cycle kernels

2026-10-13 · **Status: software foundation; candidate profiles are inferred, not physically validated.** [Plan and remaining steps](../../../plans/RC_NITRO_ENGINE_REALISM_PLAN.md); [interface, equations, generator and reproduction commands](../../../../research/glow-engines/README.md).

## Implemented boundary

Both families have pure float64 implementations under `app/physics/glow/`. The two-stroke computes delivered/trapped charge and scavenging work per revolution; the four-stroke computes independent intake work, pumping and valve loss over two revolutions. Gross premix, burned fuel, unburned discharge, indicated/brake power and nominal/successful firing cadence have explicit semantics. No manufacturer constants or universal family advantages are embedded in the kernels.

The standalone loader rejects unknown/mismatched family fields, missing provenance, wrong units, boolean/nonfinite values, unordered tables, unsupported operating support and unrepresentable fuel blends. It hashes parsed bytes and detaches loaded state. Both O.S. candidates are regenerated by `build_profiles.py`; only their manufacturer-rated power/RPM knot is constrained. Unmeasured torque shape and charge/loss coefficients remain estimates, and inferred efficiency retains its estimated dependencies. Source results preserve candidate identity.

The thermal kernel partitions indicated work and combustion/mechanical heat into storage, exhaust, ambient exchange and effective premix evaporation. It is an independently tested head/case proxy, not an installed thermal model or an engine failure law. The coupled bench uses the existing G2 stage/body/shaft solve, shared propeller equations and complete Simulation checkpoints; it does not alter catalog flights.

## Additional primary performance evidence

Hageman and Wisniewski's [AIAA 2016-5011](https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2016-5011), [author-hosted full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/305632437_Development_And_Analysis_of_a_Group_1_UAV_Series_Hybrid_Power_System_with_Two_Engine_Options), tests MAX-55AX and the similar 9.32 cc O.S. FS-a56 using alternator-cradle torque, optical RPM and one-minute fuel samples. Figure 8 supplies speed/torque/power trends; its four-stroke examples are 80 W / 0.17 N·m and 415 W / 0.32 N·m. The 55AX coverage is part throttle, limited by the hybrid regulator. The FS-a56 is a proxy, not an FS-64V test. Tests used 78.2 kPa/291.3 K conditions, with controlled intake/exhaust on repeats.

Table 1's component densities, stoichiometric ratios and heating values support the candidate fuel blend: 72% methanol, 10% nitromethane and 18% castor oil by volume. The compiler derives mass fractions and a combustible-component AFR/LHV under an explicit inert-oil assumption. It does not equate an oil-reacting equivalence ratio with this basis or copy aggregate mixture ratios without normalization. This evidence supports research/inference; it does not identify the candidate WOT curve, idle/transition law or thermal coefficients. Full curve calibration remains open for each family.

## Verification

The retained [verification manifest](verification.json) binds source/input SHA-256 to the pinned Godot version, focused test summaries, disposable faults and paired refinement reports. Python and Godot reference cases are original synthetic mathematics; manufacturer power knots are reference constraints, not measurements made by this project.

The integrated native Windows run passes **1,210 GDScript checks and 15 Python checks**, six 240/480/960 Hz candidate benches and the unsupported-stop refusal. All 518 GDScripts also parsed in the initial isolated checkout; the integrated run reparses every new glow script/test. At 240 Hz, candidate mechanical-work closure errors are 0.000975 J (2T) and 0.001030 J (4T), falling to approximately 0.000060 and 0.000062 J at 960 Hz. Angular-momentum residuals stay below 1.4e-15 N·m·s. These are numerical fixture errors, not physical accuracy bands.

Proof covers:

- Separate analytic cycle-work, fuel mass/energy, loss and cadence answers, plus cross-language scalar oracle vectors for both families.
- Rich 2T reduced firing cadence matching a true 4T exhaust rate while retaining its different mechanical cycle and profile identity.
- Closed profile/refusal cases, volume-to-mass conversion, PS-to-watt conversion and no input mutation.
- Scalar heat balance, increased airflow cooling, warming below ambient, cooling after cut and energy-creation refusal.
- Stage-local propeller loads/relative-spin reaction, independent angular momentum and rotor/translation work, 240/480/960 Hz refinement, full checkpoint/replay, pause and atomic numerical-fault rollback.
- A passing unchanged disposable control and four rejected defects: incorrect 2T cycle angle, 4T opportunity rate, premix oil accounting and missing valve loss.

The first synthetic prop map violated `J*Ct <= Cp` and allowed thrust work to exceed absorbed shaft work. The corrected fixture has bounded energy-admissible coefficients and an independent propeller energy check. Algebraic rigid-body work closure alone did not establish propeller admissibility. No unqualified map is installed in an aircraft.

## Remaining scope

This increment is **not a playable engine installation**. Aircraft v2 capability/schema integration, actuator/mixture transport, cycle phase and crank-resolved ripple, automatic rich/lean instability, plug/thermal feedback, sustaining idle, start/stop/static friction, windmilling, finite tank and variable mass/CG/inertia, audio/vibration integration and aircraft/pilot calibration remain in the plan for **both** families.

The bench supplies fuel offboard/prescribed and records consumption; it does not reduce carrier mass. Candidate carrier/rotor inertias, propeller coefficients and short admission schedule are explicit synthetic inputs. A zero-RPM point returns no moving output, but the loss law is discontinuous there and no rest event is qualified. Unsupported negative-spin trials refuse the entire step and retain the last accepted state. Diagnostic wall time is not Gate P or target-machine acceptance. Both physical validation reports remain open.
