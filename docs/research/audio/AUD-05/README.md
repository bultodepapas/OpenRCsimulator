# AUD-05 — A living field

2026-10-11 · **Status: delivered; stereo-speaker feedback, gameplay capture, focused checks and frozen full regression passed.**

## Behaviour

Wildlife is sparse, spatial and condition-dependent. Nothing changes flight physics, weather, the shared RNG or visual scenery. [`wildlife.gd`](../../../../app/audio/wildlife.gd) owns a private seeded scheduler; the existing read-only adapter supplies sun elevation, wind and atmospheric temperature. [`soundscape.gd`](../../../../app/audio/soundscape.gd) owns mixing and fixed world positions.

| Layer | Authored behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Day birds | Ten whistle, trill and chatter phrases; ordinary intervals 8–19 seconds before environmental scaling. More activity near the horizon; suppressed at night. |
| Crows | Three rasping phrases, occasional daytime visits, 35–85 second intervals. |
| Owls | Three soft hoots, active after dark, 35–80 second intervals. |
| Dogs | Three distant bark phrases from two fixed off-field directions. Sparse 70–160 second daytime intervals; roughly 2.5 times slower at night. |
| Crickets | Two independently phased 17/19-second colonies in low grass near the pilot area. Fade in after dark, diminish below 18 °C, silent at or below 8 °C. |

The intervals are artistic targets, not biological measurements. Initial waits are randomized separately. Pitch, phrase, level and perch vary without repeating a species' previous phrase. A 22% reply opportunity lets another tree answer a bird/crow/owl; responses are cancelled when conditions become unsuitable. A 3–5 second inter-event gap and at most two occupied event slots avoid a constant chorus. High wind quiets wildlife; an audible aircraft passing within 35 m of a selected perch suppresses new tree calls for six seconds. Calls already underway finish naturally.

Day/night presence fades over four seconds. Aircraft foreground ducking, distance attenuation and speaker-oriented stereo panning remain active. Bird/crow/owl sources use field treeline positions; dogs and cricket patches use authored offsets from the field pilot origin. Those offsets are not surveyed habitats. Pause freezes scheduler/RNG and fades the whole mix; restart removes old one-shot tails and pending replies.

## Assets, licenses and compatibility

Fifteen new **original synthesized** sources join the earlier library: four bird phrases, three each of dog/crow/owl, and two cricket loops. These are recognizable sound-design approximations, not recordings or species-calibrated calls. [Generation and measurements](assets.md), [manifest](../../../../app/assets/audio/soundscape/wildlife/manifest.json), [MIT notice](../../../../app/assets/audio/soundscape/wildlife/LICENSE.txt). Retain the copyright and permission notice when distributing the assets; no separate credit-screen requirement or third-party sample attribution is added.

All sources are mono 16-bit PCM at 22.05 kHz, imported with compression disabled. The pinned Godot 4.7.2 runtime sets loop endpoints from the actual PCM frame count. No middleware or runtime synthesis is required. NumPy 2.4.6 is an offline generator dependency only. Earlier free recording/tool research and primary-source license links remain in [AUD-01](../AUD-01/sources.md); no external recordings were downloaded or incorporated for this extension.
