# AUD-15: landing-gear recordings

Retrieved 2026-10-20. All four Freesound pages identify the recordings as **CC0**. The bundled inputs are the public HQ MP3 previews, not the original uploads. Source MP3s, the deterministic importer, output manifest, and separate CC0 notice are retained with the project.

| Runtime asset | Source and use | Source excerpt and edit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hard_roll.wav` | FOSSarts, [skateboard rolling slow 2](https://freesound.org/people/FOSSarts/sounds/740125/), a slow rolling skateboard-wheel proxy for hard runway/rotorpad surfaces. The page does not identify the exact ground material. | 0.35–6.50 s; 0.55 s circular overlap; 55 Hz high-pass, 4.2 kHz low-pass; target RMS 0.025, peak cap 0.42. |
| `grass_roll.wav` | FOSSarts, [stepping on grass 1](https://freesound.org/people/FOSSarts/sounds/740144/), a quiet grass-contact texture proxy, not a recording of aircraft wheels. | 0.18–3.43 s; 0.42 s circular overlap; 80 Hz high-pass, 2.6 kHz low-pass; target RMS 0.012, peak cap 0.38. |
| `gravel_roll.wav` | alexftw123, [Wheel Spin on Gravel](https://freesound.org/people/alexftw123/sounds/152657/), a wheel spinning on gravel. | 0.28–2.13 s; 0.28 s circular overlap; 70 Hz high-pass, 4.8 kHz low-pass; target RMS 0.035, peak cap 0.42. |
| `touchdown.wav` | Grotelue, [Sailplane Landing](https://freesound.org/people/Grotelue/sounds/204737/), onboard touchdown and rollout recording. | 1.86–2.34 s; 28 Hz high-pass, 5.2 kHz low-pass, 4 ms fade-in, 55 ms fade-out; target RMS 0.105, peak cap 0.72. |

The Freesound pages describe the skateboard recording as a Magneto Mini Cruiser rolling slowly, recorded on a Zoom H1 Essential (96 kHz, 32-bit stereo, 7.315 s); the grass recording as heavier footsteps on grass, also recorded on a Zoom H1 Essential (96 kHz, 32-bit stereo, 3.625 s); the gravel recording as a car wheel spinning on gravel (44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo, 6.5 s); and the sailplane clip as a 12.408 s onboard landing recording (44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo). The touchdown crop selects its first strong impact transient. The grass and skateboard excerpts are intentionally material proxies; they are not presented as airplane recordings.

[`import_landing_gear.py`](../../../../tools/audio/import_landing_gear.py) decodes with pinned NumPy 2.4.6 and miniaudio 1.71 from [`requirements-recordings.txt`](../../../../tools/audio/requirements-recordings.txt), verifies source SHA-256 values, and regenerates the mono 22.05 kHz PCM16 clips and manifest. `--check` verifies exact regenerated bytes and uncompressed Godot import settings for the loops. The loop crossfade and measured seam steps are in `app/assets/audio/landing_gear/manifest.json`.
