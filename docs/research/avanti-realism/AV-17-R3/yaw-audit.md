# AV-17-R3 — Yaw derivative and fin audit

Reviewed 2026-10-14. This is a read-only physics audit of the Avanti A200 yaw path. The current working tree includes the R3 generator correction described below; no runtime aerodynamic equation was changed for that correction.

## Findings

The vertical-fin signs and control derivatives are consistent. The manufacturer’s high-rate rudder throw is ±30°, with normal/pattern dual rate 80% and expo 60%; at the endpoint expo preserves full stick, so the normal baseline is ±24° before trim. AV-17-R3’s separate short-stick gamepad scale of 0.5 makes that route ±12° before trim. Physical radios bypass simulator rates; ±24° requires setting the recommended 80% rate in the transmitter. The data’s positive rudder convention is trailing-edge left. `ControlSurfaces` converts the pilot’s trailing-edge-right positive yaw command to negative aerodynamic rudder; `_local_loads` then negates that data angle for the fin incidence. This produces the same nose-yaw direction as the global coefficients. The generator and loader enforce `CYdr = a_v Sv/S η_r`, `Cndr = −CYdr (x_v−x_ARP)/b`, and `Cnb = a_v Sv/S (x_v−x_ARP)/b`; current values are 0.320280, −0.098109, and +0.202344. The attached local fin moment about the CG also closes exactly against the global rudder moment after the `r×F` transfer: both give `Cndr_CG = −0.092717`. There is no rudder sign or fin-arm reversal in this path. The generator did have a yaw-rate moment reference mismatch. AV-06 defines `l_v = x_fin−x_CG` and `z_v = z_fin−z_CG`, then derives `Cnp` and `Cnr` with those CG arms. Runtime treats rate-moment coefficients as about the aerodynamic reference point, then transfers the rate side force from that point to the CG. With `r_x = x_CG−x_ARP = +0.033669 m` and `b = 2 m`, the runtime adds `CYp r_x/b = −0.002654` to yaw moment per roll-rate unit and `CYr r_x/b = +0.006438` per yaw-rate unit. Before correction, serialized `Cnp=+0.005968` and `Cnr=−0.135714` therefore became CG-effective `+0.003315` and `−0.129276`—about 44% of the small `Cnp` term and 4.7% of `|Cnr|` were lost to the second reference transfer. The R3 generator now serializes `Cnp_ARP=Cnp_CG−CYp r_x/b=+0.008622` and `Cnr_ARP=Cnr_CG−CYr r_x/b=−0.142153`; the runtime transfer then recovers the intended CG values. This is a coordinate correction, not a damping retune. [R3 generator correction](../../../../research/avanti-s/av06/derive_physics.py#L528-L540) · [current generated values](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L1691-L1712)

The selector makes the unscaled manufacturer-normal rudder case a relevant release check. At ±24°, the estimated fin control effectiveness 0.484864 gives 11.637° local incidence at zero sideslip/rate. The local-flow selector’s 8°–12° transition is already 0.977 local at that incidence, while the local tail polar begins its stall blend at 12°. Only another 0.363° of local flow in the additive direction reaches onset—equivalent, to first order, to about 18°/s yaw rate at 29 m/s or 28°/s at 45 m/s, using the estimated 0.579 m fin arm. By comparison, the current short-stick gamepad endpoint of ±12° gives 5.818° incidence and stays below the selector threshold in this zero-sideslip/rate case. The tail limits (8°, 12°, 24°) are provisional values copied from the Ugly Stik model, not an A200 fin polar. Pure ±24° command alone remains below the modeled stall onset, but modest sideslip or yaw rate can enter the nonlinear curve. That is a credible sensitivity to test against the release trace, not evidence that the A200 fin physically stalls at 12°. The manufacturer publishes neither its fin section nor a measured yaw response. No sign defect was found in angular acceleration or the stored inertia tensor. AV-06’s box-and-parallel-axis estimate is `J=[0.686737, 2.4642, 3.05824, 0, −0.0297665, 0] kg·m²`; runtime applies the full tensor through `J⁻¹(M−ω×Jω)`. The P100 turbine gyro is also present: its estimated rotor inertia gives about 0.56 N·m·s at 154,000 rpm, but pure yaw rate produces a pitch gyro moment; yaw gyro moment requires pitch rate. Rotor rotation sense is estimated and unverified. These estimates are not flight-identified inertia or gyro evidence. ## Follow-up

The integrated [Avanti regression](../../../../app/tests/test_avanti_yaw_flight.gd) now checks `Cnp_CG`/`Cnr_CG` by finite differences of global loads after ARP-to-CG transfer at 45 m/s. The [release matrix](README.md#measurements) records actual rudder angle, sideslip and yaw rate. An identification of local/blended derivatives, local selector weight and fin incidence against measured high-rudder flight remains future physical validation. The existing Stik yaw-damping test and these software checks are not Avanti flight validation. Do not move the 12°/24° tail limits without an A200-specific section/polar or matched flight evidence; the current model’s nonlinear fin response is estimated.
