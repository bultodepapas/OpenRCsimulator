# AV-14 propulsion and turbine audit

2026-10-12 · Read-only source audit · baseline aircraft: SebArt A200 with the **2017 JetCat P100-RX (non-BL)**.

## Result

The live forward-flight thrust path is dimensionally consistent with the selected adapted-nozzle momentum equation, and I found no demonstrated thrust-sign or unit defect in the current Avanti flight envelope. The main propulsion concern is evidential: ram-flow/jet-velocity corrections and several intermediate-RPM schedules are model-derived, while the tests verify those same equations rather than measured Avanti installed-engine behavior. Shutdown/fuel behavior is an acknowledged missing feature (AV-05b/AV-10). A turbine spool reaction moment is a plausible question, but this audit found no P100-RX evidence that justifies adding a standalone `-Hdot` load. This was a read-only source and literature audit; no tests, generator or Godot run was performed.

## Ranked findings

1. **No demonstrated forward-flight equation defect; ram-rise calibration is unvalidated.** [NASA Glenn's thrust equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/thrust-force/) gives the adapted-nozzle form `F = mdot·(Vjet − V0)`. [`Turbine.thrust`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L90) implements gross jet momentum less captured-air axial momentum; [`Turbine.loads`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L109) applies gross thrust on the nozzle axis and the captured-air momentum vector at the intake offset. Density scaling and units are coherent with the documented model. The optional `ram_flow` and `ram_jet` terms are **derived from a cycle model**, not P100 flight-speed measurements; the installed factor is estimated. The 70 m/s check in [`test_avanti_handling.gd`](../../../../app/tests/test_avanti_handling.gd#L47) asserts a broad 85–92% ratio around the same model, and [`test_turbine.gd`](../../../../app/tests/test_turbine.gd#L97) checks its algebra with a synthetic fixture. These checks establish implementation consistency, not physical agreement. Do not retune coefficients from them. The useful validation is an installed 2017 P100-RX thrust-stand sweep at fixed RPM and several axial speeds, with the actual side intakes and thrust tube; crossflow normal force needs a separate multi-axis load measurement.

2. **The P100 `governor_tau` is a local identified equivalent, not a universal measured time constant.** AV-06 stores `0.4 s` as `kind: measured` in [`inputs.json`](../../../../research/avanti-s/av06/inputs.json#L859). The cited P100-RX multi-sine experiment reports a throttle-to-thrust corner near 0.4 Hz, while its initial step-based estimate was near 1 Hz; it also notes response differences with excitation amplitude. For a first-order equivalent, `tau = 1/(2πfc) ≈ 0.398 s`, so the code's value is reasonable for that small-signal fit. Applying that throttle-to-thrust corner to the code's RPM lag assumes thrust is a memoryless RPM map around the fit point; the study does not establish a sole ECU/governor RPM pole or a single constant for idle-to-full steps. The repository's AV-05 labels allow “measured” to include fitted coefficients from an actual engine test, so the current kind is defensible; clarify the source as a **small-signal first-order equivalent derived from the measured corner**. Only reclassify it as `derived` if AV-14 adopts a stricter distinction between direct measurements and fitted/converted values. The acceleration/deceleration schedules are explicitly estimated and should remain so.

3. **Turbine spool mount reaction is unresolved; do not add `-Hdot` from rotor conservation alone.** `FlightSession._pre_step` advances turbine RPM into the auxiliary state; `Simulation.step` then evaluates loads using that new RPM and holds `rotor_momentum` constant through its RK stages ([`flight_session.gd`](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L1503), [`simulation.gd`](../../../../app/sim/simulation.gd#L172), [`rigid_body.gd`](../../../../app/physics/rigid_body.gd#L121)). The body equation includes gyroscopic `-ω×(Jω + H)` but no `Hdot`. The broad claim in [`turbine.gd`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L109) that spool torque “balances inside the engine” is not enough to establish the omitted mounting load during a transient. A fixed mechanical rotor uses an `I·ωdot` reaction term, and NACA TN 2091 describes the P100-independent rotor acceleration balance `I·ωdot = turbine torque − compressor torque`; neither source gives the external torque for this *open* jet engine. That balance must also include inlet/exhaust angular-momentum flux and transient gas storage. A primary NASA free-power-turbine component test reports rotor-exit flow angles and derives stator-exit whirl; it confirms angular momentum is part of turbine-stage flow accounting, but says nothing about a P100-RX final-nozzle residual or mount torque. No P100-RX nozzle-swirl or spool-ramp mount-torque measurement was found. The app's [`RotorCoupling`](../../../../app/physics/rotor_coupling.gd#L1) `-I·Omega_dot·axis` solve is for a mechanically coupled solid propeller/motor rotor, and [`FlightSession._supports_coupled_shaft`](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L68) explicitly rejects turbines. Treat the absent turbine term as an **unverified modeling limitation**, not a confirmed current-flight defect. Before implementing it, obtain a mounted P100-RX torque-vs-RPM-ramp measurement or a source that closes the angular-momentum balance for this engine/nozzle. If later justified, derive the interval reaction from the actual `ΔH/dt` used by the discrete spool step and verify timestep/checkpoint behavior; an endpoint `spool_rate` is not automatically the same increment.

4. **Engine shutdown and fuel depletion are not implemented in the current turbine lifecycle.** The plan records AV-05b (start/stop/fuel cut/flameout) and AV-10 (fuel burn) as pending. In [`FlightSession._pre_step`](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L1503), a turbine that is not `engine_running` falls through with RPM set to zero; this bypasses rundown, residual thrust, relight and ECU state. [`Turbine.fuel_flow`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L128) has no production caller, and aircraft mass/CG/inertia remain fixed. This is a known scope gap, not an unexpected change in the AV-07 in-air running start or the power-off glide start. A meaningful acceptance test belongs with AV-05b/AV-10: use a real `FlightSession` to cut fuel at idle and high RPM, verify finite gradual rundown and residual thrust, check fuel/mass/CG/inertia accounting, and prove deterministic reset/replay. Do not test only the helper in isolation.

5. **Low-RPM extrapolation has an unreachable-in-flight negative-flow edge.** [`mass_flow`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L82) extends the AV-06 first linear segment below its first point. From the stored 20,000/30,000 RPM values, that line crosses zero at about 6.7 RPM and is negative below that. The common cutoff is `< STOPPED_RPM` with `STOPPED_RPM == 1`, so exactly 1 RPM passes the cutoff and produces slightly negative flow; [`fuel_flow`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L128) similarly returns the table's idle endpoint below its first RPM. Normal flight keeps a running turbine at or above idle, and the stopped path sets exactly 0, so this is not currently reachable through the Avanti session. When AV-05b adds rundown/start states, define a supported low-speed map/cutoff rather than extending an idle-fit line to zero. A focused future test should assert nonnegative mass and fuel flow over the entire supported shutdown trajectory, including the cutoff boundary.

6. **Atmosphere is uniform at field density, not sampled at aircraft altitude.** [`FlightSession.air_density`](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L137) supplies weather/field density to [`_wash_loads`](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L1138), which passes it through `Dynamics.loads` → `Propulsion.loads` → `Turbine.loads`. [`WindField._apply_config`](../../../../app/physics/wind_field.gd#L75) stores one atmosphere density for the field, and `air_density()` returns it unchanged. The air-data comment estimates less than 5% density change below 500 m. This is an explicit low-altitude simplification; revisit for high-altitude/large-vertical-excursion flight and test with an altitude-varying atmosphere before changing the turbine map.

7. **One stale code comment contradicts implemented ram flow.** The header of [`turbine.gd`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L14) says ram rise of `mdot` is not modeled, but `mass_flow` applies `1 + ram_flow·u²` and AV-06 contains the derived coefficient. This has no runtime effect; update the comment with any later source cleanup so it describes the actual option and its cycle-model provenance.

## Source and consumer trace

- `FlightSession._wash_loads` reads `AUX_RPM`, field density, and stage air-relative velocity; `Dynamics.loads` calls `Dynamics._components_from_air`; that function calls `Propulsion.loads`; its turbine dispatch calls `Turbine.loads`. Turbine force and its moment arms therefore affect the actual Avanti body integration, not just the scalar thrust display.
- `FlightSession._pre_step` calls `Turbine.spool_step` only when `engine_running` is true. The Avanti in-air start is already running; the glide start begins stopped. There is no turbine-specific ECU state machine in the flight step.
- Rotor momentum travels `FlightSession.rotor_momentum` → `Dynamics.rotor_momentum` → `Turbine.rotor_momentum` → `RigidBody.derivative`; the latter includes gyroscopic precession, not shaft spin acceleration.
- `Turbine.fuel_flow` is declared “for telemetry and the endurance check,” but graph tracing found no callers. AV-10 is its planned consumer.
- `test_turbine.gd` covers static thrust, ram drag/recovery, half-density scaling, force/moment signs, spool schedule and timestep split with synthetic values. `test_avanti_handling.gd` checks model-derived envelopes and handling scenarios. The project plan says AV-11 independent contrast remains pending because no Avanti telemetry is available.

## Primary references and variant boundary

- JetCat, [2017 Lieferprogramm catalog](https://www.jetcat.de/jetcat/Kataloge/JetCat%20Lieferprogramm%202017_web.pdf): selected non-BL P100-RX baseline; the project source records 44,000/154,000 RPM, 2/100 N and 0.23 kg/s at catalog conditions.
- JetCat, [current P100-RX-BL product page](https://www.jetcat.de/en/productdetails/produkte/jetcat/produkte/RC%20ENGINES/Engines/p100_rx-bl): identifies the brushless-pump BL revision and ECU V12 pressure/temperature sensing. Its similar headline RPM/thrust/flow figures do not establish that its ECU transient matches the 2017 non-BL engine.
- Many et al., [System Identification of a Turbojet Engine using Multi-Sine Inputs in Ground Testing](https://www.dglr.de/publikationen/2022/550281.pdf), DLRK 2021: primary P100-RX ground-test study for transient identification, flow/thrust maps and the amplitude-dependent response evidence above. Its model identifies engine-specific component behavior; it does not provide P100 installed flight-speed thrust or mount torque.
- Momin et al., [Nonlinear Model Identification and Observer Design for Thrust Estimation of Small-scale Turbojet Engines](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.08330): P160-RXi-B and P220-RXi, not P100-RX; useful only as the explicitly borrowed thrust-curve shape evidence in AV-06.
- NASA Glenn, [Thrust Force](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/thrust-force/): control-volume thrust equation supporting gross thrust minus inlet momentum drag.
- NACA, [TN 2091](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930082903/downloads/19930082903.pdf): primary generic turbojet spool-dynamics source for the rotor torque imbalance/acceleration relation; it does not demonstrate a P100-RX mount reaction.
- Kofskey and Nusbaum, [NASA-TP-1007](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19780008110): primary free-power-turbine component test with measured rotor-exit flow angle and stator-exit whirl calculation. It establishes that turbine-stage angular-momentum flow is relevant generally, not the residual swirl or mount torque of a model turbojet.
- Yepifanov, [gas-turbine engine course text, ch. 7](https://dspace.library.khai.edu/xmlui/bitstream/handle/123456789/8649/Yepifanov_2021.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1), secondary context only: discusses generic casing torque cancellation for axial inlet/outlet and residual load with circumferential exit flow. It is not evidence of P100-RX casing balance.
