# AV-17-R2 dynamics audit

2026-10-14 · Read-only model audit of the SebArt Avanti S A200. The figures below describe the simulator inputs and equations; they are not flight validation.

## Findings

The baseline local wing model creates a real near-stall roll anti-damping band. At its lowest successful clean, calm-air level trim on a 0.05 m/s sweep (18.50 m/s, α=10.345°, elevator −4.997°), the integrated wing/tail aerodynamic coefficient is CL=0.76187 and the outer-strip separation weight is 0.1996. A central rate perturbation of p=±0.01 rad/s gives dMx/dp=+9.351 N·m/(rad/s), or Clp=+0.588. The positive sign is anti-damping; it falls to +0.288 at 18.6 m/s and returns to a stabilizing −0.348 by 19.0 m/s. At 29 m/s the model gives Clp=−0.606. This local sign reversal is consistent with a small roll disturbance growing while the twisted tips are separating. Exact symmetric pitch alone cannot generate roll in a symmetric model, so this result identifies an amplifier of an asymmetry, not by itself the initiating disturbance.

The current R2 candidate keeps the existing station incidence [−0.02, 0, +0.02] rad and adds the generated wing-polar model. On the same perturbation, Clp at 18.5 m/s changes from +0.588 to −0.197; at 18.6 m/s it changes from +0.288 to −0.192. It is slightly anti-damped at its lowest successful grid trim, 17.90 m/s (Clp=+0.050), and stabilizing by 18.00 m/s (−0.022). The candidate therefore removes the broad, strong anti-damping region in this static check without retuning the twist. Its outer-strip separation metric at 17.90 m/s is 0.449; the metric comes from a different separation-weight definition in the polar branch, so it should not be compared numerically with the baseline weight.

The declared clean CLmax=0.85 gives Vs=17.719 m/s from the standard no-thrust relation `sqrt(2 m g / (rho S CLmax))`. That value is the peak of the global scalar envelope: `AircraftData._envelope` solves `a1/a2` against the requested global peak, while `Aero._local_loads` applies the six-strip induced map, station incidence and optional wing polar to local loads; at high local-flow weight those loads determine the trim. The integrated aerodynamic CL at the maximum found in the clean level-trim sweep is 0.76187 baseline and 0.81178 candidate. Their no-thrust equivalent speeds are 18.716 and 18.131 m/s respectively, 5.63% and 2.33% above the declared value. The first successful trim-grid points are 18.50 and 17.90 m/s, with immediately preceding tested failures at 18.45 and 17.85 m/s. These grid boundaries are solver results, not exact measured stall speeds. At those floor trims the modelled thrust contributes normal-force coefficients of +0.01785 and +0.02109; aero plus thrust closes the required normal force to within 6×10⁻¹² coefficient units. This explains why the powered minimum-trim speed differs from the aerodynamic no-thrust equivalent.

The contract describes `envelope.CL_max/CL_min` as inputs that derive stall transitions and says the strip slope, lift offsets and induced map reproduce the global static oracle. It does not promise that the global scalar peak equals the aircraft's usable, elevator-trimmed local strip-plus-tail peak. Treat the difference above as a closure and data-semantics gap, not authority to retune global CLmax: first decide whether this aircraft field denotes the global reference curve or usable trimmed lift. The candidate also remains an estimate: its polar is generated from the hybrid NACA 0015, Re=1,000,000 data in the [Table 3 transcription](../../../../research/avanti-s/av17-r2/sandia_naca0015_transcription.json) using the [polar builder](../../../../research/avanti-s/av17-r2/build_polar.py), not an Avanti A200 wing test.

The rigid-body rate equation uses `J⁻¹(M − ω×(Jω+h_rotor))`; for the isolated pure-pitch case the recorded rotor term contributes yaw acceleration, not a direct roll moment. It may seed sideslip, but does not explain the measured roll anti-damping by itself. The RK4 implementation evaluates each later derivative at its corresponding intermediate state and time; I found no stage-state mismatch in this path.

## Method and evidence

The paired results are in [dynamics-trim-comparison.json](dynamics-trim-comparison.json), and the repeatable probe is [dynamics_probe.gd](../../../../research/avanti-s/av17-r2/dynamics_probe.gd). The probe calls `Trim.solve("level")` at sea-level density 1.225 kg/m³ and g=9.80665 m/s² over 16–24 m/s in 0.05 m/s increments, with clean controls and calm air. It projects `Aero.loads` normal to the relative flow; propulsion's normal component is computed separately from `Dynamics.evaluate`. Clp is a central finite difference of integrated roll moment at p=±0.01 rad/s, normalized by `qbar S b²/(2V)`. The candidate's gamepad pitch scale is an input mapping and is not applied by this static trim; its behavior is recorded in the [controller audit](controller-audit.md).

The baseline input is the saved pre-R2 aircraft JSON (SHA-256 `88d5d82552dc9d16b44a4e12bf1e344cdb5608d575f6e4f6383a4d3eeb6ef3a5`); the candidate input is the captured current Avanti JSON (SHA-256 `1ec44b95c3d43a0a31c820e8dbd83b942b9d691117c9f83f8d0a5a9583a51d09`). The run used Godot 4.7.2 headless in a separate `.tools/avanti-r2-audit/project` snapshot with current aircraft physics and schema files. It did not use the shared Godot import cache and changed no runtime or aircraft data. Source locations: [`aero.gd` strip flow and separated lift](../../../../app/physics/aero.gd#L347), [`aircraft_data.gd` envelope solver](../../../../app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L1408), [`aircraft_data.gd` polar loader](../../../../app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L1495), [`AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md`](../../../../AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md#L123), [`rigid_body.gd` rate equation](../../../../app/physics/rigid_body.gd#L77), and [`integrator.gd` RK4 stages](../../../../app/physics/integrator.gd#L30).
