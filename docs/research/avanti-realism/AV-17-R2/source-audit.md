# AV-17-R2 source audit: flight feel, speeds, and performance

2026-10-14 · Research only · Original SebArt Avanti S A200, 2.00 m span / 2.22 m length, JetCat P100-RX (non-BL, 2017)

## Scope and result

This audit concerns the original A200 wood-wing Avanti S Jet represented by the SebArt 2.2 m manual and the 2017 P100-RX setup. “2.2 m” is the manual's length class; the stated span is 2.00 m. Mini/EDF Avantis, the Avanti XS, and the later Krill full-composite version are excluded. The previous [AV-17-R1 manufacturer audit](../AV-17-R1/manufacturer-audit.md) already records the manual's geometry, control throws, flap settings, CG range, and 250 km/h test warning; this document focuses on additional source value and evidence gaps.

The strongest new numerical speed statement found is a 2014 post by SebArt RC USA's Jason M. on RCU: he gives an approximate P100 maximum of 140–160 mph (about 225–257 km/h) and says vertical performance is as expected for a roughly 23 lb dry model. He does not identify a sensor, test pass, air density, fuel state, turbine variant, thrust tube, or airframe condition. Treat it as a distributor's estimate based on reported customer/Sebastiano experience, not a measured speed envelope. The original SebArt manual remains the only primary source found that says the Avanti was tested to a speed: 250 km/h, with operation above that not recommended. Its test configuration and method are also unpublished. These two statements do not establish a normal top speed or validate a drag polar.

## Additional primary-source findings

| Evidence | Source and limits | What it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intended power/performance roles | SebArt's [A200 product page](https://www.sebart.it/jets-L.html) lists 200 cm span, 222 cm length, 10.5 kg dry with P100-RX, and recommends P100 for jet beginners, P140 for FAI F3S, and P180 for unlimited 3D. | Keep the P100 trainer/aerobatics role distinct from high-power 3D behavior. These are configuration recommendations, not performance curves. |
| Structural speed caution | The [SebArt 2.2 m manual](https://www.sebart.it/download/AVANTI%20S%20JET%202.2m-Manual%20Intro.pdf), p. 3, calls the airplane a light aerobatics/3D compromise, not a speed jet, and warns that its light structure and very large control surfaces make high speed unsafe due to flutter or structural failure. It says the model was tested to 250 km/h but gives no test details. | A caution or evidence threshold around 250 km/h is justified. The source does not justify a hard in-game speed clamp, a specific flutter onset, or added drag above that speed. |
| P100-RX engine ratings | JetCat's [2017 product catalogue](https://www.jetcat.de/jetcat/Kataloge/JetCat%20Lieferprogramm%202017_web.pdf) lists the non-BL P100-RX at 44,000 rpm idle / 154,000 rpm maximum, 2 / 100 N static thrust, 0.23 kg/s mass flow, 80 / 390 ml/min fuel use, and 1,565 km/h exhaust velocity. Values are engine ratings, not an Avanti installation test. | Preserve the rated engine data and distinguish nozzle exhaust velocity from airplane airspeed. The 100 N rating is static maximum thrust; it does not specify installed net thrust through the A200 inlet and pipe at forward speed. |
| Official demonstration material | SebArt's [product page](https://www.sebart.it/jets-L.html) links an A200 video labelled “in Hovering!” and separately identifies the P180 exhaust pipe as the vectoring option. The page does not publish synchronized telemetry or flight conditions for the video. | It documents the demonstrated 3D role qualitatively. It cannot be used to make a P100 aircraft hover or to calibrate its low-speed control power. |

SebArt publishes no A200 wing area, section coordinates/name, incidence, twist, lift curve, drag polar, CLmax, stall speed, neutral point, or numerical wing loading in the sources reviewed. The manual only describes wing loading as low. The 91-page official [A200 assembly photoinstruction](https://www.sebart.it/download/AVANTI%20S%20JET%202.2m-Photoinstriction.pdf) adds build steps, hardware dimensions, and installation photographs, but no numerical aerodynamic geometry or performance data. Therefore the [AV-06 inputs](../../../../research/avanti-s/av06/inputs.json) remain explicitly estimated where they assign 0.7023 m² area, a 12–15% symmetric-section thickness range, 0.85 CLmax, stall behavior, incidence, and drag. This search found no primary source that can replace those estimates. In particular, no A200 planform or airfoil specification was found that would justify changing area or stall coefficients.

The manual gives 250 mm nominal CG, 240 mm for jet beginners, and 260 mm for unlimited 3D at p. 5, but it does not state the fuel quantity at which those positions apply. No primary source found here resolves that fuel-state ambiguity. Do not import fuel-dependent CG advice from the later Krill or smaller variants into this A200 setup.

## Pilot reports and measured-flight evidence

The RCU thread [“Sebart Avanti S with K-100”](https://www.rcuniverse.com/forum/rc-jets-120/11592298-sebart-avanti-s-k-100-a.html) is useful only as attributed field testimony. The distributor reports that P100-powered owners found the combination worked well and gives the approximate speed range above. Other owners in the same thread describe different turbine sizes, weights, tanks, CGs, and landing experiences: one Merlin 140 owner reported canopy suction/collapse at full power and enlarged inlet openings, while another pilot's 240–250 mm CG accounts of approach and touchdown feel differ. These observations show that turbine installation, mass balance, radio setup, and landing technique matter; they do not isolate an aerodynamic coefficient or establish an A200-wide handling rule.

No publicly indexed GPS, pitot, Doppler, or race-timing record was found for a matching original A200 with a P100-RX and enough setup metadata to treat its speed as a reproducible measurement. SebArt's “tested to 250 km/h” is a manufacturer statement without a published data trace. The 140–160 mph distributor range is explicitly approximate, not instrumented evidence. Measured Freewing foam EDF and other Avanti family speeds are different aircraft/configurations and do not fill this gap.

## Simulator implications

- The published material supports reproducing the manual's role split and control/flap setup, already documented in AV-17-R1. It does not support tuning the A200's stall angle, CLmax, tip-first/root-first stall sequence, pitch damping, or roll response from the videos or forum comments.
- Use 250 km/h as a manufacturer-reported tested-speed reference and safety warning, not as a verified design limit or a target that every P100 setup must attain. Add no artificial drag rise, flutter trigger, or speed governor from that number alone.
- JetCat's maximum static thrust and mass flow can anchor the bare-engine model. They do not determine installed thrust lapse, inlet pressure recovery, thrust-tube loss, or airframe drag. AV-06's installed factor, ram-flow law, ram-jet law, and clean-airframe drag remain estimates; none is validated or invalidated by the sources above.
- A useful next physical dataset would identify A200 revision, turbine/pipe, actual all-up mass and fuel state, CG, radio rates, density/altitude, and synchronized GPS or pitot speed with throttle/RPM. To tune handling, add AoA or load-factor and control-input traces through the maneuver. Until that exists, changes to speed and handling coefficients would remain assumption-driven.
