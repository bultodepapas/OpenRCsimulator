# TT-22 Turbo Lumber parts and price audit

**Observed:** 2026-10-20. **Market:** Horizon Hobby U.S. storefront, USD. Prices exclude destination tax and shipping. Stock and backorder estimates are observations, not guarantees.

## Model and package identity

The closest authoritative commercial reference is the E-flite Turbo Timber Evolution 1.5 m, B revisions. The current BNF manual lists replacement SKUs including EFL-1249, EFL105255, EFL105258, EFL17553, EFL5258, EFL5259, EFL5268, EFL5962 and EFLA521; it identifies the BNF's installed SPM-1012 motor, SPMXAE70E ESC, six SPMSA370 servos and AR637TA receiver (SPMAR637T), and recommends a separate SPMX32004S30 4S 3200mAh 30C battery. [Current BNF manual](https://www.horizonhobby.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-horizon-master/default/Manuals/EFL105250B-Manual_EN.pdf)

As of this audit, the complete-aircraft alternatives are the [EFL105275B PNP at $299.99](https://www.horizonhobby.com/product/turbo-timber-evolution-1.5m-pnp-includes-floats/EFL105275B.html) and [EFL105250B BNF Basic at $349.99](https://www.horizonhobby.com/product/turbo-timber-evolution-1.5m-bnf-basic-includes-floats/EFL105250B.html), both listed in stock. PNP includes six servos, motor, 70A ESC, slats and floats, but needs a receiver, transmitter, battery and charger. BNF includes those aircraft items plus an AR637TA receiver; it still needs a transmitter, battery and charger. These are mutually exclusive whole-aircraft package prices; never add either package to standalone parts.

The aircraft dossier is `turbo-lumber-evolution`, a fictional modeled build. A matching E-flite SKU in the reference manual confirms compatibility with the reference Turbo Timber Evolution, not that the simulator's modeled item was built from that exact product. Keep prices marked as reference-part candidates or proxies until the modeled inventory proves the mapping.

## Cost and source-data findings

These findings describe the source state before TT-22. The [implemented corrections and verification](README.md) document their resolution; the dated observations remain historical evidence.

The pre-update research dossier's $465.87 subtotal is arithmetically consistent across seven priced physical rows: wing $59.99 + four $13.99 servos = $115.95; EFL5962 prop $7.99 + EFL17553 spinner $8.99 = $16.98; motor $74.99; ESC $74.99; receiver proxy $84.99; two tail servo proxies $27.98; battery proxy $69.99. The six servo units are allocated once (4 wing + 2 tail). The prop/spinner amount is a composite of two separately sold items, not one package. Do not automatically bill the spinner for a prop-only collision. The subtotal is partial replacement-part pricing, not a full aircraft valuation; five physical groups and assembly remain unpriced, and there is no verified foam repair recipe.

Three source-catalog details need correction or explicit supersession:

1. The current EFL5268 page reports **In Stock** at $4.99; the 2026-10-16 observation in `research/crash-economics/catalog.json` says availability was not stated. Refresh it if recording a 2026-10-20 offer.
2. The catalog has two same-date SPMX324S50 observations at $69.99: one says **In Stock**, the other **Backorder | October 2026**. The current U.S. listing still shows Backorder | October 2026, and the costing dossier points to the latter observation. Mark the contradictory older stock observation superseded or remove it rather than carry conflicting current availability.
3. The current B manual lists EFL-1249 for landing gear, while the older 2021 manual listed EFL105254. Both product pages currently exist at $16.99, but the sources do not establish that their package contents are interchangeable or additive. Preserve the revision difference and leave broad gear-group allocation unpriced.

The current manual's exact recommended SPMX32004S30 battery is **discontinued** on Horizon U.S. and has no current price on its product page. The SPMX324S50 proxy is the available current price source, not an exact priced recommendation.
