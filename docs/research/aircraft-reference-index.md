Status: source navigation checked 2026-10-12. Current aircraft behavior and open work live in the linked aircraft guides. These reports retain dated observations and provenance; a reference photo or borrowed manual does not establish flight-model validation. Third-party originals under root `references/` are local-only and ignored.

## Jensen Ugly Stik 60

The Jensen plan is the primary geometry source: 60 in span, 720 in² stated wing area and a .45–.61 engine range. [Current model/finish guide](../UGLY-STIK-PLAN.md), [Jensen source notes](ugly-stik-resources.md#planos-y-documentación-del-avión) and [reference manifest/hashes](ugly-stik-resources.json) distinguish the drawing, reconstructed geometry and open measurements. Original plans: local only, `references/ugly-stik/downloads/jensen/`.

The owner's gold-head O.S. MAX photographs and separate silencer inform visual anatomy; exact model identity and dimensions remain unverified. [Photo brief and hashes](ugly-stik-visual-photo-brief.md), [equipment interpretation](ugly-stik-model-v4-equipment.md). Originals: local only, `references/.61 ENGINE NITRO OS.png` and `references/.61 AND EXHAUST.png`.

## Great Planes Extra 300S .60

The selected airframe is GPMA0236, 64 in. [Current aircraft guide](../EXTRA-300-PLAN.md), [family comparison](extra-300-family-research.md), [source inspection](extra-300-resources.md), [download manifest](extra-300-resources.json), [integration audit](extra-300-integration-audit.md) and [round-2 references](extra-300-round2.md) preserve selection and limits. The [model/metrology report](extra-300-model-v1.md) is the original preview evidence; the current aircraft flies experimentally.

The .40 kit, full-size Extra 300L/330 and electric Extra 300 EXP are comparisons, not interchangeable geometry or coefficients. Original downloads/photo/gallery: local only, `references/extra-300/`. [Aircraft tooling investigations](extra-aircraft-tooling/README.md) retain CAD/rigging/material/inspection experiments; a tool survey does not imply adoption.

## Separate Ultra Stick 120 and MoJo references

The owner-supplied RHB/Horizon Ultra Stick 120 material differs from the Jensen in dimensions and equipment. [Package findings](ugly-stik-new-files.md), [CAD audit](ugly-stik-new-files-cad.md), [inventory hashes](../../research/ugly-stik/new-files/inventory.json) and [CAD metadata](../../research/ugly-stik/new-files/metadata.json) preserve it separately. Original package: local only, `references/ultra-stick-120/Ultrastick V3/`; the redraw/manual relationship remains unconfirmed.

Published Ultra Stick 25e/120 measurements (UMN flight identification, NASA FASER wind tunnel and flight spin) and other RC airframes' data are registered in [RC-class aerodynamic data sources](flight-dynamics/rc-aero-data-sources.md). Originals: local only, `references/rc-aero-data/`.

MoJo's drawing identity and geometry remain unknown; only signature, format, size and hash were examined. Use the same CAD audit/inventory; the filename does not establish span or engine class. Original: local only, `references/mojo-60/MoJo Parts 60.dwg`.

## SebArt Avanti S A200

The model uses the original A200 (200 cm span, 222 cm length) and a P100-RX referenced to the 2017 specification. It differs from Avanti XS/Mini, Freewing EDF and newer RX-BL equipment. [Current aircraft guide/plan](../AVANTI-S-PLAN.md), [family comparison](avanti-s-family-research.md), [integration audit](avanti-s-integration-audit.md), [turbine sources](avanti-s-turbine-research.md) and [resource inventory](avanti-s-resources.md) preserve those distinctions. No complete calibrated A200 airframe plan was found.

[AV-01 metrology](avanti-s-av01-metrology.md), [controls/installation](avanti-s-controls-and-installation.md), [original AV-02 preview](avanti-s-av02-preview.md) and [geometry follow-up](avanti-s-geometry-followup.md) retain the provisional reconstruction evidence. Current Avanti flies experimentally, gear/flaps up; the older preview reports describe their own date. Originals, component gallery and study boards: local only, `references/avanti-s/`.
