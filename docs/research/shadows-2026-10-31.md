### I01 — Which quality options have distinct native behavior?

**Finding:** The pinned Compatibility implementation groups filter enums into three shader branches: base lookup for Hard/Soft Very Low, five-sample PCF for Soft Low/Medium, and 13-sample PCF for Soft High/Ultra. Blur multipliers can still distinguish members of a group. In Forward+, Hard alone does not disable variable-penumbra PCSS when the source angle/size is nonzero. [GLES3 selection](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/drivers/gles3/rasterizer_scene_gles3.cpp#L3369-L3401), [quality definitions](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/RenderingServer.xml#L5219-L5236).

**Decision — reuse and test, S02/S03/S05.** Define menu tiers by demonstrated differences, not by copying every engine enum. Record renderer, filter enum, source angle and blur together.

**Smallest experiment:** At 1080p, compare adjacent filter settings at fixed atlas/camera/exposure/blur, retaining cold and warm timing. Test zero versus current solar angle separately and acknowledge I07's projection coupling. **Risk:** A “Sharp” option could accidentally retain PCSS; additional labels might cost implementation effort without a useful visual distinction. This is new implementation detail beyond the initial capability matrix.

### I02 — Can hybrid shadows be partitioned with masks?

**Finding:** `shadow_caster_mask` controls contributors to a light's shadow map, while `light_cull_mask` controls reception of the entire light. GI/fog do not follow that receiver mask. A material's receive-shadow switch suppresses all received shadows. These controls are not a general per-caster/per-receiver shadow matrix. [Pinned light masks](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/Light3D.xml#L68-L70), [caster mask](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/Light3D.xml#L118-L120), [material flag](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/BaseMaterial3D.xml#L194-L196).

**Decision — test the ownership contract, S01; defer a split-light workaround.** Two equal-direction suns with disjoint illuminated layers could isolate aircraft self-shadow from terrain lighting. However, excluding aircraft from the terrain light's casters also loses its shadow on buildings, and additional shadowed directional lights divide the atlas. [Pinned atlas allocation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/drivers/gles3/storage/light_storage.cpp#L1626-L1668).

**Smallest experiment:** Compare native-only, projected-only and an isolated hybrid with wing, runway, roof and an offscreen caster. Check clear-surface color and every receiver before cost. **Risk:** Preventing one duplicate ground shadow can remove legitimate occlusion elsewhere. Prefer a single accepted natural-shadow route unless the hybrid demonstrates a worthwhile benefit.

### I03 — Local-light atlases, fade and cache behavior

**Finding:** Positional atlas size and quadrant subdivisions are per viewport; zero size disables that viewport's positional shadows. Pinned GLES3 allocation considers screen coverage, has reallocation tolerance and tracks cached slot versions. Spot shadows need one projection; omni modes can need two paraboloid or six cube passes. A spotlight's angle is an angular radius, not the full cone. [Viewport API](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/Viewport.xml#L409-L426), [allocation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/drivers/gles3/storage/light_storage.cpp#L1350-L1409), [pass setup](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/servers/rendering/renderer_scene_cull.cpp#L2192-L2203), [spot angle](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/SpotLight3D.xml#L20-L22).

**Decision — reuse, S08.** Start with one useful spotlight and the engine's existing cache. Compare shadow-distance fade independently of lamp brightness; tune atlas subdivisions for the actual small selected-light budget.

**Smallest experiment:** Move a light/camera across a slot-size transition with one fixed obstacle and one moving caster. Record allocation, popping, pass counts and uninstrumented GPU distributions. **Risk:** A small atlas is not automatically cheap, and an apparently static lamp can still need frequent redraws. Do not add a second cache before finding a specific native-cache limitation; I05 supplies the custom-clock case.

### I04 — Has upstream repaired the rejected contact shadows?

**Finding:** [PR #124702](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/pull/124702), merged 2026-10-20, forces the Forward+ depth prepass for screen-space effects that otherwise consume previous-frame or empty depth. The [change](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/commit/37bf013abcf60e00fee4ce2503cf997d30f6986f) is not an established remedy for OpenRC's recorded wall bands with SSAO/prepass already active.

A bounded official GitHub history search for **2026-10-15 through 2026-10-31** returned no commits touching `screen_space_contact_shadows.glsl` or `ss_effects.cpp`. The [pinned contact shader](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/effects/screen_space_contact_shadows.glsl) remains the comparison reference. This extends the retained October 25 search cutoff; it does not exclude fixes in other files, branches or unmerged work.

**Decision — retain default-off, S05/GDU-11g.** This investigation corroborates the prior rejection rather than finding a new fix.

**Smallest experiment:** Once a specific change is relevant, compare matched builds with/without that change on the previous wall and contact fixtures, then moving cameras and screen edges. **Risk:** Taking any nearby “screen-space fix” as proof of repair would erase useful negative evidence. An engine change follows its own upgrade process.

### I05 — Animated casters, invisible proxies and custom clocks

**Finding:** `SHADOWS_ONLY` permits invisible native-map geometry, but such geometry cannot contribute to screen-space contact depth. Main-camera and current-pass view matrices also differ during shadow rendering; view-dependent deformation needs checking. [Casting modes](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/GeometryInstance3D.xml#L96-L109), [contact limits](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/ProjectSettings.xml#L3183-L3187), [shader matrix semantics](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/spatial_shader.html#vertex-built-ins).

The pin's GLES3 animated-material classification recognizes specific built-in `TIME` uses; a custom-clock uniform alone does not satisfy that check. Positional-shadow refresh uses this classification. Other invalidation paths may still refresh the map, so stale shadows are a **testable risk**, not an observed OpenRC defect. [Classification](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/drivers/gles3/storage/material_storage.cpp#L2982-L2990), [refresh path](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/servers/rendering/renderer_scene_cull.cpp#L2377-L2387).

**Decision — test, S04/S06/S08.** Preserve OpenRC's deterministic `sim_clock`; do not switch shaders to `TIME`.

**Smallest experiment:** Hold spotlight, camera and bounds fixed while advancing/pausing wind; compare normal behavior with an explicit refresh control. Check visible/native/projected poses, offscreen bounds and teardown. **Risk:** A proxy fixes map coverage without fixing contact rays, and an oversized bound can hide culling defects while adding work.

### I06 — Global settings, viewport settings and live Apply

**Finding:** Directional atlas size/depth, directional filter and positional filter are global. Positional atlas size/depth/subdivisions are per viewport. GLES3 resize deletes existing textures/framebuffers and clears positional ownership. A live change can therefore cause allocation work and alter more than the visible preview. [Global directional controls](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/RenderingServer.xml#L1256-L1269), [positional filter](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/RenderingServer.xml#L3437-L3442), [viewport allocation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/RenderingServer.xml#L4401-L4417), [resize implementation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/drivers/gles3/storage/light_storage.cpp#L1247-L1286).

**Decision — reuse with one global owner, S02/S10.** Commit expensive changes on Apply or a safe world boundary; do not let each subviewport independently set global quality.

**Smallest experiment:** Apply/restore one atlas change while Home, retained flight and relevant subviewports exist. Check effective state, first-use hitch and resource release. Use existing [shadow-pass statistics](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/RenderingServer.xml#L4183-L4201) and [debug modes](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/RenderingServer.xml#L5623-L5646). **Risk:** PSSM debug replaces custom materials and is Forward+/Mobile only; it is not normal-render vegetation evidence. Changing `ProjectSettings` alone is not proof that existing renderer resources changed.

### I07 — Cascade stability during zoom and sun motion

**Finding:** Grid snapping does not freeze the projection when FOV, clipping planes, split distance, light direction or source angle changes. Godot's soft-source expansion contributes to the footprint/grid step. Its maximum-shadow-distance clamp is bypassed for orthographic cameras. [Pinned cascade fitting](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/servers/rendering/renderer_scene_cull.cpp#L1980-L2143), [Pettineo's stable-cascade analysis](https://therealmjp.github.io/posts/shadow-maps/).

**Decision — reuse existing stabilization and isolate motions, S03.** A custom CSM implementation is premature. Establish which actual OpenRC cameras are orthographic before applying that caveat.

**Smallest experiment:** At 1080p run separate translation, rotation, FOV/autozoom and sun-motion sequences over the same runway geometry. Pair normal captures with the supported split/atlas views. [Pinned diagnostic API](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/Viewport.xml#L691-L712).

**Risk:** Continuous cascade retuning can trade one shimmer for resolution pumping. A zero-angle PCF comparison also changes fitting, so it cannot be described as a pure filtering change. The new finding is the precise set of projection dependencies and orthographic behavior, rather than the already-known existence of stabilization.

### I08 — Blocker sampling versus filter softness

**Finding:** PCSS searches for blockers before filtering visibility. The pinned Forward+ directional shader rotates its kernel using screen position and `taa_frame_count`, averages blockers and returns fully lit if none are found. Missing a thin blocker is therefore a different possible failure from merely blurring its edge. [Pinned directional shader](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/scene_forward_lights_inc.glsl#L371-L409), [NVIDIA soft-shadow presentation, 2008](https://developer.download.nvidia.com/presentations/2008/GDC/GDC08_SoftShadowMapping.pdf).

**Decision — test the native PCSS path, S03/S05.** Separate persistent softness, coverage loss and time-varying noise. Internal blocker counts are not assumed to be a public Godot diagnostic.

**Smallest experiment:** Keep current atlas/splits/source angle/exposure fixed and compare filter qualities on a thin fence plus objects at contact and known lifted heights. Retain a short frame sequence. A separate matched TAA on/off control can investigate sampling interactions; it is not a repair claim for contact shadows.

**Risk:** More blur can conceal jaggedness while losing RC-scale detail; more samples cannot repair incorrect caster geometry. Report the I07 angle/fitting coupling in any PCF-versus-PCSS comparison.

### I09 — Bias and thin-geometry robustness

**Finding:** Depth bias, normal bias, light reverse culling and geometry double-sided casting solve different problems. Receiver-plane depth bias is a more involved algorithm: per-sample comparison depth is adjusted using a local derivative estimate. Derivatives evaluated after divergent cascade selection can introduce seams. [Pinned light controls](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/Light3D.xml#L112-L137), [Microsoft CSM analysis](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/dxtecharts/cascaded-shadow-maps).

**Decision — test supported controls; defer receiver-plane integration, S03/S04.** Do not invent a Godot material checkbox for an engine-level algorithm. Establish winding/thickness before using global culling changes.

**Smallest experiment:** A small depth-bias × normal-bias matrix with a contacting wheel, thin strut, slope and low sun; hold atlas/filter fixed. Record acne, lost coverage and separation, then test face/culling choices independently.

**Risk:** A bias that cleans a fuselage can erase a strut or make the wheels appear to float. A separately justified renderer modification would need precision, cascade-boundary and GPU evidence; screenshots from another engine do not establish its value here.

### I10 — Foliage mip coverage, wind and LOD

**Finding:** The pinned texture importer disables alpha-test coverage preservation by default; its threshold defaults to 0.5 independently of the material's threshold. Import guidance limits this treatment to alpha scissor, not alpha hash. [Importer properties](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/ResourceImporterTexture.xml#L73-L87), [image import guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/assets_pipeline/importing_images.html).

Camera-dependent shader deformation has a separate trap: current-pass inverse view and main-camera inverse view differ in shadow rendering. [Spatial shader reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/spatial_shader.html), [pinned main-camera built-in](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/servers/rendering/renderer_rd/forward_clustered/scene_shader_forward_clustered.cpp#L641-L649).

**Decision — inspect existing textures and shaders, S06.** Reuse the import option only after proving the asset follows that path; embedded textures and custom shaders require their own checks.

**Smallest experiment:** One foliage asset, coverage off/on with consistent thresholds, moving through mip/LOD distances under wind. Compare visible cutouts and shadow openings in normal rendering. **Risk:** Coverage-preserving mipmaps cannot fix a stale clock, wrong view matrix, truncated bounds or unsuitable shadow LOD. Standard's missing alpha-to-coverage remains an independent constraint.

### I11 — Coherent cloud shadows without a new weather system

**Finding:** The *Final Fantasy XV* SIGGRAPH 2016 presentation uses related extinction/transmittance information for cloud lighting and surface shading. Epic's volumetric-cloud guidance likewise distinguishes cloud-to-scene shading from cloud self-shadow and atmosphere interactions. These are cross-engine references, not features already installed in Godot/OpenRC. [Square Enix presentation](https://www.jp.square-enix.com/tech/library/pdf/s16_final.pdf), [Epic cloud documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/volumetric-cloud-component-in-unreal-engine).

**Decision — test a shared-data approximation, S09; defer volumetric integration.** If OpenRC's existing sky mapping supports a horizontal cloud layer, project receiver positions toward the celestial source and sample the same transported field. This proposed approximation needs bounds for near-horizon rays and receivers within/above the layer; it is not automatically valid for every sky.

**Smallest experiment:** One recognizable moving cloud, parked aircraft and runway, first at high and then low sun. Compare sky position, aircraft lighting and ground shade across time and pause.

**Risk:** A separate scrolling ground texture can look attractive while disagreeing with the sky and aircraft. Multiplying final scene color can also darken ambient/emissive contributions incorrectly; direct-light integration must be demonstrated on the relevant materials.

### I12 — Alternative algorithms and the two meanings of VSM

**Finding:** Variance shadow maps store filterable moments and can suffer light bleeding; exponential and higher-moment variants add precision/storage/bias choices. The original four-moment method reports a 64-bit-per-texel representation: a hypothetical full 8192² map alone is **512 MiB**, before other resources. [Variance analysis](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-ii-light-and-shadows/chapter-8-summed-area-variance-shadow-maps), [Moment Shadow Mapping, 2015](https://www.momentsingraphics.de/I3D2015.html), [2016 extensions](https://momentsingraphics.de/Media/I3D2016/MSMBeyondHardShadows.pdf).

Virtual shadow maps are a different technique: demand-allocated pages and caching. Epic documents invalidation from moving lights and deforming foliage. Hardware ray-traced shadows require acceleration structures and a supported backend/device. [Virtual shadow maps](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/virtual-shadow-maps-in-unreal-engine), [hardware ray tracing](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/hardware-ray-tracing-in-unreal-engine).

**Decision — defer renderer projects.** Distinguish **variance** from **virtual** whenever writing “VSM”; neither is a verified drop-in option here.

**Smallest experiment:** First establish the unresolved landing/thin-caster failure and budget with current PCSS. Only then compare a separate reference implementation. **Risk:** Animated vegetation and flowing daylight can defeat cache assumptions; another engine's screenshots and frame times do not transfer to OpenRC.

### I13 — RenderDoc as a shadow diagnosis tool

**Finding:** MIT-licensed RenderDoc supports listed Vulkan and desktop OpenGL/ES configurations, not Metal. Its unsupported legacy OpenGL compatibility *profile* is different from Godot's renderer named Compatibility. Pixel history and shader-debug capabilities depend on backend/driver; do not assume uniform OpenGL coverage. [Official repository](https://github.com/baldurk/renderdoc), [texture viewer](https://github.com/baldurk/renderdoc/blob/v1.x/docs/window/texture_viewer.rst).

**Decision — reuse externally, S00/S01/S03.** A capture can expose depth attachments, atlas viewport/scissor regions, caster draws, bias/cull state and receiver bindings. Inspect projected masks as their own render targets instead of mistaking them for the native atlas.

**Smallest experiment:** Capture the same 1080p wheel/runway frame in supported Standard desktop GL and High/Vulkan. Trace which draw produces each suspected shadow and inspect clear-surface controls. Record tool, API, driver and capture identity.

**Risk:** Resource names may be opaque, and a final color difference does not identify its cause. Capture/replay overhead makes these diagnostic frames unsuitable as normal-flight performance samples. Do not ship capture tooling or large capture files with the application.

### I14 — Choosing a profiler for the actual GPU and API

**Finding:** The retrieved [Nsight Graphics 2026.4 entry point](https://developer.nvidia.com/nsight-graphics/get-started) and [feature matrix](https://docs.nvidia.com/nsight-graphics/UserGuide/appendix.html) distinguish API/feature support; OpenGL frame debugging is not equivalent to all capture/trace features. Compatible NVIDIA hardware/drivers are required. [Radeon GPU Profiler](https://gpuopen.com/manuals/rgp_manual/) targets supported AMD hardware and Vulkan/DX12, not Standard's OpenGL. Linux capture also depends on the documented driver/trace route. [Xcode](https://developer.apple.com/xcode/) serves Metal; Apple's GPU counter collection can serialize passes. [Apple counter guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/analyzing-apple-gpu-performance-using-counter-statistics).

**Decision — conditional external tools, S00/S11.** Prefer the existing in-app collectors for repeatable acceptance, then a vendor tool for attribution on compatible hardware. Free/public download availability does not imply open-source licensing or universal API coverage; check terms, access requirements and the supported driver matrix before use.

**Smallest experiment:** One supported 1080p trace/counter capture to attribute shadow work, followed by separate uninstrumented alternating A/B flight runs.

**Risk:** Serialized counters, replayed workloads and different GPUs cannot be mixed into a single FPS claim. No vendor tool was installed or run in this investigation.

### I15 — Image differences versus temporal shadow stability

**Finding:** BSD-3-Clause FLIP evaluates spatial reference/test differences; its paper studies perceived differences when alternating still images. Applying FLIP or SSIM independently to video frames does not by itself measure temporal perception or shadow correctness. [FLIP implementation](https://github.com/NVlabs/flip), [2020 paper](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/rtr/publication/andersson2020flip/).

**Decision — reuse FLIP for stills; add a bounded temporal diagnostic, S11.** Reuse the existing capture workflow rather than introducing another image library. Retain dependency notices.

**Smallest experiment:** Capture identical baseline A twice and candidate B with matched time/camera/exposure. In a fixed-camera, stationary-shadow ROI, compare luminance change-over-time distributions against the A–A noise floor and a deliberate jitter/flicker positive control. Review normal-speed and slow video alongside FLIP maps. Moving-camera analysis requires reliable geometry/motion alignment and explicit disocclusion handling; otherwise report qualitative video evidence.

**Risk:** TAA jitter, actual movement, color conversion, compression and unequal cadence can all mimic shadow flicker. Record ROI/mask definitions and raw identities. Treat the proposed temporal series as an exploratory diagnostic, not a validated perceptual metric or a new universal pass threshold.

### I16 — CACAO, XeGTAO or stock ambient occlusion?

**Finding:** AMD's CACAO has depth/normal preparation, quality-dependent sampling, blur and optional upsampling; it outputs AO rather than direct-light visibility. Its code is MIT, but AMD's SDK 2 release notes relegate SDK 1 effects to the older SDK branch. [CACAO technique manual, effect 1.4](https://gpuopen.com/manuals/fidelityfx_sdk/techniques/combined-adaptive-compute-ambient-occlusion/), [license](https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-CACAO/blob/master/license.txt), [SDK release notes](https://github.com/GPUOpen-LibrariesAndSDKs/FidelityFX-SDK/releases).

[XeGTAO](https://github.com/GameTechDev/XeGTAO) is MIT DirectX/HLSL reference code, archived in April 2024 and explicitly discontinued. Neither library is a maintained Godot-ready replacement established by this research. Godot already supplies SSAO; the [compositor extension](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/rendering/compositor.html) is an RD renderer integration mechanism, not a portable Standard drop-in.

**Decision — test stock AO first, defer ports, S05.** A custom AO texture also needs correct ambient-light compositing, matrices, resource lifetime and render-thread handling.

**Smallest experiment:** Tune existing AO independently on the same 1080p runway/underwing/pavilion cases in Standard and High; measure halos, missed offscreen occlusion, noise and cost. **Risk:** Replacing stock AO with an inactive library adds maintenance without solving a demonstrated cast-shadow problem.

### I17 — Free Godot examples and distant-shadow add-ons

**Finding:** The MIT [official Lights and Shadows demo at `b761b4cd`](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects/blob/b761b4cd5718a7118f2c95eb55f181d0a5f004f1/3d/lights_and_shadows/README.md) exercises PCSS, projectors and area lights. Its [project configuration](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects/blob/b761b4cd5718a7118f2c95eb55f181d0a5f004f1/3d/lights_and_shadows/project.godot) declares 4.7 and its README Forward+; it was not executed on this beta.

MIT [GodotFarShadow at `aad1ab39`](https://github.com/AdrianPokojny/GodotFarShadow/tree/aad1ab39a2cb3afc56abf362f09afaf88032ca69) is a concrete free experiment, not a verified drop-in: its project targets Godot 4.5/C#/Forward+, its [camera synchronizer](https://github.com/AdrianPokojny/GodotFarShadow/blob/aad1ab39a2cb3afc56abf362f09afaf88032ca69/TwoCameras/Scripts/CamSync.cs) uses a second camera at 1/100 scale, and the README acknowledges a near/far seam.

**Decision — reuse the official example for isolated study; defer the addon, S03/S07.** No already-qualified universal shadow/cloud addon was verified in this bounded search.

**Smallest experiment:** Run the pinned official example separately, reproduce one PCSS comparison and identify actual Compatibility limitations. **Risk:** A second world/view introduces synchronization, bounds, memory and lifecycle costs; converting the far-shadow experiment into this GDScript product may cost more than tuning native maps. Preserve asset notices as well as code licenses.

### I18 — meshoptimizer and Godot's existing import pipeline

**Finding:** MIT [meshoptimizer's shadow indexing](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer#shadow-indexing) can reduce unique vertices while preserving triangles. The key must retain alpha UVs, skinning and other shadow-relevant attributes. It differs from geometry simplification. Godot already [vendors meshoptimizer 1.2](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/thirdparty/README.md#meshoptimizer).

The pinned [scene importer](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/editor/import/3d/resource_importer_scene.cpp) defaults to requesting LODs and shadow meshes, with overrides. Its [shadow-mesh implementation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/scene/resources/3d/importer_mesh.cpp#L899-L984) welds equal positions using Godot's own code and remaps LODs; it skips blend shapes and bone/weight arrays. Do not attribute that weld to meshoptimizer's shadow-index function. [ArrayMesh's contract](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/ArrayMesh.xml) requires matching source positions, including LODs.

**Decision — inspect/reuse native import optimization, S04/S06.** No additional dependency is needed for the first experiment.

**Smallest experiment:** Inspect one real static asset's import settings and actual `shadow_mesh`; compare unique vertices, draw cost and silhouette at 1080p. Test alpha/deformation separately. **Risk:** Defaults do not prove that an optimized resource exists or is used. A silhouette-simplified proxy needs a separate casting/lifecycle path, not assignment to `ArrayMesh.shadow_mesh`.

### C01 — Native bulk calls or a new GDExtension boundary?

**Question:** Where would a C++ boundary improve the already-batched collector?

**Finding:** Godot exposes a single native packed-vector transform, already used here. Official godot-cpp documentation explains that packed arrays live on the engine side and GDScript passes arguments through `Variant`; unpacking/copy-on-write can prevent a callee's mutation from changing its caller. [Pinned transform API](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/Transform3D.xml#L231-L237), [packed-array semantics](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/scripting/cpp/core_types.html#packed-arrays). The MIT [godot-cpp bindings](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-cpp) provide the public extension route, not direct access to every internal engine method.

**Decision — reuse first, test a coarse helper if needed.** Keep scene selection/ownership in GDScript. If attribution supports it, pass one immutable geometry batch to a pure helper and return explicitly owned outputs. Avoid a C++ call per vertex, triangle or node property.

**Smallest experiment:** Compare current bulk GDScript, a no-work extension round trip, and a full native batch on identical representative inputs. Report packing/copy/allocation, useful kernel work, returned-data handling and full binding separately. **Risk:** A faster inner loop can lose its benefit at the boundary. Include small workloads as well as the largest mesh; accept only the workload sizes that show a useful end-to-end gain.

### C02 — Pure C++ filtering and bounds kernels

**Question:** Which remaining CPU loop is a sensible first prototype?

**Finding:** The triangle cutoff and ordered AABB expansion are plausible bounded candidates. godot-cpp supports direct `.ptr()`/`.ptrw()` access for bulk data; the pinned [copy-on-write implementation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/core/templates/cowdata.h#L146-L168) can detach shared storage before writable access. Resize/ownership transitions can invalidate pointers. Preserve independently owned output instead of retaining pointers into live scene data. [Official data-access guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/scripting/cpp/core_types.html#packed-arrays).

GDScript scalar floats are 64-bit while ordinary rendering vectors use 32-bit components; operation order, intermediate rounding and compiler contraction matter. [Pinned scalar type](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/float.xml#L5-L8), [Clang floating-point controls](https://clang.llvm.org/docs/UsersManual.html#controlling-floating-point-behavior).

**Decision — conditional first native prototype.** Begin with faces already transformed by the accepted native operation, then filter and calculate bounds. Fusion/SIMD is a later comparison. A fallback-blur port is lower priority because repeated generation is already cached.

**Smallest experiment:** Require exact face order/count, cutoff behavior, serialized bounds and input isolation using current collection oracles. Include empty/degenerate inputs, nonuniform transforms and nonfinite-cutoff cases. For blur, preserve double accumulation order, float32 stores and final mip/image bytes. **Risk:** A float cutoff, reassociation or faster bounds formula can change accepted triangles or images. Record compiler/precision/FP flags and compare each supported architecture; no fast-math or SIMD gain is assumed.

### C03 — Native work on bounded worker jobs

**Question:** Would moving preparation off the main thread improve loading or frame pacing?

**Finding:** The pinned pool requires eventual waits to clean up submitted tasks, limits when completion checks are valid, and can reject deadlock-prone nested waits. It exposes no general cancellation method. [WorkerThreadPool contract](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/WorkerThreadPool.xml#L49-L68), [wait rules](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/WorkerThreadPool.xml#L101-L123). Active scene-tree operations and shared rendering resources are not safe general worker inputs. [Godot thread-safety guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/performance/thread_safe_apis.html).

**Decision — defer threading until synchronous work is attributed to a visible hitch.** Snapshot immutable CPU inputs on the owning thread; run bounded data-only work; install the result on the main thread only if source/world generations still match. Cap queued bytes and jobs, cooperatively abandon stale work and reap every submitted task exactly once. Preserve fallback visuals while an optional result is pending.

**Smallest experiment:** Compare synchronous script, synchronous C++ and one worker job on the same asset. Measure snapshot/copy, queue latency, completion/install, first usable frame, frame p95/p99 and peak/residual memory; exercise reset and source destruction.

**Risk:** Immediately waiting after submit defeats asynchronous scheduling. Unbounded jobs, retained scene pointers or blocking shutdown can be worse than the original construction cost. More threads can reduce responsiveness through contention without reducing total loading time.

### C04 — C++ render submission versus GPU shadow work

**Question:** Can a native producer reduce repeated-caster submission cost?

**Finding:** The pinned [RenderingServer](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/RenderingServer.xml) distinguishes scene-system overhead from a saturated GPU. `multimesh_set_buffer` provides a whole-buffer update; fetching its buffer can entail expensive GPU readback. A [MultiMesh](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/MultiMesh.xml) shares visibility bounds and light limits and ignores blend shapes. Batching does not guarantee one total draw across all surfaces, lights and cascades.

**Decision — measure existing bulk submission first.** If repeated instance-buffer preparation is a demonstrated CPU cost, compare a packed GDScript producer with one C++ producer. Retain spatially bounded batches so extra invisible casters do not erase CPU savings.

**Smallest experiment:** One representative repeated-caster group at 1080p, matching transforms, camera and light. Compare current updates, script bulk update and native bulk update; separate CPU preparation/submission from GPU shadow work, memory and full-frame timing.

**Risk:** Porting setup code cannot make PCSS sampling cheaper. [RenderingDevice](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/RenderingDevice.xml) permits custom GPU work on supported RD paths but is unavailable in Compatibility/headless; it does not establish a public extension hook to replace internal shadow passes. A compute path is a distinct renderer experiment, not a presumed consequence of using C++.

### C05 — Move suitable work to import time

**Question:** Can existing native import work eliminate repeated runtime preparation?

**Finding:** Godot already vendors MIT meshoptimizer 1.2. Its importer requests LOD/shadow-mesh generation by default, with overrides; Godot's own equal-position weld remaps existing LODs and skips blend shapes/bone-weight arrays. [Pinned dependency](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/thirdparty/README.md#meshoptimizer), [scene importer](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/editor/import/3d/resource_importer_scene.cpp), [ImporterMesh](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/scene/resources/3d/importer_mesh.cpp#L899-L984). The [ArrayMesh shadow contract](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4d10419ca/doc/classes/ArrayMesh.xml) preserves source positions, including LODs.

**Decision — inspect and reuse, without adding a library initially.** Imported native shadow meshes and OpenRC's flat projected face collection serve different consumers; one cannot simply replace the other. Only precompute/cache immutable geometry when source/generator identity, importer/engine revision, import options, transforms/cutoff and geometry-relevant material or pose changes can invalidate it correctly.

**Smallest experiment:** Inspect one real static imported asset's actual optimized resource and drawn coverage. Compare optimized/control import, cold/warm loading, bytes, CPU preparation and native 1080p shadows. For a projected cache, preserve current ordered faces/bounds and test a changed source/variant.

**Risk:** Default import flags do not prove optimization was produced or used. Engine-vendored symbols are not a public link target for an external extension. True simplified proxies still need a separate qualified caster path and regeneration through the owning source pipeline.

### C06 — GDExtension, engine module or engine fork?

**Question:** What is the smallest sustainable C++ integration?

**Finding:** GDExtension adds a shared library through public APIs without rebuilding Godot for every application change. Modules provide deeper compiled-engine integration when that boundary is insufficient. [Official module guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/engine_details/engine_api/custom_modules_in_cpp.html). Neither option inherently improves GPU performance.

OpenRC's [NK-3 lock](../../research/native-kernel/toolchain-lock.json) already pins MIT godot-cpp `10.0.0-stable`, commit `507ed9d840c01a3c5b2a39af8bb4000bfac30bf5`, API 4.7 against the 4.8-beta1 engine and an OS/RefCounted-only class profile. Its [binding README](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-cpp/blob/507ed9d840c01a3c5b2a39af8bb4000bfac30bf5/README.md) documents API targeting and custom API files. These are reusable build/identity patterns, not qualification for a new rendering library; a renderer-API helper needs appropriate bindings.

**Decision — prefer one optional pure GDExtension helper if C01/C02 prove worthwhile; defer a custom engine.** Keep visual helpers outside the physics evaluator's ABI and binary ownership. Record engine API/precision, compiler, FP flags, platform, architecture, descriptor and source/binary hashes. Verify each intended package and safe refusal of missing/incompatible libraries before use. A loader fallback cannot recover an arbitrary native process crash.

**Smallest experiment:** One release-built helper, reference fallback and target-platform package, measured against the current collector. **Risk:** A module/private-renderer patch requires compatible editor/export builds, regression coverage and upgrade maintenance. Existing [graphics native builds](../../tools/graphics/native/README.md) are a separate qualified scope, not automatic evidence for a shadow fork.

## Recommended experiments and non-adoptions

1. **Allocation and ownership first.** Hold exposure/AA/poses fixed; compare current projection, current native maps and only then an explicitly partitioned hybrid. Inventory what each caster/receiver/light contributes before identifying over-darkening as a defect.
2. **Landing and self-shadow detail next.** Tune distance/splits/bias at a fixed atlas; then compare atlas/filter candidates. Measure thin geometry, aircraft-ground separation and useful penumbra behavior in actual motion.
3. **Animated and alpha fidelity.** Hinges, foliage, windsock, people and rotor state must agree with the visible source. Native casting may solve some paths more cheaply than custom animated projection; benchmark the actual assets.
4. **Local and atmospheric coherence.** Add one useful spotlight before expanding its budget. Use existing cloud/celestial transport and visible receiver tests instead of a second atmosphere model.
5. **AO/contact as separate candidates.** Existing AO can be refined without enabling rejected contact rays. Keep the contact experiment off until a demonstrated remedy passes its previous failure cases and new edge/motion checks.

No evidence here justifies a new shadow engine, rendering-library dependency, 16K atlas, default ray tracing, baked fixed outdoor sun, or replacement foliage/aircraft asset pack. Those are separate feasibility questions. The current map size and disabled features are budget/quality choices, not defects by themselves.
