## Recommended experiments and non-adoptions

1. **Allocation and ownership first.** Hold exposure/AA/poses fixed; compare current projection, current native maps and only then an explicitly partitioned hybrid. Inventory what each caster/receiver/light contributes before identifying over-darkening as a defect.
2. **Landing and self-shadow detail next.** Tune distance/splits/bias at a fixed atlas; then compare atlas/filter candidates. Measure thin geometry, aircraft-ground separation and useful penumbra behavior in actual motion.
3. **Animated and alpha fidelity.** Hinges, foliage, windsock, people and rotor state must agree with the visible source. Native casting may solve some paths more cheaply than custom animated projection; benchmark the actual assets.
4. **Local and atmospheric coherence.** Add one useful spotlight before expanding its budget. Use existing cloud/celestial transport and visible receiver tests instead of a second atmosphere model.
5. **AO/contact as separate candidates.** Existing AO can be refined without enabling rejected contact rays. Keep the contact experiment off until a demonstrated remedy passes its previous failure cases and new edge/motion checks.

No evidence here justifies a new shadow engine, rendering-library dependency, 16K atlas, default ray tracing, baked fixed outdoor sun, or replacement foliage/aircraft asset pack. Those are separate feasibility questions. The current map size and disabled features are budget/quality choices, not defects by themselves.

## Evidence still needed
