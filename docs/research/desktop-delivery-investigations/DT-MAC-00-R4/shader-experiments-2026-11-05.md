# Apple Silicon shader audit and five isolated experiments

On 5 November 2026 the first external review audited OpenRC at `fc018b38`. The second tested candidate algorithms against `93ffbb1` with numerical fixtures, the pinned Godot shader compiler and camera APIs. Neither review collected new Metal frame timings. The later review corrected the earlier flattened-cloud diagnosis. The [project follow-up](README.md#optimization-help-follow-up-2026-11-05) records the integration checks and the [6 November native controls](README.md#dt-mac-06b-r2--dt-mac-02b-r2-native-verification-2026-11-06) adjudicate the bounded-density trial.

The [source-pinned original reports](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/3df2791752fe2c1faad132fcb11febbb0ae788d1/optimization%20help.md) retain the full derivations, source citations and original attachment references. The supplied attachments were named `OpenRC_Apple_Silicon_Audit_2026-11-05.pdf` (16 pages, 55 references) and `OpenRC_Apple_Silicon_Experimental_Kit_2026-11-05.zip` (numerical/camera fixtures, shader sketches and logs). They were supplied through a personal account; public attachment availability is unverified. The methods, numerical findings, primary sources and project controls below can be read independently. ## M1 observations used by the audit

The existing seven-core M1 Air / 8 GiB Mobile/Metal pilot-camera screen used Medium scenery, a 2560×1600 target, ten seconds of warmup and twenty seconds of measurement per setting. Halving internal scale with 4× MSAA changed average FPS from 38.3 to 89.6. About 57% of main-thread samples waited for GPU completion. Of 28.42 ms total GPU time, 22.34 ms was opaque. Separate ablations measured 11.08 ms for ground material, 3.55 ms for ground cloud shadows, 7.59 ms for sky clouds in another view, and 0.06 ms for the tested vegetation. These effects overlap. The beta2 five-pair Metal/GL circuit and beta1 three-pair thirty-minute shader-bundle comparison use different binaries and workloads. [R4 measurements](README.md) · [Historical sustained comparison](../DT-MAC-02/README.md#repeated-30-minute-metal-comparison)

At 2560×1600 output, 0.75 scale yields 1920×1200 input, and roughly two-thirds scale yields 1707×1067. The C2+C5b test measured 26.20 → 23.45 ms GPU time in one profiled pair. Separate 1920×1080 ground/sky stills differed by at most 2/255. Keep requested scale, drawable pixels, reconstruction output, physical display and moving readability distinct. [Pinned measurement record](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/93ffbb101917dff86ecc2632f07e2e45c450a178/docs/research/desktop-delivery-investigations/DT-MAC-00-R4/README.md) · [Godot scaling](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/3d/resolution_scaling.html)

## Prototype results and reproduction details

| Experiment | Method, result and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Conservative cloud bounds | Five bounded noise octaves have weights 1/2 through 1/32. The remaining normalized maximum after 2, 3 or 4 octaves is 7/31, 3/31 or 1/31. Test whether the remaining interval can affect the cluster-adjusted smoothstep. Leave ambiguous values on the original accumulation path. Across 105,144,320 parameterized float32 comparisons the screened variants had zero differing outputs. On a 256² grid, seed 1253, coverage and clustering 0.35, checking after octaves 2 and 3 avoided 44.897% of fBm evaluations (37.415% including clustering hashes). The empirical 1e-5 guard and GPU divergence needed native verification. Both original variants failed the unchanged Metal numerical gate. |
| High-octave lattice atlas | Keep the C5b low-octave cache. Store octave-3 and octave-4 hash lattices in a 1024² RG16F texture, periods 512 and 1024. Use wrapped texel centers and quintic interpolation weights. A three-offset relief evaluation exchanges 24 source-level hashes for 6 samples; persistent cost is 4 MiB/seed, plus staging. Across 65,536 coordinates and five seeds the maximum incremental density/active-cloud scalar-light differences were 0.000134/0.000247. Wrong seed and missing half-texel offset provided negative controls. Test actual filtering on Metal and analytic fallback for pending/mismatched seeds. |
| Native-resolution aircraft crop | Share the main world and pose through an off-axis camera covering a padded region that includes occluders. Geometry passed 96 crop/FOV/aspect/transform configurations, 3,168 projection checks (maximum 0.00869 px), 1,152 inclusion checks and 1,056 inverse-ray checks (maximum vector difference 2.885×10⁻⁶). Reversed offsets were detected. The crop camera's `project_ray_normal()` disagreed in 792/1,056 cases; use the main camera with crop-origin offset or the inverse crop projection. Geometry checks leave depth, exposure, transparency, fog, propeller edges, synchronized motion and full-frame cost untested. |
| Packed relief cache | Bake densities at the 0.10/0.28/0.62 light offsets into one RGBA16F tile. At 512²/1024²/2048², storage was 2/8/32 MiB per combination and maximum active-cloud scalar-light error 0.366/0.160/0.0425. Clustered low sun reached 0.0496 at 2048². A stale light-ray x offset of 0.05 increased 0.0425 to 0.0878. Interpolation loses detail after thresholding, so deprioritize this design. |
| Solar halo lookup | For halo exp(−18 acos μ), tabulate exp(−36 asin t), where t = sqrt((1−μ)/2). A 512-entry R16F table (1,024 bytes) gave maximum normalized halo error 0.00067842 over 820,197 unique float32 inputs. A uniform-dot table gave about 0.351. The disc edge shares the original angle calculation; replacing only the halo adds a square root and lookup without removing that angle. No GPU cost result. |

The atlas prototype's untested extension stores independently seeded lattices in explicit 1024², 512², 256², 128² and 64² mip levels (2.664 MiB/seed with R16F before tails/overhead). Supply each level explicitly and sample integral LODs. Automatic downsampling destroys the intended lattice. Query R32F filtering support if changing format. [Godot supplied mip data](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_image.html) · [Apple filtering capability](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal/mtldevice/supports32bitfloatfiltering) · [GPU texture-noise precedent](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems2/part-iii-high-quality-rendering/chapter-26-implementing-improved-perlin-noise)

The crop's estimated shaded-area fraction is background scale squared plus the native crop area fraction, excluding duplicate transform, lighting, target and composite costs. For a native Metal alternative, the pinned driver imports native rasterization-rate maps and binds them to framebuffers/render-pass descriptors. Query actual horizontal/vertical rates and physical dimensions, prove isolated reconstruction, then examine desktop scene-pass ownership, depth/screen readers, MSAA, transparency and UI before comparison. [Godot camera implementation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a192acf67903910a68aeb3c076c0907a4b/scene/3d/camera_3d.cpp) · [Apple rate maps](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/608/) · [Pinned Metal driver](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a192acf67903910a68aeb3c076c0907a4b/drivers/metal/rendering_device_driver_metal.cpp) · [Render pass](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a192acf67903910a68aeb3c076c0907a4b/drivers/metal/metal3_objects.cpp) · [metal-cpp](https://developer.apple.com/metal/cpp/)

## Source-review proposals and subsequent disposition

| Candidate | Condition for a useful test |
| --- | --- |
| Cache integrity and experiment identity | Publish only after validated bake, separate ground and sky cache selection and retain analytic fallback, bounded retry, copied materials, actual consumer/variant and source identity. Verify renderer recovery by activation token and shoreline clipping before lake publication. UID repairs, the benchmark validators and the targeted lifecycle fixes are recorded in the [project follow-up](README.md#optimization-help-follow-up-2026-11-05). |
| Cloud shading | Compare stock half-resolution sky against full-resolution sun, moon and IBL. Prefer a small number of shader variants. For layered decks, preserve density for solar occlusion and bound omitted relief using remaining transmittance. Price sky-heavy and ground-heavy views separately. [Godot sky shaders](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/sky_shader.html) |
| Ground and texture work | Test screen-footprint filtering, hoisting the uniform pilot ground-color anchor, or caching low-frequency material channels with weather, lighting and near detail live. Inspect compiler output before moving float hashes to CPU double precision. Compare writable bake textures against read-mostly publication, including copy/synchronization. Inventory compressed assets and actual sampling quality. [Apple texture guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/10580/) · [Arm ASTC encoder](https://github.com/arm-software/astc-encoder) |
| Passes, MetalFX and first use | Measure screen/depth reader copies, resolve costs and attachments as full-frame effects. Evaluate Spatial by net GPU cost, actual execution and moving aircraft readability. Bake shaders only through a supported native Mac export and repeat cold/warm first-use controls. Test presentation after establishing headroom. [Godot pipeline compilation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/performance/pipeline_compilations.html) · [Apple Metal tools](https://developer.apple.com/metal/tools/) · [Metal HUD metrics](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/understanding-metal-performance-hud-metrics) |
| Engine and compute boundaries | The pinned factory used Metal 3; later Metal 4 work needs explicit engine/driver support. FP16 requires compiler output, error bounds and a whole-pass result. Memoryless attachments require proof that no later sample, copy or resolve needs the data. The first review's contingent ARM64 solver proposal predates the November native-default decision.
