# LU-09-R2 — Runway lights at night

2026-10-20, America/Bogota. This follows the [low marker pass](../LU-09-R1/README.md) for the default 100 × 12 m RC grass runway. It keeps the existing white edge rows and directional green/red threshold optics. The fixture layout and flight solver are unchanged.

## Sources and decision

- The [FAA AIM runway-light section](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/aim_html/chap2_section_1.html) describes white runway edges and green-outward/red-inward threshold/end indications. These are visual references for the RC field, not an RC standard or airport-compliance claim. The full-size yellow caution zone and taxiway-blue lights are not added.
- [AMA night-flying advocacy](https://amablog.modelaircraft.org/amagov/2019/01/15/ama-response-to-faa-proposed-rulemaking/) emphasizes orientation lights on the aircraft. [Poplar Ford RC field](https://www.1nvrc.com/poplar-ford-field/) describes a grass field operated to dusk; no general RC-club runway-edge specification was found. This simulator's lighted strip remains an optional practice presentation.
- [Godot 4.7 rendering guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/engine_details/architecture/internal_rendering_architecture.html) and [MultiMesh guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/performance/using_multimesh.html) favor a batched visual treatment over one dynamic light per marker. [Godot emission guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/3d/standard_material_3d.html) distinguishes a glowing lens from illumination of nearby geometry. Dry turf should have a small diffuse pool, not a mirror-like streak; that last choice is an art inference.

## Implementation

- `field_lighting` still selects Club, Airfield or the legacy master Off. A separate `field_activation` choice is Auto, On or Off, exposed in Home/pause UI, preferences and `--field-activation=auto|on|off`. Auto follows the existing continuous +3° to −4° solar-elevation ramp. On forces the field lamps on even by day; Off extinguishes them while hardware stays visible. An old two-key night-options dictionary migrates to Auto. Campfire control remains independent.
- The 12 edge lamps share one additional MultiMesh of 0.65 m diffuse turf patches. The shader caps displayed contribution at 0.05 and follows the same activation factor. The batch is hidden at factor zero; it adds one visible draw in the close night capture, no `Light3D`, shadow pass, wet reflection or simulation state. The existing flood lights continue to illuminate the larger runway.
- The validated field-light inventory supplies 22 near-grass exclusion masks matching two pole proxies, one campfire base and 19 stake proxies. The 0.4 m clump clearance keeps low marker heads and other installations out of grass. A bounded 4 m cell index with an 8,192-reference limit preserves the scalar placement exactly and falls back to scalar checks for unusable inputs. No per-tick work or flight/contact geometry changes.
