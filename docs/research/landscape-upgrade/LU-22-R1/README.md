# LU-22-R1: lake bank material continuity

**2026-10-20.** The Club lake's exposed bank was a flat orange-brown strip in the [baseline shore](before/lake_shore.png) and [overview](before/lake_overview.png). Its outer grass was also visibly smoother than the adjoining field in a close native view. This pass changes the basin material and its bindings; it does not change the water boundary, mesh, terrain height, collision or lake definition.

The [bank shader](../../../../app/render/lake/bank.gdshader) now varies the inland grass-to-soil edge at metre scale while keeping the actual water-contact region damp. Two broader sediment frequencies break the even soil color. The bank uses the existing [Poly Haven Dirt](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt) albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness maps on the exposed soil. Its outer grass uses the same world-space [ambientCG Grass 001](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass001) maps, filtered detail texture, anti-tiling UVs, near-view fade and mean-color normalization as the adjoining field. Both downloaded sources are CC0 and were already imported; exact URLs, file hashes and licenses remain in the [RC ground manifest](../../../../app/assets/landscape/rc-ground/manifest.json). The [lake adapter](../../../../app/render/lake/lake_world.gd) binds these maps after basin construction to preserve the pinned geometry-bake source stamp.

Native Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility/OpenGL on an RTX 3090 produced the [accepted shore](accepted/lake_shore.png), [close bank](accepted/bank_detail.png) and [overview](accepted/lake_overview.png); [capture metadata](accepted/capture.json) records camera positions, image hashes and draw counts. The shore now shows granular soil and a darker wet edge, and the close view no longer has the smooth olive band at the bank-to-field transition. The earlier baseline used the full landscape capture harness, while the accepted images use a lake-only field harness at the same shore/overview camera coordinates. The pair supports visual review, not an exact pixel or performance comparison. The intermediate [first soil candidate](candidate/lake_shore.png) was too orange and was retuned.

The repeatable [lake-only capture script](../../../../tools/landscape/capture_lake_bank.gd) loads the production field without an aircraft and asserts that the soil and grass textures are bound. This avoids unrelated aircraft customization state affecting material review. Focused Godot checks passed: lake geometry bake **18/18**, lake world **27/27**, lake surface, ground contact, collision and field integration. The generated lake geometry stamp and scalar contact behavior are unchanged. The capture reports 78/32/104 visible draws for shore/close/overview; no paired frame-cost measurement was made. Independent pilot judgment and lower-end GPU cost remain open.
