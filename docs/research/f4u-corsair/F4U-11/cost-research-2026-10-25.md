## Safe costing implications

Integration outcome after the double pass: 30 dated CARF USD observations and two refreshed servo observations are retained; historical servo quotes are preserved. The cowl is a single-color price proxy; horizontal and vertical tail groups are parts-only composite estimates. Engine and pneumatic-system references are now package-level price proxies; other offers remain unattached candidates. In particular, $608 gear plus one $93 door is not a complete tail-gear replacement quote: door quantity/contents and steering linkage are unresolved. EUR observations remain research references with no invented conversion. See [F4U-11-R3](README.md#f4u-11-r3-double-price-pass-2026-10-25) for the implemented matrix and tests.

- The MOKI and CARF engine amounts are whole-engine purchase comparators. MOKI identifies integrated ignition and ring muffler; don't add a separate ignition replacement to a whole-engine replacement. The manual establishes standard cast-mount inclusion. The external ignition switch is separate and its installed identity remains unknown. Module-only replacement has no quote.
- The main retract price is for the set. CARF explicitly sells wheels, tail gear, and pneumatic system separately. Apply each only when inspection scope establishes that separate module is damaged and not already covered by a larger replacement set.
- The F4U-1D 795000 kit includes a factory-installed cowl and stab, and folding mechanics; adding those spare prices to a whole kit would double count. The kit leaves engine and retract installation for the builder and calls the fold actuation requirements out separately.
- Use the folding-wing SKU pair as a price source for the 112-degree folding runtime only after confirming paint surcharge and whether control surfaces/folding hardware are part of the replacement scope. The plugin-wing ($341 each, center plugin wing $812) offers on the CARF list are not compatible exact replacements for the folding runtime.
- CARF offers individual tail surfaces, but the authored inventory currently groups them. Do not create a composite total unless the damaged component scope is confirmed to match the exact set of parts. The full-fuselage price is similarly not a validated front/rear subcomponent split.
- The engine and ARF amounts establish purchase prices. They do not establish repair probability, salvage value, labor, inspection findings, or a repair cost.

## Double-pass findings (F4U-11-R3)

Two specialists independently rechecked engine/servo and propeller sources; the integrating reviewer checked the decisive manual and live selectors. [All 29 existing CARF USD amounts matched](checks/cost-r3/carf-list-comparison.json): 27 in the exact-kit accessory list, two on the propeller-set page. The latter two provenance URLs were corrected. The missing exact-kit USD observation was added, but stays outside component totals. Live HTML hashes and selected-option evidence are retained without copying entire vendor pages.

- [ServoCity SA1230SGP](https://www.servocity.com/sa1230sg-coreless-digital-servo/) remains **$103.39 each**, stock unconfirmed. [Horizon HRC31311S](https://www.horizonhobby.com/product/hs-311-standard-analog-sport-servo/HRC31311S.html) lists **$16.99 each**, In Stock. New observations date these rechecks without changing the old 2026-10-15 records used by other aircraft. Neither establishes installed identity or fit.
- [Aeroscale's SEP family](https://www.aeroscale.shop/products/sep-propellers-3-blade-black-hamilton-blade) starts at **$112**, but its [public variants](https://www.aeroscale.shop/products/sep-propellers-3-blade-black-hamilton-blade.js) omit 30×18. It cannot price this aircraft's exact propeller. The **$692** CARF carbon system also remains a different alternative.
- The separate [MOKI PowerBox RS switch](https://www.mokimotor.de/produkte/zubeh%C3%B6r/powerbox-7-4-volt/) is **€69 including German VAT**, shipping extra; this is an accessory reference, not a confirmed installed part. No EUR amount enters the USD catalog. The direct MOKI engine price applies to German pickup/shipping, not a landed overseas quote.
- CARF remained the only current exact USD engine seller confirmed in this focused scan. [X3 Models](https://x3models.it/16_moki-engines) lists the S250 at **€4,200 on order**. An ended US resale listing was excluded. Seller availability, engine weight differences (CARF 5.1 kg versus MOKI 5.48 kg) and propeller recommendations are not reconciled by matching a model name.

The known parts-reference subtotal rises from **$2,024.88 to $7,113.88** because the engine ($4,610) and air system ($479) now have supported package proxies, not because any recorded price increased. The kit is excluded, and the propeller and several installations remain unpriced in USD. This partial subtotal is neither the complete aircraft value nor a crash bill.
