# 07 · Field people: web survey (SC-HUM)

2026-10-24 · Research snapshot for [the field people plan](../../plans/field-people.md). It extends [01 §SC-10](01-prop-asset-sources.md#sc-10--people-static), the [animation source review](../asset-audio-animation-sources-2026-10-06.md) and the [LU-21 people research](../landscape-upgrade/LU-21/people-research.md). It replaces none of them.

**Question:** what human-factors data, engine techniques, free assets and RC-field practice should shape more realistic, efficient people at the club field in Godot 4.7 Compatibility with deterministic captures?

**Method:** 25 web searches and 6 direct page reads on 2026-10-24. Marks: **[V-page]** read on the page itself; **[V-search]** taken from search summaries, not read in full; **[U]** unverified; **[est]** our estimate. Nothing was downloaded. Licenses must be re-verified against the downloaded archives before intake.

## 1 · Human factors: how people watch a moving aircraft

| # | Source | Finding | Verdict for the plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | [Eye–head mover spectrum, arXiv 2602.06164 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.06164); Freedman & Sparks (monkey) via [Springer](https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/BF00229630) [V-search] | The head adds little to gaze shifts below ~25°. Its share grows with amplitude: about one third at 30°. People differ strongly ("head movers" vs "eye movers") | **Adopt:** a per-person dead zone of 10–20° [est] where the head holds still. Larger shifts turn the head, with a seeded "mover" factor. Eyes are invisible at 6–20 px, so the head must carry slightly more than real people do |
| H2 | [Mann et al. 2013, cricket batters, PLoS One](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3596397/); [elite baseball batters](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7739578/) [V-search] | During continuous tracking the head rotates coupled to the ball, keeping it in a constant head-relative direction. The eyes add predictive saccades 80–220 ms ahead | **Adopt:** pilots *couple* the head to their own aircraft continuously, with a small lead. Spectators step and hold (H1) |
| H3 | Observer study quoted in the same search [V-search] | Observers' heads begin moving up to 0.5 s before a thrown ball leaves the hand (anticipation) | **Adopt:** aim at a predicted point `p + v·τ` (τ 0.1–0.3 s [est]). The follower lag partly cancels it, so heads neither trail visibly nor look robotic |
| H4 | [UCLouvain free-head pursuit](https://portal.inma.ucl.ac.be/publication/155) [V-search] | Subjects use two head strategies, favouring one rotation axis depending on target direction | Small effect at our sizes; implement yaw-first, pitch-second rotation order |
| H5 | [Blink rate, conversation (PLOS One 2021)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8500445) [V-search] | ~17 blinks/min at rest, ~26 in conversation, ~4.5 while reading; large individual spread | **Adopt** for close skinned actors only: seeded 12–26 blinks/min [est], with more while chatting |
| H6 | [Gallup 2012 gaze following (2,822 pedestrians)](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/what-are-you-looking-at-people-follow-each-others-gazes-but-without-a-tipping-point-1854); [crowd gaze cascade, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3358867) [V-search] | Passers-by follow a looking group about 27 % of the time. The probability grows with the number of lookers, but there is no tipping point | **Adopt:** attention contagion. A person's chance to look rises with the number of neighbours already looking. Cap it well below 100 % |
| H7 | [McDonnell et al. 2012 "Render me Real?"](https://scholar.korea.ac.kr/handle/2021.sw.korea/108000); [Face Reality](https://edepositireland.ie/handle/2262/49128) [V-search] | Mid-realism renders are rated least appealing. Removing blinks or gaze makes characters unpleasant. A mismatch between appearance and motion realism causes the uncanny effect | **Adopt:** keep faces modest and consistent with motion quality. Caps and sunglasses for most people. Add blinks only where faces are close enough to be read |
| H8 | [Biological motion (Johansson)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_motion_perception) [V-search] | People recognise human action from ~12–15 moving joint dots, but not from a still frame | Confirms motion over texture at 6–20 px heads (plan §3) |
| H9 | [Idle animation guidance (MoCap Online)](https://mocaponline.com/blogs/mocap-news/idle-animation-loop); [Idle Weaver (UE forum)](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/araz-creates-idle-weaver-procedural-npc-motion-and-behaviour-system/2839748) [V-search; vendor sources] | A single looping idle is noticed within ~90 s. 2–4 s loops blend easily but repeat visibly. Give each character its own rhythm; split idle pools by role; trigger variation clips on a schedule | **Adopt:** per-person rhythm scale 0.85–1.15 [est], role-specific clip pools, scheduled variations (look-around, stretch, sip) from `hash(seed, floor(t/period))` |
| H10 | [Vulkan tutorial: look-at controllers](https://docs.vulkan.org/tutorial/latest/Advanced_glTF/Procedural_Animation_IK/05_look_at.html); [SBGames 2025 NPC gaze](https://sol.sbc.org.br/index.php/sbgames/article/download/45431/45192/) [V-search] | Head-only look-at looks unnatural; spread the rotation over spine, neck and head. Gate targets behind the character with a dot-product test and ease the weight in and out. Clamp eye angles to human ranges | **Adopt:** chest 10–15 %, neck 30 %, head 55–60 % [est]; the rear-gate threshold returns the head to rest |

## 2 · Engine techniques (Godot 4.7 Compatibility)

| # | Source | Finding | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | [LookAtModifier3D, 4.7 class reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_lookatmodifier3d.html) [V-page] | Properties: `origin_from` (rest, bone or external node), `origin_offset` (eye offset), `use_angle_limitation`, per-side primary/secondary limits, `*_damp_threshold`, `duration` with tween transition/ease, `relative`. The parent bone's modifier must come before the child's. `TRANS_BACK/ELASTIC/SPRING` can overshoot limits. No renderer restriction or cost data stated | **Adopt** for Layer B: chest → neck → head modifiers in order, `relative = true` over the idle clip, limits from H1. Avoid overshooting transitions. Measure the cost ourselves |
| E2 | [IK returns in Godot 4.6](https://godotengine.org/article/inverse-kinematics-returns-to-godot-4-6/) [V-search] | `IKModifier3D` family: `TwoBoneIK3D`, `ChainIK3D`, FABRIK, CCD, Jacobian and others | **Adopt** `TwoBoneIK3D` for hands on transmitter grips and on aircraft points. | E3 | [Retargeting 3D skeletons (4.7 docs)](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/assets_pipeline/retargeting_3d_skeletons.html) [V-search] | Import-time `BoneMap` + `SkeletonProfileHumanoid` auto-mapping; "Overwrite Axis/Rest Fixer" is the key option; "Fix Silhouette" for A-pose models; track-removal options can distort the body | **Adopt:** retarget Quaternius/CMU clips onto the MPFB rig at import; record the import options in the manifest |
| E4 | [AnimationMixer reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.2/classes/class_animationmixer.html) [V-search] | `callback_mode_process = MANUAL` stops automatic processing; `advance(delta)` drives it. A `deterministic` property exists; its exact guarantee was not found | **Adopt:** advance by simulation delta from one director. Test the `deterministic` flag explicitly [U] |
| E5 | [Godot "Animating thousands of fish"](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.4/tutorials/performance/vertex_animation/animating_thousands_of_fish.html) [V-search]; birds survey [R1](06-birds-sky-life-survey.md#1--rendering-and-engine-techniques) [V-page] | Bone-free vertex animation on a MultiMesh with a mask and `mix()` rotation. Multiplying by the mask shrinks the mesh | **Adopt** for Layer A head aim: rotate masked vertices with `mix()` between rest and rotated positions |
| E6 | [OpenVAT for Godot](https://forum.godotengine.org/t/openvat-for-godot-animated-multimeshinstance3d-using-vertex-animation-textures/139440) [V-page]; birds survey R9 | MultiMesh node driven by vertex animation textures. The forum page states no license or renderer. The birds survey found OpenVAT's tool is GPL-3.0, and Compatibility halves per-instance precision and has no blending | **Option** for animated pits workers without skeletons (Layer A2). Write our own small offline baker rather than adopting the GPL tool; spike precision and vertex texture fetch in Compatibility first |
| E7 | [Vertex Animation Studio (Fab)](https://www.fab.com/listings/b1840ab4-0420-4d29-a647-1b0e237df488); [Kavan et al. 2008 polypostors](https://dcgi.fel.cvut.cz/home/zara/papers/KavanEtAll-I3D08.pdf) [V-search] | Industry crowd pattern: baked vertex animation for many distant people, skeletal meshes near the camera. Vendor claim: 200 skeletal meshes = 15 fps vs 10k VAT instances at 60 fps | Confirms the two-layer split. Our 13–18 people are few, so the split is chosen by cost per actor (SC-HUM-05), not crowd size |
| E8 | [Godot renderer overview](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/tutorials/rendering/renderers.html) [V-search] | Compatibility is the least advanced renderer (OpenGL). No public skinning benchmark was found | **Gap:** measure skinned actors at 1080p in SC-HUM-05 |
| E9 | [Octahedral Impostor LOD](https://godotengine.org/asset-library/asset/5528); [SIsilicon impostors (MIT)](https://github.com/SIsilicon/Godot-Octahedral-Impostors) [V-search] | 144 baked views; targets Forward+/Mobile; custom licence. SIsilicon's plugin is a work in progress | **Reject:** static meshes only, wrong renderer, and unnecessary for ≤20 people |
| E10 | [GPU Gems ch. 16](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-iii-materials/chapter-16-real-time-approximations-subsurface-scattering); [AMD GDC 2017](https://gpuopen.com/download/Capsaicin-Cream-GDC2017-TomSanocki-DaveWilkinson.pdf); [UE subsurface model](https://docs.unrealengine.com/4.26/en-US/RenderingAndGraphics/Materials/LightingModels/SubSurface) [V-search] | Wrap lighting is a cheap scattering stand-in; real skin shifts toward red at the light/shadow terminator; forward renderers use textured diffuse wrap instead of screen-space SSS | **Adopt** in the person shader's skin branch: per-channel wrap (red widest) and a warm terminator tint. Compatibility has no screen-space SSS |
| E11 | [Vertex-coloured flag/cloth shaders](https://lukasstratmann.artstation.com/projects/LeR1k5); [Unity SRP wind/alpha conflict](https://plastichub.unity.cn/unity-tech-cn/ScriptableRenderPipeline/commit/7b039c34-8353-4ea3-b09e-6817ab423bbc) [V-search] | Sine offsets weighted by a vertex mask with pinned regions; vertex channels collide when one holds both wind weight and another mask | **Adopt:** shirt hem and sleeve flutter from the existing `wind_vec` global, masked so shoulders and collar stay fixed. Allocate vertex channels once (head weight vs cloth weight) |
