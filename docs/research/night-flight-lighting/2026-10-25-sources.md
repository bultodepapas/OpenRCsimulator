# Night-flight lighting: source ledger

2026-10-25 · FW-RGB-00 · Planning evidence, not rendered or physical validation. [Implementation plan](../../night-flight-lighting-masterplan.md).

## Method and limits

The lead inspected current repository source after graph discovery. Three independent investigations covered RC references, rendering and UX; a second wave reviewed creative direction and architecture/performance. All five requested specialist roles used the runtime-exposed `gpt-6-luna` with `max` reasoning; launches succeeded. Specialists were read-only and did not create independent reports or edit runtime files. The lead reconciled findings into the plan.

Repository base at investigation start: `d7d49b1574f968edab533f8fc34f5a6e9e4920d3`; the shared tree changed concurrently. A pre-existing High graphics edit was preserved. Source checks establish an audit snapshot, not a clean-build/runtime receipt. No new GPU benchmark, pilot trial, rendered qualification or physical measurement was performed.

External pages and indexed text were inspected; manual diagrams and product galleries are linked below. Video links are collected references with chapter metadata where available. Full video playback/frame-by-frame observation was not performed; direct YouTube fetches were unavailable during the lead's check. Do not treat descriptions of sequences below as measured observations of those videos. No photographs, video files or commercial aircraft assets were copied into the repository.

## “Jastusa / Jast USA”: identity result

Exact-name searches did not establish an RC manufacturer, aircraft or lighting system. The [JAST domain](https://jastusa.com/page/about-us) points to a game store and published games; it is not evidence of the requested RC reference.

**Plausible intended reference, not confirmed by the user at writing:** Jase “The Ace” Dussia. His [own site](https://www.jasedussia.com/) establishes the RC pilot identity. [EAA's AirVenture report](https://inspire.eaa.org/2021/07/29/3d-rc-flyer-aces-night-performance/) documents a night performance with smoke and lights. [AMA's XFC 2013 report](https://www.modelaviation.com/xfc2013) names him among night performers with illuminated wings and wingtip fireworks; its [XFCv2 coverage](https://www.modelaviation.com/XFCv2-US-competes) also documents his night-show participation. These independent event references make him a useful verified night-aerobatics reference, but do not prove that “Jastusa” was intended to mean his name.

The lead requested optional clarification while continuing the plan. Until confirmed, keep the alias unresolved and use the verified pilot's name only for attributed source references. Do not create an invented aircraft called Jastusa or label a preset as an official Jase Dussia setup.

| [Official renderer comparison](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/rendering/renderers.html) | Compatibility defaults to eight OmniLights and eight SpotLights per mesh; Mobile has per-mesh/view limits; Forward+ is clustered. | [StandardMaterial3D guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/3d/standard_material_3d.html) | Built-in materials are the first choice for ordinary lens/skin properties; custom patterns need a controlled shader path. |

Local decisive evidence: [`field_lights.gd`](../../../app/render/world/field_lights.gd) reserves one of eight spot slots (`MAX_SPOTS=7`), while [`aircraft_lights.gd`](../../../app/render/world/aircraft_lights.gd) can create paired landing spots. New allocation must account for actual overlap, especially paired installations. [`atmosphere.gd`](../../../app/render/atmosphere.gd) currently disables glow; availability is not default enablement. [`main.gd`](../../../app/main.gd) uses accepted simulation time for World/light policy and a fractional displayed-pose clock for smoke. A new RGB/VFX clock must choose that seam explicitly rather than assuming every current effect already shares fractional time.

## Reconciled specialist decisions

| Question | Lead decision and reason |
| --- | --- |
| Home lighting sheet or Workshop page? | Both concern different data. Reuse Home/Pause for environment conditions; implement the requested aircraft RGB editing in Workshop's existing appearance-profile flow. The UX review's Home-only suggestion would omit the requested per-aircraft editor. No world/time settings enter aircraft profile identity. |
| Glow unsupported or usable? | Exact beta source resolves the fact: usable but simpler in Compatibility. Keep bloom-off baseline and renderer-specific optional tuning. |
| Built-in particle trails or custom fallback? | Built-in trails/subemitters cannot serve the default renderer. Use bounded explicit ribbons; richer enhanced effects remain optional. |
| Radian versus Neon? | Make Radian diffuse and slow; make Neon crisp and segmented. Remove competing Radian periods. |
| Festival continuous sparkle or choreographed highlight? | One paired outward-wave highlight per cycle, then a quiet phase; scene-wide caps include residuals. Spectacle benefits from contrast in time. |
| Exact factory replica or inspired treatment? | Inspired treatment with authored-capability/strip-adaptation labels. No imported model or lighting installation was verified as an exact commercial replica. |

All proposed timings, colors, budgets and pilot thresholds in the masterplan are project design targets. These sources do not validate their performance or realism.
