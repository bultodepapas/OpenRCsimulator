# Independent verification of the native physics audit

**OpenRC Simulator v0.4.0, 28 November 2026.** The independent review retained 39 bounded claims at production commit `8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b`. **18 are confirmed bugs, 12 are documented simplifications, 6 are implementation limitations and 3 are native performance opportunities.** Two previously reported collision mechanisms are corroborated separately. The machine-readable ledger gives the code locations, independent check, evidence files, applicable vehicle and limits for each decision. [Per-finding ledger](../../../research/native-physics-audit-2026-11-28/evidence/verification-ledger.json)

The publication base is `c84c2f8b987617a619f7e77397f025daee28a262`. I verified identical Git objects for the physics, simulation, data, aircraft-native and ground-native trees and the project settings at both commits. All 60 cited source blobs and the 616-file materialized fingerprint also match. The original runtime receipts retain their audit commit. A later helicopter-only component repeat ran on the identical native source at the publication base. [Publication scope](baseline-and-scope.md)

The strongest runtime counterexamples use actual production functions. A passive quad-frame impact increased complete rotor/body kinetic energy by 0.0107881 J. An FPV Angle command jumped 190.4266°/s across a 2×10⁻⁸ rad pose change. The upstream yaw torque limit allowed an integral update farther into saturation. Three valid airship authoring boundaries exposed continued current after charge exhaustion, incorrect near-sphere added mass and an excessively energetic stiff-contact rebound. The engine witnesses use specified operating points and models. None measures normal occurrence or pilot response. ## Four distinctions that change the engineering decision

**Rotor power needs a declared control volume.** For HELI-01 the legacy law admits a shaft-axis inflow and an empirical profile term, while the body force follows the tilted tip-path plane. At the two prescribed ±8° equilibria, $P_{\rm shaft}-\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf U$ is 663.8135 W and 999.5596 W, both positive. The original helicopter source explicitly omits in-plane hub loads and fits effective fuselage drag, and its project contract calls the exact force tilt a kinematic extension requiring qualification. The paired result identifies a model closure to test. Selecting one requires consistent inflow/work definitions and measured trim power. HELI-02 has a different verdict because the caller contradicts the project’s explicit clearance contract. NASA’s independent planar construction demonstrates sensitivity to downstream wake geometry and provides no calibration of this particular X-Cell. [Original helicopter contract](../helicopter/HELI-05/README.md) · [Ground-effect input contract](../helicopter/HELI-04/README.md) · [NASA NDARC theory, §12-4.1.2](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20250010468/downloads/Johnson%20TP-202510468.pdf)

**Frame impact requires complete kinetic energy.** With the published signed vertical rotor convention, the kinetic energy of translating body and rotors adds $\sum_i(\tfrac12J_i\Omega_i^2-s_iJ_i\Omega_i\omega_z)$ to the locked-inertia rigid-body expression. Holding a relative shaft speed fixed while changing body yaw alters the inertial speed of an unstruck free shaft. On the stock gate fixture the rigid contribution falls 0.0064924 J while the complete value increases 0.0107881 J. The zero-shaft case dissipates energy. A physically coupled impulse should keep each unstruck inertial axial rate continuous while enforcing the passive contact law. [Actual production response](../../../research/native-physics-audit-2026-11-28/probes/drone_frame_energy.gd)

**Electrical ports and control saturation are separate issues.** The full-charge FPV model permits a floating bridge node and algebraically closes its signed work balances. Presenting that node as a battery terminal creates the ELEC-02 reporting defect. The controller also explicitly predicts static thrust using sampled open-circuit voltage. Independently solving the four loaded rotors at full duty yields 17.11467 N per rotor at 20.24290 V, versus 24.13121 N from the OCV map. At 0.2 duty it gives 1.52297 and 1.53753 N respectively. A zero-resistance pack recovers the map. This declared control-authority approximation uses estimated resistance and cannot measure the upstream anti-windup failure. CTRL-02 is established directly by the raw, clipped and allocated requests on the same actual controller sample. [Electrical report](domains/electric-edf.md) · [Control-law report](domains/flight-controls.md)

**Airship admission matters at both ends of a calculation.** The 1−10⁻¹⁰ prolate shape completes derivation with wrong coefficients. At 1−10⁻¹² the document loader accepts the geometry and the final mass factorization refuses it. For the separate contact fixture, $m=1.225\pi/3$ kg, $k=10^6$ N/m and zero damping give $t_\mathrm{contact}=\pi\sqrt{m/k}=3.558215$ ms. Evaluating the four unilateral RK4 slopes for an initially 1 m/s descent gives $v_\mathrm{out}=1-h^2k/(3m)=-3.511194532$ m/s on the first 240 Hz step. The actual model/integrator reproduces the resulting 12.328487 energy ratio. At 3,840 Hz the ratio is 0.99984342. The default 450 N/m, damped gear and the physical near-sphere limit require different evidence. ## Claims rejected or left open

- **Control-surface footprint.** A commanded aileron displacement outside its physical hinge is a geometric model choice. A real wing can still acquire lift changes inboard through induced circulation. The Extra result establishes no 7.02° physical root deflection or global roll-rate error.
- **Helicopter power and descent.** A positive or negative residual against the isolated tilted-disc reference cannot establish spontaneous energy creation. The f=1 vortex-ring seam cannot be assigned to the stock f=0 trainer. High-advance-ratio low-head concerns need a compatible flight trace before estimating their incidence.
- **Multirotor work and telemetry.** Static isolated rotor thrust can exceed its steady useful-power budget in a prescribed climb. The complete vehicle’s energy balance includes other forces and storage. Similarly, the floating bridge result cannot establish physical cell overvoltage or a pack heating rate. The existing contact protocol explicitly samples a shaft endpoint, so DRONE-04 identifies the consequence of that policy against an event-time alternative.
- **Manufacturer data.** Two source rows establish an RPM-conditioned static thrust mismatch. Hover-stick position, installed thrust, motor phase current and endurance require matched hardware. A different tandem-rotor experiment cannot set the stock FPV neighbor-loss percentage. The gasoline proxy’s corrected intercept also requires engine-specific flow data. [F50/F5146 manufacturer table](https://www.ligpower.com/product/f50-5inch-racing-drone-motor.html)
- **Controllers.** One upstream-limited update shows the anti-windup blind spot. The ±0.06 N·m integral clamp precludes unbounded growth. Settling time, overshoot and the flight effects of finite actuator response remain open.
- **Airship cases.** The more extreme near-sphere model is refused when its derived mass matrix fails positive-definite factorization. The stiff-contact energy ratio belongs to the extreme admitted fixture, leaving default landing performance unmeasured.
- **Native CPU.** Eighteen hundred thirteen bit differences rule out freely substituting the shaft torque for the separately grouped blade torque. Allocation counts, file inventory, one stage flag, a backend answer or nominal FPS cannot supply a target latency or prove a completely native accepted frame. The latest guard revision caps fast traversal per root and can fall back to full watches. It does not globally cap aggregate work or reject all affected configurations. ## Controls and negative findings

The checks below give positive evidence for retaining specific behavior. They do not extend to every admitted state.

**Rigid body and reference points.** The common convention is right-handed NED world coordinates, FRD body coordinates and a scalar-first body-to-world quaternion. Wind and gravity enter body axes with the transpose rotation. Local point speed is $\mathbf v_{\rm CG}+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r$, and a force contributes $\mathbf r\times\mathbf F$ about the chosen reference. For fixed locked inertia and internal relative rotor momentum, the angular balance is $\mathbf J\dot{\boldsymbol\omega}=\mathbf M_{\rm ext}-\boldsymbol\omega\times(\mathbf J\boldsymbol\omega+\mathbf h_{\rm rel})-\dot{\mathbf h}_{\rm rel}$, with the relative derivative taken in body axes. Open systems additionally require consistent mass and angular-momentum flux. The demonstrated omission in PROP-01 is specific to its gas-propeller handoff. The manufactured free-fall/spin benchmark gives 240 Hz world-velocity error of 1.444×10⁻⁵ m/s and quaternion geodesic error of 2.965×10⁻⁷ rad. Both fall by about sixteen on doubling the rate. Unit quaternion norm alone would miss the phase error. [Rigid-body, event and efflux benchmarks](domains/mathematics.md) · [NASA NESC flight simulation check cases](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150001263)

**Mass and inertia.** The co-moving distributed-fuel fixture checks body plus shed linear and angular momentum about a fixed inertial origin. Its 240 Hz residuals are 1.95×10⁻⁶ kg·m/s and 2.36×10⁻⁵ kg·m²/s. Omitting the reference-point correction yields approximately 0.2435 kg·m/s and 5.61 kg·m²/s even as the integration step shrinks. The stock airship force balance includes helium mass once and applies gross buoyancy at the center of buoyancy. At illustrative sea-level density, 2.502001 m³ gives 30.0569 N gross lift and the default 0.080 kg heaviness gives 0.78453 N net downward. This preserves an intentionally constant-volume, release-atmosphere model. The geared helicopter stand separately closed its instantaneous angular-work identity to 2.84×10⁻¹⁴ W. [Mass and contact report](domains/mechanics.md) · [Helicopter work controls](domains/helicopter.md)

**Atmosphere and air data.** The standard vertical column takes geometric height as field elevation minus NED down, converts to geopotential height, then evaluates tropospheric pressure and temperature. Independently substituting the configured constants gives 281.6510 K, 89,876.28 Pa and 1.111660 kg/m³ at 1,000 m, and 255.6755 K, 54,048.26 Pa and 0.736429 kg/m³ at 5,000 m. For the uniform humid-air resolver, partial dry and water-vapor densities add to aerodynamic density, while engine charge uses dry density. Assigned uniform atmospheres and the standard vertical column have distinct contracts. The reviewed paths establish units and signs within those operating domains. Turbulent weather accuracy, boundary-layer wind, and site-specific profiles require observations. [Native column](../../../research/native-kernel/src/nk_weather.hpp) · [Atmosphere resolver](../../../app/physics/atmosphere.gd)

**Time integration and contacts.** The outer input clock is 240 Hz. Smooth stage dynamics and right-endpoint-held inputs have different convergence rates. The manufactured depletion event has a 1.291 μs physical event-time error at 240 Hz despite a picosecond-scale bracket on the numerical map. For ground mechanics the isotropic undisturbed equilibrium gives negligible heading difference, and the identical-wall-load comparison agrees with zero friction. These controls identify the specific failure mechanisms without calling the general RK4 law, every static tire or every contact defective. [Numerical controls](domains/mathematics.md) · [Ground controls](domains/mechanics.md)

**Propulsion and energy.** The six finite glow installations satisfy the chosen nonnegative steady actuator-disc lower-bound check across the sampled $J=0\ldots2$ grid. The finite glow passive-blade route returns signed stopped-blade load. The older tabular stopped-blade limitation therefore belongs to its own model. The P100 static manufacturer anchors and the two equal-RPM fuel outputs do not identify transient exhaust or fuel dynamics. A separate turbine casing-torque or energy assertion would need angular/momentum flux and an actual engine control volume. [Combustion, glow and turbine coverage](domains/combustion-turbine.md)

## Implementation and release interpretation

The priority plan appropriately starts with operating-point equivalence, passivity, supported configuration admission and geometric continuity before proposing detailed empirical laws. Proposed tolerances define prospective tests. Proposed engineering days are estimates. Unqualified timing or physical effect has no measured value in this audit. Pure C++ components should own new production numerical laws. The existing reference scripts, analytical controls and validated source measurements can remain independent oracles.

For a changed law, run the discriminating fixture through its installed family and transaction path, preserve the 240 Hz control sample during internal refinement, and compare eligible reference/native trajectories and restores. For a numerical refactor, check operation-sensitive outputs, stage ownership, event ordering, failure/rollback and accepted commit before measuring the target. For a physical claim, record vehicle geometry, load/inertia, propeller, actuator commands, atmospheric conditions and independent held-out observations. These are separate acceptance conditions in the [implementation plan](improvement-plan.md) and [reproduction guide](../../../research/native-physics-audit-2026-11-28/README.md).
