## Fixed-wing acceptance must exercise the intended load path

**AERO-04 remains a documented simplification.** A proposed 12-strips-per-side runtime test exceeded `nk_kernel.cpp`'s 16-total-station limit; spatial-flow admission additionally requires six. More subtly, the attached-flow dispatcher can return the authored global coefficient, so a passing installed roll derivative can hide the local-strip error the test is intended to detect. The revised plan forces the local path, checks its derivative, then verifies the installed blend. Refinement is an independent offline quadrature or exact integration across control edges; increasing native capacity is separately scoped.

For the stated Extra tapered wing, the exact hinged first area moment is **0.074178415228 m³**. Applying overlap area at three full-strip centroids gives **0.073028212258 m³**, a **1.55059%** geometry difference. This measures that quadrature choice, not flight accuracy. Selig's relevant panel/control discussion is **§IV.A, pp. 5–6**; the source index's previous section label is corrected. [Selig 2010](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pubs/Selig-2010-AIAA-2010-7635-FullEnvelopeAeroSim.pdf)

**AERO-02 remains a documented simplification.** A narrower wing-only vertical disturbance of ±0.5 m/s at 15 m/s stays inside the provisional three-degree attached domain. The independent source is ±2.250416412 m/s, reaching ±2.235253226 m/s after 60 ticks from zero with a 0.05 s lag. Uniform, tail-only, mirrored and checkpoint controls distinguish source transport from direct tail response. The committed wake updates once per accepted outer tick. **AERO-01's** no-wash control must zero axial, swirl, sidewash and transported wash; a stopped shaft with lingering wake is separate. ## Helicopter geometry and power boundaries

**HELI-02 remains a confirmed input-contract bug.** Both terrain normal and normal clearance enter the downstream ray intersection. At vertical h/R=1.1 and forward speed V=3vh, the independent distances are **10.02074945R** over flat ground, **2.92473740R** on a plane rising 15° along the wake, and no forward intersection on the opposite slope. The slope cases check geometry only: the cited NDARC empirical construction assumes horizontal ground. The existing Hayden factor is **0.9832958363 at 3R**. A hard cutoff to one would introduce a **0.0167041637** discontinuity. The plan now calls for an explicitly versioned continuous taper, joint boundary checks and consistent stage/diagnostic/restore evaluation. It does not claim the candidate is calibrated for the X-Cell. [Helicopter equations, source anchors and NASA references](domains/helicopter.md)

**HELI-01 remains a documented simplification.** The work-port check is `F·Uhub = F·UCG + (r×F)·ω` in uniform air, with pure hub couples accounted separately. It tests frame consistency; the existing tilted-disc residual does not prove spontaneous creation of complete-system energy.

**HELI-04 remains a drivetrain simplification.** The analytic inelastic clutch oracle uses a fixed carrier and common gear-referred coordinates: `Jdriver=n²Jengine`, `ωdriver=Ωengine/n`. Its manufactured case gives **109.266258659 rad/s** and **7.394474410 J** nonnegative engagement loss. A free carrier or differently aligned shafts requires the coupled angular-momentum/energy impulse instead. Equal-speed engagement needs an absolute zero-loss tolerance.

**HELI-03's** off-design pole still needs an explicit supported envelope or an identified dimensional branch; a clamp is insufficient evidence. **HELI-05's** enabled-law numerical work must include its C=0.75 seam even at zero instability increment, both crossing directions, grazing and coincident stop. The original sixteen-RHS experiment used known event timing and does not establish automatic detector cost. Autorotation, VRS and actuator parameters retain separate physical identification gates. [Model-scale identification methodology](https://publications.ri.cmu.edu/storage/publications/pub_files/pub2/mettler_bernard_2000_1/mettler_bernard_2000_1.pdf)

## Multirotor energy, power and event controls

**DRONE-03 remains a confirmed coupled-impact defect.** A balanced-shaft negative control exposes a weak acceptance gate: the old frame impulse can dissipate total energy and conserve total angular momentum while imparting **8.64e−6 N·m·s** of unintended generalized impulse to each unstruck shaft. The new independent generalized-mass/Schur-complement checks require every unstruck shaft's impulse to vanish, in addition to energy, angular momentum and absolute axial-spin continuity. The imbalanced original witness remains **+0.010788070268 J**; the coupled candidate oracle gives **−0.006462519405 J**. This is an equation oracle for a proposed response, not an implemented repair. Normal restitution is checked after its normal impulse, then friction work and final nonpenetration after the declared sequential friction stage. [Current production function and original eight assertions](domains/multirotor.md)

**DRONE-02 remains a documented propeller simplification.** Efficiency ≤1 is necessary but weak. In the declared steady isolated unshrouded positive axial branch, ideal momentum theory gives

\[
P_{\min}=\frac{T}{2}\left(V+\sqrt{V^2+\frac{2T}{\rho A}}\right).
\]

At Ω=1000 rad/s and V=8 m/s the current model's useful efficiency is **0.767611**, but its **16.125875 W** shaft power is below the **18.559785 W** ideal minimum. The hover control needs 10.710989 W and passes. This bound is not transferred unchanged to shrouds, ground effect, neighboring wakes, descent or unsteady stored-flow energy. [MIT actuator-disc derivation](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node86.html)

A changed torque/thrust law also affects the yaw allocator. `Q/T=dQ/dT` throughout a positive-thrust interval requires proportional Q and T; coincidence at one trim is insufficient. A synthetic nonlinear case yields **0.0048 N·m** actual yaw against **0.0042 N·m** under the frozen ratio. The plan now shares the versioned map across trim, inversion, allocation prediction and anti-windup, with two trims and finite excursions. This is a dependency of the proposed model change, not another stock bug.

**DRONE-04 remains a collision-model limitation.** A manufactured capsule overlap spanning 0.1–0.3 tick can contain rotor activation at **915.468223 µs** while both overlap endpoints straddle the 300 rad/s rule. The component trajectory integrates the published motor/pack equations; it is not a recorded race. One-sided positive-duration eligibility, exact threshold controls, event-state charge/energy and remaining-step commit are now explicit. **DRONE-01/05** still require independent bench runs and isolated/pair/full-quad holdouts at stated supply, height and spacing. Neither a same-run fit nor another tandem geometry's deficit identifies the X1 installation. [Experimental source index](sources.md)

## Propulsion and electrical boundaries

**PROP-01/02 remain confirmed bugs.** The hub-flow correction must feed thrust, shaft load, wake source, friction and source-off preview consistently without doubling rigid rotation on explicit spatial inputs. Brake idle power must use the admitted full capped torque model. New binding-cap, signed-power and unchanged fixed-state torque controls complement the original depletion/mass-property tests. [Equations and source files](domains/combustion-turbine.md)

**PROP-03 remains a documented simplification.** At 30 m/s the current off-engine P-51 model has repelling/restoring roots near **988/3,243 rpm**, and the F4U near **867/2,187 rpm**. An endpoint-only bracket can miss both. Signed locked torque, static friction and branch stability must supplement a power inequality, which at zero RPM cannot test breakaway torque.

**PROP-04 remains a turbine surrogate simplification.** An independent piecewise-exponential/linear-fuel integral confirms the old six-second step traces. A fuel-metering-only repair leaves restrained-stand RPM/thrust timing unchanged before depletion if governor and thrust maps stay fixed. Separate metering and thrust-dynamics acceptance. Many et al. report about **5 Hz distinct ECU samples**, **100 Hz thrust** and about **0.5 s relative clock drift in eight minutes**; resampling is not higher-bandwidth evidence. Require independently calibrated fuel mass, synchronized traces and uncertainty with units/coverage. The proposed 5% integral and 0.2 s timing targets use `|error|+U95≤target`; less informative instrumentation yields an inconclusive result. Different JetCat engine/ECU idle specifications are not interchangeable calibration data. [Many et al., §§3.1–3.2](https://www.dglr.de/publikationen/2022/550281.pdf) · [JetCat 2019 catalog](https://www.jetcat.de/jetcat/Kataloge/190905%20JetCat%20ENGINES%202019.pdf)

**ELEC-01a/b remain confirmed launch-input bugs.** Dry temperature/pressure pairs hold both density and engine charge ratio constant: the legacy common **11,380.9289 rpm** root differs from native installed-temperature equilibria **11,293.2573/11,504.0672 rpm**. `_hand_launch_rpm` performs a direct solve; cruise/trim acquisition uses the separately reviewed cache, whose source identity omits temperature. No installed warm-cache sequence was run. Warm/cold cache order, both invalidation routes, correct hub flow and restoring-root controls form the future acceptance bundle.

**ELEC-02 remains a reporting bug with a separately simplified circuit.** In its full-pack stand a 0.6 A auxiliary consumes **41.07106 W** at the modeled link or **15.12 W** at the pack tabs. Moving that current silently creates **25.95106 W** of unassigned ledger power. A display fix must preserve the existing numerical plant and cumulative counters; a topology revision names auxiliary, heat, limiter and storage ports and versions their meanings. These values are not measured hardware heating. **ELEC-03's** diagnostic still needs source current distinguished from motor current; its inferred duty is not a measured ESC duty. [Electric findings and manufacturer boundary](domains/electric-edf.md) · [maxon current/power-port explanation](https://support.maxongroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016161594-Motor-controllers-Input-current-versus-Motor-current-Power-Conversion)

**ELEC-04 remains a missing capability, tracked by existing EDF work in PR #18.** NASA's negative high-J net-thrust observations were bracket-tare corrected and excluded from the chosen DOE design above J=1.24. Preserve observed off-design signs with a supported branch; do not extrapolate the selected thrust fit as validated there. Require separate shaft load and installed net force, finite locked/off limits and no duplicate inlet/nacelle loads. ## Mechanics: geometry, response and solver units

**MECH-01/02/04 remain confirmed defects.** A tire solver's force-update tolerance in newtons is not an acceleration residual in m/s²; the brake-update threshold is in N·m. The plan now distinguishes those convergence statements. The stock wall cap is **80 sphere/box tests per stage**, and a 1/120 s wall-step maximum alone does not subdivide a 1/240 s tick. A valid steered forward vector and axle cannot both be singular against the road plane: the loaded singular branch can use `axle_world × normal` with the established sign, without a world-axis fallback. The airborne fix must retain later wheel drive/brake/gyro terms. [Mechanics source anchors](domains/mechanics.md) · [Generalized contact-space equations](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/3.8.0/computation/#constraint-solver)

**MECH-03 remains the original confirmed collision defect, with a newly verified geometric subcase.** Under synchronized linear CG motion and full-tick quaternion nlerp, a candidate envelope reaches **134.531202 µm** while the actual synthetic witness reaches **134.656289 µm**. The independent rational polynomial finds entry/exit at **0.081552969546/0.085144882281 tick**. Its geometric incoming speed is **0.01671688 m/s**, below the ordinary nonterminal response's 0.05 m/s floor. This proves the broad-phase geometric bound is unsound; it does not prove a lost ordinary response event for that fixture. The original stock spinner supplies its separate event witness.

The new plan begins with a conservative enclosure, preserves full-tick nlerp time and independently isolates static-box plane roots. A second control has clear endpoints and two real crossings; a shifted face is a true miss. Response velocity, true initial overlap, stable ties and work exhaustion remain distinct. V-MECH-CAP and V-MECH-LAKE retain their prior scopes and do not become new current full-flight results.
