## DRONE-01 · Static thrust is high around the shipped hover point

**Implementation limitation · High priority · Medium effort, moderate handling risk.** The generator fits T = kₜΩ² through the 50% and 100% vendor rows. The published F50/F5146 table has ten rows. The active plant uses that single coefficient at every positive speed. The file's most-loaded equilibrium rotor speed is 933.25 rad/s (8,911.9 RPM), below both fitted rows. At the two vendor RPMs that bound that hover point, the comparison isolates the propeller by using measured RPM directly.

| Vendor RPM | Vendor thrust | Stock law | Excess |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5,436 | 0.37265 N | 0.50141 N | 34.55% |
| 10,238 | 1.49061 N | 1.77853 N | 19.32% |
| 14,754 | 3.34407 N | 3.69361 N | 10.45% |

The discrepancy is measured against the manufacturer's bench on the named hardware. It does not identify aircraft throttle error or prove that a flying quad has a particular hover RPM. A six-knot trial interpolating T/Ω² by RPM left the 20%, 40%, 60% and 80% rows out. On those four rows it reduced mean absolute percentage error from **7.281% to 0.461%**. Its worst error was 1.352%, its largest absolute error 0.0250 N. This is interpolation within one vendor run. The torque display has 0.01 N·m resolution and its low-speed relative accuracy is much poorer. `research/drone-racing/openrc-fpv5-x1/sources.json:18–32`, `derive.py:122–141,247–257`, `app/data/multirotors/openrc-fpv5-x1.json:751–773,811–836`, `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:444–449`. [Manufacturer's ten F50 2150 KV/F5146 measurements](https://www.ligpower.com/product/f50-5inch-racing-drone-motor.html)

**Native work and acceptance.** Prepare prop-specific coefficient tables once, evaluate them in the four-shaft native stage, and use the same forward and inverse law in the actuator map and asymmetric loaded-hover calculation. Update the owning generator, data hash, law ID, and finite low/high-RPM policy. Keep the existing law for earlier data and replay. Recheck `app/physics/multirotor/actuator_map.gd:47–74` and `app/physics/multirotor_data.gd:148–196,729–765`. A proposed first gate is ≤5% on every held-out thrust row, continuous monotone thrust, bounded torque residuals in absolute N·m and zero output at rest for the static fixture. Request repeat bench readings around 5–15k RPM before a hardware-specific hover claim. A short table adds a bounded lookup per rotor. No speed benefit was measured. Other props need their own data.

## DRONE-02 · Static thrust and torque continue into axial flight

**Deliberate documented simplification · High priority for racing-envelope fidelity · Medium to large effort.** With intact forward-spinning rotors, T and Q depend only on Ω². The hub's in-plane velocity changes horizontal force. Axial inflow, rotor lift and load torque stay uncoupled. Density is computed for airframe drag. The stock FPV session exposes only calm or steady uniform wind at the reference density, so a variable-density defect in stock weather would be an overstatement. Axial motion already exists on the stock route. `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:20–26,374–385,438–465`, `app/physics/multirotor/airframe.gd:8–14`, `app/sim/multirotor/session.gd:229–250`, `app/physics/wind_config.gd:71–81,303–318`.

With Ω = 1,000 rad/s and 15 m/s of climb along the thrust axis in calm air, the stock prop produces T = 1.54730 N and Q = 0.0161259 N·m. At J = 0.72756, propulsive work T·V is **23.2095 W** while shaft work Q·Ω is **16.1259 W**, a ratio of **1.4393**. A steady isolated motoring propeller loses power to the wake, so its useful propulsive work must be below its shaft input. This counterexample concerns one rotor at a prescribed operating point, independent of airframe drag or the battery. It does not establish net energy creation by the complete aircraft. UIUC's different DA4002 5×4.92 prop at 6,041 RPM measured Cₜ falling from 0.1413 at J ≈ 0.193 to 0.08976 at J ≈ 0.714. It supplies a separate coefficient-lookup test on its own geometry. [MIT propulsion derivation](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/SPRING/propulsion/notes/node86.html) · [UIUC wind-tunnel data](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-2/data/da4002_5x4.92_1195ga_6041.txt)

**Native work and acceptance.** Use direction-resolved hub air velocity, a declared static reference density and matched T/Q data. A first native version can use prepared Cₜ and Cₚ tables by advance ratio and RPM, with oblique-flow and reverse/descent branches limited to their measured domains. Compute T = ρn²D⁴Cₜ and Q = ρn²D⁵Cₚ/(2π), with n in revolutions/s. Feed this same torque to the shaft, keeping the existing reaction/gyro coupling. At zero RPM evaluate a declared finite stopped-prop branch. Separate controlled windmilling from powered climb. Verify 0 ≤ TV/(QΩ) ≤ 1 within roundoff on the positive quasi-steady axial branch, T/Q continuity at the hover join, reference-density agreement, and reference/native parity. For a selected-build claim, target ≤10% held-out thrust and torque error above instrument resolution across the measured envelope and report the actual uncertainty. UZH's 400 Hz dataset includes individual shaft speeds, derivatives and body accelerations for testing trends after FLU-to-FRD conversion. Its vehicle differs from X1. NASA's isolated-rotor descent law gives a mean-inflow comparator, not a measured turbulent FPV propwash signature. [UZH data and frames](https://rpg.ifi.uzh.ch/neuro_bem/Readme.html) · [NASA TP-2005-213477](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20060024029)

## DRONE-03 · A nonterminal frame bounce can increase rotor-plus-airframe kinetic energy

**Confirmed code-level physics bug · High priority for graded contacts · Medium effort, moderate regression risk.** Normal and friction impulses use the locked inertia and leave relative shaft speeds fixed. Energy guards consider only the rigid body. Earlier in the same plant the leaf impulse correctly uses the effective inertia and updates every shaft to retain its inertial spin. For an external frame-only impulse with finite motor torque, the untouched rotor's inertial axial speed r − sᵢΩᵢ is continuous. The complete energy is ½m|v|² + ½ωᵀI_lockedω + Σ(½JᵢΩᵢ² − r sᵢJᵢΩᵢ). A body-only energy decrease can hide an increase in this quantity. `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:469–477,537–550,1196–1211,1477–1484,1515–1528,1543–1569`, `app/sim/scenery_collision.gd:307–345`. [General multibody impact equations](https://underactuated.mit.edu/multibody.html)

**Stock geometry witness.** Use the midspan of the upper banner of the south-west gate on Club Field's Practice oval 1. Its lower edge is 3 m above local terrain. Place a level X1 at heading 80°, CG 21 mm below this edge, closing at 0.2 m/s east. The upper front-right edge catches the banner while the prop discs pass underneath. The gate normal in body axes is (−cos 10°, −sin 10°, 0). The clipped frame contact is approximately (0.071968, 0.072592, −0.026993) m. Initial body rates are zero, with admitted shaft speeds (1,600, 600, 600, 1,600) rad/s in model order. The gate uses restitution 0.2 and friction 0.4. The current normal and tangential impulses give **ΔK_body = −0.0064924 J**, **ΔK_complete = +0.0107881 J** and a **1.23432 rad/s** instantaneous axial speed change in every untouched rotor. The coupled calculation gives **ΔK_complete = −0.0064625 J** and preserves each rotor's inertial axial speed. The before-impact complete energy is about 20.45 J. This is a constructed contact using stock geometry and supported state values. Frequency during ordinary piloting is unmeasured. `app/data/race/tracks/openrc-practice-oval-1.json:9–12`, `app/sim/race/track.gd:271–296`, `app/sim/multirotor/frame_obstacle_contact.gd:370–420`, `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:1040–1057,1081–1098,1640–1647`.

**Native work and acceptance.** Use I_eff = I_locked − ΣJᵢ e_z e_zᵀ for external frame normal and friction impulses, then update Ωᵢ by sᵢΔr before testing full energy. A blade-contact impulse has its own direct spin moment and needs separate treatment. Use the prepared inverse and return a coupled body/shaft frame. Require |Δ(r − sᵢΩᵢ)| ≤ 10⁻⁹ rad/s for every untouched shaft and ΔK_complete ≤ 10⁻¹⁰ max(1 J, K_before) across both spin imbalances, equal-speed and zero-speed controls, and nonzero products of inertia. Check restitution and Coulomb bounds together, then replay graded gate/terrain contacts. The correction affects external impacts with yaw impulse, including electric custom quads. It adds four scalar updates per relevant impulse. A runtime saving from reusing the prepared inverse needs profiling.

## DRONE-04 · Swept prop contacts use end-of-tick shaft speed

**Implementation gap in the declared endpoint law · Medium to high priority · Medium effort for eligibility, large for full event integration.** The contact geometry locates the first intersection. Candidate end-of-tick shaft speed decides whether to sweep the entire interval and whether to apply wear. An impact also rewinds the body to its collision fraction while the shaft speeds, pack charge/energy and event clock can still describe the full interval. The current deterministic endpoint policy documents part of this behavior. Event-time eligibility is a different, testable target. Both strict and graded paths use the same swept eligibility check. `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:555–573,733–776,1136–1156,1225–1261`, `app/sim/multirotor/swept_prop_contact.gd:14–18,63–83`. **Reproducible witness.** A 5 mm radius vertical capsule, outboard of the front-right hub, intersects the swept disc only from fractions 0.10 to 0.30 of a level 0.0625 m chord. Relative to the initial hub the capsule is 0.012500 m forward and 0.0694895 m right. The frame clears. ## DRONE-05 · Ground proximity and neighboring rotor wakes have no aerodynamic input

**Deliberate documented simplification · Medium priority, contingent on selected-build data · Large effort and high calibration risk.** The active rotor loop sees its own Ω/health, one uniform wind sample and rotational sweep. Ground is used for physical support/strikes. Holding the hub velocity and shaft state fixed, moving the ground under one rotor has zero effect on its aerodynamic loads. Altering another shaft's speed has zero direct wake effect on this rotor. The battery and rigid-body responses can still couple those rotors indirectly. The stock nearest spacing is 0.14518 m, or 2.2415 radii, with a 15.64 mm tip gap. In the stock pad equilibrium the disc height is 21.5 mm (0.332R). `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:20–26,374–388,438–466,638–648`, `app/data/multirotors/openrc-fpv5-x1.json:773–797,838–874`. The planned advanced law is identified in `docs/plans/drone-racing.md:63–91`.

Sánchez-Cuevas and colleagues measured a pitch disturbance on two multirotors with a plate beneath one rotor at h/R = 2. Atte and colleagues measured aft-rotor deficits in wind-tunnel tandem, plus and X layouts. Their largest reported 24% deficit was a **tandem** case at 2.1R spacing. These establish direction and experimental design on their respective vehicles. They do not quantify the X1's force or pitch. [Ground-effect experiments](https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/1823056) · [Rotor interaction experiments](https://doi.org/10.4050/F-0078-2022-1182)

**Native work and acceptance.** After DRONE-02, qualify per-hub terrain distance and orientation plus a rotor-specific near-surface load law. Handle near-pad saturation, sloped terrain and an isolated lifted plate. Independently test a deposited wake or measured pairwise interaction using geometry and the upwind rotor's load. Four rotors require at most 12 directed pairs per evaluation before pruning. Reuse the native helicopter isolated-inflow helper only in demonstrated domains. Keep reverse operation and rapidly changing near-wake behavior separate from steady validation. Compare h/R = 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 at several RPMs, fixed-RPM versus fixed-power results, isolated versus paired rotors, opposite flight directions, and roll/pitch signs under partial ground. Require held-out T, Q and pitch/roll moment errors to improve over the isolated model beyond twice the experimental repeatability. Count work and measure whole-tick p50/p95/p99 near the pad and in open air. The implementation may add cost and warrants promotion only after measured benefit.

## Checked interfaces and remaining evidence

- **Rotor placement, frames and ordinary gyro coupling.** The rigid body stores NED position, FRD velocity/rates and a scalar-first quaternion from body to NED. The data loader sorts explicit rotor IDs, admits two of each spin and vertical thrust axes, links each shaft's inertia and location to one inventory component, and checks wrench rank/hover balance. A positive CCW rotor has h_z = −sJΩ. Each hub receives v_air + ω × r, its force has moment r × F, and the free-air electrical stage accounts for aero torque, motor acceleration and ω × (Iω + h) once. The audit found no positive-thrust or gyro sign reversal on this route. All four share the declared static aerodynamic law.
