# Aerospace review of the native fixed-wing model

The strongest corrections concern wing downwash in a powered approach and direct aileron actuation outside its physical span. The first changes how two existing force models compose. The second uses stock geometry to improve where a commanded control changes incidence. Two further candidates cover spatial gust feedback and Reynolds-dependent aerodynamic data.

This review used commit `8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b`. The accompanying `aerospace_probe.py` compiles C++17 against its native headers, imports the relevant stock JSON and runs frozen-state component experiments. Run `python3 research/native-physics-audit-2026-11-28/probes/aerospace_probe.py .` from the repository root on Linux with g++. No native flight, stock flight-error magnitude or performance improvement was measured in these experiments. Costs, tolerances and performance expectations below are proposals.

## AERO-01 · Ground-effect downwash is lost in the powered-tail increment

**Bug.** The stage applies ground effect to the base longitudinal force, then adds the difference between washed and free tail loads. In the base tail incidence, the wing’s speed-weighted lift source W is scaled by the local ground factor σ. Both sides of the separately calculated wake increment use unscaled W. Even when ground effect has already altered the generation of W, σ describes the distinct change in downwash per unit wing lift. The intended increment is T(washed, σW) − T(free, σW). Stock Extra 300S, P-51D and F4U declare ground effect, wing downwash and prop slipstream. The Jensen has no declared prop slipstream.

**Evidence.** Trace `research/native-kernel/src/nk_aero.hpp:155–194,642–683,908–926`, `nk_stage.hpp:303–314,341–349` and `nk_wake.hpp:187–209,255–277,483–509,574–585`. Stock declarations appear in `app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json:1304,1355,1843`, `p51d_mustang_120.json:1646,1697,2279` and `f4u_corsair_250.json:1739,1808,2514`. The owning plan already records this interaction in `docs/plans/advanced-ground-flight-physics.md:480–486`.

The probe uses the Extra’s segmented tail wake and geometry at 15 m/s, 3° incidence, zero body rates and 1.225 kg/m³. Prescribed values are W = 6 m/s, thrust = 16 N, shaft torque = 0.30 N·m and 9,000 rpm. These exercise the force law without identifying a real engine operating point. The table gives the installed wake increment and the result when both tail evaluations receive the same ground context as the base. Positive body z points down.

Wind-tunnel work on two low-aspect-ratio models also found geometry-dependent changes in near-ground pitching and flap-related downwash. Lifting-line work establishes that planform and lift coefficient affect ground-effect predictions. Neither supplies an Extra tail correction magnitude. [NACA TN 4044, figures 13–20](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930084743/downloads/19930084743.pdf) · [Phillips and Hunsaker](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/mae_facpub/87/)

**Native work and qualification.** Carry the effective downwash from a single stage context to the free and washed evaluations, including both segmented and smooth/swirl paths, finite lag and the instantaneous-source sentinel. Estimate 2–4 engineer-days. The arithmetic is constant per stage with the existing ground factor. Risks are multiplying σ twice and disturbing zero-thrust or far-ground behavior. Check all six increment components against paired tail evaluations within 10⁻¹⁰ N or N·m on this fixture, exact zero at no wash, and unchanged behavior beyond the existing ground-effect ceiling. Sweep height, bank and positive/negative lift. Run a separately identified native powered-approach log at 240/480/960 Hz before attributing a flight-level effect.

## AERO-04 · Conventional ailerons directly deflect unhinged root strips

**Simplification with a stock geometry mismatch.** `research/native-kernel/src/nk_aero.hpp:343–369,519–549,783–795,856–875` applies the same signed direct incidence shift to every strip on a semispan. The Extra’s source geometry places its aileron from y = 0.2620 to 0.8042 m on a 0.8128 m semispan. Its innermost equal-area strip extends only to 0.220662 m. The affected wing-area fractions on the three right strips, using the modeled trapezoid, are 0, 0.8280 and 0.9781. These fractions describe footprint. Chord ratio and hinge geometry also affect control effectiveness. See `assets/aircraft/extra-300s-60/geometry.json:16–24,599–602`, `app/physics/aircraft_data.gd:842–869` and `app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json:1215–1220`.

At 10° incidence and 15° aileron, the native header changes the direct incidence at y = 0.107788, 0.346397 and 0.637925 m from 10° to 17.018635° at **each** station. The stock calibration in `research/aero/aileron_effectiveness.py:130–159` targets integrated attached roll and uses that same per-side assumption. The Avanti similarly reduces its position-dependent control geometry to one effectiveness in `research/avanti-s/av06/derive_physics.py:378–384`. A partial-span control can still change unhinged-root *lift* through circulation. NACA’s measured pressure increments actually extend inboard of the physical control on a 47.5° swept wing. Correct the placement of imposed deflection and retain induced coupling. An RC-scale full-envelope simulation paper describes local-area control actuation, a modeling precedent. [NACA RM L51H15, figures 24–26](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930086886/downloads/19930086886.pdf) · [Selig, Full-Envelope Aerodynamics Modeling, §III](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pubs/Selig-2010-AIAA-2010-7635-FullEnvelopeAeroSim.pdf)

**Native work and qualification.** Prepare a control footprint/efficacy once from known airframe geometry. Reuse it in local forces, regime detection, lift-source evaluation and the calibration fixture. Enroll known aircraft explicitly. Estimate 3–6 engineer-days for a first airframe and its admission. The hot loop gains at most one stored factor per strip and needs no allocation. Retuning the gain can change attached roll and stall onset. Target ≤10⁻¹² rad direct shift on uncovered strips, correct differential symmetry and integrated attached roll derivative within 1% of the accepted target at 0° and 4° incidence. Check normalized moment convergence within 2% for 3/6/12 strips per side on a defined benchmark, or integrate exactly across control edges. Compare to independent semispan load data with matching geometry before claiming a stock spin or roll-rate improvement.

## AERO-02 · A wing-only spatial gust cannot drive the stored downwash source directly

**Documented limitation.** The native spatial sampler returns datum and strip velocities. `research/native-kernel/src/nk_wake_spatial_flow.hpp:94–140` and `nk_pre_step.hpp:179–198,320–332` show that the committed-state downwash target consumes only the datum velocity. The helper in `nk_aero.hpp:330–394,403–410,425–449` reconstructs strip flow without spatial deltas. The direct local-load correction in `nk_wake_spatial_aero.hpp:137–142,192–200` already senses the gust and holds the same tail downwash in each pass. This is documented in `docs/WIND-ATMOSPHERE-REFERENCE.md:192–194,288` and owned by M5-W-AUD-18 in `docs/WIND-ATMOSPHERE-ROADMAP.md:83`.

With six symmetric strips, 0.5 m² wing, V = 15 m/s, zero incidence/rates, no induced matrix and uniform +1 m/s body z at the wings only, native direct force changes by −20.827037 N. The installed source stays zero. The independently derived speed-weighted lift source is √226 × 4.5 × atan2(1,15) = 4.503325209 m/s, matching the native strip evaluation with the prescribed deltas. For the manufactured 0.05 s time constant, 60 updates at 240 Hz accumulate 4.472982043 m/s versus zero for the installed driver when frame and disturbance are held fixed. Free-running pitch and velocity could subsequently change the datum. NASA’s unsteady-tail analysis explains the physical pathway from changing wing circulation through delayed downwash to tail force. Its fighter and transport examples do not establish an RC lag constant. [Klein, NASA/CR-1999-209547](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19990100653)

**Native work and qualification.** Feed validated per-wing velocities into the existing committed-state strip source and transport filter. Preserve one held wake state across trial stages and avoid adding a second direct gust load. Estimate 3–5 engineer-days. The source touches six already sampled wing velocities with no new environmental query per trial stage. Check the manufactured source within 10⁻⁹ m/s, retain byte identity for uniform flow and tailless models, verify a tail-only gust leaves the wing target unchanged, and verify the signed mean cancels for antisymmetric perturbations in a symmetric linear fixture. For deployment, log stock spatial gradients and measure tail-force phase against matched gust data. Magnitude on present stock weather is unestablished.

## AERO-03 · Static aerodynamic coefficients lack a Reynolds-number input

**Model limitation and conditional data project.** At fixed angle and controls, the Jensen’s dimensionless steady force and moment coefficients remain constant across speeds that span multiple low-Reynolds-number test conditions. The native model correctly scales load with dynamic pressure. The static strip coefficient tables receive angle alone. `research/native-kernel/src/nk_aero.hpp:19–25,55–104,116–139,222–256,593–631,710–726,782–819`, `nk_weather.hpp:46–51,196–223` and `nk_stage.hpp:278–309` trace atmospheric density, temperature and coefficient inputs. For Jensen chord 0.3048 m, ρ = 1.225 kg/m³, μ = 1.7894×10⁻⁵ Pa·s, α = 2°, the native results are CL = 0.266672159483, CD = 0.043509596177 and CM about aircraft reference = −0.053037460984 at 9, 15 and 25 m/s. Those conditions correspond to Re ≈ 187,796, 312,993 and 521,655 and Mach 0.026–0.073. See `app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:11–27,1103–1125,1148–1188`. The Avanti explicitly documents a reference-speed drag build-up and a hybrid NACA 0015 poststall polar at Re = 10⁶ in `app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json:1602–1606,1905–2009`.

Sydney’s wind-tunnel measurements for two sub-kilogram research aircraft report minimum drag coefficients of 0.0184 ± 0.0020 to 0.0162 ± 0.0011 for model NG and 0.0190 ± 0.0019 to 0.0173 ± 0.0011 for model NT between 15 and 25 m/s. The drag uncertainty intervals overlap. Model NT’s tabulated pre-stall Cm slope changes from −0.130 ± 0.036 to −0.264 ± 0.023 per radian in those tests. The methods give Reynolds ranges of roughly 187k–312k for the authors’ NT model and 227k–379k for their NG model. These data allow a held-out sensitivity benchmark. Neither article is a Jensen, and 2-D UIUC section data cannot by themselves identify a finite-wing or complete-aircraft moment. [Rasines Mazo et al., ICAS 2026, §2.2 and Tables 2–3](https://www.icas.org/icas_archive/icas2026/data/papers/icas2026_0801_paper.pdf) · [UIUC Low-Speed Airfoil Tests, volumes 1–5](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/uiuc_lsat.html)

**Native work and qualification.** Begin with a matched, multi-speed dataset and documented roughness, transition, chord and moment reference. Compare a constant-coefficient fit to a bounded Reynolds-indexed fit on held-out runs. Budget 3–5 engineer-days for a fixture and 6–10 more for an initial opt-in law if the difference is resolved above uncertainty. Data acquisition is additional. Use ρVplane c/μ(T) and bounded interpolation between measured slices. Connect the contribution consistently through the attached global calculation and transition, where the current local wing polar is bypassed. Prevent duplicate induced drag, preserve the existing unconfigured law and time the extra interpolation on enrolled aircraft. Require held-out CDmin within combined two-sigma uncertainty and a predeclared ≥20% normalized RMS reduction where improvements exceed sampling uncertainty. For stall-resolving data compare CLmax angle within 2°. Report uncertainty and retain a constant law where the data cannot discriminate.

## Coverage and limits

- **Frames and moments.** Native relative flow subtracts the world wind transformed to FRD, then adds ω×r at each station. α = atan2(w,u), β = atan2(v,√(u²+w²)), and q = ½ρV². Rate derivatives use an algebraically equivalent ¼ρV form. Body load directions, horizontal/vertical lever arms and r×F follow the documented sign convention in the sampled paths. See `nk_aero.hpp:116–139,459–478,685–707,855–875`.
- **Mass, CG and inertia.** The live-fuel code transfers moments from the model reference to the instantaneous CG and shifts inertia with the parallel-axis theorem before Euler propagation. See `nk_liquid_stage.hpp:98–126,156–158`, `nk_liquid_mass.hpp:115–168,171–218` and `nk_tick.hpp:52–105`. Physical inventory quality and uncertain stock damping derivatives remain validation subjects. UMN's flight-based Ultra Stick 25e identification illustrates independent holdout methodology for a different airframe. [Dorobantu et al.](https://experts.umn.edu/en/publications/frequency-domain-system-identification-for-a-small-low-cost-fixed/)
- **Wash, terrain and atmosphere.** Density can vary with atmospheric state. The terrain model uses a datum-height sample and accounts for bank geometry. Tail wash is spatially selective and can include swirl. The Extra's aileron starts at 0.262 m versus a nominal disk radius of 0.1524 m. The probe's zero change in the isolated *tail-wash* roll increment under opposite aileron commands alone gives no stock wing-wash error. RC tunnel tests show changes in wing loads and slipstream profiles that depend on propeller geometry, placement, and advance ratio. [Ananda, Deters and Selig, wing/propeller experiments](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pubs/AnandaDetersSelig-2014-AIAA-2014-2152.pdf) · [Deters, Ananda and Selig, seven-hole-probe tests](https://commons.erau.edu/publication/957/)
- **Stall and poststall.** The wing has smooth static polar/flat-plate blending and rate-induced local incidence. Static curves supply no independent separation-memory state. Time-resolved load and flow measurements on a 0.2 m chord NACA 0018 wing at Re 80k–100k show pitch-rate-dependent transients. Related measurements on other low-Re wings found little or no quasi-static hysteresis in their tested cases. A geometry-specific transient benchmark is justified before adding a fleet-wide state. `nk_aero.hpp:207–279,383–394,780–820`. [Waterloo stall/reattachment experiments](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/transient-dynamics-of-stall-and-reattachment-at-low-reynolds-number/F97A76B0038B843B58BDC6625B67A8F2) · [UIUC low-Re finite-wing experiments](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pubs/AnandaSukumarSelig-2015-AeroSciTech-LowReWings.pdf)

Future law revisions need distinct replay identity. Preserve same-law native versus reference parity where it applies and qualify changed physics using manufactured identities, same-control flight logs and independent measurements. The probe demonstrates native component behavior only.
