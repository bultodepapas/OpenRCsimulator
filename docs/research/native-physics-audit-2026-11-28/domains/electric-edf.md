# Electric propulsion, battery and ducted-fan audit

The stock MoonRunner X99 can start with the wrong shaft speed because its launch solver sees different inflow and battery temperature from the first flight stage. The stock FPV5 X1 can expose its regenerative bridge voltage as a cell measurement. A separate F50 data diagnostic confuses motor-port and source current. Electric ducted fans still need a distinct installable propulsion law.

**Evidence boundary.** Source baseline `8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b`. A standalone C++17 probe calls the checked-out fixed-wing motor, propeller and pack ECM headers. An independent Python calculation evaluates the published FPV equations and stock data. The coordinator reports 6,527 passing reference checks across 27 suites, including 1,057 electric-motor, 84 battery-ECM and 32 bus checks. The component probe used GCC 14.2. No qualified GDExtension was staged, and the package compiler pin is GCC 13.3. The evaluated points establish numerical and configuration defects. Full native sessions, flight frequency, physical calibration, package compatibility and target-hardware timing require separate verification.

## ELEC-01 · Use the flight stage's electrical and airflow conditions at launch

**Confirmed initialization bug. High priority for powered starts. Estimated 3–5 engineering days for a native launch-source adapter and verification.** The MoonRunner's hand-launch contract specifies a 6.5 m/s ground-relative throw, 5° climb, full throttle and a motor already at its steady shaft speed for the release. The actual flight consumes that speed and initializes a rested finite pack. Two inputs to the root solve differ from the state it initializes.

- **ELEC-01a · Uniform wind.** In uniform wind, the release is deliberately ground-relative and points into the runway headwind. `_hand_launch_rpm` uses body ground velocity. It reconstructs stage-local propeller air velocity only inside the spatial-weather branch. In the stock 3 m/s steady breeze, the real propeller sees 6.5 + 3 cos 5° = 9.488584 m/s at release. This affects ideal and finite supply modes when their load map varies over those advance ratios. `app/sim/flight_session.gd:897–901,994–1012,4709–4725,4755–4799`; `app/physics/air_data.gd:14–30`; `research/native-kernel/src/nk_stage.hpp:317–319,379–389`.
- **ELEC-01b · Pack temperature.** `Propulsion.pack_trim_block` and `_electric_equilibrium_source_state` always use 288.15 K. `_pack_initial_block` initializes the battery at the starting atmosphere's temperature. The launch trim request contains dry-air engine charge ratio and density, with no pack temperature. All three stock finite-class pack configurations in the catalog share this equilibrium path. MoonRunner provides a directly reproducible hand launch. The Stik and Turbo Lumber have fixed landing gear and can use the airborne trim path. The probe quantifies only MoonRunner. `app/physics/propulsion.gd:20,127–130,270–291,330–339`; `app/physics/trim.gd:307–320`; `app/sim/flight_session.gd:1038–1063,4637–4652,5645–5652,5694–5708`; `app/app_state/aircraft_catalog.gd:77,134,140–149,263–266`.

The probe reads the stock 6S 5 Ah finite pack and verifies the pack and cell-template hashes. It holds pack SOC, zero initial RC polarization and the preset's density fixed within each comparison. It finds the restoring root of the actual native motor torque minus propeller load torque at each input pair. The third column reproduces the setup assumptions by an independent bisection, rather than capturing a Godot session. Positive residual means the electrical drive produces more torque than the propeller absorbs.

At 35°C the initialized shaft has 0.56414 N·m motor torque against 0.50734 N·m load. This is a motor–propeller balance at the release. Airframe angular acceleration can make an additional contribution to the relative shaft derivative. The resistance-versus-temperature curve in this aircraft is estimated, but a fitted curve is unnecessary to establish the mismatch inside the implemented equations. The existing hand-launch test checks the proper true airspeed and that full-throttle RPM exceeds cruise RPM. It does not check this torque residual. `app/tests/test_hand_launch.gd:115–127`; `app/physics/wind_config.gd:189–205`; `app/data/batteries/cells/lipo-bak503562-galeotti-v1.json:434`. [Galeotti et al., measured cell data and operating protocol](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/stcppt2r68/1)

**Native correction and acceptance.** Give the native launch solver one prepared operating point containing the actual atmosphere, propeller-axis hub inflow, held throttle/source state and the exact rested pack block that will be installed. For a six-axis trim, evaluate the local inflow for each candidate attitude. Use the native stage's existing load, pack and motor equations. Include launch temperature and other genuine numerical inputs in the worker/cache key and reset invalidation, with the shared transaction committing the solved state. A cold start still has zero initial polarization. Test both isolated errors above, an oblique breeze, reference calm, a uniform spatial field with matching inputs, fixed voltage and two atmospheres of equal density but different temperature. Require `abs(Qmotor-Qprop) ≤ 10⁻¹⁰ max(1 N·m, |Qmotor|, |Qprop|)` in the stated steady-shaft launch frame, plus reference parity and identical warm/cold-cache results for the same input. The main costs are launch-time root evaluations, new cache identity and changed start fingerprints. No additional per-tick root is needed.

## ELEC-02 · Separate regenerative bridge voltage from the pack's cell-voltage report

**Confirmed reporting defect built on a documented braking approximation. Medium-high priority. Estimated 2–4 engineering days for typed port reporting on an available native FPV stage, plus 5–9 days for a bounded bridge/pack law and fixtures.** The experimental X1's four signed motor currents share one bus. The current bound uses available charge headroom divided by 0.2 s. Once it binds, the algebra increases a floating bridge voltage until the requested pack current is met, then places the entire voltage difference in the pack-loss channel. The telemetry passes this voltage to the HUD as battery voltage and divides it by six for “V/cell.” The after-flight statistics likewise treat the bus as pack voltage. `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:45–55,478–481,580–615,1677–1684`; `app/vehicles/multirotor/multirotor_hud.gd:1124–1135`; `app/vehicles/multirotor/osd_model.gd:421–429`.

The following is a prescribed symmetric stage using the stock air-start shaft-speed formula, 929.268985 rad/s, the stock idle duty 0.055, motor-equivalent resistance 0.13292832 Ω, 0.030 Ω pack resistance and 0.6 A auxiliary load. An actual air start begins disarmed. The table combines stock parameter values to isolate the admitted braking operation. It does not claim that a particular rendered frame recorded these values. Positive pack current denotes discharge.

| Charge headroom | Pack current | Floating bridge | Average voltage presented per cell | Base Thevenin terminal voltage | Assigned pack loss / base ohmic loss |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 C | ≈0 A | 68.45177 V | 11.40863 V | 25.20000 V | ≈0 / ≈0 W |
| 0.01 C | −0.05 A | 67.90248 V | 11.31708 V | 25.20149 V | 2.13512 / 0.000075 W |

With four identical drives, the constrained branch sets `Vlink = (4dE/Rm − Iaux − Iaccept)/(4d²/Rm)`. The actual unconstrained first-row circuit would give 25.31779 V and −3.92634 A. The difference tests behavior at the acceptance boundary. The implemented identity `Voc·Ipack = Vlink·Ipack + Plabelled_loss` closes by construction. It cannot identify voltage at the cell tabs or distinguish current-limiter dissipation from battery resistance. The FPV pack has no cell-resolved or thermal state. Manufacturer documentation for another drive demonstrates the physical distinction between energy retained in a link capacitor and energy dissipated in a braking element. It supplies a circuit comparison, not a component value or protection policy for the X1. The existing test intentionally expects the bus to float and checks this ledger. `app/tests/test_mr_pack_charge.gd:62–95,108–123`; `app/sim/multirotor/session.gd:412–425`. [NXP MC3PHAC application note, regeneration control and resistive braking](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN2988.pdf)

**Native correction and acceptance.** Publish pack-side terminal voltage, DC-link voltage, signed pack and motor currents, and separately owned loss terms. A protected-side terminal and a floating bridge require an explicit connection. For direct wiring enforce the circuit relation and change effective bridge duty/mode when charge acceptance binds. If measurements support an isolated link, model or reduce its capacitance, protection threshold and genuine dissipation path. For a capacitance model, include the change in ½CV² in the ledger and use an analytically stable or bounded implicit electrical update. Derive per-cell estimates only from the pack side and label them as an average. Separate physical data acquisition comprises pack-tab/link voltage, pack current, an appropriate motor-current measurement, speed and firmware brake settings at partial and near-full SOC. No evidenced F50/ESC phase cap follows from the nameplate.

Require the full-pack case to keep SOC ≤ 1, pack current ≥ −10⁻⁹ A, the reported average from the pack terminal divided by six, and base ohmic loss `Rp I²`. Require link, limiter and pack energy residuals ≤ 10⁻⁹ of the largest stage power or 1 W, continuity across the activation boundary under the chosen physical law, and held-out braking-decay and pack-current data before hardware claims. Include zero duty, mixed signed currents, reverse, disarm and near-full starts. The stock FPV stage currently registers a framed/sampled GDScript callback. Its native stage and transaction admission are dependencies shared with the drone specialist. Checkpoint/replay identities, once-per-240-Hz control and battery/full-charge event order must be retained or versioned. `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:304–324`; `app/sim/simulation.gd:739–748,1279–1295`. Measure the computational cost of an added link state in a qualified full FPV tick.

## ELEC-03 · Correct the F50 part-throttle electrical diagnostic

**Confirmed offline calibration diagnostic error. Low runtime priority, high priority before using the diagnostic to fit an ESC. Estimated 0.5–1 day.** The manufacturer reports 23.88 V, 10.97 A, 262 W and 22,194 rpm at the 50% test-command row. Multiplication gives 261.9636 W, consistent with a DC-source measurement. `fit()` substitutes 10.97 A directly for equivalent motor-port current in `d=(E+R I)/V`. It stores 0.49334231 as inferred duty. The runtime's own ideal averaged bridge uses `Ibus = d Imotor`. At that inferred duty it would draw 5.41197 A and 129.2377 W. With the same assumed constants and measured source current, the positive root of `Vd²−Ed−R Ibus=0` gives **d = 0.54443845** and **Imotor = 20.14920 A**. Neither reconstruction measures the actual bridge duty or phase RMS current. `research/drone-racing/openrc-fpv5-x1/sources.json:18–29`; `derive.py:122–141,247–257`; `app/data/multirotors/openrc-fpv5-x1.json:752–759`. [LiGPOWER F50/F5146 ten-row static bench table](https://www.ligpower.com/product/f50-5inch-racing-drone-motor.html) · [maxon, controller input and motor current](https://support.maxongroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016161594-Motor-controllers-Input-current-versus-Motor-current-Power-Conversion)

The 100% row is used under an explicit full-duty assumption. The fitted 132.928 mΩ includes motor, ESC and wiring effects. The published 50 mΩ motor specification measures a different boundary with an unspecified conversion to this equivalent model. The source's 10 V no-load specification is an upper bound of 1.6 A. It does not independently establish the generator's assumed 1.0 A. The displayed torque is rounded to 0.01 N·m. None of these quantities identifies efficiency or a current limit across operating conditions.

**Correction and acceptance.** Name the measurement port and use the quadratic for this existing offline generator. Include an independent native fixture for reconstructing source current and power, full duty and zero-current coast. Regenerate provenance with its pinned generator, verify the 50% row at ≤10⁻⁹ A algebraic error, confirm the 100% control, and check that the active motor/propeller coefficients and same-command flight derivatives remain identical. The loader recognizes the diagnostic name. Stock flight stages consume the fitted electrical constants, independently of this diagnostic. Any regenerated file hash still needs the usual recording compatibility decision. Measure command-to-effective-duty with the selected ESC before adopting a runtime map. This is distinct from the drone specialist's thrust-versus-RPM fit.

## ELEC-04 · Add electric ducted fans as a distinct native propulsion family

**Missing capability. Medium strategic priority, high evidence cost. Estimated 10–18 engineering days for a synthetic native stand after the interface is agreed, then 15–30 days for a single playable installation and its software gates.** These effort ranges exclude measurement lead times and cross-platform promotion. The current aircraft schema dispatches `electric_prop`, `glow_prop` and `turbine`. The electric branch requires open-propeller geometry with `Ct(J)` and `Cp(J)` tables. It samples hub inflow, computes thrust and shaft load and couples the motor, pack and rotor. It has no electric fan/duct installation identity, fan/stator reaction split, inlet capture or installed nozzle map. The stock Vantage Jet remains a JetCat P100-RX turbine. `app/physics/aircraft_data.gd:1225–1235,1289–1335,1369–1387`; `research/native-kernel/src/nk_prop.hpp:105–129`; `research/native-kernel/src/nk_electric_coupling.hpp:33–50`; `app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json:2118–2119`.

NASA tested the 130 mm-class Jetfan 130 Pro with its inlet ring in a low-speed tunnel. The measured maximum wind-off thrust was 21.25 lbf (94.52 N). Thrust at the same fan speed declined as dynamic pressure increased. In the measured cases above advance ratio 1.24, net thrust was negative. Their selected thrust fit includes axial and lateral advance-ratio terms plus tip Mach, and the published normalized validation RMSE is 0.753% on their data. Their electrical input power cannot be substituted for rotor load torque times speed without accounting for drive losses. This comparable-size fan supplies an independent fixture and sign checks. It does not calibrate an installed Schübeler fan in the A200. [Weinstein and Simmons, Tables 1, 6, 16–17 and Figure 5](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230017299)

**Native work and acceptance.** Reuse the existing EDF-00…15 plan in open draft PR #18 at `aaa219960a47eb173a5358b770c3912d570a6647`. It proposes a 128 mm reference and separate 152/195 mm experiments, with distinct physical promotion gates. Implement EDF-01/02a/03/04 for one 128 mm synthetic stand before selecting the A200 installation. One option is an installed net-force map and shaft-load map over joint measured support in speed, inflow/incidence and density or similarity variables. Where stations and data exist, an independent axial control-volume oracle uses `Fnet = ṁ(Vexit − V∞) + (pexit − p∞)Aexit` for the declared ambient inlet and area/velocity conventions. Apply ram and pressure corrections once. Model rotor torque, stator reaction, acceleration and inlet/nacelle forces at their true stations; a near-zero whole-unit balance moment alone does not specify shaft torque. Use the same pack, explicit ESC losses/limits and coupled shaft dynamics; avoid an extra fitted spool lag when inertia already accounts for that lag. [NASA Glenn, general thrust equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/thrust-force/) · [Original EDF plan and validation matrix](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/pull/18)

First require zero-flow/rest and powered-static limits; a finite signed low-RPM/off-drag branch; analytic force, torque, energy and unit tests; range/holes reporting; and a joint-domain holdout of static/wind-on runs. For the Jetfan comparison require the observed negative-force sign at the published operating points and reproduce published coefficients using the authors' variable centering and measurement conventions. Track electrical input, shaft and flow power separately. For the selected installation, predeclare absolute limits near zero thrust and uncertainty-aware held-out static thrust, current, spool-up/down and mission Wh/Ah gates before a hardware claim. The existing project targets of ±10% current/thrust and ±15% endurance can serve where their quantity and measurement boundary apply. Performance is a separate gate. EDF-05/06 must prove actual native calls throughout the required operating path, with identical replay, terrain/contact integration at 240 Hz and package coverage. Prepare all coefficient tables once and profile installed flight and stand work separately. A calibrated high-incidence/near-stall model is the largest acquisition risk.

## Coverage and additional evidence

- **Motor and pack laws.** Fixed-wing `Kt = 60/(2π Kv_rpm/V)`, `E = rpm/Kv`, `q̇ = −Ipack`, `Ipack = d Imotor + Iaux` and the positive-part drive/coast boundary are dimensionally consistent. Across 32 enabled/disabled, stalled, driven and coasting points the compiled native fixed-wing motor closed `Vbus Ibus = Qshaft ω + I²Rm + Qloss ω` within 4.90 × 10⁻¹³ W. Its equivalent motor current needs a documented winding/commutation convention before comparison to a phase RMS sensor. The fixed-wing coast sends no charge back. On the damped FPV law, signed equivalent motor-port current may brake even at zero duty while regeneration requires negative net pack current after auxiliaries. `app/physics/electric_motor.gd:27–66`; `research/native-kernel/src/nk_electric.hpp:41–89`; `nk_battery.hpp:124–181`; `app/physics/multirotor/electric_bus.gd:17–25,51–72,88–105`.
- **ECM and events.** Fixed-wing ECM supports zero to two RC branches, bilinear clamped tables by SOC and temperature, average/weakest series group, coulomb counting, RC relaxation and lumped temperature. It distinguishes cell heating from interconnect loss. Debounced soft/hard low-voltage cutoff samples an accepted endpoint and holds the resulting limit for the following tick. Exhaustion has a separate root, charge projection and residual interval. The FPV X1 uses its own OCV/R and charge acceptance with endpoint depletion; its pack has no weak-cell, RC, temperature or equivalent elapsed-undervoltage supervisor. A documented lower-bound tolerance permits a small negative accepted FPV charge. The fixed-wing native bound for one-quadrant depletion cannot be dropped into a bidirectional FPV law without direction-aware charge and energy events. `research/native-kernel/src/nk_pack_ecm.hpp:196–218,246–305,316–345`; `nk_tick.hpp:394–451`; `app/physics/battery/esc_supervisor.gd:50–92`; `app/physics/electric_battery_step.gd:80–96`; `app/physics/multirotor/controlled_plant.gd:20–25,778–784,1656–1665`.
- **Motor and ESC limits and physical calibration.** The Stik installation dossier records a 194.85 A model startup from rest at full duty. The ideal motor/ESC law has no current control, startup switching sequence, winding thermal state or thermal derating. The Scorpion 12×8E point at 22.2 V gives 49.92 A, 10,334 RPM and 3,265 g; the repository's joint equilibrium predicts 43.86 A, 10,678 RPM and 36.83 N. The electrical fit uses this row, so it cannot serve as an independent validation run. A motor/ESC rating supplies neither a transient clamp value nor its time behavior. For EL3/4–6 acquire electrical-port, RPM, dynamometer and temperature channels on the exact configuration, fit loss/derating only where identifiable, and test held-out throttle steps and mission energy. The fixed-wing LiPo template's 1/3 A data come from a small pouch at 25°C. Its other temperature rows borrow scaling from a cylindrical Li-ion cell. Pulse-test the installed high-rate packs before assigning a product accuracy to loaded voltage, weak-cell margin, temperature or cutoff time. `research/propulsion/stik-6s/README.md:14–36`; `docs/research/battery/BAT-05/README.md:245–254`. [Scorpion manufacturer bench table](https://www.scorpionsystem.com/files/i2%2C653_data_chart.htm) · [Galeotti et al., original pouch-cell data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/stcppt2r68/1)
