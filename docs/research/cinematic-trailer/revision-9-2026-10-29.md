## Corsair deck test (owner request, 2026-10-30)

A standalone 19.4 s clip, not yet in the trailer: `C:/DEVELOPMENT/OpenRCsimulator-exports/corsair-deck-test-2026-10-30/`. One real flight (`corsair_deck_director.gd`) on the runway in calm air:

1. Wings fold (8 s cycle) and the canopy opens.
2. Flaps go full (30°).
3. The wings spread and lock while the canopy closes. The throttle is held at idle until then.
4. Flaps go to 10°. The model's CARF manual advises 0°, but the owner requested a reduction.
5. Full throttle, liftoff at 22.0 s, gear up at 22.8 s (2.5 s cycle), flaps up at 25.4 s.

The clip has three shots with game audio only. Cues: `FOLDING WINGS.`, `SPREAD AND LOCKED.`, `TAKEOFF FLAPS.`, `GEAR UP.`, `FLAPS UP.`. The frame records confirm the gear status: down, then retracting, then up.

Problems found and fixed:

- **Gear came back down:** a toggle retry recycled it.
- **Mid-take pause:** one take froze at 18.4 s; it was re-shot.
- **Stale title frames:** `crystal_titles.render_cue` reused an older `static.png` in a reused work directory, so a corrected cue kept its old text. The file is now deleted first.
- **Missing glyph:** Barlow Condensed has no `→`, which renders as a box.
