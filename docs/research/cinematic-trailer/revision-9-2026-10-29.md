## Revision 9.6 — size trio in one frame (owner request, same day)

The owner asked for the Giant, normal and Tiny aircraft to fly together, so the size difference reads in formation.

`capture_formation.gd --sizes` flies three Ugly Stiks line abreast at the Workshop's 500 %, 100 % and 10 % sizes (7.62 m, 1.52 m and 15 cm span). Its first framing put them at different depths, so the Giant read as nearer rather than larger. The accepted version fixes this:

- The camera is near-frontal, so all three sit at one depth.
- Wingtip gaps are tight: 0.43 m Giant to Original, 0.15 m Original to Tiny.
- After the wide shot, the camera closes to 1.9 m between the Original's wingtip and the Tiny, where the 15 cm model reads beside the 1.5 m one.
- Minimum centre separation is 0.99 m.

The shot uses 7 beats from 2.5 s (the push completes inside the cut), with the cinematic label and the card `SAME PLANE. ANY SIZE.`, detail "500 % · 100 % · 10 % — 7.62 m · 1.52 m · 15 cm".

The real Giant flight stays for 3 beats as evidence of the Workshop trial. The separate mini pass is replaced.
