## Capture defects found

- A connected XInput gamepad froze the session at tick 3 while the manifest still read `complete`. `capture_flight.gd` now detaches desk controllers after startup. Earlier takes in this set were checked: their commands match the director's.
- Concurrent tests from other tracks on the shared `.godot` cache caused one transient livery refusal and an access violation. `app/aircraft/custom_skin.gd`, under edit by another track, briefly failed to parse. The capture scripts now retry up to three times. Every accepted take completed with an advancing tick.
