## Editorial conclusions

The user replaced the initial longer proposal with an exactly 60-second film. Review covered the [current feature table](../../../README.md#current-features), [track registry](../../README.md#tracks-plans-and-step-ids), player guide and feature-owner documents. The plan's feature matrix records direct screen coverage, features carried inside other shots, reviewed extras and planned-only exclusions. Secondary settings and engine variants do not each receive a title card. The title `EVERY INPUT MATTERS.`, act structure, mix targets and shot allocations are editorial proposals, not research measurements.

Specific follow-up findings:

- [Sky/world controls](../../FIELD-WORLD-PLAN.md#sky-and-cloud-controls) describe nine profiles, cloud transport, distant storm towers/rain curtains, branched lightning and distance-delayed CC0 thunder. Current [lightning renderer](../../../app/render/weather/lightning.gd), [storm cell](../../../app/render/weather/storm_cell.gd) and [event timeline](../../../app/environment/storm_events.gd) confirm separate presentation components. The airplane route supports the storm; helicopter and Natural Random retain classic. Rain physics, tornadoes, spatial storm forces and cloud-volume traversal remain unimplemented.
- [Wind](../../WIND-PLAN.md) provides physical mean wind, repeating gusts and seeded temporal turbulence through the air-relative velocity field. This is separate from lightning/cloud appearance; neither a dramatic sky nor screen shake proves a gust response.
- [RC driving](../../../research/rc-monster-truck/README.md#drive-and-experiment) includes both trucks, a ramp/roller/slalom circuit, water/mud behavior, cosmetic spray/grime, recovery and research/tuning tools. The four-second car slots prioritize clear vehicle action; mud can be incorporated within a take rather than creating another sequence.
- [Workshop](../../AIRCRAFT-WORKSHOP.md), [Natural Random](../../plans/natural-random-flight.md), [emergency practice](../../plans/failure-mode.md), atmosphere, lighting, scenery, audio, equipment, overlays, controls, accessibility and recording were reviewed. Capability and vehicle limits stay with their owners; old document counts do not override the current catalog. The main film need not enumerate each setting to represent these features honestly.

The revised storyboard reserves 18–23 seconds for the playable helicopter, 23–31 for the two trucks, 31–38 for electrical storm plus physical wind, 45–48 for the real accident-cost report and 50–57 for a simultaneous formation. A three-second end card completes exactly 60 seconds.

The main technical uncertainties are the four-actor presentation scene, visual replay channels, high-resolution frame/audio timing and the selection of a genuinely usable crash report. A short pilot should resolve these before full-fleet filming. No physics tuning or purchase-price research is needed to produce this plan.
