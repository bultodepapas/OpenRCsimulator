# Avanti S AV-06: airframe, mass, fuel, gear and handling data

2026-10-06 · AV-06 research · **Research done; no app change.** No source publishes the A200 wing area, chords, airfoil, wheel sizes or gear geometry. Found: the stock tank volume (3.2 L kero + 1.3 L smoke), owner weights with larger engines, the original pneumatic gear and its pressures, and qualitative handling reports with a few speed and distance numbers.

Scope: SebArt Avanti S Jet 2.2m ARF, **A200 original (2.00 m span, 2.22 m length)**, JetCat P100-RX. Not repeated here: dimensions, 10.5 kg dry, CG 240/250/260 mm, throws, D/R and expo ([controls and installation](avanti-s-controls-and-installation.md), [family](avanti-s-family-research.md)); P100 engine data ([AV-05](avanti-s-av05-turbine-dynamics.md)).

Labels: **manufacturer** (SebArt or its documents), **dealer** (a SebArt distributor's listing), **owner** (a builder's forum post: a measurement unless marked "claim"), **derived** (computed here from cited numbers), **estimate** (judgement, stated). Units are converted from US fl oz, lb and psi. A forum post is one owner and one airframe. It is not a test.

Forum threads read in full or in part:
- **RCN**: rc-network.de "Avanti S", 21 pages, 2013–2020, German.
- **RCU-KT**: RCUniverse "Sebart Avanti S Build Thread w/KT 180". Pages 1–3, 11, 18, 21, 22 and 25 read through the Wayback Machine; the rest are not archived.

**Tank position.** The tank sits mid-fuselage between the side rails, under the turbine mounting rails (DrScoles RCU-KT; Krill manual §15). Fuelling moves the CG forward. An A200 owner: filling the original tank makes the model nose-heavy (mustang6, RCN #236). The usual practice is to set the CG with a full hopper and an empty main tank (kasi122, RCN #237). The Krill 2.1 m manual quantifies it: **260 mm with landing fuel, about 240 mm with a full tank**. That is a Krill figure, though the tank bay is the same design.

## 5. Landing gear

**Original A200 kit gear:** pneumatic retracts. Mains in the wings, steerable nose leg.

| Size class | "25 kg/ 55 lbs size" | dealer | olnico, RCU-AV #32 |
| Brakes | pneumatic, pad acting on the wheel hub. Owners report they lock easily. Fix: separate brake air tank at 40–45 psi (2.8–3.1 bar); only ≈15 psi (1 bar) needed for useful braking | owner | RCU-KT pp. 18, 21 |
| Retract air | 100–110 psi (6.9–7.6 bar) used. One gear retracts on the ground from ≈6 bar, another needed ≥8 bar. Cylinders judged small | owner | RCU-KT p. 21; BoerniH RCN #355; Kevin_W RCU-AV |
| Lock | mains collapsed after landing when the valve cut pressure: needs pressure to stay locked | owner | wfield0455, RCU-KT #513 |
| Springs | mains compress fully under the model's own weight ("soft springs land nicely") | owner | beta, Tim Kleinschmidt RCN #121–122 |
| Fit | early batches: wheels do not fit the wells by 3–4 mm. Fixes: file the leg stop or move the mount forward | owner | RCN pp. 2–5 |
| Wheel diameters, track, wheelbase, leg length | **not found** | – | – |

Later and aftermarket gear: GZ-Retracts electric conversion (dealer and owner). Pahl (PMF) electric gear designed for the Avanti, with pneumatic or Electron electric brakes. Electron ER-40 set "Avanti S (SebArt)". One owner used Behotec VL170 mains, a Jet-1A nose leg and **Electron 100 mm wheels**: aftermarket, not the kit wheel. The current SebArt USA and Gator listings say "Electric Aluminum landing gear and doors with electric brakes are included". That is a later production standard, not the A200 kit photographed in the manual. The Krill manual: with an empty tank the model "will fall back on its tail", so the mains sit close to the empty-fuel CG (Krill).

## What this does not prove

- No wing area, chord, airfoil or stab and fin size for the A200 exists in any source found. The 0.70 m² remains an estimate.
- Forum masses come from single airframes with other engines and equipment. None is a P100 build. Fuel densities are estimates.
- The 3.2 L / 1.3 L tank volumes come from a dealer listing, not from SebArt documents. Owners' "3 L" agrees but is approximate.
- Speeds, distances and the hover are pilot impressions or uncalibrated GPS, mostly with 120–220 N engines. None validates P100 performance. The "15–20 ft" takeoff claim is implausible for a 10.9 kg jet at 140 N and is kept only as a quote.
- The stall reports show sensitivity to an aft CG. They do not give a stall speed, CL_max or the stall's direction.
- Servo specs come from retailer copies of JR data; no JR datasheet was reached.
- The Krill manual's CG shift is for the 2.1 m composite variant.
