Upstream's latest published release checked today is [EOSG 2.3.1](https://github.com/3ddelano/epic-online-services-godot/releases/tag/2.3.1), at [`56238973e2cd7ac9ac99ca14f88934465f0a8997`](https://github.com/3ddelano/epic-online-services-godot/tree/56238973e2cd7ac9ac99ca14f88934465f0a8997). No package, product, account or service was provisioned and no exploit or runtime behavior was demonstrated.

## Decision

**EOS remains a candidate; the inspected EOSG high-level peer is blocked for adoption.** Its no-fee service offering does not establish wrapper correctness. Require a reviewed sender-binding/packet-validation fix and adversarial multi-client evidence, or separately estimate another native adapter. Keep ENet LAN/direct rooms independent. The [implementation plan](../../plans/multiplayer.md) retains a **3–5 developer-day investigation**, not a promise to repair a wrapper and qualify every platform within that time.

## Identity, credentials and reachability

EOS Game Services use [Connect identity](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/epic-online-services/eos-overview?lang=en-US); an Epic player account is not inherent to every Game Services flow. [CreateDeviceId](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/api-ref/functions/eos-connect-create-device-id?lang=en-US) documents a reusable local guest credential and `DuplicateNotAllowed` as an existing-ID case that proceeds to login. [DeleteDeviceId](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/api-ref/functions/eos-connect-delete-device-id?lang=en-US) distinguishes desktop credentials, which uninstall does not automatically remove, from Android/iOS removal. Losing an unlinked credential can lose access to its associated data. Do not promise cross-device recovery, verified personal identity or offline service login. Use separate machines/OS profiles for distinct test identities. Source text was verified in Epic's official HTML payload where the web text renderer returned no readable lines.

A trial needs the owner's configured developer product, sandbox/deployment and least-privilege game client. [Epic's policy guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/epic-online-services/eos-fundamentals/client-and-client-policy/client-policy-guide) requires particular care with trusted-server clients: those credentials must not ship in a public build. Ordinary SDK client credentials are recoverable from a distributed client; give them only the enabled user-required game permissions. Keep administration, deployment and signing secrets separate. [No-fee licensing](https://onlineservices.epicgames.com/licensing) is not a guarantee of unlimited capacity or availability; verify the actual product policy before reliance.

For the network trial, [NoRelays, AllowRelays and ForceRelays](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/en-US/api-ref/enums/eos-e-relay-control) have different connection policies. Set a mode **before a fresh connection**: [SetRelayControl](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/api-ref/functions/eos-p-2-p-set-relay-control?lang=en-US) does not immediately alter existing connections. Record both ends' configured mode and established network type, not only “connected.” Test matching direct-only, automatic and forced-relay pairs on different networks; treat incompatible settings as unsupported even if an occasional connection succeeds. Epic relay routing and our application host's forwarding are different layers.

EOSG 2.3.1's release notes fix Godot 4.7 loading; that does not qualify OpenRC's official **4.8-beta2**. Record the actual downloaded asset hash, SDK version, engine/export-template version, architecture and license notices. Execute Windows x86_64, Linux x86_64, macOS ARM64 and Intel/Rosetta combinations that will be claimed. Do not infer bundled binaries from a manifest or successful compilation. [macOS export guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/export/exporting_for_macos.html) distinguishes development ad-hoc signing from distribution signing/notarization; use the project's actual qualified distribution route and include any new cost in the decision.

## Five-day decision matrix

The timebox starts when the necessary approved product, machines and testers are available. Missing prerequisites produce an **inconclusive/deferred** row; they do not justify an invented pass or an automatic extension.

| Day | Work | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Package/engine inspection; source-hardening feasibility; offline app with service unavailable | Exact hashes/versions; parse/load/export failures; sender-binding fix scope and estimated maintenance. Stop early if a safe adapter is not feasible within the investigation |
| 2 | Fresh and returning Connect guest on separate machines/profiles | First-ID, existing-ID, InvalidUser/CreateUser, peer initialization, repeat-launch identity, expiry/failure recovery. No destructive helper against an existing user's identity |
| 3 | Isolated admission and packet semantics after a candidate fix | Actual sender versus claimed peer ID; mismatches/truncated headers rejected; host forwarding; control reliability, pose reordering and channel behavior; bounded cleanup |
| 4 | Independent networks and a restrictive/hotspot route | Fresh direct-only/automatic/forced-relay sessions, each direction; both endpoint network-type receipts, success/failure, RTT/loss; no universal-connectivity claim |
| 5 | Available export/OS pairs, lifecycle and decision | Host/client combinations, cancel/rejoin/host loss/outage/offline fallback; untested targets listed; adopt for a separately scoped integration or defer |

Required receipt fields: source/asset/SDK/engine identity, OS/architecture, product deployment alias without secrets, selected client-policy permissions, relay mode, observed network type, start/end time, participants, steps, expected/actual result and log path/hash. Packet evidence redacts capabilities, tokens and full user identifiers. Neither a lobby membership event nor a successful return from a send method proves that the game payload arrived correctly.

**Do not adopt** with unresolved sender binding, malformed-header safety, destructive guest behavior on the chosen path, missing fresh-user initialization, unverified required export, absent forced-relay evidence, unbounded queues, or a required new invoice. A small experiment may identify a repair; a production wrapper fork is not hidden inside the original 3–5 day estimate.
