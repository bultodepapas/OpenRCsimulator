# Low-Re unsteady stall, gust and wing response

Research snapshot: 2026-10-18; retrieval timestamps in [sources.json](sources.json) use UTC. Part of the [RC validation library](../README.md). The collection prioritizes measured transient loads and flow at low Reynolds number. Local originals are in the ignored `references/rc-validation-library-2026-10-18/unsteady-stall/` directory; checksums, access notes and reading depth are in the manifest. These studies inform transient-model structure and validation targets, but idealized sections and wings do not establish full-aircraft RC behavior.

The Zenodo ASCII archive is the only item in this tranche found as a direct public measurement download. The Waterloo papers state that their measurements can be requested from the authors; their supplementary movies are not raw force/PIV data. Other accessible papers provide plots and experimental descriptions but no separate raw series. Tsang's linked thesis host required an interactive access flow, and the 2026 publisher PDF endpoint returned 403; neither access restriction was bypassed.

The retained ASCII ZIP contains 1,025 members across CENER (42), GLASGOW (705), NREL (229) and Oldenburg (49). A representative CENER run exposes `Time`, `AoA`, `Flap`, `Cl`, `Cdp` and `Cm` columns with numeric rows. Match each program's units, Reynolds number and coefficient definitions before using it; in particular, do not assume its `Cdp` channel is whole-aircraft total drag. These member counts are not counts of independent campaigns or RC aircraft.

## Search and inspection

Representative searches: `low Reynolds dynamic stall experiment direct force`, `gust wing encounter low Reynolds experiment PIV`, `airfoil stall reattachment transient`, `unsteady wing response transverse gust`, `Goman Khrabrov model low Reynolds validation`, and `Zenodo unsteady aerodynamics dataset`. Existing library manifests were checked to avoid repeating prior sources. The 2018 Corkery paper and 2019 thesis are explicitly marked as overlapping; Ayancik & Mulleners reuses an already-indexed Le Fouest dataset.

Downloaded PDFs were checked for PDF signatures and searchable title text; the ZIP archive passed its CRC integrity test. Downloaded originals are retained locally only and are not redistributed here. See `sources.json` for per-source authors, hashes, exact access status, relevance and limitations.
