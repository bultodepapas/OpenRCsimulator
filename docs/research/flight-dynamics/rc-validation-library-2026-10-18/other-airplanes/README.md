# Other RC airplanes and research conversions

Research snapshot for additional named conventional and fixed-wing RC platforms, retrieved 2026-10-19 UTC. The collection has **19 source records**: nine with a verified local original and ten without one. A source record is not necessarily a new aircraft or an independent campaign: related papers can reuse a research platform and flight program. These findings support comparison and future validation design; they do not establish physical validation of this simulator, and no coefficients were imported into production.

The manifest at [sources.json](sources.json) is authoritative for citations, access status, read depth, limitations, local paths, file hashes and byte counts. The original PDFs are ignored/local-only files under `references/`; they are not transferred by Git.

## Relations and interpretation

The two 2017 Penguin BE theses are related to DLR's cooperative-landing program and use actual flight data; they should not automatically be counted as independent campaigns. The three GT TwinStar papers cover real flight identification and a damaged-wing case, but share a research platform and program. The 2025 Urban Condor papers are separate tunnel campaigns (whole-aircraft dynamics and probe calibration); the 2026 Urban Condor X source is a later free-flight identification campaign on a related Kadet-derived aircraft. These relationships matter when counting independent validation evidence.

Flight demonstrations, trajectory tracking and sensor tests show that a configured research aircraft flew and produced selected measurements. They do not by themselves establish a transferable aerodynamic model. Wind-tunnel data describe their fixture and test article, while simulated sensor augmentation or controller validation should remain distinct from measured open-loop vehicle response.

Representative searches included: “SIG Rascal 110 flight test system identification thesis”; “Multiplex TwinStar II aerodynamic system identification flight tests”; “Penguin BE DLR flight test system identification”; “Multiplex EasyStar automatic landing flight test”; “Multiplex FunCub output error flight data”; “SIG Kadet LT-40 Urban Condor wind tunnel and flight system identification”; “Hobbico NexStar accelerated flight power required thesis”; “Skywalker 1900 flight test thesis”; and “Aerosonde CAMEX-4 flight data NASA GHRC.” Discovery prioritized institutional repositories, NASA Earthdata, DOI/publisher records and university flight-control laboratories. The search pass excluded repeated Bixler, Telemaster, Ultra Stick/FASER, Cub, Big Stik, X8 and Extra 300 holdings already covered in other library topics. Cularis and other sailplane sources are covered by the [sailplanes guide](../sailplanes/README.md).

Direct retrieval attempts for the AFIT Rascal, AIAA TwinStar/Maja, ODU Air Titan and 2026 Urban Condor X PDFs returned HTTP 403; the NexStar repository returned a challenge HTML page; the Smith thesis requires institutional access; and the NASA Aerosonde granule requires Earthdata login. The accessible 2026 paper was inspected in a browser, and remains explicitly marked as having **no local original**. No access challenge, login or paywall was bypassed. Failed responses were not stored as source files. All nine retained PDFs passed signature and text inspection; the catalog validator checks their local hashes, sizes and PDF parsing.
