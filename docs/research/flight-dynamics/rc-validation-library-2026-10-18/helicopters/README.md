# Small RC helicopter flight dynamics and rotor evidence

Research expansion of the [RC validation library](../README.md), checked 2026-10-19. This collection covers small radio-controlled single-main-rotor helicopters and model-scale rotor tests. It prioritizes measured flight response, system identification, rotor/servo measurements, and documented hardware. It is an evidence index, not proof that the simulator is physically validated.

The older [HELI-01 research record](../../../helicopter/HELI-01/README.md) synthesizes several X-Cell, Raptor, Yamaha R-50, and flybarless-model studies. No original files were present in `references/helicopter/` at intake. The 26-record [manifest](sources.json) is authoritative for citations, original links, local paths, hashes, access, rights, limits, and inspection depth. It indexes 15 public author or institutional PDFs; they remain ignored local research copies, and public availability does not itself grant redistribution rights.

These related lab studies are not full-aircraft flight tests. |
| Three Align T-Rex 600E variants | [Kumar dissertation](https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/items/8784e3b3-00e1-4db7-a903-45b53baba649) | Indoor wind calibration and ship-side flights using RC helicopters as sensors. Flight/sensor inputs are measured; the spatial ship-airwake map is inferred and compared qualitatively with CFD. |
| Zhejiang University small-scale helicopter, model unspecified in abstract | [Chen et al., ground-effect model](https://doi.org/10.3785/j.issn.1008-973X.2014.01.024) | Actual hover load/height tests and collective-lift calibration; ground-effect lift is inferred, then IGE/OGE simulation replays are compared with measured height/vertical-speed traces. No rotor-flow field is reported in the abstract. |

The Yamaha R-50 and R-MAX have multi-metre rotors and different mass, Reynolds-number, hardware, and operating regimes from hobby RC models. Treat them as modeling-method analogues, not direct small-helicopter performance targets. Rotor-stand findings apply only to their tested geometry, RPM, pitch, clearance, and flow conditions. Identified closed-loop flight models are not open-loop aerodynamic truth. No retained paper provided a general-purpose raw flight-telemetry archive; most expose results as plots, fitted parameters, or model comparisons.

## Search coverage and access limits

Representative searches included `"X-Cell 60" helicopter system identification flight test`, `"T-Rex 450" rotor PIV hover`, `"Raptor 50" flight dynamics identification`, `"Yamaha R-50" helicopter system identification`, `"miniature helicopter" system identification flight data`, `RC helicopter autorotation measured`, and `model scale helicopter rotor ground effect thrust torque thesis`. Chinese-language searches included `小型无人直升机 飞行动力学 模型辨识 飞行试验` and `小型无人直升机 旋翼 地面效应 试验`; Japanese searches used `模型ヘリコプター 飛行データ システム同定 ロータ`. Searches covered institutional repositories, CMU/MIT/Stanford pages, journal pages, conference proceedings, and theses; they were not exhaustive.

Access failures and exclusions are recorded in the manifest. The Zhejiang University abstract is public, but its official PDF action returned a waiting/challenge page, so no PDF was retained. A 2019 JR700 hover-identification article surfaced in search, but the official BUAA journal page required campus-network access and it is excluded. Direct retrieval also failed for some author/publisher PDF endpoints (including the Bogdanov paper, Raptor 50 forward-flight paper, and T-Rex 700 thesis); those are link-only where public metadata was available. No challenge or paywall was bypassed. Japanese searches returned multirotor or unrelated results rather than a suitable single-main-rotor primary study.
