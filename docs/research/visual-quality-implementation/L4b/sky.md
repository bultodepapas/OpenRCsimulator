# L4b — grouped clouds and sky light cues

Date: 2026-10-07. Status: implemented; software-renderer validation complete, owner/GPU review remains open.

The sky keeps the L4 five-octave cumulus deck, seed, colour, scale, coverage, horizon fade, sun disc and haze calibration. The shared field in `app/render/cloud_field.gdshaderinc` now owns its Hoskins integer hash, quintic value noise and coverage threshold. `cloud_density(cells, seed, coverage, cluster_strength)` returns raw 0–1 coverage before any horizon fade. Its cells are in base noise units; the lattice repeats every 64 cells. The 16-cell coarse octave shifts the local coverage threshold by at most ±0.35 at strength 1, leaving broader clear gaps. Strength 0 uses the original L4 threshold exactly.

The sky shader adds three optional controls, each defaulting to zero so an A/B run can reproduce the original L4 sky. `cluster_strength` groups the base clouds. `cirrus_strength` blends a separate, stretched noise field with a 0.34 coverage threshold; the 3/64 by 51/64 frequency transform makes 17:1 streaks while preserving the 64-cell period. `silver_strength` adds a forward-scatter rim proportional to `4 × density × (1 − density) × max(dot(EYEDIR, sun_dir), 0)^24`; its maximum additive strength is 0.08 times the control. Cirrus starts fading in above `EYEDIR.y = 0.02` and is full by 0.18. All three effects are disabled below the cloud horizon fade; the L2 haze colour and sun scatter remain unchanged. The silver term adds no more than 0.08 × `cloud_lin`.

Cloud coordinates are dimensionless angular-deck coordinates: `EYEDIR.xz / EYEDIR.y × cloud_scale + cloud_offset`. The default field's pilot station is at world origin, so the ground shadow projection at 1,500 m uses the matching world projection multiplied by `cloud_scale / 1500`. With scale 6, `0.004` cells/s maps to an apparent −1 m/s drift and `0.0015` cells/s to −0.375 m/s; positive offset moves the sampled pattern in the opposite direction. The sky currently follows the fixed pilot-origin view and does not add a camera-world-XZ phase, so a custom field with an offset pilot station needs that origin supplied before claiming exact sky/shadow registration. Cirrus is a second angular noise deck, not volumetric cloud geometry or a physical altitude model.

The cloud colours and all L2 sky/fog formulas are unchanged. The new features use no textures, `TIME`, global colour grade or external dependency. Lattice ratios for the base and stretched fields are integer multiples of 1/64, so the CPU's modulo-64 drift contract remains valid. Compatibility shader compilation and a same-view A/B with all feature strengths at zero produced byte-identical sky pixels against the L4 shader. A diagnostic canvas render measured the stretched field at coverage 0.34: 7.01% of pixels exceeded raw density 0.05 and 1.37% exceeded 0.5; the shader's cirrus control caps the actual sky blend at 0.12. This is noise-field coverage, not a GPU cost or human-readability result.

A second llvmpipe probe compared canvas samples at two coordinate offsets separated by 64 cells. The values are mathematically periodic by construction; floating-point raster samples differed by at most one 8-bit level in 306 of 921,600 pixels. The second offset was outside the CPU contract [0, 64), so this bounds shader-coordinate rounding rather than demonstrating a CPU wrap jump. The Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility probe confirms shader compilation and legacy zero-strength output; it does not establish target-GPU performance, human readability or exact registration for non-origin camera stations. L6c readability and the owner/GPU gate remain open.

## Default-feature ablation

A new external probe rendered the frozen candidate at 1280×720 with production defaults (`cluster=0.35`, `cirrus=0.12`, `cirrus coverage=0.34`, `silver=0.18`) and then zeroed one feature at a time. In the sun-facing 225°/45° view, removing cirrus changed 134,891 pixels (107,538 by more than one 8-bit level; maximum 38 levels). Removing silver changed 32,617 pixels, but only 3,055 changed by more than one level and the maximum was two levels. In the anti-sun 45°/45° view, removing silver changed zero pixels; cirrus still changed 103,874. The [side-by-side comparison](sky-feature-ablation.png) and [per-view metrics](sky-feature-ablation.json) show the rendered effect. Silver is directional and measurable at the chosen default, though very restrained; increasing its control scales a term capped at 0.08 × `cloud_lin`.
