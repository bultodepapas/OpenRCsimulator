## Plan audit

The lead audited the branch plan against the code; an independent Good Enemy review (Sonnet,
read-only) challenged both the plan and the proposed redesign. Accepted findings and their changes
are in the [plan audit table](../../../plans/stutter-diagnostics.md#2-audit-of-the-branch-plan-2026-11-19).
The decisive ones: bridge budget flags cannot see drops above the target; summary-window polling
cannot cover more than its row cap per poll; a large snapshot is itself a stall; any request
switched on 20 Hz state publishing; a cursor needs an epoch and launch guard.

### Live probe (headless app, muted, isolated profile on U:)

`debugctl launch --headless --mute`, about 145 callbacks/s at Home. Faults were induced through
`eval expr=OS.delay_msec(N)`; `frames reset=true` forced a new epoch.

| Watch | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| 45 s, first candidate | 84 polls, ≤121 rows each; 300 ms stall and 4,000.9 ms freeze each captured as a complete incident (600 lead, 300 recovery rows) with a valid v1 capture; epoch reset became segment 2 read from sequence 1; stutter/stall/hitch counts equal the bridge deltas; 2 snapshot-perturbed intervals excluded. Two defects found: the freeze was not reported as a `hang` (two-ping rule needed > 4 s), and the 300 ms stall also fired `drop` (mean window) |
| 35 s, after fixes | 62 polls, ≤140 rows; stall incident `{stall: 1}` only; freeze reported as `hang` 2.1 s after onset and joined with its measured 4,001.2 ms stall; exact reconciliation; clean stop (`crash: null`, `leaked_objects: 0`). A later real 11.9 ms relative stutter's lead-up overlapped the freeze, which led to separate `worst_ms` / `lead_worst_ms` |

| 30 s, after candidate review | 54 polls. A 4 s freeze became a `hang`, confirmed by two pings showing the same engine frame, at 2.3 s, and was joined with its 4,001.0 ms stall. A 1.2 s freeze stayed a `stall`. 0 slow polls; exact reconciliation; clean stop |

Outputs were inspected as JSON. The browser automation available here did not connect, so the HTML
has not been reviewed in a browser yet.

### Rendered session (1920×1080, Windows, muted, profile and outputs on U:)

These are software checks of the watch on the real renderer, not product performance qualification.
The window was not focused (the agent drives it), so the flight opened on the pause menu.

| Watch | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| 90 s, Home → Fly → cameras → Home | 9,238 intervals, 165 polls (≤168 rows), exact reconciliation (108 stutters, 27 stalls). Home → Fly load: a 2,341 ms interval inside an 18-stall incident; a `starting` route of 32 intervals at 14 FPS with stalls up to 638 ms; Home steady at 119 FPS (65.7 FPS 1% low). The 2.3 s load was a slow poll, not a hang, because the second ping saw the engine move. **Defect:** `stutter` captured 13–17 ms spikes after ~8 ms frames, which still meet the 60 FPS budget, and used all 10 incidents. |
| 60 s, airborne flight after the fix | `stutter` now needs a budget-breaking interval (`stutter_min_ms`). Five incidents. A **7,706 ms main-thread freeze** right after Resume and the first stick command became a `hang` (client lower bound 6.4 s), with no log lines, negligible object/memory change, `focused: false` and 12-tick physics catch-up as a consequence. It is **unattributed**. Flight stalls of 47–133 ms roughly every 5 s coincide with the scripted camera switches, a correlation only. 81 FPS mean; exact reconciliation (127 stutters, 9 stalls); clean stop. |

Both rendered findings, the 7.7 s unfocused freeze and the stalls around camera switches, need a
focused, owner-driven repeat before anyone attributes a cause.

## Candidate review

A second Good Enemy review of the actual candidate found nine defects; all were fixed.

- Any error mid-run lost every result; the output cap could also starve the final files.
- Any failed poll counted as a freeze.
- `triggers=stutter` dropped stall frames.
- The baseline was poisoned after a drop recovered.
- A stale observer marker survived an epoch change.
- The drop detector's blind period was 11 s, against the 2 s documented.
- Two tests were weaker than their names.

The review's suggestion to compare the ping frame with the last polled row was not adopted. Up to one
poll interval of frames can separate them, so a real freeze would look like progress. A second ping is
used instead.

## Limitations and open work

- No rendered 1080p flight, organic incident, observer-cost comparison (bridge absent vs watch)
  or Linux/macOS run yet: plan step S2b/S6.
- Hang duration is a client-side lower bound; the drop detector cannot see slow gradual decline.
- Bridge edits are not covered by the loopback contract scripts in this session (`passive` is a
  network-thread change exercised only by the live probe).
