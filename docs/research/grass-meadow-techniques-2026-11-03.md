# Runway meadow: game and ecology techniques for natural plant distribution

2026-11-03. Owner: landscape **L**, step **L11a-R3** in the [grass quality plan](../plans/grass-quality.md#3-reviewable-implementation-sequence). Complements the [rendering/budget research R01–R44](grass-quality-2026-10-17.md), which did not cover how games and ecology distribute plants. Implementation: [runway_meadow.gd](../../app/scenery/runway_meadow.gd), test [test_runway_meadow.gd](../../app/tests/test_runway_meadow.gd).

## Problem observed

Baseline 1080p captures of the default field showed 150 small imported tufts, evenly sprinkled inside 30 m of the pilot. They read as isolated "planted sprouts". The 100 m runway margins had no plants, although free photographic grass cards, clover and flowers were already downloaded. A first per-cell scatter of the cheap cards still read as speckles: uniform random placement is the wrong model.

## Resulting design (L11a-R3)

- **Colonies, not scatter.** Colony centres are hashed per 2.6 m world cell and accepted against a layered density. Kinds: tussock, clover mat, flower stand, mown-lip weeds; plus a sparse single-tuft layer.
- **Clump-shared parameters.** Height factor, dryness tint and facing; members spread over a jittered Vogel spiral at ~0.3 m spacing, larger at the centre.
- **Species budget.** Costly accents are capped by stable rank on the complete high set; surplus members become 20–26 triangle photographic cards with unchanged position and size. Quality keeps nested colony and member subsets.
- **Masks and contracts.** Same masks as the imported near grass; runway, rotor pad and pilot station stay clear. No colliders or simulation state. Shadows off, existing bounded wind.

## Measurements (2026-11-03, RTX 3090, Compatibility, 1920×1080, MSAA 4×)

Scratch capture of the production field; 120-frame GPU means, meadow hidden/visible alternating twice. A 3,639-plant candidate added roughly **+0.1 to +0.4 ms** GPU per view (noisy); the first speckle candidate (9,587 plants) cost +0.4 to +0.6 ms. The final high tier has 4,492 plants and 153,704 source triangles in 22 batches (16 culling cells of photographic cards, six capped accent batches); layout takes about 130 ms during field construction.

[Scene attribution](../../tools/scenery/README.md) at 1280×720 on the same host: worst drawn cost 134,254 (pilot) / 153,346 (ground) / 81,992 (aerial) primitives, 23 draws. An unchunked build drew 186,262 in every view. The subsystem allocation is recorded in [scene_budget.json](../../tools/scenery/scene_budget.json). On this host the class totals already exceeded their limits before the meadow (pilot 1,362,894 primitives / 362 draws against 1,307,000 / 360), so class-level acceptance needs an owner allocation or a matching reduction. Meanwhile the meadow is a player choice: **Settings → Graphics → Meadow** (On/Off, saved as `runway_meadow` in `display.cfg`, default On; `OPENRC_RUNWAY_MEADOW` or `--runway_meadow=` override it). Off removes the whole layer cost. Not a full-session qualification; the paired protocol and owner gates of the grass plan remain open.
