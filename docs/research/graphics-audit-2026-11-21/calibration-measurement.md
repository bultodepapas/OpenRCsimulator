# Automatic graphics policy and evidence audit

Source reviewed: OpenRCsimulator `graphics-audit` at `57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e`; engine pin `4.8.beta2.official.cafc17a19`. All findings below are source inspection or explicitly synthetic policy examples, not new hardware performance measurements. The application and engine were not launched by the specialist who authored this source review. The lead executed the separately described synthetic policy probe using the unchanged dependencies and pinned engine; no GPU workload was rendered. Paths and line numbers refer to that source tree.

## C1. A calibration can mix workloads and label the result with only the final one

**Trigger and evidence.** During the first eligible flight, pause after sampling has started, open Settings, change MSAA, reconstruction, window size, VSync or the frame cap, and resume. [app/render/auto_calibration.gd:100-107](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/auto_calibration.gd#L100-L107) stops collecting while flight is inactive and resets the interval timestamp, but retains `_active_s` and the prior sample arrays. At completion `:124-134` assigns one `current_workload()` and one refresh/floor to the entire accumulated sample. Pause opens Settings at `app/app_root.gd:1971-1977,3451-3492`, and `:2089-2108,2132-2136` applies presentation changes to the existing viewport. [app/render/reconstruction.gd:254-270](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/reconstruction.gd#L254-L270) applies reconstruction scale and MSAA. The `session.cancel_calibration()` call in `open_pause()` is the radio wizard ([app/sim/flight_session.gd:1927-1931](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/sim/flight_session.gd#L1927-L1931)), not `GraphicsAutoCalibration`.

**Confidence and limits.** High for the source path; no mixed calibration was run on a GPU. Later size/MSAA matching cannot distinguish old and new samples already combined into one persisted p95. The current pause behavior does correctly exclude the paused wall interval. Different camera views and scenery are legitimate variation, but silently assigning one resolution/AA identity to mixed settings is a narrower, testable issue.

**Alternative and validation.** Track a small identity covering the effective workload and pacing at sampling boundaries. Restart or explicitly mark the sample incomplete if that identity changes; do not reject ordinary pause/resume with no material change. Test unchanged pause, AA/scale changes, display/refresh changes, provider fallback, changed-then-reverted preview, resize, and cleanup. On real target hardware compare reset versus uninterrupted collection, and report polling overhead. This is a residual issue beyond the repaired F04/F07 stored-pixel checks.

## C2. High rejection survives an incompatible 3D workload

**Trigger and evidence.** An eligible High flight stores a complete rejection at 1080p under 4× MSAA. The player later reduces MSAA or the 3D input size, keeping the same screen. [app/render/auto_calibration.gd:343-351](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/auto_calibration.gd#L343-L351) rejects High using only adapter, method and output pixels; it does not call the MSAA/internal-pixel matcher at `:316-340`. `app/app_state/renderer_preference.gd:109-116,137-154` feeds that veto into Automatic startup. Once startup uses Standard, [app/app_root.gd:1759-1769](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/app_root.gd#L1759-L1769) can measure Standard's scene detail, but High is not active for a new High measurement; the Forward+ rejection remains separately stored. Existing tests at [app/tests/test_auto_calibration.gd:280-323](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/tests/test_auto_calibration.gd#L280-L323) establish the repaired one-sided output rule and scene-workload checks but do not exercise this combination.

**Confidence and limits.** High for policy asymmetry; the injected workload and rejection below do not show a real machine crosses the threshold. A rejection at a smaller output is intentionally conservative for a larger output; that alone is not a defect. Explicit High choice is preserved by the startup planner, and Restore graphics defaults clears stored records.

**Alternative and validation.** At startup, compare the persisted High verdict against the intended High workload, not the currently running Standard viewport. Define what to do when the selected provider is unavailable or the future effective workload is unknown; either invalidate the veto only on a clearly relevant change, or offer a bounded, guarded retry. Test same size with changed AA and 3D scale separately, as well as same settings, explicit High, renderer-guard failure, restart loop, and larger/smaller output. A real-device paired test must establish whether the new High workload meets the target before recommending it.

**Executed witness.** The checked pinned-engine probe in [`calibration_probe.gd`](../../../research/performance/graphics-audit-2026-11-21/calibration_probe.gd) verifies actual storage and planner functions with synthetic inputs. It checks unchanged settings, AA-only, internal-size-only, both changes, and the already-fixed smaller-output control. The final run passed 24 assertions with zero engine warnings. Its renderer selection is configuration only under headless Dummy execution; it is not a rendered test. See [reproduction and strict regression status](review-and-validation.md).

**Reachability qualification from independent review.** AA-only is the clearest player path: Standard can change native MSAA, while High-only reconstruction controls are generally unavailable in Standard ([app/ui/settings_screen.gd:1480-1502](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/ui/settings_screen.gd#L1480-L1502); [app/render/reconstruction.gd:170-185](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/reconstruction.gd#L170-L185)). The internal-scale examples prove policy behavior, not that Standard always exposes FSR controls. A player can change settings while still in High after a completed calibration, then restart before another eligible High flight; the completed calibrator has already deleted itself. Starting another eligible High flight first can replace the verdict, so persistence is not inevitable. Once in Standard, its own renderer slot does not overwrite Forward+'s rejection. Future High workload must be resolved from saved intent and actual capabilities, not the currently running Standard viewport.

## C3. Same pixels and MSAA do not identify the reconstruction algorithm

**Trigger and evidence.** On supported High, compare Native at 1.0 scale with MSAA off against FSR2 Native AA. [app/render/reconstruction.gd:27-30](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/reconstruction.gd#L27-L30) assigns both a 1.0 scale; `:254-270` applies different 3D scaling modes and makes temporal reconstruction disable MSAA. Both consequently have equal output pixels, nominal 3D pixels and MSAA, the only workload fields [app/render/auto_calibration.gd:316-340](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/auto_calibration.gd#L316-L340) stores and compares. `needed()` at `:358-367` can reuse one verdict for the other. Shadow, lighting and other effect settings also change cost without this small identity, but not all settings necessarily merit persistent keys.

**Confidence and limits.** High for the identity collision; the resulting timing difference and threshold direction have not been measured for the exact same-AA pair. The 3090 matrix [docs/research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/rtx3090-matrix-2026-11-17/README.md:31-40](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/docs/research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/rtx3090-matrix-2026-11-17/README.md#L31-L40) compares Native 4× with FSR2 Native AA, so it does not isolate provider cost at matched AA. The F07 repair did add output, 3D-pixel and MSAA checks for scene-detail reuse; it should not be described as absent.

**Alternative and validation.** Include a bounded effective provider or scaling-mode identity and an explicit fallback/unknown state in the record, avoiding a hash of every setting. `Reconstruction.describe()` exposes policy and mode, but its selected MetalFX provider does not by itself prove GPU execution. Test Native-off/FSR2 Native AA, same-mode reuse, provider failure/fallback and return, then compare matched-size/AA flight and ground views on a supported native GPU.

## C4. A positive GPU poll is not proven to be a fresh, complete frame measurement

**Trigger and evidence.** `app/render/auto_calibration.gd:113-119,221-241` appends every finite positive root-viewport GPU poll and labels the decision complete after 300 such polls. The existing diagnostic states that this getter is a cached last reading without completed-frame ID or age (`app/render/frame_diagnostics.gd:7-16,35-40`), and the 3090 matrix describes its GPU polls as freshness-unverified (`rtx3090-matrix-2026-11-17/README.md:17-19,119-121`). By construction, 300 copies of a cached 3 ms value satisfy the sample-count rule and can produce an up verdict with light CPU and no other viewport; this is a classifier counterexample, not proof that all 300 real polls are stale. Equal values alone also do not prove stale data.

The root query excludes extra viewports. Current code has already repaired F08 by tracking activity throughout the sample (`auto_calibration.gd:65-70,110-112,157-177`) and blocks upward adjustment if one was active. It does not measure the omitted work; root-time down/rejection decisions remain scoped to the root. On Apple Silicon the graphics-ladder methodology says Metal viewport timings undercount on the tile GPU and turns them off ([research/performance/graphics_ladder/README.md:75-84](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/research/performance/graphics_ladder/README.md#L75-L84)), whereas calibration is only gated for headless and macOS Compatibility ([app/app_root.gd:1759-1762](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/app_root.gd#L1759-L1762); [app/render/frame_diagnostics.gd:215-220](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/frame_diagnostics.gd#L215-L220)), leaving Mobile/Metal eligible.

**Confidence and limits.** High for the mismatch in evidence semantics and route. Backend-dependent freshness, actual Metal bias and specific incorrect verdicts require hardware. Render timing is not presentation timing, and a timing count is not a fresh-frame count. Existing `frame_diagnostics.gd` appropriately reports freshness as unknown; do not call it a zero-timing bug. Separate-thread timing reads are already throttled to 50 ms (`auto_calibration.gd:43-47,147-154`).

**Alternative and validation.** Keep unverified/partial scope in the verdict or use a pinned-engine-compatible frame/completion identity if it can be associated to the viewport timing; do not deduplicate equal numerical values. Compare on/off instrumentation and poll intervals, root versus independently supported secondary-viewport captures, Metal command-buffer/encoder timings, thermals and clock state on each backend. A bounded neutral fallback is better than treating unavailable data as zero. Apple's [Metal Performance HUD guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/monitoring-your-metal-apps-graphics-performance) distinguishes command-buffer and encoder timing and notes encoder-tracking overhead; it is a tool description, not a result from this audit.

## C5. A 60 Hz policy floor is not a 120 Hz presentation test

**Trigger and evidence.** [app/render/auto_calibration.gd:189-197](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/auto_calibration.gd#L189-L197) sets the floor to at least 16.667 ms, even on a faster display. This is intentional and pinned at [app/tests/test_auto_calibration.gd:108-115](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/tests/test_auto_calibration.gd#L108-L115); `docs/plans/automatic-graphics.md:256-264,320` leaves a refresh-aware target decision open. With synthetic 120 Hz VSync, 16.67 ms process intervals, 3 ms root GPU, 1 ms render CPU, and no active secondary viewport, `evaluate()` at `auto_calibration.gd:221-241` marks `cpu_bound=false` and can raise Medium to High, despite process cadence being half of nominal refresh. That satisfies the coded 60 Hz target; this is not proof of a physical presentation miss or a claim that the policy promised 120 Hz.

**Confidence and limits.** High for the arithmetic; no target display was exercised here. [app/debug/debug_bridge.gd:2113-2119](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/debug/debug_bridge.gd#L2113-L2119) explicitly labels its frame data process wall intervals, not presentation. VSync, VRR, compositor behavior, caps, refresh and focus affect the relationship. The Windows ladder reports a real Home → Fly route on one RTX 3090 at 4K/120, but its windows are bridge intervals; even its stated VSync runs alone do not establish displayed-frame or input-to-photon behavior ([research/performance/graphics_ladder/README.md:75-92](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/research/performance/graphics_ladder/README.md#L75-L92); `graphics-ladder-2026-11-19/README.md:350-360`).

**Alternative and validation.** Choose explicitly whether the target is 60 Hz or display cadence. Keep process interval, GPU timing and display-present telemetry as distinct fields. On a named display, correlate OS-supported presentation events and monitor identity, VSync/VRR/cap and compositor mode with complete active-flight intervals, then repeat across native 1080p and the applicable higher/native Retina targets. [PresentMon's own metric definitions](https://github.com/GameTechDev/PresentMon/blob/main/README-CaptureApplication.md) distinguish display change and presentation events; confirm the tool actually covers the selected Windows path, and mark unavailable fields missing.

## C6. The Settings summary can report the heuristic instead of the planned mode

**Trigger and evidence.** For a GPU whose pure policy recommends High but whose valid stored rejection or failed guard selects Standard, `app/render/auto_graphics_report.gd:33-49,53-82` takes `mode` from the raw `decisions.renderer` and displays any complete calibration value; it does not incorporate the startup planner's rejection/guard or check the displayed record against the current workload. `app/app_state/renderer_preference.gd:104-119,137-154` is the planner that can choose Standard, and [app/render/scene_detail.gd:115-125](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/scene_detail.gd#L115-L125) already ignores a mismatched scene calibration. [app/ui/settings_screen.gd:1212-1224](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/ui/settings_screen.gd#L1212-L1224) phrases the summary as Automatic's mode and a measured p95. Existing snapshot tests at [app/tests/test_auto_graphics_snapshot.gd:90-106](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/tests/test_auto_graphics_snapshot.gd#L90-L106) cover pure summaries but no veto or stale record. `runtime()` loads only the **current rendering method** slot (`auto_graphics_report.gd:62,82`): after a High-to-Standard restart it normally reads a Standard record, not the rejected Forward+ record. The raw heuristic mismatch and stale same-method measurement problem are separate. The executed formatter witness uses a synthetic Standard record and does not execute the UI or `runtime()`.

**Confidence and limits.** High for differing source outputs; the final user-facing semantics need an owner decision because a deliberate explicit mode and an automatic future recommendation are different concepts. No native UI was run. A complete historical record is useful evidence when it is labeled with its workload and age/scope; it need not be deleted.

**Alternative and validation.** Report raw recommendation, planned automatic mode and active mode separately, and show a prior measurement with its dimensions/provider/AA and compatibility status. Test injected planner/summary cases for valid veto, guard failure, explicit mode and stale workloads; then confirm startup/restart wording in an exported native package.

## Evidence boundaries to preserve when integrating the audit

- `app/data/gpu/capability.json:4-11,53-60` and [app/render/gpu_capability.gd:133-140](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/app/render/gpu_capability.gd#L133-L140) extrapolate one RTX 3090 anchor by relative GPU scores. A model-derived value or matching a GPU name is not a measured capability for that hardware. [docs/plans/automatic-graphics.md:107-111](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/docs/plans/automatic-graphics.md#L107-L111) identifies the scaling weakness; the assertion at `:132` that dedicated GPUs can hold 60 fps is not established by the table. The M1 correction was fit to its own externally cooled, mirrored 1080p measurements, not independent native-panel validation (`automatic-graphics.md:24-28`).
- The GTX 1070 row is extrapolated (`capability.json:889-895`): with `rel=0.32`, the 1080p 1.25× Extreme estimate is approximately 4.19 ms against a 4.3 ms model threshold. That narrow calculated margin is not a measurement or a robust bound across GPUs/CPUs/power conditions. The 3090 worst-case campaign was an editor-binary, mostly scripted view, had no visual confirmation of the weather/lens flags, and omitted real flying/companion qualification (`worst-case-2026-11-18/README.md:3-12,44-50`). Its 1.25× factor is a screening assumption, not a guaranteed worst case.
- The first Intel UHD 630 comparison found Compatibility faster than Mobile on the named Linux/Mesa, LXC, headless-Weston host (`docs/research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/igpu-uhd630-2026-11-20/README.md:9-17,20-36`). It was a scripted High-detail view, not the device's Low Automatic or a physical-display qualification. It falsifies the blanket Mobile-faster assumption for that setup only; it does not establish Windows or AMD results.
- Recent fixes already present include logical/output pixel consistency (F04), one-sided output-size validation for the High veto (part of F05), applied Mac reconstruction scale (F06), output/internal/MSAA matching for scene-detail reuse (part of F07), and secondary-view tracking over the sample (F08). Incomplete-no-retry and the p95 order statistic are documented tradeoffs, not newly discovered regressions (F09/F10). The 3090 controller stall was already repaired; the separate-render-thread experiment remains unqualified.
- The Mac display probe did not exercise the Retina branch ([docs/research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/mac-display-probe-2026-11-18/README.md:18-38](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/57abea025f62b20233ca281d58d2acbee4ab8b8e/docs/research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/mac-display-probe-2026-11-18/README.md#L18-L38)). Preserve the distinction between screen/output, actual drawable and internal 3D pixels, and preserve native Retina as the Apple Silicon target. None of these observations supplies a new FPS improvement.
