## S01 — Selected Nitro car synthesizes PCM on the process thread

**Trigger and source.** Ground-vehicle default is electric Bisonte; MONSTER CAR 2 is a separately selected Nitro vehicle ([app/vehicles/ground/vehicle_catalog.gd:4-13](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/vehicles/ground/vehicle_catalog.gd#L4-L13)). Audio is on by default and creates its Nitro player when that vehicle is selected ([app/vehicles/ground/ground_scene.gd:110,300-301,487-490](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/vehicles/ground/ground_scene.gd#L110-L300)). Each process callback snapshots latest telemetry, calls `observe`, and then `refill` ([app/vehicles/ground/ground_scene.gd:717-718,736,778-781](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/vehicles/ground/ground_scene.gd#L717-L718)). `refill` executes `voice.render(frame_count)` synchronously and submits the resulting PCM with one `push_buffer`, not one push per sample ([app/audio/nitro_engine_audio.gd:69-99](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/audio/nitro_engine_audio.gd#L69-L99)). The per-sample synthesis loop is GDScript ([app/audio/nitro_voice.gd:71-145](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/audio/nitro_voice.gd#L71-L145)). This is a selected-car CPU candidate, not fixed-wing audio F15/F16.

**Exact work bounds.** The configured generator rate is 22,050 stereo frames/s; the target is `ceil(0.05 × 22,050) = 1,103` frames. Consequently a normal refill renders at most 1,103 frames; the voice’s more general public cap is 4,096 ([nitro_engine_audio.gd:7-8,69-95](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/audio/nitro_engine_audio.gd#L7); [nitro_voice.gd:5-6,71-75](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/audio/nitro_voice.gd#L5)). Under sustained, unpaused playback without underruns and with a roughly steady queue, synthesis averages about 22,050 frames per second. At positive current RPM, nine exhaust harmonics plus two crank-rattle sines execute per sample. All nine exhaust harmonics are below the 11,025 Hz Nyquist ceiling throughout the clamped 0–45,000 RPM range: the ninth is at most 6,750 Hz. This gives about 242,550 sine evaluations/s for those eleven calls, plus up to three per sample for clutch and transient layers, or at most about 308,700/s under those same sustained conditions. Actual frame cadence and underruns change the work performed per wall second; these are operation counts, not timings.

**Mute and ownership.** The scene Sound toggle or pause stops/refuses refill ([nitro_engine_audio.gd:42-54,78-80](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/audio/nitro_engine_audio.gd#L42); [ground_scene.gd:985-987](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/vehicles/ground/ground_scene.gd#L985)). Master volume zero only mutes the Master bus ([app/app_root.gd:2082-2086](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/app_root.gd#L2082-L2086)), so it does not exercise that gate. An engine-ineligible transition flushes/restarts the player, but does not itself set `_paused_or_disabled`; later `refill()` can continue producing silent output and updating voice state. Do not remove it without preserving smoothing, decay, phase, noise, output gating and restart behavior. The separate aircraft glow voice already has a bounded worker pump with explicit lifetime/locking rules ([app/audio/glow_audio_pump.gd:1-6,57-88,162-215](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/audio/glow_audio_pump.gd#L1-L6)), but it is not automatically a drop-in Nitro solution.

**Experiment and acceptance.** Profile the detached `NitroVoice.observe/render` component, then the complete vehicle with sound on/off and a no-underrun counter; compare component work with full callback time and real device output. If it is material, prefer an equivalent native C++ producer or a rigorously owned worker, still emitting bounded `PackedVector2Array` buffers through the existing single `push_buffer`; account for GDScript/native crossings, queue lead, and thread-safe playback/lifetime. Preserve the original as a reference. [app/tests/test_rcv_nitro_audio.gd:26-42,80-178](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/tests/test_rcv_nitro_audio.gd#L26-L42) supplies a small telemetry fixture and checks finite/limited output, 2-stroke tone, load, pause, clutch, skid and malformed data. Its full runner also writes a WAV under `res://../docs/...` (`:180-181,203-256`), so use a disposable copy or a scratch-only pure-voice harness rather than running it in a source-read-only tree. Extend comparisons to every output sample and relevant internal state (`phase_cycles`, `current_rpm`, smoothing/gate/transient/noise state and diagnostics) across different block splits and observed-state transitions; document numeric tolerance if native library math differs. Separately check restart, disabled/paused and underrun behavior and listen on target audio devices. Audio must never feed float32 presentation values back into simulation.

## S02 — Native propeller work is repeated within qualifying stage invocations

### Verdict and reachability

The proposed duplicate is real in the C++ source, with narrower default-aircraft reachability than a generic fixed-wing claim. In the successful native stage's valid aero/prop-air branch, `!turbine && !m.glow.ready && m.spatial_weather && m.wake.enabled` evaluates `prop_thrust_torque(prop_air.velocity.data(), rpm, m.prop, rho)` at [research/native-kernel/src/nk_stage.hpp:323-327](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_stage.hpp#L323-L327). The wake path then evaluates the identical function with the identical arguments at `:335-338`. Both results feed the same stage; `rpm`, `rho`, `m.prop` and `prop_air` are unchanged. The pure computation and cutoff are at [nk_prop.hpp:105-118](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_prop.hpp#L105). This is two source-level evaluations, not a measured hotspot or proven FPS improvement. When RPM is below `kStoppedRpm`, both evaluations immediately return zeros, so avoiding one has little arithmetic to save.

The default `jensen-das-ugly-stik-60` and the separate player-selectable `stik-electric-6s` do **not** author slipstream; `AircraftData` only sets it when present, and the electric parser rejects it as an unknown propeller key ([app/physics/aircraft_data.gd:1223-1229,1277-1283,1323-1326,1369-1388](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L1223-L1229); [app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:1510-1663](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1510-L1663); [app/data/aircraft/stik_electric_6s.json:1485-1487](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/data/aircraft/stik_electric_6s.json#L1485-L1487)). Empty wake decodes as disabled ([research/native-kernel/src/nk_wake_binding.hpp:30-41](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_wake_binding.hpp#L30-L41)). Neither Stik is evidence for the two-call wake branch.

Ordinary selectable **experimental** P-51 and F4U stock entries do author a shaft, quasi-static slipstream and finite-liquid consumption ([app/app_state/aircraft_catalog.gd:90-110](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/app_state/aircraft_catalog.gd#L90-L110); [app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json:1766,2279,2453-2454,3831](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L1766-L2279); [app/data/aircraft/f4u_corsair_250.json:1877,2514,2688-2689,3814](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/data/aircraft/f4u_corsair_250.json#L1877-L2514)). Their propulsion's raw string `glow_prop` is a historical parser family, **not** proof of a prepared glow blade-element stage: `Propulsion.is_glow` tests normalized `prop.has("glow")`; only a `glow_installation` attaches that state ([app/physics/propulsion.gd:73-79](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/physics/propulsion.gd#L73-L79); [app/physics/aircraft_data.gd:265-286](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L265-L286)). The Extra 300 has a real glow installation and falls outside this reuse case ([app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json:1527,1843](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json#L1527-L1843)).

Actual native selection must be verified from the installed package/receipt and session, not inferred solely from the default request.

### Also check nonspatial gas: one of the two calls is hidden inside `prop_loads`

For a nonspatial, non-glow gas propeller, `explicit_air` is null and `local_hub` is false; `prop_stage_air_velocity` copies `air.v` into `prop_air.velocity` ([nk_stage.hpp:310-313](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_stage.hpp#L310); [nk_prop.hpp:56-102](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_prop.hpp#L56)). Thus those vectors are bitwise copied values, although they have different addresses. For `rpm >= 1` and `prop.has_axis`, `prop_loads` calls `prop_thrust_torque` internally ([nk_prop.hpp:215-221](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_prop.hpp#L215)), and the wake then separately calls it with the same datum-air values ([nk_stage.hpp:328-338](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_stage.hpp#L328)). The authored P-51/F4U wake requires the parser to install the propeller axis ([aircraft_data.gd:1263-1280](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L1263)). This qualifying subcase can occur with the ordinary nonspatial gentle-weather preference, subject to actual aircraft/backend admission; it must not be described as a spatial-only opportunity.

This is not the same count as the spatial path: at `rpm < 1`, `prop_loads` returns before its internal call but the wake still invokes its short-cutting call. For a no-axis propeller, `prop_loads` has its own inlined Ct/Cp arithmetic and the `NK_MUTATION` negative-control branch ([nk_prop.hpp:215-234](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_prop.hpp#L215)); do not automatically treat that as a proved reusable function result. For electric/glow, nonspatial `prop_air` can include hub rotation and need not equal datum air. A safe nonspatial prototype must explicitly guard the established same-source case, preserve the existing stopped behavior, and compare bitwise results; it cannot unconditionally swap `prop_loads` for `prop_loads_from_thrust_torque` at low/invalid RPM.

### Safe experiment and existing evidence seams

## S03 — Accepted support events repeat validation, serialization and proof hashing

**Trigger and source.** Ordinary, non-scripted interactive fixed-wing flight enables incident accounting unless disabled; capture and trace routes omit it ([app/main.gd:1343-1358](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/main.gd#L1343-L1358), [app/main.gd:3099-3104](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/main.gd#L3099-L3104)). The session’s accepted-tick handler constructs a support event for every compressed support point ([app/sim/flight_session.gd:2580-2614,2752-2769](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/sim/flight_session.gd#L2580-L2614)). `support_event()` validates each constructed event ([app/sim/aircraft_contact_events.gd:290-305,308-322](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/sim/aircraft_contact_events.gd#L290-L305)). The synchronous incident observer then obtains a deep-copied contact snapshot ([app/main.gd:3030-3042](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/main.gd#L3030-L3042); [app/sim/flight_session.gd:2682-2685](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/sim/flight_session.gd#L2682-L2685)) and serializes the whole event array to check its byte bound, validates each event again, checks its strings, then serializes and SHA-256 hashes each event ([app/economy/incident.gd:524-572,631-647](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/economy/incident.gd#L524-L572); [app/sim/aircraft_contact_events.gd:205-235](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/sim/aircraft_contact_events.gd#L205-L235)). This is additional detail beyond existing N3/F11’s gear-compression cost, not a replacement for that finding.

**Counts and limits.** With 240 accepted ticks per real second and three support points continuously compressed, with valid bindings and fresh accepted events there are three persistent event digests per tick, or 720 event digests per second; the count scales down with actual accepted tick rate and contact count. Every event ID contains its tick ([app/sim/aircraft_contact_events.gd:554-570](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/sim/aircraft_contact_events.gd#L554-L570)), so adjacent persistent events are not identical. The proof window is capped at 128 events; once full, every fresh event inserts a proof and removes the oldest. For the same three-support illustration, that is approximately 720 removals per second after saturation. A support ending can emit an `ended` event; genuinely empty airborne batches do not incur per-event hashes, but the current path still serializes `[]` and runs the rest of the empty-batch logic. These counts are not physical disk traffic: default archive and valuation use background workers. SHA-256 and Godot JSON serialization are already built-in native operations; a wrapper alone is not evidence of a speedup.

**Smallest experiments.** First profile matched incident-on/off product flights, separating airborne, taxi, touchdown/liftoff and post-damage settlement, with full callback cost and tail frames. A narrow empty-array branch could skip its provably redundant byte serialization and empty per-event loops while retaining the existing result, tick/cursor, count, stale/refusal, crash, restore and terminal semantics. For nonempty batches, investigate whether a properly detached, immutable, provenance-bound same-tick receipt can share work; do not trust a public dictionary or reuse a digest merely because an ID matches. The batch bound uses `JSON.stringify(events)` while proof digest uses `JSON.stringify(event, "", true, true)`; their encodings are not interchangeable without exact byte proof. A binary proof format or changed digest would alter stored evidence: checkpoint capture and validation include `seen_order`, proof floor and canonical journal hashes ([app/economy/checkpoint.gd:30-55,106-120,304-329](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/economy/checkpoint.gd#L30-L55)), so that would require an explicit version/migration rather than being a transparent optimization. A bounded circular internal representation is another measured candidate only if exported proof order remains identical.

**Acceptance.** Extend the focused persistent-event and checkpoint cases in [app/tests/test_crash_cost_incident.gd:60-100](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/6b2228c7633a34e38cb05d92e3ca50893810e02b/app/tests/test_crash_cost_incident.gd#L60-L100), plus duplicate/conflicting payload, same-tick amendment, invalid/oversize, stale and evicted proofs, changing bindings, restore and crash on an empty batch. Compare full event arrays, refusal/status, ledger/checkpoint and replay evidence byte for byte over touchdown, liftoff, gear movement, terrain, lake and circuit. Keep all physical work and fixed-240-Hz transaction ordering unchanged.
