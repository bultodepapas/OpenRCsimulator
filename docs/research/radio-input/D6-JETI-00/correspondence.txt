Hi Giuseppe,

thank you for your will to support our transmitters in your RC simulator 
app. I will try to help you understand how our transmitters works and 
how to interpret it's inputs.

When connected to a PC, our transmitters implements USB HID protocol. So 
they are detected as a "gaming device" without any further 
configuration. First thing you are probably interested in is USB VID and 
PID numbers. They are as follows:

  * 0x0483 (Vendor ID, it corresponds to STMicroelectronics -
    manufacturer of our MCU)
  * 0xA07C (Product ID)

In the attachment you can find dumped USB descriptors, co you can find 
valuable information there. I decided to put rest of the information in 
a pdf document. It will be better to read than here in a email. In 
short, I tried a transmitter in your simulator and basic functionality 
worked out-of-box. I was able to take off and control the airplane. The 
only problem was with buttons and I tried to explain how they works and 
maps to the transmitter in the document. Also a 
"controller_profiles.cfg" file created by the simulator is attached to 
the email. The pdf document was made in rush, but I hope it will help.

If you have more questions or want further assistance then you can write 
directly to me. I am afraid that I won't be able to give you enough 
support at work, but I am willing to help you in my free time. I am able 
to test a "JETI" controller profile or even help you to create a one. I 
won't be so responsive, but I think during evenings/weekends I will find 
some time to help.

Best regards,

Jakub Uhlík
