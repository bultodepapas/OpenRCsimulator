# Numerics and ground-contact audit

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Scope:** rigid-body equations, gravity/frame handling, inertia and rotor gyros, RK4 stage consistency and refinement, binary64 state, landing-gear contact/friction, and crash-plane crossing for the current Ventage Jet and Jensen Ugly Stik 60.  
**Result:** no rigid-body, inertia, free-fall, free-rotation, or RK4 refinement invariant failure was found. A separate current Avanti runway-start suite does reproduce a crosswind equilibrium-solver failure, documented below. This is software/numerical verification; it does not validate the aircraft coefficients against flight or ground measurements.

The repository had unrelated uncommitted edits during the audit. All commands ran against that working tree on 2026-10-14; raw logs are retained in [`research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/numerics-ground/`](../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/numerics-ground/README.md).

## Rigid-body and solver evidence

The state and derivative use packed float64 arrays. [The rigid-body derivative](../../../../app/physics/rigid_body.gd#L70) maps body velocity to NED position rate, rotates NED-down gravity into body axes, uses `q̇ = ½ q ⊗ [0,ω]`, and applies the full symmetric-tensor equation `ω̇ = J⁻¹(M − ω × (Jω + h_rotor))` ([translation and gravity](../../../../app/physics/rigid_body.gd#L90), [rotation](../../../../app/physics/rigid_body.gd#L121)). [RK4](../../../../app/physics/integrator.gd#L20) evaluates the four classical stages; its nonautonomous form advances stage time explicitly, and final attitude is normalized ([finish step](../../../../app/physics/integrator.gd#L30)). [Simulation.step](../../../../app/sim/simulation.gd#L156) holds tick inputs/auxiliary samples fixed while evaluating stage-dependent loads and validates both trial and accepted states.

The diagnostic [loads the production aircraft records through `AircraftData.load_file`](../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/numerics-ground/current_aircraft_invariants.gd#L18), then independently diagonalizes each symmetric inertia tensor. For the Ventage Jet, mass is 11.705089 kg and principal moments are `[0.686363260118, 2.464195907089, 3.058615378580] kg·m²`; the principal-moment triangle margin `J₁+J₂−J₃` is 0.091943788627 kg·m². For the Jensen Stik, mass is 2.885058291 kg and principal moments are `[0.114929778272, 0.387419780982, 0.484250425286] kg·m²`; the margin is 0.018099133967 kg·m². Both tensors are positive definite and satisfy the rigid-body triangle condition. At 85,000 rpm the Avanti turbine rotor momentum is 0.311541271481 N·m·s along the modeled shaft; the Stik propeller value at 7,500 rpm is 0.274889357189 N·m·s.

The same production tensors were used in a tilted, zero-force ballistic probe at 240 Hz. After 1.25 s, both aircraft matched `½gt² = 7.6614453125 m` down and `gt = 12.2583125 m/s` to within `1e-14 m` and `5e-14 m/s`, respectively. Five seconds of torque-free rotation at 240 Hz conserved inertial angular momentum to `2.941e-11 N·m·s` for the Avanti and `1.08e-12 N·m·s` for the Stik; relative rotational-energy errors were `1e-14` and `1.6e-13`, and the final quaternion norms were 1.0.

`test_rigid_body` passed 15/15 checks; `test_dynamics` passed 7/7, including the rotor gyroscopic Jacobian term. `test_integrator` passed 9/9 with fourth-order error ratios 15.9 and 15.9 when halving the step. Coupled-state RK4 passed 42/42 checks with ratios 14.57466 and 15.37821; the time-dependent coupled RK test passed 22/22, with fourth-order ratios 14.90122 and 15.40752 and a 240 Hz reference error of `3.8654e-12`. Full-step shaft coupling passed 43/43; shaft refinement passed 3/3. The smooth coupled path had ratios 16.35/16.17. A held downwash lag produced ratios 4.086/4.066, consistent with the test's documented second-order lag approximation rather than a fourth-order claim. Logs are linked in the evidence index.

For glow/electric-style shaft coupling, [the body derivative includes the rotor momentum](../../../../app/physics/glow/flight.gd#L231) and the equal-and-opposite shaft reaction is solved with body acceleration ([coupling solve](../../../../app/physics/rotor_coupling.gd#L43)). Rotor tests passed 17/17 and 65/65; torque-free angular-momentum residuals were approximately `1e-14–1e-13 N·m·s`. The Avanti turbine includes steady rotor gyroscopic momentum, but [transient mounting reaction is explicitly omitted](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L145): an open-system rotor/inlet/exhaust angular-momentum balance is required, and the transient term is unverified. Treat this as a model limit, not as a numerical defect demonstrated here.

## Ground, contact, and crash evidence

[Ground contact](../../../../app/physics/ground_contact.gd#L75) computes spring/damper normal reaction, contact-point moments, steering-frame longitudinal/lateral loads, friction-circle limiting, and optional static anchors. The integrated wheel path is in [GroundWheels.evaluate](../../../../app/physics/ground_wheels.gd#L98); wheel rim speed is a continuous state, but wheel gyroscopic coupling is omitted ([wheel moment assembly](../../../../app/physics/ground_wheels.gd#L166)). The friction/stiction tests establish implementation signs, bounds, dissipation, and repeatability for the authored coefficients, not real tire behavior.

The current Avanti retractable-gear path has a shared `set_wheel_brake(amount)` demand. It enters the six-element input vector, reaches each contact through the ground evaluator, and appears in the gear trace input layout ([session input and setter](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L2103), [load path](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L2344), [trace layout](../../../../app/sim/trace.gd#L58)); the desktop B key applies full demand while held ([input handler](../../../../app/main.gd#L1151)). The Avanti profile assumes optional main-wheel brakes at an estimated 1.0 N·m each, with no nose-wheel brake ([profile](../../../../app/data/ground/wheel_profiles.json#L87)). The Jensen Stik profile declares no brakes and zero brake torque on all three wheels ([profile](../../../../app/data/ground/wheel_profiles.json#L5)).

The loaded Stik contact tests passed: `test_ground_contact` 22/22, `test_ground_friction` 26/26, `test_ground_stiction` 25/25, and `test_ground_surfaces` 29/29. Measured software checks include:

- Static three-wheel support after a 6 s drop gave total force `[-0.1935, -0.000007, -28.2921] N` against `m·g ≈ 28.2921 N`, with net moment components below `5e-5 N·m`; mean compression was about 18.1 mm against 18 mm declared sag.
- A 0.35 m drop settled on all three wheels without crash. A 2.0 m drop triggered the declared nose-gear travel-limit crash at 2.7 m/s sink.
- At 240 vs 480 Hz, the 1 s bounce differed by `1.92e-7 m` in altitude, `4.55e-5` in quaternion components, and `2.25e-6 m/s` in sink rate. A normal-force-only 1 s drop drifted north `2.96e-7 m` at 240 Hz and `6.03e-9 m` at 480 Hz.
- On the modeled runway, the 2 m/s coast-down acceleration was `0.39218 m/s²` versus the independent constant-drag calculation `0.3923 m/s²`; low-speed full-right steering gave a 0.977 m radius versus 0.964 m from wheelbase and steer angle. At 3 m/s the modeled radius opened to 1.348 m from tire slip. These are internal consistency checks, not measurements of either aircraft.
- With the authored idle thrust and mown-runway values, 60 s parked Stik drift was 0.0876 mm; ring-down energy did not rise (`−4.89e-8 J` maximum per tick). The static anchor's largest tested combined breakaway force was 3.5321 N.

The Avanti brake checks in `test_retractable_gear_ground` passed: with the same modeled 3 m/s roll, applying both estimated main-wheel brakes changed the terminal speed from 2.620 to 2.507 m/s; a full stop changed from 6.003 m/3.95 s free-roll to 4.926 m/3.38 s braked. These verify the implemented brake path, not the provisional 1 N·m calibration. That suite did **not** pass completely: twice, its steady-crosswind runway-start check failed. The [assertion](../../../../app/tests/test_retractable_gear_ground.gd#L256) checks whether `_session(240, crosswind)` returns `ok`; the false result comes from `reset_on_runway` failing in `GroundStart.solve`, not from a later stationary-roll check. With wind `1.5 m/s` from `0°` and the test's default eastbound runway spot (`north=15 m`, `east=−47 m`, `heading=π/2`), [stage 2](../../../../app/physics/ground_start.gd#L100) (idle engine, fixed anchors) reports `idling pose did not converge (residual 9.239795830815291)`. The Newton residual combines linear and angular accelerations, so its maximum-component (L-infinity) norm mixes units. `reset_on_runway` returns false and the test counts one failure (24/25 checks pass). A separate fixed-direction magnitude probe at the same Avanti spot accepted starts at `0.0`, `0.5`, and `1.0 m/s`, then rejected `1.5 m/s` with the same residual; this only brackets an acceptance change within the tested interval for this setup and does not establish a general crosswind limit or the physical cause of solver non-convergence. This is a reproducible initialization-solver failure in the current working tree; it does not establish that the corresponding real aircraft cannot sit in that crosswind. Both raw test runs and the probe output are retained in the evidence bundle.

Hard ground crashes are swept when the session's collision query is active. [The callback compares flat-ground and scenery hits](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L1611); hull-plane crossing solves for the first contact root and returns an explicitly labeled interpolation estimate ([crossing method](../../../../app/physics/hull_crossing.gd#L1), [root selection](../../../../app/physics/hull_crossing.gd#L24)). The estimate linearly interpolates position and uses shortest-path normalized quaternion interpolation; it is not a reconstruction of the within-tick RK4 trajectory and it does not infer contact velocity ([snapshot contract](../../../../app/physics/impact_snapshot.gd#L17)). The hull-crossing test passed 40/40, including 100 mixed attitudes, a narrow enter/exit pair with both endpoint depths clear, tangent ambiguity, and invalid/overflow input rejection. Crash-snapshot passed 42/42 and confirms gear-limit snapshots leave unknown deforming-wheel contact speed unset.

## Physical limits and interpretation

The current data sources themselves label the Avanti as an experimental estimate, not flight-identified, with provisional turbine/gear assumptions ([Avanti record](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L5)). The Stik inventory includes an explicitly virtual reference build rather than measured installed hardware ([Stik record](../../../../app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L67)). Its test log also carries a loader warning that `Jyy = 0.3871 kg·m²` is 2.11× a scaled UltraStick reference; that cross-aircraft comparison is not evidence by itself that the Stik inertia tensor is wrong. This audit's eigenvalue and free-rotation tests establish mathematical consistency only.

The GP-05 ground-physics record notes that independent rollout, drop, spin-up, and taxi measurements were not supplied and that the physical/pilot gates were open when that report was written ([GP-05 limits](../../../../docs/research/ground-physics/GP-05/README.md#L98)). The current wheel-force path explicitly omits wheel gyroscopic coupling ([runtime source](../../../../app/physics/ground_wheels.gd#L166)); Avanti wheel-only inertias, spin relaxation, and main-brake torque remain labeled estimates in its current profile ([wheel profile](../../../../app/data/ground/wheel_profiles.json#L88)). The shared brake command and the failing crosswind start do not change the broader limit: this audit cannot substantiate realistic touchdown, taxi, steering, braking calibration, or turbine transient response.
