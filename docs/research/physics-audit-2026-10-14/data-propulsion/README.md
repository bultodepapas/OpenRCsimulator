# Data and propulsion physics audit — 2026-10-14

This audit checks current aircraft data and propulsion behavior for the SebArt Avanti S A200 / JetCat P100-RX (`sebart-avanti-s-a200-p100rx`) and the Jensen Das Ugly Stik 60 (`jensen-das-ugly-stik-60`). It distinguishes measured or manufacturer values, model-derived values, and open approximations. It is a dated research record, not a claim of flight validation.

The codebase graph was searched first for the aircraft-data loader, turbine, propulsion and dynamics paths; current source was then read directly because stored graph line ranges may lag. The read-only executable audit is [audit.gd](../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/data-propulsion/audit.gd), and its exact successful output is [godot-audit.log](../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/data-propulsion/godot-audit.log). It reports input hashes `74043c9b…` (Avanti) and `5fe8ece9…` (Stik), using Godot 4.7.2.

## Findings

**Confirmed turbine force discontinuity at zero axial flow.** [`Turbine.loads`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L145) models captured-air momentum as `-ṁ·v_air`, but [`turbine.gd:152`](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L152) switches the entire vector force off whenever the axial component `u` is not positive. With the full-flow map, `ṁ` is 0.23 kg/s. The shared independent lead probe found that `[ε, 20, 0]` m/s produces about 4.6 N of lateral ram force, while `[0, 20, 0]` produces zero; the gross axial thrust is unchanged. That is a roughly 5% static-thrust-sized lateral step at the branch. The Avanti has two side intakes, so its real crossflow capture law needs measurement; regardless, the all-or-nothing vector cutoff is a numerical discontinuity and the prior AV-14 audit's request for a multi-axis test did not test across this boundary ([AV-14](../../../../docs/research/avanti-realism/AV-14/propulsion-audit.md#L11), [turbine tests](../../../../app/tests/test_turbine.gd#L110)).

**Fuel burn, mass, CG and inertia are fixed by an explicit model policy.** Avanti data declares a 1.28 kg half-tank state and labels it “no burn modelled” ([aircraft data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L1202)); Stik likewise declares a 0.12 kg half-tank state ([Stik data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L252)). Current session output records turbine `fuel_flow_kg_s` and `fuel_mass_policy="prescribed_fuel"` ([flight session](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L2260)), but does not subtract that flow from inventory or recompute mass properties. At the P100 maximum-flow endpoint, `1.28 / 0.0052 = 246.15 s` (4.10 min) is the **nominal full-flow fuel budget inferred from the authored initial fuel and map endpoint**, not simulated endurance: the current model allows fuel flow telemetry to continue without depleting its fuel inventory. That fuel is 10.94% of the current 11.705 kg Avanti model mass. Thus long full-throttle runs cannot reproduce fuel-dependent acceleration, weight, CG or inertia changes; this is documented scope, not a hidden arithmetic error.

The declared fuel positions show why the omission matters, subject to their estimated tank location. Recomputing the inventory first moment at the same assumed tank point gives Avanti CG `x=0.2400 m` at its declared half-tank state and `x=0.2633 m` empty, a 23.3 mm aft shift (6.6% of its 0.3511 m reference chord). A hypothetical full tank at twice the same half-tank mass gives `x=0.2213 m`; full-to-empty span is 42.1 mm. The analogous Stik half-to-empty shift is 9.6 mm (3.2% of the 0.3048 m chord). These are reproducible model sensitivities, **not measured aircraft CG changes**: the Avanti tank centroid is explicitly estimated from owner reports ([Avanti data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L1210)); the Stik fuel quantity and position are estimates ([Stik data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L252)).

**The baseline Stik has thrust but no propwash control path.** Its current data has no `propulsion.slipstream` component; [`Dynamics._components_from_air`](../../../../app/physics/dynamics.gd#L96) only adds tail wash when that optional data exists. The independent lead probe found 41.25 N static thrust and no RPM-dependent elevator/rudder authority at 0, 5 or 15 m/s. This materially omits the accelerated propeller wake over the tail at low speed and high throttle. The data itself describes D5 as prescribed RPM with first-order throttle lag and says in-flight prop unloading awaits a shaft model ([Stik data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1366)); treat both the absent wake and load-coupled RPM response as known scope limits.

**Local flow at the Avanti intake omits body rotation.** The intake centroid is offset 0.33 m forward of the CG datum ([Avanti data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L2432)). `Dynamics._components_from_air` passes the CG air velocity to the turbine while only the glow-prop path gets hub-local velocity ([dynamics.gd](../../../../app/physics/dynamics.gd#L81)); turbine loads apply the resulting capture force at the intake offset ([turbine.gd](../../../../app/physics/turbine.gd#L152)). At maximum flow, a 1 rad/s pitch rate changes local intake velocity by about 0.33 m/s from this offset alone, corresponding to approximately 0.076 N of capture-momentum force and 0.025 N·m about the CG under the current uniform-flow assumption. This is a small, unvalidated local-flow correction, not evidence of a large thrust error; crossflow around the twin intakes needs data before refining it.

## Mass, CG, inertia and geometry provenance

The area/span/chord quantities are dimensionally coherent. Avanti has `S=0.702275 m²`, `b=2.000 m`, and `S/b=0.3511375 m`, matching its stored `0.351137 m` mean chord within rounding ([Avanti data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L10)). Its area is derived from the AV-02 estimated visual blockout, while span and the 240 mm beginner/P100 CG point come from the SebArt manual; the manual gives no fuel-state qualifier for CG ([Avanti data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L62)). The inventory places that model at half tank and adds virtual balance mass to match the target ([Avanti data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L1233)).

Stik's `720 in²` is `0.464515 m²`, its `60 in` span is `1.524 m`, and `S/b=0.3047999 m`, matching its stored 12 in chord `0.3048 m`; those values agree with the Jensen plan's title-block dimensions ([Stik data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L10)). The plan CG mark is digitized as 4.76 in aft of the leading edge with an explicit reading tolerance ([Stik data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L42)). Neither aircraft has an as-built mass-property measurement in these inputs. The loader sums declared inventory masses and first moments and calculates box/parallel-axis inertia ([aircraft_data.gd](../../../../app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L301)); matching a plan CG therefore does not validate the component positions or inertia tensor.

The Avanti diagnostic loads 11.705 kg total, including 0.3451 kg (2.95%) of virtual reference-build balance mass; the Stik loads 2.885 kg, including 0.2841 kg (9.85%) virtual balance mass. The Stik loader warns that `Jyy=0.3871 kg·m²` is 2.11× a scaled UltraStick donor value. This warning and the effect of virtual ballast on inertia are already documented in [AS-00](../../../../docs/research/aircraft-standardization/AS-00/ugly-stik-reference.md#L7); they are validation limits, not newly discovered source errors. The Avanti inventory also labels its 1.08 kg engine “incl. valves” while its 0.30 kg estimated accessories bundle says “ECU, fuel pump, valves, UAT/hopper” ([Avanti data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L662)). The source does not say whether valve mass is included twice; check the as-built parts list before treating the total and derived inertia as measured.

## Propeller and turbine dimensional checks

The 2017 JetCat catalog is the appropriate variant source for the modeled non-BL P100-RX: 44,000/154,000 RPM, 2/100 N, 390 ml/min maximum fuel and 0.23 kg/s airflow at catalog conditions ([JetCat catalog, p. 26](https://www.jetcat.de/jetcat/Kataloge/JetCat%20Lieferprogramm%202017_web.pdf)). At the model's stated kerosene density of 0.8 kg/L, 390 ml/min converts to `0.0052 kg/s`, matching the current full-RPM fuel-flow endpoint. The diagnostic gives 0.23 kg/s and 92 N at static max RPM because the code applies the estimated 0.92 installed factor to the bare 100 N endpoint; it gives 81.99 N at 70 m/s under the derived ram-flow/jet model ([AV-05 assumptions](../../../../docs/research/avanti-s-av05-turbine-dynamics.md#L63)). The 0.92 factor is explicitly an estimate. Do **not** call the difference between this installed result and the AV-05 bare-engine 90.6 N at 70 m/s a demonstrated defect: there is no P100-RX/Avanti installed thrust-vs-speed measurement, and the two numbers use different installation semantics. Likewise, applying the factor before the ram correction is the model's current chosen convention, not independently validated installation physics.

At the static endpoint, current modeled fuel use divided by modeled installed thrust gives `0.0052×3600/92=0.2035 kg/(N·h)`, compared with the manufacturer's 0.187 kg/(N·h) bare-engine endpoint; the ratio follows directly from reducing thrust to 92% while retaining the catalog fuel-flow value. This is internally dimensionally consistent, but the installed SFC remains an estimate. The atmosphere path supplies field density uniformly rather than sampling air density with aircraft altitude; AV-14 documents this low-altitude simplification, so the diagnostic's `75.10 N` at 1.0 kg/m³ and `67.59 N` at 0.9 kg/m³ are sensitivity results, not altitude validation ([AV-14](../../../../docs/research/avanti-realism/AV-14/propulsion-audit.md#L21)).

Avanti rotor inertia is an estimate (`3.5e-5 kg·m²`) and its assumed spin sense is explicitly unverified. The diagnostic's `0.564 N·m·s` rotor momentum is dimensionally `I·ω` at 154,000 RPM, but neither rotor inertia nor gyro sign is confirmed by the P100-RX data ([Avanti data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/sebart_avanti_s_a200.json#L2409)). AV-14 correctly treats transient turbine mounting torque and exhaust angular-momentum flux as open questions; a solid-shaft `-Hdot` term cannot be inferred without closing the open-engine flow balance ([AV-14](../../../../docs/research/avanti-realism/AV-14/propulsion-audit.md#L15)).

The Stik propulsion output uses `n=RPM/60` to obtain shaft power `Q·2πn`: the diagnostic reports 0.8341 N·m and 973.8 W at the derived 11,149 RPM static point. The engine's source value, 1,397.4 W at 16,000 RPM, is a conversion of the O.S. catalog's 1.9 PS rating, and the current static RPM is itself an estimated power balance ([Stik data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1368), [official O.S. MAX-61FX specification](https://www.os-engines.co.jp/english/line_up/engine/air/aircraft/catalog/17750.htm)). At that fixed RPM the modeled prop load rises to 982.6 W at 5 m/s and 1,000.3 W at 15 m/s. The existing D5 model does not solve engine/prop shaft equilibrium in flight, as its data description says; these are propeller demand values, not a claim that the glow engine actually holds 11,149 RPM while absorbing excess power.

The 12×6 Sport propeller's 46 g inventory mass agrees with APC's 1.62 oz product listing ([APC LP12060](https://www.apcprop.com/product/12x6/?v=84de8e2b14bb)). Its `0.00035 kg·m²` rotating inertia is an estimate from a 37 g blade rod plus estimated hub hardware; the rod calculation `mD²/12` gives `0.000286 kg·m²`, dimensionally consistent with the rounded total, but actual mass distribution is unmeasured. Stik thrust/power coefficient tables are borrowed from UIUC APC Sport 11×6 measurements and applied to the 12×6 by similarity scaling; their source notes interpolate from static to `J=0.373` without measurements in that interval ([Stik data](../../../../app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1460)). UIUC's directly cataloged 12×6 Thin Electric data is a different propeller, so it is not a validation substitute ([UIUC propeller database](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/propDB.html)).

## Provenance and stale-report checks

- The Avanti manual identifies 2.00 m span, 2.22 m length, about 10.5 kg dry RTF with P100, and the P100 beginner CG point ([SebArt manual](https://www.sebart.it/download/AVANTI%20S%20JET%202.2m-Manual%20Intro.pdf)). Current inventory minus declared fuel is 10.425 kg, within 0.075 kg of that coarse dry-mass reference, but contains a virtual balancing mass and estimated component locations; the agreement does not validate its inertia or internal mass distribution.
- The baseline identity is the original Jensen/Phil Kraft 60 in plan, Outerzone oz1253, not every later model marketed as “Ugly Stik” ([Jensen plan scan](https://outerzone.co.uk/plan_details.asp?ID=1253)).
- AV-14's line saying `Turbine.fuel_flow` has no production caller is stale: current `FlightSession` records it in the endpoint telemetry ([AV-14](../../../../docs/research/avanti-realism/AV-14/propulsion-audit.md#L30), [current consumer](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L2273)). Its larger conclusion that fuel burn, mass/CG/inertia change, and engine lifecycle are not implemented remains correct.
- NASA's net-thrust relation separates exhaust momentum from inlet ram drag ([NASA Glenn](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/thrust-force/)); it supports checking gross and net thrust dimensions, but does not validate the repository's fitted ram-flow or high-crossflow intake coefficients.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, run:

```powershell
& .tools\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --path app --audio-driver Dummy --script "$PWD\research\physics-audit-2026-10-14\data-propulsion\audit.gd" 2>&1 | Tee-Object -FilePath research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/data-propulsion/godot-audit.log
```

The command completed with exit code 0. It loads current production aircraft data and calls existing read-only turbine/propulsion helpers; it does not edit application sources, change aircraft data, run flight validation, or establish physical accuracy. Key exact output includes Avanti `mass=11.705089 kg`, half-fuel `1.280000 kg`, max-flow `0.005200 kg/s`, static/70 m/s thrust `92.000/81.994 N`; Stik `mass=2.885058 kg`, half-fuel `0.120000 kg`, and current 0/5/15 m/s prop outputs `41.252/38.366/32.594 N` at prescribed 11,149 RPM.
