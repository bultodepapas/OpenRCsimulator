# R02 powered-wing immersion candidate

This is an offline geometry prototype for integrating a straight propeller wake over a planar wing panel. It is a candidate method for later review, not a runtime change, load model, or physical validation. The kernel and reproducible outputs are in [`research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/r02-powered-wing-candidate/`](../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/r02-powered-wing-candidate/).

The method slices the panel by the exact quadratic cylinder cross-section. In a plane basis whose second axis is the wake axis projected into the panel, each fixed-y section has either a finite x interval or the exact parallel-axis band limit. Polygon-edge crossings and ellipse tangencies split the y integration into smooth intervals. The existing E0b2 axis-aligned polygon clip handles finite wake start/end planes; Gauss-Legendre nodes come from `scipy.special.roots_legendre`, with NumPy/SciPy versions pinned for this candidate. A global intersection-area prepass prevents tiny local slices or nearly zero signed moments from setting a vanishing adaptive error scale. The smooth occupancy option splits each x slice at the inner-radius boundary before integrating the existing squared-radius C1 weight.

The result includes raw area, first position moment and full symmetric second position moment, plus occupancy-weighted equivalents and diagnostic occupancy-weighted velocity moments. Occupancy is a load-mixture weight: each in-tube point carries the full signed Δv, while occupancy separately weights the load increment. Sample `air_velocity_body_mps` is that full-increment point velocity with occupancy reported as its own scalar; it does not represent a radially reduced local Δv. The integrated velocity moments likewise are weighted geometric summaries, not force integrals or a radial speed profile. Δv is independent of the tube's geometric path: a downstream path may point aft while the modeled air-velocity increment points forward. The optional squared-radius C1 occupancy follows [`Slipstream.profile_moments()`](../../../../../app/physics/slipstream.gd#L347), but does not claim a measured radial velocity profile. These are geometric/statistical ingredients; the prototype does not convert them to aerodynamic forces or sum a wing load.

## P-51 source geometry

`p51_physics_panels()` reads the active P-51 aircraft JSON and constructs six candidate flat trapezoid polygons from the authored span and chord endpoints. Their strip boundaries use the same equal-area split equation as [`AircraftData.station_edges()`](../../../../../app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L608), and their plane height comes from the authored aerodynamic reference point. A straight leading edge at LE-frame `x=0` is an explicit candidate construction. The runtime does not define or consume these vertices, so the helper's historical name/docstring must not be read as an exact runtime surface definition. The polygons do not import estimated render dihedral, twist, washout, sweep, or movable-control geometry.

Current [`Aero._wing_flow()`](../../../../../app/physics/aero.gd#L267) and [`Aero._local_loads()`](../../../../../app/physics/aero.gd#L306) evaluate strip flow and force arms at the ARP x/z coordinates and `ARP.y + station_ys`. They do not place wing forces at the candidate polygons' area-centroid x coordinates. The [`Dynamics` slipstream call](../../../../../app/physics/dynamics.gd#L98) adds `Slipstream.loads()` separately rather than feeding wake flow into those wing samples.

The separate [mapping probe](../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/r02-powered-wing-candidate/p51_runtime_sampling_mapping.py) and [receipt](../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/r02-powered-wing-candidate/p51-runtime-sampling-mapping.json) query the six runtime points against the same candidate static hard tube; this is a geometry comparison, not an existing runtime wing-wash calculation. All six ARP-based points lie outside that tube; the nearest is 0.252732 m from the axis against a 0.233484 m radius. The candidate polygons still overlap on strips 2 and 3, totaling 0.167142463 m², while all six polygon centroids are outside.

The inputs are mixed-provenance aircraft data: wing area 1.372494 m² and chord endpoints 0.6664/0.307 m are derived; span 2.82 m, plan CG, 0.6604 m propeller diameter, and 1.75° down/1° right thrust angles are estimated. The hub location is derived from the thrust line. These entries and their provenance are in [`p51d_mustang_120.json`](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L12) (area), [span](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L18), [root chord](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L30), [tip chord](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L36), [aero reference plane](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L42), [CG](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L63), [propeller diameter](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L1837), [thrust angles](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L2050), and [hub](../../../../../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json#L2250). The static radius uses the current sharp-boundary runtime constant `Slipstream.RADIUS_MIN = 0.7071`, exactly as authored in [`slipstream.gd`](../../../../../app/physics/slipstream.gd#L27); it is 0.23348442 m for this propeller. The tube begins at the hub (`s ≥ 0`) and follows the negative prop-axis vector downstream. The P-51 file does not enable the optional smooth `edge_fraction` profile.

## Evidence

On Python 3.11.9 / NumPy 2.4.6 / SciPy 1.17.1, `test_geometry_quadrature.py` passes 9/9 checks. The fixture suite compares disk and oblique-ellipse areas and moments to closed forms; approaches the exact parallel band through μ = 0.1, 0.01, 0.001, 1e−5, 1e−8, 0; checks tangent circular caps down to 1e−7 m using a cancellation-safe series reference; checks radii down to 1e−5 m; and covers finite axial clipping, reverse signed Δv, crossflow, rotation, and the C1 occupancy area and second moment. The generated [`candidate-results.json`](../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/r02-powered-wing-candidate/candidate-results.json) includes the source hashes, errors, and quadrature evaluation counts.

For the near-parallel rectangle (`R=0.2 m`, x/y bounds ±1 m), the exact band area is 0.8 m². At μ = 1e−8 the candidate returns 0.7999999999999992 m², 8.9e−16 m² from the exact band; the exact μ = 0 case returns 0.8000000000000005 m². Relative error against the closed-form ellipse/band area is at most 3.8e−15 across the sequence. The 1e−7 m circle cap has area 5.9628478453e−11 m² and differs from the stable series reference by 8.9e−10 relative, using 640 x and 80 y quadrature evaluations.

For the P-51 static tube, the six authored-source strip areas sum to 1.372494 m². Only the two innermost strips intersect the hard tube: strip 2 covers 0.0931602329481 m² (40.73% of its strip), strip 3 covers 0.0739822296078 m² (32.34%); the other four are empty. Total intersection is 0.167142462556 m² (12.18% of wing area). All six strip centroids lie outside the tube, including both strips with nonzero intersection. This demonstrates why a strip-centroid inclusion test cannot stand in for area integration for this geometry.

The P-51 values were independently checked with a body-y adaptive `scipy.integrate.quad` oracle: at each y it solves the tube's quadratic x interval and intersects that interval with the trapezoid chord. Candidate-minus-oracle area is 1.18e−13 m² for strip 2 and 5.59e−15 m² for strip 3; QUADPACK reports absolute errors of 3.3e−14 and 9.6e−13 m², respectively. These are numerical cross-checks for this static geometric fixture. They are not proof bounds for every orientation or a validation of the authored geometry or wake physics.

Root review also uses an [independent raw-source Sobol probe](../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/r02-powered-wing-candidate/root_review_p51_sobol.py), without importing the candidate kernel or converting to its FRD frame. Its [retained result](../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/r02-powered-wing-candidate/root-review-p51-sobol-01.json) samples 1,048,576 points per strip in LE coordinates, with the chord Jacobian. It finds overlap only in strips 2 and 3, totals 0.16714522 m², and differs from quadrature by at most 1.84e−6 m² per strip. This deterministic sampling cross-check has no claimed statistical or rigorous error bound; its pre/post source hashes match.

The separate [`coordinate-covariance-results.json`](../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/r02-powered-wing-candidate/coordinate-covariance-results.json) checks a tilted synthetic panel with a finite axial window, nonzero ambient flow, signed Δv, and body rotation under a proper rotation and a reflection. Raw and occupancy-weighted position moments and occupancy-weighted velocity moments transform covariantly for both hard and smooth occupancy: the largest normalized L2 residual is 9.03e−16. Pointwise samples avoid exact hard radial and finite-window discontinuities by 1e−7 m; their maximum occupancy and air-velocity errors are 3.78e−15 and 3.56e−15 m/s. The reflection transforms angular velocity as an axial vector, `ω′ = det(Q) Qω`; the intentionally incorrect polar-vector control differs by 1.15–1.22 m/s. This is a coordinate-invariance check on the implementation, not physical validation.

## Limits before any runtime integration

This prototype does not establish where a real wake boundary or radial profile lies. The P-51 overlap is a model consequence of estimated installation geometry and the current actuator-disc radius constant. It does not include free-stream transport or drift, wake contraction downstream beyond the selected radius, swirl, fuselage shielding, wing/tail interaction, propeller blade flow, ground effect, aircraft flexibility, control deflection, or measured surface geometry. It does not solve induced circulation or decide how wing wash couples to the existing tail increment; the six current aero strips are too coarse to justify collapsing local wash into one centroid value. The separately reviewed circulation candidate integrates force along bound-vortex span samples; this area kernel can supply local samples later, but area quadrature and vortex-line quadrature remain distinct.

Any integration must first choose the physical coupling law and its ownership of free-stream versus powered-wing loads, prevent double-counting with existing wing aerodynamics, and validate the geometry and wake against a documented aircraft or measurement. No calibration or runtime change is proposed here.
