# M5-ATM-4 / VAL-AUD-R09 integration

2026-10-14. The [kernel report](README.md) owns constants, source references and independent values. This document describes the production consumers and timing contract.

The [five-family domain-boundary sweep](boundary-sweep-20261014/README.md) verifies both altitude limits, rejection of outward RK stages before commit, unchanged accepted state after rejected starts, and successful interior control steps. Its fixture ground context and failed setup attempts are recorded explicitly.

`FlightSession.environment_at(body)` evaluates geometric altitude as `field_elevation_m - body.down`. Aero, propeller load, wash, gear drag, coupled shaft, electric propeller, turbine capture and Glow cooling/charge use the current RK candidate body. Repeated calls are pure and consume no wind RNG. Components share one body-datum atmosphere at a stage: a 2 m vertical separation corresponds to roughly 0.02% sea-level density change under this lapse rate, which is intentionally omitted. Spatial weather and measured engine corrections are separate work.

Trim evaluates the requested starting altitude rather than the previously accepted flight endpoint. Changing start altitude triggers a refit at that altitude; reset preserves its chosen position. The runway solver queries the environment at each residual pose. A rejected/out-of-domain sample supplies failure or nonfinite sentinel data to the existing bounded transaction, never a silent sea-level fallback. The qualified runway idle policies are prescribed-RPM, turbine idle, or stopped electric/Glow; this does not qualify an arbitrary load-responsive ground equilibrium.

Weather `openrc-weather v4` admits the explicit `standard_column` mode. Column checkpoints retain the atmosphere law and weather/datum identity and validate both current and previous positions before restore. Preferences use outer schema 5 for weather v4; legacy weather/lighting combinations retain their earlier schemas. The EN/ES conditions editor exposes the column and its bounds; temperature/QNH/humidity are disabled inert drafts in this mode. Helicopters explicitly reject the column pending their rotor/environment integration. Ground vehicles acquire no invented atmospheric force.

Column CSV is `openrc-trace v11`. The metadata atmosphere object is a field-datum preview, not the density for every row. Endpoint `rho_kgm3`, pressure, temperature and EAS correspond to the accepted body. Added `loads_rho_kgm3`, `loads_pressure_pa` and `loads_temperature_k` correspond to the k1 body at `loads_t_s`; `geometric_altitude_m` belongs to the row endpoint. An invalid environment prevents the CSV from being accepted as complete. Legacy uniform/reference trace versions remain unchanged, including electric/gear/Glow extensions.

[`test_atmosphere_column_session.gd`](../../../../../app/tests/test_atmosphere_column_session.gd) passes **96 checks** in the [stable integrated native run](../integrated-native-01/atmosphere_column_session.log). It compares five actual family callbacks—Stik prescribed RPM, Jet turbine, P-51 coupled shaft, Timber electric and finite-Glow—at 1,000 m against separately configured uniform pressure/temperature at the same candidate state, while the accepted owner state remains at a different altitude. It verifies forces, auxiliary derivatives, rotor/body terms, six-axis re-trim, altitude-changing endpoint/k1 telemetry, checkpoint continuation, failed-restore atomicity, observer independence, runway stationarity, preferences and the rotorcraft capability guard. Kernel checks are separate: **83 checks**. The UI-specific test adds **12 checks**.

These tests verify implementation and state timing. The dry ISA model's mathematical profile is a controlled reference, not the weather measured at a field or physical validation of an aircraft's high-altitude performance.

The offline reader also checks **calm** v11 standard-column exports with `check_atmosphere_trace.py --column`. Five real CLI exports cover prescribed RPM, turbine/gear, coupled shaft, electric and finite Glow. Four Python tests pass, including corrupted metadata/rows, the direct CLI dispatch and refusal to replace retained evidence. The independent reader recomputes the dry column and endpoint/k1 timing; it deliberately rejects wind/gust/turbulence rather than claiming to reconstruct those states. See [commands and scope](../../../../TOOLS.md), [retained exports](cli/) and [`test_atmosphere_column_cli.py`](../../../../../app/tests/test_atmosphere_column_cli.py).
