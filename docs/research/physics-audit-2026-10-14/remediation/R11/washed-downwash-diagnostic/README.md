# R02 integration: wing wake and powered tail

2026-10-14. **Open model issue; P51-22's proposed denominator patch is rejected.** The P-51 ground/handling regressions are traced to R02 in the [P51-22 bisection](../../../../p51-realism/P51-22/README.md). They must not be dismissed as unrelated catalog failures. Conversely, restoring a passing loop or takeoff test does not establish a physically admissible replacement law.

## Independent counterexample

The proposed `atan2(gain * U, V_free)` gives a nonunique loaded-tail limit. With `V_free = ε` and the current wing source `U = ε CL(α)`, the angle tends to `atan(gain * CL(α))`, which depends on the direction of approach. At exactly zero flow the source is zero and the code returns zero downwash. Propwash keeps the tail loaded, so the disagreement does not vanish with dynamic pressure.

The root [read-only probe](../../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/washed_downwash_limit_probe.gd) independently composes that candidate angle with a fixed 20 m/s tail wash and the valid loaded P-51 model. It evaluates 21 states over 1 to 1e-8 m/s and α = −5°, 0°, +5°. At 1e-8 m/s, candidate full-tail force differences from the exact-zero state are 5.423, 2.417 and 10.257 N; pitch differences are −6.572, +2.939 and +12.501 N·m. The current closure's force differences are below 8e-9 N. [Full-precision output](zero-flow-limit/loads.json), [command/source identity](zero-flow-limit/identity.json), [reproduction runner](../../../../../../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/run_washed_downwash_probe.py). The fixed wash is a surface-law counterexample, not a prediction of a real stationary aircraft.

A [separate implementation review and actual P-51 propeller/piece calculation](independent-review/README.md) confirms the defect with the exact proposed patch in a disposable physical copy. At the catalog RPM, the largest actual-slipstream discontinuity among the three approach directions is **1.516 N and 1.818 N·m**. The patch also breaks production/public-helper composition by up to 2.464 N·m. These smaller values concern only the authored immersed pieces and the model's computed wash; they must not be confused with the full-tail fixed-wash probe.

The **existing, unchanged** `test_aero_continuity.gd` independently rejects the patch on its manufactured Stik propwash case. [Baseline](existing-continuity-baseline/test_aero_continuity.gd.log) passes; [exact candidate](existing-continuity-candidate/test_aero_continuity.gd.log) fails the positive-axial approach with normalized error 0.003243069 against its unchanged 1e-7 bound. Both [baseline identity](existing-continuity-baseline/verification.json) and [candidate identity](existing-continuity-candidate/verification.json) record no source drift. No tolerance, frozen oracle, production law or golden flight was changed to obtain this result.

The independent review checks [NACA Report 690](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930091767). Its measured configurations support investigating wing/propeller coupling; they do not establish the proposed universal lower bound of free-stream downwash for every propeller jet. The report therefore does not justify this denominator substitution.

## Required integrated replacement

The current scalar wing wake has no powered-wing interaction. A replacement must address that missing source together with its tail consumers, not infer a new angle from a vanishing denominator. The next R02/R06 slice requires:

1. Define the physical flow field and ownership: ambient/body-rate flow, propeller induced flow and wing-induced flow, including their application points, direction and reference frame. A wing contribution must be generated from the same local flow and circulation proxy used for its forces; a fitted tail-only gain must remain an explicit estimated/calibrated parameter, not a hidden aircraft-specific correction.
2. Resolve wing immersion with geometry and quadrature that can represent a narrow propeller jet crossing the wing. Six current equal-area wing samples can miss a narrow central jet. Specify convergence and prevent double counting between the free-wing load and any washed-wing increment. Use existing authored geometry/provenance; new measurements remain separate evidence.
3. Derive the resulting dimensional downwash source and its convection to each tail piece. Define steady powered rest, stopped propeller, decaying stored wake, reversal, crossflow and ground-effect composition. The [known R06 stopped-wake discontinuities](../../R06/stopped-wake-idle-review.md) require direction/radius/occupancy semantics, not only a sign fade.
4. Use one declared source/consumer contract through local and attached loads, free/washed tail increments, distributed swirl, lag state, trim and RK stages. Public helpers and independent compositions must agree in their stated domains. Any added state gets an explicit layout/law version; old checkpoints are rejected rather than reinterpreted.
5. Qualify force/moment continuity from independent Cartesian approaches at zero and reversal, finite-difference convergence away from branch boundaries, zero-prop recovery, force/moment/power accounting and wing/tail quadrature convergence. A stopped source with nonzero stored wake is a separate required case. Keep the existing continuity test and this rejected candidate as negative controls.
6. Preserve the recorded Stik/Jet baselines and compare complete same-input candidates with their own exact replay. Add P-51 takeoff/loop traces, source/data identities, trim/control slopes and lag response. Qualify all admitted propulsion families and explicit unsupported combinations; test success and regression targets remain software evidence until independent measurements support physical validation.

The present R02 closure remains versioned and operational, but the P-51 power-coupling regressions remain an open integration gate. Neither this rejection nor the existing continuity pass establishes its physical accuracy. The P51-19 power-off stall-recovery envelope discrepancy is a separate calibration/acceptance question.

## Implemented research prerequisites, 2026-10-15

The [geometry candidate](../../R02-powered-wing-candidate/README.md) implements step 2's planar tube/panel intersection and local quadrature in an offline module. Closed-form disk, ellipse, tangent-cap and parallel-band comparisons exercise its limiting cases. For the explicit candidate flat P-51 surface and static tube, the two central strips intersect by 0.09316023 and 0.07398223 m² although both centroids are outside. The actual runtime's ARP-based sample points also miss that assumed tube. This is a concrete counterexample to center-only immersion, not an identified physical wake radius or runtime-defined surface geometry. The geometry result remains distinct from the force and circulation quadratures.

The [circulation candidate](../../R02-circulation-candidate/README.md) implements an attached, positive-axial dimensional source solve and a discrete bound-line force comparison. It uses the exact production `S/b` chord for the exported map, and a separate authored-geometry lane for D11f. Its failed pointwise self-wake draft is retained: shared-edge singularities prevent using that draft as a converged wake model. The accepted research variant holds induced downwash at panel centers and samples external flow along each bound panel; this defines a limited discrete closure, not the required full-envelope replacement.

Before combining these modules, establish sourced section/force semantics, a consistent wake-field evaluation and a zero/reverse-flow domain law. In particular, do not transplant whole-aircraft `CD0` into section drag as if it were measured, convert area occupancy to one averaged velocity before squaring it, or add the candidate Kutta–Joukowski load on top of the existing wing load. Source ownership, powered rest and stopped stored-wake behavior still need the derivation and independent checks in steps 1, 3–5. Runtime promotion must also pass step 6's complete aircraft/session comparisons and the ordinary failing P-51/yaw gates.
