### Yaw-damping verification and the independent D11f comparison

**Dated interpretation correction (2026-10-16).** An earlier description assigned `+0.0002584` to circulation/downwash. That attribution was incorrect. At α=11°, the zero-camber D11f split has `circulatory_cnr = 2.7441579e−16` (numerically zero) and `profile_cnr = −0.0138064046`, equal to the total to rounding. The `+0.0002584` is the α=0 to α=11° change in the analytic profile term, `−CD0/3 × (1 − 1/(4·3²)) × cos(α)`. The original raw output is unchanged. The [retained AVL 3.52 inputs, raw outputs, matched 6/20/80-panel sensitivity and hash receipt](avl-reference-20261016/README.md) document the correction. These are model-comparison results, not physical qualification.

The current [`test_yaw_damping.gd`](../../../../../../app/tests/test_yaw_damping.gd) has 17 checks: 16 pass and the original whole-range 7% fin-plus-wing-profile comparison remains an ordinary failing assertion. Its worst deviation is 7.75% at α=11°. The α=0 local check, fin-rate/Cnβ checks, attached-domain checks and independent chain-rule checks pass. The chain-rule calculation includes fin and wing profile, induced and lift-projection terms; it matches production central differences over α=0–11° with maximum absolute Cnr errors 6.496e−6 at h=0.05 and 1.506e−6 at h=0.0125, with every fourfold refinement reducing error. The actual ±h strip angles are checked to remain attached, and asymmetric strip-CL0 or induced-map fixtures are rejected. See [`d11g-repair/test_yaw_damping-R02-open-gate-v5.log`](d11g-repair/test_yaw_damping-R02-open-gate-v5.log). This derivative agreement verifies implementation consistency only; it does not clear the 7% gate or validate the force law physically.

The independent wing comparison is recorded in [`d11g-repair/yaw_r02_vs_kj_comparison-v4.json`](d11g-repair/yaw_r02_vs_kj_comparison-v4.json), generated by [`d11g-repair/yaw_r02_vs_kj_comparison.py`](d11g-repair/yaw_r02_vs_kj_comparison.py) from a read-only production input export. The unchanged [`research/aero/d11f/wing_yaw_damping.py`](../../../../../../research/aero/d11f/wing_yaw_damping.py) solves a rate-dependent generalized Weissinger circulation model and evaluates Kutta–Joukowski loads with trailing-leg downwash. Its zero-camber run at α=11° gives wing CL 0.790394, profile Cnr −0.0138064, a numerically zero circulatory term (+2.74e−16), and total wing Cnr −0.0138064; at α=0° the profile and total are −0.0140648, and at α=4° the profile term is −0.0140306. The α=0 to α=11° profile-term change is +0.0002584 from the analytic cos(α) factor, not circulation/downwash. The current R02 reproduction matches the production GDScript wing Cnr at the seven sampled α values to a maximum absolute difference of 6.9e−11. The comparator uses max section-plane speed in the donor normalization, while `Aero._wing_flow` uses max full 3-D strip speed. The retained [normalization probe](d11g-repair/yaw_fullspeed_normalization-v1.json) computes the current Stik inventory CG and shows that at α=11°, r=±0.05 rad/s, the omitted side velocity is 0.001825 m/s; max full speed exceeds max plane speed by 1.108e−7 m/s, the maximum donor-weight difference is 7.371e−9, and including the side component changes wing Cnr by −2.083e−11. This bounds a small reproducer simplification; it does not explain the +0.0083802 wing-model residual. At α=11° it gives wing CL mean 0.909009 and wing Cnr −0.0054262 (profile −0.0138088, induced drag −0.0020214, chord-axis lift projection +0.0104040), a +0.0083802 residual toward less damping relative to D11f. At α=4° the wing-only residual is +0.0021905. These are wing-only values and must not be confused with the full-aircraft test, whose α=11°, h=.05 derivative is −0.1217572 and whose independent attached-law derivative is −0.1217507.

The comparison matches AR≈5, six equal-area strips, section slope 6.08175/rad, CD0=0.0434, speed 15 m/s, density 1.225 kg/m³, neutral controls, and central difference h=.05 rad/s. The static operator recovered from production E agrees with D11f's kernel to a maximum absolute error of 1.45e−7. D11f starts with zero strip CL0; its fixed-incidence matched variant reproduces production CL0 at α=0, then gives Cnr −0.0134806 at α=11°—only +0.0003258 relative to original D11f, so the CL0 match alone does not close the residual.

For the attached linear Weissinger model with every section axial speed u_i>0, the dimensional source g_i=u_i·CL_i is an exact algebraic rewrite, not a small-angle approximation: multiplying M(r)CL=W/u+α₀ by diag(u) gives M₀g=W·1+u·α₀ and therefore g=a₀E(W·1+u·α₀). The comparison's rate-dependent solve and dimensional-g solve agree to at most 3.33e−16 in CL. This identity does not extend the model through zero or reverse axial flow, stall, or the current R02 law. Replacing exact W/u by the geometric-angle RHS changes α=11° Cnr by +0.0001805; the angle-RHS variant is +0.0001805 less damped.

D11f uses the exact positive-axial W/u_i incidence source and a rate-dependent u_j/u_i induced operator. It forms circulation as Γ_i=0.5u_i c CL_i and uses Kutta–Joukowski force with trailing-leg downwash. The D11f source stores loads per unit density; in the dimensional matched comparison its profile term is 0.5ρ(u_i²+W²)cΔy CD0 along the D11f x axis, without a freestream-direction projection. R02 instead uses atan2 section angles with a fixed E map, weights off-diagonal angle donors by normalized section speed, and applies the whole-aircraft k=0.0814934, CL_minD=0.23 drag parabola per strip. Its axial profile-force term scales with Vp_i·u_i, while its chord-axis lift projection scales with Vp_i CL_i W; D11f's circulation uses u_i and its Kutta–Joukowski axial force uses W−w_down. The closures therefore remain different even when their strip CL inputs match. With the same matched dynamic CL at α=11°, R02 force closure gives Cnr −0.0098863 and D11f Kutta–Joukowski closure gives −0.0134806, a −0.0035943 force-closure difference. This is a sensitivity result, not a declaration that either closure is correct.

Selected α=11° variants show where the comparison is sensitive without identifying a validated correction:

| Diagnostic variant | Wing Cnr |
| --- | ---: |
| Current fixed-E R02 law with donor-speed weighting | −0.0054262 |
| Same R02 law with off-diagonal donor weighting disabled | −0.0067018 |
| Fixed-E dimensional source `g=u·CL`, exact `W+u·α₀` RHS, R02 force closure | −0.0098863 |
| Rate-dependent `uⱼ/uᵢ` operator with geometric-angle RHS, R02 force closure | −0.0097058 |
| D11f Kutta–Joukowski force closure using current fixed-E R02 CL | −0.0119062 |
| D11f Kutta–Joukowski closure with CL0-matched incidence | −0.0134806 |

These rows change distinct parts of the model and are not interchangeable predictions. At this point the donor weights range 0.995853–1.0; removing them changes Cnr by −0.0012756 but leaves a material residual. A current-law x-only profile-drag variant is −0.0054238, only 2.4e−6 from the current result, so profile-drag axis projection alone does not explain the discrepancy. The D11f force closure on the same current CL changes the result materially, while the exact-`W/u` and rate-dependent-operator variants also change the local circulation law. The remaining issue is open: a matched comparison must resolve strip camber, geometric-angle versus `W/u` incidence, the induced operator, local versus whole-aircraft polar use, and force/downwash accounting before any physical conclusion or law change.

## P-51 stripped-gradient case

`test_downwash_lag.gd` originally removed only `aero.surfaces.horizontal.downwash_gradient` for each aircraft, then expected the modified candidate to validate and apply. The P-51 candidate still contained its authored `aero.ground_effect`. The current loader requires the derived horizontal `free_slope` for that ground-effect law; `free_slope` is derived only when `downwash_gradient` is present. The candidate was therefore invalid before lag allocation was tested.

The read-only probe used the same catalog raw files and operations as the test. GP Extra and Avanti validate and apply after their gradient is removed and end with `downwash_index() == -1`. P-51 returns the exact loader error:

```text
aero.ground_effect: requires aero.surfaces.horizontal.downwash_gradient (the free tail law whose downwash it reduces)
```

Its `_apply()` returns false and the existing active P-51 session remains on its original lag index (13). The failed assertion is therefore a fixture/schema incompatibility, not evidence that a valid no-gradient P-51 allocates a lag entry. Keep the loader contract. If P-51 must cover the no-lag branch, use a clearly synthetic candidate that also removes the dependent ground-effect law, or make the P-51 branch explicitly assert this combination is rejected; the existing GP Extra and Avanti cases already cover valid no-gradient models.

The current fixture now first asserts this invalid combination is rejected without changing the active lag allocation, then also removes the dependent optional ground-effect law from the in-memory candidate before asserting a valid no-gradient/no-lag apply. The updated test passes 22/22, including the 2,000/2,000 settled-lag identity comparisons, plunge-theory check, 240/480 Hz refinement, and checkpoint replay. The diagnostic source and raw output remain [`gradient_stripping_probe.gd`](gradient_stripping_probe.gd) and [`gradient-stripping-probe-windows.log`](gradient-stripping-probe-windows.log).
