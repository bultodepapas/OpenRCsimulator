## Accepted events and telemetry

- The quad publishes crash/race events before `tick_advanced`. The blimp observes accepted contacts and can announce a crash before its tick notification. Ground announces the accepted tick before flushing its events, and suppresses notifications during replay reconstruction. Associate each event with its accepted tick and generation, deliver it once and reseed the presentation after restore. A fault, replay or reset during a pending presentation update makes a useful counterexample. [Quad session](../../../app/sim/multirotor/session.gd), [blimp session](../../../app/sim/airship/session.gd), [ground session](../../../app/sim/ground_vehicle/session.gd).
- Fixed-wing HUD reads `observer_air_and_wind`. Its AGL uses the composed dry terrain when installed and valid, samples the ground table when the environment is absent, and can be unavailable. The existing fixed-wing native `published_endpoint_air()` provides a narrower wind/air-velocity/speed answer under guards on the published state, auxiliary state and weather. Use it only for matching fields/time and retain the observer path for spatial weather, derived sessions and other refusals. An accepted-tick value and the drawn camera pose have different sample times. [HUD snapshot](../../../app/render/overlays/overlay_manager.gd), [flight session](../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd), [NK-7i scope](../native-kernel/NK-7i/README.md).
- The blimp HUD calls a lazy telemetry reader for its numerical refresh at about 12 Hz and skips it in the menu. Its session caches the detailed force/contact evaluation for a tick. Share that demand with the OSD. Check the call count with both visible, one hidden and both hidden while still observing critical events. [Airship HUD](../../../app/vehicles/airship/airship_hud.gd), [telemetry owner](../../../app/sim/airship/session.gd).

## Native delivery constraints

The current GDExtension has a receipt-checked loader, target-specific export rules and a source identity that changes when its native inputs change. Ordinary macOS exports currently omit the experimental physics payload. A successful Windows physics flight gives no evidence that an airship or quad can load a camera method from a fresh export. Test presentation selection after a cold launch with reference physics as well as with each supported production physics backend. Record the actual presentation implementation and fallback reason. [Loader](../../../app/sim/native_stage.gd), [export plugin](../../../app/addons/native_kernel/export_plugin.gd), [build identity](../../../research/native-kernel/build.py), [native roadmap](../../plans/native-kernel-roadmap.md).

Godot already supplies the active camera, its projection and its world-to-viewport methods. Its interpolation guide describes the effect of a moving parent even when a child's local interpolation is disabled. Use the owner’s final transform once, then compare independent screen-space fixtures against the active camera in the pinned build. Godot's visible rectangle and UI scaling also make resize, letterboxing and native Retina pixels distinct test cases. [Camera3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_camera3d.html), [camera interpolation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/physics/interpolation/advanced_physics_interpolation.html), [multiple resolutions](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/rendering/multiple_resolutions.html).

NK-8a shows a measured gain for one batched presentation operation. NK-7i qualifies a particular endpoint consumer and weather window. Neither measures FPV. Capture the legacy costs and test a coarse native call against the same reference inputs, including conversion/copy cost, before enabling it by default. [NK-8a measurements](../native-kernel/NK-8a/README.md), [NK-7i measurements](../native-kernel/NK-7i/README.md).
