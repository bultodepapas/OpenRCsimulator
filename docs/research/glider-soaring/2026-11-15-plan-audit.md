## 1. Verdict and limits

**Ready to begin bounded implementation under the revised plan.** The audit found concrete control-flow, dependency and evidence-gate gaps and corrected them in the authoritative documents. It found no unresolvable design blocker within the inspected scope. The first implementation packets are absent-propulsion admission and a calm solo activity, with synthetic pair feasibility and physical-data preparation in parallel.

This is a planning-readiness verdict. All SG implementation and qualification work remains pending except SG-00's documented review portion. No simulator run, numerical convergence campaign, real-radio session, rendered evaluation, package/CI execution, physical aircraft/rope measurement or learner study was performed for this audit. Source inspection and analytic consistency do not establish those results.

The release scope is explicit: an estimated engineering prototype, a physically representative configuration, a usable practice exercise and a causal learning-effect claim require different evidence. All retain the applicable software, numerical, lifecycle, observer, device and repository release gates. No tolerance, break strength, real aircraft speed or performance budget was invented to close a finding.

## 2. Audit method

The lead checked source identity and integrated the changes. Three independent read-only reviews examined (1) physical/numerical assumptions, (2) delivery and player-flow counterexamples, and (3) source/ownership/evidence traceability. Reviews used the frozen published documents and selected source files at the runtime baseline. The repository is represented locally by selective snapshots, not a complete Git checkout; remote blob/ref checks establish the document baseline.

Scope included the master and four topic guides, 24 GI cards and their acceptance mapping, current aircraft/kernel/weather/logging contracts, and targeted identity, intended-hull support, session and statistics consumers. Existing external research was retained; this was not a new exhaustive review of every paper, manual, file license or repository consumer. Existing audit findings retain their original owners. Findings below are defects or ambiguities in the proposed plan, not reproduced runtime regressions.

Severity is relative to planning: **high** can strand a required flow or misplace a core pair-admission gate; **medium** can misdirect sequencing, interpretation or qualification. “Resolved” means the documentation now specifies the correction, not that its implementation test passed.

## 4. Two implementation-boundary clarifications

**GPA-C1 — Absent propulsion includes identity and observations.** The plan already required a capability extension. The audit makes its end-to-end boundary explicit: the current [physical identity](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/physical_identity.gd), [normalized aircraft data](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd), [propulsion runtime](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/sim/aircraft_propulsion_runtime.gd) and session trace/contact metadata must agree on the AS-versioned absence representation. A loader-only change or invented motor/rotor identity is insufficient. SG-01/A01/A02 now name those consumers and powered-identity compatibility.

**GPA-C2 — Trial quaternion rotation is part of SG-07's numerical choice.** The hook equations assume rotations with a consistent inverse. The [rigid-body transform implementation](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/rigid_body.gd) uses a unit-quaternion convention; finiteness/nondegeneracy alone does not establish an orthogonal trial transform. The physics guide and T06 now require a consistent trial q-to-rotation policy across hook/aerodynamic transforms, power accounting, accepted projections and convergence. A normalized temporary evaluation rotation is a candidate, not permission to mutate stored RK trials or change legacy flight without proof.

For GPA-09, the targeted source evidence is [intended hull support](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/intended_hull_support.gd), [accepted session observations](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/sim/flight_session.gd), [statistics profiles](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/statistics/profiles.gd) and [fixed-wing statistics adapter](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/statistics/fixed_wing_adapter.gd). The observation is distinct from force generation and final outcome classification.

## 5. Retained strengths and genuine open gates

The review found the stated glide, air/ground-velocity, constant-mass TE, rigid-hook, unilateral-line and finite-thermal relationships internally consistent within their declared assumptions. The plan retains shared-stage coupling, float64 state, one installed VehicleModel, checkpointed sampled controllers, whole-pair rollback, accepted-state observers, source-owned assets and native-1080p priority. Analytic consistency is not a measured accuracy result.

The 24 investigations remain useful decision evidence. The five weather recipes remain proposed finite authoring seeds; no new runtime presets or generic near-ground lift are claimed. Three propeller diameters remain a source measurement extent, not a wake-clearance rule. Main-source issues inherited from existing audits stay with their original owners.

Open work is intentional and bounded: exact glider/tug build and permitted assets; independent mass/CG/inertia, polar/control/release data; installed rope history and any break law; the SF pair-layout/event/rotation choice and measured cost; reachable controllers and recovery; geometric/contact scope; weather/instrument/task evidence; and actual device/platform qualification. The master names who resolves each decision and which milestone or claim depends on it. None justifies pretending the evidence exists; none prevents starting the explicitly synthetic contract/solver work.
