# R10 — Mass, CG and inertia measurement

**Decision: integrate the measurement workflow now; prototype/measure before changing aircraft data.** The tools exist; physical build measurements and added-air corrections do not. Reviewed at baseline `08f0c08348395c0d6d9f7e224f66460828f59a7f`, 2026-11-15; no physical measurement or test run was made.

## Why this decision matters

The basic Jensen inventory contains a **virtual** 0.247701 kg balance item 0.85 m aft of the leading edge. The previous audit calculates its individual parallel-axis contribution to estimated pitch inertia as 0.13586 kg·m², roughly 36.8% of the modeled total; removing it also changes CG, so that number is not the result of a rebalanced aircraft. `aircraft_data.gd` reduces component boxes/points; `mass_inventory.gd::prepare()`/`evaluate()` aggregate first moments, inertia and finite fuel about a fixed datum. The fuel distribution is an approximation. The electric and finite-fuel configurations have distinct mass distributions and must be weighed separately, including the actual battery location and fuel state.

`research/validation/weighing/reduce.py` already reduces calibrated non-collinear level support readings to mass and horizontal CG, including uncertainty and shared gains. It cannot determine vertical CG. `research/validation/inertia/reduce.py` accepts bifilar or compound loaded/tare swing data with uncertainty and an optional calibration plank; synthetic examples are not Stik measurements. The tool explicitly returns **in-air** estimates and does not correct aerodynamic added inertia, prove small-angle/alignment assumptions, or automatically average repeats. Do not write its output directly into the aircraft file as an unqualified rigid-body tensor.

## Independent evidence

[NACA TN-351 (1930)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930081105) [PHY-S16] describes an improved pendulum method and calibration on known bodies; the reported accuracy applies to that apparatus. [Lehmkühler, Wong and Verstraete's 2016 small fixed-wing UAV measurement research](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/aeronautical-journal/article/abs/methods-for-accurate-measurements-of-small-fixed-wing-uav-inertial-properties/507615BF6196D73BAE1747286D49F3FA) [PHY-S17] reports aerodynamic added-mass corrections as large as 25% in the investigated configuration and warns that full-scale correction methods did not apply directly. The size is an example, not a universal correction for this Stik. [NACA TR-467](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930091541) [PHY-S25] likewise treats air effects in swinging tests.

## Reproducible campaign and acceptance

1. Identify build, covers, propeller, installed servos/receiver, gear, ballast, fuel/pack state and date. Level consistently and record datum, calibrated support positions, raw gross/tare readings, scale gain correlations, calibration and repeats; reduce full/half/empty fuel and electric-ready configurations. Treat plan CG as a design reference, not a measured CG.
2. Align a loaded and tare fixture on each CG axis; record wire full spacing, length, mass, cycles, elapsed time, amplitude, orientation, fixture and environmental conditions. Use a uniform calibration plank of measured dimensions and independently known mass. Repeat in both orientations and amplitudes; quantify drift, fixture interaction, air effects and configuration changes. Bifilar `J = m g D² T²/(16π²L)` assumes small oscillation and CG axis alignment; account for tare about the same axis.
3. Separately assess air-added inertia using suitable experiments/corrections and bound remaining systematic error. The existing tool's first-order uncertainties assume listed correlations and omit unknown added-air and alignment error; a nominal plank agreement is not proof for the airplane. Off-diagonal tensor terms require additional orientations or a justified symmetry bound; three axial values do not identify them.

Acceptance for data adoption requires reproducible raw records, uncertainty including common and unmodeled systematic terms, physically valid positive-definite tensor, correct LE-to-body frame conversion, and independently repeated configuration-level mass/CG/inertia. Change the source inventory only through its generator with actual ballast distinguished from virtual balance; compare re-trimmed and fixed-CG responses separately against held-out flight evidence. Until then, keep current data labeled estimates.
