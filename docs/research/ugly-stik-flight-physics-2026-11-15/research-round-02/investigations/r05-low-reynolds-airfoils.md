# R05 — Low-Reynolds airfoil evidence

**Decision: prototype/measure first.** Public low-Re section data are suitable as bounded sensitivity references, not as measured Jensen coefficients. Baseline `08f0c08348395c0d6d9f7e224f66460828f59a7f`; source review 2026-11-15. No numerical or physical test was run for this investigation.

## Question and repository finding

Which measurements overlap the operating regime, and what must be identified before adoption? `app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json` declares a 0.3048 m mean chord, 1.524 m span, and donor-derived attached whole-aircraft coefficients; it has estimated stall limits and no `wing_polar`. `app/physics/aero.gd` blends the whole-aircraft attached model with six local strips and an algebraic separation/flat-plate law. A useful geometric hint does exist: `assets/aircraft/ugly-stik-60/geometry.json` records a manually traced rib from the plan for visual geometry and expressly infers no polar. It is not proof that an actual covered wing matches that contour. The previous audit supplies the arithmetic and data provenance in `physics-and-aerodynamics.md`, sections 4 and 6.

At assumed ρ=1.225 kg/m³ and μ=1.81×10⁻⁵ Pa·s, `Re=ρVc/μ` is approximately 196,000 at 9.5 m/s, 309,000 at 15 m/s and 619,000 at 30 m/s. These are illustrative chord and free-stream values, not a measured flight envelope; local propwash, rotation, temperature and angle change the relevant flow.

## Evidence and decision

The [UIUC oil-flow study, 2003](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pubs/McGranahanSelig-2003-AIAA-2003-4067-LRN-Airfoils.pdf) [PHY-S01] covers several named sections at 200k, 350k and 500k and documents transition/separation structure; none is established as this wing. [UIUC's 2011 tunnel comparison](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pubs/SeligDetersWilliamson-2011-AIAA-2011-875-LRN-AirfoilWindTunnelTesting.pdf) [PHY-S02] compares E387 lift, moment and drag against NASA at 100k–460k: agreement varies, with unexplained larger drag discrepancy at 100k, and post-stall/end effects complicate comparison. The [UIUC tested-airfoil index](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/uiuc_lsat_airfoilsTested.html) [PHY-S27] is a starting shortlist, not evidence of geometric identity. Surface finish, leading-edge shape, covering sag, seams, trip state, turbulence and Reynolds range must accompany any choice. The high-speed example extends beyond some of the listed datasets.

A 2D polar does not contain finite-wing, fuselage, tail, propeller or complete aircraft drag and moments. Replacing the global `CD0` with section drag or copying an Extra polar risks double counting and donor compensation. A matched geometry plus XFOIL-style calculation can provide a sensitivity hypothesis, but transition and separated flow need experimental checks; a fixed polar is simpler yet conceals speed dependence. UIUC's [airfoil-data terms](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pd.html) [PHY-S03] permit copying subject to attribution and redistribution conditions, including commercial-use conditions; review the exact dataset's terms and project compatibility before importing any data. The paper's republication rights are separate.

## Integration and acceptance

First document build identity and obtain independent rib/covered-wing templates at several stations, incidence, control gaps/deflections, surface finish and uncertainty. Select any external polars only after geometric and operating-range comparison; retain original URLs, dataset version, run conditions and licensing. A shared-kernel **experimental**, explicitly sourced Re-dependent section-polar interface could interpolate only inside documented ranges, retain a clear out-of-range outcome, and avoid adding whole-aircraft drag twice. Evaluate clean static lift, drag and pitching moment at several speeds, both signs and control settings, then predict held-out flights without refitting. Require unchanged default/identity behavior, units, continuity, finite endpoints, and mesh/time convergence. No measured target polar or target-aircraft validation was located in this review; no stock coefficients should change now.
