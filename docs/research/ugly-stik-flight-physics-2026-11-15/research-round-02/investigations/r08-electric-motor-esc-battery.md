# R08 — Motor, ESC and battery limits

**Decision: prototype/measure first.** Keep the finite-pack and ideal-ESC distinctions explicit; do not infer a specific ESC, brake, thermal limit, or current rating from an aircraft name. Reviewed 2026-11-15 at runtime baseline `08f0c08348395c0d6d9f7e224f66460828f59a7f` without new bench runs.

## Current installation

`app/physics/electric_motor.gd::evaluate()` uses Kv, winding-equivalent R and no-load current in an ideal direct-drive motoring model, with nonnegative motor current and loss torque; its header excludes regeneration, active braking, current limit and battery state. `aircraft_data.gd::_electric_propulsion()` accepts only `ideal_motoring` and identity mapping, and explicitly scopes motor resistance apart from ESC/wiring. The 6S ideal-bus JSON and the normal-flight finite `pack_ecm` route have different source semantics. The finite pack models charge, sag and low-voltage supervision; its parameters are estimated cell-class values, not a measured installation. `electric_battery_step.gd::advance()` locates charge exhaustion and composes rotor-stop events; that is numerical event treatment, not evidence of measured controller protection. `docs/research/ugly-stik-flight-physics-2026-11-15/follow-up-01/electric-integration-plan.md` explains existing constraints.

## External evidence and alternatives

The 2021 [small fixed-wing UAV measurements](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1702/9/12/333) [PHY-S09] fit an equivalent motor/ESC arrangement using identified quantities and bench data, illustrating a practical low-order option but not this 6S installation or all transient regimes. [Hobbywing's Platinum 60A V4 description](https://www.hobbywing.com/en/products/platinum-60a-v473.html) [PHY-S10] lists distinct disabled, normal, proportional and reverse brake modes and protection features. It is evidence that ESC behavior depends on model and settings, not that the Stik uses that unit or responds with any particular timing. [NASA's thermal-model workflow](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20205006303) [PHY-S11] describes thermal dependencies and modeling; its chemistry and conditions cannot supply an RC pack's parameters.

Alternatives are (1) the current ideal equivalent, cheapest and honest in its stated domain; (2) separately identified ESC efficiency/voltage losses and current-limit behavior atop the finite pack; or (3) measured electro-thermal states and controller logic. A single extra resistance may reproduce one steady point yet confound winding, connector and switching losses. Phase/motor current and battery input current differ at partial duty; phase-to-phase and equivalent winding resistance conventions must be recorded. Do not equate a nominal C rating, vendor peak, torque, or catalog thrust to a measured transient limit. Coasting does not prove a real ESC is braking or regenerating.

## Integration and acceptance

Identify the exact propeller, motor, ESC firmware/settings, battery age/SOC/temperature, leads, cooling and measurement instrument bandwidth. Record time-synchronized command, voltage at pack and ESC, battery current, shaft RPM, thrust and when possible shaft torque and temperatures for static plateaus, small/large throttle steps, coast, brake settings, and cutoff/recovery across charge/temperature conditions. Use the existing static-prop and RPM-step reducers within their documented positive-powered domains; they do not infer shaft torque from electrical input or identify stopped-prop behavior.

Only then prototype an opt-in source/ESC interface separating battery, motor and ESC currents/power and using clearly defined current and thermal limits. Its integration must preserve current ideal-route identity and replay when absent, bound switching/event semantics, and conserve electrical, shaft, loss and stored energy across steps. Compare peak and steady current, sag/recovery, temperatures and spool-up/down against independent held-out runs with measurement uncertainty; include low-voltage cutoff hysteresis, restart and step-size refinement. No installation-specific parameter change is justified by the cited analog hardware.
