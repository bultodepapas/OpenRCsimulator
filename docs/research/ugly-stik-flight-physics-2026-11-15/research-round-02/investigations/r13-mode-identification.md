# R13 — Modal identity, subspaces and nonsymmetric solvers

**Decision: prototype and measure an offline diagnostic; do not replace the runtime classifier yet.** Preserve raw spectra and explicitly distinguish a block projection, a coupled fixed-RPM linearization and a complete flight simulation. No mathematical classification of the modeled matrix is a measurement of the aircraft.

## Current evidence and boundary

At runtime baseline `08f0c08348395c0d6d9f7e224f66460828f59a7f`, `app/physics/flight_modes.gd:23–61` solves a level trim and constructs a float64, central-difference Jacobian at fixed controls and RPM. Its diagonal longitudinal and lateral blocks exclude cross-axis terms; `:72–96` identifies modes from the count of real and complex roots. `app/physics/linearize.gd:10–33,145–163` forms the Jacobian and uses a small-matrix polynomial solver. The flight card applies another reporting heuristic at `research/flight-test/flight_test.gd:1144–1167`. These are documented estimates, not an 8/9-state coupled-flight guarantee.

The retained checked FS-64V analysis in [follow-up 01](../../follow-up-01/mode-and-trim-diagnosis.md) found a slow projected complex pair at 17 m/s and two slow real roots at 18 m/s; at 20 m/s its heuristic names the slow real pair short period and the faster complex pair phugoid. The reported 20 m/s full matrix has a +0.05705/s root while the diagonal-block spectra are stable. A replicated full Jacobian at three difference steps retains that sign and the default step agrees with the capture. This is a concrete label/projection concern in a frozen-control/fixed-RPM model. Sparse speed samples do not prove a continuous mode identity, and this is neither measured nor nonlinear aircraft instability. Other tested Stik and Extra points show the usual two-pair structure, so the concern is not a fleet-wide finding.

## External methods and alternatives

[SciPy 1.17 general `eig` (REV-S01)](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.17.0/reference/generated/scipy.linalg.eig.html) and [LAPACK nonsymmetric `DGEES` (REV-S02)](https://netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/d5/d38/group__gees_gab48df0b5c60d7961190d868087f485bc.html) support a general real nonsymmetric matrix and, respectively, left/right eigenvectors and real Schur subspaces. The latter can represent a nearly coalescing pair as a subspace without pretending its individual eigenvectors are well determined. Neither source validates OpenRC's equations. Do not use symmetric/Hermitian `eigh` for an arbitrary nonsymmetric flight Jacobian.

[NASA TP-2000-209591 (REV-S03)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20010038495/downloads/20010038495.pdf) discusses a phugoid example becoming real roots in a Harrier semi-jet-borne condition. It is a V/STOL aircraft example, not proof that any particular OpenRC roots are phugoid. Near coalescence, order by frequency alone can exchange labels; eigenvectors can also exchange or become ill conditioned. Nondimensionalize consistently, assess left/right residuals and eigenvalue/subspace condition, and track invariant subspaces across neighboring equilibria. Under coordinate changes the spectrum is invariant under similarity but raw vector magnitudes and a 2-norm condition number are scale-dependent.

The least disruptive immediate option is a **report-only contract**: retain each computed root, state ordering, derivative step, model/trim/propulsion scope and whether it came from a diagonal block; mark ambiguous mode names explicitly. For a complex pair, report undamped natural period `2π/|λ|` and damped oscillation period `2π/|Im λ|` as separately labeled fields. For an isolated nonzero real root report its sign and growth/decay time; a zero root has no finite such time. An overdamped second-order natural frequency needs an explicitly justified pairing and must not be invented from one root. Maintain old fields as versioned compatibility outputs rather than silently changing their meaning. A more ambitious offline prototype can match neighboring root clusters and their Schur subspaces with a declared nondimensional scaling; it must allow “unknown” when overlaps or conditioning fail. SciPy is already used in retained offline evidence, but that does not authorize a new production dependency.

## Acceptance

Re-run captured FS cases at 11.791, 17, 18, 20 and 22.4 m/s and the retained basic, electric and Extra controls under identical solver/model identities. Check conjugate pairing, residuals, a denser speed sweep, root-cluster continuity and sensitivity to several coordinate-scaled derivative steps. Include synthetic near-repeated roots, genuinely overdamped fast *and* slow branches, and `[[−1,2],[2,−1]]`: stable diagonal blocks do not prove a stable full matrix. Require no fabricated period for real roots, explicit unknown on ambiguity, and stable schema/units. A matched nonlinear small-perturbation experiment is a separate proposed test with the same frozen assumptions; actual-device and physical-aircraft qualification remain open.
