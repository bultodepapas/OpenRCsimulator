# R06 — Stopped propellers, windmilling and restart

**Decision: prototype/measure first.** The existing electric power-off gap is real in the equations; its correct physical closure remains installation-specific. Reviewed against runtime baseline `08f0c08348395c0d6d9f7e224f66460828f59a7f`, 2026-11-15, without new simulation or hardware runs.

## Current seam

In `app/physics/propulsion.gd`, `prop_torque()` and the non-glow wrench return zero below `STOPPED_RPM`, and advance ratio uses nonnegative axial speed. The 6S Ct/Cp tables have no negative Cp, and extrapolation clips Cp to zero when none is authored. `electric_motor.gd` models ideal motoring/coast with loss torque but no active brake, regeneration or current limit. Thus a stopped electric propeller has no blade drag in this path and the present positive-RPM table cannot supply driving aerodynamic torque to restart it. The earlier audit's 15 m/s fixed-state outputs are equation diagnostics, not a glide. `app/physics/glow/propeller.gd` instead has an estimated passive annular surrogate including stopped and windmilling flow; its section parameters are not measured maps and its applicability to the electric propeller is unproved. `research/validation/static-prop` expressly excludes stopped and negative-thrust cases.

## Evidence and alternatives

[UIUC Volume 4 v1, 2022](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-4/propDB-volume-4.html) [PHY-S04] includes nominal APC 12×8 Thin Electric measurements for static and several powered forward-flow RPMs. The [original test paper](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/pubs/DantskerCaccamoDetersSelig-2022-AIAA-Paper-2022-4020-APCEPropTestsVol4.pdf) [PHY-S05] says tests stop when torque approaches zero/windmill brake. Therefore exact nominal size is useful for powered-domain comparisons but is **not** a negative-power or stopped-propeller dataset. Production-era, true diameter and blade-Re differences still matter. [NACA TR-464, 1934](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930091538) [PHY-S06] measured negative thrust/torque and reports effects of the body behind the propeller and blade setting, but its four-foot adjustable metal propeller cannot supply RC coefficients. A [JSBSim developer discussion](https://github.com/JSBSim-Team/jsbsim/discussions/850) [PHY-S28] is a bounded implementation lead, not evidence that its law or a selected table models this motor/propeller at rest.

Options: a measured signed thrust/torque surface with a separate zero-speed parameterization; or a continuous blade-element model with measured blade geometry/section characteristics and a wake treatment. A naïve `J=V/(nD)` table diverges as n→0, so adding one high-J row cannot define the stop. A fixed drag constant may hide shaft energy and cannot determine restart torque. Coast and a programmed ESC brake have different boundary conditions. Reverse flow, negative shaft speed and regeneration should be separately supported or explicitly outside the admitted domain.

## Proposed integration and acceptance

Measure matched installed propeller axial force and shaft torque over speed and RPM including locked blade orientations, near-zero RPM and both sides of zero aerodynamic shaft power. Record axial inflow, density, blade/lot, hub, mounting-body interference, uncertainty, motor breakaway friction and ESC mode. Implement a versioned opt-in shared propeller evaluator supplying both force and signed torque; do not silently transplant the glow surrogate. Near zero, handle static friction as a bounded breakaway condition rather than an arbitrary positive torque. With a settled passive flow and consistent conventions, check that aerodynamic work on translation plus shaft (`F·V + τ_air·ω`) does not create energy, allowing stated numerical tolerance; account separately for wake/transient energy if later modeled. Test stop/restart, continuity and finite limits, positive/negative torque, timestep convergence, energy bookkeeping, explicit unsupported states and matched replay. Compare held-out wind-tunnel/glide observations before changing shipped data. No reusable negative-power map is established here; no third-party code or data import is authorized.
