# Ugly Stik flight physics and propulsion audit

**Audit source:** OpenRC Simulator commit `ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f`. Repository paths and line numbers below refer to that snapshot. **Scope:** source inspection, dimensional arithmetic, external primary-source review, and one controlled production-equation probe executed by the lead. Research dates and older result identities are preserved below. **No changes to simulation code or data, no parameter tuning, and no real-aircraft flight, bench, or pilot-feel qualification were performed.**

Evidence labels: **T** means a controlled current-source production-equation probe produced the stated result; **S** means a conclusion follows directly from the inspected code/data; **A** means a comparison uses independent but nonidentical aircraft, speed, or operating conditions; **H** is a hypothesis requiring discrimination. Older repository experiments are identified as historical, not reruns at this commit. A numerical test of current equations would verify software behavior only.

## What the simulator actually loads

The Home stock list contains a basic Jensen and a separately named experimental 6S electric Stik; the three finite-fuel 61FX/55AX/FS64V installations remain technical/saved routes rather than new stock choices (`docs/UGLY-STIK-PLAN.md:11-24`). These are different physical hypotheses, not interchangeable names for one engine.

- **Basic:** `app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json`; fixed 0.149961 kg half-tank (`:253-258`), prescribed RPM with idle 2,800, maximum 11,149 rpm, and 0.25 s lag (`:1514-1542`). No engine shaft or `glow_installation` is declared. [`app/physics/propulsion.gd:63-79`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/propulsion.gd#L63-L79) makes the steady target `2800 + 8349*throttle`; airspeed does not alter it. This is the intentionally preserved baseline, not the experimental NITRO engine. A change at constant power command therefore does not generate realistic RPM unloading through shaft torque.
- **6S:** normal flight defaults to the finite pack, whereas the ideal, 22.2 V constant-bus file is used by the explicitly identified Workshop trial (`docs/UGLY-STIK-PLAN.md:15-22`; `docs/research/propulsion/ugly-stik-implementation-2026-11-08.md:5-10`). [`app/data/aircraft/battery/stik_electric_6s_pack.json:1529`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/battery/stik_electric_6s_pack.json#L1529) has `pack_ecm`; [`app/data/aircraft/stik_electric_6s.json:1529-1540`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/stik_electric_6s.json#L1529-L1540) is fixed voltage and ideal motoring. Both contain the same 12x8E Ct/Cp tables; this was checked by JSON structural equality. The finite pack has modeled sag, discharge, and cutoff, but it is an estimated cell-class model; neither route implements the specific ESC firmware, motor/ESC current limiting, or thermal protection (`docs/research/propulsion/ugly-stik-implementation-2026-11-08.md:96-100`).
- **NITRO technical installations:** [`app/physics/aircraft_data.gd:261-266`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L261-L266) attaches the installation when explicitly declared. The propeller is a different 12-annulus surrogate, with stopped/windmilling flow ([`app/physics/glow/propeller.gd:1-5,67-103`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/glow/propeller.gd#L1-L5)). Do not use the legacy Ct/Cp tables shown in their outer JSON as though they were the running NITRO propeller law; [`app/physics/propulsion.gd:437-440,652-656`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/propulsion.gd#L437-L440) dispatches to `GlowProp`.

[`app/physics/dynamics.gd:86-135`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/dynamics.gd#L86-L135) combines body aero, propulsion and opt-in wash; body moments use CG arms and spinning-rotor angular momentum is present (`:17-25`). [`app/physics/aero.gd:1-9,115-246,408-694,968-1001`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aero.gd#L1-L9) uses a donor-derived attached whole-aircraft law and blends to six local wing strips, tail surfaces and optional body terms; it does not use dynamic stall memory. This is a capable, deliberately reduced-order implementation, not proof that the target aircraft's individual coefficients are measured.

## Prioritized findings

### 1. Electric power-off propeller loses both self-windmilling and stopped-blade drag — T/S

The 6S tables have no negative Cp (ideal file [`app/data/aircraft/stik_electric_6s.json:1621-1690`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/stik_electric_6s.json#L1621-L1690); finite-pack copy [`app/data/aircraft/battery/stik_electric_6s_pack.json:1705`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/battery/stik_electric_6s_pack.json#L1705)). [`app/physics/propulsion.gd:29-31,45-59,431-446`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/propulsion.gd#L29-L31) interpolates/extrapolates with Cp bounded below by zero when the table has no negative entries. For positive RPM the modeled air load therefore never applies a driving shaft torque. The ideal motor has one-quadrant drive with friction, [`app/physics/electric_motor.gd:33-48`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/electric_motor.gd#L33-L48). At RPM < 1, `prop_torque` is exactly zero and the entire non-glow propeller wrench is exactly zero regardless of forward velocity ([`app/physics/propulsion.gd:441-446,652-658,671-676`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/propulsion.gd#L441-L446)). Thus a disabled or zero-command 6S propeller cannot be driven out of rest by forward flow in this model, and a fully stopped propeller presents no blade drag. The finite-pack mode shares the propeller table/law; its battery affects the motor, not this absent aerodynamic branch.

**Observed diagnostic:** at 15 m/s and disabled drive, the current-law probe returned 0 N and 0 N·m at 0 RPM, and 0 N·m at 500 RPM (the corresponding force was −0.0734 N and the instantaneous shaft derivative −1133.5 rpm/s). At 5000 RPM, the result was +3.217 N, +0.1191 N·m aerodynamic load and −5816.5 rpm/s. These are isolated equation evaluations at prescribed state, not a simulated stop, restart or glide.

**Impact:** the aerodynamic drag and shaft state during power-off glides, descents and restart are a model limitation. Its actual effect on a particular flight and real ESC/propeller is unmeasured. Do not assume all electric setups windmill: a configured ESC brake, bearing friction and installation matter. Reverse axial flow also uses J=0 through `maxf(u,0)` (`:445,662`), outside the table's physical coverage.

**Action and test:** obtain matched forward-speed thrust and torque data through positive and negative Cp, include measured stopped-prop drag and ESC brake/coast setting, and implement a continuous zero-speed closure with correctly signed energy transfer in the shared electric-propeller law. Exercise zero and near-zero RPM, both directions of torque, continuous transition, shaft stop/restart, passivity and conservation, then compare unchanged-input glide replays. Do not transplant the NITRO surrogate without assessing its domain or changing only the final Ct row. The prepared `physics_audit_probe.gd` includes static samples at 15 m/s; a trajectory and real measurement remain separate.

### 2. Power does not wash Stik controls — T/S

The basic, both 6S files, and inspected 61FX/55AX/FS64V technical Stiks declare no `propeller.slipstream`, `normal_force` or `pfactor_moment` data (basic propulsion block [`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:1513-1662`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1513-L1662); 6S block [`app/data/aircraft/stik_electric_6s.json:1485-1717`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/stik_electric_6s.json#L1485-L1717)). [`app/physics/dynamics.gd:109,131-135`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/dynamics.gd#L109) adds slipstream only if the normalized model declares it; [`app/physics/slipstream.gd:59-64`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/slipstream.gd#L59-L64) returns a zero wrench when absent. Therefore changing RPM does not directly augment Stik tail/wing aerodynamic loads through a propeller wake. Gyroscopic, shaft reaction and axial thrust remain modeled and must not be described as absent. At exactly zero aircraft velocity and angular rate, the unwashed aero control forces are zero even with surface deflection, independently of the nonzero propeller thrust ([`app/tests/test_aero.gd:91-97`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/tests/test_aero.gd#L91-L97) describes this aerodynamic-law property; total powered load still includes thrust/torque).

**Observed diagnostic:** at zero body speed/rates, an arbitrarily fixed 10,000 RPM and −10° data-convention rudder, the basic model produces 33.187 N propeller thrust and zero change in rudder side force/yaw moment; both 6S representations produce 32.306 N and zero response. The opt-in Extra produces 30.362 N thrust and a rudder-minus-neutral change of −0.5816 N side force and +0.4376 N·m yaw moment. This demonstrates different implemented paths at selected states, not a matched-power or real-aircraft comparison.

This is not a claim that propeller wash wholly covers the wing or that any particular low-speed response is physically wrong. Airframe geometry and local wake contraction determine immersed surface area. The Extra opts into modeled tail wash, prop normal force and P-factor ([`app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json:1653,1748,1843`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json#L1653)); P-51 also opts in. Existing wash implementation targets tail pieces ([`app/physics/aero.gd:709-745`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aero.gd#L709-L745)), so adding it does **not** automatically establish washed wing/aileron modeling. The electric parser rejects all three optional fields ([`app/physics/aircraft_data.gd:1315-1318`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L1315-L1318)) and omits them from its normalized output (`:1361-1374`); adding JSON alone cannot enable electric wash. The current guide already states electric propwash is absent (`docs/UGLY-STIK-PLAN.md:22`).

**Action and test:** measure/derive actual installed disc, thrust line, wake contraction/decay and tail immersion; add opt-in data and shared loader/runtime support with capability/identity checks. Keep the electrical loader and propulsion source semantics explicit. At fixed airspeed, surface, and RPM vary one factor at a time: verify zero wash in zero-thrust conditions, signs, powered-minus-unpowered increments, stage-local and replay consistency, sensitivity to alignment, and eventually compare measured throttle/rudder/elevator steps. A source-matched fixture comparing powered Stik and Extra at an arbitrary common RPM illustrates code routing, not an aerodynamic comparison or target.

### 3. Side force changes law and the Stik has no explicit body-side term — T/S; severity in flight A/H

The basic Stik has `aero.surfaces.body_cn_beta` and `wing_body_cl_beta` but no `body_cy_beta` or `body_crossflow_area` ([`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:1342-1474`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1342-L1474)); the electric and NITRO aircraft inherit the same surface law. [`app/physics/aero.gd:668-693`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aero.gd#L668-L693) adds a fuselage side force only if the relevant optional terms are present. At high sideslip local wing strips have no spanwise force (`:581-595`) and the modeled vertical tail supplies side load. The global donor law does include CYβ (`:150-159`), and `:791-805,968-1001` blends between laws depending on α, β, rates and local surface angles. The `15–25°` beta range is a component of the selector, not a guarantee that the actual blend weight depends only on beta.

**Observed diagnostic:** for the basic Stik at 15 m/s, α=0, β=+10°, zero rates, neutral controls and no prop/ground effect, body `Fy/(qS)` was −0.09178 global, −0.04411 local and −0.06795 production at local weight 0.5. At β=+20° these were −0.17401, −0.05539 and −0.05539 at local weight 1. This is **body-axis** force, unlike the historical wind-axis table below. It shows the selected model regime, not which branch is correct for the real airplane.

**Older model evidence:** the 2026-10-18 investigation records a Stik maneuver at β 20.7° with local weight 0.60 and wind-axis CY values −0.130 oracle, −0.031 local and −0.070 blended (`docs/research/flight-dynamics/stik-lateral-directional-2026-10-18/README.md:130-139`). The trace is historical, with its own source identity and inputs; it was not rerun in this audit. The same report's NACA analog gives only order-of-magnitude fuselage bounds and notes uncertainty; the oracle may be wrong as well as the local law. Extra has explicit body side terms ([`app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json:1221-1232`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json#L1221-L1232)), which establishes only a feature difference.

**Action and test:** measure target side force and yaw/roll moments versus β and α, preferably airframe plus isolated body, then fit a common physical decomposition through the global/local blend. As an intermediate check, perform β sweeps with signs, continuity, force/moment arms, roll/yaw damping and controlled rudder/airspeed, including zero rates and actual dynamic maneuvers. Compare with matched flights and do not force the local law to equal a borrowed oracle solely to flatten a curve. NACA TN 3551's bodies are analogs, not target data.

### 3a. Local induced-flow weights have a known receiver-speed inconsistency — S, with historical numerical evidence

[`app/physics/aero.gd:325-332,354-369,500-505`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aero.gd#L325-L332) scales the influence from other strips by `section_plane_speed_k / max(full_3d_station_speed)`, reduced for source stall. For uniform, attached flow, zero rates and sideslip β, the first ratio becomes cosβ: at β=25° it is about 0.906 even though source and receiver have equal section-plane speed. For nonuniform yaw-induced spanwise velocity, the ratio also differs from a lifting-line argument based on source circulation divided by the *receiver's* local speed. This affects the local branch and its derivatives; it does not prove a particular flight error or that a singular textbook correction is ready for production.

The prior R02 investigation explicitly diagnoses this operator and compared source-derived wing yaw damping against a matched six-panel AVL model (`docs/research/flight-dynamics/stik-lateral-directional-2026-10-18/README.md:62-80`). At α=11° its historical numbers were AVL −0.01349, production −0.00543 and a simple candidate −0.00928. The candidate is not recommended: it divides by potentially vanishing strip speed, changes other cross-derivatives and only partly addresses the force closure; an exploratory bounded circulation approach had multiple roots through stall (`:101-106`). Those numbers are historical, not rerun here.

**Action and test:** develop a bounded, physically coherent circulation/force closure as an isolated shared-kernel experiment, with uniform-flow and analytic small-angle references, fixed-geometry strip-refinement, nonuniform yaw, ±β, nearly stagnant/reverse stations, stall roots, passivity, actual aircraft regression, and identical-input replay. Establish how and when a continuous wake/separation state selects among nonlinear solutions. Do not apply the direct speed-ratio patch merely to improve one damping derivative.

### 4. Stall and spin are not physically qualified; analogous measured data expose a structural tradeoff — S/A

The Jensen uses estimated `CL_max=1.1`, `CL_min=-0.8` and 6° blend width ([`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:1101-1126`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1101-L1126)), no `wing_polar`, and the legacy flat-plate blend ([`app/physics/aero.gd:484-528`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aero.gd#L484-L528)). Every strip sees an algebraic stall law without a dynamic-separation state (`aero.gd:1-9`); the six-strip resolution is fixed (`:19-20`). The optional `WingPolar` feature exists but is not adopted for this aircraft. The Extra has an analog-fitted polar ([`app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json:1380-1446`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json#L1380-L1446)); copying it to the Jensen would ignore differences in airfoil, Reynolds number, control geometry and CG.

Repository history compared the production Stik law against NASA FASER's different Ultra Stick 120: 2026-10-19 results list RMS normalized lift-retention error 0.327 and roll-damping error 0.957 over 10–17.5°, with 4/6 unstable values, while two analog-fitted fixtures improved those quantities but no longer produced a developed model spin (`docs/research/flight-robustness/D8b-R2/analog/README.md:11-23,32-50`). Forced-oscillation damping includes a sideslip-rate contribution; deep-stall comparisons are not equivalent to a pure `Clp` measurement, as the authors explicitly correct (`:20-23`). The maneuver history records limits including digitization and a different geometry (`docs/research/flight-dynamics/spin-post-stall-2026-10-19/README.md:47-57,74`). NASA's primary FASER report confirms measured damping depends on rate and reduced frequency in stall/post-stall [1]. A model's ability to spin does not establish spin fidelity.

**Action and test:** retain the legacy law until independent evidence supports a replacement. Record local angle, load, stalled fraction, applied controls and effective CG through slow/fast pull-ups, spin entry/held spin/recovery, at multiple speeds and both directions; run a fixed-geometry strip refinement independent of coefficient recalibration. Compare static lift, drag, roll damping and held-out maneuvers together. Test a stateful separated-flow model only as an isolated shared-kernel experiment with complete RK-stage, replay and convergence evidence, and with target measurements; do not tune a single lift peak or one video. The discrepancy is **analog evidence**, not proof that the Jensen has FASER's curve.

### 5. Mass, CG and inertia assumptions can dominate handling — S for arithmetic, A/H for physical implications

The basic inventory adds an expressly virtual balancing mass of 0.247701 kg at x=0.85 m aft of LE ([`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:643-669`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L643-L669)). Components are uniform boxes/points; [`app/physics/aircraft_data.gd:1153-1170`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L1153-L1170) uses their intrinsic inertia and parallel-axis shifts. Applying that formula to authored inventory gives the following **inventory-state estimates**, not a measured Jensen or proof of initial session fill:

| Configuration | Inventory mass kg | Inventory x CG from LE | CG/chord | Ixx / Iyy / Izz kg m² |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Basic, fixed half tank | 2.878662 | 0.109392 m | 35.89% | 0.1157 / 0.3692 / 0.4661 |
| Electric 6S | 2.600082 | 0.120900 m | 39.67% | 0.1128 / 0.1794 / 0.2772 |
| Finite-glow 61FX authored full inventory | 3.028623 | 0.099024 m | 32.49% | 0.1158 / 0.3757 / 0.4726 |

For the basic inventory alone, the virtual item's longitudinal lever term `0.247701*(0.85−0.109392)^2 = 0.13586 kg m²`, about 36.8% of total Iyy; this is only one term, not a counterfactual effect of removing and rebalancing it. The dry-balance plan CG is 0.1209 m (`:41-69`); the 0.149961 kg forward half tank moves the total inventory CG forward 11.51 mm, about 3.78% chord. For the technical full 61FX inventory the shift is about 21.88 mm, 7.18% chord. Using the source's estimated neutral point 43.5% chord (`:29-37`), static-margin estimates are approximately 7.61%, 3.83% and 11.01% respectively, **conditional on that fixed unpowered neutral point and those inventory fuel states**. This is an engineering sensitivity calculation, not measured stability; power, nonlinear aerodynamics, changing fuel state and uncertainty in neutral point matter. Fuel resource geometry maintains a fixed shape while density scales ([`app/physics/mass_inventory.gd:1-10,115-155`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/mass_inventory.gd#L1-L10)), itself an approximation to free-surface motion.

**Action and test:** measure the specific ready-to-fly build on scales, locate CG for full/half/empty fuel and with the battery, and measure roll/pitch/yaw inertia by pendulum with uncertainty and configuration. Include prop, wing and gear. Rebuild generated inventories through their source pipeline; distinguish real ballast from mathematical balancing. Compare response and re-trim separately at the measured CG/inertia; do not use manual trim behavior as a substitute for independent inertial measurements.

### 6. Reynolds dependence, attached coefficients, controls and trim remain empirical gaps — S/A

At ρ=1.225 kg/m³ and assumed dynamic viscosity μ=1.81×10⁻⁵ Pa·s, the 0.3048 m Jensen chord gives `Re = ρVc/μ ≈ 20,629 V`: approximately 196,000 at 9.50 m/s, 309,000 at 15 m/s and 619,000 at 30 m/s. The source's `CL0=0.1068`, `CLa=4.58/rad`, `CD0=0.0434`, `CL_minD=0.23`, and `k=0.0814934` are borrowed ([`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:1101-1102,1148-1194`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1101-L1102)); `Cm`, lateral derivatives and control derivatives mix donor values, geometric estimates and derivatives produced by this same model (`:1214-1339`). They are not independent Jensen measurements. The aerodynamic equations use these fixed coefficient dictionaries, rather than a measured target-Re lookup ([`app/physics/aero.gd:115-159,408-445`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/aero.gd#L115-L159)). Density and local velocity still affect dimensional loads, and fixed coefficients are an explicit approximation rather than a proven bug.

UIUC's low-Reynolds airfoil tests identify separation bubbles and give experimental data at Re 200k, 350k and 500k [2], the range relevant here. They do not identify the Jensen's actual section or produce a measured whole-aircraft polar; obtain the covered, built section and roughness/transition first. A theoretical level 1g balance with the authored `CLmax`, no propwash, level lift, `V_s=sqrt(2mg/(ρS CLmax))` yields 9.50 m/s for basic and 9.03 m/s for 6S (inventory masses). The same calculation using |CLmin|=0.8 gives inverted estimates 11.14 and 10.59 m/s respectively. These are reference calculations, not simulator stall results: tail force, actual lift coefficient, thrust, finite flight trajectory and atmospheric conditions matter.

Throws are estimates ±20° aileron/elevator and ±25° rudder with a 0.14 s neutral-to-full servo assumption ([`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:1027-1052`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1027-L1052)); low-rate ratios and expo are transferred from other aircraft (`:1054-1091`). The sampled servo model is a rate limiter ([`app/physics/control_actuators.gd:17-21`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/control_actuators.gd#L17-L21)); dynamic hinge load, flex, voltage and backlash are not established by this data. Track actual surface angles versus transmitted commands, perform static and in-flight control step/reversal tests at known CG and airspeed, and measure incidence and trim. For handling use identical recorded input tapes with exact replay and actual inputs; independent pilots and held-out flights are necessary to assess subjective feeling.

### 7. Ground effect is included but approximate — S/A

The Stik opts into a height-based induced-angle/drag correction and tail-downwash correction ([`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:1475-1501`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1475-L1501); [`app/physics/ground_effect.gd:24-84`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/physics/ground_effect.gd#L24-L84)). It is not accurate to report the Stik as having no ground effect. At strip height/span h/b=0.10, the implemented faded functions give approximately 0.514 for induced effect and 0.206 for tail-downwash scaling; at 0.25 they give 0.813 and 0.627. These are coefficients for parts of the modeled correction, not percentage changes in overall lift, touchdown distance or pitch. The fit assumes a flat-surface image approximation with estimated geometry, per-strip heights and one ground height under the CG; bank, irregular terrain and actual tail height/flow need separate validation.

**Action and test:** test monotonic/continuous factors, far-field recovery, one-wing-low symmetry, actual ground-height sampling, and low-altitude glide/flare at fixed height and α. Validate against a measured height-sweep or a suitable geometry-matched experiment; preserve the existing ground-effect module and contact/prop-clearance paths. No measured gear stiffness, tire curve or actual landing feel follows from these formulas ([`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:867-1003`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L867-L1003) contains estimates).

### 8. Propeller and engine provenance has a useful new independent cross-check — A

The basic nominal APC 12x6 uses a measured **11x6 Sport** analog transferred by nondimensional coefficients, with interpolation between static and J=0.373 ([`app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json:1554-1641`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/aircraft/jensen_ugly_stik_60.json#L1554-L1641)). Its nominal static point at 11,149 rpm, Ct=0.113, Cp=0.0471, D=0.3048 m and ρ=1.225 implies `T=Ct ρn²D⁴ ≈ 41.25 N`, `P=Cp ρn³D⁵ ≈ 973.8 W`, and thrust/weight ≈1.46 for the 2.878662 kg inventory. This is arithmetic from its authored propeller estimate, not a measured engine output. The O.S. specification gives 1.9 PS at 16,000 rpm and 550 g [3], which does not determine the torque curve at 11,149 rpm or validate this loaded point. The technical 61FX annulus chord is itself transferred from a 55AX/12x7 calibration; its declared source predicts 12,550 static rpm and identifies the mismatch ([`app/data/glow/stik_61fx_2t.json:19-23,161-189`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f/app/data/glow/stik_61fx_2t.json#L19-L23)).

For the exact APC **12x8 Thin Electric**, UIUC Vol. 4 has measured static and forward-flow data [4–6].
