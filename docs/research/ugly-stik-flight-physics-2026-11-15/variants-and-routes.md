# Ugly Stik variants and selection-route audit

Date: 2026-11-15. Baseline: `ad21070798bfb9658785576a9c0b8bfc9a71fc7f` on `main`. The initial read-only source checkout was sparse; missing research and asset sources were subsequently inspected in the isolated branch checkout at `/workspace/scratch/d289586b400e/OpenRCsimulator`, initially at that same commit. All path and line references below are relative to that checkout. Except for the specifically identified documentation edits, findings describe the baseline. This review inspected code and data statically; it did not run Godot, render, fly, replay logs, or requalify physics. Existing test files are identified as intended coverage, not as tests executed in this audit.

## Verdict

The existing selection architecture already implements **two stock player-selectable Ugly Stiks**: the basic prescribed-fuel reference and the experimental electric 6S conversion. Three additional, materially different finite-glow configurations remain in the technical registry for direct development routes, old Workshop documents, research, testing, export and economy. They are deliberately excluded from ordinary Home and Workshop new-stock choices. Deleting them, aliasing the 61FX fixture to the basic reference, or filtering the global technical registry would break documented identity and coverage contracts. No ordinary stock-picker leak was established by this static trace.

The baseline root README nevertheless advertised three extra glow “ways to fly” without stating that they were technical-only, omitted the electric stock Stik, and gave a stale total of ten airplane configurations. A documentation-only correction is now applied to `README.md:77,95,132` and `docs/AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md:133–138` in the branch checkout. It names the two stock choices, distinguishes the retained compatibility configurations, corrects the stock fixed-wing count, and includes the electric in the aircraft-capability table. The runtime/data design is unchanged. Separate inherited qualification and evidence-labeling gaps below should not be misrepresented as fixed by those edits.

## Findings and recommended boundary

1. **Resolved in the assigned documentation edits — stale user-facing inventory.** Baseline `README.md:77` claimed ten airplane configurations, while the catalog has eleven technically flyable airplanes; its `:132` advertised three glow alternatives without naming their technical-only status or the stock electric. The corrected README distinguishes eight stock airplanes and the two Stiks. Baseline `docs/AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md:133–143` listed the basic, hidden glow, other airplanes and synthetic fixtures, but omitted the stock electric; the current corrected table adds it. The canonical pre-existing distinction was already stated in `docs/UGLY-STIK-PLAN.md:15–24`, `docs/GLOW-ENGINES.md:3–7` and `docs/AIRCRAFT-WORKSHOP.md:74,119`. These edits describe existing behavior; they do not create the two-choice policy.
2. **Open inherited qualification — electric landing statistics.** This is explicitly fail-closed and tested rather than a hidden stock choice. A future targeted fix should independently review and record the electric's own datum-relative support IDs and geometry in both ideal and pack configurations, then extend the reviewed-profile and detector cases. Do not copy the donor's statistics coordinates merely because the physical wheel alias exists: each installation can shift its mass datum. No statistics change is included here.
3. **Open inherited evidence-labeling risk — runway capture energy mode.** The technical scenery fixture will use electric's ideal source while ordinary flight defaults to pack, and its output omits source identity. This was established by code inspection; no capture was executed. Treat as a separate fixture improvement, not grounds to alter player selection or physics.
4. **Historical media wording — clarified by the integrator.** At the audited baseline, `docs/media/README.md:39` said all 13 catalog/ground configurations were “playable” and describes the glow Stik images as redundant. It reports an older rendering campaign; the three glow specimens are now hidden, and current electric presentation has a separate visual family. The integrator now clarifies the historical technical roster/visibility in `docs/media/README.md`, preserves the original capture scope and explicitly states that it does not qualify electric presentation. The default studio helper still enumerates technical IDs, as documented at `docs/media/README.md:53`.
5. **Programmatic Workshop seam — bounded exposure, not a confirmed picker leak.** The internal editor setter accepts hidden IDs for technical flows; ordinary stock selectors and debug setup/fly do not. Existing compatibility callers and tests depend on the broader seam. Gate future arbitrary-ID stock callers at their entry point instead of globally closing catalog lookup or breaking frozen documents.

No unexplained duplicate catalog alias or safe-to-remove Stik data path was established. The alternate propeller and synthetic records have active research or test references. `docs/plans/ugly-stik-two-variants.md:7–20,57–90` documents the implemented policy, superseded earlier proposal and physical/compatibility reasons; it is not a fresh instruction to remove files.
