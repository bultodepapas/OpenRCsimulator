# Ray tracing in Godot: OpenRC audit and bounded experiment

Research snapshot: **2026-11-15**. Audited OpenRC `e488ef7653e8dd0f0a2e7db18e396bb4da0aaccc`; documentation-only analysis. Engine pin: official Godot `4.8.beta2.official.cafc17a19`. All external sources below were accessed on 2026-11-15. Findings are code/document inspection unless a previous native experiment is explicitly identified. No OpenRC GPU timings, new hardware tests, product implementation or visual qualification were produced in this audit.

## Decision

Do not present hardware ray tracing as a stock Godot effect or as an installed OpenRC feature. Also do not say that Godot lacks a ray-tracing API: upstream provides low-level Vulkan ray-tracing pipeline and acceleration-structure primitives, and a ray-query capability interface. They require scene data, shaders, synchronization, materials, integration and device checks supplied by the application or engine. The existing native and projected shadows, sky/probe reflections and optional planar lake capture remain the baseline.

If an attributed pilot-visible defect and measured 1080p headroom justify work, first prototype **one opt-in, bounded directional-sun visibility effect** on a controlled opaque fixture, then investigate an actual field and moving aircraft. The experiment must separate the sun's existing direct-light term before applying the RT visibility; multiplying the final color would incorrectly darken ambient light, emissive surfaces and unrelated lights or double-apply shadows. That separation may need engine work. A diagnostic hit, a feature flag, or an isolated grayscale mask is not a rendered, correct shadow effect. Default to the current raster result whenever coverage, creation, execution or lifetime cannot be proven. Full path-traced gameplay is not a recommended default.

This snapshot is a feasibility input to the existing [VQ-07 advanced-graphics plan](../../plans/advanced-graphics.md), [VQ-04b shadows plan](../../plans/shadows.md), [GDU engine upgrade](../../plans/godot-4.8-upgrade.md), and [DT-MAC-13d1–d3 MetalFX plan](../../plans/apple-metalfx.md). They retain ownership, status and acceptance; no new step namespace or duplicate authority is created here.

**Expanded implementation and visual-quality handoff:** [14](14-ray-tracing-quality-and-delivery-plan.md) ranks where RT could matter during real RC flight and details a bounded NVIDIA/AMD execution sequence. Its [vendor appendix](ray-tracing/01-nvidia-amd-integration-guide.md) maps Vulkan, NRD, RTXDI, DLSS Ray Reconstruction and AMD techniques to actual prerequisites; its [cloud/material/water appendix](ray-tracing/02-clouds-materials-and-water.md) audits the existing shaders and paired scenes. High's default/current native soft configuration already has four-cascade PCSS with a 0.53-degree sun; user settings can override it, so record the actual effective control. A deterministic one-ray hard-shadow fixture proves visibility, not better softness. Compare against the applicable current PCSS and lake planar path before accepting a quality or cost claim.

## Exact version and technical boundary

- [`app/get-godot.sh`](../../../app/get-godot.sh#L4) pins `4.8-beta2` and `4.8.beta2.official.cafc17a19`; [`app/get-templates.sh`](../../../app/get-templates.sh#L6) pins the matching template release. The official [beta 2 announcement](https://godotengine.org/article/dev-snapshot-godot-4-8-beta-2/) identifies the engine commit; [beta 3](https://godotengine.org/article/dev-snapshot-godot-4-8-beta-3/) was released on 2026-11-09. A newer beta is not authorization to change the editor or templates.
- The [GodotCon Vulkan integration description](https://talks.godotengine.org/godotcon-ams-2026/talk/JDYVQ3/) describes BLAS/TLAS, ray-generation, miss, hit and intersection shader stages and dispatch through `RenderingDevice` (RD). The [4.7 RD reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_renderingdevice.html) documents `blas_create`, `blas_build`, `tlas_create`, `tlas_build`, pipeline/SBT commands and separate `SUPPORTS_RAY_QUERY` and `SUPPORTS_RAYTRACING_PIPELINE` checks. Its RT entries are experimental. RD is unavailable in Compatibility and headless mode. These low-level facilities do not build OpenRC's scene or enable a complete renderer effect.
- Vulkan [ray query](https://docs.vulkan.org/refpages/latest/refpages/source/VK_KHR_ray_query.html) traverses from an existing shader and returns intersections to that shader; an RT pipeline uses separate shader stages and a shader binding table (SBT). Do not assume either capability implies the other. Shader compilation, `RD.has_feature`, AS allocation and an actual hit must all be verified on the selected real device and backend.
- Upstream [issue #121286](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/issues/121286) confirms the 4.7 query-feature request bug; a maintainer reported success with fix #122051, which closed the issue for the 4.8 milestone on 2026-08-04. Independent inspection of the [exact beta 2 Vulkan source](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a192acf67903910a68aeb3c076c0907a4b/drivers/vulkan/rendering_device_driver_vulkan.cpp#L594) confirms the previously missing request is present: it requests `VK_KHR_ray_query`, queries and stores the device feature, enables it during device creation, and reports it separately from pipeline support. The 4.7 missing-request defect is therefore not present in this source. A real `has_feature` and actual hit remain untested here; no device support or execution is implied.
- There is a more specific pinned-base risk. Exact beta 2 [`tlas_build`](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a192acf67903910a68aeb3c076c0907a4b/servers/rendering/rendering_device.cpp#L582) rejects a zero hit-SBT range for an instance with a BLAS; its normal [`hit_sbt_create`](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a192acf67903910a68aeb3c076c0907a4b/servers/rendering/rendering_device.cpp#L692) allocation route requires a ray-tracing pipeline. The check itself tests nonzero, not SBT ownership; fabricating a scalar is not established as a supported workaround. The experimental Metal lane adds its own narrow exception. This is source inspection, not a reproduced Vulkan failure: query-only devices may not be usable through stock RD without a fix. Inspect a query-only device safely if available; do not call pipeline creation without its enabled feature/entry point. Until a separately verified query-only route exists, require both query and pipeline plus the normally allocated SBT, or treat query-only as unsupported. The pinned [Vulkan BLAS/TLAS implementation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a192acf67903910a68aeb3c076c0907a4b/drivers/vulkan/rendering_device_driver_vulkan.cpp#L6406) sets `BUILD` mode for both structures. The two inspected RD/Vulkan source files expose build commands, not a refit/compaction command; experimental `ALLOW_UPDATE`/`ALLOW_COMPACTION` flags alone do not establish an accessible update path. Recheck the full interface and run update/ownership tests before depending on either.
- Current Godot [RD documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_renderingdevice.html) describes the two generic RT capabilities as Vulkan-only and notes MoltenVK's macOS/iOS limitation. For this official pin, direct inspection of the [Metal driver](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/cafc17a192acf67903910a68aeb3c076c0907a4b/drivers/metal/rendering_device_driver_metal.cpp#L2380) confirms that its BLAS/TLAS, pipeline and RT command methods are unsupported stubs; see also the [existing exact-source audit](../desktop-delivery-investigations/metalfx-20-investigations-2026-11-14.md#i14--which-rt-engine-and-geometry-operations-are-missing). Therefore Windows/Linux Forward+ **with the Vulkan driver** and a checked eligible GPU is the first Vulkan integration candidate, subject to the exact-pin corrections below; D3D12, native Metal, OpenGL Compatibility and headless do not inherit that result. Verify actual renderer and driver, not OS or GPU name alone.
- Apple [documents Metal ray tracing](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal/accelerating-ray-tracing-using-metal) separately from Godot's RD implementation. Apple identifies hardware-accelerated intersection in its [M3/A17 Pro family 9 GPU talk](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111375/) and [shader guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111373/). M1 is Apple7 and lacks that Apple9 dedicated intersection acceleration, but can expose Metal ray-tracing API capability. OpenRC's [M1 observations](../desktop-delivery-investigations/DT-MAC-11a/README.md#rt-and-validation-follow-up) report such capability and an isolated RT/denoising witness; this is neither a hardware-acceleration nor performance claim. Avoid an M3-name whitelist: require actual OS, API, descriptor and execution checks, then qualify each device/workload.

**Later exact-pin prerequisite:** the [fixture handoff](15-ray-tracing-fixture-handoff.md#2-exact-engine-findings-that-change-the-first-implementation) identifies a Vulkan AS-address contract mismatch, incomplete creation-error propagation and a same-TLAS repeated-build input risk. These are source/spec findings, not reproduced GPU failures. The first positive portability witness must use an isolated, reviewed correction based on the exact pin, with matching editor/templates; safe stock capability/negative checks remain distinct. The [native work order](ray-tracing/03-vulkan-fixture-work-order.md) and [tested offline answers](../../../research/performance/graphics_handoff/rt_fixture_README.md) supply the next steps. A coincident backing-buffer/AS address on one device does not resolve the API contract.

## Distinguish techniques and integration routes

SSR traces the visible depth/color representation, so off-screen or occluded geometry remains a limitation. Godot can mix SSR with probes; see [Environment and post-processing](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/3d/environment_and_post_processing.html). SDFGI, VoxelGI, reflection probes and baked lightmaps use different approximations and update/coverage rules; see Godot's [feature list](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/about/list_of_features.html). None establishes a hardware-RT scene pipeline. Godot's 4.7 docs give a broad transparency limitation, while OpenRC's [4.8 SSR analysis](../advanced-graphics-2026-10-21/round-2.md#r11--lake-reflections-and-the-changed-ssr-path) records a prior-frame path; test the pinned version's alpha, depth and history behavior instead of assuming a universal transparency rule.

| Candidate route | Feasibility and limits | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock Vulkan RD + compositor / GDScript or GDExtension | Godot's experimental [CompositorEffect](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_compositoreffect.html) runs a callback on the render thread and can request depth, motion and Forward+ normal/roughness resources. This is a possible place to run a custom compute/query pass, **not** an automatic scene-AS feed, material system or separate sun term. Prove actual buffer formats, access timing and GPU lifetime on beta 2. A compositor alone cannot safely relight an already-composited frame. | Start with a small isolated fixture; use a native extension only where it materially helps. Check versioned binary/export availability and keep main-RD ownership/render-thread rules. |

An NVIDIA SDK or a root MIT notice is not proof of portable deployment. |

## Proposed execution under existing owners

These estimates are rough engineering effort for one experienced rendering engineer, **not measured durations or commitments**. Hardware availability, asset coverage, qualification, CI and review are additional. Stop at each decision if its prerequisite fails; retain a smaller experiment rather than promoting an incomplete effect.

| Stage | Work and decision evidence | Rough effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capability and baseline (VQ/GDU/PT) | Reproduce the reported sun/grounding defect against native/projected shadows; freeze actual 1080p output, camera, aircraft, light and quality settings. Record exact upstream engine identity and the required reviewed corrections, compile query and/or RT shader, probe both flags, then build/trace a known triangle on that corrected candidate and compare hit/miss/instance identity with the independent offline answers. Explicitly test the stock SBT requirement, creation failure and absent device. Measure baseline headroom before allocating a candidate budget. | Historical combined estimate: 3–7 engineer-days, **not additional days after M0**. The controlling [plan](05-integrated-implementation-plan.md) caps all M0 repair/baseline work at five days total and the first admitted M4 feasibility decision at two days; hardware access is separate. |
| Isolated signal (VQ/GDU) | Build one bounded opaque floor/occluder scene, including an off-camera caster and moving rigid aircraft-like object. Start with one ray per selected receiver sample and a deliberately bounded ray length/region; output the raw visibility plus receiver/depth/normal and generation debug views. Prove the extracted caster set and render-thread lifetime. Resolve stock query-only/SBT feasibility before assuming a pure compute route. | 2–4 engineer-weeks after capability proof; a fixture only. |
| Correct integration and motion (VQ/GDU, DT-MAC separately) | Reuse static geometry BLAS while its source is unchanged; update/rebuild TLAS for rigid plane, propeller and hinge transforms. For same-topology skin/wind deformation use a refit only if the exact backend exposes and verifies it; otherwise rebuild or reject the scene. Rebuild on mesh/LOD/topology or instance-set changes. Implement alpha-tested UV/texture sampling and candidate confirmation for cutouts, material identity, masks, visibility and motion or explicitly reject affected scenes. Preserve opaque/transparent material behavior and all relevant off-screen casters. Split existing direct sun visibility so it is applied exactly once, then composite in linear HDR. | 6–12 engineer-weeks, highly uncertain; an engine shader/scene compiler or material-system prerequisite can be larger. Until all relevant casters and receivers are supported, retain full raster for the viewport, not silently missing RT geometry. |
| Noise, settings and qualification (VQ/UI/PT/DT) | Add temporal/spatial reconstruction only when necessary and validated. Supply correctly aligned depth, normals, motion and material data; reject disocclusions, moving/deformed objects and invalid history, reset on camera cuts, zoom, resize, world/aircraft swap and pause/resume. Compare raw, denoised and same-model higher-sample reference with independent convergence checks. Add default-off, saved requested/effective state and explicit failure reason only after the candidate proves useful. Verify disabled means no RT histories, AS, shader work or per-frame extraction; release only after matching editor/templates, export and actual target-host gates. | 4–8 engineer-weeks after the signal works, plus platform, package and pilot qualification; estimates overlap only with independent staff. |

A query shader that handles alpha cutouts must explicitly inspect candidate intersections, fetch the correct instance/material/UV and alpha texture, and accept or discard each candidate. A query does not automatically run the scene's Godot material shader. Keep a defined treatment of propeller blur discs, transparent glass/water, particles, smoke and weather; if these affect the chosen light or receiver and no correct treatment exists, keep the baseline rather than falsely labeling the view complete. Motion-vector availability and normal/roughness access in a compositor do not prove correct vectors for transparent, vertex-deformed or newly visible geometry. Existing DT-MAC work already records a real alpha-motion rejection; do not bypass it by copying opaque vectors.

### Budgets and acceptance

- **Proposed screening ceilings, not measurements:** at actual 1920×1080 output, cap the entire extra effect at **2.0 ms GPU p95** and **128 MiB incremental peak allocated GPU resources** on a named candidate GPU, including scene inputs, BLAS/TLAS, scratch, ray buffers, all history/denoising, compositor and in-flight frames. These are initial stop/review ceilings, not a promise or entitlement: the stricter measured VQ-07c whole-frame headroom wins. If headroom is absent, retain Off. Report transient build spikes and first-use compilation separately. Account for alignment, frame-ring duplication and SDK-private allocations where observable; allocations are not necessarily physical VRAM residency, and unified-memory allocations must not be double-counted as independent RAM.
- Use the [VQ protocol](../../plans/advanced-graphics.md#numerical-and-human-acceptance): at least three alternating A/B pairs, each with 10 s warmup and 60 s measured active flight, same source, engine, GPU/driver, settings, input/ticks, field, aircraft, camera, weather, daylight and output dimensions. Report GPU build/update, ray, denoise and composite cost separately where measurable, plus total CPU/GPU/frame distributions, memory, warmup, errors and exclusions. Check 60 Hz p95 ≤18 ms, p99 ≤25 ms, simulation/wall 0.99–1.01, and attribute recurring >50 ms intervals; report uncapped real renders against 16.67 ms. A 720p capture or synthesized frames do not establish the 1080p real-render target.
- Test pilot, chase, zoom and fast pans; a small high-contrast aircraft at 20/50/100 m and farther accepted ranges, rolling and low landing, propeller/hinge movement, ground contact and off-screen casters, moving alpha foliage/wind, day/dusk/night and affected smoke/water/transparent cases. Randomized matched clips and the owner/pilot readability gate must show no new repeatable loss of attitude, markings or contact cues. Validate history reset, ghost/trail and disocclusion behavior, not only stationary screenshots.
- Exercise failed/partial allocation, shader compilation, unsupported backend, device loss, generation changes, resize, minimize, switching settings, scene/aircraft replacement and GPU teardown. A failed or stale resource must never be sampled as success. Test at least 20 warm ownership cycles without unexplained growth; desktop release gates remain in their owning plan. Verify actual packaged builds on each advertised Windows/Linux/macOS backend and device. Unavailable devices remain unqualified.
- Preserve the simulator boundary: rendering reads accepted presentation transforms and `sim_clock`, never writes physics or invents a collider, and does not change 240 Hz simulation, inputs, aircraft data or golden flights. Existing exact/tolerance checks and replay are necessary but do not replace native image, latency or physical-display review.
