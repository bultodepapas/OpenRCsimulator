## Question and code findings

**Question:** Can the tutorial's BLAS/TLAS and shadow design be transferred to a moving OpenRC aircraft? **Decision:** Adopt its explicit geometry, instance, visibility and lifetime concepts. Do not transplant its static, synchronously waited sample loop, whole-scene path tracing, or sample numbers into flight rendering.

In [02_basic.cpp](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_raytracing_tutorial_KHR/blob/952a1a8e8b85f25781db7014d4d2bda124435e79/raytrace_tutorial/02_basic/02_basic.cpp#L752), `primitiveToGeometry` supplies vertex/index addresses and counts and marks the geometry opaque. One BLAS is built per mesh primitive. The [TLAS code](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_raytracing_tutorial_KHR/blob/952a1a8e8b85f25781db7014d4d2bda124435e79/raytrace_tutorial/02_basic/02_basic.cpp#L880) converts each instance's column-major matrix to Vulkan's row-major 3×4, points at the corresponding BLAS address, gives it a custom index and mask, then uploads the instance buffer. It is a concrete example of the mesh-versus-instance distinction; neither instance transform nor opacity classification may be guessed from a physics collider.

The [generic AS builder](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_raytracing_tutorial_KHR/blob/952a1a8e8b85f25781db7014d4d2bda124435e79/raytrace_tutorial/02_basic/02_basic.cpp#L783) queries sizes, aligns scratch to device properties, allocates, records `vkCmdBuildAccelerationStructuresKHR` in `BUILD` mode, **submits and waits**, then destroys scratch. Instance data is also held through its submitted build. On shutdown it waits for the queue before freeing structures and the SBT. This is intentionally simple educational code, not evidence that a per-frame queue wait is acceptable. Production needs explicit render-graph dependencies, reusable bounded scratch, in-flight ownership and deferred retirement after last GPU use; update/refit must be proved on the actual Godot version, not inferred from Vulkan flags.

The sample's [shadow helper](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_raytracing_tutorial_KHR/blob/952a1a8e8b85f25781db7014d4d2bda124435e79/common/shaders/shadow.h.slang#L40) biases the origin, selects finite point-light distance or an infinite directional distance, terminates at the first accepted hit, skips closest-hit but permits any-hit, and interprets the miss payload as visible. [05_shadow_miss](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_raytracing_tutorial_KHR/blob/952a1a8e8b85f25781db7014d4d2bda124435e79/raytrace_tutorial/05_shadow_miss/shaders/rtshadowmiss.slang#L110) uses a dedicated miss index and applies visibility to its light contribution. A GPU ray's epsilon, range and masks still need scene-scale validation. A nonopaque material requires actual material/UV handling; permitting any-hit does not implement OpenRC's Godot shader semantics.

The engineering dialogue matters: in [issue #98](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_raytracing_tutorial_KHR/issues/98), a reader reported an uninitialized Slang payload and artifacts on Intel DG2/Mesa; a maintainer [confirmed](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_raytracing_tutorial_KHR/issues/98#issuecomment-4601212256) and the issue records the fix. The [inspected shader](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_raytracing_tutorial_KHR/blob/952a1a8e8b85f25781db7014d4d2bda124435e79/raytrace_tutorial/02_basic/shaders/rtbasic.slang#L210) now initializes it. This does not independently reproduce the user's driver report; it shows why exact sample versions and deterministic payload tests matter.

## OpenRC action and stop

Map stable mesh geometry to cached BLAS only while source geometry is unchanged; track the current transform and visibility of [aircraft hinges](../../../../app/render/airplane.gd) and [propeller blur](../../../../app/render/propeller_blur.gd) in current TLAS instances. Mesh/LOD, cutout, deformation and off-camera caster changes require correct rebuild/update or rejection. [Treeline wind and alpha scissor](../../../../app/render/treeline.gdshader) cannot be treated as the sample's opaque triangles. Apply the result exactly once to isolated direct sun, never the final shaded image; preserve the [existing shadow controls](../../../../app/render/shadow_settings.gd).

Within [VQ-04b/VQ-07h](../../../plans/advanced-graphics.md) and [GDU](../../../plans/godot-4.8-upgrade.md), use the existing RT feasibility work. After a demonstrated visual defect and measured 1080p headroom, the two-day checkpoint is a known hit/miss and safe teardown on a named device. At two weeks, show a visible bounded opaque moving-occluder sun result with an off-camera caster or stop; an isolated witness does not qualify normal flight. A candidate must retain complete relevant scene coverage or full raster fallback, default Off, unchanged physics and matched flight measurement. The initial **proposed**, not measured, ceilings are 2.0 ms GPU p95 and 128 MiB incremental peak allocations for the whole effect; stricter whole-frame headroom controls. This investigation adds no engine change or standalone test harness and does not establish tutorial performance on OpenRC.
