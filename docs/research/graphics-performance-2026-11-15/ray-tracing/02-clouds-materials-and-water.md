## What could actually look better?

| Candidate | Specific visual opportunity and matched control | Costs and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Selected-sun visibility** | A moving aircraft or structure could cast a better resolved, off-camera or close-contact shadow than the current map on a proven problematic view. Compare current native/projected paths with a fixed opaque receiver, then the real pilot approach; check wing/ground separation and readable markings. | Best first *RT feasibility question*, not a guaranteed win. Scene extraction, off-screen casters, dynamic transforms, light integration, ray work and any denoise cost all count. Start only after a demonstrated defect and headroom; unsupported relevant scene content means retain raster. |
| **Lake and glossy aircraft reflections** | The static lake probe omits the moving flight aircraft; a reflection ray could find it when absent from screen space. Compare the probe, the **existing optional planar capture**, and an RT candidate at identical camera, wave, weather and exposure. For a Workshop aircraft, compare an existing probe/environment on actual paint. | A candidate must beat the already available planar path in useful appearance *and* total cost on the selected view. Water's transparent/custom shader, wave normals, camera, alpha materials, off-screen geometry and temporal stability make this a later, more complicated study. No claim that RT is faster than planar. |
| **Indirect lighting / GI** | A nearby structure might give a more coherent color bounce or occlusion on an aircraft in shade. Test a specific near-structure scene against current ambient/probes and relevant SSAO, then at ordinary pilot distance. | Low priority for a mostly outdoor scene until a visible defect is proven. Multiple ray samples, materials, emitted and direct light separation, noisy histories, ghosting and scene maintenance can exceed the likely pilot benefit. No full-scene GI or path-tracing promise. |
| **Cloud quality** | Improved cloud shape, backlighting, parallax or shadow coherence may make an overcast/sunset scene more convincing. Compare existing layer/relief/impostor paths and their current performance first. | A bounded volume raymarch is a separate atmospheric experiment; it is **not** a hardware triangle-RT feature and need not depend on RTX, NVIDIA, AMD, DLSS or a particular upscaler. Keep existing sky as fallback and stop when the pilot view or budget does not justify the cost. |
| **Texture and material fidelity** | Correct channel mapping, roughness, normal scale, mip/anisotropic filtering, and consistent linear reflection lighting can make existing paint, foam, water and foliage more credible, especially close up. | RT changes illumination/reflection visibility, **not texture texel density or source detail**. Test the simpler existing material controls before an RT shading system. Keep distant attitude, contrasting markings and stable edges more important than close-up sparkle. |

The existing [VQ-07 lake report](../../../plans/advanced-graphics.md) records a recognizable reflected aircraft with the optional planar trial, and only two valid historical 720p keyboard-flight cost pairs on an RTX 3090, about 2.0–2.1 ms higher **wall p95**. That prior, incomplete screen is not a 1080p GPU-time result, not a forecast for either vendor, and not a new observation in this research. The pilot-motion and repeated full-flight qualification remain open.

## Two different kinds of rays

Hardware triangle ray tracing accelerates intersections against a scene represented by bottom- and top-level acceleration structures (BLAS/TLAS); the application still supplies current geometry, instance transforms, material behavior and shading. A ray query returns candidate intersections to the invoking shader; it does not automatically run OpenRC's Godot material shader. An RT pipeline may instead use ray-generation, hit and miss stages. Godot's [4.7 RenderingDevice reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_renderingdevice.html) describes low-level, experimental facilities; the [exact-pin audit](../04-ray-tracing-in-godot.md#exact-version-and-technical-boundary) describes the query/SBT and build-interface risks. They are not a stock one-click OpenRC RT effect. First candidate: actual Forward+ **Vulkan** device with capability and known-hit tests, not a GPU-name assumption; Compatibility is the application default. NVIDIA and AMD need their own GPU/driver results.

Volume raymarching instead samples a density and lighting field along a view ray, accumulating scattering and extinction. It can run on ordinary shader hardware on either vendor and can be expensive even without triangle RT. The original authors' [Nubis 2017 presentation](https://advances.realtimerendering.com/s2017/Nubis%20-%20Authoring%20Realtime%20Volumetric%20Cloudscapes%20with%20the%20Decima%20Engine%20-%20Final%20.pdf) separates density, lighting, raymarching and composition; [Nubis³ (2023)](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/nubis-cubed) illustrates why immersive voxel clouds add modeling, memory and sampling work. Neither is an OpenRC benchmark or a reason to reproduce an entire external engine.

A material normal changes the shading frame, BRDF and potentially the direction of a reflected ray. A normal map **alone does not move triangle vertices, create silhouette detail, or change a geometric ray hit**. Vertex displacement, tessellation or another explicitly represented surface is needed for a different intersection. Tracing also does not repair a low-resolution albedo, wrong color space, missing normal/roughness map, incorrect mip or UV scale. Conversely, changing texture resolution cannot fix a missing off-screen object or stale acceleration structure.

## Existing source and the missing representations

**Sky and clouds.** [sky.gdshader](../../../../app/render/sky.gdshader#L199) composes up to eight altitude-ordered projected density layers, horizon fade, analytic directional relief and sun/moon terms. Relief is background-only and excluded from the cubemap pass; the shader deliberately has different background/cubemap treatment. [storm_bank.gd](../../../../app/render/weather/storm_bank.gd#L1) builds at most 30 localized textured puff cards plus rain, and [its shader](../../../../app/render/weather/storm_bank.gdshader#L1) faces each card toward the camera and writes alpha. These are useful approximations with parallax and time/lighting behavior, not a three-dimensional participating medium. There is no basis here to claim the current sky looks flat or that any proposed cloud improvement has been seen.

[Atmosphere](../../../../app/render/atmosphere.gd#L232) resolves layer offsets and selects a shadow layer; [cloud_transport.gd](../../../../app/render/weather/cloud_transport.gd#L1) advances presentation-only offsets from simulation time. [ground.gdshader](../../../../app/render/ground.gdshader#L313) and [near_grass.gdshader](../../../../app/render/near_grass.gdshader#L65) project that cloud density toward the resolved directional light. Ground's `light()` attenuates its diffuse and the selected soil direct specular; near grass attenuates directional diffuse. The [shared tile cache](../../../../app/render/cloud_shadow_map.gd#L1) can substitute validated seed-specific RG16F noise, with analytic fallback; it is not itself a complete universal cloud-shadow solution. Other materials, water and aircraft need an explicit receiver audit. The selected deck projection is an approximation and does not automatically integrate all visible layers or the separate storm cards.

**Lighting exactly once.** For the chosen light, the desired conceptual direct contribution is `L_sun_direct × V_geometry × T_cloud`, where `V_geometry` includes the existing shadow-opacity/fade policy; diffuse and direct specular both participate. A raw blocked ray removes all direct sun only at full shadow opacity without fade. Preserve separate ambient/IBL, emissive light, other lights, sky and fog. Where a material already applies `T_cloud`, the new path must not apply that same transmission a second time. The current ground and near-grass `light()` functions apply that projected transmission to **every** `LIGHT_IS_DIRECTIONAL`, not a verified Sun identity. Preserve the existing second directional light's baseline during this bounded change and test a lightning-like second directional light explicitly. Replace the designated sun's existing raster visibility inside the supported region rather than multiplying raster and RT shadows or darkening the final frame. Verify real light identity and renderer seam: a directional light is not necessarily the sun, and a post-opaque combined color is not a separate sun buffer. [10](../10-reflex-and-ray-integration-design.md#2-direct-sun-ray-tracing-real-seams-and-limits) explains the pinned Forward+ seam and prepass dependencies; check its exact code before patching.

For reflections, a geometric miss must resolve a **specified environment radiance** at the relevant time/direction, with consistent cloud, sun, fog, linear color and exposure policy. Sampling a stale probe or cubemap will not automatically match the on-screen background: cloud relief is omitted there and camera-facing storm cards are separate scene geometry. Conversely, tracing those cards as opaque triangles would falsely turn transparent clouds into solid occluders. Choose a coherent shared sky evaluation or explicitly bounded approximation, test its energy once, and do not overlay a second cloud/sun after an environment sample that already contains it. Cloud effects on a reflected path and on surface-to-sun transmission are different light paths; each path's chosen transmission is applied once, not once per available system.

**Water.** [water.gdshader](../../../../app/render/lake/water.gdshader#L106) displaces vertices vertically using the shared analytic lake waves and `sim_clock`, then computes fragment normals from their slopes. It reads screen/depth to form depth tint and bounded refraction, sets dielectric metallic/roughness/specular and `ALPHA = 1`, and when enabled replaces `RADIANCE` with linear-HDR planar capture while retaining engine Fresnel and direct sun. Actual wave geometry and its shading normal are different data: a ray built from the undisplaced mesh can intersect the wrong surface even if the fragment normal is correct. Use a stable geometric origin/bias and intended shading normal, test self-intersection, and do not feed render approximations into lake physics.

The [probe](../../../../app/render/lake/lake_reflection.gd#L14) refreshes on lighting or requested sky edits and excludes the moving flight aircraft and water. The optional [planar path](../../../../app/render/lake/lake_planar_reflection.gd#L29) is High-only, generally half-size with a cap, captures the aircraft, uses a linear exposure-one environment, and disables itself when the receiver/camera is ineligible. It clears the probe's receiver mask during active capture to prevent a later probe override. Any RT result must likewise select one reflection radiance source at a time, keep the probe fallback, and avoid applying exposure or sun twice. A reflection miss alone cannot shade a hit: direct light, material textures, roughness/normal, emission and the visibility of relevant off-screen scene objects must be defined too.

Godot's [4.7 screen-reading reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/shaders/screen-reading_shaders.html) describes nonlinear depth requiring inverse projection and normal/roughness available only in Forward+ for objects in the depth prepass. The water source branches explicitly between OpenGL and RenderingDevice NDC depth; test the selected pin's reverse-Z, pixel extent, MSAA and coordinate conventions. [Spatial shader documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/spatial_shader.html) warns that writing `ALPHA` selects the transparent pipeline. Water also requests `depth_draw_always`, so do not infer its exact availability in a particular prepass/compositor from that general statement. The exact-pin [SSR review](../../advanced-graphics-2026-10-21/round-2.md#r11--lake-reflections-and-the-changed-ssr-path) identifies a prior-frame path: test water, canopy, smoke and prop-disc ordering on the actual candidate, not a blanket transparency rule. A screen-space ray can only search the represented depth/color view; the [AMD SSSR 1.5 documentation](https://gpuopen.com/manuals/fidelityfx_sdk/techniques/stochastic-screen-space-reflections/) uses an environment fallback for misses.

**Materials and scene updates.** [vehicle_paint.gd](../../../../app/render/vehicle_paint.gd#L140) already handles albedo, metallic, roughness, clearcoat, emission and optional detail maps, with anisotropic mip filtering; low detail intentionally removes some maps and retains an adjusted mean sheen. Ground has its own normal perturbation and roughness; water has a view-space wave normal; [treeline.gdshader](../../../../app/render/treeline.gdshader#L40) uses alpha-scissored cards, mip-dependent alpha, wind deformation and a deliberately shaped crown normal. [Windsock fabric](../../../../app/render/windsock_fabric.gdshader#L1) deforms vertices and changes normal/tangent in the shader; the [propeller blur](../../../../app/render/propeller_blur.gd#L1) transitions between originals, copies and a transparent, non-shadow-casting disc. The [storm shader](../../../../app/render/weather/storm_bank.gdshader#L1), aircraft lights and reflective canopy introduce additional transparency/emission policies. A ray hit group or query must translate the material/UV/cull/alpha test, mip and dynamic shader parameters that matter to the effect; a TLAS of undeformed imported triangles is not equivalent. The treeline's raster `fwidth` coverage rule has no automatic ray-hit equivalent: choose and validate a ray footprint and mip policy, including distant cards, rather than copying its threshold alone. Rigid transform changes differ from vertex deformation, topology/LOD or visibility changes. Current pin's available update operations must be proven; do not assume refit/compaction just because a flag exists.

For cutouts, inspect each non-opaque candidate with the right instance, UV, texture and threshold, confirm/reject it, and continue traversal as appropriate. Do not mark leaf cards or a prop blur as solid merely to save any-hit work. [NVIDIA’s 2020 RT guide](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/best-practices-using-nvidia-rtx-ray-tracing/) and [AMD](https://gpuopen.com/learn/rdna-performance-guide/) both discuss reducing unnecessary work and treating truly opaque geometry efficiently; neither vendor's advice permits falsely labeling transparent geometry opaque. A bounded fixture may explicitly exclude it, but an unsupported relevant caster/receiver in normal flight requires full viewport raster fallback. Preserve off-camera casters, MultiMesh instances, layer masks, hinge updates, resource retirement and instance generation. Sky is better treated as an environment/miss policy than a giant overlapping TLAS sphere.

## Paired scenes and falsifiable oracles

Use real 1920×1080 output first. Freeze candidate/control source, engine/templates, renderer/API, GPU/driver, scene, aircraft/skin, quality, weather/time, view/FOV, exposure, wave/wind phase and recorded inputs. Lock unrelated settings. On one available eligible NVIDIA or AMD configuration, screen the chosen defect; repeat on the other vendor only when a candidate warrants qualification. Do not infer either result from brand or from a software renderer. Use randomized labeled A/B clips and the existing human pilot/owner circuit in addition to numeric image or hit tests.

1. **Pilot-distance flight and landing:** same aircraft and bright/dark background, 20/50/100 m and farther accepted ranges, rolling/turning, low landing and off-camera caster. Compare baseline shadow and candidate with frozen cloud phase, then moving cloud/wind. Record projected aircraft size, contrast and ability to identify attitude, markings, ground height and contact; reject repeatable losses, detached shadows, flicker, noise or trails.
2. **Workshop close-up:** same aircraft skin under controlled light and moving view, then pilot-distance repeat. Compare paint/film/metal/clearcoat roughness, normal detail and emission. Change a single material or reflection parameter at a time; judge texture scale, stable markings, highlight width, shading seams and no clipping, not the visual sophistication of a still alone.
3. **Sunset lake:** compare probe, planar and candidate with aircraft entering and leaving the main screen, rolling, wave phase, shore and grazing view held equal. Check reflected silhouette placement, Fresnel, cloud/sun environment, horizon and linear exposure. Inspect motion, edge exits, clipping, disocclusions, camera offsets and transitions to/from the probe; compare cost including the planar viewport and candidate's full scene resources.
4. **Canopy/foliage landing:** flight against treeline, wind, aircraft canopy and prop blur. Inspect alpha holes, normals, LOD switches, shadows and readability across the actual path. Treat transparent canopy/prop and deformation as explicit coverage questions; if not supported, the normal-flight RT image does not pass.

An opaque fixture first checks known triangle hit, miss, distance and instance identity against an independent CPU reference; vary parameters to catch cross-driver packing bugs. Then test raw `V=1` and `V=0` with nonzero ambient, emission, a second directional light, direct specular, cloud transmission and shadow opacity/fade; only the selected sun's direct components change according to its effective visibility policy. For a future reflection fixture, compare hit identity/distance, known roughness and normal cases, a stable higher-sample reference and defined miss environment; record raw and filtered results separately. Temporal methods need correct depth, normal, motion, roughness, disocclusion and invalidation data; moving shadow history can disagree even when receiver motion is correct. [AMD's Denoiser 1.3 reference](https://gpuopen.com/manuals/fidelityfx_sdk/techniques/denoiser/) illustrates these dependencies, not a drop-in installation or vendor-specific requirement.

Existing starting points are [test_cloud_shadow_map.gd](../../../../app/tests/test_cloud_shadow_map.gd), [test_cloud_transport.gd](../../../../app/tests/test_cloud_transport.gd), [test_sky_relief_layers.gd](../../../../app/tests/test_sky_relief_layers.gd), [test_storm_bank.gd](../../../../app/tests/test_storm_bank.gd), [test_lake_planar_reflection.gd](../../../../app/tests/test_lake_planar_reflection.gd), [test_lake_probe_ambient.gd](../../../../app/tests/test_lake_probe_ambient.gd), [test_propeller_blur.gd](../../../../app/tests/test_propeller_blur.gd) and [test_tree_wind.gd](../../../../app/tests/test_tree_wind.gd). They cover their existing contracts, not an RT effect. Reuse the [atmosphere capture/probe tools](../../../../tools/atmosphere/README.md), [graphics area checkers and frame diagnosis](../../../tools/graphics.md), [debug bridge](../../../../tools/debug/README.md) and existing VQ/lake evidence; add a focused oracle only when an implementation creates the missing boundary. Headless checks and llvmpipe are not native performance or visual qualification. No checks were run as part of this source-only document.

## Cost gate and stop rule

M0 remains **five engineering days total**, using existing diagnostics and one native screen; it is not permission to build another profiler. Only after a reproduced defect and 1080p headroom, make the existing **two-day RT feasibility decision**: exact device/backend features, working triangle/parameter oracle, selected-sun insertion seam and scene/resource lifetime. If a required seam or proof is absent, retain raster/defer. By the two-week checkpoint, show a useful, correctly lit **opaque fixture** with explicit missing coverage and cost, or stop. This is not a promise of full flight; moving scene integration and each additional device/OS remain separate work and qualification.

At 1080p, the existing proposed screening ceilings are **≤2.0 ms incremental GPU p95 and ≤128 MiB peak incremental allocated GPU resources for the entire effect**, not measurements or guaranteed allowances; stricter measured whole-frame headroom wins. Count extraction, BLAS/TLAS, scratch, updates, rays, denoising/history, compositor and in-flight duplication; report build spikes, memory observability and allocation versus residency. The opaque-fixture checkpoint includes a bounded fixture-specific cost screen. For a later normal-flight candidate after complete relevant coverage, use the existing VQ protocol: at least three alternating native A/B pairs, each 10 seconds warmup plus 60 seconds measured active flight; report whole-frame CPU/GPU distributions separately from the 60 Hz **process-loop** p95 ≤18 ms, p99 ≤25 ms gates, simulation/wall 0.99–1.01, recurring >50 ms intervals and real-render throughput. Match pilot/readability gates and preserve 240 Hz float64 simulation, accepted inputs and `sim_clock`. If benefit, required data, timing, allocation or safety of resource lifetime is unproven, keep the current scalable raster path. Higher resolution follows 1080p; 720p is later compatibility evidence.

Do not add a universal RT toggle, claim supported NVIDIA/AMD models, sell volumetric clouds as RT-core-only, double-apply shadows/exposure, force opaque foliage, replace the lake physics mesh with a shading approximation, or promise a full-flight or frame-rate gain from this research.
