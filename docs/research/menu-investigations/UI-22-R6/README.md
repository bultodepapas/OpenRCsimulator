# UI-22-R6 — twenty investigations for restrained crystal UI

2026-10-24. Owner: product shell. Baseline: `8c10ee55`; verified UI source also present in `16b87e98` at completion. Godot 4.7.2, native Windows, Compatibility renderer. This is the owner's requested research and implemented refinement of the radioactive lime accent. The [menu guide](../../../MENU-REDESIGN-PLAN.md#color-and-surfaces) owns current behavior.

## Direction and method

Keep neutral black translucent backgrounds. Concentrate the radioactive lime on actions and selection, with readable native text and focus. Give glass an apparent thickness through light at its contour, then let motion settle. “AAA 2026” is an ambition for craft and consistency, not a measurable certification or a universal style trend.

The twenty investigations below opened distinct sources, rather than counting search queries. Game cases inform art direction; official engine documentation informs feasibility; accessibility sources constrain the result. Most visual examples were assessed through the authors' descriptions. The lead also inspected the actual NBA exploration sheet and Returnal gameplay image linked below. Those are respectively explorations and a HUD example, not evidence that the shipped menus match our treatment. No commercial artwork is included in the application.

| 13 | [Godot 4.7 CanvasItem shaders](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/canvas_item_shader.html) | The existing unshaded canvas shader can compute its own decorative lighting while preserving alpha. **Retain:** a noninteractive shader surface behind native content. Do not add Light2D/normal-map dependencies or distort labels. |
| 14 | [Godot 4.7 environment and post-processing](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/3d/environment_and_post_processing.html) | Glow is a broader rendering treatment whose scale and rendering setup affect the resulting haze and cost. **Skip:** enabling viewport glow to style a menu accent. ## 15–20: material, motion and usability

| # | Inspected primary source | Finding and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | Apple design team: [Meet Liquid Glass, WWDC25](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/), transcript, especially Dynamics and Principles | Layered highlights, shadows and light in response to interaction communicate a material's presence. **Interpret for OpenRC:** a narrow directional bevel and a small lower-edge light return suggest thickness. This is stylized lighting, not Apple's implementation, lensing or a physically simulated caustic. Neutral backdrops take priority over ambient color spill. |
| 16 | IBM: [Carbon motion](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/motion/overview), updated 2026-08-12 | Productive and expressive motion have different roles; expressive moments should be selective. **Apply:** retain immediate native feedback, with a finite primary-action glint. Avoid bouncing layouts, continuous pulses or motion added to every row. No Carbon package is installed. |
| 17 | Microsoft: [XAG 102 — Contrast](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102) | Contrast on a varying background must consider its least favorable area. **Retain and check:** dark primary text against the darkest gradient/press endpoint, plus native focus against the luminous fill. Visual comfort also depends on user choices; this is not a full accessibility conformance claim. |
| 18 | Microsoft: [XAG 117 — Visual distractions and motion settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117) | Motion behind stationary text can still distract; users benefit from stopping motion and opaque backdrops. **Retain:** existing reduced-motion and opaque-panel options, with live cancellation of active decoration. No idle animated grain, moving background tint or flashing edges. |
| 19 | W3C: [Understanding WCAG 2.2 SC 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) | Nonessential interaction-triggered movement should be disableable. **Apply as a transferable principle:** the sheen remains optional and the static material still communicates the control state. WCAG's “AAA” level is unrelated to the user's AAA-game quality request. |
| 20 | Google: [Material Design 1 duration and easing](https://m1.material.io/motion/duration-easing.html), historical guidance | Desktop state changes should feel short and responsive, with timing fitted to travel and purpose. **Apply:** keep 90/180 ms press/release shading and immediate input; shorten the separate decorative sweep from 720 to 560 ms with sine ease-in/out. The 560 ms value is our tuning, not Google's recommendation for an interaction transition. |

## Implemented selection

- A continuous convex highlight replaces the abrupt halfway band, preserving the saturated lime body.
- A narrow directional bevel follows straight edges and corners. A faint inset light return adds apparent thickness near the bottom of primary buttons.
- The finite sweep is narrower and brighter at the contour, with less light across the label area. It eases across the surface in 560 ms, without holding input.
- The native top stroke is one pixel. Native text, focus, disabled appearance, layout, event routing and bounded halo remain authoritative.
- Panels receive smaller neutral reflections; their alpha and the opaque-panel preference are unchanged. No textures, particle systems, wall-clock shader, screen-copy pass or runtime dependency was added.

The source is [crystal.gdshader](../../../../app/ui/shaders/crystal.gdshader), [crystal_surface.gd](../../../../app/ui/crystal_surface.gd) and [ui_theme.gd](../../../../app/ui/ui_theme.gd). These are intentional aesthetic choices informed by the sources, not reproductions of a commercial game or proof of realistic glass optics.
