# Turbo Timber Evolution propulsion reference

**Status:** Research note, 2026-10-10. Official product literature and the Timber integration evidence were reviewed; no simulator data or code changed. **Serves:** ROADMAP M4b EL1/EL5 and Timber TT-08/TT-16.

## Candidate hardware baseline

The two motor part numbers share published 800 Kv and 14-pole ratings, but are different products. Do not treat those matching nameplate values as proof that their winding resistance, no-load current, rotor inertia, or installed performance match. The 40 g differences between the old and B manual mass references are published differences; the documents do not attribute that entire change to one component.

There is a manufacturer-document discrepancy for the legacy propeller: its manual calls EFL5962 11 × 7.5, while the B manual and the current EFL5962 product listing say 11 × 7.3. The current B candidate below uses the latter specification. If researching an unsuffixed aircraft, verify the physical propeller marking and exact part in hand before selecting a map; the literature alone does not resolve whether the old entry is a typo or a hardware change.

The B manual recommends a 14.8 V, 3200 mAh, 30C 4S LiPo; its parts list identifies `SPMX32004S30`. Product pages allow 3S or 4S, 2200–5000 mAh packs, so the simulator must select one pack rather than mix that range into a single data set. Horizon's `SPMX32004S30` listing was marked discontinued when checked; use it only if that exact pack is in hand, otherwise identify and measure the replacement pack explicitly. The manual's 2.190 kg with-battery minus 1.860 kg without-battery reference implies 330 g by subtraction; it is not a direct pack weighing. The cited pack listing does not establish its as-flown mass or loaded voltage.

**Selected manual-backed reference configuration (not a claim about the supplied asset's revision):** B-revision BNF Basic `EFL105250B`, listed in stock on Horizon's product page when checked on 2026-10-10, with motor `SPM-1012`, ESC `SPMXAE70E`, prop `EFL5962` 11 × 7.3 three-blade, and the manual-recommended 4S 3200 mAh 30C pack class. Use `SPMX32004S30` only if that exact pack is available; otherwise record the replacement pack's exact ID. Set wheels installed, floats and slats off, flaps retracted, and use the plan's in-air start. Record actual identifiers and weigh this exact configuration. The BNF includes an AR637TA: SAFE Select may be disabled, but AS3X remains active. Thus a no-assist simulator baseline is not a stock BNF flight configuration; for an unassisted physical comparison use a PNP/receiver setup without stabilization, or model the BNF receiver as a separate control layer. No popularity or sales ranking is inferred from the product page's stock status.

This is a measurement target, not a ready-to-fly parameter set. TT-00-R1 found the supplied r1 asset does not identify the exact commercial hardware revision and reports its motor, propeller, battery, and mass as estimates. Do not infer B-suffix identity from the mesh or use r1's mass ledger as flight data. The B manual prints one mass pair without stating whether it includes the BNF receiver, so confirm the exact BNF airframe mass and loadout directly.

## Evidence boundary for EL1 and Timber

| Reusable EL1 fixture | Timber physical validation |
| --- | --- |
| A named electric Stik or isolated mathematical fixture can check the shared motor/ESC/battery equations against hand calculations, shaft equilibrium, trim, numerical convergence, and reset behavior. Any deliberately synthetic constants must remain marked as synthetic in that fixture. A passing fixture proves implementation behavior for that fixture. | A Timber claim requires the exact installed hardware and loadout above, measured motor/ESC/propeller/battery data, mass/CG/inertia evidence, and matched flight observations. An electric Stik result, APC two-blade table, or synthetic fixture does not validate the Timber's three-blade EFL5962 installation. Static EL5 agreement validates a static operating point only; it does not validate aircraft aerodynamics or handling. |

No numerical Timber values are supplied here for motor resistance `R`, no-load current `I0`, motor/propeller rotational inertia, or propeller `Ct`/`Cp`. Horizon's cited literature publishes motor Kv/pole count, ESC rating/cell range, and nominal propeller size, but no such measured motor constants, inertia, or thrust/torque maps. Its 70 A ESC rating is not an installed-system current measurement. The existing plan correctly treats nominal 14.8 V as distinct from loaded pack voltage and requires the selected propeller's own load data.

## Missing evidence and measurement recipe

| Item | Published evidence | Required evidence before Timber propulsion data can be treated as physical |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact revision and motor/ESC/prop IDs | Product/manual parts lists distinguish the SKUs, but TT-00-R1 does not map its visual asset to one. Legacy and B manuals disagree on EFL5962 pitch. | Inspect the actual carton/airframe and component labels; record exact model and component IDs plus propeller embossing. Keep the configuration fixed in every trace. |
| Motor `R`, `I0`, and effective `Kv` | 800 Kv is nominally published for both motor revisions. `R` and `I0` are not published. The B replacement motor page publishes 2815, 800 Kv, and 14 poles only. | Follow the EL1 equivalent-motor convention. Four-wire phase-pair resistance measurements at recorded winding temperature must use the model's declared equivalent-resistance convention; establish winding connection before any conversion. With the propeller removed, record voltage, current, and rpm across several free-running points, and separate ESC/controller loss from motor no-load loss or label the fitted constants as a combined effective model. Cross-check the fitted speed constant against the nameplate. Drela's [motor note](https://web.mit.edu/drela/Public/web/qprop/motor1_theory.pdf) describes the equivalent `Kv`, `R`, and zero-torque-current measurements. |
| Motor and propeller rotational inertia | Not published in the cited product literature or Timber delivery. | Measure motor rotor and propeller inertia separately or together with a documented pendulum/torque-acceleration method; state the reference axis and uncertainty. Do not derive it from the mesh without validated mass/material distribution. Airframe inertia remains a separate TT-07/TT-16 requirement. |
| Propeller thrust and torque (`Ct`, `Cp`) | The B manual and replacement listing provide nominal three-blade 11 × 7.3 dimensions, not a performance curve. | On a calibrated stand, measure axial thrust, shaft torque, rpm, bus voltage/current, and ambient pressure/temperature using the actual propeller. Static runs establish only `J = 0`. For advancing-flow data, repeat in a tunnel or controlled axial-flow rig over the intended advance-ratio range. Use measured diameter and density; compute `n = rpm/60`, `P = 2πnQ`, `Ct = T/(ρ n² D⁴)`, `Cp = P/(ρ n³ D⁵)`, and `J = V/(nD)`. These are the UIUC [standard coefficient definitions](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/propDB.html); a different propeller's table is not a substitute. |
| ESC command and installed response | The B ESC is specified as 70 A, 3S–6S; no Timber throttle-to-shaft map or throttle-step response is published. | With the exact motor/ESC/prop, log throttle command, bus voltage/current, rpm, thrust, and torque synchronously through a low-to-high and high-to-low throttle step. Record ESC settings, pack identity/state, temperatures, and test conditions. This measures the installed static system response, including whatever ESC effects the test does not separately isolate. |
| Battery state and sag | Recommended type/capacity/C rating and connector; no as-flown pack mass, internal resistance, or voltage-vs-SOC/temperature curve in the cited Timber manual. | Weigh and locate the exact pack; log cell and bus voltage/current through repeatable load steps at recorded temperature and state of charge. EL1's ideal battery may support implementation tests, but EL2 needs these battery observations. |
| Whole-aircraft properties and flight response | Manual CG is 60 ± 5 mm behind the wing-root leading edge for the documented slat-off setup; no inertia tensor or matched flight log is supplied. Published mass is not configuration-resolved by BNF/PNP. | Weigh the selected loadout, measure CG at the defined datum, measure airframe inertia independently, and collect matched flight/telemetry traces (throttle, rpm, voltage/current, airspeed or GPS, and attitude/controls). These are required alongside the propulsion bench data before TT-16 can make a physical validation claim. |

For the bench sweep, use the exact stock powertrain and propeller on a rigid, calibrated fixture with thrust and torque transducers, an optical tachometer, and a wattmeter at the battery/ESC input. Record ambient pressure and temperature, pack identity/state, motor and ESC temperature, and the control signal. Take repeatable static points from idle through the operating range and log synchronized steady values; add throttle steps to capture response. Calibrate all sensors and repeat a point to quantify measurement scatter. A static sweep is not a substitute for nonzero-`J` propeller data or flight validation.

The manufacturer manuals/products and the incoming r1 audit were checked on 2026-10-10. Product listings can change; implementation should retain the exact manuals, part-label observations, source hashes, test conditions, and raw bench files with its own EL/TT evidence step.
