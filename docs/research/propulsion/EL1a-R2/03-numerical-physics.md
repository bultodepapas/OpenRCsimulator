# EL1a-R2 — Numerical physics for electric propulsion

2026-10-10 · **Status:** primary-source review complete; numerical model choices and acceptance checks remain planned. Scope: EL1c–d, EL1f, EL2a–b. Sources accessed 2026-10-10. Read with the [electric propulsion plan](../../../ELECTRIC-PROPULSION-PLAN.md).

## 07 — When is an algebraic motor current accurate enough?

**Decision question.** Does EL1's memoryless motor law resolve an electric transient, or does the simulator need a winding-current state?

**Verified findings.** A DC-equivalent winding obeys `L di/dt + R i + ke ω = V`; speed obeys `J dω/dt = kt i − bω − Qload`. UC San Diego's note gives these equations and `τe=L/R`. Its zero-inductance limit sets `i=(V−keω)/R`. **Our linearization** of the mechanical equation gives `τm=J/(b + kt ke/R + dQload/dω)` when the denominator is positive; without viscous friction or load slope, this is `J R/(kt ke)`. [U1] [U2]

PWM averaging and zero-inductance elimination are separate: duty averaging drops switching detail; algebraic current also drops `τe`. Record `h/τe`, `h/τm`, and coupled eigenvalues; the app's 240 Hz tick is `h≈4.17 ms`. Retained current means RK4 must resolve its fastest mode; eliminated current means its transient must lie outside the accepted bandwidth. With frozen speed and voltage, `i∞=(V−keω)/R` and `i1=i∞+(i0−i∞)exp(−h/τe)` are exact. Changing speed requires an exponential convolution, so that frozen-speed update is not an exact coupled step. [U1] [U2]

**EL1c–d decision and rejecting test.** Keep EL1a's zero-inductance averaged source until winding `L` and current-transient data exist; do not add an unidentified lag. [`Electric.evaluate`](../../../../app/physics/electric_motor.gd#L10) has no current state. Its [`_transient()` fixture](../../../../app/tests/test_electric_motor.gd#L166) tests mechanical RPM with algebraic current, not electrical settling. The coupled route still calls combustion [`Propulsion.shaft_rate`](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L1036); EL1d needs electric dispatch and a separate current-step oracle if that boundary changes. Reject claims that the RPM test validates inductance or PWM ripple. [L1] [L2]

## 08 — How should rotor momentum, body rate, and motor work be counted?

**Decision question.** Which torque belongs to the airframe, which belongs to relative rotor spin, and which angular velocity should define the shaft state?

**Verified findings.** NASA's aircraft equations include propulsor momentum `h=Irot Ω a` in body axes and body-rate cross terms. For this implementation, interpret `Ω` as body-relative shaft speed: absolute axial rate is `a·ω+Ω`, where `ω` is inertial body rate expressed in body axes. Then `Jlocked·ω` includes rotor inertia as if locked to the body, and `h` adds relative spin. The corresponding energy is `½ω·Jlockedω + Irot Ω(a·ω) + ½Irot Ω²`. [N1] [G1]

[`RotorCoupling.response`](../../../../app/physics/rotor_coupling.gd#L11) solves both accelerations; [`rigid_body.gd`](../../../../app/physics/rigid_body.gd#L125) applies `ω×(Jlockedω+h)` once. [`FlightSession._coupled_response()`](../../../../app/sim/flight_session.gd#L1036) supplies aerodynamic `−Qprop` as external moment and `Qmotor−Qprop` as relative rotor torque. Do not add a separate stator torque or gyro moment. This route is combustion-only today. [G1] [G2]

**EL1d decision and independent limiting-case oracle.** Keep body-frame relative speed and require positive carrier inertia after removing axial rotor inertia from `Jlocked`. For an axial test without aerodynamic load, expect `Ωdot=Q/[Irot(1−Irot/Jlocked)]`, `ωdot=−Irot Ωdot/Jlocked`, conserved momentum, and energy rate `QΩ`. EL1a's `_coupling()` already checks a closed-form axial acceleration; carry that oracle through the future electric session. With `Qmotor=Qprop`, verify zero absolute axial shaft acceleration, `Ωdot+a·ωdot=0`; body acceleration can make relative `Ωdot` nonzero. Keep aerodynamic `−Qprop` in the external moment exactly once. Existing [`test_rotor_coupling.gd`](../../../../app/tests/test_rotor_coupling.gd#L45) checks tilted work and momentum/energy over ten seconds. Extend this evidence through electric dispatch rather than replacing those tests. Inertia provenance remains a measurement limit. [G2] [G3]

## 09 — Should propulsion and battery curves stay linear or become PCHIP?

**Decision question.** Would a smoother interpolator improve RPM integration without inventing coefficient signs or data coverage?

**Verified findings.** [`Propulsion.coefficient()`](../../../../app/physics/propulsion.gd#L20) linearly interpolates, holds the first value for `J≤0`, extrapolates beyond the last knot, then applies floors. The loader requires finite values and increasing `J` from zero; static `Ct/Cp` must be positive, while shaft tables allow negative values for windmilling. Runtime lookup has no source-gap or Reynolds dimension. [`test_propulsion.gd`](../../../../app/tests/test_propulsion.gd#L37) checks negative high-`J` thrust and the windmill floor. [L1] [L2]

PCHIP preserves monotone data without overshoot and has continuous first derivatives; second derivatives can jump at knots. Fritsch–Carlson/Butland shape preservation does not supply missing observations or make a non-monotone curve monotone. UIUC reports measured coefficients at specific RPMs and sampled advance-ratio ranges; APC labels its geometry-based performance output as computational and includes software version/date. Source, RPM/Re conditions, gaps, and signs remain part of the model. [S1] [S2] [S3]

**EL1b/EL1f/EL2 decision and rejecting test.** Keep linear coefficient lookup. Preserve source knots and gap masks; reject queries beyond coverage unless an explicit extrapolation rule applies. Do not force `Ct/Cp` positive in windmill operation. Start future `OCV(SOC)` and pack-resistance curves with bounded linear lookup; test PCHIP only within a documented monotone segment. Reject a change if a coefficient sign crossing disappears, a gap is bridged, voltage/resistance becomes nonpositive, or held-out loads/voltage shift beyond uncertainty. No electric battery-table helper or Timber propeller map exists yet, so EL2 curve shape and EL1f coverage are unknown. [L1] [L3]
