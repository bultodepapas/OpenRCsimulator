**Findings and disposition.**

1. **Atmosphere availability, I04 — resolved in physics research.** Fixed-wing already supports a stage-position-sampled dry ISA `standard_column` as well as reference/custom uniform fields. `app/physics/atmosphere.gd:98-120`, `app/physics/wind_field.gd:178-190`, and `docs/WIND-ATMOSPHERE-REFERENCE.md:149` establish its altitude datum and −500…11000 m domain. Avoid claiming an extension is necessary for any altitude-varying air; an observed or richer moist sounding is separate future work.
2. **Competing boundaries, I05 — resolved in physics research.** `app/sim/vehicle_model.gd:77-85` permits one declared kernel-located event unless a model composes a policy; fixed-wing contact also has its own post-step/swept-contact behavior. An earliest-boundary rule for arbitrary combinations of ignition, source cutoff and contact is a proposed design and acceptance criterion, not a description of an already implemented generic arbiter. Tick-sampled requests/cuts and physically located events must be distinguished.
3. **Depth convention, I13 — resolved in graphics research.** Godot's 4.7 advanced-postprocessing page describes near=1/far=0 reverse-Z sampled depth and renderer-specific NDC reconstruction, while its own 4.7 renderer feature matrix describes Compatibility as 24-bit without reverse Z. Do not settle the apparent documentation conflict as a verified fact for the pinned 4.8-beta2 renderer. Preserve renderer-specific inverse-projection handling and require a near/far depth probe on each relevant renderer.
4. **Audio node count, I19 — resolved in audio research.** The proposed 14 Soundscape base + one installed dry + one wet + one transient = 17 is a scoped subsystem subtotal, not the entire flight scene. `app/audio/flight_audio.gd:71-77` also creates ground/mechanical audio, and `app/audio/ground_audio.gd:378-396` creates further players. Measure the full scene separately.
5. **Rotational example, I12 — resolved in graphics research.** At 1 m radius and 3 rad/s for 0.2 s, 0.6 m is arc path length. The chord displacement is about 0.591 m. The research now labels it path length.

**Integration attribution, I23 — resolved in integration research.** The previous plan already listed the propulsion runtime adapter. The new investigation correctly adds its separately computed fuel-event offset and callback whitelist; its wording now reflects that distinction.
