# CAM-09 — chase obstacle shortening and 3D follow

2026-10-25. Plan step CAM-09 ([camera plan §5.2](../../../plans/camera-views.md#52-chase-v2)), after [CAM-06/06b/28/14/18](../CAM-06-28/README.md). Checks ran on the shared VM (Godot 4.7.2, Mesa llvmpipe). They establish behaviour, not comfort or readability on the target display.

## What changed

| Part | Files | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clearance query | [camera_collision.gd](../../../../app/render/camera/camera_collision.gd) `free_fraction` | Sweeps a 0.4 m sphere from the aircraft to the camera against the scenery's collision proxies: the world the simulation collides with, through its own exact segment tests (`SceneryCollision._segment_proxy`). It walks the world's 32 m bins directly and rejects proxies by their bounds. 0.4 m covers the corner of a 0.25 m near plane at 55° vertical, 16:9 (0.36 m from the eye). Objects whose largest side is under 0.5 m do not count: the 18 runway stakes (12 cm) and small parts of parked models. They can neither hide the aircraft nor fill the view, and game cameras let such props through |
| Boom guard | `camera_collision.gd` `BoomGuard` | Pulls in at once. Lengthens only after 0.3 s of clear way, then eases out (half-life 0.35 s). This follows Cinemachine's deoccluder (minimum occlusion time, damping when occluded) and Unreal's spring-arm probe. A post swept past costs one pull-in, not a pumping boom |
| Chase | [chase_view.gd](../../../../app/render/camera/chase_view.gd) | A building, trunk, crown, vehicle, person or the flight-line barrier between the aircraft and the camera shortens the boom, to no less than 15 % of its length. The look-ahead shrinks with the boom, so the aircraft stays near the centre |
| Orbit | [orbit_view.gd](../../../../app/render/camera/orbit_view.gd) | Turned into a hangar or a tree, the eye comes in front of it; no closer than half the orbit's minimum distance |
| Fly-by | [flyby_view.gd](../../../../app/render/camera/flyby_view.gd) | A spot whose line of sight to the aim point is blocked is drawn again, alternating the mirror side, up to six times. Then it is pulled in front of the obstacle. Without a world, the draws and placements are those of CAM-14 |
| 3D follow | `chase_view.gd` `style`, [camera_rig.gd](../../../../app/render/camera/camera_rig.gd) `select_view` | A second chase style for aerobatics. Key **2** (or the controller's chase action) pressed again in the chase toggles it, and the panel names it "chase 3D". Settings and `--view` never toggle it. The boom rides a copy of the attitude damped with half-life 0.2 s and is lifted above the terrain. The picture's up is 0.7 × world up + 0.3 × the aircraft's up (`roll_follow` 0.3: Cinemachine's "lock to target" between no roll and full roll). On a vertical line it falls back to the aircraft's up. The look-ahead is 3D. Like the horizon chase (CAM-24), it runs in sub-steps of at most 1/240 s, with the attitude interpolated inside the frame |
| Routes | [main.gd](../../../../app/main.gd), [helicopter_main.gd](../../../../app/helicopter_main.gd) | Both keep a reference to the session's collision world (no copy) and pass `_camera_clearance` to the rig, including during the crash aftermath |
| Help | [controls_reference.gd](../../../../app/ui/controls_reference.gd), [es.po](../../../../app/i18n/es.po), [FIRST-LAUNCH](../../../FIRST-LAUNCH.md) | Views row: "chase (again: 3D)" |

### Deviations from the plan

- **Foliage counts.** The plan ignored tree crowns. The collision world's crowns, bushes and hedges are soft for the aircraft (`soft_foliage_impulse`), yet a camera inside one fills the screen with close leaf cards. The chase boom runs behind and above the aircraft, so foliage enters it only when the aircraft is in or at the tree; the guard's dwell keeps a crown swept past from pumping the boom.
- **Probe radius 0.4 m**, not 0.3 m: the near-plane corner above.
- **Bins instead of `CollisionWorld.nearby()`.** The pilot area's 32 m bins hold thousands of fence, barrier and people proxies, and `nearby()` duplicates each candidate: 41.7 µs per query, above the plan's 30 µs budget. Walking the bins with a bounds reject takes about 8 µs there.

Not done: the "air" and "north" chase alignments, the speed-dependent FOV, a Settings choice of `roll_follow` or of the 3D style as first view, and obstacles for the close-up view. The pilot view needs none: the pilot stands at the station.
