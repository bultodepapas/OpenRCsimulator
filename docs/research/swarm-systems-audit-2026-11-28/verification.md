## Source challenges and scope decisions

- The live Tree detail UI reaches `app_root.gd:2163,2285–2314`. Tree-only changes do not trigger the lens rebuild at lines 2381–2389. SUN-09 uses visible geometry for the pilot skyline and separately samples on-screen depth. A registered mesh is a valid component witness. The headless dummy renderer does not supply the real MultiMesh buffer, so screenshots, stock treeline appearance and on-screen depth are outside its scope. The fixed-eye fresh-skyline comparison and movement-triggered rebuild distinguish stale state from an intentionally permanent obstruction.
- The live Birds setting explicitly animates departures and supports later re-enable; `test_birds.gd:576–590` covers both. Once all birds are hidden, `birds_runtime.gd:68` still reaches `Birds.pending`, which must check every state. An environmental update still evaluates all species and instances. With every state HIDDEN, `Birds.write_buffer` only assigns visible_instance_count after scanning; it submits zero per-instance transforms or custom data. Construction under saved Off retains the ability to enable the layer immediately. Operation and object counts can establish an opportunity. Measured frame or GPU impact requires separate evidence. Prior A7/A24 and Round 1 contain no direct claim for this owner in the reviewed reports.
- HELI-04 explicitly documents the normalized inflow limit of 10⁶ as a numeric guard unrelated to aircraft calibration. At stock nominal speed the load component and inflow have distinct domains. Tiny nonzero thrust can exceed that normalized bound at ordinary dimensional crossflow, with signed and zero controls. The new result concerns the dimensional consequence of a known guard. A public coupled reference integrator receipt is stronger than an isolated kernel evaluation; synthetic near-null states do not measure stock flight incidence.
- A raw turbine throttle table endpoint up to 1 RPM above the maximum passes the loader tolerance. A positive spool derivative persists at the hard session maximum; the next stage may be rejected. Compare with exact endpoint on the same admitted state and distinguish the witnessed initial condition from a from-idle WOT flight. The custom mass-flow table is an extension of AV-14's documented low-RPM extrapolation. Limiting the authored knots can move negative mass flow into stock idle RPM, which conflicts with the documented nonnegative capture rate. This concerns accepted authored records, not untouched Avanti behavior.
