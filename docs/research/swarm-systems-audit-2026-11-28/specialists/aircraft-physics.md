# Aircraft physics

Reviewed source `8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b`. The experiments used the stock Vantage Jet and X-Cell records in pinned Godot 4.8.beta2 (`cafc17a19`) on Linux. The two turbine cases each change one table in a separate copy of the Vantage Jet record. Both go through the normal aircraft file loader. Session runs explicitly select the GDScript reference backend. The final turbine probe passed 34 checks, and the X-Cell probe passed 33.

## Full-throttle command can drive an admitted turbine beyond its state limit

The turbine loader accepts a throttle-map endpoint within one RPM of the stated maximum. The governor tracks that endpoint, while the RK stage rejects any shaft speed above the maximum. With the final ordinate changed from 154,000 to 154,000.5 RPM, a normal finite-fuel session started at its calculated 93,100.656303 RPM. Holding full throttle produced 1,235 accepted ticks (5.145833 s) before the next tick was refused. The last accepted speed was 153,999.994916 RPM. At that speed, the 0.4 s governor lag reaches the hard maximum after 4.04647 ms, inside the next 4.16667 ms tick. This follows directly from the exponential governor solution. The rejected tick preserved its tick number, body state and shaft speed. [Loader comparison](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b/app/physics/aircraft_data.gd#L2105) · [Governor demand](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b/app/physics/turbine.gd#L92) · [Stage state guard](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b/app/sim/flight_session.gd#L5407)

The unchanged record started at the same RPM, accepted all 2,400 ticks and reached 153,999.999997 RPM. An endpoint of 154,001.5 RPM failed file validation. Reject unreachable governor targets at load time, or canonicalize a deliberately tolerated rounding difference before the target can enter an accepted flight. Cover exact, just-above and just-below endpoints and run sustained full throttle from the normal start. The native finite-turbine source also compares against the strict maximum. Native execution remains unverified. [Native guard](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b/research/native-kernel/src/nk_liquid_propulsion.hpp#L283)

## Positive capture-map points can produce negative airflow at running idle

The loader checks that tabulated air mass flows are positive and RPM knots increase. It accepts an incomplete map whose first line segment extrapolates through zero inside the idle-to-maximum operating range. With the synthetic points (100,000 RPM, 0.10 kg/s) and (150,000 RPM, 0.20 kg/s), the straight line gives −0.012 kg/s at 44,000 RPM idle. At sea-level reference density and zero axial flow this reverses the sign of the reported gross force and of the lateral inlet reaction. The stock and custom records also passed real flight setup and the public runway start. At 44,000 RPM after one accepted idle tick, session telemetry reported +0.06571 kg/s and +1.84644 N for stock, and −0.012 kg/s and −1.84644 N for the custom map. The lateral-velocity reversal control gave equal and opposite lateral loads in all three component cases. Require positive, finite capture flow across the accepted running RPM range, including both extrapolated endpoints where extrapolation is supported. A strictly positive check matters because the nozzle calculation divides by reference mass flow. Add independent idle, knot and upper-end controls for any chosen endpoint policy. AV-14 had already identified negative extrapolation below roughly 6.7 RPM in the stock map. The new evidence concerns a loader-admitted custom record at ordinary running idle. It establishes software behavior for authored data. No measured jet, stock-idle failure or Workshop editing route was demonstrated. The native finite-turbine source repeats the extrapolation. ## X-Cell control case for the existing inflow domain

HELI-04 explicitly caps crossflow divided by ideal hover speed at one million. At 10 m/s crossflow, with the catalog 0.775 m radius and density 1.225 kg/m³, the limit corresponds to a thrust magnitude of 4.622951765×10⁻¹⁰ N. Exactly zero thrust has its own accepted branch. At 167 rad/s the isolated rotor returned signed thrusts of magnitude 1.706142373×10⁻¹¹ N at ±10⁻¹⁴ rad collective. The coupled rotor stage refused both. It accepted the exact zero and the two larger ±10⁻¹² rad controls. [Documented normalized domain](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b/docs/research/helicopter/HELI-04/README.md#L45) · [Conversion from dimensional thrust](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/8d4169f8febfd7d78a028eaa817414217a54f24b/app/physics/rotorcraft/inflow.gd#L54)

In a constructed full 19-state reference RK4 step at 240 Hz, the stock idle-up linkage delivered exactly zero collective from neutral sticks. At 10 m/s forward speed and zero prior wake, changing only vertical velocity from zero to either ±10⁻¹² m/s caused `aerodynamic_stage_refused`. Exactly zero and ±10⁻⁹ m/s accepted the step. The ordinary advance ratio was 0.077265, below the rotor's 0.15 indicator. This records a dimensional consequence of an existing guard, distinct from the low-RPM H02 pole and previously documented pure-sideflow jump. No occurrence rate in pilot flight was established.
