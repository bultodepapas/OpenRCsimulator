# Telemetry client second pass — 2026-11-22

This follow-up examines additional client delivery, input attribution and per-frame work at the original audited runtime baseline [`7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/commit/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464). The first-pass [audit](README.md) recorded 13 findings across the system; the two correctness findings below are additional, and the optimization opportunity is not counted as a measured performance defect. No runtime source or live service was changed; the live deployment was not inspected.

The error-only probe used actual service code, injected time and a bounded in-memory sender in an isolated project. The lead ran it headlessly on Linux with Godot `4.8.beta2.official.cafc17a19` through the checked runner: exit 0, no errors or warnings, capture `1d73c604-fcaf-4d68-abd9-a3a312022839`. The probe's success means it reproduced the baseline behavior, not that the behavior satisfies the proposed acceptance tests. The [probe](../../../../research/telemetry/audit-2026-11-22/round2/errors_only_idle_probe.gd) and [sanitized receipt](../../../../research/telemetry/audit-2026-11-22/round2/evidence/client-probe.json) support the result. Ground input attribution is source-traced and has not been reproduced through AppRoot. No target-hardware performance measurement was made.

## TA22-R2-F01 — Medium: error-only observations can wait indefinitely

**Condition and consequence.** After an opted-in session has delivered its last event batch, an error can be the only outstanding observation. If the game stays minimized, unfocused or otherwise unable to close a performance window, periodic processing does not independently seal that error. It can wait through arbitrarily many seal intervals; exiting or revoking consent then clears it. Software failures become undercounted even when telemetry remained enabled and the service was available for much longer than its ordinary batching interval. This does not assert that the receiver rejected a batch or that an error was transmitted after revocation.

**Source and reproduction.** [`note_error`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/telemetry/telemetry_service.gd#L149-L153) increments a bounded dictionary. The [process loop](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/telemetry/telemetry_service.gd#L188-L199) enters its timed seal only if `_pending` already has an event. [`_flush_errors_into_pending`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/telemetry/telemetry_service.gd#L399-L427) is reached by sealing, while [_stop](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/telemetry/telemetry_service.gd#L235-L250) clears the counter. An actual producer records `asset_load_failed` on a [flight preparation failure](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/app_root.gd#L1965-L1985).

The probe drained the initial accepted batch, recorded one error, then advanced the injected clock for 600 seconds while the context was ineligible. The counter remained 1, the total sends remained 1—the initial batch only—and there was no sealed or numbered counter batch. Status reported zero pending and the pending-data preview was empty; the existing [preview description](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/docs/plans/telemetry/consent.md#L40-L50) already explains that error counters enter the preview when a batch is sealed. After an eligible warmup event closed, the counter was sent. The probe also confirmed clearing on the off transition, no off-state send, and no counter replay after re-enabling.

**Confidence and boundary.** High for the service state sequence. This is not a claim about frequency of real player failures, a rendered minimize flow or a live receiver. The first-pass TA22-C05 discussed the differing player-facing and detailed lifetime descriptions; this finding is the distinct inability of error-only data to trigger delivery. Its loss on exit follows the intentional no-final-flush design, but the wait before exit is not bounded by the normal seal interval.

**Acceptance.** Give error-only observations a bounded opportunity to seal while admission is on, without sending while off or exceeding the existing queue and traffic budgets. Add a deterministic test for an empty queue plus an ineligible error across multiple seal intervals; verify a batch eventually contains exactly the bounded count. Test a full pending batch, multiple categories, queue expiry and budget exhaustion, then disable or suspend during each phase and verify cancellation, purge and no historical replay. Continue to document best-effort loss when the player quits before an eligible attempt completes.

## TA22-R2-F02 — Medium: ground input attribution follows the aircraft selection

**Condition and consequence.** Ground driving intentionally maintains its own controller selection. Product telemetry nevertheless reads the shared aircraft selection to label `input_class`, regardless of which activity is active. For example, if the shared flight selection is an RC radio but the car uses its locally selected gamepad or keyboard, ground activity is associated with `rc` rather than the car's selected control class. The same mismatch can occur in the other direction and biases the bounded input-mix field. This concerns the selected control class; it does not imply transmission of a device name or identifier.

**Source trace.** AppRoot [passes its shared registry to the new ground scene](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/app_root.gd#L920-L960). The ground scene then [creates a separate registry and selects an eligible driving gamepad](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/vehicles/ground/ground_scene.gd#L362-L381). Its garage [changes the local selection](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/vehicles/ground/ground_scene.gd#L405-L410); its [controls read that local registry](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/vehicles/ground/ground_scene.gd#L539-L579). In contrast, [AppRoot's telemetry projection](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/app_root.gd#L3269-L3269) identifies `ground_vehicle` as the activity and [classifies `controllers.registry` instead](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/app_root.gd#L3307-L3319).

An existing ground controller fixture sets the shared flight selection to a radio and verifies the [local gamepad selection is different](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/tests/test_controller_car.gd#L73-L88). It later chooses [local keyboard, drives, and verifies the shared radio selection is preserved](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/tests/test_controller_car.gd#L197-L215). The source trace combines those established selection semantics with AppRoot's projection; that fixture does not itself assert the telemetry context.

**Confidence and boundary.** High that the two owners can diverge and that the baseline projection reads the shared owner. The resulting AppRoot-to-wire behavior is source-traced, not newly exercised in a rendered or full-AppRoot test. This is different from first-pass TA22-C01, which concerns a window spanning a context change.

**Acceptance.** Project the active ground activity's authoritative local selection through the same finite allowlist, using `unknown` when ownership or state cannot be determined. Test shared radio/local gamepad, shared radio/local keyboard, shared gamepad/local keyboard, local gamepad changes and disconnects. Inspect the resulting AppRoot context and sealed batch while ensuring the shared aircraft selection and private device identifiers remain unchanged. Include the context-boundary requirements from TA22-C01 when changing input during a window.

## TA22-R2-O02 — Optimization opportunity: repeated per-frame projection work

**Observation.** A telemetry-enabled service invokes the context provider in every [_process](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/telemetry/telemetry_service.gd#L188-L193) via [`_observe`](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/telemetry/telemetry_service.gd#L260-L261). In an ordinary active fixed-wing flight, [AppRoot checks four properties](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/app_root.gd#L3269-L3285), each through [`get_property_list()` and a scan](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/app_root.gd#L5368-L5372). It builds a context dictionary and [classifies backend status](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/app_root.gd#L3293-L3319) every frame, although the activity accumulator [captures backend at activity or slice creation](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/telemetry/telemetry_service.gd#L328-L356). When the sender is idle, the same process loop invokes queue `begin`; its [expiry routine constructs a new array even for an empty queue](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/app/telemetry/telemetry_queue.gd#L59-L75).

The [verification plan](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/docs/plans/telemetry/verification.md#L89-L101) proposes fixed counters and no per-frame dictionary allocation, with an observer CPU p95 below 0.10 ms. Those are explicitly **unmeasured design targets**, not an enforced hard contract. The source establishes repeated operations, but not their cost, a failed p95, gameplay degradation or the benefit of a particular change.

**Measurement and acceptance.** Profile the actual observer and allocation sites in the approved, isolated, non-shipped qualification route. Follow the [target-hardware campaign](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/7940a4081e10994f34c858c4acac3d7a1a271464/docs/plans/telemetry/verification.md#L79-L101): at least five alternating off/on runs after a cold record, matched build, flight tape, renderer, settings, workload and power conditions, with variation and actual output/drawable/internal pixel sizes. Prove the on case generated and serialized events; separately verify the ordinary off path remains idle. Compare observer and seal p95 as well as the stated whole-frame criterion, and report inconclusive if noise obscures the effect.

Only then evaluate cached capability information, cheaper activity-boundary projection or an empty-queue fast path. Preserve correct immediate consent closure, original queue expiry and cancellation, and timely context/reset detection required by the first-pass findings; an optimization that merely postpones those checks is not equivalent.
