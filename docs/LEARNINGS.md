## Prove what the airplane actually does

- **A regression can preserve the defect.** Correct rudder signs and golden replay coexisted with extreme sideslip and persistent rotation. Expectations derived from the same coefficients cannot validate those coefficients. Use independent applicable data, inspect the complete trajectory and recovery, and label known-defect characterizations. [Rudder audit](research/ugly-stik-rudder-audit.md), [VAL-3](research/validation/VAL-3/README.md).

- **A scripted maneuver can measure its pilot instead of the aircraft.** A proportional pilot failed to reach its rotation target; another requested more lift than the wing could supply. Check the applied commands, achieved attitude or angle of attack, saturation and maneuver geometry before tuning the law to make a test pass. [P51-22 correction](research/p51-realism/P51-22/README.md#resolution--2026-10-16), [FT-01](research/flight-test/FT-01/README.md).

## Make evidence capable of rejecting a defect

- **Validate completion before interpreting a result.** A one-row flight and non-finite endpoints passed the old trace checker; timed-out captures could reuse stale images. Require the caller's requested duration, continuous finite samples, fresh artifacts and clean engine logs. A process exit code or a tool's PASS line alone does not establish success. [C7-R1](research/trace-integrity/C7-R1/README.md), [VQ-01a](research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-01a/README.md).

- **Show that the check reaches the intended failure.** Malformed-input probes appeared effective because their control already failed. First accept a valid control, then inject a bounded defect and verify the specific rejection; an earlier guard or unrelated script error is not that proof. For visual checks, prove the mask or ablation affects the active rendered path. [VAL-4](research/validation/VAL-4/README.md), [EL2a-P1](research/propulsion/EL2a-P1/README.md#verification-record), [L9c-R1 correction](research/visual-quality-implementation/L9c-R1/README.md#club-field-ablation-repair-2026-10-21).

- **Freeze the comparison, then distinguish preservation from correction.** Concurrent regeneration changed flight fingerprints during a refactor; an older flight-card baseline included unrelated ground changes. Use one identified source/data snapshot and isolated settings for paired runs. Complete state/load equality establishes preservation, including old defects; intentional physics changes need explained trajectories and the [flight logging workflow](FLIGHT-LOGGING.md). [H12](research/simulation-state/H12/README.md), [H13/H14](research/simulation-state/H13/README.md), [FT-04](research/flight-test/FT-04/README.md).

## Respect the boundaries between systems

- **Exercise the real session and its active branch.** A trace omitted trim, standalone contact tests omitted Main's lake wiring, and direct integrator probes bypassed crash detection. Test from the production entry point through inputs, model, committed state and consumers. A configured capability does not prove which physical branch ran. [E0a2b](research/aero-consistency/E0a2b/README.md), [GP-19](research/ground-physics/GP-19/evidence/dry-lake-scrape/README.md), [X8-07](research/skywalker-x8/X8-07/README.md).

- **The rigid body is not the whole transaction.** Wake, shaft, sampled forcing, RNG and discrete state can evolve outside the body array; an observer can reject after the kernel has committed. Define ownership, integration timing and rollback for the complete state, then test continuation after rejection. [H8](research/simulation-state/H8/README.md), [M5-W04a](research/wind-implementation/M5-W04a/README.md), [SF-03](research/simulation-foundation/SF-03/README.md).

- **Follow generated data and repairs to their actual consumers.** A generator's own freshness check accepted a rounded table that the loader refused. A report also retained a patch and passing logs while the runtime still used the old loader and the cited test source was missing. Verify generated output with the receiving loader/session, and confirm reported repairs and checks exist in the current tree. [EX-14h](research/extra-300-realism/EX-14/README.md#ex-14h--post-stall-wing-polar), [D1-R5 integration](research/aircraft-validation/D1-R5/integration/README.md).

## Measure under conditions that support the claim

- **A kernel timer is not the application cost.** Native load savings did not establish a whole-flight budget; accepted-tick observers later consumed about half the profiled script time outside the kernel timer. Profile the running app, measure evolving trajectories and include adapters, observers and cold-load effects. State whether a statistic describes individual ticks, batch averages or whole frames. [Gate P](research/simulation-state/Gate-P/README.md), [VQ-01c attribution](research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-01c/tick-observers-2026-10-27/README.md).

- **Scope a conclusion to what observed it.** Software-rendered captures establish pixels, not target-GPU timing or human attitude recognition. Archive inspection does not establish native launch or signing trust. Engine and renderer quirks remain tied to the tested version/backend until reproduced; a plausible source-level explanation is not shipped-path evidence. [L6c](research/visual-quality-implementation/L6c/README.md), [DT-00-R2](research/desktop-delivery-investigations/DT-00-R2/README.md), [SC-01](research/scenery-implementation/SC-01/README.md).

## Keeping this useful

Add a lesson only when an observed failure or surprising result changes a future implementation or verification decision and the existing guidance does not already cover it.

1. **Merge before adding.** Search this page and the three topic guides. Keep shared failure patterns here and specialized mechanisms in one topic guide; use topical headings, not dated appendices.
2. **Keep the actionable core.** State the trigger or cause, the consequence, what to do differently, and a linked evidence report with its step ID. Include version, backend or platform when the lesson depends on it. A short paragraph is usually enough.
3. **Replace superseded advice.** Keep the corrected recommendation and link the correction. Remove obsolete workarounds, generic rules already covered by AGENTS, repeated examples and routine results; retain valuable evidence in its owning report. Age alone does not make a lesson obsolete.
4. **Keep other records in their homes.** Status and next actions belong in the owning plan, measurements and reproduction steps in evidence, architectural choices in [DECISIONS](DECISIONS.md), and shipped changes in release notes. Do not copy commands, test counts, benchmark tables or machine inventories here.
5. **Check the edited document.** Verify local links and heading targets, and update incoming links when moving a lesson. Do not promote a report's historical claim into a statement about the current runtime without checking it.
