2026-10-06 · Revision 3 (English; revision 2 was in Spanish) · **P51-00 to P51-09, P51-12 and P51-13 done:** the P-51D flies with physics cross-checked against NACA flight data and RC-class data, with a shaft-balance engine, a calibrated propeller, P-factor, tail slipstream, a taildragger gear and a spanwise stall, and its envelope is flown by tests; it stays *experimental* in the catalog until a pilot's review (Gate 2). ## 1. Choice

The request was "a P-51 for an engine of about 120 cc, using one from the internet as a guide". The [research](research/p51-family-research.md) found **no commercial P-51D ARF for 100-150 cc**: the largest documented ARFs are the CARF 2.54 m (50-85 cc) and the Hangar 9 60cc (2.26 m). The 120 cc class is the **1/4-scale P-51D** (2.82 m) built from plans or a short kit (Chad Veich, Jerry Bates, FokkeRC), flown with a DA-120, DLE-120, 3W-110 or Kolm 155.

Decision: **geometry = the full-size P-51D scaled exactly 1/4** (published dimensions, the public-domain AN 01-60-3 three-view, UIUC section ordinates, NACA rigging); **installation = DA-120 with a 4-blade 26x12** (scale look; heavy for a 120 cc, see the realism report); CG 27 % MAC; throws from the Hangar 9 Mustang 1.50 manual. No commercial plan is copied.

## 2. Conventions

As the Extra: `assets/aircraft/p51d-mustang-120/` is the visual source (`source.json` → `build_geometry.py` → `geometry.json` → `compile_geometry.py` → `app/aircraft/p51d_geometry.gd`); `app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json` is the physics data (`openrc-aircraft v1`), generated by `research/p51/p51-05/derive_physics.py` from the geometry, `research/p51/p51-06/` (propeller calibration, engine anchor) and `research/p51/p51-13/` (section analysis). Model axes: +X right, +Y up, −Z nose; z = 0 at the root leading edge on the centreline, y = 0 on the propeller axis. The physics `le` frame shares that datum. Every value carries its evidence kind; never hand-edit generated files.

## 4. Known limits

- Gear fixed down (drag included), no flaps, no brakes; the tail-wheel spring is softer than a real one (240 Hz explicit-tick rule).
- The 4-blade propeller table is a calibrated prediction (no 4-blade datasheet); the windmilling branch and the engine's torque shape are uncalibrated.
- Slipstream on the tail only, without lag; wash factors from one RC airplane (Selig 2010).
- The fin offset (1° left on the full-size airplane) is carried by the solved rudder trim (no Cn0 in the v1 coefficient set).
- The neutral point is calibrated with one lumped term; inertias come from the component inventory (no measured P-51 inertia exists).
- `test.sh` budget: the two envelope scripts take ~32 s and ~20 s of the 60 s per-script limit on an idle machine.
