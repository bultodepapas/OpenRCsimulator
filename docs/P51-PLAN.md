The native model includes articulated controls/flaps, retractable taildragger gear with sequenced doors, a four-blade propeller, canopy/pilot, smoke outlet and scale-light anchors. Its generic natural-metal finish is implemented; owner paint choice remains open.

Current [aircraft data](../app/data/aircraft/p51d_mustang_120.json) enables simulated flaps, retractable gear and `finite_liquid_fuel`. Club runway starts are supported; airborne practice starts at 25 m/s with gear/flaps stowed. Flap positions are 0°/15°/45°, with estimated 2.5-second travel. Gear contacts/drag, braking and the on-wheels retraction interlock use shared mechanisms. [Player controls](FIRST-LAUNCH.md) owns bindings.

The gasoline shaft model supplies torque-balance RPM, reaction/gyro effects, angled thrust, propeller normal force and P-factor. Tail slipstream includes piecewise swirl and opt-in `propeller_sidewash`; the fuselage directional term, spanwise stall and planform ground-effect fit are active. Installed calibration remains experimental.

The estimated 1 L main tank uses `gasoline_bsfc_v1` with estimated consumption. Fuel burn changes mass, CG and inertia; empty-tank balance targets and live loaded CG are distinct. Pickup/feed labels do not establish a gasoline pressure or slosh simulation. Home owns launch fill; the fuel standard owns depletion and reset.

## Limits and next evidence

Mass distribution, rigging, CG, throws and installation include estimates. The four-blade 26×12 map is a BEM extrapolation fitted to two/three-blade data; the DA-120 curve and static-RPM anchor are not installed dynamometer measurements. Virtual balance mass is not a hardware recommendation.

The full-size glide neutral point (34.2% MAC) and tail-flow calibration do not establish Reynolds similarity. The P3 CG/neutral-point review from the elevator investigation remains open: identify the RC build's mass/CG/inertia, rigging and model-scale stability before retuning handling. Fixed fin incidence, dynamic stall, measured tires/oleos and richer wake/wing coupling remain future work.

Preserve a baseline and exact replay before physics changes using [flight logging](FLIGHT-LOGGING.md). Recheck numerical behavior under current fuel/configuration identities and retain failure attribution. Obtain matched engine/propeller and flight measurements with uncertainty. Pilot review, clean-checkout packages and slowest-target cost remain independent gates. Keep the aircraft experimental until those gates have evidence; coordinate shared mechanisms with their [registered owners](README.md#tracks-plans-and-step-ids).
