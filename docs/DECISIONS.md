# Decisions

A small record of what we choose and why. The two guiding principles remain established. Every other decision is our current direction and can change as we learn. When an entry is replaced, mark it *superseded* and keep it, so its context remains visible. The route these decisions serve is in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

GDU-03 supersedes earlier decisions to retain the production 4.7.2 engine pin.
Version-specific measurements and backend findings retain their original scope;
[4.8 adoption evidence](research/engine-upgrade/GDU-03/README.md) identifies the
behavior rechecked on beta1. Earlier performance results are not requalified by
the engine switch. The separate native DLSS experiment remains experimental.
