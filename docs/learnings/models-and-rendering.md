# Models and rendering

**Status:** curated reference. Lessons from 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-13; reports hold detailed measurements, assumptions and reproduction steps, while plans hold step status. See [LEARNINGS](../LEARNINGS.md) for shared engineering lessons.

## Normalize texture data in the renderer's actual color space

An RC grass candidate compiled and passed geometry tests while native Compatibility captures clipped almost white: an encoded texture sample had been divided by a linear source mean. Keep means and samples in the same space, check `OUTPUT_IS_SRGB`, and retain a rendered brightness comparison. `source_color` alone is not evidence of identical sample representation across rendering methods. [L9d repair and paired captures](../research/rc-ground-implementation-2026-10-15/README.md#color-space-repair-found-during-integration).

## Recheck night exposure when changing renderers

A Forward+ port looked usable by day but rendered the night sky white: RD sky debanding scales ±1/255 noise by exposure, which can exceed 49,000 in the existing night adaptation. The Compatibility inverse-color helper also over-brightened stars and lamps when reused on linear RD ALBEDO. Keep backend-specific conversions at the output boundary and test actual day/night pixels, including sky postprocessing, before adding effects. [VQ-07 diagnosis and native/package comparisons](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/high-trial-2026-10-21/README.md#renderer-corrections).

## Camera-dependent emitters must use the live projection

A fixed FOV/viewport reference makes a minimum-pixel light grow into a large surface when the camera zooms. An opaque flux-compensated LED strip then becomes a dark patch over the airframe at dusk. Use the current projection and viewport, additive emission and a directional visibility guard. Prove the repair with on/off rendered pairs, a visible-light control, zoom/resolution changes and an opaque blocker; geometry placement tests alone missed this defect. [FW-62-R1](../research/field-world/FW-35/README.md#2026-10-13--fw-62-r1-distant-wing-blocks).

## Aircraft models and references

- **Keep evidence tied to an aircraft and configuration.** A similar name, paint scheme, gallery, manual, or plan bundle does not prove that two references describe the same variant, engine, propeller, or construction. Record geometry, finish, and installation provenance separately, and keep conflicting configurations as alternatives until identified. ([EX-00](../research/extra-300-photo-investigation.md), [aircraft reference index](../research/aircraft-reference-index.md), [US-00](../research/ugly-stik-new-files.md))

- **Treat scans and drawings as conditional measurements.** Calibrate each view and axis independently, keep pixel picks separate from derived dimensions, and reserve at least one station or dimension outside the fit as a check. Agreement with one nominal dimension can support a scale choice without proving the whole drawing is calibrated. ([US-01](../research/ugly-stik-model-v1-calibration.md), [US-02/03](../research/ugly-stik-model-v3.md), [P51-02b](../research/p51-silhouette-review-v1.md))

- **Keep geometric, visual, and aerodynamic evidence distinct.** A traced or decorative shape does not establish flight coefficients; control throws may be specified as linear travel at the widest part of a surface, and tapered wings require an explicit chord convention such as local chord, `S/b`, or MAC. Carry each value with its source, units, and meaning before deriving physics from it. ([EX-00](../research/extra-300-resources.md), [EX-00 tooling review](../research/extra-aircraft-tooling/README.md))

- **Check the constructed model, not just its source table.** Probe built mesh nodes and cross-sections in physical units, then compare rendered silhouettes with the same dimensions; this catches a builder or transform error even when the source data is correct. When moving a major datum such as the nose, move attached equipment with it and verify those relationships in the built scene. ([US-02](../research/ugly-stik-model-v2.md), [EX-02](../research/extra-300-visual-review-v1.md))

- **Exercise the actual articulated geometry.** Sweep the real command path through hinge poses and measure transformed mesh endpoints, rod lengths, clearances, and control direction; a correct solver can still drive a malformed or inverted mesh. Check containment as well as surface distance, and require a useful positive clearance rather than accepting exact contact as “no intersection.” ([US-V01–08](../research/ugly-stik-model-v4.md), [US-04/05](../research/ugly-stik-model-v3-installation.md), [EX-04](../research/extra-300-visual-review-v1.md))

- **Joint closure does not prove joint orientation.** HX-700 rods retained their lengths while a world-neutral aiming rule cancelled the carrier rotation and turned socket faces away from their supports. Carry the authored local frame through its current parent before aiming, and compare terminal bases as well as endpoints against the source. Check actual rod mesh axes separately: imported mesh axes can differ from their terminal's local axis. ([HELI-26a correction](../research/helicopter/HELI-26a/README.md#terminal-rotation-correction--2026-10-16))

- **Validate winding, normals, and holes as separate mesh properties.** Unit-length normals can still point the wrong way, and an extruder that handles an open notch may cap a closed inner contour as another solid. Test built faces and winding as well as normal magnitude, and add a closed-hole fixture before relying on a helper for cutouts. ([US-V01–08](../research/ugly-stik-model-v4.md), [AV-02 v3](../research/avanti-s-contour-refinement-v3.md), [tooling investigation 01](../research/ugly-stik-tooling-investigations/01-polygons-and-meshes.md))

- **Separate acceptance for dimensions, articulation, appearance, and flight behavior.** A playable model can still have estimated dimensions; a geometrically faithful model is not thereby aerodynamically validated. Keep those claims and their evidence separate, and judge readability in the intended pilot view as well as in close-up inspection views. ([US review v2](../research/ugly-stik-model-review-v2.md), [US-06](../research/ugly-stik-model-v3-readability.md), [EX-02](../research/extra-300-visual-review-v1.md))

- **Compare revisions with frozen cameras and held-out views.** Fixed camera, lighting, and reference alignment make changes attributable; fit on selected landmarks, then inspect reserved landmarks and other views for overfitting. Uncalibrated photographs are useful for qualitative shape and detail, but not for claiming dimensions, and overlays should state which parts were excluded. ([AV-02](../research/avanti-s-transparency-comparison.md), [P51-02b](../research/p51-silhouette-review-v1.md), [P51-visual review](../research/p51-visual-review-v1.md))

- **Make geometry generators repeatable and regenerate before verification.** Write absolute values from source data or a template instead of applying cumulative offsets, and ensure generated geometry and its source are synchronized before running checks. A stale generated file can let a working copy pass while the current source would produce a different model. ([P51-02b](../research/p51-silhouette-review-v1.md), [US-02](../research/ugly-stik-model-v2.md))

- **Check MakeHuman/MPFB defaults and asset files before trusting a generated person.** MPFB's default macros are gender 0.5, so LU-21's "man" was androgynous; set the macros explicitly. A CC0 `.mhclo` without a `material` line (the newsboy cap) exports untextured. Read the thumbnail and the asset before use: one CC0 hat carried a political slogan, the "jeans shorts" were women's short shorts and a pair of flats kept 25,920 triangles after decimation ([SC-HUM-07](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-HUM-07/README.md#sources-and-rejections)).

- **A 0.5 alpha cut erases thin strands once mipmaps are on.** Eyebrows and fine hair average below the threshold in mips and disappear, so MPFB faces looked browless; an alpha cut of 0.3 restored them without visible hair-edge bloat at 1080p ([SC-HUM-08](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-HUM-08/README.md#faces-and-skin-sc-hum-07-remainder)).

- **Measure a rig's joint axes before posing it.** On MPFB's default rig, fingers flex about local Z (mirrored left and right) and the thumb tucks about −X. Curling about X, which is what an earlier pose code assumed, does not close a hand. A CC0 motion-capture pose that bends only the outer finger joints reads as a claw. Measure by moving one bone and checking which way the fingertip moves relative to the thumb and the wrist ([SC-HUM-07-R1](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-HUM-07-R1/README.md#measured-rig-facts-mpfb-2018-default-rig)).

- **Take an asset's licence from its publisher's catalog, and record any conflict with its file header.** Three MakeHuman CC0 asset-pack items still carry `license AGPL3` in their `.mhclo` headers, and one has no licence line. A record claiming "CC0 in each file" was wrong even though the assets are CC0 ([SC-HUM-07-R2](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-HUM-07-R1/README.md#sc-hum-07-r2--faces-and-licence-audit-2026-10-25)).

- **Check `.import` settings after any bulk import.** Re-creating an `.import` file resets it to Godot's defaults, silently dropping VRAM compression, mipmaps and normal-map mode. A commit did exactly that to the people's normal atlas; a hash-and-settings guard in the area's tests caught it ([SC-HUM-07-R1](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-HUM-07-R1/README.md#verification)).

- **Reduce a detailed hard-surface model per part, not as one mesh.** One collapse decimation of the joined 36k-triangle owner transmitter, to 800 triangles, tore holes in its flat face panel while small knobs kept their triangles. Merge coplanar faces of each part first (planar dissolve, materials delimited), then share the triangle budget by surface area with a small floor per part. The housing kept its outline at a similar total ([SC-HUM-01](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-HUM-01/README.md#recipe)).

## Rendering and visual evidence

- **Separate vertex metadata from display colors.** In Godot 4.7.2, raw packed vertex values reach `vertex()` in both renderers. Applying an sRGB transfer to material tags or variation seeds can preserve classification while collapsing variation and shifting UVs. Diagnose the actual stage with native reference panels; keep ALBEDO color decoding separate. [Barrier/shelter GPU evidence](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/material-tags-2026-10-22/README.md).

- **Reload generated resources with a real renderer before accepting a bake.** Headless snapshots saved empty texture data, and imported `PortableCompressedTexture2D` resources discarded their compressed buffers on re-save. Retain image buffers before saving, deduplicate identical payloads, then reload and inspect native captures. Check wheel contact against referenced triangle vertices: unused source vertices can extend an AABB below the visible tires. Recheck the final LOD geometry and collision offset together. [SC-09-R1](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-09-R1/README.md).

- **Include shader defaults when auditing assigned textures.** On pinned Godot 4.7.2, `Shader.get_shader_uniform_list()` omits uniforms with default textures. Enumerate RenderingServer parameters and resolve material overrides/default textures explicitly; a native fixture should prove shared consumers and unchanged assignments. Keep assigned resources separate from visible draws, and CPU image bytes separate from resident VRAM. ([VQ-02a inventory](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-02a/README.md))

- **Differentiate a spatial signal before applying its screen-footprint filter.** Feeding a `dFdx`/`dFdy`-dependent filter into another derivative creates undefined higher-order derivatives. LU-12 keeps the grain height independent of that filter, differentiates it once, then attenuates the gradients. Compare detail-off pixels for filtering and restore the original material for cost measurement: a zero-strength shader still executes. ([Material checks](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-11/materials.md))

- **Derive collision bounds through the complete render transform.** Atlas occupancy alone missed the tree card's 1.125001 m frame scale; a centred prefab box missed an asymmetric veranda. Include card/frame scale, yaw, bounded animation and the actual prefab origin, then test occupied edge rows and intended openings. Conservative boxes still need their empty-space overcoverage documented. ([Coverage review](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-11/collision-coverage.md))

- **Apply optical fades after the exposure cap.** A lamp's distance fade applied before a saturated display cap can leave it bright until the last fraction of the fade range; pre-capping radiance before screen-size compensation can instead erase distant lamps. Size the source using the actual projection/viewport, cap the sized energy with the dusk factor, then apply range fading. Check real pixels against an opaque blocker: a passing shader-source test missed the fixture cap obscuring the optical core. ([LU-09 native evidence](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-07/README.md), [pixel checks](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-07/night/marker-checks.json))

- **Make isolated GPU measurements independent of presentation.** Native capture trials stalled while awaiting automatic post-draw signals. An always-updating off-screen viewport with the automatic render loop disabled and explicit `force_draw(false)` completed reliably and matched the visible-window control pixel for pixel. Preserve raw viewport CPU/GPU samples, alternate revisions, and distinguish static shader cost from whole-app frame time and radiance refresh. Headless dummy rendering cannot provide this evidence. ([L4c](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L4c/README.md))

- **A clock's restart selection is different from its current instant.** Rate edits preserve fractional ticks; explicit time selections reset them and establish the restart value. Validate each serialized transition against its predecessor, bind its frequency to the physics timestep, and reject unsupported histories before restoring physical state. A valid per-record config hash cannot detect a hidden time jump. ([TOD-09c1](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-09c1/README.md))

- **Freeze the render pose as well as simulation ticks for pixel comparisons.** A paused tick does not freeze `Engine.get_physics_interpolation_fraction()`. Resampling between fresh-build and in-place captures changed 47 aircraft pixels while the field matched. Pin an explicit physical pose, assert the aircraft transform stays unchanged, and retain exact pixel equality; do not mask the mismatch or increase tolerance. ([TOD-09b diagnostic](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-09b/README.md#capture-oracle-diagnostic)) For fresh-build comparisons, also match resource-allocation context: identical static mesh arrays received different opaque draw order after rebuilding in one process. Independent startup processes preserve exact assertions without changing the camera or masking pixels. ([Mesh-context controls](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-09b/static-cell-diagnostic/README.md#follow-up-mesh-allocation-context))

- **Audit a DCC rig before treating its hierarchy as a runtime contract.** The Timber source stores metre-scale Blender transforms beside millimetre CAD metadata, a posed root, driver-controlled hinges, and fixed motor mounts under the propeller's parent. Record each representation's units and rest frame; separate fixed and rotating children before wiring simulator poses. Static inspection identifies these risks but does not replace an evaluated export/import check. ([TT-00](../research/timber-integration/TT-00/README.md))

- **Probe the pinned renderer instead of generalizing from tutorials or another backend.** Small tests against the project's Godot version and Compatibility renderer have exposed differences in per-instance uniforms, shader bounds, color handling, and particle behavior. A probe establishes only the behavior it actually exercises. ([VQ-00](../research/visual-quality-round2/README.md), [SM-PLAN](../research/smoke-investigations/README.md)) For clip-space mask passes, compare asymmetric geometry against an independent CPU projection and reject a deliberately flipped shader: a plausible silhouette can still be mirrored by the render-target convention. Isolated SubViewport cameras must also be excluded from gameplay-camera counts and environment lookup without weakening their one-world assertions. ([VQ-04a](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-04a/README.md)) Compatibility also ignores `GeometryInstance3D.transparency` and lights cull-disabled back faces with the normal flipped, so winding decides the lit side; fade through a material of your own and keep the stopped state byte-identical. ([VQ-05](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-05/README.md))

- **Match sky and spatial fog in linear color.** In Compatibility, sky-shader `COLOR` is interpreted as sRGB and decoded after `sky()`, while spatial `FOG` and engine fog operate in linear space. Do atmospheric math in linear values and encode the sky result so the decoded value matches the fog; direct assignment can create a visible horizon seam. ([L1/L2 investigation](../research/landscape-investigations/02-atmosphere-aerial-perspective.md), [sky shader](../../app/render/sky.gdshader))

- **Check darkness through every lighting path.** A dark background does not prove dark ambient or metallic reflections: the pinned Compatibility probe retained daytime radiance for several INCREMENTAL frames. Unshaded grey contact decals can also brighten a nearly black surface despite being called shadows. Initialise radiance before exposing a new scene and make night contact cues darkening-only. ([TOD-01](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-01/README.md), [TOD-04](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-04/README.md))

- **Bound local lights in the exposure domain they actually use.** LU-03's added veranda lights clipped white under FW night exposure, while the same authored lights disappeared on the fixed-SDR TOD path. Share the actual environment exposure with local-light caps, reset global exposure when rebuilding between paths, and capture both routes. Use solar elevation for continuous dusk switching; disc visibility can disappear before the geometric sun crosses the horizon. The display gains remain art controls, not a photometric calibration. ([LU-03](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-03/README.md), [integrated evidence](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-06/README.md))

- **Check unshaded VFX against the exact engine color path.** Godot 4.7.2 GLES3 linearizes ALBEDO and applies Environment exposure even to additive unshaded meshes; its unshaded branch ignores EMISSION. A bounded display contribution therefore needs `encode_for_engine(linear_cap / exposure)`. Dividing the encoded color directly made the earlier lightning probe disappear and led to an incorrect exposure-bypass assumption. Verify production day/night pre/peak/post frames, with both material and scene-light caps; keep transient flashes out of shared Sky uniforms. ([FW-84 correction and native evidence](../research/field-world/FW-84/README.md#fw-84b--integrated-electrical-bank-2026-10-14))

- **Keep scene-linear cloud radiance separate from display caps.** For a blended unshaded material in Compatibility, the engine linearizes ALBEDO and then applies exposure. FW-83 initially multiplied sky radiance by exposure on the CPU, producing a white moonlit bank. Pass raw linear radiance, shade/mix in linear space, and encode once with the existing exact engine inverse. Check day and night; an additive lightning cap is not the same material contract. ([FW-83](../research/field-world/FW-83/README.md#runtime-integration))

- **Keep a CPU copy of shader globals sent at runtime.** Godot's global-shader-parameter readback methods are editor-only; in a real renderer they log errors and return null, while headless probes can appear to work. Diagnostics and captures should report the values the application sent instead of relying on a misleading readback. ([ShaderClock](../../app/render/shader_clock.gd), [L0d](../LANDSCAPE-PLAN.md)) Detached material construction may still write those globals. Preserve and restore the live values during preparation, then test rejected restores at a nonzero cloud/animation time; building outside the tree alone does not establish atomicity. ([TOD-08a](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-08a/README.md))

- **Bounds must include shader motion and transparent padding.** Automatic mesh bounds describe undeformed geometry, so vertex-shader displacement can be culled unless `custom_aabb` covers its full range. For tree cards, endpoint bending can shift the interpolated trunk root; use a root-preserving transform and include padded card extents in culling bounds. ([VQ-00](../research/visual-quality-round2/godot-probe/README.md), [L15b](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L15b/README.md))

- **Verify renderer data through the renderer that will consume it.** Compatibility stores `MultiMesh` custom data at float16 precision and truncates rather than rounds (1000.3 reads 1000.0, 100.3 reads 100.25), which is unsuitable for exact world coordinates; pack discrete grid identity into bounded integer-like channels, or quantize on the CPU to values a half float holds exactly so the CPU reference and the GPU agree ([SC-BIRD-08](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-BIRD-08/README.md)). Vertex colors arrive linearized, and instance color alpha can be zero unless `use_colors` is enabled, so avoid a second sRGB conversion and test the effective shader inputs. Headless dummy-renderer transform buffers are not placement evidence; inspect real instance transforms on OpenGL. Keep the CPU transforms passed to MultiMesh for geometry collectors that also run headlessly. When moving props out of static cells, migrate their projected and contact-shadow membership too; hiding an entire legacy batch can otherwise erase the remaining foot/tire contacts. ([LU-21 integration](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-21/README.md)) ([L6b](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L6b/README.md), [SC-02…24](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-03/README.md), [L11a](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L11a/README.md))

- **Use zero-feature pixel parity to catch renderer-default changes.** The zero-strength ground comparison exposed an unintended lighting change; restoring the original Lambert fallback returned byte parity. A custom shader's implicit diffuse mode need not match `StandardMaterial3D` defaults. Check the actual baseline path before changing the BRDF. ([L4b](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L4b/README.md), [parity method](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L4b/capture-tool.md))

- **Budget what the renderer pays for, attributed per subsystem.** A pooled full-detail source-triangle cap blocked content while missing the real costs. People and cars used half of it but drew 3,116 primitives in the forward pilot view (counted per instance, [LU-34-R1](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-34/README.md#revision-lu-34-r1-instanced-lod-meshes-were-undercounted)); full-detail field meshes, the fleet's 179 draw calls and 489 MiB of textures (against 43.7 MiB of buffers) went unmeasured. Hide one subsystem at a time and subtract the counters; Compatibility's shadow counter reads zero, but its totals include the shadow pass. ([LU-34](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-34/README.md))

- **Headless imports leave 3D textures lossless.** `detect_3d/compress_to` only acts when the editor sees the texture used in 3D, so CI and headless imports keep `compress/mode=0`. Downloaded trees decoded 113–142 ms each on cold load and used four times their GPU-compressed memory, with no visible gain. Textures bound only from script also keep `mipmaps/generate=false`, so a `filter_*_mipmap*` sampler reads level 0 at every distance: LU-33's roof slates broke into dot grids and moiré. Set `compress/mode` and mipmaps explicitly in the `.import` file. Flag normal maps only when the shader reconstructs blue (`hint_normal`). Pin the choice in a test. ([LU-34](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-34/README.md#optimization-applied-gpu-compressed-vegetation-textures), [VQ-02a-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-02a/roof-mipmaps/README.md))

- **MultiMesh LOD reads error in source units, and generated LODs delete leaf cards.** Godot selects a MultiMesh level from the node transform, not the per-instance scale. A Fagus authored 1 m tall and placed at 9.5 m dropped to 9 % of its triangles by 80 m, while a 60 m-authored Quercus never simplified. Set `lod_bias` to the batch's largest instance scale. Generated LODs simplify bark gracefully but remove whole alpha-scissor leaf cards (chains fall to 1–14 %), so crowns turn bare while their shadows look full. Keep only foliage levels with enough cards, and inspect `RenderingServer.mesh_get_surface(...).lods` per surface before trusting a downloaded tree at range. ([L6c-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L6c/species-variety/README.md#distant-landscapelab-foliage)) One MultiMesh also takes one level for all its instances, from the nearest point of its bounds, and Godot switches only below 1 px of error. One batch of 127 trees at 313–600 m therefore drew all of them at full detail. Split such batches into cells, and let bark switch at a few pixels while foliage keeps 1 px, so crowns do not pop. ([L8a-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L8a-R1/README.md#cost))

- **Check downloaded PBR scalars against the texture, not only the shape.** Every LandscapeLab GLB authors placeholder `roughnessFactor` 0.5 and `metallicFactor` 0.5 on its leaves. Glossy leaf cards mirror the sky in every orientation, so the 3D crowns rendered grey-blue (blue/green 0.78) against yellow-green leaf textures (0.43), and the cards looked wrong beside them. Compare a render's mean colour with the unlit texture, then toggle specular and roughness to find the term that adds the cast. ([L8a-R2](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L8a-R2/README.md))

- **Compatibility's primitive counter undercounts instanced meshes with LODs.** Godot 4.7.2's GLES3 `_fill_render_list` adds a LOD surface's selected-level primitives once per draw, but multiplies surfaces without LODs by the instance count. 79 beeches read 9,312 primitives and drew 735,648. Measure such a MultiMesh alone: hide it for its counted cost, then show one instance for the per-instance part, with its AABB pinned, because Godot rebuilds the AABB from the visible instances. Its drawn cost is the instance count times one instance. ([LU-34-R1](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-34/README.md#revision-lu-34-r1-instanced-lod-meshes-were-undercounted))

- **Clamp the foliage mip instead of disabling leaf mipmaps.** Box-filtered leaf atlases keep their alpha-cutoff coverage until a mip texel exceeds the leaf size (mip 6 for the 1K LandscapeLab atlases), but distant cards sample mips 7–9 and vanish. Unmipped atlases keep coverage but speckle up close. Clamping only alpha leaves near-black colour, because transparent texels are black, and un-premultiplying over-brightens. Sampling the whole albedo no coarser than the last coverage-preserving mip kept both near look and distant crowns. Godot 4.7.2's `mipmaps/limit` import option has no effect. ([L6c-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L6c/species-variety/README.md#distant-landscapelab-foliage))

- **Byte parity between two equivalent shaders is a native-GPU check.** On Mesa llvmpipe, L15b-R2's hand-mirrored StandardMaterial3D shader differed from the source material by tens of isolated pixels whenever its `vertex()` displaced `VERTEX`, even by exactly zero. The accepted L15b-R1 shader failed the same strict gate by 21–77 px per view. Bisect by removing code, compare with the accepted shader's noise in the same fixture, and keep the exact gate on the native host. Godot 4.7.2 binds no GDScript route to a BaseMaterial3D's generated shader (`get_shader_rid` is internal). Mirror the generator for the features you accept and fail closed otherwise. ([L15b-R2](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L15b-R2/README.md#native-captures-llvmpipe))

- **State shader displacement in world units.** L15b-R1 applied 0.01 mesh-local units of sway: 1.7 mm on the 59.6-unit Quercus and 1.4 cm on a 7.3-unit Quaternius tree, both scaled to 10 m. Convert with the instance scale (`length(MODEL_MATRIX[1].xyz)` for upright uniform scale) and bound the motion in metres against the collision envelope it must stay inside. ([L15b-R2](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L15b-R2/README.md#collision-stays-unchanged))

- **Check view-dependent atlases against the source silhouette.** Blender's glTF importer maps glTF −Z to Blender +Y, so a bake camera at azimuth *a* returns to Godot as the source-local direction (cos *a*, 0, −sin *a*). The woodland and hedge cards selected frames with `+atan(z, x)` and showed the far side from north and south. Render the source orthographically from each frame's direction and require that frame's silhouette IoU to be best. ([Impostor view frames](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L6c/tree-completion/impostor-view-frames/README.md))

- **Select reference-scene features explicitly.** Gameplay defaults can change at the owner's request; a capture that inherits those defaults silently changes its reference scene. Pin layer switches in baseline tools and exercise default behavior separately through the production renderer. ([SC-02a](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-02a/README.md))

- **Packed slope data is not an ordinary normal map.** Godot's normal-map import can select two-channel compression and discard blue. If blue stores a scalar such as turf tone, disable normal-map conversion, keep linear sampling and mipmaps, and verify the exported texture through rendered pixels. Filtering encoded slopes before normalizing also avoids giving unresolved normals their full high-frequency amplitude. ([L9e](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L9e/README.md))

- **Budget visible surfaces and culling, not node count alone.** In Compatibility, each visible surface contributes a draw; shared materials and merging can reduce cost, but merging an entire field removes useful per-object culling. The first desk-research recommendation for a merge API was contradicted by an app-render probe: it relit mirrored cars, while `SurfaceTool.append_from` reproduced their separate rendering; a narrow view also favored smaller cells over a larger merge. Measure representative wide and narrow views before fixing chunk size. ([SC-01 research](../research/scenery-investigations/01-prop-asset-sources.md), [SC-01 renderer probe](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-01/README.md))

- **Bake complex scenery models outside the flight-time builder.** Constructing parked aircraft through live model builders made field loading expensive; offline baked meshes with indexed geometry can use LOD generation and be merged into scenery cells. Index a merged triangle list before generating LODs, choose the LOD from the target size, and profile GDScript expansion/allocation separately from rendering. ([SC-02…24](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-03/README.md), [tooling notes](../research/visual-quality-round2/03-profiling-and-asset-tools.md))

- **A field ID does not prove its authored scenery still fits.** A custom copy can retain `default` while moving the runway or pilot. Gate implicit scenery on normalized spatial geometry, share the decision between visuals and collision composition, and retain full validation for explicit layouts. Tests that manually build scenery must also disable automatic attachment when scenery becomes a default. ([LU-06 regression evidence](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-06/README.md#functional-and-regression-checks))

- **Count imported geometry by indices, surfaces and instances.** Vertex-count/3 and surface-zero-only budgets undercount indexed, multi-material assets. Multiply each surface triangle count by its MultiMesh instances, then measure actual distance LOD in the native renderer. Externalize identical glTF images to shared paths so duplicate poses share texture resources. ([LU-21](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-21/README.md))

- **Spatial splits need a rendering comparison even when transforms are identical.** Splitting the 17 close trees into 64/128/256 m MultiMeshes preserved transforms and damage IDs but increased visible submissions/primitives and changed pixels. Godot's per-batch LOD selection makes bounding-box changes relevant beyond culling. Keep instance-identity tests, but also compare native images and actual submitted geometry before enabling a cell size. ([L7-R2 controls](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L6a-R1/README.md#verification-and-performance-scope)) Correction: the primitive increase was a counter artifact; the extra draws are real. The counter counts each LOD'd draw once (see the counter lesson above), and a cell is never nearer than its whole batch, so cells cannot raise the true count. Counted per instance, 256 m cells cut the 3D front trees from 972,696 to 726,428 triangles. ([L8a-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L8a-R1/README.md#cost))

- **Treat renderer formulas as hypotheses until measured in the app.** The initial depth-buffer estimate predicted a large depth step at 5 km, but the Compatibility probe rendered a 6 m-separated turbine cleanly; a 2 cm shadow lift also remained stable through a 100 m camera height. Keep those observations attached to the tested backend, camera range, and scene rather than turning a theoretical estimate into a universal rule. ([SC-01 renderer probe](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-01/README.md))

- **Choose visual metrics that match the scene and question.** Mean contrast hid a small airplane at a tree/sky boundary, while a repetition metric became misleading when its camera framed the runway's deliberate pattern. Mask the subject, inspect local or low-percentile visibility, and confirm the named background is actually present. ([L6c](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L6c/README.md), [landscape improvement](../research/landscape-improvement-2026-10-07/implementation/README.md))

- **Validate time-dependent rendering with a time series.** Initializing a particle trail at the final pose loses its history, and a still leaning tree can pass a calm-versus-wind comparison. Replay the relevant simulation path, require adjacent-time images to change inside the effect mask, and check the raw shader clock through its wrap boundary. A mask built only from the still frame can miss a clipped tree entering the image under wind; bound that frame-edge allowance separately and keep an injected off-tree defect test. ([SM-PLAN](../SMOKE-PLAN.md), [emission timing probe](../research/smoke-investigations/godot-evidence/README.md), [L15b](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L15b/README.md), [tree checker](../../tools/trees/README.md#l15b-tree-wind))

- **Compare both simulation state and pixels in a scripted scene.** Record one shared state row for every instant across cameras, alongside time, commands, rpm, propeller angle, clock, and image outputs. Comparing state and images distinguishes a physics change from a visual-only change and checks that rendering follows the simulated timeline. ([SC-25](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-25/README.md)) For trace-driven stills, validate the actual rendered CG, basis, hinges and camera against the selected recorded state, not just echoed inputs; retain the same shadow in airplane-on/off pairs so it cannot masquerade as airplane pixels. ([E3c2b](../research/ground-contact/E3c2b/README.md))

- **Check the exact asset package and license.** A provider's current catalog page may not describe an older downloaded pack, and a bundle can give different terms for models, textures, examples, and add-ons. Preserve the source archive or its hash and the included license evidence; validate converted assets independently from the original. ([VQ asset review](../research/visual-quality-supplement-2026-10-06.md), [SC-02…24](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-03/README.md)) Compare per-item internal metadata with the catalog: LU-21 rejected a seated pose whose metadata conflicted with the listing, then selected one with matching CC0 evidence. Keep GPL authoring software separate from the license of its exported asset data. ([Human source audit](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-21/people-research.md))

- **Exercise pooled presentation through its real lifecycle and backend.** A hidden copied root hid the wreck's surviving core; re-enabling particle emission without restarting reused old particles. Dummy audio retained stopped WAV playback resources, while native WASAPI freed them. Test source/copy visibility, a second crash, pause/reset, silent-backend setup and native playback separately; do not infer audible mix or renderer behavior from headless passes. ([CR-22a](../research/crash-damage-investigations/CR-22a/README.md))

- **Budget reflection atlas capacity as well as probe count.** Adding one probe can allocate the configured atlas capacity. In Compatibility, the default 64-slot reservation made this scene report about 244 MiB more video memory than a two-slot atlas. Set capacity explicitly for the bounded use case and measure the native lake-on/off pair; final measured increment was 17.32 MiB. ([LU-22 reflection evidence](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-22/README.md#rendering-and-query-cost))

- **Gate per-frame sky uniforms on whether the radiance is used, and prove engine gates with a pass-specific amplifier.** In pinned Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility every sky uniform change marks the radiance dirty, but the cubemap is rebuilt only when ambient light or reflections come from the sky (directly or through a sky background). The field-world environment (ambient COLOR, reflections DISABLED) can therefore move the moon every frame for free, while a sky-ambient route rebuilds the cubemap every frame. Frame-time medians on the loaded VM spread 53–331 ms for one case; a loop that runs only under `AT_CUBEMAP_PASS` showed the answer at once (+2 ms against +69 ms). ([FW-MOON-01](../research/field-world/FW-MOON-01/README.md#31-radiance-when-the-engine-renders-the-cubemap-q1-q2))

- **Dump time-driven visual state per step before blaming the obvious event.** A 26 % twilight brightness pop sat exactly where the light passed from sun to moon, but the cause was a piecewise formula: refraction was cut to zero below a true elevation of −1°, where it is 0.65°, so the sun dropped 0.73° in one step. Make every input of a time-flowing picture continuous (taper cut-offs), then check each candidate event (light swap, lobe swap, table ends) separately; deterministic state dumps and step-bound tests found the defects in seconds, renders only confirm them. ([FW-MOON-06](../research/field-world/FW-MOON-06/README.md#1-audit-what-jumped-at-twilight))

- **Reject a measured pixel when any channel clips, and calibrate the display scale on the same frame setup.** A rendered aureole measured 0.1× its model near the horizon. The bluish night sky, at a raised exposure, clipped its blue channel while its luminance stayed below the luminance-only clip test. Rejecting any channel at 250/255 gave 1.01. A uniform-sky frame of known radiance measures the actual radiance-to-display scale (here 0.996 of the tonemap exposure); if its level is not scaled with the exposure, it saturates too. ([FW-MOON-08](../research/field-world/FW-MOON-08/README.md#2-verification))

- **Measure small rotations from the quaternion's vector part.** `Quaternion.get_angle()` is 2·acos(w); in float32 it returns 0 below about 0.04°, so a "no jump" bound on per-frame rotations passed while the rotation was missing. Use 2·asin(|xyz|), or `angle_to` between basis columns, and give every bound a positive control. ([FW-MOON-03b](../research/field-world/FW-MOON-03b/README.md)) The same floor affects equality checks: the shared retraction rig binds an imported gear only if `angle_to` against its first sample stays below 1e-4 rad, and float32 `acos` of a dot product a few ulps under 1 already exceeds that. Write a sampled rig's first pose from the GLB's own numbers after checking agreement with a stable measure, 2·atan2(|a−b|, |a+b|) ([F4U-05](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-05/README.md)).

- **Nodes added during `SceneTree._initialize()` are not inside the tree yet.** `global_basis` then returns identity with an engine `ERROR:`, so a probe that measured world-up compression in a strut's parent frame passed only because the down-pose strut happened to be vertical. Compose model-frame transforms through the parent chain instead (`imported_model.gd` `relative()`), or measure after the first frame, and treat an engine error in a probe's log as a failed check ([F4U-05](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-05/README.md)).

- **Hide detail under an overlay with blur or the local mean, never with darkening alone.** A dark masking patch over a dark model left it 32 % of its Weber contrast, because darkening scales the model and its background alike. Blurring the image over the patch (screen-texture mipmaps on a canvas layer) left 10 % ([SUN-01…05](../research/sun/SUN-01-05/README.md)).

- **Do not mix a dark and a bright colour in one Compatibility overlay pass.** The renderer multiplies an unshaded ALBEDO by the exposure and tonemaps it per material ([FW-84](../research/field-world/FW-84/README.md)), so a weighted pre-exposure mix is not the display mix: a dark afterimage under a bright veil came out as a warm ring. Draw them as separate passes, or put display-space screen effects on a canvas layer that reads the finished image (SUN-03).

- **Read MultiMesh instances through `buffer`, and only with a real renderer.** The headless dummy renderer keeps no instance data, so every `get_instance_transform()` returns the origin and headless checks silently lose all trees. On GLES3 each call reads back from the renderer (~0.8 ms), so 2,810 trees cost 2.3 s. One `buffer` read per MultiMesh (12 floats per instance, row-major 3×4) fixed both the cost and the clarity; run tree-dependent checks under `--rendering-driver opengl3` ([SUN-09](../research/sun/SUN-02b-06-09/README.md)).
