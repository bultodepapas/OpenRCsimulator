# Models and rendering lessons

Lessons that change how to inspect aircraft, scenes and rendered evidence. Reports retain the measurements and reproduction details. See [LEARNINGS](../LEARNINGS.md) for shared lessons and the [visual guide](../VISUAL-QUALITY-PLAN.md) for current practice. Engine observations below apply to the version and backend named; recheck them against the current [engine pin](../TOOLS.md#developer-setup) before prescribing a workaround.

## Aircraft models and references

- **Identify the configuration before using a reference.** A similar name or finish does not establish the same variant, installation or construction. Keep geometry, finish and equipment provenance separate; treat a visual master's incidence or decorative outline as visual evidence, not rigging data or aerodynamic coefficients. [EX-00](../research/extra-300-photo-investigation.md), [F4U-07](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-07/README.md#method-corrections-found-while-deriving).

- **Calibrate drawings and reserve independent checks.** Scale each view and axis separately, preserve pixel picks, and hold out a station or dimension. A fit to one nominal dimension does not establish the accuracy of the remaining drawing. Record the chord convention when translating a tapered outline into a physical quantity. [US-01](../research/ugly-stik-model-v1-calibration.md), [P51-02b](../research/p51-silhouette-review-v1.md).

- **Measure the built model and its actual transforms.** Source tables and transformed bounding-box corners missed errors in the visible mesh. Use transformed referenced vertices and cross-sections for extents and compare contact geometry with the built model using its manifest datum. Include nearby equipment when a datum moves. [TT-02/03/05](../research/timber-integration/TT-02/README.md), [GP-06](../research/ground-physics/GP-06/README.md), [GP-07](../research/ground-physics/GP-07/README.md).

- **Test a rig's delivered pose and orientation, not just its structure.** A Corsair export passed name, scale and count checks with the gear in the wrong pose; another rig preserved rod length while its sockets faced away. Sweep the actual command path, verify endpoints, terminal bases, mesh axes, control direction and contract-specific export pose. Exercise a known motion before trusting samples. For Blender custom-property drivers, tag the control and driven objects before evaluation. [F4U-04](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-04/README.md), [F4U-06](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-06/README.md), [HELI-26a](../research/helicopter/HELI-26a/README.md#terminal-rotation-correction--2026-10-16).

- **Check every newly adjacent or moved part for interference.** Existing clearance pairs missed a rudder/fuselage overlap, and a generator bending fuel lines moved them through the engine mount. Include the actual delivered meshes, require positive clearance where appropriate, and exclude only intentional joins. Open or overlapping imported shells invalidate closed-volume ray-parity assumptions; test surface crossings and containment separately. [P51-V14](../research/p51-realism/P51-V14/README.md), [US-V18c](../research/ugly-stik-finish-v6.md#us-v18c--fuel-and-radio-installation), [TT-19](../research/timber-integration/TT-19/README.md).

- **Make small-geometry collision probes prove they can hit.** In the US-V09c investigation on Godot 4.7.2, the triangle-intersection epsilon missed millimetre-sized triangles expressed in metres. Scale the probe and geometry consistently, retain a known intersection and use a deliberate overlap to verify rejection. Recheck the threshold on a different engine version. [US-V09c](../research/ugly-stik-finish-v6.md#us-v09c--throttle-linkage).

- **Winding, shading normals and holes need separate checks.** An inward-wound open fuselage passed structural preflight and appeared correct in Blender's two-sided renders, but Godot showed its inner wall. Check outward facing area and preview with back-face culling. In the US-V13 Blender import, flipping a face also flipped its custom corner normals; preserve and restore those vectors when repairing that case. Test closed-hole fixtures separately from open notches. [F4U-18](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-18/README.md), [US-V13](../research/ugly-stik-finish-v6.md#us-v13--os-61-sf-engine-from-the-owners-blender-model), [mesh tooling](../research/ugly-stik-tooling-investigations/01-polygons-and-meshes.md).

- **Indexed and unindexed meshes can merge invisibly.** In US-V17, `SurfaceTool.append_from` kept all positions but dropped unindexed plate triangles when merged with indexed primitives. Index custom triangles before the merge and inspect resulting index counts and rendered geometry; bounds and vertex counts alone did not catch it. [US-V17](../research/ugly-stik-finish-v6.md#us-v17--servos-and-control-hardware).

- **Verify imported rig frames and small rotations.** A glTF pivot's rest basis can be lost when a `rotation` component replaces its basis; carry the rest transform on a mount and drive an identity child, then check a witness vertex. Float32 `acos`-based angles can also report zero or spurious differences for tiny rotations; compare with a stable vector-part measure and a positive control. [TT-05](../research/timber-integration/TT-05/README.md), [FW-MOON-03b](../research/field-world/FW-MOON-03b/README.md), [F4U-05](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-05/README.md).

- **Audit the exact imported assets and their settings.** Source packages can contain mixed or conflicting per-item license metadata; retain the package identity and publisher/included terms, and resolve conflicts before reuse. Bulk reimport can reset compression, mipmaps or normal-map mode, and an asset may lack a material despite a suitable thumbnail. Inspect the delivered files and imported settings. [SC-HUM-07-R2](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-HUM-07-R1/README.md#sc-hum-07-r2--faces-and-licence-audit-2026-10-25), [SC-HUM-07-R1](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-HUM-07-R1/README.md#verification).

## Rendering and visual evidence

- **Measure the actual subject and production path.** An airplane difference mask included its shadow and HUD, a scene comparison changed field presentation, and an ablation bypassed the active shader. Hold camera, lighting and scene switches fixed, retain the same shadow in airplane-on/off pairs, and prove masks and active-path ablations reject an injected defect. Inspect local or low-percentile contrast; a broad mean can hide a small aircraft or seam. [L11a](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L11a/README.md), [L9c-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L9c-R1/README.md#club-field-ablation-repair-2026-10-21), [E3c2b](../research/ground-contact/E3c2b/README.md), [L6c](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L6c/README.md).

- **Freeze pose and resource context for exact pixel comparisons.** Pausing simulation ticks did not freeze the physics interpolation fraction; rebuilding meshes in one process also changed opaque draw order. Pin the rendered physical pose, assert transforms, and use independent process starts when allocation order matters. Record state alongside frames so a visual change is not mistaken for a physics change. [TOD-09b](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-09b/README.md#capture-oracle-diagnostic), [allocation controls](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-09b/static-cell-diagnostic/README.md#follow-up-mesh-allocation-context), [SC-25](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-25/README.md).

- **Measure temporal artifacts against the right motion.** A pixel difference includes expected camera motion, and one still cannot establish a particle trail or tree animation. Use a zero-effect control and an independent, higher-resolution motion reference for aliasing; replay the effect over time and check adjacent frames, including entry at image edges and clock wrap. [L9c-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L9c-R1/README.md), [L15b](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L15b/README.md), [smoke timing](../research/smoke-investigations/godot-evidence/README.md).

- **Use the renderer that owns the data.** Headless MultiMesh transforms and texture snapshots were dummy or empty. Validate instance placement, imported/generated textures and shader inputs with the consuming renderer, then reload saved bakes and inspect captures. In an October 2026 Compatibility/llvmpipe probe, MultiMesh custom data truncated to half-float precision, unsuitable for exact world coordinates; the report does not record the engine version. SUN-09 also found individual Godot 4.7.2 GLES3 transform reads costly. Verify precision and retrieval on the target renderer. [L11a](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L11a/README.md), [SC-09-R1](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-09-R1/README.md), [SC-BIRD-08](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-BIRD-08/README.md), [SUN-09](../research/sun/SUN-02b-06-09/README.md).

- **Keep metadata, texture samples and lighting in their actual color spaces.** A grass shader divided encoded samples by a linear mean and clipped white; raw vertex tags lost variation when treated as colors. Check the backend's representation and `OUTPUT_IS_SRGB`, and compare rendered reference panels rather than assuming `source_color` resolves every path. Packed data such as blue-channel turf tone must not be discarded by normal-map import. [RC grass repair](../research/rc-ground-implementation-2026-10-15/README.md#color-space-repair-found-during-integration), [VQ-07 tags](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/material-tags-2026-10-22/README.md), [L9e](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L9e/README.md).

- **Check tangent direction, not just its existence.** Godot 4.7 generated an arbitrary tangent frame for a US-V09b mesh lacking authored tangents, so a normal map looked wrong despite unit and orthogonality checks. Compare stored tangents with UV-derived directions; also test whether texture precision and import compression preserve the intended small slopes. [US-V09b](../research/ugly-stik-finish-v6.md#us-v09b--film-over-structure).

- **Probe backend-specific shader behavior.** Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility ignored per-instance transparency and lit two-sided geometry according to winding; its result cannot be generalized to another version or backend. A derivative-dependent screen filter fed to another shader derivative can also produce undefined higher-order derivatives. Test a small native fixture and compare pixels on the consuming renderer. [VQ-05](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-05/README.md), [LU-12](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-11/materials.md).

- **Test exposure and color across rendering backends.** In the recorded Compatibility night path, encoding, sky cubemap exposure and even unshaded output behaved differently from a naive linear calculation; reusing its inverse conversion in Forward+ made night lights too bright. Probe known pixels at day and night exposures, keep backend-specific conversion at the output boundary, and match sky and fog in linear color. Do not reuse the 4.7-era formulas on another engine/backend without measurement. [FW-24](../research/field-world/FW-24/README.md), [VQ-07](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-07/high-trial-2026-10-21/README.md#renderer-corrections), [L1/L2](../research/landscape-investigations/02-atmosphere-aerial-perspective.md).

- **Reject saturated color channels when measuring rendered light.** A luminance-only cutoff accepted pixels with clipped blue, making a moonlit aureole appear a tenth of its predicted brightness. Reject pixels with any clipped channel and calibrate radiance-to-display scale in the same frame setup. [FW-MOON-08](../research/field-world/FW-MOON-08/README.md#2-verification).

- **Rebuilds must retire the old global visual state.** A scene-local lighting snapshot left a global sky controller free to overwrite it, and prepared materials could change shader globals outside the live tree. Pass one explicit snapshot to all consumers and test a transition from a running world, including rejected preparation at nonzero animation time. Track values sent to shader globals in CPU state when runtime readback is unavailable. [Rendering integration](../research/branch-integration-2026-10-10.md), [TOD-08a](../research/time-of-day-investigations/TOD-08a/README.md).

- **Size and fade optical effects in the live projection.** A fixed camera reference made a wing light grow into an opaque patch when zooming; fading before a saturated cap made another lamp stay bright until the end of its range. Use current FOV and viewport, cap sized energy before the range fade, and compare real on/off pixels across zoom and exposure with a visible-light control and an opaque blocker. [FW-62-R1](../research/field-world/FW-35/README.md#2026-10-13--fw-62-r1-distant-wing-blocks), [LU-09](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-07/README.md).

- **Inspect the full render transform and deformation envelope.** Collision bounds based only on atlas occupancy missed card scale and asymmetric origins; undeformed AABBs missed shader motion. Include instance scale, placement, animation and transparent padding in render/collision checks. Express sway in world units when assets use different source scales. [LU-11](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-11/collision-coverage.md), [L15b-R2](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L15b-R2/README.md#collision-stays-unchanged).

- **Choose import and LODs from rendered results.** Headless imports left script-bound textures without expected compression or mipmaps, producing memory and distant-aliasing defects. Generated tree LODs removed leaf cards; coarse alpha mips could erase what remained. Pin intended import settings, inspect each surface's LODs, and compare near and distant pixels before accepting simplification. [LU-34](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-34/README.md#optimization-applied-gpu-compressed-vegetation-textures), [VQ-02a-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/VQ-02a/roof-mipmaps/README.md), [L6c-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L6c/species-variety/README.md#distant-landscapelab-foliage).

- **Budget instancing with actual draws and LOD selection.** A MultiMesh selects LOD for a batch rather than separately for every tree; changing spatial cells changes both culling and appearance. In the Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility experiment, the primitive counter undercounted instanced LOD surfaces. Attribute visible surfaces and texture memory per subsystem, measure representative views and per-instance cost, verify the counter, and keep image comparisons when changing batching. [L8a-R1](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L8a-R1/README.md#cost), [LU-34-R1](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-34/README.md#revision-lu-34-r1-instanced-lod-meshes-were-undercounted).

- **Budget radiance use and reflection atlas capacity.** In Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility, changing a sky uniform dirtied radiance, but rebuilt the cubemap only when the environment used sky radiance; a pass-specific shader amplifier distinguished the cases from noisy frame times. One reflection probe also reserved the configured atlas capacity, not merely one slot. Verify the actual environment and atlas allocation on the target backend. [FW-MOON-01](../research/field-world/FW-MOON-01/README.md#31-radiance-when-the-engine-renders-the-cubemap-q1-q2), [LU-22](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-22/README.md#rendering-and-query-cost).

- **Keep field geometry and the pilot's sightline together.** A barrier outside the runway footprint still hid its near edge, and custom fields retained a default ID despite moved geometry. Check placement against the rendered field and the actual pilot-eye sightline; share that decision with collision and compare on/off projected runway pixels. [L10b](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L10b/README.md), [L10c](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L10c/README.md), [LU-06](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-06/README.md#functional-and-regression-checks).

- **Treat renderer options and source inspection as hypotheses.** Godot 4.8-beta1 read Forward+ multi-bounce occlusion at renderer startup, while contact-shadow settings had a different runtime path. Verify fresh processes and actual pixels on the selected backend; source code, a headless compile or a different renderer cannot establish the visible result. [GDU-11g/h](../research/engine-upgrade/GDU-11/implementation.md), [SC-01](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-01/README.md).

## Cameras and presentation

- **Spring the camera offset and test moving targets.** A spring on world position trails steady flight by a speed-dependent distance. Spring the target-relative offset; a closed-form step is exact only for a stationary goal during that step. Test turns and loops at different frame rates with interpolated target motion, and seed look-ahead from simulation velocity when entering the view. [CAM-08](../research/camera-views/CAM-01-10/README.md), [CAM-24](../research/camera-views/CAM-23-27/README.md).

- **Give pixel targets a reference height.** A fixed 30-pixel target rendered the aircraft half as large on 4K as on 1080p, while an independent warning used 720p. Define readable size as a share of the stated 1080p reference and derive all consumers from the same target. Qualify the primary 1080p view first; 720p is a fallback. [CAM-23](../research/camera-views/CAM-23-27/README.md), [resolution policy](../../AGENTS.md#display-resolution-priorities).

- **Profile collision queries where the camera actually operates.** The CAM-09 clearance query was cheap beside an isolated obstacle but exceeded budget at the pilot station, where many proxies shared the same bins and a general nearby query copied them. Test dense and sparse locations, and bound candidate work before exact intersection tests. [CAM-09](../research/camera-views/CAM-09/README.md).

- **Presentation physics must preserve the flight model's invariants.** Plausible wreck-part mass shares moved the intact wreck CG away from the aircraft's CG, and reducing a settling damper exposed contact tunnelling it had masked. Compare shared mass/CG invariants and establish what an existing clamp or damper compensates for before adjusting it. [CR-09d](../research/crash-damage-investigations/CR-09d/README.md).
