## Step-ID namespaces

Use the registry's prefix for new work. Repair substeps use `<step>-R<n>`; spell `P51-V01` and `US-V01` when bare visual IDs would be ambiguous. M1…M5 belong to the main roadmap. A new track registers its prefix and owned paths here before implementation. Do not duplicate a step's status across plans.

## Conventions for documents

1. Write current guides and substantively revised plans in English, short and precise. Date research snapshots and keep their original evidence scope clear.
2. A guide states what exists, where it lives, how to use/check it and its limitations. A plan additionally states owner, prefix, owned paths, pending steps and acceptance evidence.
3. Give each topic one home. Link to canonical commands, controls and contracts instead of repeating them. Active plan steps use `ID | work | proof/status`; routine results belong beside the evidence.
4. Keep sources, provenance, raw measurements and required release/decision records. Consolidate useful current information before deleting completed/superseded plans or disposable notes. Do not accumulate plan archives or cleanup reports.
5. Use relative links that resolve in a fresh checkout. `references/`, `.tools/`, `app/captures/` and `dist/` are local/generated outputs, not committed documentation targets. Commit new evidence together with its links.
6. Generated artifacts name their source/generator and are regenerated, never hand-edited. Preserve historical manifests and hashes as records of their original snapshot.
7. Record decisions in DECISIONS; reusable lessons follow [LEARNINGS criteria](../LEARNINGS.md#keeping-this-useful). Preserve research under `docs/research/` and scripts/data under root `research/`.

The original platform work is retained in [the initial notebook](research/initial-notebook.md) and the frozen [bake-off spec/comparison](../prototypes/stage0/COMPARISON.md). Those records explain earlier choices without defining current runtime behavior.
