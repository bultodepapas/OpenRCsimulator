# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.2-rc.1

> **PRE-RELEASE CANDIDATE.** v0.1.1 remains the latest stable release. This candidate is for early software testing and does not establish physical flight validation.

A prerelease candidate for the next patch after v0.1.1. OpenRC Simulator remains an early-development project; automated checks and model behavior do not establish physical flight validation.

## Deferred physical acceptance for this prerelease — 2026-10-16

These physical checks are deferred for this prerelease; none counts as a pass:

- **Ugly Stik rudder recovery:** the local 15 m/s doublet ends at 15.13°/s against a 15°/s limit; see the [rudder-authority diagnostic](../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/rudder-authority-diagnostic/README.md).
- **Yaw damping (R02):** the comparison remains explicitly open at 7.75% against a 7% limit; see the [R02 damping diagnostic](../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/damping-lag-diagnostic/README.md).
- **P-51D P51-22:** powered takeoff and inside-loop acceptance remain pending; see the [P51-22 report](../research/p51-realism/P51-22/README.md) and details below.

The suite executes both Stik checks with dedicated allow-known arguments and reports one pending result from each. Their standalone scripts remain strict by default. Missing or non-finite samples, analytic consistency, stability and refinement failures remain fatal. The aircraft selector discloses the Stik's pending recovery and damping acceptance in English and Spanish. CI rejects a stable release tag while these deferrals are enabled.

## Experimental P-51D: P51-22 remains pending

The aircraft selector labels the P-51D as experimental with P51-22 pending in English and Spanish. Its known powered wing/tail coupling regression delays lift-off and prevents the intended coordinated inside loop. These physical acceptance results are deferred for this prerelease, not passed. See the [P51-22 report](../research/p51-realism/P51-22/README.md).

The release suite still executes both maneuvers and reports their three known acceptance failures as `PENDING P51-22`; this deferral does not turn them into passes. Numerical safety, loading, crash, ground-roll and other handling checks remain mandatory. Run `test_p51_ground.gd` and `test_p51_handling.gd` directly without `--allow-known-p51-22` to enforce the original strict physical acceptance checks. No physics coefficients or acceptance thresholds were changed.

## Changes since v0.1.1

- Adds a Settings screen for windowed or borderless fullscreen mode, interface scaling, opaque panels, reduced motion, language and menu volume. A window-mode change reverts after 15 seconds unless kept; other presentation changes have explicit Keep/Revert controls.
- Rebuilds Home as a compact, scrollable flight setup rail with separate Flight, Aircraft, Practice, Workshop and Garage pages while keeping the primary flight actions available. Adds menu focus and action sounds with an adjustable level. [Menu evidence](../research/menu-investigations/README.md).
- Adds profile-based gamepad actions for menu navigation and flight controls, with optional actions for supported aircraft equipment.
- Adds simulated retractable landing gear and flaps for the P-51D and Ventage Jet. P-51 flap settings are 15° for takeoff and up to 45°; Ventage Jet settings are 20° for takeoff and up to 50°. Gear mechanics, actuator times and aerodynamic behavior still include estimates and have not been flight-identified.
- Adds a post-crash incident report with component damage, repair and replacement estimates, and catalog assumptions. Pending inspection or unknown condition keeps eligible salvage and total-loss values unknown instead of displaying zero; historical reports are not rewritten. Reports can be incomplete; prices, labor and repair outcomes are estimates, not market quotes or repair instructions. [Salvage-estimate correction](../research/crash-damage-investigations/CR-31h/README.md).
- Extends the shared simulation kernel with a common environment interface, stiff-contact substeps and generalized-coordinate solves. A research-grade multirotor plant also uses the shared kernel; a flyable drone or race activity is not included.
- Adds optional configured radio, gamepad or joystick input to the detached articulated HX-700 mechanics preview, using existing controller profiles. Focus loss or device disconnect pauses the preview; it remains separate from the flyable helicopter trainer and does not change its flight model. Physical control and flight qualification remain open. [Inspector evidence](../research/helicopter/HELI-26a/README.md).
- Adds the experimental MONSTER CAR 2 nitro vehicle alongside Bisonte MT8, with its supplied articulated model, engine speed and fuel state. Windows and Linux packages include the qualified native ground evaluator; macOS uses the GDScript reference implementation. Physical handling and target-machine performance remain unqualified.
- Refreshes field visuals with runway and mown surfaces integrated into the ground pass for supported layouts, reusable generated lake geometry, and updated aircraft-light and shadow framing. Cloud shadows and haze remain in the ground pass; L9c motion acceptance and target-hardware performance remain open. [Surface evidence](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L9c/README.md); [landscape evidence](../research/landscape-upgrade/README.md).
- Adds detailed tubular-frame folding chairs at the pits, retaining their seeded placement and fabric colours; this is a visual estimate with no new downloaded asset. [Chair verification](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-08-R4/README.md).
- Reduces parked-car geometry and bench tessellation to keep the detailed field within its unchanged geometry limits. [Landscape verification](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-21/assets-research.md#release-budget-verification--2026-10-16) covers the original downloaded models, regenerated outputs and paired appearance captures.

## Verification and limits

An earlier [native Windows source smoke](../research/release-readiness/PT1-v0.1.2/README.md) at `6ce6eec9` passed five three-second trimmed flights and 24 controller pause-recovery checks. It is evidence from that source revision, not qualification of this candidate package or physical aircraft behavior.

The release workflow gates publication on application checks, the archived prototype tests, native Windows/Linux evaluator checks and all three desktop exports. These checks cover software and package behavior; they do not establish physical flight accuracy, radio compatibility, pilot acceptance or target-machine performance. Aircraft configurations remain experimental, and their modeled flap and gear behavior needs independent measurement and pilot review.
