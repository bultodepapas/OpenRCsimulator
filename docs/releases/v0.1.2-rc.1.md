# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.2 — release work draft

> **UNPUBLISHED DRAFT.** v0.1.1 remains the latest stable release. No public v0.1.2 stable or prerelease package is available. These notes summarize current-source work and are provisional until release blockers are resolved and the final source is frozen.

This is working documentation for the intended next stable patch after v0.1.1, not a release announcement. The change list is provisional and must be reconciled against all commits since v0.1.1 when the source is frozen. OpenRC Simulator remains an early-development project; automated checks and model behavior do not establish physical flight validation.

## Open physical acceptance before a stable release — 2026-10-16

For stable v0.1.2, these acceptance checks remain open; these notes neither waive them nor claim a pass:

- **Ugly Stik rudder recovery:** the retained baseline reaches a body yaw-rate magnitude of 15.125273°/s at 240 Hz and 15.200616°/s at 480 Hz against a 15°/s screen. The 12-second extension is diagnostic, not acceptance evidence. See the [rudder-authority diagnostic](../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/rudder-authority-diagnostic/README.md) and [retained exact-replay evidence](../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/rudder-authority-diagnostic/stable-20261016/README.md).
- **Yaw damping (R02):** the α=11° comparison remains open at 7.75% against a 7% limit; see the [R02 damping diagnostic](../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/damping-lag-diagnostic/README.md).
- **P-51D P51-22:** powered takeoff and inside-loop acceptance remain pending; see the [P51-22 report](../research/p51-realism/P51-22/README.md) and details below.

The stable-release work must resolve and reverify these gates on the final frozen source. Their pending status is not physical qualification; analytic consistency, finite-sample, stability and refinement checks remain software requirements as well.

The current suite still uses narrow `--allow-known-` arguments to report these failures as pending while other software checks run. Standalone physical checks remain strict, and CI rejects a stable tag while those arguments remain. A green development run therefore does not clear these physical gates.

## Experimental P-51D: P51-22 remains open

The aircraft selector labels the P-51D as experimental with P51-22 pending in English and Spanish. Its known powered wing/tail coupling regression delays lift-off and prevents the intended coordinated inside loop. Stable-release acceptance remains open. See the [P51-22 report](../research/p51-realism/P51-22/README.md).

The intended release must keep the powered takeoff and inside-loop checks strict. Numerical safety, loading, crash, ground-roll and other handling checks remain mandatory. The pending selector status does not mean these physical checks passed.

## Current-source changes under review

- Adds a Settings screen for windowed or borderless fullscreen mode, interface scaling, opaque panels, reduced motion, language and menu volume. A window-mode change reverts after 15 seconds unless kept; other presentation changes have explicit Keep/Revert controls.
- Rebuilds Home as a compact, scrollable flight setup rail with separate Flight, Aircraft, Practice, Workshop and Garage pages while keeping the primary flight actions available. Adds menu focus and action sounds with an adjustable level. [Menu evidence](../research/menu-investigations/README.md).
- Adds profile-based gamepad actions for menu navigation and flight controls, with optional actions for supported aircraft equipment.
- Adds simulated retractable landing gear and flaps for the P-51D and Ventage Jet. P-51 flap settings are 15° for takeoff and up to 45°; Ventage Jet settings are 20° for takeoff and up to 50°. Gear mechanics, actuator times and aerodynamic behavior still include estimates and have not been flight-identified.
- Adds a post-crash incident report with component damage, repair and replacement estimates, and catalog assumptions. Pending inspection or unknown condition keeps eligible salvage and total-loss values unknown instead of displaying zero; historical reports are not rewritten. Reports can be incomplete; prices, labor and repair outcomes are estimates, not market quotes or repair instructions. [Salvage-estimate correction](../research/crash-damage-investigations/CR-31h/README.md).
- Extends the shared simulation kernel with a common environment interface, stiff-contact substeps and generalized-coordinate solves. A research-grade multirotor plant also uses the shared kernel; a flyable drone or race activity is not included.
- Adds optional configured radio, gamepad or joystick input to the detached articulated HX-700 mechanics preview, using existing controller profiles. Focus loss or device disconnect pauses the preview; it remains separate from the flyable helicopter trainer and does not change its flight model. Physical control and flight qualification remain open. [Inspector evidence](../research/helicopter/HELI-26a/README.md).
- Adds the experimental MONSTER CAR 2 nitro vehicle alongside Bisonte MT8, with its supplied articulated model, engine speed and fuel state. The source selects the native ground evaluator on Windows/Linux and the GDScript reference implementation on macOS. Physical handling and target-machine performance remain unqualified.
- Refreshes field visuals with runway and mown surfaces integrated into the ground pass for supported layouts, reusable generated lake geometry, and updated aircraft-light and shadow framing. Cloud shadows and haze remain in the ground pass; L9c motion acceptance and target-hardware performance remain open. [Surface evidence](../research/visual-quality-implementation/L9c/README.md); [landscape evidence](../research/landscape-upgrade/README.md).
- Adds detailed tubular-frame folding chairs at the pits, retaining their seeded placement and fabric colours; this is a visual estimate with no new downloaded asset. [Chair verification](../research/scenery-implementation/SC-08-R4/README.md).
- Reduces parked-car geometry and bench tessellation to keep the detailed field within its unchanged geometry limits. [Landscape verification](../research/landscape-upgrade/LU-21/assets-research.md#release-budget-verification--2026-10-16) covers the original downloaded models, regenerated outputs and paired appearance captures.

## Verification and limits

An earlier [native Windows source smoke](../research/release-readiness/PT1-v0.1.2/README.md) at `6ce6eec9` passed five three-second trimmed flights and 24 controller pause-recovery checks. It is evidence from that source revision, not verification of the current full source tree or physical aircraft behavior.

All release checks must be rerun against the final source and export packages before publication. Software and package checks do not establish physical flight accuracy, radio compatibility, pilot acceptance or target-machine performance. Aircraft configurations remain experimental, and their modeled flap and gear behavior needs independent measurement and pilot review.
