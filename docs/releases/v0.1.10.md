# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.10

Software release of the early-alpha simulator, following the v0.1.9-beta.1 preview. Aircraft and other vehicle models are experimental; software checks do not establish physical flight accuracy or pilot acceptance. The release tag and accompanying manifest identify the source commit and packaged files.

## Changes since v0.1.8

These include everything previewed in v0.1.9-beta.1.

### Aircraft and flying

- The experimental F4U Corsair uses the g2.2 visual model with thirteen paint schemes, folding wings, a sliding canopy, a tailhook and crash geometry. The canopy and tailhook are presentation mechanisms; cowl flaps await a new model delivery. The flight model remains unvalidated. [F4U plan](../F4U-PLAN.md) · [Paint evidence](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-20/README.md) · [Mechanisms](../research/f4u-corsair/F4U-21/README.md)
- Adds the experimental MoonRunner X99 flying wing with elevons, an electric pusher, a finite 6S battery and a **Hand launch** start for aircraft without landing gear. Its flight model and throw require physical and pilot validation. [MoonRunner plan](../X8-PLAN.md)
- The Ugly Stik model gains an O.S. MAX 61 SF engine, an epoxy birch-ply firewall, a two-servo wing, a servo tray with throttle linkage, and its fuel and radio installation. Its ground contacts now follow the drawn airframe, with per-surface friction; the runway strip behaves as turf.
- **Trim practice** on Home starts an airplane naturally out of trim, as a new model often is, so you can learn to trim it in flight. Your trims stay after crashes and restarts, and when you fly it again.
- Optional training **Failures**: set a per-flight probability, choose the aircraft's available failure types and whether to include catastrophic ones. At most one failure is scheduled per flight, 20–120 seconds after takeoff and at least 5 m above the terrain.
- Adds local flight statistics for supported fixed-wing Home flights, including pilot and aircraft views, history, reset controls and an optional session overlay. Helicopter, ground-vehicle, replay and Workshop trial activities do not contribute. [Statistics guide](../FLIGHT-STATISTICS.md)
- Adds opt-in spatial wind, smooth gusts and optional kinematic thermal fields sampled at aerodynamic and propulsion locations. Uniform weather remains available and the interactive default is unchanged. [Wind guide](../WIND-ATMOSPHERE-REFERENCE.md)
- Adds configurable aircraft lighting profiles and Workshop controls for colors, patterns and bounded show effects, including paired landing lamps. Night readability and performance gates remain open. [Lighting implementation and qualification](../research/night-flight-lighting/implementation.md)
- Crashes sample the ground they hit and leave ground marks.

### The field and landscape

- The landscape is more natural: grass color measured from a real mown lawn, less visible texture repetition, front-lit tree crowns, mowing stripes, runway touchdown wear and a patchwork of fields in the distance. [Landscape evidence](../research/landscape-improvement-2026-10-07/implementation/README.md)
- Meadow plants grow around the runway and pilot stations, scaled by the Scene detail preset.
- Birds fly over the field by time of day and weather: vultures and storks in thermals, kites, swallows and evening swifts. A **Birds** setting chooses how many.
- Pilots and spectators stand at the field, with natural hand poses and a Radiolink T12D transmitter.
- An optional RC car dirt circuit can be shown on the airplane field (Settings > Display > **RC circuit**). Aircraft wheels and collisions use the same circuit surfaces and track furniture. [RC monster truck guide](../../research/rc-monster-truck/README.md)
- New **Shadows** settings, with explanations when a shadow option is unavailable.

### Performance

- **Linux:** flight physics runs on a native (C++) kernel by default for the aircraft catalog. On the test machine, a physics tick cost 1.65–2.76× less than the previous GDScript path across glow, electric, liquid-fuel and turbine aircraft (median 2.11×), with outputs verified against the reference. Unsupported configurations fall back to the reference automatically. Windows and macOS keep the reference path. [NK-3 evidence](../research/native-kernel/NK-3/README.md)
- **macOS:** observer and shader optimizations gave 17.6–23.2% higher sustained frame rate over three 30-minute flights each on a MacBook Air M1 (8 GB, Mobile/Metal renderer, Medium scenery). A steady 60 Hz is not yet reached on that machine. [Apple Silicon evidence](../research/desktop-delivery-investigations/DT-MAC-02/README.md#repeated-30-minute-metal-comparison)
- Lighter grass, cloud, ground, sky and water shaders; cheaper ground, hull and lake contact checks with unchanged results; and fewer repeated lighting, paint and audio updates.
- Smoother presentation: loading stays responsive while the first trim is solved, and several audio and camera hitches are fixed.

### Controls

- Calibrate a radio from Home's **Controls**, without starting a flight. The calibration goes into your draft settings and is applied when you save.
- Calibration now asks you to start and finish the throttle step at LOW, and rejects a step in which more than one channel moved (check your transmitter mixes if this appears).
- Airplane dual rates work with gamepads (press the left stick) and joysticks.
- When saving controller settings fails, the field at fault is highlighted and explained.

### Cameras and graphics

- New camera aids: a **Fly-by** camera, a **Magnifier** inset for a distant aircraft, an adjustable chase-camera field of view and better wreck framing, with tooltips for camera settings.
- The optional High graphics profile adds selectable FSR2 reconstruction and sharpness. A separate Windows NVIDIA build (not part of these downloads) adds DLSS, DLAA and Reflex. [Graphics modes](../../tools/graphics/README.md)
- Changes the pinned engine from Godot 4.7.2 to official **4.8-beta2**, a prerelease. Compatibility remains the default renderer. [Beta 2 migration](../research/engine-upgrade/GDU-12/README.md) · [Remaining engine gates](../plans/godot-4.8-upgrade.md)

### Languages

- The interface is available in nine languages: English, Español, 简体中文, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Русский and 日本語. English remains the first-run default. [Interface languages](../../app/i18n/README.md)
- The aircraft paint kits include sharing instructions in all nine languages.

### Fixes

- Saving controller settings after calibrating a radio or joystick no longer fails with "Temporary controller profiles failed validation". Real axis readings did not survive the profile file's text format exactly; v0.1.8 and v0.1.9-beta.1 are affected. [D6-GP-02-R1](../research/controller-support-2026-10-14/README.md#calibrated-profile-save-2026-11-04-d6-gp-02-r1)
- Controls no longer polls hidden controller previews.
- Turbo Lumber custom paints made with the v0.1.9-beta.1 paint kit must be packed again with this release's kit; the paint images and UV layout are unchanged.
- Aircraft validation messages show the actual reason, weather preset lists fit at large text, the MoonRunner X99 rests on its hull in the Home view, and Home Statistics keeps keyboard navigation.

## Downloads and verification

The release provides Windows x86_64, Linux x86_64 and macOS universal ZIP archives, `release-manifest.json`, `linux-abi.json` and `SHA256SUMS`. Archives are unsigned; the macOS app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. [Release procedure](../TOOLS.md#release-sequence) · [First-launch guide](../FIRST-LAUNCH.md)

The AI companion profile was re-bound to the current solver after its nine retained tapes replayed byte-identically against the previously accepted source. [Receipt](../research/ai-companions/AFC-02/rebinds/2026-11-04-90fb5ae1-to-880d1911.json)

Performance figures are from named machines and workloads; they do not qualify other hardware or whole-application frame rate. Native Linux desktop X11/Wayland and macOS execution, physical radio/device behavior, target-GPU performance and pilot/readability acceptance remain separate from export and headless checks. The Stik sideslip and R02 yaw comparisons, Gate 2 and Gate P remain open. [Platform limits](../research/engine-upgrade/GDU-03/README.md#remaining-platform-scope) · [Roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md)

Aircraft, helicopter and RC ground-vehicle models remain experimental or under evaluation. Multirotor work is research groundwork, with no playable drone or racing activity. [Supported vehicle paths](../STACK.md#supported-vehicle-paths)
