# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.2 — Draft, unpublished

> **DRAFT — UNPUBLISHED.** v0.1.1 remains the latest published release. The proposed v0.1.2 package and checksum links below are not available yet.

A patch update to v0.1.1. OpenRC Simulator remains an early-development project; automated checks and model behavior do not establish physical flight validation.

## Open release blockers — 2026-10-16

Publication is blocked by unresolved regression and acceptance gates:

- **P-51D lift-off and loop:** the P51-22 powered wing/tail regression remains open; see the [P51-22 report](../research/p51-realism/P51-22/README.md).
- **Ugly Stik rudder recovery:** the local 15 m/s doublet ends at 15.13°/s against a 15°/s limit; see the [rudder-authority diagnostic](../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/rudder-authority-diagnostic/README.md).
- **Yaw damping (R02):** the comparison remains explicitly open at 7.75% against a 7% limit; see the [R02 damping diagnostic](../research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/damping-lag-diagnostic/README.md).
- **Scenery triangle ceilings:** the 2026-10-16 local checkout preflight measured 3,184/3,000 architecture triangles and 654,903/650,000 complete-field triangles. The checks are [`test_landscape_architecture.gd`](../../app/tests/test_landscape_architecture.gd) and [`test_landscape_vegetation.gd`](../../app/tests/test_landscape_vegetation.gd); these local results do not establish a qualified published package.

## Changes since v0.1.1

- Adds a Settings screen for windowed or borderless fullscreen mode, interface scaling, opaque panels, reduced motion, language and menu volume. A window-mode change reverts after 15 seconds unless kept; other presentation changes have explicit Keep/Revert controls.
- Adds profile-based gamepad actions for menu navigation and flight controls, with optional actions for supported aircraft equipment.
- Adds simulated retractable landing gear and flaps for the P-51D and Ventage Jet. P-51 flap settings are 15° for takeoff and up to 45°; Ventage Jet settings are 20° for takeoff and up to 50°. Gear mechanics, actuator times and aerodynamic behavior still include estimates and have not been flight-identified.
- Adds a post-crash incident report with component damage, repair and replacement estimates, and catalog assumptions. Reports can be incomplete; prices, labor and repair outcomes are estimates, not market quotes or repair instructions.
- Extends the shared simulation kernel with a common environment interface, stiff-contact substeps and generalized-coordinate solves. A research-grade multirotor plant also uses the shared kernel; a flyable drone or race activity is not included.
- Adds a detached articulated HX-700 mechanics preview for inspection. It is separate from the flyable helicopter trainer and does not change its flight model.
- Adds the experimental MONSTER CAR 2 nitro vehicle alongside Bisonte MT8, with its supplied articulated model, engine speed and fuel state. Windows and Linux packages include the qualified native ground evaluator; macOS uses the GDScript reference implementation. Physical handling and target-machine performance remain unqualified.

## Verification and limits

The release workflow gates publication on application checks, the archived prototype tests, native Windows/Linux evaluator checks and all three desktop exports. These checks cover software and package behavior; they do not establish physical flight accuracy, radio compatibility, pilot acceptance or target-machine performance. Aircraft configurations remain experimental, and their modeled flap and gear behavior needs independent measurement and pilot review.
