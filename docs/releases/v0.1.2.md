# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.2 — release notes

OpenRC Simulator v0.1.2 is a stable software release of an early-alpha simulator. Aircraft and experimental features remain under evaluation; this release does not claim independent physical flight validation.

The experimental-aircraft limits below are disclosed model limitations. They do not block this software release and are not represented as passes.

## Experimental-aircraft limits — 2026-10-16

- **Ugly Stik rudder recovery:** the retained baseline reaches a body yaw-rate magnitude of 15.125273°/s at 240 Hz and 15.200616°/s at 480 Hz against the 15°/s internal screen. The 12-second extension is diagnostic, not acceptance evidence. Independent aircraft evaluation remains open. See the [rudder-authority diagnostic](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/rudder-authority-diagnostic/README.md) and [retained exact-replay evidence](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/rudder-authority-diagnostic/stable-20261016/README.md).
- **Yaw damping (R02):** the α=11° comparison is 7.75% against the 7% screening threshold. This model comparison is not a physical measurement; evaluation remains open. See the [R02 damping diagnostic](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R11/damping-lag-diagnostic/README.md).
- **Experimental P-51D (P51-22):** powered takeoff and inside-loop acceptance remain pending. The aircraft selector identifies the model as experimental and shows this status. See the [P51-22 report](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/p51-realism/P51-22/README.md).

These items are not stable-release gates. They remain visible limitations for anyone evaluating the affected aircraft; a software release does not qualify their flight behavior.

## Changes

- Adds Settings for windowed or borderless fullscreen, interface scale, opaque panels, reduced motion, language and menu volume. A window-mode change reverts after 15 seconds unless kept; other presentation changes have explicit Keep/Revert controls.
- Rebuilds Home as a compact, scrollable flight-setup rail with separate Flight, Aircraft, Practice, Workshop and Garage pages while keeping the primary flight actions available. Adds menu focus and action sounds with an adjustable level. [Menu evidence](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/menu-investigations/README.md).
- Adds profile-based gamepad actions for menu navigation and flight controls, with optional actions for supported aircraft equipment.
- Adds simulated retractable landing gear and flaps for the P-51D and Ventage Jet. P-51 flap settings are 15° for takeoff and up to 45°; Ventage Jet settings are 20° for takeoff and up to 50°. Gear mechanics, actuator times and aerodynamic behavior still include estimates and have not been flight-identified.
- Adds a post-crash incident report with component damage, repair and replacement estimates, and catalog assumptions. Pending inspection or unknown condition keeps eligible salvage and total-loss values unknown instead of displaying zero; historical reports are not rewritten. Reports can be incomplete; prices, labor and repair outcomes are estimates, not market quotes or repair instructions. [Salvage-estimate correction](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/crash-damage-investigations/CR-31h/README.md).
- Extends the shared simulation kernel with a common environment interface, stiff-contact substeps and generalized-coordinate solves. A research-grade multirotor plant also uses the shared kernel; a flyable drone or race activity is not included.
- Adds optional configured radio, gamepad or joystick input to the detached articulated HX-700 mechanics preview, using existing controller profiles. Focus loss or device disconnect pauses the preview; it remains separate from the flyable helicopter trainer and does not change its flight model. Physical control and flight qualification remain open. [Inspector evidence](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/helicopter/HELI-26a/README.md).
- Adds the experimental MONSTER CAR 2 nitro vehicle alongside Bisonte MT8, with its supplied articulated model, engine speed and fuel state. The source selects the native ground evaluator on Windows/Linux and the GDScript reference implementation on macOS. Physical handling and target-machine performance remain unqualified.
- Refreshes field visuals with runway and mown surfaces integrated into the ground pass for supported layouts, reusable generated lake geometry, and updated aircraft-light and shadow framing. Cloud shadows and haze remain in the ground pass; L9c motion acceptance and target-hardware performance remain open. [Surface evidence](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/visual-quality-implementation/L9c/README.md); [landscape evidence](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/landscape-upgrade/README.md).
- Adds detailed tubular-frame folding chairs at the pits, retaining their seeded placement and fabric colours; this is a visual estimate with no new downloaded asset. [Chair verification](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/scenery-implementation/SC-08-R4/README.md).
- Reduces parked-car geometry and bench tessellation to keep the detailed field within its unchanged geometry limits. [Landscape verification](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.2/docs/research/landscape-upgrade/LU-21/assets-research.md#release-budget-verification--2026-10-16) covers the original downloaded models, regenerated outputs and paired appearance captures.

## Verification and limits

The release workflow gates publication on automated application, capture, native and platform-package checks. These checks verify software and archives; they do not establish physical flight accuracy, radio compatibility, pilot acceptance or target-machine performance. Aircraft configurations remain experimental, and flap and gear behavior, Stik rudder recovery, R02 yaw damping and P51-22 acceptance need further evaluation.
