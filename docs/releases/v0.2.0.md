# OpenRC Simulator v0.2.0

Early-alpha software release. Aircraft and other vehicle models remain experimental; software checks do not establish physical flight accuracy or pilot acceptance. The release tag and manifest identify the source and packaged files.

## Changes since v0.1.10

### Flying and feedback

- The optional Fuel tank instrument shows accepted fuel quantity with animated liquid that responds to aircraft attitude and load. The motion is a visual approximation, not a fuel-feed or physical slosh prediction. [Fuel instrument evidence](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/research/flight-overlays/HUD-01-10/README.md#liquid-fuel-tank-2026-11-06-hud-13-r14)
- Flight alerts have severity-colored cards, optional sound and motion, and controls for detail, advisory categories and training cues. Critical conditions bypass advisory switches and remain named in the overflow summary. Training thresholds are display cues, not aircraft operating limits. [Alert guide](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/plans/flight-overlays.md#warning-triggers)
- Wind-only Conditions presets now preserve custom atmosphere settings and unfinished atmosphere edits. The presets that replace both wind and atmosphere are labeled **Wind + Atmosphere**. [Weather preset scope](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/research/menu-investigations/UI-32/README.md#wind-preset-scope-ui-32-r1-2026-11-06)
- Glow-engine firing counters now follow accepted fuel-source transitions, including fuel loss. Older glow trace formats remain readable; normal checkpoint restore requires the current law. Glow installations requiring multiple landing-gear contact substeps are rejected at load rather than failing on contact. [Glow guide](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/GLOW-ENGINES.md)
- Flight statistics use the current field, lake and optional RC circuit for terrain and support, avoiding false airborne classification above raised circuit features. [Statistics guide](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/FLIGHT-STATISTICS.md)

### Graphics

- Apple Silicon now uses the Mobile renderer on Metal for the Standard profile, with a per-build recovery guard that can restart in Compatibility after an unconfirmed launch. MetalFX Spatial is available on supported Metal configurations; **Auto** selects it at sufficiently large output sizes and otherwise uses Native. Earlier unflagged Native preferences migrate to Auto. [Apple Silicon status](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/plans/apple-silicon-performance.md)
- Cloud rendering reuses a noise tile for ground shadows and sky detail. Performance and image-quality evidence remains specific to the tested configurations; broader hardware and sustained-flight qualification remain open. [Apple Silicon evidence](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/research/desktop-delivery-investigations/DT-MAC-00-R4/README.md)

### Fixes

- Hidden flight overlays stop redrawing warning cards. Alerts and the battery instrument keep clear on shorter screens; additional alerts remain in the overflow summary, including every hidden danger. The latency camera marker keeps its 192 px square stationary as alerts change.
- Workshop presets change only their declared settings and protect pending edits. Draft recovery and navigation preserve work through interrupted or refused departures. [Workshop guide](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/AIRCRAFT-WORKSHOP.md)
- Creating a new controller profile no longer reuses the saved profile's identity. Bound gear and flap commands retain their last accepted positions across pause, menu and focus boundaries until the channel is armed again.
- When saving recordings or statistics fails during exit or end flight, recovery offers retry, discard or stay choices. Keyboard focus recovery is improved when pause-menu controls disappear.

## Downloads and limits

The release provides Windows x86_64, Linux x86_64 and macOS universal ZIP archives, `release-manifest.json`, `linux-abi.json` and `SHA256SUMS`. Archives are unsigned; the macOS app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. [Release procedure](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/TOOLS.md#release-sequence) · [First-launch guide](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/FIRST-LAUNCH.md)

The AI companion solver profile was re-bound after all nine retained tapes produced byte-identical results against the previously accepted solver. This comparison preserves the existing qualification scope; it is not physical validation. [Rebind receipt](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/research/ai-companions/AFC-02/rebinds/2026-11-06-880d1911-to-84cac259.json)

The engine remains official **Godot 4.8-beta2**, a prerelease. Linux x86_64 can use the native flight evaluator for qualified configurations; unsupported or unverified configurations fall back to the GDScript reference. Windows and macOS use the reference flight path. [Native backend scope](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/research/native-kernel/README.md#normal-flight-backend-selection)

Native Linux desktop execution, exported macOS packages, physical radios, target-GPU performance and pilot/readability acceptance remain separate from software and export checks. Some live Windows captures showed intermittently missing HUD glyphs; full-scene readability remains open. [Engine gates](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/plans/godot-4.8-upgrade.md) · [HUD evidence](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/research/flight-overlays/HUD-01-10/README.md#invertedknife-edge-motion-and-translucency-2026-11-06-hud-13-r14-r1)

Aircraft, helicopter and RC ground-vehicle models remain experimental or under evaluation. Multirotor remains research groundwork, with no playable drone or racing activity. [Supported vehicle paths](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.2.0/docs/STACK.md#supported-vehicle-paths)
