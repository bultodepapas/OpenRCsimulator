# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.0-rc7

An experimental integration release with runway starts, configurable weather, day/night flying and expanded propulsion and flight verification. The aircraft menu still offers Ugly Stik 60, Extra 300S, P-51D and Avanti S. Pilot and physical-model acceptance remain open.

## Changes since rc6

- **Runway start:** the Stik's Home start selector offers an experimental idle start near the runway threshold. The saved choice also applies to restart and crash recovery. Other catalog aircraft retain airborne starts.
- **Weather:** Home's English/Spanish editor adds uniform wind, repeating gusts and seeded temporal turbulence, with presets, precise numeric inputs and sliders. Airspeed and horizontal ground speed are reported separately.
- **Atmosphere:** optional field elevation, temperature, QNH and humidity drive shared moist-air density in trim, aerodynamics and propulsion. The default reference density remains 1.225 kg/m³. The coupled naturally aspirated shaft model gains an estimated dry-air charge correction.
- **Day and night:** Home offers saved time presets, moonlit/dark nights and an "Evening, time passing" choice. The world includes solar/lunar lighting, stars, view-dependent exposure, field lamps and an experimental aircraft night-light kit. The evening choice advances the visual sky at 20× on simulation time; the default Day setting retains the fixed sun. The pause menu also supports time-of-day editing.
- **Propulsion experiments:** expanded coupled-shaft verification and synthetic 4S/6S electric development routes, including battery depletion, stop events and recorded model identity. A bounded electric-kernel optimization preserves the documented oracle results. These fixtures are not catalog aircraft or validated Timber models.
- **Flight diagnostics:** optional provenance-checked wing-polar tables, an explicitly labeled experimental Stik launch route, pull-up invariants and reproducible roll-departure investigations. Default aircraft do not enable the candidate polars; this release does not claim a validated repair for the reported high-speed Stik departure.

Verify the attached `SHA256SUMS` and follow the [first-launch guide](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.0-rc7/docs/FIRST-LAUNCH.md). Start with the Stik, Day and calm/reference weather. Fly defaults to trimmed airborne flight; select runway explicitly to try takeoff. Keyboard/mouse operate menus; a connected radio controls flight. Move radio throttle to low to arm, including after calibration. Club scenery remains opt-in (`-- --scenery=on`).

## Verification and limits

Publication is gated by the tagged CI workflow: aircraft schema/loader agreement, generated-data checks, offline-tool regressions, the full headless app suite and rendered captures. Export checks fly the Linux binary, verify all three resource packs and their build identity, and inspect macOS universal architecture/ad-hoc signing and platform version fields. Release downloads include checksums for all three ZIPs.

These are software checks. Native Windows/macOS launch, physical radios, target-GPU performance, night readability and pilot acceptance remain separate. Night lighting, weather presets, shaft environmental response, electric data and candidate polars include documented estimates or synthetic fixtures. The electric 500 µs tick target remains open, with a separate exhaustion-event timing spike. No validated Turbo Timber flight model, persistent structural damage, full engine-operation simulation, calibrated endurance, installers or paid signing is claimed.
