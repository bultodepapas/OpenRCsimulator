# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.0

The first official desktop release of OpenRC Simulator, an open-source RC flight simulator built in Godot. This is an early development release: aircraft models and target-machine performance remain under evaluation.

## Included

- RC airplane flight with keyboard, gamepad and USB radio input, calibration and throttle arming.
- Jensen Das Ugly Stik 60, two experimental glow-engine installations, Extra 300S, P-51D, Ventage Jet and electric Turbo Lumber Evolution. Each aircraft keeps its in-app qualification label.
- A research helicopter trainer with separate rotorcraft physics and an HX-700 visual surrogate; it is experimental and not flight-validated.
- Runway and airborne starts, weather and time-of-day controls, flight overlays and a Workshop for supported airplane adjustments.
- An expanded flying field with vegetation, lake presentation, wildlife, positional engine audio and night lighting.
- Ground contacts, propeller strikes, scenery collisions and persistent crash presentation with aircraft-specific wreck parts, effects and audio.
- Versioned aircraft data, flight traces and build identity for reproducible investigations.

Check the attached `SHA256SUMS`, extract the ZIP and follow the [first-launch guide](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.0/docs/FIRST-LAUNCH.md). Keep the throttle low while arming a radio. R restarts the flight. Existing preferences and radio calibration remain in the user data folder.

## Verification and limits

Publication requires the tagged aircraft contract, generated-data, offline-tool, headless app and rendered-capture checks. Export checks exercise the Linux binary, inspect all three resource packs and verify platform build identity, macOS universal architecture and ad-hoc signing.

These software checks do not establish physical flight validation. Aircraft and propulsion parameters include estimates; experimental models retain their labels. Native Windows/macOS launch, physical radio compatibility, target-GPU performance and pilot acceptance remain separate qualifications. Downloads are unsigned ZIP packages, without an installer or trusted developer signing.
