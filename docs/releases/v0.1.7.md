# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.7

Stable software release of the early-alpha simulator. Aircraft and ground-vehicle models remain experimental and need independent measurements and pilot evaluation.

## Changes since v0.1.6

- Trims fixed-wing runway and airborne launches for level flight at 50% throttle, while retaining the selected launch on restart. The trim and takeoff behavior remain subject to flight testing.
- Expands Club field scenery, vegetation, mountain backdrops, dusk and night lighting, and RC truck course detail. Green paradise remains the separate, flat practice landscape.
- Refines Turbo Lumber's visual model and gear presentation. Adds more aircraft, landing-gear and vehicle sound material, and optional white show smoke for the stock Ventage Jet.
- Adds an O.S. MAX-61FX muffler installation and sourced propeller work; extends the Extra 300S experimental wing and ground-effect research. These changes do not establish physical accuracy.
- Extends source-bound AI companion and sortie research, flight-quality diagnostics, and checked Godot test evidence. AI companions remain an experimental, narrowly qualified activity and are off by default.
- Refreshes generated woodland-edge source and clearance evidence after scenery changes, makes the control-presentation teardown safe for the headless renderer, keeps the aircraft contract check independent of fleet ordering, and separates headless audio smoke checks from native-driver timing checks.

## Verification and limits

The tagged release workflow publishes these archives only after the complete app matrix, visual captures, native evaluator builds, frozen prototype checks, and three-platform export/package checks pass. The tag must match `app/project.godot` and a clean source commit. Generated aircraft geometry and physical data retain their own source checks; the woodland-edge placement proof is refreshed from its generator.

Automated checks verify software behavior, not physical aircraft accuracy, pilot handling, radio compatibility, target-machine performance or native macOS gameplay. The Stik sideslip screen and R02 yaw-damping comparison remain open, alongside the [roadmap gates](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.7/ROADMAP.md). See the [tools and release procedure](https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/v0.1.7/docs/TOOLS.md#release-sequence) for reproducible checks.
