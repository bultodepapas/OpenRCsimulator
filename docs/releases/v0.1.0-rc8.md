# OpenRC Simulator v0.1.0-rc8

An experimental integration release with a richer field, persistent crash presentation, stricter aircraft contracts and a new visual-only aircraft preview. The four existing flight models remain under evaluation.

## Changes since rc7

- **First flight:** new Home preferences select the Stik runway, gentle wind/gusts/turbulence and passing evening. Saved choices take precedence. Choose Airborne, Calm and Day for the earlier practice setup. Direct command-line flights retain airborne, calm/reference and fixed-daylight defaults.
- **Aircraft preview:** Turbo Lumber Evolution joins the menu with its articulated model and Spanish name, Turbo Tronco Evolución. It is a PREVIEW: Fly is refused because electric propulsion and flight data are not integrated. Ugly Stik, Extra 300S, P-51D and Avanti S remain the four flight choices.
- **Field and sound:** expanded landscape materials, scenery/population integration, wildlife and positional soundscape. Scenery is enabled by default; `-- --scenery=off` opts out.
- **Ground and crashes:** per-wheel spin/contact, terrain profiles and approximate static scenery collision proxies. Ordinary flights enable crash effects and keep the aftermath until R or Pause → Restart; `--crash-effects=off` restores timed recovery. Wreck deformation, particles and crash audio are presentation effects, not flyable structural damage.
- **Aircraft data and diagnostics:** the current fleet uses the strict `openrc-aircraft v2` contract with attributed quantities, explicit capabilities and conditional modules. Expanded P-51/Avanti inventory, geometry and maneuver checks, and electric event/localization regressions retain their evidence and scope.
- **Home:** improved keyboard focus/navigation and an update notice for exported builds. The notice checks GitHub at most once a day; downloads and replacement remain manual. Settings and radio calibration remain in the user data folder.

## Verification and limits

Publication is gated by tagged CI: aircraft schema/loader agreement, generated-data checks, offline-tool regressions, the full headless app suite and rendered captures. Export checks execute the Linux binary, inspect all three resource packs and build identity, and verify macOS universal architecture/ad-hoc signing and platform version fields. The three ZIPs carry SHA-256 checksums.

These are software checks. Native Windows/macOS GUI launch, physical radios, target-GPU performance, night readability and pilot/physical-model acceptance remain separate. Packages are unsigned test builds. Ground behavior, collision geometry, aircraft parameters and scenery include engineering estimates; electric development fixtures are synthetic. No flyable Turbo Lumber model, calibrated endurance, persistent flyable damage, installer or trusted developer signing is claimed.
