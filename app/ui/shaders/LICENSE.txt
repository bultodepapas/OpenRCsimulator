OpenRC crystal UI shader — upstream notices

Adapted 2026-10-24. The shader uses the rounded-box mask and edge-lighting approach
from Liquid Glass UI v2 by sentinelcmd, and the projected specular band from
Shine Sweep for items and UI by DimensionAlmios. OpenRC supplies the palette,
layered surface lighting, explicit motion parameters and integration. The screen
sampling/refraction and automatic-clock modes are omitted. No upstream images,
videos, fonts or other depicted assets are included.
