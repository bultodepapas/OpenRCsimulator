Stock vehicle paint textures

Unless noted below, generated by tools/skins/build_vehicle_paints.py (verify
with --check) from assets already in this repository:

- hx-700/*_canopy.png: designs baked through the canopy UVs of
  assets/aircraft/hx-700/hx-700.glb (owner-supplied model). The "HX-700" lettering
  is rasterized from assets/fonts/NotoSansCJKjp-Regular.otf (SIL OFL 1.1,
  assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt).
- hx-700/*_boom.png: recoloured from assets/aircraft/hx-700/hx-700_hx700_botalon_mascaras.png.
- avanti-s-a200/verde_neon_carbono_*: the "Verde Neón Carbono" jet livery, designed in the
  scene space of assets/aircraft/avanti-s-a200/avanti_s.glb (owner-supplied model) and baked
  through each paint material's UVs, with the shared CC0 twill carbon and Noto lettering.
- avanti-s-a200/sabre_plata_*: the "Sabre Plata" natural-metal tribute to early-1950s USAF
  fighters (fictional FU-861 / 20861 markings; no official affiliation implied), designed in
  the same scene space. Albedo, roughness, metallic mask and a normal map baked from a height
  field; reuses the shared ambientCG CC0 brushed-metal grain, the public-domain USAF insignia in
  tools/skins/sources/silver-ace/ (see its README.md) and Noto lettering.
- avanti-s-a200/steampunk_cobre_*: the "Steampunk Cobre" original Victorian steam-age livery
  (riveted copper and iron plates, brass hoops, pipes, a meshing gear train with piston and
  connecting rod, gauges, portholes, firebox, fin clock), designed in the same scene space with
  the same four maps as Sabre Plata plus an emission map. Plaque lettering is rasterized from
  IM FELL English SC (SIL OFL 1.1; tools/skins/sources/steampunk/).
- avanti-s-a200/sponsors/: Jumper RC and JETI model logos (trademarks, used as sponsor decals;
  see that folder's SOURCE.txt). Godot ignores the folder (.gdignore); the builder bakes them in.
- monster-car-2/*_livery.jpg and *_emission.png: the green body colour of the
  owner-supplied Monster Car 2 livery and emission map, re-tinted.

shared/ holds CC0 material maps from ambientCG (twill carbon, brushed metal,
rubber); provenance in shared/LICENSES.txt. The generator does not write them.

Bindings: app/data/vehicle_paints.json. Runtime: app/render/vehicle_paint.gd.

ugly-stik/neon_ronin_*.png: NEON RONIN cyberpunk artwork authored by
tools/skins/build_stik_cyberpunk.py (verify with --check). Reuses the shared
ambientCG CC0 twill weave and bundled NotoSansCJKjp-Regular.otf (SIL OFL 1.1).
Albedo, selective emission and roughness share the unchanged Stik covering atlas.

ugly-stik/silver_ace_*.png: SILVER ACE natural-metal USAF tribute, authored by
tools/skins/build_stik_silver_ace.py (verify with --check). Albedo, roughness,
metallic and OpenGL normal maps share the stock atlas. Panel/rivet/wear artwork
is original; brushing reuses ambientCG Metal009 (CC0, shared/LICENSES.txt).
The public-domain USAF roundel is from Wikimedia Commons (original: United
States Army Institute of Heraldry; vectorisation: Zscout370 and contributors):
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roundel_of_the_USAF.svg
Black Ops One stencil lettering uses Google Fonts commit d50a0d29b (SIL OFL 1.1).
Exact sources, hashes, licence and modification notes:
tools/skins/sources/silver-ace/README.md and sources.json.

ugly-stik/blackbird_*.png: BLACKBIRD SR-71/A-12-inspired RC tribute, authored by
tools/skins/build_stik_blackbird.py (verify with --check). Original charcoal
panels, thermal joints, flush rivets and sparse muted-red geometric marks.
Reuses MetalAtlas utilities, shared ambientCG Metal009 brushing (CC0) and the
bundled NotoSansCJKjp-Regular.otf (SIL OFL 1.1). No third-party insignia used.
Albedo, roughness, metallic and OpenGL normal maps use the unchanged stock UVs.

ugly-stik/honeybee_*.png: HONEYBEE honey-yellow/charcoal artwork authored by
tools/skins/build_stik_honeybee.py (verify with --check). Uses shared ambientCG
CC0 carbon twill, bundled NotoSansCJKjp-Regular.otf (SIL OFL 1.1), and "Simple bee
from above" by matzekatze, Openclipart (CC0 1.0):
https://openclipart.org/detail/174161/simple-bee-from-above
The original bee is aspect-preserving, monochrome-recoloured and baked into the
stock atlas. Original SVG/PNG, licence, hashes and changes are recorded under
tools/skins/sources/honeybee/. No geometry or physics changes.
