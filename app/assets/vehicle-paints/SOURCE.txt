Stock vehicle paint textures

Generated by tools/skins/build_vehicle_paints.py (verify with --check) from assets
already in this repository; no external or downloaded asset is used:

- hx-700/*_canopy.png: designs baked through the canopy UVs of
  assets/aircraft/hx-700/hx-700.glb (owner-supplied model).
- hx-700/*_boom.png: recoloured from assets/aircraft/hx-700/hx-700_hx700_botalon_mascaras.png.
- monster-car-2/*_livery.jpg and *_emission.png: the green body colour of the
  owner-supplied Monster Car 2 livery and emission map, re-tinted.
- shared/carbon_weave.png: contrast-lifted copy of the Monster Car 2 carbon weave.

Bindings: app/data/vehicle_paints.json. Runtime: app/render/vehicle_paint.gd.
