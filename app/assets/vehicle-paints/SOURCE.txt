Stock vehicle paint textures

Unless noted below, generated by tools/skins/build_vehicle_paints.py (verify
with --check) from assets already in this repository:

- hx-700/*_canopy.png: designs baked through the canopy UVs of
  assets/aircraft/hx-700/hx-700.glb (owner-supplied model). The "HX-700" lettering
  is rasterized from assets/fonts/NotoSansCJKjp-Regular.otf (SIL OFL 1.1,
  assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt).
- hx-700/*_boom.png: recoloured from assets/aircraft/hx-700/hx-700_hx700_botalon_mascaras.png.
- avanti-s-a200/verde_neon_carbono_*: the "Verde Neón Carbono" jet livery, designed in the
  scene space of assets/aircraft/avanti-s-a200/avanti_s.glb (owner-supplied model) and baked
  through each paint material's UVs, with the shared CC0 twill carbon and Noto lettering.
- monster-car-2/*_livery.jpg and *_emission.png: the green body colour of the
  owner-supplied Monster Car 2 livery and emission map, re-tinted.

shared/ holds CC0 material maps from ambientCG (twill carbon, brushed metal,
rubber); provenance in shared/LICENSES.txt. The generator does not write them.

Bindings: app/data/vehicle_paints.json. Runtime: app/render/vehicle_paint.gd.

ugly-stik/neon_ronin_*.png: NEON RONIN cyberpunk artwork authored by
tools/skins/build_stik_cyberpunk.py (verify with --check). Reuses the shared
ambientCG CC0 twill weave and bundled NotoSansCJKjp-Regular.otf (SIL OFL 1.1).
Albedo, selective emission and roughness share the unchanged Stik covering atlas.
