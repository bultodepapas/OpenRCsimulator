# Radio appearance artwork

The original schematic SVGs `jumper_t18_h7_schematic.svg` and
`radiolink_t12d_schematic.svg` were created for OpenRC Simulator on 2026-11-19
and are covered by the repository's MIT license. They contain original vector
geometry, without copied manufacturer photographs, logos or firmware screens.
They are simplified presentation layouts, not dimensional hardware drawings.
Product names identify the selected appearance and do not imply endorsement.

The existing `jeti_ds24_ii_orange.png` and `radiolink_t16d.png` are retained
unchanged; this note does not assign new rights or provenance to those files.

The catalog in `app/render/overlays/radio_catalog.gd` owns canvas, gimbal and LCD
coordinates. OpenRC paints its own simulated readings over the LCD. Choosing an
appearance does not change controller calibration, USB mappings or firmware.
