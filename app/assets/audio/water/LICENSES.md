The source pages identify the assets as CC0; the license deed is [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Peludo says the splash recordings were made with a webcam and bucket of water, and requests that games reference an itch.io page. The OGA page does not identify that itch.io URL; this manifest records the available OGA source page. RandomMind identifies the five-minute ambience as waves recorded on the Vistula river.
