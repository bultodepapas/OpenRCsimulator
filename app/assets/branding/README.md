# Official OpenRC identity

`openrc-official.svg` is the unmodified **OpenRC-Simulator-ALPHA-FINAL.svg** supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-24. Its contours, colors, SIMULATOR lettering and ALPHA badge are authoritative. The source includes a black background; the animation excludes that rectangle and preserves every logo path.

`logo-segments.svg` and `logo_segments.gd` are generated by `python tools/branding/build_logo.py`. The atlas packs 43 original contours with padding; metadata maps them back to the source coordinates. No fonts, bitmap tracing, downloaded dependencies or geometry simplification are introduced. `--check` detects stale outputs. The atlas rasterizes at twice the source coordinate scale for the 1080p composition and above-1080p displays.

The lightweight startup scene and its presentation live in `app/ui/startup.gd` and `app/ui/brand_intro.gd`. See [the shell guide](../../../docs/MENU-PLAN.md) for behavior and verification.
