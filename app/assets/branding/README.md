# Official OpenRC identity

[`openrc-refined.svg`](openrc-refined.svg) is the editable master used by the simulator, README and website. Refined at the owner's request on 2026-10-24, it preserves the segmented silhouette, blue/cyan palette, SIMULATOR lettering and ALPHA badge. Straight polygon edges replace the raster-trace stair steps; stray R/C fragments are removed, lettering is regularized and the badge uses smooth cubic corners. Its cropped canvas is transparent. All lettering is outlined: no fonts, embedded bitmaps or external resources are required.

`openrc-official.svg` preserves the unmodified **OpenRC-Simulator-ALPHA-FINAL.svg** supplied by the owner as provenance; it is not the active master.

`logo-segments.svg` and `logo_segments.gd` are generated by `python tools/branding/build_logo.py`. The atlas packs the refined master's 40 contours with padding; metadata maps them back to the source coordinates. Polygon and cubic control-point bounds retain the rounded badge without clipping. `--check` detects stale outputs. The atlas rasterizes at twice the source coordinate scale for the 1080p composition and above-1080p displays. Public derivatives and the social PNG are regenerated with `python tools/branding/build_public.py --raster`; see [public identity](../../../site/BRANDS.md#openrc-identity).

The lightweight startup scene and its presentation live in `app/ui/startup.gd` and `app/ui/brand_intro.gd`. See [the shell guide](../../../docs/MENU-PLAN.md) for behavior and verification.
