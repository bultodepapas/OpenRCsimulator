Only the SVG vector prompts needed for common face buttons, shoulders, stick presses, D-pad directions, menu, and back controls were copied. Source directories were `Xbox Series/Vector`, `PlayStation Series/Vector`, `Nintendo Switch/Vector`, and `Generic/Vector`; local files are renamed by physical position/control for stable runtime paths. No artwork was generated or modified.

## Source-file mapping

Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo each contain `face_bottom.svg`, `face_right.svg`, `face_left.svg`, `face_top.svg`, `menu.svg`, `back.svg`, `shoulder_left.svg`, `shoulder_right.svg`, `stick_left_press.svg`, `stick_right_press.svg`, and `dpad_up.svg`, `dpad_down.svg`, `dpad_left.svg`, `dpad_right.svg`. Generic uses four shared glyphs, for 46 SVG files in total.

- Xbox Series: `xbox_button_a.svg`, `xbox_button_b.svg`, `xbox_button_x.svg`, `xbox_button_y.svg`, `xbox_button_menu.svg`, `xbox_button_back.svg`, `xbox_lb.svg`, `xbox_rb.svg`, `xbox_stick_l_press.svg`, `xbox_stick_r_press.svg`, and the matching `xbox_dpad_{up,down,left,right}.svg`.
- PlayStation Series: `playstation_button_cross.svg`, `playstation_button_circle.svg`, `playstation_button_square.svg`, `playstation_button_triangle.svg`, `playstation5_button_options.svg`, `playstation4_button_share.svg`, `playstation_trigger_l1.svg`, `playstation_trigger_r1.svg`, `playstation_stick_l_press.svg`, `playstation_stick_r_press.svg`, and the matching `playstation_dpad_{up,down,left,right}.svg`.
- Nintendo Switch: `switch_button_b.svg`, `switch_button_a.svg`, `switch_button_y.svg`, `switch_button_x.svg`, `switch_button_plus.svg`, `switch_button_minus.svg`, `switch_button_l.svg`, `switch_button_r.svg`, `switch_stick_l_press.svg`, `switch_stick_r_press.svg`, and the matching `switch_dpad_{up,down,left,right}.svg`.
- Generic: `generic_button.svg` supplies the face, menu, back, and D-pad marks; `generic_button_trigger_a.svg` and `generic_button_trigger_b.svg` supply shoulders; `generic_stick_press.svg` supplies both stick marks.
