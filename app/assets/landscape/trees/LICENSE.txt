Changes made
------------

The source trees are normalized to a 1 m height with a scene-root scale and
translation wrapper for the offline bake. The source geometry and material
identities are retained by that wrapper. The selected maple model was extracted
from its source pack, converted to glTF with its supplied textures, and given a
stem-ground origin. The bake renders the three sources into atlas tiles and
derives the aligned foliage material mask from their source materials.

The Maple source remains credited under its original CC BY 4.0 notice; the
combined atlas and mask carry the CC BY-SA 4.0 terms required by the included
LandscapeLab material. These credits do not imply endorsement by the creators.
Per-slot source notices are in `licenses/`.

Historical source
-----------------

The earlier tree atlas used Quaternius's Stylized Nature MegaKit Standard,
recorded as CC0-1.0 for the exact downloaded archive in
`assets/landscape/PROVENANCE.json`. That archive is historical provenance for
the retired atlas; it is not a source of the current tree atlas.
