# Mountain material sources

Aerial Grass Rock and Aerial Rocks 01 by **Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven** are
released under **CC0-1.0**:

- https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
- https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_rocks_01
- https://docs.polyhaven.com/en/faq
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The downloaded JPG files are unchanged. Godot creates compressed mipmaps;
the shader uses photographic luminance with art-directed colors and scales.
Exact downloads and SHA-256 identities are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).

The relief and habitat maps derive from SRTM data courtesy of the
U.S. Geological Survey, distributed as Mapzen Terrain Tiles.
Elevation data are public domain:
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md#srtm

These maps are rotated, scaled, filtered and artistically classified.
They do not represent surveyed simulator-field terrain or vegetation.
