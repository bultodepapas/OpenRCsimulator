# Countryside surface photograph

`aerial_asphalt_01_diff_1k.jpg` is **Aerial Asphalt 01** by **Rob Tuytel**,
downloaded unchanged from Poly Haven on 2026-10-20.

- [Asset and author](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_asphalt_01)
- [Exact 1K diffuse JPG](https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/aerial_asphalt_01/aerial_asphalt_01_diff_1k.jpg)
- [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license): CC0-1.0
- [CC0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)

The source describes a 30 m footprint. Godot imports the photograph with VRAM
compression, mipmaps and anisotropic filtering. The shader uses restrained
luminance detail; road paint is generated analytically in metres.
Exact downloaded identity is retained in `source.json`.

The same material also reuses the existing CC0 Grass001 and Dirt photographs
from [rc-ground](../rc-ground/LICENSES.txt); it adds no duplicate copies.
