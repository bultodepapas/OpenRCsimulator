tall variants, removed gallery placement, baked the source Z-up basis into the
vertices for glTF Y-up, retained source topology, UVs, normals, tangents and
texture files, and changed grass alpha from BLEND to MASK with a 0.5 cutoff.
No geometry was created or decimated.
