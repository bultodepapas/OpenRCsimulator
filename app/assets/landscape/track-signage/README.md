Kenney Racing Kit, version 1.0 (2018), CC0 1.0.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit

`billboardLow.glb` and `flagCheckersSmall.glb` are unmodified extracts from
`kenney_racing-kit.zip`. Their geometry is reused by
`app/vehicles/ground/track_furniture.gd`; the scene overrides the original
fictional sponsor material with course-specific colors and lettering.
Godot extracts `billboardLow_tankcoBanner.png` and
`flagCheckersSmall_checkers.png` during import. The bundled `License.txt` is
copied alongside the models.
