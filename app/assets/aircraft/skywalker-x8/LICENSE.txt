Skywalker X8 game asset r4 (skins abisal and bolido) — authorship and terms

Authorship
  3D model, scripts and documentation prepared for Estudiante 4
  (estudiante4@bootcampinnovaitors.com, Innovaitors SAS) with Claude (Anthropic), 2026.
  Geometry is an independent parametric approximation built from public information: the
  Skywalker official three-view and spec sheet, kit photographs, and Gryte et al.,
  "Aerodynamic modeling of the Skywalker X8 Fixed-Wing Unmanned Aerial Vehicle", ICUAS 2018.
  The liveries ABISAL and BOLIDO (textures, renders and painter scripts) are original designs
  made procedurally for this package. They contain no third-party artwork, logos, insignia or
  registrations; the names ABISAL, X8-08, ABISAL-08, BOLIDO, VR-08, the mission emblem and all
  stencils are invented. BOLIDO is inspired by the general construction of the Space Shuttle
  thermal protection system (tiles, blankets, carbon-carbon); no NASA marking or artwork is
  reproduced, and the package is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA. References were only
  consulted (Wikipedia article on the Space Shuttle thermal protection system; NASA photographs of
  Endeavour and Columbia viewed on Wikimedia Commons); nothing was copied, traced or embedded, and
  no reference image is part of this package.

Components
  Every file in this package was produced for it by the scripts in source/scripts (geometry,
  paint, layers, textures, renders, checks). No third-party models, textures or images are
  embedded. Each skin's provenance is also recorded in metadata.json -> skins[].provenance.

Fonts
  Text in the textures was rasterised with DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Mono (Bitstream Vera
  licence with DejaVu changes; free to embed in images). Only the pixels are included, not
  the font files.
