The cracked-lens pattern in lens_stamp.gdshader (crack_field) adapts the technique of
"Impact Glass Shader" by Lord0Sanz, published at https://godotshaders.com/shader/impact-glass-shader/
under the MIT License (retrieved 2026-11-12). The lens-wetness layer in lens_film.gdshader (static_drops)
adapts the StaticDrops function of "Rain on Glass" by Gerardo LCDF, published at
https://godotshaders.com/shader/rain-on-glass/ under the MIT License (retrieved 2026-11-12). The drop outline in
lens_drop.gdshader (sd_uneven_capsule) is Inigo Quilez's 2D uneven-capsule distance function
(https://iquilezles.org/articles/distfunctions2d/), MIT License. Copyright holders are named as the source pages
name their authors.
