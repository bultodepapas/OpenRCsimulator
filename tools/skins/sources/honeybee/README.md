# HONEYBEE authoring source

`bee.svg` and `bee.png` are the original vector and Openclipart's transparent
800 × 538 PNG rendering of [Simple bee from above](https://openclipart.org/detail/174161/simple-bee-from-above)
by matzekatze, published 2013-01-10. Openclipart identifies its artwork as
[CC0 1.0](https://openclipart.org/share); the PNG also embeds that dedication,
the title and the author's name. The CC0 legal text is reproduced in
`CC0-1.0.txt`. Credit is not required, but is retained here.

The downloaded files are unmodified. The PNG download URL requests a 2000-pixel
image, but returned 800 × 538 on 2026-11-14. Exact URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in
`sources.json`. The icon is a roughly bilaterally symmetric top view; its wing and
antenna tips touch the source frame. The skin builder may extract its dark ink as a
mask, resize, rotate and recolour it for the aircraft atlas. These operations do
not change the preserved originals.
