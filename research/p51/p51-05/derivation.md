## Known simplifications

- Propeller tables: blade-element model calibrated on Mejzlik's manufacturer-SIMULATED 26x12 2/3-blade tables (rms 6.5 %); the 4-blade is a prediction. The windmilling branch is the uncalibrated BEM (Mejzlik keeps Cp slightly positive past zero thrust).
- Engine: generic two-stroke torque shape scaled to one static-rpm anchor; carburettor lag, idle instability and fuel use are not modelled.
- Slipstream: tail only (fin, stab halves), no lag, no wing wash; wash factors borrowed from Selig 2010 (an RC 3D airplane), swirl factor estimated.
- Propeller normal force and P-factor: McCormick's formulas (via Selig 2010); the near-static jet normal force is omitted.
- Flaps: shared hinged-trailing-edge law (P51-11a), kit 15/45 deg settings; retractable gear (P51-11b), airborne starts stowed. Main-wheel brakes from the GP-03 wheel profile (P51-11c; limited by the shared tyre-slip law). Tail-wheel spring softer than a real one (240 Hz explicit tick rule).
- A full-size report notes 1 deg left fin offset on P-51B/D. This model has no fixed fin-incidence/yaw-bias term; a rudder control trim does not represent that geometry, so the effect is omitted.
- The neutral point is calibrated to the full-size glide value through one lumped fuselage/scoop/propeller term (K_fus 0.033/deg, above Raymer's 0.005-0.015).
- The tail's zero-alpha flow angle is calibrated to the full-size glide trim curves through one lumped term (-2.30 deg at the tail, P51-19); the stabilizer stays at its +2 deg leading-edge-up setting (NACA ACR 4K02 confirms the sign). Reynolds similarity for the RC model is not established.
- Body directional destabilization (P51-25) is one lumped term calibrated to the P-51D steady-sideslip rudder slope (RM L6J25), applied as a Munk-type couple that fades out over the declared sideslip blend (15-25 deg) because its evidence is small-sideslip attached flow; it is not separated into fuselage, canopy and propeller parts and has no damping contribution.
- The induced-flow strip map calibrates section slope and offsets so integrated wing-plus-horizontal-tail lift slope and
  zero-alpha lift reproduce aircraft-level CLa and CL0. This is a consistency calibration, not independent section data;
  Clp and detailed spanwise attached/post-stall loading remain model predictions at the current strip resolution.
- CL-dependent cross terms (Clb wing part, Clr, Cnp, adverse yaw) are frozen at CL_ref = 0.40.
- Ground effect (P51-20): the shared M5-GE-1 law with constants fitted to an image-method VLM of this wing and tail; no fuselage or
  bound-vortex image in the runtime law, separated flow unchanged, not measured on a model.
