## Discriminating stage-local cooling comparison

At the valid, full-fuel, stock Extra **trimmed airborne start**, `Glow.point()` evaluates these current model values at one instant: airspeed 22.0000 m/s, axial propeller inflow 21.96176 m/s, shaft 7,473.19 RPM, thrust 5.17050 N, density 1.225 kg/m³, head proxy 390 K, ambient 288.15 K. No maneuver or cooling trajectory is simulated by the comparison.

Production `app/physics/glow/flight.gd:447`–`:458` computes a scalar cooling-flow proxy

`v_cool = |v_hub| + sqrt(T/(2ρA))`, `A=πD²/4`,

where the added term is the ideal actuator-disk induced speed **at zero inflow**. The installed head/cowl location, actual local airflow and cooling coefficient remain estimates. For comparison alone, an ideal steady, aligned, axial, positive-thrust actuator disk has the relation `T=2ρA v_i(U+v_i)` and therefore

`v_i = 2v_0² / (sqrt(U²+4v_0²)+U)`, `v_0=sqrt(T/(2ρA))`.

The alternate expression agrees with the current added term at zero axial inflow. At this actual trimmed start the two values are:

| Quantity at the same frozen source/body state | Current proxy | Ideal aligned-disk comparison |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Added induced speed | 5.37803 m/s | 1.24626 m/s |
| Proxy cooling heat | 487.389 W | 436.891 W |
| Head-proxy temperature rate | −2.02342 K/s | −1.66272 K/s |

The 50.4985 W cooling and 0.360704 K/s rate differences follow the **existing estimated** coefficients: airflow conductance 0.12 W/(K·m/s), heat capacity 140 J/K, and ΔT=101.85 K (`app/data/glow/extra_61fx_2t.json`; `app/physics/glow/thermal.gd:29`–`:31`). The alternate is evaluated with the same source result and thermal state; it is not integrated, retuned, or substituted into production. It is useful because a pilot's throttle/airspeed changes can affect estimated temperature and subsequently the firing fraction, but neither the effect on a maneuver nor any measured engine error was established. A real engine head may see upstream/downstream, off-axis and cowl-modified flow, and static disk theory is not a measured local head-cooling law. The transverse inflow is nonzero in this trimmed pose, while the comparator assumes alignment. A repair cannot be justified by the algebra alone; matched installed airflow/temperature data and separately justified model changes are needed.
