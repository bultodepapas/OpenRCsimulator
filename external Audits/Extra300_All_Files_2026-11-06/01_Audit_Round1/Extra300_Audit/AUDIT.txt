1. E300-01 — AERODYNAMIC MOMENTS MIX REFERENCE POINTS

Evidence and cause

The aircraft contract states that aerodynamic moment coefficients are about the aerodynamic reference point (ARP), using body axes. Aero._global_loads then transfers the associated resultant force to the center of gravity:

    M_CG = M_ARP + (r_ARP - r_CG) cross F.

The Extra generator instead uses the loaded-CG fin height in the fin part of Clb:
    z_v = fin_cy - cg_model_y
    Clb = Clb_wing + dihedral_low + CYb_v * z_v / b

Other nearby derivatives, including rudder roll and static yaw, correctly use ARP arms. This is a mixed-reference data-generation defect, not a reason to remove the runtime's shared transfer.

Sources:
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/docs/AIRCRAFT_CONTRACT.md#L61-L70
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/research/extra-300/ex05/derive_physics.py#L346-L391
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/physics/aero.gd#L216-L246

Mathematical diagnosis

Let CYb_fin = -a_v*S_v/S and use the documented structural z-up coordinates. The fin's coefficient supplied to this consumer must be:
    Clb_fin_ARP = CYb_fin*(z_fin - z_ARP)/b.
The generator supplies:
    Clb_fin_generated = CYb_fin*(z_fin - z_CG)/b.
Their difference is:
    excess = -CYb_fin*(z_CG - z_ARP)/b.

At the stock full-tank reference:
    CYb_fin             = -0.355109230808814 /rad
    z_CG - z_ARP        = 0.028424293699338 m
    b                   = 1.6256 m
    excess              = +0.006209232942825 /rad.

This shifts the fin contribution toward less stabilizing roll in the tested attached-flow regime.

Measured witness

A production-code test used rho=1.225 kg/m^3, V=20 m/s, alpha=0, beta=+5 degrees, zero body rates, neutral controls, a settled wake, no ground effect and 12,000 rpm. Only Clb was adjusted in a diagnostic model copy by the independently derived fin-reference correction.

                                 Stock             Reference diagnostic
Aerodynamic roll, N m           -0.328073977873    -0.431661020058
Combined external roll, N m     -0.919020790990    -1.022607833175
Difference, N m                                    -0.103587042185

The aerodynamic difference is 24.0% of the diagnostic aerodynamic roll moment; it is approximately 10.1% of the diagnostic combined roll moment at this positive-beta state. Do not present 24% as an error in the whole aircraft's roll response. The same magnitude changes sign at beta=-5 degrees. The other five load components and propeller/slipstream contributions stay unchanged. The local-flow selector is exactly zero, so this is not a stall-blending artifact.

The rate coefficients have a related, smaller inconsistency. Cmq is explicitly generated and labeled about the CG, while Cnr's fin term uses a squared CG arm. The attached consumer nevertheless transfers the accompanying rate forces again. Central differences at rates +/-1e-5 rad/s give:
    pitch: generated CG Cmq -9.360761; effective -9.157953337632;
    yaw: generated Cnr -0.180871; effective -0.178733953118.
These match the transfer terms CLq*(cg_x-arp_x)/c and CYr*(cg_x-arp_x)/b. They are approximately 2.17% and 1.18% less negative. This does not establish that every derivative is wrong; wing/fuselage approximations and rate-velocity reference points require individual treatment.

Repair and regression

Correct the Extra generator's fin static Clb arm to the ARP. Keep the wing/body estimates distinct. The fuselage side force is already defined at the ARP; a blanket correction using total CYb would incorrectly move it as if it were fin force. For rate moments, distinguish the arm defining rotational local inflow from the arm defining the moment about ARP. Review the associated force derivative and all component contributions before changing Cmq or Cnr.

Add independent component-force/arm assertions to the generator and compare both signs of beta and rates in production loads. The loader already has an optional fin/wing Clb relation, but enabling wing_body_cl_beta also changes the local-regime roll couple at aero.gd:666-671. That flag is not a validation-only fix. A narrow generator invariant is safer.

Regenerate the owned aircraft output and dependent identities. Qualify transitions between global/local models, fuel states and fixed-datum/live-CG transfers, replay and reference/native behavior. A diagnostic moment-datum shift in the new test proves the algebraic -delta_r cross F relation; it is not an accepted-tick fuel-burn test.

Evidence: probes/aero/test_reference_witness.gd; receipts/aero/reference-witness-datum-wi2nfqxi. 29 checks passed. Existing body-side-force (25 checks) and Extra-post-stall (7 checks) programs passed on the baseline. See supporting/AERODYNAMICS.txt for all details and exploration limits.

2. E300-02 — PROPELLER CROSSFLOW TABLES USE A DIFFERENT BLADE SECTION

Evidence and cause

The installed Extra propeller has zero_lift_angle_rad = -0.10223236266152576, approximately -5.8575 degrees. The runtime's annular model correctly uses:
    alpha = beta - phi - zero_lift_angle.

However, derive_physics.py constructs the section dictionary used to generate normal_force and pfactor_moment with lift slope, profile drag, induced drag and blade count, omitting zero_lift_angle_rad. The Python annular mirror defaults that missing value to zero. The generator's own comments and generated provenance say these coefficients come from the same installed glow propeller.

Sources:
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/research/extra-300/ex05/derive_physics.py#L493-L527
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/research/glow-engines/calibration_report.py#L470-L500
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/physics/glow/propeller.gd#L39-L89
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/data/glow/extra_61fx_2t.json#L203-L207

The source-section assembly can be corrected with the existing optional parameter:
    "zero_lift_angle_rad": gp.get("zero_lift_angle_rad", 0.0)

This restores declared-method consistency; it does not independently validate the crossflow law or the borrowed propeller fit.

Measured table differences

All 44 shipped rows (22 for each coefficient) exactly reproduce the unmodified generator block. Adding only the omitted section parameter yields:

Advance J      CN stock       CN consistent       CM stock       CM consistent
0.30           0.001884       0.002474            0.002999       0.005058
0.50           0.004456       0.006239            0.002978       0.006713
0.70           0.006712       0.010410            0.000957       0.006488
0.80           0.007221       0.012175            0.000000       0.005572

At J=0.5, stock CN is 28.58% below the consistent result; stock CM is 55.64% below it. At J=0.8 the current table clips CM to zero while the same declared method with installed camber remains positive.

An independent simplification of the generator's equations gives, before clamping and six-decimal rounding:
    delta_CN = 3*J^2/(2*pi^2) * delta_CT
    delta_CM = J/(2*pi) * delta_CT.
The probe checks these identities as well as a zero-camber negative control.

At 12,000 rpm, D=0.3048 m and J=0.5, axial speed is 30.48 m/s. The symmetric intermediate model would produce -0.18517 N thrust while the installed cambered model produces +19.66442 N. The game already uses the latter thrust. The defect is that it combines that correct installed thrust with crossflow tables generated from the former section.

Production-load consequence

Using the real Propulsion functions at rho=1.225 kg/m^3 and a 10-degree disc-flow angle:

                                 Stock             Consistent table diagnostic
Normal-force magnitude, N        0.3272437365       0.4581852945
P-factor moment magnitude, N m   0.0666600779       0.1502649775

For the vertical-crossflow test, the full propulsion wrench changes by about -0.083602 N m in yaw and +0.047535 N m in pitch (including the normal-force arm). Shaft thrust and shaft torque are byte-identical between the two in-memory table variants. Both vertical and horizontal crossflow directions, zero RPM and shaft-aligned controls are checked. These are valid static operating points, not trimmed-flight or real-aircraft validation.

Repair and regression

Use one installation-to-section adapter so the generator cannot silently omit a newly added optional section parameter. The existing calibration reader already carries camber correctly at calibration_report.py:77-82; avoid another manually maintained incomplete mapping. Regenerate the aircraft tables and dependent descriptors through the owning pipeline. Record the resulting model change intentionally.

The current test_extra_installation passes 17/17 but computes its normal-force and P-factor expectations from the same shipped tables. It is an excellent assembly check; it does not detect this generator-to-installation disagreement. Add an independent generated-table/installed-section consistency check while retaining the existing test.

Evidence: probes/propeller/probe_propeller_camber.py; probes/propeller/probe_camber_loads.gd; results/propeller/camber_tables.json; results/propeller/runtime_loads.json; patches/generator_camber_candidate.patch. Production-load witness: 46 checks passed.

3. E300-03 — IDENTICAL MASS PROPERTIES ARE COMPUTED TWICE PER ORDINARY EXTRA STAGE

flight.point() calls _stage_mass_context and obtains mass, CG, inertia and inverse. It only returns that result as evaluation.mass when an airframe result was needed for the optional fuel-feed model. Stock Extra has no fuel_feed. flight.evaluate() therefore receives no mass and recomputes properties from the same ordinary single-tank state.

Sources:
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/physics/glow/flight.gd#L445-L462
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/physics/glow/flight.gd#L569-L583
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/physics/glow/flight.gd#L666-L682
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/physics/mass_inventory.gd#L228-L276

This rebuilds the six inertia components and their inverse, with packed-array/dictionary work, rather than merely rereading a scalar. At four RK stages and 240 Hz, the ordinary route incurs 960 duplicate evaluations per simulated second. This count excludes event trials, endpoints, observers and extra contact substeps, and is not a timing measurement.

If soft-hull evaluation activates near the flat-ground reference, _ground_evaluation calls properties yet again. Its optional properties argument already supports reuse, but _airframe does not supply it. Source:
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/sim/flight_session.gd#L4373-L4393

Guarded experiment

The included one-file patch exposes a private stage-only reuse payload under a narrow guard:
    no fuel_feed; no feed_line; line mass exactly zero; 0 < fuel <= capacity.
The default public point() payload stays unchanged. Other cases use the existing recomputation. It removes one evaluation per qualifying stage: 2 to 1 airborne and 3 to 2 on the qualifying soft-hull route. It does not implement the additional ground reuse.

Checks:
- 1,010 finite amount samples plus nonfinite controls compared the local mass functions.
- 90 direct stages: 72 accepted and 18 refused; exact serialized-output digests match.
- Default public point results match for the same cases.
- 48 accepted airborne/engine-cut ticks, 16 runway ticks, four near-exhaustion ticks and two event records match.
- Existing glow-flight physics: 110 checks passed on baseline and candidate.

Limits and implementation direction

This prototype is not production-ready. An explicitly requested point(..., true) payload changes. Supported callback dispatch, possible mutation between callbacks, solver identities, native/reference behavior, full recordings/checkpoints/replays and the wider supported fleet still require qualification. Do not infer a CPU percentage from removed call counts or from runner wall time.

Use one private, validated, stage-local context and pass it to the consumers that need it, retaining exact evaluation/refusal ordering and safe fallbacks. Do not cache across stages or fuel events. A controlled whole-session benchmark should decide practical priority after parity is complete.

Evidence: probes/dynamics/probe_mass_reuse.gd and probe_stage_parity.gd; patches/stage-mass-reuse.patch; paired receipts under receipts/dynamics. See supporting/DYNAMICS.txt for the guard proof and limits.

4. E300-04 — INDEX LOFTS FOR STORAGE, WITH A MEASURED BUILD-TIME TRADEOFF

The Extra _loft() emits repeated triangle corners and directly commits the SurfaceTool. An experimental st.index() immediately before commit preserves complete vertex attributes and triangle order while allowing identical vertices to share indices.

Source:
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/aircraft/extra_300s_model.gd#L71-L105

Measured stock/candidate:
- Stored vertices across the 31 distinct mesh resources: 53,204 to 10,878.
- Serialized vertex/attribute/index buffers across 27 ArrayMesh resources: 1,317,552 to 373,176 bytes.
- Buffer reduction: 944,376 bytes, 71.68%, about 0.901 MiB per stock model.
- Mesh instances: unchanged at 32. Instance triangles: unchanged at 19,502.
- After custom-paint preparation: 1,825,800 to 1,475,672 bytes, only 19.18% smaller, because the paint adapter expands target triangles.

These are exact inspected engine buffer lengths, not total RAM or GPU allocation measurements.

The checked comparison re-expands indexed meshes and compares every present corner attribute, triangle order, local transform and relative mesh path, both stock and custom-prepared. Godot value equality is exact; signed-zero bit equality and rendered-image parity were not tested. Position-only vertex merging would be insufficient because normals and UV seams must remain distinct.

A rejected alternative indexed after committing and decoding the mesh through SurfaceTool.create_from(). That extra encode/decode cycle changed normals/tangents; it is not the supplied candidate.

Timing

Pinned headless Linux Godot on Xeon Platinum 8573C, CPU affinity 0, three warmups per builder and 16 alternating AB/BA pairs:
    baseline build median: 96.1925 ms
    indexed build median: 103.238 ms
    median paired change: +8.505%
    paired range: -22.802% to +32.249%; 4/16 pairs favored indexing.
Only builder.build() was timed. Freeing, import, loading and renderer work were excluded.

A separate custom-prepare-only comparison was noisy; it does not establish a complete customization speedup. See raw samples and MODEL_PRESENTATION.txt.

Recommendation

Treat the patch as a storage tradeoff. If adopting indexing, consider doing it once in an owned build/preparation lifecycle or caching verified immutable mesh resources while keeping per-instance nodes, hinges and paint overrides separate. The existing Home preview cache already retains complete models; this is not a discovery that Home lacks caching. Qualify actual reset/customization/render/manifest behavior and measure on the target device.

Evidence: probes/model/geometry_probe.gd; results/model/geometry_counts.json; patches/extra_loft_index_candidate.patch; receipts/model/geometry-custom-assert-j_0ahqce and bench-index-g_b1yxdo.

5. E300-05 — CUSTOM PAINT RECREATES IDENTICAL SHADERS

The stock Extra finish correctly shares one shader among five materials. The duplication occurs in the custom-paint adapter. _prepare_material() creates a new Shader for each painted surface; paint() builds another Shader for each distinct prepared material.

Sources:
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/aircraft/procedural_skin.gd#L120-L150
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/aircraft/procedural_skin.gd#L302-L364
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/aircraft/custom_skin.gd#L181-L215

The headless Extra probe found 14 prepared shader objects with one exact code string. Applying a representative opaque, untextured paint record generated 14 final shader objects with one exact code string. This is a resource-allocation observation; it does not prove 28 hardware compilations, a GPU-memory saving or a frame-rate improvement. An actual .orskin import was not run.

Reuse immutable Shader resources by exact generated source and relevant texture/alpha variant. Keep separate ShaderMaterial uniform values and instance ownership. Do not merge independently painted materials merely because their shader code is equal. Preserve the adapter's prepare-then-commit behavior and bounded cache lifetime; cover multiple liveries, alpha, textures and resets.

Evidence: results/model/geometry_counts.json, probes/model/geometry_probe.gd and the checked geometry receipt.

6. E300-06 — THE VISUAL PROP BLADE TRAVELS TRAILING-EDGE FIRST

The +X blade is authored with its leading edge toward +Y. The finite-glow render path uses negative rotation about local Z for positive shaft RPM. At a point on +X, omega cross r therefore points toward -Y. The two conventions disagree.

Sources:
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/aircraft/extra_300s_model.gd#L661-L685
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/render/airplane.gd#L143-L149
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/main.gd#L1367-L1371
https://github.com/bultodepapas/OpenRCsimulator/blob/e72d878b6b4a89209dfe1a992ba119921dcdd34c/app/main.gd#L3170-L3175

The witness locates the authored leading and trailing points in the actual mesh and evaluates:
    (leading - trailing) dot (omega cross midpoint).
At radius 0.1112777921 m with unit negative-Z angular speed, it is -0.0023391903378 m^2/s; reversing the test spin gives the positive control. Both blades share this relative handedness.

Correct the blade geometry's leading edge/camber/pitch convention together while keeping the physics-consistent rotation convention. Verify the selected shaft axis/hub, blur, stopped appearance, and strike visuals. This finding concerns presentation; physical thrust and shaft solver signs were not shown wrong. No rendered perceptibility or gameplay cost was measured.

Evidence: probes/model/spin_probe.gd and receipts/model/spin-probe-2fcpk9u6.
