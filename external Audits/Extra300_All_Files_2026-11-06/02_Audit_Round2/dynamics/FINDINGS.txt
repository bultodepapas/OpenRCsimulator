## Known unqualified contact behavior, excluded from new findings

The older project R05-B policy expressly uses locked-rotor inertia and preserves relative RPM for nonterminal scenery impacts, while leaving a freely spinning shaft impulse and full-system energy qualification open (`docs/research/physics-audit-2026-10-14/remediation/R05/R05-B-glow-contacts.md:47`–`:53`; its tracked blob is identical in the full checkout at `/workspace/scratch/bc7264df490c/audit_round3/repo`). The existing body-only energy gate therefore cannot be presented as a new generic missing-coupling discovery.

A staged stock-Extra, nonterminal wingtip foliage event quantified the declared limitation: at 7457.73 rpm and roughly 0.122 m/s incoming normal speed, reported body-only kinetic change was `−0.00155166335 J`, matching independently computed impulse work within `1e-13 J`; locked body plus relative-rotor kinetic change was `+0.05010401547 J`, dominated by cross term `I_r Ω (a·Δω)=+0.05165567882 J`. Total angular impulse residual was `3.73e-15 N m s`. A distinct zero-rpm control started and ended at zero RPM with zero forward airspeed and had combined energy change `−0.001648946999 J`. This is a synthetic posed collision, not a demonstrated flown trajectory, and the control has different forward speed. See `probe_scenery_rotor.gd` and `checks/scenery-rotor-control-m3ihqosi/` for details. Existing dynamic-mass contact tests passed 38/38 in `checks/dynamic-contact-existing-*`.

The full-energy expression for this accounting is `K = 0.5 m |v_O + ω×c|² + 0.5 ω·I_C ω + I_r Ω a·ω + 0.5 I_r Ω²`, with locked inertia `I_C` and relative shaft speed `Ω`; the documented free-rotor carrier block is `I_C − I_r aaᵀ`. Assessing that different collision law would require an authorized numerical/model change and event/replay tests, not merely relabeling the current approximation as a fresh defect. Coasting-prop strike GP-17 was also excluded as already planned.
