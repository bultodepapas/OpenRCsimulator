# Round-two Extra 300 aerodynamic review

Baseline `8e5f2f0ba6aacd44efa1c4ac48c4023f181c47bd`, clean on entry; Godot `4.8.beta2.official.cafc17a19`, Linux x86-64, isolated float64 runtime with 240 Hz configured. The unchanged consolidated ledger (SHA-256 `772a484f0aad5f36241bca4b13c09437fdc1b00b43def686ebbe180abb7a55fa`), prior `AUDIT.txt`, and physics/repository guidance were reviewed. Compared with the previous baseline the only production runtime change was the known, now implemented downwash-air reuse. No production files were changed. The following are observations, **not established new bugs, calibrated corrections, or physical validation**.

1. **Polar attachment and source retention use distinct heuristics.** `_wing_flow`, `app/physics/aero.gd:358–369`, uses the legacy envelope to attenuate the induced-map sources. The active Extra polar supplies a separate per-strip attached fraction (`aero.gd:515–527`; `wing_polar.gd:84–123`). At 20 m/s, α −20°, p=0, all source weights are zero while the table fractions are 0.566–0.619. A disposable diagnostic replacing only source retention with polar attachment changes Fz 87.3318→109.6978 N, pitch moment 12.7227→13.0510 N m, and wake −11.2387→−15.1104 m/s. At +20° the Fz change is about −0.0052 N; at α=0° outputs agree; all 33 no-polar control comparisons are byte-equal. The negative branch is an unmeasured recipe, and no contract states the two fractions must be equal. EX-14h fitted the positive-angle curve. This comparison demonstrates numerical/model sensitivity, not that the diagnostic is physically correct or that it should be adopted. Probe `runtime/polar_source_compare.gd`; receipt `checks/polar-source-compare-n5xlsipp/`.

2. **Maximum station speed introduces a small symmetric cusp.** `aero.gd:354` uses a maximum over the six station speeds to normalize the source weights. At α=14°, V=20 m/s, zero other rates and controls, `Aero.loads` has one-sided Fz derivatives around p=0 approaching −0.264606/+0.264606 N/(rad/s); the symmetric response is continuous but not differentiable at that point. A fixture-only diagnostic freezing the denominator to its 20 m/s zero-rate value gives derivatives −0.0000252/+0.0000252 at h=1e−5 rad/s. It matches at p=0. At α=20°, where source weights are zero, original and diagnostic match at all sampled steps and slopes tend to zero. Freezing that denominator is **not a viable general repair**: it is used only to attribute the cusp. About 0.026 N is the first-order scale at 0.1 rad/s; no maneuver, numerical-integrator failure, or material handling effect was established. Probe `runtime/cusp_attribution.gd`; receipt `checks/cusp-attribution-qxc_pld8/`; broader exploration `cusp_scan.gd` / `checks/cusp-scan-zc2nct6a/`.

3. **Spanwise flow affects source normalization despite unchanged section-plane inputs.** At α=14°, fixed u/w corresponding to a 20 m/s section-plane speed, zero rates and controls, changing v from 0 to ±10 m/s leaves every section-plane speed and angle unchanged but changes each source weight 0.959956→0.858610 and summed wing Fz −107.7514→−103.8262 N. Both signs give the same value; at α=20°, fully attenuated sources leave wing loads unchanged. The total-speed denominator in `aero.gd:354` explains this; the behavior sits uneasily with the shorthand comment at `:666–668` that the flat strips see no spanwise flow, but the normalization may be intentional. This strong-crossflow state is not physically validated. No generic change of wake law is recommended. Probe `runtime/spanflow_scan.gd`; receipt `checks/spanflow-scan-yajl9iha/`.

Checked runner success and completion markers are required; these are short fixed-state numerical probes, not accepted-tick flights, real-aircraft tests, or performance measurements. Tests do not establish why the source weighting was designed as it was. The previous frame mismatch, propeller-camber omission, negative-stall selector asymmetry, reverse-flow discontinuity, wing-wash absence, lower-rudder reference and ground-effect/wash convention are excluded as already known.

## Short sign, unit and coordinate pass

No additional distinct defect was established. The pilot-side degrees-to-aerodynamic-radians mapping negates the four conventional Extra surface angles (`control_surfaces.gd:9–19,25–35,55–63`); right aileron up decreases right-wing lift in the local law, elevator up produces a nose-up tail moment, and rudder right produces a leftward aft-tail force and nose-right yaw. These are code/sign inspections, not full control-session acceptance. Weather station sampling uses body FRD arms, body-to-NED position rotation, reference-minus-station wind converted back to body axes, and adds one `omega × arm` term (`wind_aircraft_flow.gd:230–245,284–345`); `test_wind_aircraft_flow.gd:184–188,234–267` supplies a hand-rotation oracle on a synthetic aircraft, not a new Extra-specific trajectory. Reading the local aero and slipstream source found no additional input-state mutation; this is not an exhaustive alias or lifecycle audit. E300-01/02 and the prior ledger findings remain separate and excluded.
