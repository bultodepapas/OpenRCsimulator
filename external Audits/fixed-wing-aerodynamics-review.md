# Fixed-wing aerodynamics review

**Verdict:** No new serious production defect is established by this review. The candidates below are retained to prevent treating a documented or uncertain modeling choice as a proven bug. Runtime source was reviewed at `3df2791752fe2c1faad132fcb11febbb0ae788d1`; it was not edited. Numerical checks used Godot `4.8.beta2.official.cafc17a19`, float64 state, an isolated sparse app copy and an isolated user profile. These checks are software/model consistency tests, not aircraft validation.

## Ruled out: slipstream and ground effect

`app/physics/aero.gd:637–650` scales tail downwash by the ground-effect factor; `app/physics/slipstream.gd:114–118` does not receive that factor when computing its washed-minus-free tail increment. The Extra, P-51 and Corsair stock data declare both modules. This is not reported as a new defect: `docs/research/ground-effect/M5-GE-1/README.md:72` explicitly documents that the slipstream tail increment remains unchanged by height. Changing that assumption would be a physics-law change requiring separate evidence.

## Ruled out: Extra lower-rudder longitudinal arm

The stock Extra has two vertical wake pieces. The second is the rudder below the fuselage, whose authored polygon-centroid x is `0.980531 m` versus the vertical tail's model reference `0.849684 m` (`app/data/aircraft/gp_extra_300s_60.json:1310–1320,1878–1955`; `research/extra-300/ex05/derive_physics.py:528–546`). `app/physics/slipstream.gd:576–591` uses the tail-reference x for segment loads, while the wake occupancy uses the piece root. On the unchanged stock model at 15 m/s, zero rates, 12,000 rpm and 25 degrees rudder, the lower piece contributes `Fy = 2.077987 N` and `Mz = −1.563346 N m`; all-piece wake yaw is `−4.462022 N m`. If the geometric centroid instead defined the aerodynamic force arm, the missing arm contribution would be `−0.130847 × 2.077987 = −0.271898 N m`. This is **not a proven error**: the generator calls it a polygon centroid, not a measured pressure center; the implementation states its legacy tail-reference convention, and `docs/research/propwash/E0b3b/README.md:17` explicitly describes reference-plane treatment for the smooth route. Do not assert that this geometric alternative is the correct pressure law without an independent contract or aerodynamic evidence.

`probe_piece_arms.gd` decomposes the untouched production wake into the actual stock pieces and checks that the sum equals the full wake in 20 cases. The `checks/piece-arms-*` runner report is `ok: true`, with zero decomposition failures. Only the temporary in-memory piece selection is changed for decomposition; the stock geometry, force law, and data remain intact. This is a force-component witness, not a measured handling change.

## Unresolved low-impact selector asymmetry

At `app/physics/aero.gd:792–795`, the initial global angle-of-attack selector uses `a1` for either sign; the wing-station selector at `:801,831–835` selects `a1` or `n1` according to sign. For the stock Turbo Lumber, `a1=15.095627°`, `n1=17.383578°`, and the attached limit is 8°. At −14°, 20 m/s, neutral controls and no rates, the unmodified selector weight is `0.9358366063`; a diagnostic variant that uses `n1` in the first selector gives `0.7037029102`. The production aerodynamic pitch moment is `5.118864385 N m`; the diagnostic variant gives `5.071713366 N m`, a difference of `0.047151019 N m` (about 0.92% of the production aerodynamic pitch moment). The isolated variant changes one expression solely to expose the difference; it is not a recommended or physically validated fix. It remains unproven that the selector must use the negative start, and no substantial flight consequence has been established.

`probe_stall_selector.gd` checks seven stock aircraft and the positive-angle controls against the one-expression diagnostic. The checked run reports `ok: true`, with zero load failures. `app/physics/aero_sign_probe.gd` in the isolated copy is generated from the exact source with that one substitution; the original checkout remains read-only. See `checks/stall-selector-*` for all source values and cases.
